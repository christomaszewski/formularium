# Aichi Prefecture 2019: grape downy mildew forecast and spray advice

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Advisory No. 4 of 2 July 2019 (Reiwa 1): downy mildew expected 'somewhat high' across the prefecture (l. 1-2, 10-11).
- June survey, 18 fields: diseased leaf rate 0.22 % (average 0.18 %, previous year 1.17 %); diseased-field rate 11.1 % (average 5.1 %, previous year 27.8 %) (l. 13-14).
- Both rates the third highest in ten years (l. 15).
- The one-month forecast gave more rain and less sunshine, favourable to infection (l. 16-17).
- Infection needs only small water drops; continuous rain spreads the disease quickly (l. 22).
- Soft young leaves, high-nitrogen orchards and young trees are most affected (l. 24).
- QoI resistance confirmed in Aichi in 2011; QoI products not to be used (l. 28-30).
