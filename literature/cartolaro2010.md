# Cartolaro et al. n.d.: early E. necator groups and bunch disease

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Powdery mildew (Erysiphe necator), southern France, 2007: 32 vineyards sampled in April, 16 again in September (l. 72-77).
- Of 659 flag-shoot isolates in April, 67% were group A and 33% group B; September lesions amplified less often (42.4%) and were all group B (l. 116-141).
- Spearman rho = 0.905 (P < 0.001) for cluster damage and early group B frequency, in 13 vineyards (l. 150).
- Only group B isolates detected at the start gave high final severity (l. 153).
- The authors suggest only group B makes the sexual stage (l. 250).
