# Mundy et al. 2022: cultural controls of Botrytis bunch rot in New Zealand

A review of non-chemical controls of botrytis bunch rot in New Zealand vineyards. It covers leaf removal, mechanical shaking and thinning, and calcium and nitrogen nutrition, and sets them against fungicide resistance.

## What it holds

- Losses of up to US$2 billion a year worldwide (l. 54). In cool regions such as New Zealand, direct losses of up to NZ$5000 per ha and control costs of NZ$1500 per ha (l. 54–56).
- Leaf removal cut bunch rot by 82% in a 1985 Californian trial on Chenin blanc (l. 416–418). Five New Zealand trials gave reductions of 41–86%, depending on the timing of removal (l. 420–423).
- Biocontrol gave up to 75% efficacy in one study, and only 20% in another (l. 119–121). Up to 10 biocontrol sprays may be needed in a warm, wet season (l. 133–134).
