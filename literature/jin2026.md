# Jin et al. 2026: a bowl-and-cover naturally ventilated radiation shield

A shield with a CFD-optimised airflow guide is compared in the field with a reference shield,
  and an MLP correction is fitted.

## What it holds

- **Problem:** near-surface radiation errors of about 1 C (l. 16-17).
- **Field test:** reference Model 076B and comparison Model 41003 shields; mean uncorrected
  error 0.12 C against 0.59 C for the 41003 (l. 24-28); a time series of 14 to 16 December 2025
  (l. 526).
- **Correction:** MLP with RMSE 0.051 C and MAE 0.043 C, r 0.999 with the reference (l. 28-32).

## Dependence

- CFD and a multilayer perceptron (record).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s5 (sensor error): sizes of radiation error by shield type, 0.12 against 0.59 C, and an error
  model from wind, scattered radiation and altitude. A simulator of vineyard logger bias could
  take the shield-type contrast. Not read in full.
