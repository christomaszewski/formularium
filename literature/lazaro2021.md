# Lázaro et al. 2021: a meta-analysis of 80 field experiments finds decision support systems use a median 43 % fewer fungicide sprays than calendar programs

A meta-analysis comparing decision-support-based and calendar-based fungicide programs, all crops, with untreated controls.

**Read this first.** An all-crop synthesis: grape downy mildew is one of the diseases covered (l. 101), but the grape subset is not separated in the main text, so none of its numbers is a grape figure. The experiments are researcher-run trials, so the numbers are for advice followed fully, not for how many growers follow it. Only the introduction, results and discussion were read; methods and the supplementary table of the 80 experiments were not.

## What it holds

- **Data set:** 80 field experiments in 22 articles, 1982 to 2015, all crops, comparing DSS-based and calendar-based fungicide programs with untreated controls (l. 21). Of the 80, 19 were in Europe (Italy, Lithuania, Spain), 51 in North America and 7 in Brazil (l. 73).
- **Spray counts:** the median number of fungicide sprays with DSSs was 43 % lower than with standard calendar programs (l. 379). Observed counts, first quartile / median / third quartile: DSS 3 / 4 / 6 sprays, calendar 4 / 7 / 10 sprays (l. 348).
- **Cost of halving sprays:** when sprays were halved with DSSs against the recommended calendar program, the increase in disease incidence never exceeded 5 % (l. 402); with calendar programs, halving the sprays raised incidence by about 10 %, twice the DSS figure (l. 409).
- **Disease coverage:** includes downy mildew of grape among the diseases (l. 101).

## Dependence

- Published experiments only (1982 to 2015); the supplementary data list the experiments (not fetched). The DSS programs were run by the researchers.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Savings when advice is followed (grower.dss_saving):** a median of 4 sprays with a DSS against 7 with a calendar program (IQR 3 to 6 against 4 to 10), 43 % fewer, across crops and countries (l. 348, 379). Kind: meta-analysis. It is an all-crop figure, to be weighed against the grape-specific trial savings (17 to 50 %) in `cabrefiga2025` and `deliere2015`.
- **Why it matters for a rule:** following a DSS when sprays are halved costs at most about 5 points of incidence, and a calendar program that halves its sprays about 10 points (l. 402, 409); a relative gap in incidence, not yield, and not specific to grape downy mildew.
- **Compliance:** not measured; trials are researcher-run.
