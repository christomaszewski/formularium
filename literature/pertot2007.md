# Pertot et al. 2007: organic control of grapevine downy mildew, SafeCrop booklet

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Organic-growing booklet (2nd ed. 2007) on downy mildew; the cycle chapter is the data part (l. 338-694).
- Oospores mature in winter, start to germinate from April or May, and can germinate up to five years after formation (l. 395, 487, 555).
- Oospore germination follows a degree-day sum above 8 °C from 1 January, 170 in the book (l. 399-402; Gehmann 1987).
- Primary infection needs 24 h mean above 10 °C, rain above 10 mm and a 10 cm shoot (l. 400-401); no source.
- Incubation: 5 days minimum, up to 18; Goidanich's table by temperature and humidity (l. 411, 442-443).
- Wetness for infection: 2-3 h at 20-25 °C, 9 h at 6 °C (l. 469-470; Blaeser 1987).
- Genotype data: 112 lesions (58 %) from one genotype at Bommes 2001; 142 and 370 genotypes in two Trento plots in 2000 (l. 598, 643).

## Dependence

- (1) Borrows: the book reproduces the Goidanich incubation table as Tab. 1 (l. 391-408 and l. 442-445), 'modificata da Goidanich, 1964'. That is the engine-list incubation table (Goidanich 1957 in the brief; the book cites 1964), so the two should be checked for being the same table. (2) Shares a form: the oospore germination threshold is a sum of daily temperatures above 8 °C from 1 January (170, Gehmann 1987), the same form as VitiMeteo's oospore rule (140 °C-days from 1 January), with a different value; the book computes nothing. (3) Shares a form: the primary-infection conditions (24 h mean above 10 °C, rain above 10 mm, shoot at least 10 cm; l. 400-401) are a temperature-and-rain start threshold of the kind of the 3-10 rule, with different numbers. (4) Shares a form: the wetness-duration table (2-3 h at 20-25 °C, 9 h at 6 °C, none above 29-32 °C; Blaeser 1987, l. 469-471) follows the temperature-dependent wetness form of Blaeser & Weltzien 1979; the book cites Blaeser 1987, a shared author. (5) Sporulation bounds (night, above 12 °C, optimum 18-20 °C, above 93 % humidity; l. 418-421) are temperature bounds of the kind of Lalancette 1988 and Caffi 2013; the book cites neither. Flags only: Gobbin, Gessler and Pertot co-wrote the Gobbin et al. 2003-2005 papers.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- It reproduces the engine-list Goidanich incubation table and the Blaeser wetness form, and gives observed genotype counts from two field plots, so the truth may cite its genotype data; its thresholds are unsourced in places and must not be borrowed for the truth.
