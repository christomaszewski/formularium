# Skakun et al. 2022: the Cloud Mask Intercomparison eXercise

Research article, Global (multi-site reference datasets for Landsat 8 and Sentinel-2).

## What it holds

- Ten cloud-masking algorithms for Landsat 8 and Sentinel-2 are scored against existing reference cloud-mask datasets, with confusion-matrix metrics per dataset.
- Agreement is good for thick cloud and poor for thin cloud, and results depend on the reference dataset. (The cover page prints the byline as 'Skakun, Swevers [sic], ... et al.'; the full list is in the manuscript, lines 3-8.)

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Cloud masks miss thin cloud, and the scores depend on the reference set: the clear-scene loss and error a satellite operator should carry.
