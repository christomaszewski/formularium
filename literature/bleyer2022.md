# Bleyer et al. 2022: VitiMeteo Plasmopara: twelve years of trials

Conference paper, Freiburg and Ihringen, Baden-Wuerttemberg, Germany.

## What it holds

- Gives the history of the VitiMeteo Plasmopara forecast model and reports 12 trial years (2010-2021) of a leaf-area-based spray strategy against untreated controls in Freiburg and Ihringen.
- The model's equations are not given; the trial results (Table 1, mean untreated severity 60.5%) are strategy validation data.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- Shared authors, flags only (D27): Bleyer, G., as an author of vitimeteo.oospores; Kassemeyer, H.-H., as an author of vitimeteo.oospores.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)
