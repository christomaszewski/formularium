# Prodorutti et al. 2006: seven seasons of oospore germination delay, Trentino

## What it holds

- Leaves from an untreated vineyard at Rovereto, 1998-2004; germination delay by Hill's
  floating leaf discs (15 discs a box, 20 °C, 7 days), 1999-2005; a delay over 14 days
  counted as no germination.
- No mathematical relation with weather found yet; germination needs days above 10 °C and
  rain; a warm, dry summer prevents fast germination.

## Dependence

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- A seven-season series of oospore readiness, the kind of observation VitiMeteo-type rules
  are tuned on (data shown only in figures).
