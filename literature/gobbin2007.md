# Gobbin et al. 2007: one infection event, 130 m

The Freiburg case behind "130 m once" in [rossi2013](rossi2013.md).

## What it holds

- **The event:** in vineyard PRO, between 10 and 13 June 2004, downy mildew incidence
  went from 0 to 99 % of vines.
  - Infected vines bore 45-60 lesions each, about 220,000 lesions per ha, not aggregated.
  - A second vineyard, FUT, about 50 m away, had been inoculated three weeks before.
- **Genotyping:**
  - 555 samples, 20 unique genotypes. One caused 80 % of sampled lesions in both vineyards.
  - The three genotypes behind 95 % of lesions were those inoculated in FUT.
- **Distance:** sporangial migration up to 130 m in a single infection event, "the first
  report of definitive and quantitative evidence".

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- **The far tail of a dispersal kernel:** one event can carry a clone 130 m and infect a
  whole vineyard at once, against the usual under 20 m per cycle. A truth's kernel should
  allow it rarely.
- **A case for a near-total, non-aggregated infection** from a nearby heavy source in one
  wet spell.
