# Magyar et al. 2017: integrated forecasting of airborne fungi, Hungary

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Review of integrated disease-triangle forecasting for airborne fungi in Hungary; no new data (l. 48-57).
- Hirst-type spore trap: 10 L a minute, 14.4 m3 a day (l. 257); drum 2 mm an hour, one full turn in 7 days (l. 259).
- Spore trapping can warn 18 to 20 days before infection (l. 313-314) and cut tomato spraying cost by 50 to 80 % (l. 311-312), both cited.
- A Spanish Plasmopara spore-trap model explains 95.9 % of spore variance (l. 323-324, cited).
- That model is listed as combining the Goidanich index with spores (l. 509, ref 31).
- Poland's SPEC: ten stations since 2004 (l. 333); 71.0 % of growers use it (l. 328), cited.
- Hungary's ÁNTSZ network: 19 stations, one per 20 km radius (l. 351, l. 357).
