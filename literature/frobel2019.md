# Fröbel & Zyprian 2019: histology of P. viticola in grapevine tissues

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Greenhouse inoculations at 30,000 sporangia per ml, leaves collected at 6 days, climate chambers at 80% RH and 16 h light at 23 °C (l. 84-89, 169).
- An unsprayed field plot at Geilweilerhof: first natural infections on 1 May 2018; every susceptible cultivar infected, the resistant Solaris not (l. 109, 203-208).
- Petiole classes from 104 samples: 17 uninfected, 60 with mycelium and haustoria, 21 with fan-shaped hyphae, 6 fully colonized (l. 267-276).
- 50 of 51 shoots infected (l. 282).
- Berries sampled 11 June and 27 August 2018; August seeds infected, penetrating about the first 40% of the seed volume (l. 338, 352, 359).
- Infected seeds lacked endosperm and embryo, so they are dysfunctional (l. 378).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- The field classes and dates from one unsprayed 2018 plot are observed data a truth could use to check where and when infection shows in the canopy, but it records no weather or dates of infection, so it cannot test an infection or incubation model.
