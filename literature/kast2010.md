# Kast & Bleyer 2010: OiDiag-2.2 for powdery mildew sprays

## What it holds

- From over 53 years of Weinsberg records: first spray = three-leaf date + (1.3T - 5D + 12)
  days, T the lowest winter temperature and D last year's disease (r 0.45 and 0.35; no n
  or R²). The paper's own example (T = -10, D = 5) gives -26 days, not the stated result.
- A daily index = ontogenetic term x climate term; temperature term ((0.11TM - 0.0025TM²) -
  0.6) x 1.63 from Delp 1954; humidity (H80 + 0.7 H65)/24; rain and wetness weights.
  Spray intervals 6-23 days by index class and fungicide (Table 1).
- 2009: 34 of 43 vineyards matched or beat the system's timing; disease at most 2.4 %.

## Dependence

- None on the engine's models (the UC Davis index is a different form).
