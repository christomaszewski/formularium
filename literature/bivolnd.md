# Bivol et al. n.d.: new fungicides against grape mildew, Moldova

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Nine named products (e.g. Profilux, Zahist, Champ, Coprantol) were tested against downy mildew on Chardonnay at Ialoveni, Moldova, 2012-2015 (l. 51, 141, 186).
- The untreated control had 68% (leaves) and 74% (bunches) of organs attacked (l. 177, 210).
- Efficacy ranged from 89.3% (leaves) and 91.0% (bunches) for Profilux to 79.1% and 77.1% for Coprantol (l. 184, 193).
- The standard, Presto Plus at 3.0 kg/ha, gave 90.1% and 92.2% efficacy (l. 181, 216).
- The untreated intensity is printed as 47% and 56% in the text but 64% and 78% in Table 2 (l. 212, 177).
- Sprays were 7-10 per season from May to July (l. 159-161).
- Site: 340-435 mm rain, 3200-3300 C thermal sum, about 180 days above 10 C (l. 77, 144, 149).

## Dependence

- none. The paper runs no weather, infection, incubation, sporulation or wetness model. Spray timing is set by monitoring of phenology, attack intensity, oospore reserve and variety resistance (l. 86-89), with no named model. No shared equation, code or data with the engine models.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Little for the truth or the engine: a Moldovan copper and metiram efficacy trial with no weather series and an untreated control that cannot be reproduced from its own table, so it can be cited for the product efficacy only with that caveat.
