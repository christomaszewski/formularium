# Kast & Bleyer 2010: spray timing against grapevine powdery mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Field spray trials at Weinsberg, Württemberg, 1999 to 2009, on three varieties, 12-vine plots, four replicates (l. 43-47).
- Regime 1 (three sprays, from one week before flowering to berries 2 mm) against Regime 2 (seven sprays) (l. 14-16, 63).
- Mean disease reduction: 88.7% for three sprays, 92.6% for seven (Table 2, l. 175; the 92.6 read from the page image).
- A 2002 sprayer accident left an 18-day gap in the window; both regimes fell to 46.2% reduction (l. 125, 167).
- The window is named the Open Window Period, from BBCH 55 to 73 (l. 136).
- The findings became an ontogenetic index in OiDiag (l. 150).

## Dependence

- none on the engine's list. The chapter says its window findings were built into OiDiag as an ontogenetic index (l. 149-150); OiDiag is a spray-timing model that is not on the engine list (see the kast2010 note).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A ten-year field record that the spray-timing window around flowering matters more than the number of sprays, and that gaps inside it cannot be made up later, which a truth could use as observed fungicide-efficacy data but not as infection timing.
