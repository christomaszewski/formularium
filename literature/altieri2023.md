# Altieri et al. 2023: biocontrol agents on berry substrates and grey mould control

Four commercial biocontrol agents grew on artificial berry substrates at four stages and 15 to 35 °C. Their control of grey mould on ripe berries depended on temperature.

## What it holds

- Four BCAs: Bacillus amyloliquefaciens (BAD), Aureobasidium pullulans (APD), Metschnikowia fructicola (MFN) and Trichoderma atroviride (TAS) (l. 13-14).
- Substrates mimicked four berry stages, from pea-sized to ripe (l. 15-16).
- Population size: BAD rose with temperature and MFN fell. APD and TAS were bell-shaped, lower at 15 or 35 °C (l. 17-21).
- Control on ripe berries, applied 1 to 13 days before inoculation: highest at 25 °C for BAD and APD, 15 to 20 °C for MFN, and 25 to 30 °C for TAS (l. 22-23).

## Dependence

- None recorded. The introduction cites the weather-driven Botrytis risk model (refs 4 and 27) as context, not as an input.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (grey mould), with t1 for the spray-to-infection gap. The pattern by temperature (best at 25 °C for BAD and APD) could rank biocontrol products in a growers' engine.
- Tests used artificial substrates and detached berries in controlled temperatures. There are no field data. Not read in full.
