# Gatti et al. 2016: pruning after budburst delays the season

Twelve potted Sangiovese vines in Piacenza, 2015, pruned on 19 February (dormant), 13
April (two leaves unfolded) or 29 April (seven leaves): the only paper in the search that
measures the pruning-date effect on budburst in days. A pot trial of one season, with
whole-canopy gas exchange.

## What it holds

- **Treatments:** standard winter pruning 19 February; late at BBCH 12 (13 April, day
  103); very late at BBCH 17 (29 April, day 119); five-year-old vines on SO4 in 40 L pots,
  four blocks of three (l. 141-149).
- **Phenology** (Table 1): budburst day 103, 120 and 134, so 17 and 31 d later; flowering
  12 and 22 d later; onset of ripening 3 and 13 d; berries touching 26 and 36 d; 19 °Brix
  on day 238, 3 d earlier (late) and 6 d later (very late) (l. 311-323). Full bloom to
  ripening onset 53 d against 62 (l. 300); budburst to harvest 135 d against 113 (l.
  311); heat from budburst to ripe 1,784, 1,657, 1,647 degree-days, base and source not
  printed (l. 313).
- **Canopy:** leaf area removed at pruning 0.32 and 1.09 m², 9 and 39 % of the final
  canopy (l. 547-553); peak net CO2 exchange per leaf area 9.69, 13.3 and 11.8 µmol/m²/s,
  reached 34, 22 and 22 d after the first positive gain (l. 401-406); cumulated carbon by
  day 309 1,042, 1,243 and 772 g a vine (l. 414; the abstract's +17 % is about +19 %).
- **Yield:** 1,765, 1,271 (−28 %; the discussion says 26 %) and 145 g a vine (Table 3,
  l. 564-566); yield against removed leaf area y = −0.1397x + 1721.5, R² 0.87 (Figure 9);
  a recommendation to remove no more than 10 % of the final leaf area (l. 1066).
- **Fits:** quadratic shoot elongation on day of year per treatment (l. 351-355); shoot
  length against leaf area y = 0.67x² + 2.76x (l. 188).

## Dependence

- **None:** descriptive fits; gas exchange after Long & Hallgren 1985.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- Chris's pruning-date item, measured: pruning 7 and 11 weeks after dormant pruning
  delayed budburst 17 and 31 d but harvest only −3 and +6 d, the season compressing
  behind it. One pot trial; a direction and size, not a model.
