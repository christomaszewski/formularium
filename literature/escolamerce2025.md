# Escola Agrària Mercè Rossell i Domènech, INCAVI & Sanitat Vegetal 2025: programme of the Penedès plant-health day of 2025-12-03

The one-page leaflet of a technical day on the 2025 plant-health season in the Penedès, with a short presentation text and a list of talks.

**Read this first.**
- It is a programme, not the talks. The season review it lists was not obtained, and the leaflet prints no data, dates of first spots, rain or spray counts.
- The leaflet's presentation text is the organisers' summary of the season, with no stated method.

## What it holds

- **Event:** a technical day at Sant Sadurní d'Anoia on 2025-12-03, in the hall of the Escola Agrària de Viticultura i Enologia Mercè Rossell i Domènech; organised by the Department of Agriculture, Livestock, Fisheries and Food, with INCAVI and the Servei de Sanitat Vegetal.
- **Season summary (l. 12):** the 2025 season in the Penedès "was complex" for plant health.
- **Downy mildew (l. 15):** the many spring rains caused an "extremely early" start of the first mildew infections, which obliged growers to work hard to protect vines (l. 17).
- **Control (l. 19):** good management of sprays and the lack of rain from the end of May allowed good control, with insignificant economic damage (l. 19). Later in the season the grape moth and Cryptoblabes caused trouble in places; not downy mildew.
- **The review it lists (l. 19):** a talk at 09:35 on the plant-health balance of the 2025 season, by Mireia Galimany Vives, technician of the Estació d'Avisos del Penedès, Servei de Sanitat Vegetal. The leaflet gives no content for it.
- **Other talks:** green leafhopper monitoring (IRTA) and trials, grape moths and mating disruption, drones for sprays, and the integrated farm record (Quadern Integrat d'Explotació).
