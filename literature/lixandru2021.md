# Lixandru & Fendrihan n.d.: downy mildew and gray rot under leaf treatments, Romania

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Hotarele (Giurgiu County, Romania), 2021, Muscat Ottonel: 3 blocks of P and K doses (100-200 kg/ha) with foliar fertiliser at 0.4, 0.5 and 0.6% (l. 63-73).
- Abstract: downy mildew 0.01% and grey rot 0.04% in the best plots, against controls at 0.13% and 0.54% (l. 17-18). Results give the control downy value as 0.10% (l. 100).
- Assessed on 50 leaves and 50 bunches per variant; climate is Oltenita station monthly means (l. 74-79).
- Best quality: V8 and V12, sugar 209.1 and 213.7 g/L (l. 192, 200).
- Cites Romanian attack levels (2019, 1.36-5.63%; Tarnave grey rot 0.5-1.5%) and the Kennelly and 3-10-type thresholds (l. 33-49).
