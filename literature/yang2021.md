# Yang et al. 2021: STICS calibrated on flowering, harvest and yield in the Douro

STICS v9.1 run for Touriga Nacional and Touriga Franca on four Douro plots, 2014 to 2019,
with a grid of 703,125 parameter vectors a site and an objective function that adds the
nRMSE of flowering, harvest and yield. Flowering is a degree-day sum from budburst to a
threshold. The first author's surname is Yang; the PDF is named chenyao_2021 after the
given name.

## What it holds

- **Site and data** (l. 145-151, 197-204, 280-284): Douro Demarcated Region, about
  250,000 ha, mean slope 30 % (l. 150-151). Four plots, single and double cordon, six
  years. Table 1 six-year means: flowering day of year 132, 139, 135, 140; harvest 261,
  262, 261, 261; yield 5156, 6871, 5793, 5263 kg/ha. Flowering is the day 50 % of sampled
  vines are open (l. 222). Yearly values are in a supplement (Table S1), not here.
- **Phenology form** (l. 311-314, 354, 375): dormancy to budburst by BRIN (l. 311-312; its
  equations and parameters are not printed here). After budburst a growing degree-day sum
  with base 10 °C (l. 313-314). Flowering depends on one parameter, RG (stlevdrp), 250 to
  450 °Cd in Table 2 (l. 354, 375).
- **Other Table 2 ranges:** VG (juvenile onset to veraison) 600 to 1400 °Cd, FS 50 to 350,
  WD 100 to 300 (l. 364-378). VG sets the length of the LAI period, which stops after
  veraison (l. 836). Seven genotype parameters, five values each, 703,125 combinations a
  site (l. 337-339).
- **Fitted values** (Table 3, l. 805-811, 1083): under the objective function RG is 250
  (Touriga Nacional, single cordon) and 300 for the other three (l. 810, 1083); VG 800 or
  1000 for the first and 600 for the others (l. 809); WD 300, 250 or 300, 150 or 200, 300
  (l. 811).
- **Harvest and yield** (l. 349-352, 1144): harvest when berry water content reaches 77 %
  (sugar relation after Garcia de Cortazar-Atauri et al., cited); 12.5 % alcohol is the
  criterion in the discussion. Yield is cluster number (fixed at 11, 19, 8, 10 a vine,
  l. 289) times cluster weight times density.
- **Fit on the four plots** (Table 4, objective-function column, l. 1053-1068, 993-996):
  flowering MBE 4, 1, 1, 2 d and MAE 4, 4, 4, 4 d; harvest MBE -2, -1, -1, -2 d and MAE 6,
  4, 10, 5 d (the text says 3 to 6 d, 10 and 12 for harvest under Touriga Franca, l. 994);
  yield MBE -232, -16, 104, 159 kg/ha and nRMSE 24, 25, 26, 31 %; yield MAE 1168 to
  1417 kg/ha (l. 994). Phenology nRMSE is below 20 % for all vectors, median 4 to 8 %
  (l. 554-555).
- **Evaluation** (Table 5, l. 1161-1178): double cordon, Touriga Nacional 2012 to 2014 and
  Touriga Franca 2012 to 2013, the data Fraga et al. 2015 used. Flowering MBE 10 and 3 d,
  nRMSE 7 and 2 %; harvest MBE 0 and -15 d (from the reading's summary); yield MBE -466 and
  619 kg/ha.
- **Leaf area:** not simulated or measured here; LAI is named as a need (l. 864).
- **Not printed:** budburst day, days from budburst to flowering, canopy porosity, the
  BRIN equations, the yearly dates.

## Dependence

- **Shares model and data with Fraga et al. 2015.** The model is STICS v9.1 as Fraga et
  al. calibrated it for the Douro (l. 157-158, 308); the Table 5 evaluation data are those
  Fraga et al. calibrated on (l. 524-527, 1161). Fixed parameters, such as the dormancy
  chilling requirement, are taken from Fraga et al. (l. 341-342), and RG is close to
  theirs (l. 859-860).
- **Borrowed:** harvest sugar equations from Garcia de Cortazar-Atauri et al. (l. 351-352);
  initial reserves after Bates et al. (l. 211-212); objective function and nRMSE weights
  after Buis et al. 2019 and Wallach et al. 2016 (l. 399-400).
- **Inputs:** E-OBS, ERA5-Land and HWSD soil, public data sets (l. 233-241, 1277-1278). A
  shared input is not a dependence. Reis is a co-author and cited for the flowering data
  (l. 200, 223): a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **The engine's forms:** a degree-day sum from budburst above 10 °C to a threshold (RG,
  250 to 300 °Cd fitted) and dormancy by BRIN. That is the heat-sum threshold the truth
  must not share with the engine.
- **Truth candidate:** no. It shares model and data with Fraga et al. 2015, and simulates
  no LAI. No record.
- **Usable as observed values:** the Table 1 means and the Table 5 evaluation errors, as a
  weak check on flowering and harvest dates in the Douro.
