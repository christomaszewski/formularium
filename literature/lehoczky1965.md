# Lehoczky 1965: oospores of P. viticola in floating grape leaf discs

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Floating grape leaf discs (18 mm, ten per dish, 50 ml water) inoculated in five places, at 19 to 25 °C in diffuse light (l. 42-45).
- Conidiophores appeared within 5 to 7 days; positive results in 12 to 36% of inoculation sites (l. 68-69).
- Oogonia or young oospores seen 9 to 12 days after inoculation; up to 86 oospores in a 4.8 mm2 area (l. 73-76).
- Oospores 24.5 to 45.5 µm, most common 31.5 µm (l. 77).
- Grunzel's view that late-summer cold does not start oospores is supported by these lab results (l. 79).
