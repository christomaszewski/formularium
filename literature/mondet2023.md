# Mondet et al. 2023: how fast vine leaf litter breaks down, with and without woodlice

Microcosms of 10 g of Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Sauvignon or lime leaves at 20 °C for 49
days, with or without ten woodlice: the mass lost, what the woodlice ate and assimilated,
and how the litters' traits explain it. Numbers for the litter that carries downy mildew's
oospores through the winter, from the laboratory.

## What it holds

- **Setup** (l. 63, 264, 282-315): three of five grape litters kept (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir,
  Sauvignon) and lime as control; 200 Armadillidium vulgare of 58.5 ± 22 mg in groups of
  ten; 10 g of leaves a microcosm, 20 °C, 49 days.
- **Mass loss without woodlice** (l. 494-496): Sauvignon 38.65 ± 1.71 %, Chardonnay 30.98 ±
  0.82 %, lime 21.94 ± 1.30 % in 49 days. **With woodlice** (l. 500-502): Sauvignon 54.79 ±
  1.82 %, lime 48.95 ± 1.29 %, Pinot Noir 35.82 ± 1.82 %.
- **Feeding** (l. 370-387, 512-537): consumption by David 1998's formula with the control's
  decomposability, lime 0.084 and Pinot Noir 0.031 g g⁻¹ d⁻¹; feces 0.070 g g⁻¹ d⁻¹ on lime;
  assimilation 0.014 (lime), 0.017 (Chardonnay), -0.004 (Pinot Noir); efficiency 16.7, 34.5,
  37.8 % and a Pinot Noir value (-3.9 %) that does not follow from its parts.
- **Traits** (l. 614-700): lime N 1.02 %, lignin about 13 %; Pinot Noir copper 981 mg/kg.
- **Animals** (l. 544-550, 726): 17 deaths, none tied to the litter (P 0.39); mortality
  given as 5-17.5 % elsewhere; mass gain printed in g where mg is meant.
- **Not printed in this copy:** Table 1 and the supplementary tables; no decay rate is
  fitted.

## Dependence

- Observed masses; the consumption formula is David 1998's (borrowed, not fitted). Brunetto
  is cited (l. 104); `brunetto2018.leaf_litter_decay` is the held decay record, with no
  values to check here.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- For Agrarium's autumn inoculum strand (the 10-18 search): 22-39 % mass loss of vine leaf
  litter in 49 days at 20 °C without macrofauna, 36-55 % with woodlice, cultivar-dependent;
  a laboratory point beside `brunetto2018.leaf_litter_decay`. No record.
