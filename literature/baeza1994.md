# Baeza 1994: leaf area of irrigated Tempranillo on four training systems, Madrid

A Madrid doctoral thesis comparing a curtain, a VSP double Guyot (espaldera) and two bush
vines, 1990-1992, for canopy, light, physiology and yield. Read for leaf area per shoot and
per vine.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 4029-4040, 4319-4357): Tempranillo on Richter 110 planted 1982, ETSI Agrónomos
  campus, Madrid, 595 m; drip 298, 378 and 328 mm (1990-1992) against rain of 353 to 376 mm
  and ET0 about 1,209 mm. Spacings: curtain 3.0 × 1.75 m, espaldera 2.0 × 1.35 m (double
  Guyot), high bush 3.0 × 1.90, low bush 2.7 × 1.10. Shoots a vine after green pruning,
  espaldera 11.9, 18.3, 25.9 (1990-1992). Stages (l. 4258-4262): budburst 26 March 1990, 13 April
  1991, 16 April 1992; flowering 2, 2 and 4 June; veraison 17 July, 2 and 4 August; harvest
  6, 10 and 21 September. Topping through the season, dates not given (l. 4453).
- **Leaf area** (Tabla 23, l. 5150-5175; from shoot-length regressions on marked shoots
  stripped after harvest; coefficients not printed). Shoots per m², leaf area a shoot (m²,
  unit inferred), LAI, exposed area SA, LAI/SA, espaldera: 4.392, 0.481, 2.108, 1.577, 1.349
  (1990); 6.772, 0.343, 2.331, 1.678, 1.387 (1991); 9.577, 0.330, 3.172, 1.721, 1.837 (1992).
  The other systems run LAI 1.22 to 3.70, 0.33 to 0.70 m² a shoot. Some cells are garbled
  in the text layer (curtain 1990 shoots "3.2.65", LAI "1.83ía").
- **Per vine** (my calculation, LAI × ground): espaldera 5.7, 6.3 and 8.6 m² (1990-1992);
  curtain 9.6 to 18.3; bushes 5.3 to 15.1.
- **Canopy** (l. 5336-5358, 5895; Figure 22a, approximate): espaldera width about 0.31 to 0.68
  m; curtain about 0.6 m taller; espaldera cluster-zone leaf layers 1.67 with 1 % gaps (1992).
- **Cited** (l. 2920): full leaf development 30 to 40 days after budburst (Kriedemann et al.
  1970, via Chaves 1986), a single leaf, not the canopy.
- **Not printed:** LAI by date, main against lateral leaf area, leaf counts, single-leaf size.

## Dependence

- Point quadrat after Smart 1982; LAI/SA index after Smart 1985; the leaf-area regressions
  are the author's own. Lissarrague directs this thesis and the later Madrid ones
  (`linares2009`, `fernandezgomez2013`): a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Irrigated Spanish VSP, an upper comparator:** 0.33 to 0.48 m² of leaf a shoot after
  topping; at Agrarium's 15 shoots that is 5.0 to 7.2 m² a vine. Unirrigated vines carry
  less.
