# Troendle 2017: cost and benefit of virus-tested grapevines

An MSc thesis on the cost and benefit of virus-tested vines against leafroll disease in California and New York.

## What it holds

- **California, 2006 to 2015**: cumulative benefits of $1.51 billion (2002 dollars) against $12.9 million of costs, a benefit–cost ratio of 117 (l. 45–47; l. 1678–1680). Net benefits reach $3.26 billion by 2025 (l. 49–51).
- **New York**: benefits of $19.5 million by 2025, against $2.7 million of funding for the grape centre. Benefits do not start until 2020 (l. 52–57).
- **Welfare**: grape growers gain most, because supply is inelastic relative to demand (l. 63).

## Dependence

- The yield loss and cost inputs are the thesis's own. Its 25 % yield reduction and 20 % initial incidence are assumptions (l. 2189–2190).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t5 (damage and economics): a loss-and-cost structure a growers' engine could take when planting stock is the choice. Its yield loss is assumed, not measured.
