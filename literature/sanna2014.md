# Sanna et al. 2014: how station calibration moves EPI's warning

## What it holds

- **Subject:** metrology. A station's air temperature was calibrated against a reference
  thermometer (six points, residues up to 0.037 °C) and its RH at INRiM; EPI was then run
  with and without the measurement uncertainty.
- **EPI** (Strizyk 1983): potential plus kinetic energy; no equations printed.
- **Result:** without uncertainty EPI starts the risk on 13 April; the uncertainty variants
  move that by 7, 7, 12 and 8 days. Symptoms were seen on 9-10 May.
- **Not printed:** site, varieties, year and EPI's version.

## Dependence

- Computes EPI (`epi1983.corrected`, in an unprinted version).
- The reference infection date is back-calculated from symptoms with Giosuè et al. 2002 and
  Caffi et al. 2011's incubation, so EPI is scored against a model's date.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Only a reminder that sensor error of a few tenths of a degree moves a threshold model's
  date by a week or more.
