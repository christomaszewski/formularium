# Puopolo et al. 2014: Lysobacter compound toxic to Plasmopara sporangia

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Lab and greenhouse trials: L. capsici AZ78 culture filtrates gave 74 % protection against P. infestans on tomato (l. 364) and 66.2 % against Pl. viticola on grapevine (l. 368).
- Cells gave 92.4 % on grapevine (l. 369); copper hydroxide 90.7 % (l. 370).
- Sporangia of both oomycetes kept about 80 % viability in untreated controls after 60 min (l. 378).
- Fractions A and B cut P. infestans sporangial viability to 49 and 34 % (l. 380); Pl. viticola fell to 19 % (l. 382).
- The active compound is cyclo(L-Pro-L-Tyr), identified by NMR and MS (l. 389).
- On detached tomato leaves, cyclo(L-Pro-L-Tyr) at 100 ug/ml gave 26.67 +/- 4.21 % (l. 425); sporangia used at 2.5 x 10^5 per ml (l. 134).
