# Dry & Coombe 2004: the modified E-L growth-stage scale

One page: Figure 7.3 of the chapter on phenology, the modified Eichhorn-Lorenz scale
revised from Coombe 1995, with the major stages marked. The reference for the stage codes
the catalogue's phenology records use.

## What it holds

- **Budburst** is E-L 4, leaf tips visible (l. 14); the 1995 system's stage 4 was removed
  and budburst moved to the former stage 5, and not all varieties show woolly bud or
  green tip (caption, l. 85-86, after May 2000).
- **Shoots 10 cm** is E-L 12, five leaves separated (l. 23-25); flowering begins at E-L 19
  (about 16 leaves, l. 35); **flowering** is E-L 23, 17-20 leaves separated and 50 % of
  caps off (l. 44); setting E-L 27 (berries over 2 mm), pepper-corn E-L 29 (4 mm), pea
  size E-L 31 (7 mm) (l. 48-58).
- **Veraison** is E-L 35, berries beginning to colour and enlarge (l. 69); **harvest** E-L
  38, berries harvest-ripe, with no sugar value (l. 73); over-ripe 39, cane maturation
  complete 41, leaf fall 43 to 47 (l. 74-82).
- No data, model or number beyond the stage definitions.

## Dependence

- **None:** a scale. Coombe 1995 is the source; May 2000 the note on budburst stages.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- The stage codes behind `ferguson2014.prosser`, `schrader2020.iowa` and `saxena2023`
  (E-L 4 budburst) and the BBCH equivalents the European records use.
