# Viret et al. 2017: Changins oospore maturity only from 8 May 2017, four weeks after the 160 C sum

A Swiss vintage review in which a warm, dry spring delayed oospore maturity: germination within 24 h only from 8 May at Changins, while the 160 C sum was reached around 10 April.

**Read this first.**
- The 24 May first spots came from infections of a leaf litter strongly contaminated in 2016 (l. 491 to 492), not from an untreated control plot. The leads file notes that Dubuis 2019 prints 23 May for the same year.
- The 10 April date is the model's temperature sum (160 C), not an observation. The review says this sum is "généralement très bien corrélée" (generally very well correlated) with oospore maturity, and that 2017 is an exception.

## What it holds

- **Oospore maturity (l. 393):** effective maturity (germination in 24 h) only from 8 May at Changins, with the warm, dry spring said to have slowed the winter eggs markedly (l. 390 to 391).
- **Model sum (l. 393 to 394, 485):** the sum of temperature at the 8 C threshold reached 160 C around 10 April 2017, about four weeks before the test date (arithmetic: 28 days).
- **Authors' reading (l. 485 to 487):** under warm, dry spring conditions the maturity of the winter eggs is shifted in time ("L'expérience montre", experience shows).
- **First infections (l. 488 to 490):** Agrometeo indicated them on 25 and 26 April with very little foliage and a minor risk, so no fungicide was needed at that time.
- **First oil spots (l. 491):** observed on 24 May at Changins, from the infections of 19 May on a litter of vine leaves strongly contaminated in 2016.

## Dependence

- **Own observations** of the Changins group (the germination test, wording "germination en 24 heures"); the 10 April date and the first infections are from the Agrometeo/VitiMeteo temperature sum and infection model. Dubuis et al. 2012 say the test serves to validate VitiMeteo.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **literature/oospore_maturity_2026-11-03.csv (Agrarium D67):** adds Changins 2017-05-08 (laboratory test) and, as a model row, 2017-04-10 (160 C). It is a year where the temperature sum was wrong by four weeks, the case most relevant to an oospore model starting too early.
