# Kaya et al. 2025: three spray programmes for powdery mildew, table grapes, Bursa

A two-season field trial on two table grape varieties. It compares the UC Davis risk index,
a DSS spray model and the Turkish phenology calendar, with an unsprayed control, and it
measures fungicide residues at harvest. The UC Davis and calendar programmes did best.

## What it holds

- Field: Michele Palieri and Muscat Hamburg on 1103 Paulsen, Bursa Uludağ University,
  2021 and 2022; three replicates (l. 20, 128).
- Leaf severity efficacy, Abbott-corrected (Table 6, l. 144 to 147):
  - Michele Palieri 2021: UC Davis 90.18%, calendar 82.32%, DSS 46.35%; control 57.00%.
  - Michele Palieri 2022: 77.71%, 80.86%, 39.06%; control 83.58%.
  - Muscat Hamburg 2021: 95.36%, 100%, 72.24%; control 23.70%.
  - Muscat Hamburg 2022: 90.70%, 79.11%, 61.64%; control 39.16%.
- Residue: in 2021, thiophanate-methyl at harvest was over the Turkish limit of 0.1 mg/kg in
  the calendar and UC Davis plots of both varieties: 0.497 and 0.613 mg/kg (Michele Palieri),
  0.989 and 1.292 mg/kg (Muscat Hamburg) (l. 173; table l. 179, 182). The text names only
  Michele Palieri. DSS and control were under the limit. Kresoxim-methyl and penconazole
  were under it in all plots (l. 174).
- The text gives the 2021 calendar severity as 10.8% (l. 139); Table 6 gives 10.08%.

## Dependence

- Runs the UC Davis risk index (Gubler-Thomas, ref. 9) and the DSS model (ref. 21, 11) as
  published, with the Turkish Ministry calendar. No data or equations are taken from another
  study.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t3 (powdery mildew) and t1 (fungicide protection). Weather-timed programmes beat a
  calendar on one site and held up in a second season. A decision engine could test a
  risk-index rule against a calendar on the same weather.
- Residue is a real limit on the choice. Thiophanate-methyl went over the limit in four
  plots, all calendar or UC Davis, in 2021 (table l. 179, 182), so a fungicide choice needs
  a residue check. Two seasons and
  small plots: a site test, not a general rule. Not read in full.
