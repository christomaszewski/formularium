# Gülbasar Kandilli et al. 2022: disease and fruit quality of 12 Vitis spp.

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Twelve Vitis cultivars at Yalova, Turkey, grown in 2019 and 2020 with four fungicide sprays per season and natural infection (l. 40, 283).
- Winkler index 2133 degree-days on the 2019-2020 mean, against 1884 long-term (l. 484; Table 4).
- Hydrothermic index 4476 C mm on the mean, above the 2500 mildew-risk threshold (l. 441, Table 4).
- Powdery mildew scored higher than downy mildew in both years (l. 640); Alden the most resistant to both (l. 644).
- Downy mildew mean 2.4 for Ülkemiz and Rizpem (l. 667); the conclusion's 'not affected' overstates this (l. 687-688).
