# Hegyi-Kaló et al. 2019: noble rot by year, month and cultivar

Research article, Somolya vineyard, Eger wine region, Hungary.

## What it holds

- Three-year study (2013-2015) of noble rot incidence and in vitro mycelial growth of Botrytis cinerea isolates at 15, 20 and 25 °C, plus morphological types, by year, month and cultivar (Turán, Olaszrizling).
- It is isolate-phenotype data for noble rot, not a weather-driven infection or risk formulation.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Noble rot isolate phenotypes in Eger; not a weather-driven formulation. Nothing for either tool.
