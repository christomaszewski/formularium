# Su et al. 2022: leaf area index of Thompson Seedless as a curve of degree-days

Five curve forms of relative LAI against degree-days from one season of drip-irrigated
table grapes in Turpan, with the maximum LAI a line on seasonal water consumption.

## What it holds

- **Data:** 12-year-old Thompson Seedless at Tie-Tier village, 12 km south-east of
  Turpan (42.87 °N, 89.20 °E, 32.8 m), 1 April to 1 October 2009, six drip quotas of
  637.5-1,215 mm, three vines a treatment on 15 sampling days (l. 165-168, 380-418).
  LAI from leaf counts and a vein-length allometry (0.4829 L^2.6655, R² 0.9946, p. 4).
- **Degree-days** (p. 5, Eqs 2-5): base 10 °C, maximum and minimum capped at 38 °C
  (FAO method); the start date is not printed. Two prunings at about 400 and 700 °Cd;
  the measured LAI peak near 1,690 °Cd (l. 579-581).
- **Curves** (Table 3, p. 9; `su2022.lai_curve`), RLAI = LAI/LAIm fitted to the six
  treatments' mean: modified logistic 1.4 / (1 + exp(3.7437 − 4.8303e-3 GDD +
  1.2340e-6 GDD²)), R² 0.9837, relative error 7.45 %; log-normal 0.9771 exp(−0.5
  (ln(GDD/2274.5)/0.9142)²), 0.9805, 8.16 %; modified Gaussian 1.0146, 1983.1, 983.96,
  2.5757 (0.9877, 6.49 %); Gaussian 1.0924, 1979.6, 862.44 (0.9671, 10.60 %); cubic
  (0.9881). The fitted peaks sit at 1,980-2,275 °Cd against the measured 1,690.
- **Maximum LAI** by treatment X1-X6: 5.84, 5.26, 4.27, 3.78, 3.18, 2.73 (Table 4), and
  LAIm = 0.0056 WS − 0.7418 on seasonal water consumption WS in mm, R² 0.9654 (l. 919-952).
- **Yield** from LAIm through a harvest index quadratic (Eqs 27-30), errors to 3.1 %.

## Dependence

- **Fitted** to its own 2009 observations; the curve forms are cited to earlier crop
  work (refs 37, 40, 41). Nothing engine-related by equation or data.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **Growth on thermal time:** LAI is a function of a degree-day sum alone, the engine's
  form (its stages and shoots run on degree-days above 10 °C), so a kin report lists it
  with the engine's six degree-day pieces by shared structure. Said so, not hidden.
- One season, one table-grape cultivar, a desert climate, and the peak lands late: the
  curve's shape is the usable part: the modified logistic peaks at 1.02 near 1,960 °Cd and
  passes half of that near 840 °Cd (own calculation). Read for the
  truth growth search.
