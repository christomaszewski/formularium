# Junk et al. 2016: a night-time RH, temperature and rain rule for wheat leaf rust, run on hourly output of a 1.3 km regional climate model

Runs a threshold-based wheat leaf rust infection rule on hourly output of the regional model COSMO-CLM for 1991-2000, 2041-2050 and 2091-2100 at two Luxembourg sites. No record: an impact study. A context paper.

**Read this first.** Model weather, not a weather generator, and no check of its hourly relative humidity (RH) against stations is reported in what was read. The rule's RH cut is 60 %, so it says nothing about the saturated tail.

## What it holds

- **Rule** (l. 22, 196-202): after a first rain of 0.1 to under 1 mm, 12 h with air temperature 8 to 16 degC, RH above 60 % and rain under 1 mm in two consecutive nights (night 20:00 to 05:00). Inputs are hourly. The RH cut of 60 % is far below 90 %.
- **Weather for the future** (l. 148-151, 172-177): hourly temperature, RH and precipitation read straight from COSMO-CLM (1.3 km, nested in ECHAM5 under A1B), three 10-year slices (1991-2000, 2041-2050, 2091-2100) at two pseudo stations. ENSEMBLES data (daily temperature and precipitation only) are used for context. No bias correction of RH is stated.
- **How the regional run was judged** (l. 232, 460): its temperature falls "within the spread" of the ENSEMBLES multi-model range, so it is "suitable". Warming is 1.6 K for 2041-2050 and 3.7 K for 2091-2100 (l. 233-234). No hourly RH check against stations.
- **Rule evaluation** (l. 255-261): on observed disease at the two sites, 2003-2009, false alarm ratio 0.06 to 0.20, probability of detection above 0.97, critical success index 0.80 to 0.94; 2010-2013 POD above 0.95 and CSI at least 0.90. The text does not say which weather drove these runs, and no equivalent score is given for the model-weather runs.
- **Result on model weather** (l. 305-312): frequencies of infection hours per year (12 to 20 h) are higher at Burmerange than Reuler, but differences between the reference and future periods are not significant (Mann-Whitney test); winter infection hours appear (none in the reference winter at Reuler; up to 15 h in the near and 18 h in the far future, l. 350-352).
- **Humidity statistics checked:** none on the regional model's RH; disease runs on model weather were not compared with the disease rule on observed weather in the same period.

## Dependence

- Infection rule of El Jarroudi et al. 2014a,b (shared author El Jarroudi, a flag); COSMO-CLM nested in ECHAM5; ENSEMBLES; Luxembourg field experiments 2003-2013.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **D45:** not a check on humid hours, and not a generator. It uses hourly RH from a regional model with no stated correction or check, in a rule whose RH cut (60 %) is far from saturation.
- **D60:** a caution that climate-model hourly RH is used as is in published disease studies; the result "no significant change" cannot be read as a validation of the RH.
- **Not shown:** model-weather runs against observed-weather runs; any threshold near 90 %; any vineyard disease.
