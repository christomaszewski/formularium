# Coronelli et al. 2025: an on-site qPCR assay for P. viticola

## What it holds

- A qPCR assay on the ITS1 region (PLAV19 primers): limit of detection 1.5 fg/µL, of
  quantification 15 fg/µL, no cross-reaction with other grapevine pathogens; checked on
  inoculated and field tissue (Bari).

## Dependence

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- An observation operator: a molecular test's sensitivity, for M2's sampling.
