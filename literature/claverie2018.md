# Claverie et al. 2018: the Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 data set

Research article, Global land (v1.3 covers 10.35 million km2).

## What it holds

- Describes the NASA HLS processing chain (atmospheric correction, cloud masking, co-registration, BRDF normalization, bandpass adjustment) and its S10/S30/L30 products, evaluated against MODIS.
- It reports CE90 co-registration of 6.2 m (S10) and 18.8 m (L30), deviations from MODIS not exceeding 11%, and many Sentinel-2 cloud-mask omissions.
