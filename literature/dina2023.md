# Dina et al. 2023: downy mildew and the weather in an untreated Sauvignon blanc at Murfatlar, 2021-2022

Two seasons of twice-weekly downy mildew records in untreated and treated Sauvignon blanc on
Romania's Black Sea coast, beside a station in the vineyard.

**Read this first.** The untreated plots were wetted artificially to favour infection (l. 103,
107), so their severity is not natural pressure; the first-spot dates and the weather are the
usable part. The weather is shown in figures only, not in a table.

## What it holds

- **Outcomes:** first oil spots in the untreated variant on 17 May 2021, at BBCH 15 (l. 191),
  and on 14 June 2022 in both variants (l. 194); frequency, intensity and degree of attack on
  leaves and bunches through the season.
- **Weather:** an iMetos 3.3 station in the centre of the plot (l. 115), with leaf wetness;
  rain 339.3 mm in the 2021 season (l. 160).

## Dependence

None recorded: field observations.
