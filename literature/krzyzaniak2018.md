# Krzyzaniak et al. 2018: a plant extract as resistance inducer and oomycide

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Greenhouse: plant extract at 5 g/L cut P. viticola sporulation by 67% or 74% at 6 dpi (l. 273-274).
- Cell viability fell 20% to 40% at 1 to 3 g/L; no effect at 0.5 g/L (l. 285-287).
- Extract at 0.5 g/L stopped zoospore motility in vitro (l. 389).
- In planta: fewer infection sites at 24 h and tenfold less hyphal colonisation at 48 h (l. 433-434).
- Extract residues remained on the leaf at 72 h (l. 583).
- Authors conclude it acts as both a resistance inducer and an oomycide (l. 586).
