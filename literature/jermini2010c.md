# Jermini et al. 2010c: a downy mildew year costs yield the year after

Two stress-and-recovery pairs of seasons (1995-1996, 1997-1998) in Merlot at Cadenazzo: an
untreated canopy with only a cluster spray in the stress year, then normal spraying, against
the standard schedule throughout.

## What it holds

- Two trials at the Centre of Cadenazzo (Cugnasco plots), 1995-1996 and 1997-1998, with
  untreated canopy (A) and standard schedule (B). Each year of stress was followed by a
  normally sprayed recovery year (l. 40-50, 59-66).
- Stress year 1995: first symptoms on 16 June (l. 93). Must soluble solids lower by 1.39
  °Brix (1995) and 0.57 °Brix (1997) in untreated canopy (l. 102-103).
- Crop yield (kg per m2), Table 1: 1995 1.009 vs 1.198 (p 0.022); 1997 0.893 vs 1.005
  (n.s.); recovery 1996 1.202 vs 1.791 (p < 0.001); recovery 1998 1.438 vs 1.815 (p 0.005).
  Yield reductions in recovery years 0.589 and 0.377 kg per m2 (l. 254-256).
- Recovery 1996: shoot length on 13 June 81.99 vs 104.02 cm (p 0.003); difference at topping
  22.03 cm (1996) and 22.4 cm (1998) (l. 127-135; Table 2).
- Flowers per cluster (1996) 351.7 vs 371.0 and clusters per shoot 1.69 vs 1.81: no
  significant differences (Table 5).
- 1998 main leaf area 102.49 vs 128.02 cm2 (p 0.005) (Table 4).
- Stress 1997: roots lost 57 % of starch and 37 % of total reserves (cited from JERMINI et
  al. 2010 c, l. 270).
- Recovery 1998: total reserves 5.32 % and 5.95 % lower in shoot and trunk (order as
  printed, l. 283-285); roots and cane not different.
- Discussion states untreated must was lower by 0.78 and 0.35 °Brix in recovery years, which
  Table 1 contradicts (l. 262).

## Dependence

- Its own field data; the reserve figures for the stress year are cited from the companion
  paper (literature/jermini2010yield).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's truth across seasons (t5): defoliation by downy mildew in one season cut
  the next season's yield by 0.589 and 0.377 kg per m² (1.202 against 1.791 in 1996, 1.438
  against 1.815 in 1998; Table 1) and shoot length by about 22 cm, with flowers and clusters
  unchanged; one normally sprayed year rebuilt root reserves. A carry-over of damage a
  multi-season run would need.
- Its discussion's sugar differences for the recovery years are contradicted by its Table 1
  (l. 262).
