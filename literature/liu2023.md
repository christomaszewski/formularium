# Liu et al. 2023: a naturally ventilated shield for near-ground air temperature

A two-plate shield designed with CFD, whose radiation errors are fitted by a back-propagation
  network into a correction equation and checked outdoors.

## What it holds

- **Problem:** unshielded sensors can err by up to 1 C from solar radiation (l. 16-17);
  uncorrected RMSE of this system 0.35 C (l. 121).
- **Method:** CFD of effects of solar, scattered and long-wave radiation, wind speed and ground
  reflectivity; BP network fitted to give a correction equation (l. 21-27).
- **Result:** outdoor RMSE 0.095 C and mean difference from reference 0.084 C (l. 29-31).

## Dependence

- CFD (k-epsilon, SIMPLE) and a BP neural network (record).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s5 (sensor error): near-ground shielded temperature, error 0.35 C before and 0.095 C after
  correction, with ground reflectivity as an input. A simulator of sensor bias at 0.5 to 1 m
  above ground could take these sizes. Not read in full.
