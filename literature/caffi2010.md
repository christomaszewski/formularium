# Caffi et al. 2010: evaluating a primary-infection warning system

Research article, Emilia-Romagna, northern Italy (Castelfranco Emilia, Mandriole, Camerlona).

## What it holds

- Tests a warning system built on the Rossi et al. oospore-cohort model, short-term forecasts and SMS alerts in six experiments (2006-2008, three Emilia-Romagna vineyards) against unsprayed and grower treatments.
- The model's equations are not given; the paper reports spray counts, incidence and cost, and one threshold rule (a cohort of at least 2% of the seasonal oospore dose for Warning B).
