# Laroche-Pinel et al. 2021: vine water status from Sentinel-2

Research article, Southern France (Hérault, Occitanie), 36 plots, 2018-2020.

## What it holds

- Machine-learning regressions (five algorithms) of stem water potential on Sentinel-2 bands and indices for 36 vine plots over 2018-2020, with scores in Tables 8-9.
- It is about vine water status, not disease, so it is context for satellite retrieval skill in vineyards.
