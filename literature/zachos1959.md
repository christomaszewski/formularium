# Zachos 1959: downy mildew biology and forecasting in Greece

A 160-page monograph from the Benaki Phytopathological Institute, in French.

## What it holds

**Incubation, his own measurements (chapter II).**
- **Method:** artificial inoculations every ten days from early April (just after budburst)
  to the end of October, for two years. Sites: the Vello region (vineyard of the raisin
  office's annex at Coconi, with a weather cage) and the Patras phytopathological station.
- **Material:** two vines each of Corinth, Sultanina, Rhazaki, Rhoditis, Phraoula and
  Sideritis; leaf 4 or 5 from the shoot tip in year one, leaves 4-6 in year two (12, then
  36, leaves per date). Conidia from oospore-infected forced vines for the first dates.
- **Inoculation:** 8-10 drops of conidial suspension on the underside, a wet plastic bag
  overnight; done at 18-20 h once spring warmed, since bags overheated by day.
- **Endpoint:** the first oil spots, examined morning and evening. Incubation is "the
  shortest interval after which new conidia may appear", in practice to oil spots.
  Temperature and humidity from a thermohygrograph read every two hours.
- **Curves (Figs 3-4, from Tables X-XI, shortest durations on Rhoditis, Rhazaki, Phraoula,
  Sideritis and Sultanina):**
  - Vello: 8 days at a mean of 14 °C; 3 days from 16 to 20 °C; 2.5 days to 25 °C; rising
    to 6 days at 28 °C.
  - Patras: 9 days at 14 °C; 4 days at 17 °C; 2.5 days from 21 to 24 °C; 5 days at 27 °C.
- **Table XIII:** probable shortest durations by ten-day period from twelve years of mean
  temperatures; conidiophores taken as at least 2 days after the oil spots. The scan's
  table is partly garbled.
- **Also:** autumn durations are longer at equal temperature (Table XII); Corinth adds 1-2
  days; older leaves incubate longer (Table XIV); macroconidia and conidia give the same
  incubation (Table XV).
- **A degree-day formula was tested and rejected:** the sums of active temperature for the
  observed durations were 24, 42.5 and 120 °C, not constant.

**Conidia (sporangia) viability, his own experiments (chapter III, pp. 254-261).**
- **Method:** leaves with oil spots bagged moist overnight; conidia 5-7 h old, with
  excellent germination, taken with a needle onto concave slides; after the treatment
  the hollow filled with water and held at 22-23 °C; germination scored at 2 h ("rapid")
  and at 24 h. Group A open to the air, group B under a bell jar in saturated air.
- **Sun (Table XVI; 28 and 30 May, 4 June; 22-31 °C, 35-75 % RH):** after 15 min most
  conidia no longer germinated within 2 h, though they did after 24 h in water; after
  1 h none germinated rapidly; beyond 1 h nearly all were destroyed for good. He
  supposes heat dries them past recovery.
- **Weather screen (Table XVII):** 20 May (means 17.5-22.3 °C, RH 63-88 %): A kept rapid
  germination 4 days, B 6 days. 28 May (means 25-28.5 °C, maxima 31-33 °C): both 1 day.
- **Laboratory (Table XVIII):** constant 23 °C, 68 % RH: A 1 day, B 8 days; constant
  25 °C, 65 % RH: A 1 day, B 2 days.
- **Conclusions (p. 260):** wind-borne conidia landing on sunny days are dead; in shade
  about 4 days if the mean is under 22 °C and the maximum at most 26 °C, 1-2 days at means
  of 22-25 °C; 6-8 days only in saturated air at means up to 23 °C, else at most 2 days.
  Newly made conidia meeting conditions for infection at once are the danger.
- **Cited, not his:** Ravaz (year garbled in the scan, 1912 or 1914): 15 h at -12 °C did
  no harm, and in humid air conidia keep their power "pas au delà de cinq jours";
  Istvánffi & Pálinkás 1913: three weeks at 6-10 °C or two months at 0 °C, germinating
  only after 24-48 h.
- Tables XVII-XVIII are partly garbled in OCR; the values above are the text's.

**The incubation literature, as he compares it (Fig. 5, pp. 247-252).** Separate
datasets, not one lineage:
- Ravaz & Gège 1919, Montpellier: about 9 days for primary, 7 for secondary infections.
- Müller & Rabanus 1923 (saturated air, 13.5-29 °C) and Müller & Sleumer 1934 (natural
  conditions, 1932-33): falling from 13 to 4.5 days between 12 and 17.5 °C, then flat at
  4.5-5 days to 24.7 °C. The "incubation calendar".
- Schad 1936, Clermont-Ferrand: 9-10 days primary, 6-9 secondary.
- Baldacci 1950, Maccarese near Rome: falling to 17 °C, then flat at 7 days.
- **Casarini 1957, Emilia: two curves, high and low humidity, from artificial and natural
  inoculations.** Casarini co-wrote Goidanich et al. 1957's table.
- His own two curves.

## Dependence

- His incubation data are his own; he compares other curves and adopts none.
- No shared author with any model Cooptera runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- **An incubation independent of Goidanich's table and Rossi's curves** (catalogue
  `zachos1959.incubation`): its own data, a table form rather than Rossi's polynomial.
  A truth using Rossi's incubation holds Goidanich's table out of the engine and keeps
  this. Recommended to Cooptera as the policy's second incubation (its decision 21).
- **It explains the Goidanich lineage:** the table probably rests on Casarini's Emilia
  data, not Müller's (inferred; recorded in `datasets.py`, `goidanich1957`).
- Magarey et al. 1991's incubation cubic was fitted partly to these data
  ([magarey1991](magarey1991.md)).
- Not yet read here: the oospore chapters and the forecasting system. Rotem & Palti 1969 cite it for April
  sprinkling triggering oospore infections.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- **A sporangia survival independent of the engine's** (catalogue
  `zachos1959.sporangia_survival`): his own experiments, a table of days, not Blaeser &
  Weltzien's index. It shares only its author with `zachos1959.incubation`, now an engine
  model: a flag, and different data.
- **It sides with Kennelly et al. 2007 against Brischetto 2020's eq. 2** where they differ:
  sun kills within the hour, and warmth shortens survival even in saturated air. Eq. 2
  agrees with it only in mild shade. For Agrarium's D30: a basis for an evaluation family's
  survival, once a continuous form (rates between his whole days, and a sun term from the
  truth's radiation) is registered as `assumed` and checked first.
