# Piancatelli et al. 2024: elicitors and low copper against grapevine mildews

A three-year organic vineyard trial in Ancona. It compares elicitors (COS-OGA, Swinglea glutinosa) and low copper, alone and combined, with copper, sulfur and untreated controls.

## What it holds

- Trial: 2021, 2022 and 2023 in an organic vineyard, cv. Montepulciano, Ancona (l. 131-134).
- Downy mildew, 2023: the best strategy (COS-OGA with low copper and an adjuvant) cut the McKinney index against untreated vines by 74% on leaves and 39% on bunches (l. 31-33).
- Powdery mildew, 2022: reductions of 85% on leaves and 46% on bunches (l. 33).
- Strategies and doses in Table 1 (l. 165). Index formulas (1) to (4) at l. 221-227.

## Dependence

- McKinney index, from an earlier reference (l. 211, 219).
- EPPO standards for plant resistance inducers (l. 123).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strands t1 (fungicide protection, copper and sulfur reduction) and t3 (powdery mildew). A decision engine could use the copper and sulfur arms and the reduced-copper adjuvant result.
- The elicitor arms are not fungicides, and no simulator term fits them. Not read in full.
