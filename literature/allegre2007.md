# Allègre et al. 2007: infected leaves keep their stomata open

## What it holds

- Glasshouse Marselan cuttings: in tissue colonised by P. viticola, stomata stay open in
  darkness and under water stress, and do not close with ABA; seen by conductance and
  thermography before symptoms, limited to the colonised area.
