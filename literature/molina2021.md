# Molina et al. 2021: ERA5 hourly 10 m wind speed against HadISD

Hourly ERA5 10 m wind is compared with HadISD observations at 245 European stations using a
  frequency-distribution score.

## What it holds

- **Data:** 245 stations with valid values in at least 90 % of hourly steps, 1979 to 2018 (l.
  143-152).
- **Result:** most stations score 0.8 to 0.9 on the frequency score; ERA5 reproduces the annual
  cycle with slightly larger winter and smaller summer medians (l. 29-37).
- **Time scale:** agreement rises at 6 to 24 h averages (l. 42-44).

## Dependence

- ERA5 and HadISD quality control (record).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s2: how well hourly ERA5 wind matches stations in Europe (score 0.8 to 0.9 on the
  distribution); wind enters the sweep only through drying and dispersal. A simulator could
  take the score and the seasonal median bias as a check on ERA5 wind. Not read in full.
