# Furiosi et al. 2025: simulated drone spraying against downy mildew

A drone sprayer held to the ground simulated aerial application of metalaxyl-M and copper in 2024. Metalaxyl-M from the simulated aerial spray matched ground spraying before flowering, and copper did not.

## What it holds

- **Before flowering:** ground spraying gave 70 to 75% efficacy. The simulated aerial metalaxyl-M gave about 68%, and the simulated aerial copper about 47% (l. 500-502).
- **Bunch closure:** the two simulated aerial treatments gave 63 to 67% in the upper canopy and 45 to 60% in the lower canopy (l. 509-512).
- **Deposits:** the simulated aerial sprays put more deposit in the upper canopy (abstract l. 43-45).
- **Design:** efficacy read at 1, 3, 5 and 7 days after treatment (l. 318-319). Piacenza and Grugliasco, 2024 (l. 176-178).

## Dependence

- Italian law prohibits aerial spraying, so the UASS was held to the ground (l. 93-94; l. 254-258).
- Efficacy formula and Tukey test (Methods).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (fungicide protection):** the copper gap and the upper-canopy deposit pattern warn that aerial fungicide use may reach the bunch zone poorly. It is one 2024 season at two sites, and the paper asks for more years.
