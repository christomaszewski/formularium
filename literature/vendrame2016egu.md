# Vendrame et al. 2016: bulk drag rises with leaf area, and wind in the lower canopy turns along the rows

The one-page abstract of the Padova hedgerow-vineyard campaign that became the thesis `vendrame2017`.

**Read this first.** A conference abstract with no numbers for drag or wind. The values are in `vendrame2017`.

## What it holds

- Five sonic anemometers on a 5 m tower, sampled at 20 Hz from the beginning of April to the end of July 2015, with leaf area density (LAD) profiles and canopy architecture measured about weekly (l. 24).
- The canopy bulk drag coefficient increased during the growing season, so the coupling between the vegetation and the atmosphere rose with LAD (l. 27).
- As the canopy grew, the mean wind direction in its lower part was increasingly steered along the rows: a growing channelling effect (l. 33).

## Dependence

The same campaign as `vendrame2017` (its numbers) and `vendrame2023` (the journal paper); not independent of them. The channelling finding agrees with `miller2017`, an independent Oregon vineyard.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **dm.row_barrier (2, range 1-4):** wind in the lower canopy turns along the rows as leaves grow, which fits an along-row path for air near the vines. No ratio. Wind direction is not dispersal distance.
