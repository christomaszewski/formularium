# Douillet et al. 2022: LAMP for airborne downy mildew inoculum in the field

From the IFV (Blanquefort) and INRAE Bordeaux.

## What it holds

- LAMP (ITS2, Kong et al. 2016 primers, l. 180) run on crude KOH lysates of sporangia caught by a rotating-arm sampler (l. 132), checked against droplet digital PCR (ddPCR) on the same lysates.
- Field: Chateau Dillon, Blanquefort, Merlot, 13 March to 14 October 2020, 88 samples (l. 135); 0 to 243 sporangia/m3 (l. 42); 6.22 m3 of air per hour (l. 148).
- Limits: LoD 50-500 sporangia/ml of lysate, LoQ 500/ml (l. 286, l. 293); LoQ 3.3 sporangia/m3 (l. 38).
- LAMP vs ddPCR in the field: sensitivity 0.80, specificity 0.75, LR(+) 3.2, LR(-) 0.27, Kappa 0.53 (l. 330, l. 332); false-negative probability 0.36 (l. 336).
- Early epidemic (before 15 May): ddPCR positive in 37.5% of samples, LAMP in 8.3% (l. 373).
- Rt vs log ITS2 copies: R2 0.791 in pure cultures (l. 310), 0.523 on field samples (l. 319).
- The 2.52 sporangia/m3 risk threshold is Brischetto et al. 2020, not this paper's (l. 386).
- Observation data only; no equation for the truth.

## Dependence

- Kong et al. 2016's LAMP primers, against the authors' ddPCR. Its 2.52 sporangia/m³ risk threshold is Brischetto et al. 2020's, cited.
- Raynal and Delmotte: no engine model.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Clear: a field spore-trap operator's sensitivity (0.80) and specificity (0.75) against ddPCR, and its weakness early in the season (8.3% of samples detected before 15 May, against 37.5% by ddPCR).
