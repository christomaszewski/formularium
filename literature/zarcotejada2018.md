# Zarco-Tejada et al. : Pre-visual symptoms of Xylella fastidiosa infection revealed in spectral plant-trait alter

Research article, Puglia (Apulia), southern Italy, 15 olive orchards, 2016 and 2017.

## What it holds

- Airborne hyperspectral and thermal imagery, checked by qPCR, detects Xylella fastidiosa in olive trees before symptoms with overall accuracy above 80% (SVM on pigment, structure, fluorescence and temperature traits).
- It is an author manuscript (journal, year and DOI not printed); the host is olive, not vine.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Pre-visual detection above 80% accuracy for another pathogen and host: an upper bound on what an airborne operator might do, not a vineyard number.
