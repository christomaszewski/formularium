# Srinivasan & Jeyarajan 1976: grape downy mildew in India, foliar and fruit infection

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Downy mildew on Anab-e-Shahi grapevine in Tamil Nadu, scored by leaf colour class; red and green-with-orange leaves had no symptoms (l. 113).
- Jade-green leaves were the youngest to show symptoms, at 24 days old (l. 114); agathia-green reached grade 3 soonest, 11 days after symptoms appeared (l. 120).
- Incubation: 18 days in red leaves, about 10 days in older leaves (l. 177, 375).
- Stomata per mm2 rose from 143 to 227 with leaf age (l. 215); inoculated flowers and mustard-sized berries dropped, pea-sized berries lost 53.1 per cent (l. 303).
- Table 1 disease percentages reproduce only with the diseased-leaf denominator (l. 59-63).
