# Masson & Knutti 2011: climate model genealogy

## What it holds

- **Method:** a Kullback-Leibler distance between climate models' unforced control
  simulations of surface temperature or precipitation, then hierarchical clustering into a
  "family tree". Dependence is judged by the similarity of output, not by provenance.
- **Findings:** models from one institution are most alike; models sharing a component
  (the same atmosphere) are alike across institutions; successive versions cluster; a
  perturbed-parameter ensemble stays apart from structurally different models
  ("structural model differences seem to be important").
- **Advice:** equal weighting ignores dependence; ensembles should maximise diversity;
  "conclusions drawn from ensembles should at least test the sensitivity to how models are
  selected"; "eliminating a model from an analysis is easy, extrapolation beyond the range
  covered by the ensemble is nearly impossible".

## Bearing (2026-10-07)

- The basis of Agrarium's decision D27: hold out by shared components (equations, code,
  data, form), treat shared authors as a flag, run the author-only hold-out as a
  sensitivity arm, and measure behaviour beside provenance.
- It studies models of the same variables only; it does not show that dependence is
  confined to models of the same thing (an earlier citation here claimed that; corrected).
