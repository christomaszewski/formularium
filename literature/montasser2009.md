# Montasser & Mahmoud 2009: residues of three grape sprays

Residues of azoxystrobin, fenhexamid and lufenuron on grape leaves and fruit, after one spray at the recommended rate in an Egyptian plot. The paper gives dissipation, half-lives and pre-harvest intervals.

## What it holds

- Sprayed once on grapes in June, at the recommended rates, in a plot at Dakahlia (l. 46–49).
- After 10 days, leaf residue fell by 87.83% (azoxystrobin), 99.23% (fenhexamid) and 99.29% (lufenuron) (l. 14–16). On fruit, 93.55%, 99.62% and 99.99% (l. 17–19).
- Half-lives on leaves and fruit: azoxystrobin 3.01 and 2.8 days (l. 221). Fenhexamid 2.87 and 2.4 days (l. 242, 250). Lufenuron 1.8 and 1.7 days (l. 249, 289).
- Pre-harvest intervals of 6, 10 and 7 days for the three (l. 20–21).

## Dependence

- Extraction and clean-up follow earlier methods: Mollhof 1975, Krause 1980, Johnson 1963 and Al-Samariee et al. 1988.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t1 (fungicide protection) only in part. The paper measures residues, not disease control: no disease was scored, so it is off the point for efficacy. A spray-timing rule could take the pre-harvest intervals and half-lives.
- One plot and one dose. Not read in full.
