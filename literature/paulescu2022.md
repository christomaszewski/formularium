# Paulescu & Paulescu 2022: a semi-analytical diffuse-fraction model

A general equation for the diffuse fraction from clearness index, relative sunshine and clear-sky transmittance, valid from 1 min to 1 day, tested at BSRN Magurele (l. 13-26). No record.

## What it holds

- **Method:** a diffuse fraction equation derived from basic principles, with clearness index, relative sunshine and clear-sky atmospheric transmittance as predictors (l. 16-20).
- **Test:** BSRN station Magurele, Romania, 44.34 N, 26.01 E, 110 m (l. 22-23, 218-219), a temperate continental climate.
- **Result:** a maximum possible accuracy of about 7 % nRMSE for the diffuse fraction (l. 23-25); nMBE 0.053 and nRMSE 0.067 in the case noted (l. 412).

## Dependence

- Builds on BSRN radiometric data and on empirical separation models in the literature.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s3 and s6:** a way to split measured global radiation into beam and diffuse when only a global value exists. A simulator could take it as the diffuse step ahead of any slope or canopy radiation calculation. One site, one climate; no vineyard.
