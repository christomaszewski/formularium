# Balduque-Gil et al. 2024: PCR of two mildews from spore-trap tapes

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A lab method for detecting P. viticola and E. necator DNA from adhesive tapes of airborne-spore cyclone collectors (l. 19-29).
- Four DNA extraction methods were compared: T-CTAB, U-CTAB, PrepMan and Xnar (l. 164-167).
- Tapes were inoculated with spores from a Mazuela vineyard in Cariñena PDO, 24 fragments of tape A and 8 of tape B (l. 122-123, l. 158).
- The Nad9 cob pair (P. viticola) and Uncin144/Uncin511 pair (E. necator) gave specific PCR results (l. 26-27, l. 1884-1887).
- The Giop pair also amplified E. necator tape samples (l. 697).
- DNA yields were too low for reliable quantification (l. 1880).
- The authors propose later use in DSSs and disease risk models (l. 1895-1898).
