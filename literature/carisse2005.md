# Carisse et al. 2005: airborne conidia to time fungicide sprays, onion

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Botrytis leaf blight of onion is common, and its epidemics vary widely between years (l. 56).
- Airborne conidium concentration (ACC) and lesions per leaf are linearly related (R2 = 0.87, l. 253).
- ACC of 10-15 conidia per m3 goes with about 1 lesion per leaf; 25-35 with 2.5 (l. 61).
- A spray programme started at 10-15 conidia per m3 matched the grower schedule with fewer sprays (l. 67).
- In 2002 and 2003 ACC-based sprays cut fungicide applications by 75% and 56% (l. 73).
- A daily inoculum production index from temperature and RH (0-25, start at 7) is used as a second trigger (l. 230-236).
- Plots were sprayed with mancozeb at 2.4 kg/ha every 7 days (l. 168).

## Dependence
