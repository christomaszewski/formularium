# Serey et al. 2007: how long Botrytis fungicides protect, and how late they cure

Laboratory tests on mature Thompson Seedless berries of boscalid, cyprodinil + fludioxonil,
captan, BAS 600 KBF and mixtures against B. cinerea and four other rot fungi: berries
inoculated at intervals after treatment (protection) or treated at intervals after
inoculation (curative action), with 75 % control as the criterion.

## What it holds

- **Design:** a 12 µL drop on wounded berries, inoculated 0-96 h later, or berries immersed
  60 s and inoculated 1-21 days later; curative tests with drops 0-48 h after inoculation
  (l. 92-98); four replicates of eight berries (l. 105).
- **Protection:** 0-4 days with drops, 0-21 days after immersion (l. 116); cyprodinil +
  fludioxonil protected 4 days against B. cinerea by drop (l. 116). At 48 h every fungicide
  controlled B. cinerea above 78.8 % after drops (l. 122); captan fell from 88.6 % (drop) to
  45.3 % (immersion) (l. 122).
- **Curative action:** 'always short, below 24 h' (l. 130); against B. cinerea under 12 h
  for cyprodinil + fludioxonil and captan, none for boscalid (l. 130-132).
