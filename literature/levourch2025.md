# Le Vourch et al. 2025: airborne Phytophthora infestans inoculum in Belgium

Daily Burkard spore-trap samples with qPCR at four Belgian sites, 2019 to 2022, compared with
  three decision support systems for potato late blight.

## What it holds

- **Measured:** daily airborne inoculum at Ath, Gembloux, Libramont and Louvain-la-Neuve, 2019
  to 2022, 0 to 4903 sporangia equivalent a day (l. 31-36); 7-day Burkard traps (l. 141-142).
- **Result:** late blight appeared shortly after the first airborne inoculum was detected (l.
  21-24); the DSSs often recommend sprays before any inoculum is airborne (l. 24-28).
- **Trials:** two field trials, 2021 and 2022, show fewer sprays when the first one is delayed
  until inoculum is present (l. 28-31).

## Dependence

- Compares three DSSs that assume primary inoculum is ubiquitous (l. 25-26); not read.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)
