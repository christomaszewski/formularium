# Al-Najjar & Al-Khazzar 2017: a polynomial hourly diffuse-fraction correlation for Baghdad

A third-degree polynomial of diffuse fraction on clearness index, fitted to hourly Baghdad measurements and compared with eight literature models (l. 25-33). No record.

## What it holds

- **Data:** hourly global and diffuse radiation at Baghdad, 33.33 N, April to September (l. 25-27, 88); the data set cites 2015 (l. 89).
- **Result:** linear correlation 0.885, mean percentage error 6.24 % and RMSE 8.0 % (abstract, l. 30-32); MBE 0.005 % (l. 161).
- **Comparison:** of eight literature models the South American one fits best, with mean percentage error 16 % and RMSE 11.5 % (abstract, l. 33-37).

## Dependence

- Builds on literature diffuse correlations, notably Wong and Chow and Khorasanizadeh and Mohammadi (l. 18-40).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s3 and s6:** another site-fitted diffuse-fraction curve; shows that such curves are local, since foreign ones give RMSE 11.5 percent or worse. A simulator would not take the coefficients for a Mediterranean vineyard.
