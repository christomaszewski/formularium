# Ferreira et al. 2014: decay of cover-crop residue in a vineyard

A litter-bag study of black oat and hairy vetch residue in vine rows (with and without plastic
  shelter) and between rows; it concerns nutrients, not disease.

## What it holds

- **Design:** commercial vineyard at Bento Gonçalves, October 2008 to February 2009; bags
  collected at 0, 33, 58, 76 and 110 days (l. 23-30, 267).
- **Treatments:** vine row without shelter, vine row with plastic shelter, between rows (l.
  27-29).
- **Tables:** residue composition in Table 1 (l. 286), remaining mass and nutrients in Table 2
  (l. 323); shelter kept more dry matter but slowed nutrient cycling, as the record states. I
  did not extract the percentages.

## Dependence

- Litter bag method (record); no model.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s1, as a method: a litter-bag decay series under and beside a plastic cover, for 110 days. A
  simulator of overwintering leaf litter could borrow the design and the shelter effect, not
  the numbers (cover-crop residue, not grape leaves). Not read in full.
