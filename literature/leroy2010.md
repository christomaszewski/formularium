# Leroy et al. 2010: a bio-economic model of mildew strategies

## What it holds

- A daily plot model on STICS-Vigne's leaf area: new infected area = K_L x sporulating x
  susceptible / total leaf area, for downy mildew only on days with rain at or above a
  threshold; yield loss from sporulating area, sensitivity and protection.
- Downy mildew calibrated by RMSE to IFV's untreated-plot severities (2007-2008); no
  parameter values, statistics or validation printed.
- Merlot at La Ferrade, 1988-2008, five strategies; results in figures, and a tax factor of
  3-7 or a 4-10 % price rise to match margins.
