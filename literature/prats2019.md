# Prats-Llinàs et al. 2019: bunch and shoot counts of irrigated Raïmat Chardonnay under post-harvest deficit irrigation

A four-season irrigation trial in a Chardonnay vineyard at Raïmat (Lleida) that counts shoots, inflorescences and clusters per vine; it is a Catalan Chardonnay comparison, not a Penedès one.

**Read this first.** The held `prats2020` is a different paper. Raïmat is in Costers del Segre (Lleida), drip-irrigated, at a wider spacing than Penedès rows, and the counts are treatment means from an irrigation experiment. The only budbreak result is a visual remark for one year. The text layer duplicates several lines (garbled overprint).

## What it holds

- **Site:** a 13-year-old commercial Chardonnay vineyard at Raïmat (41°39′50″ N, 0°30′27″ E), 2013 to 2016 seasons (l. 106-107); SO4 rootstock, 3.0 m × 2.0 m (6 m² a vine, computed), north-south rows, vertical shoot positioning with a bilateral spur-pruned cordon 1.0 m above ground, 10 to 15 spurs a vine (l. 112-115); drip irrigation (l. 141). Mean annual rainfall 341 mm and reference evapotranspiration 1,060 mm (l. 116-117).
- **Treatments:** full irrigation (C); post-harvest deficit irrigation started at harvest when stem water potential fell below −0.9 MPa (RDIL SP, sparkling base wine) or below −1.25 MPa (RDIM SP, and RDIM W for wine) (l. 20-26).
- **Phenology:** weekly visual inspection; a stage was recorded when 50 % of the shoots reached it, on the BBCH scale (l. 197-199). Shoots and inflorescences were counted per vine at the onset of the vegetative period (l. 200).
- **Budbreak:** in 2016 field observations showed budbreak "more than seven to ten days" more advanced in the RDIM vines than in C (l. 507-510). No dates are printed.
- **Yield components** (Table 2, means 2014 to 2016, per vine; l. 565-577): control 44.7 shoots, 46.0 inflorescences, 9.1 kg, 35 clusters, 166 berries a cluster, berry dry weight 0.33 g; the three deficit treatments 33 to 35 clusters, 42.8 to 45.0 shoots, 8.7 to 8.9 kg. Treatment had no significant effect on clusters per vine (P = 0.0659) or kg per vine (P = 0.6536) (l. 571); year did (l. 573). A cluster of about 260 g follows from 9.1 kg over 35 clusters (my division).

## Dependence

None recorded; the paper's own trial.

## Bearing (2026-10-22)

- **host.bunches_per_vine:** 33 to 35 clusters per vine, and 44.7 shoots, at 6 m² a vine under drip irrigation is about three times the assumed 12 (range 8 to 20). It shows how high a counted Chardonnay vine can go, but it is irrigated, in Lleida, and wide-spaced; the rainfed Penedès Chardonnay of `serrano2022` gives only kg a vine. No Penedès count comes from here.
- **host.vine_sd_days:** a management effect, not a spread: up to 10 d of budbreak between irrigation treatments in one vineyard in one year, visually scored. It is an upper bound on how far a plot's budbreak can move with vine reserves, not a vine-to-vine SD.
- **host.budburst_doy and cultivar susceptibility:** nothing; no dates are given and no disease is studied.
