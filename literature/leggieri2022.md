# Camardo Leggieri et al. 2022: DEFHAZ model for hazelnut kernel defects

A mechanistic model of Diaporthe eres defects in hazelnut kernels. It was run and checked on orchard data from 2013 to 2020.

## What it holds

- **Inputs and output:** hourly air temperature, relative humidity and rainfall. The output is a daily cumulative index over the season (l. 19-21).
- **Threshold:** 1% defective hazelnuts defines the risk of an occurrence (l. 21-22).
- **Data:** orchard records from the Caucasus region and Turkey, 2013 to 2020 (l. 17-18).
- **Accuracy:** more than 80% correct predictions, with under 16% false negatives (l. 25).

## Dependence

- Bete equation for the temperature term, from Analytis (l. 306, 339).
- Pscheidt and Heckert's empirical hazelnut mould model (l. 113-115).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Class C: hazelnut kernel defects, not grapevine, so it sits off the t1 to t7 strands. The Bete form is a method to read, not a grape input.
