# Alkhalidi et al. 2025: ERA5 wind speed and direction checked at Kuwait stations

A validation of ERA5 hourly wind speed and direction against ten coastal and offshore stations in Kuwait, 2010 to 2017 (l. 17). A reanalysis check; no record.

## What it holds

- **Test:** ten coastal and offshore stations, 2010 to 2017 (l. 17); Taylor diagrams and error metrics (l. 24).
- **Result:** offshore correlations up to 0.85 and Perkins Skill Score up to 0.94 (l. 19-20); at coastal stations correlation dropped to 0.35 (l. 21-22).
- **Finding:** ERA5 underestimates wind variability and extreme winds, most at coastal stations (l. 20-21); it reproduces the dominant northwest wind but is biased in transitional seasons (l. 22-24).

## Dependence

- Builds on ERA5 and the Perkins skill score; no other model.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s2 (weather for the sweep) and s5 (sensor error):** a measured size of ERA5 wind error near a coast: weak correlation and damped variability where land and sea meet. A simulator could take it as a caution that reanalysis wind is too smooth for local leaf-drying, not as a number to transfer to a vineyard.
