# Magarey et al. 2010: powdery mildew control review, Riverland vineyards

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Riverland (South Australia) spray diaries for 2006/07 from local wineries: 1,206 Chardonnay patches, 1,450 Shiraz, 13 Verdelho (l. 65-66).
- 73 per cent of Chardonnay patches had good powdery mildew control; 6 per cent of growers had fruit rejected (l. 71, 77).
- Sprays ranged from 2 to 13 per season, average 6 to 7 (l. 82); the best control came with as few as four (l. 139).
- Under-vine sprinkler patches had more powdery mildew than drip patches (Table 1, l. 236, 258).
- Recommendation: four sprays per season as a benchmark (l. 386).
