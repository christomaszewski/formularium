# Diez-Navajas & Ortiz-Barredo 2010: spore traps and station risk in Rioja Alavesa

## What it holds

- Twelve vineyards with iMetos stations and sticky-slide traps; four reported. Downy mildew
  is rare there; 2007 had an outbreak after a wet spring and summer.
- The stations' MetwinII risk rose several days after the first spore peak. No counts,
  equations or statistics are printed.
