# Sun 2024: cross-dataset evaluation of tomato disease classifiers, PlantVillage against field-like sets (bioRxiv preprint, tomato)

Trains ResNet, EfficientNet, Inception and MobileNet on 80 % of one tomato disease image set and validates them on 20 % of the same set and of the others. Nothing synthetic is used; it re-measures the lab-to-field drop. No record: a benchmark; a preprint (not peer reviewed); tomato and flowers, not grapevine.

**Read this first.** A preprint, tomato and flowers. Some validation classes were absent from the PlantVillage training set (leaf miner and spotted wild virus in Tomato-Village), which pulls the headline F1 down; the author reports that excluding them still leaves F1 at 0.092 (l. 266). The split is random within each set, and the other-set validation is a random 20 % of that set, five repeats averaged (l. 174 to 177). The text read gives no error bars.

## What it holds

- **Sets** (l. 100 to 128): PlantVillage (PV), Tomato-Village (TV, mostly PV-like isolated leaves on uniform backgrounds with a smaller field subset), and PlantDoc merged with Tomato Leaf Disease (TLD), from which images originally from PV were removed; categories with fewer than 50 images were dropped (l. 130). The flower sets are not used below.
- **Design** (l. 174 to 177): each architecture trained on 80 % of one set, validated on the remaining 20 % and on 20 % random samples of the other sets, five repeats averaged.
- **Result for ResNet trained on PV** (l. 252, 253): F1 0.987 when validated on PV; 0.012 on TV and 0.130 on TLD, "even though" TV includes many PV-like images (l. 254).
- **Class mismatch** (l. 264 to 266): 85.5 % of images in the two classes absent from training were misclassified; even without them, F1 on TV was 0.092, mainly from healthy leaves predicted as diseased.
- **Bidirectional** (l. 255, 259): models trained on TV and TLD also struggled on PV; a model trained on all three sets lost little on PV (which has a high share of PV images) but still declined on the others.
- **Architecture** (l. 34, 218): the degradation was consistent across all four architectures; UMAP shows images separating by dataset, not by disease.

## Dependence

- PlantVillage, Tomato-Village, PlantDoc (`singh2020`) and Tomato Leaf Disease. Nothing else recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **For judging render- or generator-trained models (D58, D60):** a third measurement of the shift (with `mohanty2016` and `singh2020`), tomato this time, with F1 down from 0.987 to 0.012 to 0.130. A model fitted on Agrarium's rendered lesions has to be tested on real images from a different source, not on a split of the training set.
- **Mixed training helps:** training on all sets mitigates the drop, which matches the pattern that real data from the target domain are what repair the shift.
- **Not shown:** grape, any synthetic data, or error bars. Part of the F1 drop is a class mismatch, not only a style shift.
