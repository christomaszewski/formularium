# Godfrey et al. n.d.: milk components against grapevine powdery mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Milk tested against powdery mildew (Erysiphe necator) in vitro and in two 2009/2010 small-plot trials in South Australia (l. 163).
- Full-cream milk at 1 in 5 cut germination to 2.2% against 26.2% on water agar (l. 112-113).
- At 8 dpi, milk at 1 in 10 matched the sulfur control and cut severity to 0.6% against 76.7% for water (l. 117).
- Lactoferrin showed neither protective nor curative activity in vitro (l. 130).
- At harvest, milk-treated vines had 1.5% bunch severity (l. 211); the wetting-agent and untreated vines had 5.4% and 7.3% (l. 213).
