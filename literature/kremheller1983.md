# Kremheller & Diercks 1983: forecasting hop downy mildew from airborne sporangia

## What it holds

- Field and chamber work in Bavarian hop yards: infection needs rain wetness (dew matters
  little); the forecast combines the concentration of airborne sporangia (counted per 3 m³
  of air in 24 h, with thresholds lower for cones than for leaves) with the duration of rain
  wetness. From 1976 to 1982 it halved fungicide applications on average.

## Dependence

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.
