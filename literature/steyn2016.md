# Steyn et al. 2016: vine vigour, balance and leaf area per gram of fruit

A review of vine balance and vigour. It gives published leaf area per gram of fruit and Ravaz index thresholds, and it runs no model of its own.

## What it holds

- **Leaf area per gram:** cited optima of 11 to 14, 10 to 12, 7 to 14, 8 to 12 and 8 to 14 to 19 cm2 per gram of fresh grapes, depending on cultivar, trellis, climate and canopy management (l. 239-243).
- **Howell's ratio:** a leaf area to yield ratio of 7 to 14 cm2/g spans "a rather large 2X range" (l. 266-267).
- **Ravaz index:** 1 Ravaz is 1 kg of fruit per kg of winter pruning weight (l. 60-61). The cited thresholds disagree, from 0 to 3 (Ravaz) to 5 to 10 (Smart and Robinson) (l. 75-82).

## Dependence

- None recorded. It draws on Winkler's vine-balance concept (l. 40-47) and Ravaz's 1911 index (l. 55-58) as ideas, and runs no model.
