# Bahmani et al. 2025: Ascophyllum and Pseudomonas vs downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Greenhouse and leaf-disc tests of ANE (Ascophyllum nodosum extract), P. fluorescens CHA0 and both together against P. viticola on grapevine (l. 8, 34).
- Combined treatment held greenhouse incidence to about 22%, against 40-50% for single agents and 70% in controls (l. 57, 58).
- In leaf discs the combination cut spore count by about 66% and zoospores by 89-93% (l. 53, 55).
- Plants were three-month-old Chardonnay in a greenhouse at 70-80% RH and 24/21 °C (l. 147, 148, 161).
- The authors report defence enzymes and genes rising under the combination, and call it a synergy (l. 664).
