# Bove, Savary, Willocquet & Rossi 2021: partial resistance in the Bove 2020 simulator

The sister paper of `bove2020` (the simulator) and `bove2020resistance` (the measured
components): the four relative-resistance terms put into the simulator and swept. No
field data; no validation.

## What it holds

- **The simulator:** Bove et al. 2020a's one-plant, daily-step model run for 200 days,
  10,000 foliage sites and 20 clusters (l. 107-124, 831-832); scenario weather modifiers
  and parameters from Bove et al. 2020b (l. 118). Scenario 1: primary infection rate P
  0.2 per day from day 40 (day of year 130) for 60 days, latent period 6 d, infectious
  period 20 d, RcT 1 (l. 294-301); scenario 2 (wet, cold): RcT 0.8, LP 10 d, IP 15 d (l.
  330, 368); scenario 3: moisture modifier RcW 0.6 (l. 307).
- **How resistance enters:** RPI = P (1 − RRIE); LP = LPs / (1 − RRLP); IP = IPs (1 −
  RRIP); RcV = 1 × (1 − RRIE)(1 − RRSP) as the varietal modifier of the secondary
  infection rate (l. 237-290); infection efficiency is taken proportional to the
  infection frequency measured on leaf discs, which under-estimates it when several
  infections share a site (l. 205-229; Lalancette et al. 1987 for the definition; Gregory
  1948 for the non-linearity, l. 800).
- **Runs:** 81 combinations of the four RRs at 0, 0.1 and 0.4; single sweeps at 0 to 0.40;
  and 48 runs for 16 varieties (Merlot and 15 resistant ones measured in controlled
  conditions, Bove & Rossi 2020) in the three scenarios (l. 313-317, 343-371). Table 2:
  Merlot's foliage AUDPC 400,407 (scenario 1), 43,897 (2), 71,127 (3), all 20 or 17
  clusters diseased; Rkatsiteli 212,357 with only RRIP 0.37; Bronner 390 with the largest
  measured RRs (RRIE 0.43, RRIP 0.67, RRSP 0.96; RRLP at most 0.17, Johanniter) (l.
  382-414, 820, 838-839). RRIE cuts the foliage AUDPC most (to 4 × 10³ at 0.40 in scenario
  3), RRLP least (3.0 × 10⁴) and leaves the clusters at 20 (l. 441-466); combined RRs act
  more than additively (l. 824).
- **Inconsistencies:** the scenario 2 and 3 paragraphs quote each other's Table 2 values
  (l. 583 against 398, 678 against 414); an AUDPC of 7.1 × 10⁵ in the text against 71,000
  in the figure inset (l. 471, 525).

## Dependence

- **Borrows** the Bove et al. 2020a simulator (`bove2020`: values from Goidanich, Caffi
  2013, Rossi 2008, Lalancette) and Bove & Rossi 2020's measured components
  (`bove2020resistance`); no engine model is cited for an equation here, and no data
  were fitted.
- **Authors:** Rossi (engine models), Savary and Willocquet (`savary2009`): flags.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- A structural account of how resistance components change a Savary-type epidemic; for a
  truth with cultivar effects, the four entry points and the measured ranges are the
  usable part, with the caveat of `bove2020` that the simulator's own parameters are the
  engine family's.
