# Sajó 1901: a powdery year and a downy year in Hungary

## What it holds

- Compares 1899, a powdery mildew year, with 1900, a downy mildew year, in one district
  (Ör-Szent-Miklós), and links them to the weather, counting days of south-west wind from 1
  April to 31 August (34 in 1899).
