# Padró et al. 2019: UAV georeferencing accuracy

Research article, Catalonia, Spain (study area section 2.1, near Riner; lines ~132-135).

## What it holds

- Compares the absolute positional accuracy (ASPRS RMSE) of four UAV georeferencing methods (raw GNSS RMSEr ≤ 1.062 m;
- PPK1 ≤ 0.256 m;
- PPK2 ≤ 0.036 m;
- GCP ≤ 0.023 m) and their costs.
- It is only of indirect use, as geolocation error of UAV imagery with no disease content.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Positional error of UAV images by method (raw GNSS about 1 m; ground control points about 2 cm): a parameter for a drone operator.
