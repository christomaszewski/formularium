# Bruisson et al. 2019: leaf bacteria vs Botrytis and Phytophthora

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- 194 bacteria isolated from leaves of three grapevine cultivars (Solaris, Pinot Noir, Chasselas); 78 non-redundant strains tested (l. 23, 148).
- Six Bacillus strains reduced B. cinerea mycelial growth by 57-74 % (l. 455-458); the abstract gives above 60 % (l. 38).
- Seven strains strongly inhibited B. cinerea, above 50 % in full-plate assays (l. 742-744).
- Six Bacillus strains cut P. infestans mycelial growth by 86.2 to 96 % on direct exposure (l. 500-501).
- Three epiphytic strains inhibited P. infestans through volatiles; trimethylpyrazine, dihydrochalcone and L-dihydroxanthurenic acid were candidates (l. 53, 67, 546-547).
- Germination counts used about 60 zoospores per picture, read after 4 h, and about 75 sporangia read after 24 h at 18 C (l. 333, 339).
