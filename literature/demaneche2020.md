# Demanèche et al. 2020: amoeba lysate active substance vs downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Lysate of dead Willaertia magna C2c Maky amoebae (Amoéba, France) as a candidate biofungicide against grapevine downy mildew (l. 83-86).
- In vitro: sporocyst release 96 % to 5 % over 0.03-5 g/L; zoospore germination zero from 0.3 g/L (l. 171-173).
- Greenhouse: sporulation inhibited 77.5 % at 1 g/L and 100 % at 3 g/L; severity 56.7 % to 30.3 % over 1-5 g/L (l. 189, 207).
- Hungary 2019: untreated 4.5 % leaves and 21 % bunches; 2 kg/ha gave 2.0 % and 12.5 % (l. 246-247).
- Germany 2019 and France 2019: AXP01 77 % on bunches in Germany; AXP07 53 % on bunches in France (l. 421, 425).
- All authors are company employees (l. 756); the field trials were run by contract organisations (l. 699, 711, 728).
