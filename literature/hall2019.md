# Hall & Blackman 2019: modelled budbreak and maturity spread over three Australian wine regions

Heat-accumulation phenology run on 30 m interpolated daily temperature maps for 20 years (1998-2018) in three regions; the paper compares year-to-year and within-region variability.

**Read this first.**
- The budbreak spread is model output, not an observation. The paper's own caveat: modelled budbreak date has an RMSE of 5 to 10 days (de Cortázar-Atauri et al. 2009 and Ferguson et al. 2014, cited) (l. 551).
- Table 2 is headed "Temporal distribution": its columns are the 5th to 95th percentiles of years. Reading of the writer: the SD row is the spread over map pixels of the modelled budbreak date for the map of that percentile year (the text calls it spatial variability and spatiotemporal variability, l. 353-365). It is not a year-to-year SD and not a measurement of vines.
- The file is a Wayback capture; the text runs to page 159 and the reference list, so it is not truncated. The table's layout is wide and the row labels sit on neighbouring lines.

## What it holds

- **Method:** daily maximum and minimum temperature interpolated to topoclimate maps at 30 m resolution for 20 years (l. 37), then a heat-accumulation phenology model to estimate budbreak and maturity for every 30 m pixel (l. 39).
- **Main conclusion:** within-region temporal variability exceeded within-region spatial variability (l. 44), for growing season temperature, cool night index and post-harvest GDD.
- **Budbreak SD, Canberra District (Table 2, l. 447):** 8.56, 9.42, 8.85, 9.33 and 10.28 days at the 5th, 25th, 50th, 75th and 95th percentile (days after 30 June); mean date 100 to 116 (l. 446).
- **Budbreak SD, Orange (l. 447):** 7.54, 8.31, 8.08, 8.32 and 9.00 days.
- **Budbreak SD, Yarra Valley (l. 447):** 18.27, 19.16, 20.14, 19.68 and 21.19 days, about twice the others; the mean date there is 88 to 107 days (l. 446). Coefficient of variation 0.20 to 0.21 against 0.07 to 0.09 elsewhere (l. 449).
- **Range quoted in the lead:** 7.5 to 10 days is the Canberra and Orange range; the Yarra figure is higher and the paper does not explain it in the part read.
- **Model error:** RMSE of 5 to 10 days for modelled budbreak date (l. 551).

## Dependence

- Own temperature interpolation and a heat-accumulation budbreak method (the text cites Moncur et al. 1989 for the budbreak threshold, l. 194, 244). No field budbreak scoring is used for Table 2.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **host.budburst_sd_days (5), context only for Agrarium:** a modelled within-region spread of about 7.5 to 10 days (Canberra, Orange) and 18 to 21 days (Yarra) over 30 m pixels.
