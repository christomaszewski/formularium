# Vélez et al. 2024: leaf spectroscopy for powdery mildew

Leaf reflectance from 350 to 2500 nm, measured on Tempranillo leaves at one organic vineyard in Valladolid, to grade powdery mildew infestation. The authors also propose a new index for severity.

## What it holds

- Leaves were graded into five classes by area affected, following EPPO guidelines, on 31 July and 10 September (l. 22–24, 275–276).
- Partial least squares regression gave R² 0.74 for calibration and 0.71 for validation. The RMSE was 12.1% and 12.9% (l. 27–28).
- Healthy and infected leaves differ most near 450 and 700 nm, and at 1050, 1425, 1650 and 2250 nm (l. 29–31).
- The index PMVI = (R755 − R675)/(R755 + R675) gave R² 0.7 for severity (l. 33–37).

## Dependence

- Leaf grading follows the EPPO guidelines the paper cites (l. 22–24).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t2 (observation) and t3 (powdery mildew). Leaf-level data from one cultivar and two dates. A vineyard model could take the five-class scale and the index as an observation input. Hand-held, so not a landscape signal.
