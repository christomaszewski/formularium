# Dalla Marta et al. 2006: SWEB wetness driving PLASMO

## What it holds

- SWEB, a physical energy-balance wetness model, adapted to Sangiovese at Mondeggi-Lappeggi
  (Chianti) from standard station data; checked against visual observations on 40 leaves
  over 12 dry nights in 2003 (percent correct 84 %, CSI 0.55; the text and table labels
  disagree on which comparison this is).
- PLASMO run on sensor and on SWEB wetness: 5 against 3 infections in 2002, 21 against 20 in
  2003, with similar simulated disease; sensors overestimated wetness by about 2 h.

## Dependence

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- PLASMO's wetness input; for Agrarium, a physical wetness model (SWEB) clear of the
  engine's RH thresholds. Dalla Marta and Orlandini also wrote Sentelhas et al. 2008: a flag
  only.
