# Pérez-Rodríguez et al. 2015: a raindrop-by-raindrop model of copper wash-off

Laboratory wash-off of three copper fungicides under a single-raindrop simulator, with a
stochastic model of cumulative detachment by randomly placed drop impacts. Only the
abstract was read.

## What it holds

- Losses measured as total, dissolved and particulate copper; most copper left as
  particles of 0.3-1 µm (abstract).
- Best-fitting parameters (abstract): the area one impact clears, 1.7 cm² (large drops)
  and 0.38 cm² (small); the impacts on one area that exhaust the non-rainfast fungicide,
  2.5 ± 0.5 large or 4.2 ± 3.0 small drops at the high detachment level and 30 ± 10 large
  or 40 ± 88 small at the low; the mass one impact removes, from 1.27 ± 0.2 µg to 3 ± 1 ng
  of copper.
- In a factorial test of drop size, fall height and dose, dose mattered most for all three
  formulations (abstract).

## Dependence

- None recorded from the abstract; its own laboratory data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's grower and Cooptera's E6: the only mechanistic wash-off model found for
  copper, with a non-rainfast fraction exhausted by a few drop impacts; turning it into
  loss per mm of rain on a vine canopy needs the paper's equations and the canopy's drop
  interception, which the abstract does not give. The full text is closed (on the unread
  list).
