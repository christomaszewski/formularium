# Hubbard et al. 2021: soil classes and vigour in a Bordeaux vineyard

Research article, Pessac Léognan Appellation, Bordeaux, France.

## What it holds

- EMI geophysical and NDVI data (2 m) from one Pessac Léognan vineyard are used to map Bordeaux soil classes and relate them to grapevine vigor (r = 0.75, p < 0.01).
- It is about soil and vigor; no disease is treated.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Soil and vigour mapping; nothing for disease.
