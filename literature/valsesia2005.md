# Valsesia et al. 2005: quantifying P. viticola DNA in leaves by real-time PCR

From ETH Zurich and the Istituto Agrario di San Michele all'Adige.

## What it holds

- This is a method paper for screening biocontrol agents, not a field study (l. 46). All infections are artificial (l. 89; 2 x 10^5 conidia/ml, 12 h, 20 C, l. 92).
- Multiplex TaqMan qPCR: P. viticola primers/probe Giop (ITS1/5.8S, 208 bp, l. 142) and a V. vinifera reference set on resveratrol synthase I (63 bp, l. 162).
- Minimal detectable P. viticola DNA is 0.1 pg (l. 29). Quantifiable from 0.1 pg to 1 ng, R2 0.9981 (l. 245, l. 244). Spread of replicates grows below 1 pg (l. 247).
- The infection coefficient is CT(vine) / CT(pathogen) (l. 75), corrected with a calibrator DNA (l. 80).
- Leaf-disc to leaf-disc variation is the main source of error (l. 297).
- It assumes equal rDNA copy number among isolates (l. 384).
- Primers were designed from 30 GenBank oomycete sequences (l. 97). No disease model or model-dated data is involved.

## Dependence

- A method paper with artificial greenhouse infections; its Giop primers are reused by Yang 2023 and, credited to Si Ammour 2020, by Heger 2026.
