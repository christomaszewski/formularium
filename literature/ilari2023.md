# Ilari et al. 2023: LiDAR sprayer and agrochemical spread in vines

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- One vineyard, Arcevia (Marche, Italy), 2021: LiDAR on/off sprayer against a conventional sprayer and an untreated control (l. 630).
- Water-sensitive paper: most frequent coverage class above 20% in standard tests, 10-15% with LiDAR (l. 25).
- Downy mildew incidence on 10 Sept 2021: 9.34% untreated, 3.81% LiDAR, 11.21% conventional (l. 1149-1152).
- LiDAR cut incidence by 59% against untreated and 66% against conventional (l. 1126; matches Table 7).
- Powdery mildew severity on 23 July 2021: 1.83 untreated, 1.33 LiDAR, 1.00 conventional (l. 1149-1152).
- Spring 2021 was the second driest in the Marche Region since 1961 (l. 1046).

## Dependence

- none. No disease model is computed, borrowed or fitted: the disease assessments use incidence, severity and the McKinney index (cited to refs 43-44), and the weather is observed (ASSAM station). The 'simple prediction model' (l. 134) is cited to ref 26 and not used here. No shared authors flagged.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A one-season field trial of spray distribution with downy mildew incidence on bunches (l. 1149-1152): a possible check for a spray-efficacy value in the truth, but no model to borrow for the truth or the Cooptera engine.
