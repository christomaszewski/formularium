# Nogueira Júnior et al. 2020: photosynthetic cost of defence in grapevine

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Greenhouse study of photosynthesis in grapevine resistant (Solaris, Cabernet Blanc, Phoenix) and susceptible (Riesling, Niagara Rosada, Merlot, Moscato) cultivars after Plasmopara viticola (l. 36-37, 164-166).
- In Solaris, net photosynthesis was 25 per cent lower before HR and 57 per cent lower one day after HR was seen (l. 39-41).
- At 12 dai, susceptible cultivars lost 41 to 56 per cent of net photosynthesis (l. 317); the authors give an average 55 per cent cut in resistant cultivars (l. 354).
- Stomatal conductance and transpiration fell from 4 dai on resistant cultivars (l. 275).
- Susceptible cultivars reached 34 to 53 per cent disease severity (time not stated, l. 299).
- Conclusion: infection carries a carbon cost that persists to 12 dai in resistant cultivars (l. 60, 562-566).

## Dependence
