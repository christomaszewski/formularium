# Lin et al. 2020: a review of how crop canopies partition rain and sprinkler water, with no grapevine in the text

A concise review of 138 studies on throughfall, stemflow and interception for more than 60 crop species.

**Read this first.** This is a review, and every number in it is an ANALOGUE for a vine. A search of the whole text read finds no grapevine, vine or Vitis; whether the supplementary Table S1 lists one was not checked (the supplement was not obtained). The canopy storage range (0.6 to 6.0 mm) is cited from the review's reference 46, which was not identified and not read.

## What it holds

- **Scope:** 138 studies, more than 60 crop species, all major climate types (l. 22).
- **Partitions across crops:** the interquartile range of throughfall, stemflow and interception is 58-83 %, 2-26 % and 11-32 % of rain and sprinkler water (abstract; l. 169). The text says the partitions vary greatly across crop types.
- **Stemflow by crop group:** tree-dominated crops (fruits, nuts, cloves, coffee) were the lowest stemflow producers, with group medians of about 2 to 5 % of rainfall (l. 175). Cereals, for contrast, had a median of 33.4 % (l. 174).
- **Maximum canopy water storage:** crop canopies are "reported" to hold 0.6 to 6.0 mm per event, up to 11.2 mm for multi-layer agroforestry, cited from reference [46] (l. 192-194). It is a depth of water per canopy, not per unit LAI. The store available before an event depends on how far the canopy dried in the dry period between events (l. 194-196).
- **Event-size model (Eqs. 1 and 2, l. 199-201):** throughfall or stemflow fraction y = alpha (1 - exp(-beta P)); interception fraction yI = alpha exp(-beta P) + c, with P the event size in mm. The interception fraction falls with event size toward a minimum c.
- The review also says partitions differ with the size of the rain or irrigation event and the stage of crop growth (l. 28-29).

## Dependence

- A review of other authors' studies; the storage range comes from its reference 46, not identified. Its crop groups and medians depend on which studies exist (mostly non-vine).

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **micro.interception (0.6, range 0.3-0.9):** the review's interception range (11-32 % interquartile, across crops) is a whole-event fraction, not a share caught until a store is full; it is far below 0.6 but not the same quantity. The event-size model, with a falling interception fraction as events grow, has the shape of "catch until full" (a fixed store, then rain passes), which supports the structure of the rule, not its 0.6.
- **micro.storage_mm_per_lai (0.25):** the cited 0.6 to 6.0 mm per event is a depth per canopy, not per LAI. An assumed LAI of 2 to 3 (my assumption) gives 0.5 to 0.75 mm for the registry's store (arithmetic, not printed), at the bottom of that range. No vine value; for tree crops, ANALOGUE only.
- **Wetness threshold and drying parameters:** not addressed.
- **Kind of result:** review of mostly non-vine studies; no vine data. Not usable to set a vine value.
