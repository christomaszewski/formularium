# Buffara et al. 2014: diagram scale for grapevine downy mildew severity

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Scale of seven levels: 1, 3, 6, 12, 25, 50 and 75 % leaf area with downy mildew, in colour and black-and-white (l. 32, 153-154).
- Built from 30 leaves of the Italia cultivar in a commercial vineyard at Lages, SC; measured with ASSESS (l. 98-106).
- Eight raters (four inexperienced, four experienced) scored the same 30 leaf images (l. 118-120).
- Mean R2 for actual against estimated severity: 0.8877 without the scale, 0.9369 colour, 0.9341 black-and-white (Table 1, l. 230-240).
- Most assessment errors fell at 20-45 % severity (l. 193).
- The text's accuracy 88 % and precision 95 % (l. 48-49) do not match the table means.

## Dependence

- none. The paper uses the Weber-Fechner stimulus-response law to space the scale levels (l. 111-112, 146) and ASSESS image software to measure the reference severity (l. 106). Neither is a listed engine model, and the paper computes no infection, wetness or sporulation output.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Offers the truth an observation-error reference for field scoring of downy mildew (rater R2 about 0.89 without a scale and 0.93-0.94 with one, over 30 leaves); it has no weather or infection data, so it helps only the observation operator.
