# Zorrilla et al. 2024: Glechoma hederacea extracts suppress Plasmopara viticola

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Lab leaf-disc test (5 dishes of 5 discs per treatment, 2.5 x 10^4 sporangia/mL, severity at seven days): aqueous G. hederacea extract 11.4 % severity, against 78.0 % in water and 2.3 % for copper hydroxide (l. 272-292, overprinted).
- Six pure compounds from the extract; caffeic acid 18.8 % and methyl caffeate 21.2 % severity at 500 µg/mL against 65.6 % in the negative control (l. 515-521).
- Carvacrol, cirsimaritin and apigenin gave 41.0-48.0 % (l. 517); rosmarinic acid gave 66.0 %, no effect (l. 519).
- Dichloromethane extract 20.4 %; n-hexane 38.4 %; ethyl acetate 59.6 % (l. 431-446).
- Activity above that of the single compounds suggests synergy or addition (l. 687-689).
- Field trials are not in this paper; the authors cite 70-94 % field efficacies for other plant extracts (l. 97-99).
