# Luo & Michailides 2001: latent infection of prune fruit by Monilinia fructicola

From UC Davis (Kearney Agricultural Center). Prune, not grape.

## What it holds

- Own inoculations on prune branches in 3 California orchards: one bloom and four fruit stages each (l. 18) (l. 151).
- Five spore doses, 1,000 to 30,000 conidia/ml (l. 75); wetness 4-24 h at bloom and 4-16 h on fruit (l. 84) (l. 163). Bags made the wetness; field temperature was logged and not controlled (l. 162).
- Measure: share of fruit that sporulated after freezing and 5 days at 23 C (ILI), not infection counted directly (l. 186).
- Susceptibility changed with fruit stage; mean ILI at bloom 8.6% (l. 291).
- ILI rose about 0.5% per 1,000 conidia/ml (l. 312), linearly with wetness at bloom (0.37-0.56% per hour) (l. 314) and exponentially at fruit stages (l. 22); coefficients 0.20-0.52 at pit hardening (l. 318), 0.11-0.45 late embryo (l. 326), 0.02-0.10 before harvest (l. 327).
- Optimum adjusted temperature 15.2 C at pit hardening (l. 335), 14.0 C at embryo growth (l. 346); 23 C at 8 h wetness (l. 366).
- Temperature was adjusted with a Gaussian (peak 21.75 C, SD 5.90 C) taken from the authors' blossom-blight paper (l. 255) (l. 251). Eq. 2 is printed without 1/n (l. 272).

## Dependence

- Its own inoculations in three orchards. Its temperature analysis is binned on an "adjusted temperature" weighted by a Gaussian (peak 21.75 °C) taken from the authors' own blossom-blight paper (l. 251-255), so the optima it reports rest on that paper's curve. Eq. 2 prints no 1/n (l. 272).
- No author of an engine model; the institution (UC Davis) is no flag under D27.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Clear. Its stage-dependent susceptibility (exponential in wetness at fruit stages, linear at bloom) is a pattern, not a formulation Agrarium needs.
