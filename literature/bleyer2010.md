## What it holds

- VitiMeteo system overview (2010 volume): Agrometeo database; VitiMeteo Plasmopara (2002) calculates downy mildew life-cycle stages; Oidium module uses Kast's OiDiag (l. 21-47).
- Expert models parameterise primary infection, sporulation and secondary infection; the authors name these as the parts to optimise (l. 53-54, 78-80).
- Validation of Plasmopara against field observations is asserted, with no data given; validated in northern Italy and South Tyrol from 2008 (l. 64-72).
- Since 2009 the Meteoblue forecast gives a 5-day forecast of incubation and growth (l. 82-90).
- Figures 2 and 3 (Kirchheim and Freiburg, May 2009): model leaf area 165 and 106 cm2 on 7 May (l. 278, 317).
