# Comba et al. 2020: vineyard LAI from 3D point clouds made from UAV multispectral images

## What it holds

- **Method:** LAI by multivariate linear regression on canopy descriptors (thickness, height, leaf density along the wall) from dense point clouds (abstract, l. 20-30).
- **Validation:** one vineyard at Serralunga d'Alba, Piedmont; leaf area of 704 vines measured by inclined point quadrat; six UAV flights (l. 30-31, 157, 229). R2 0.82 against the manual method (l. 30-35).
