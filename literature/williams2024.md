# Williams et al. 2024: cover-crop spectra in vineyard monitoring

Research article, Southeast England, a large commercial wine producer; three growing seasons.

## What it holds

- Uses UAV and Sentinel-2 multispectral data with XGBoost to predict grape yield and quality, finding that pure cover-crop UAV pixels predict as well as pure vine pixels (R2 0.37 to 0.45) and that cover crop dominates the Sentinel-2 signal.
- It bears on mixed pixels in satellite vineyard monitoring but contains no disease content.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Cover crop dominates the Sentinel-2 signal of a vineyard: a reason a satellite operator should see disease poorly.
