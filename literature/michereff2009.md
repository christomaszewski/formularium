# Michereff et al. 2009: diagrammatic scale for melon downy mildew

A diagrammatic scale for melon downy mildew severity, checked by eight raters on 50 leaves. Accuracy, precision and repeatability were all between 87% and 94%.

## What it holds

- Eight levels: 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64, 82 and 96% of affected leaf area (l. 16).
- Mean accuracy 87.5%, precision 94% and repeatability 94%. Reproducibility was 90% or more in 90.3% of cases (l. 25-28).
- Raters underestimated. Each 1% of electronically measured severity gave 0.85% and 0.87% estimates in the two rounds (l. 116).
- Trial: 50 leaves, rated twice by eight raters 15 days apart (l. 19-23).

## Dependence

- Reference severities come from the Assess software (l. 21, 99).
- Accuracy and precision by linear regression, after Nutter and Schultz 1995 (cited in the methods).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t2 (observation). The underestimation bias (about 13 to 15 percent of the true value) is a check a scoring step could apply to visual severity.
- The scale is for melon, not vines, and the trial year is not printed. Not read in full.
