# Nascimento et al. 2020: a 12-level diagram for powdery mildew on watermelon

A 12-level diagram for powdery mildew on watermelon leaves. Sixteen novice raters rated with and without it, and it raised precision, accuracy and agreement.

## What it holds

- **Diagram:** 12 levels from 0.07 to 100% leaf area (abstract, l. 29-31).
- **Method:** 16 raters each rated 40 leaf images unaided, then 40 again with the diagram. Each image was shown for 30 s (l. 112-119).
- **Agreement:** Lin's rho-c rose from 0.734 to 0.952. The bias factor Cb rose from 0.830 to 0.982. The intraclass correlation rose from 0.759 to 0.928 (abstract, l. 34-38).
- **Mismatch:** r without the diagram is 0.878 in the English abstract (l. 34), but 0.787 in the Portuguese abstract (l. 53).
- **Surfaces:** upper and lower leaf severity correlate at r 0.959 (l. 148-149).

## Dependence

- Lin's concordance and the epiR package (l. 126-128). Intraclass correlation from the irr package (l. 130-131).
- Linear class intervals after Nutter and Esker 2006 (l. 109-111).
- Quant software for true severity (Vale et al. 2003) (l. 98-99).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t3 (powdery mildew). The rater-error design (novice raters, with and without a diagram, judged by Lin's concordance) could be copied for a grape powdery mildew diagram. A simulator cannot take the watermelon numbers.
