# Levrault, Brisson, Pieri and Bosc 2010: regional summaries of the CLIMATOR vine runs (French)

The regions part of the green book: for each zone, what the A1B runs give for vines. Despite the title (South-West), the file holds all the regional chapters; the English twin is levrault2011.

**Read this first.** Same runs as brisson2010; nothing new in the models. No downy mildew (no "mildiou" in the file); Botrytis appears once, as a result quoted from the health chapter (l. 830).

## What it holds

- **Limits (l. 189):** the models ignore slope; results need adjusting for hillside vineyards; the reference weather station may lie outside the vine area.
- **Feasibility (l. 252, 817):** Chardonnay becomes possible at every station in the distant future; in the Centre-North vines become feasible sporadically around the 2030s and regularly from about 2050; Grenache only sporadically at the end of the century.
- **Sites:** Lusignan soon suitable for merlot (l. 2198); Avignon (South-East, grenache) already a vine region, with strong ripening warming and irregular aquifer recharge (l. 2410).
- **Irrigation:** need rises even with a low-input rule (ETR/ETM held at 0.3 or more, buried drip; l. 175) in the South-West and South-East.
- **Botrytis (l. 830):** low to moderate risk at the newly feasible Centre-North sites.

## Dependence

- Summary of brisson2010's runs; counts once. Climate series TT and QQ are the downscalings named there.
