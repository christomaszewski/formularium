# Chechi et al. 2021: rain after fungicide spraying and soybean rust

A greenhouse test of simulated rain 30 to 240 minutes after spraying. Rain sooner after spraying cut rust control. The effect was largest for protectant fungicides at 70 L/ha.

## What it holds

- **Design:** five fungicides, two penetrants and three protectants, at 70 and 150 L/ha. Rain at six intervals from 30 to 240 minutes (l. 23-26; l. 79-86).
- **Timing:** penetrants gave the best control with rain at 120 to 180 minutes. Protectants gave the best control with rain near 240 minutes (l. 29-32).
- **Untreated check:** 98 and 117 uredinia per leaflet in 2017 and 2018 (l. 134-135).
- **Table 1:** trifloxystrobin plus prothioconazole at 70 L/ha gave 74.0 uredinia per leaflet with rain at 30 minutes and 14.7 at 240 minutes, in 2017 (l. 145-150).

## Dependence

- Abbott (1925) control formula (l. 118).
- Detached-leaf method from Scherb and Mehl (2006) (l. 101-102).
- Polynomial regression of control on rain interval (l. 120-122).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (fungicide protection):** a rain-interval multiplier on protection could be tested in a spray engine. The host is soybean, and the regression coefficients are not legible in this text layer.
