# Albelda et al. 2005: airborne spores at Cenlle (Ribeiro), 2004

## What it holds

- **Site and trap:** Cenlle, Ribeiro; a Lanzoni VPPS-2000 at 2 m, 15 April to 22 September
  2004 (161 days); weather from a station a few metres away. Seven fungicide sprays.
- **Season totals:** Botrytis 6,519 conidia, Uncinula 1,554, Plasmopara 311 sporangia.
  Plasmopara peaked at 18/m³ on 22 September and was absent on more than a third of days;
  its daily peak hour was 17-18 h.
- **Regressions** (in-sample, adjusted R²): Botrytis 0.466, Uncinula 0.436, Plasmopara
  0.135. The Plasmopara F is 8.191 in Table 5 and 3.135 in the text.
- **Cited rule:** primary infection after more than 10 mm of rain on 1-2 days at a mean
  above 12 °C (Hidalgo 1993).
