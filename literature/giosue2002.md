# Giosuè et al. 2002: maps of when primary infections came, Emilia-Romagna

Only the abstract is held. The volume and pages are as Sanna 2017 cites them, not checked.

## What it holds

- **Data:** the first symptom dates in the same 127 vineyards, 1993-2000, as Rossi et al.
  2002 ([rossi2002](rossi2002.md)).
- **Method:**
  - The possible infection period is found by counting incubation back from the first
    symptoms. Within it, the probable infection day is set from the weather.
  - For each vineyard, the mean of its yearly deviations from the regional mean date is
    kriged (GS+) on the UTM coordinates of its comune.
- **Results:**
  - Primary infection came late in hill zones of Reggio Emilia and Forlì, and early on the
    plains of Reggio Emilia, Modena, Bologna and Ravenna. The first symptoms followed the
    same pattern.
  - The differences between zones "si mantengono sufficientemente costanti nei diversi
    anni".

## Dependence

- The abstract does not say which incubation was counted back. Sanna 2017 cites this paper
  for the regressions "adapted to the evaluation table of ... Goidànich". Rossi et al. 2002
  states it outright (p. 265).

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Zones keep their order from year to year: a spatial pattern of onset for the truth's
  history matching, if the maps could be had. Ask Vittorio Rossi for the paper.
