# Shishkoff 2019: oospores of impatiens downy mildew

## What it holds

- Plasmopara obducens forms oospores in single isolates (homothallic). A month or more at 0
  °C induced germination and infection; constant -10, 10 and 20 °C did not; fluctuating
  -10/0 and 0/10 °C induced some.

## Dependence

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Another Plasmopara: a cold requirement for oospore germination that a P. viticola truth
  could be compared with in kind only. For Cooptera, an ornamental disease.
