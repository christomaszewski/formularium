# Escalona et al. 2012: whole-plant carbon balance of potted young vines

Four varieties (Cabernet Sauvignon, Malvasía, Tempranillo, Grenache) rooted from cuttings in
16 L pots, irrigated or stressed for 30 days, with whole-plant respiration measured. Read
for leaf area; the plants are potted cuttings, not field vines.

## What it holds

- **Plants** (l. 84-133): one-year-old ungrafted cuttings, outdoors three months, one main
  shoot of 1.5 m with 20-22 mature leaves at the start; field capacity or watering stopped
  until noon stomatal conductance fell to 75 mmol m⁻² s⁻¹.
- **Leaf area** (Table 3, l. 443-460; m² a plant, initial and final): irrigated 0.19-0.27 to
  0.36-0.46; stressed to 0.25-0.31 (Tempranillo 0.21 to 0.40 irrigated, 0.28 stressed).
