# Funes Mesa et al. 2024: water needs and the growing cycle of three Catalan PDOs to 2100

IRTA and the Meteorological Service of Catalonia run a FAO-56 water balance and a
degree-day phenology at 1 km over the Empordà, Pla de Bages and Penedès vineyards, on
MPI-ESM downscaled to 1971-2100 under RCP4.5 and 8.5. The project's region; the
phenology is a single Catalan parameter set with its data unpublished.

## What it holds

- **Climate:** MPI-ESM statistically downscaled to 1 km by an analogue method with a
  covariance correction (l. 240-266), 1971-2005 control (Table 1 says 1972), 2006-2100
  (l. 218-248); a 1 km observed grid 1971-2015 as the base (l. 245).
- **Phenology** (`mesa2024.gdd`; p. 6): degree-days base 10 °C, optimum 25 °C and a top
  threshold printed as 3 °C, from 1 January: budbreak 406, flowering 517, fruit set 798,
  pea size 1,322, harvest 1,962 °C·d, dormancy restarting at 107, after Ramos et al.
  2008, from Catalan BBCH records of a few varieties, data not shown (l. 315-338).
- **Water:** FAO-56 with Hargreaves-Samani ET0, Kc as a growth function of degree-days
  (Funes et al. 2021), a daily plant-soil balance from 1 October, net water needs as ETc
  minus ETa, no stress coefficient (l. 328-358, 745); Penedès soil maps, 80 mm of
  available water assumed elsewhere (l. 274-309).
- **Results** (Table 2): flowering 8.4 (Empordà, 2030s) to 44.6 d (Bages, 2090s) earlier;
  the budburst-to-harvest cycle 7.0 to 68.4 d shorter, the Penedès baseline cycle
  287 d (budburst near 1 January, unexplained); harvest 45-87 d earlier in the 2070s under
  RCP8.5 (l. 443-530); water needs two to almost four times today's by 2100 (l. 433);
  ripening minima +1.4 to +2.9 °C (l. 50). The abstract rounds these to weeks and months.

## Dependence

- **Borrows** FAO-56, Hargreaves-Samani, Winkler's base, Anderson et al. 1986's optimum,
  Ramos et al. 2008's stage method; cites `ramos2017`'s Penedès shortening of 20 d for
  Chardonnay (l. 666). Nothing fitted in print.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- The regional default a Catalan agency uses for every vineyard pixel: a yardstick for
  Agrarium's phenology (406 °C·d to budburst from 1 January, base 10) and a warning that
  its data are not shown and its upper threshold misprinted.
