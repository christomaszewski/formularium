# Hamada et al. 2012: climate favorability map of downy mildew, Brazil

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A monthly map of climatic favourability for grapevine downy mildew across Brazil, made in the Idrisi GIS (l. 12-13).
- Input: the 1961-1990 climatological normal (l. 14).
- Bands set from mean temperature and leaf-wetness period; the band values are not printed (l. 14-15).
- January to May are favourable in all producing regions (l. 16-17).
- The second half of the year has the most unfavourable areas (l. 17).
- The Northeast is unfavourable until December; the South until September (l. 18-19).
- A one-page conference abstract: Summa Phytopathologica 38 (supplement), February 2012 (l. 21).

## Dependence
