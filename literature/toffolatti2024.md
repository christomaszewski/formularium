# Toffolatti et al. 2024: anti-resistance spray programmes

Research article, Northern Italy (Zoppola, Friuli; Santa Maria della Versa, Lombardy).

## What it holds

- Three-year field trials (2019-2021) in two Italian vineyards on anti-resistance spray programmes with mandipropamid and oxathiapiprolin, with EC50 and PvCesA3 G1105S/V resistance allele frequencies, and a proposed flow cytometry method for sporangia.
- It gives no epidemic or weather-driven formulation.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.
