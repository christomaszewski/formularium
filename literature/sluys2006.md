# Sluys 2006: temperature and a frost case in the Waipara Basin (MSc thesis)

A Master's thesis on climate and grapevine development at one vineyard and three others in the Waipara Basin, with HOBO loggers on two terraces and a frost case study.

**Read this first.**
- Qualitative on cold pockets: it gives a coldest logger value but no temperature deficit per metre of depth. The thesis does not print the elevation difference between its terraces in the passages read.
- The frost case study is dated 2nd-3rd June 2003 (l. 4581), yet it sits in the section on the growing season of 27 September to 29 December 2004 (contents). The date may be a slip; it was not resolved.
- The coldest case-night value (-1.51 °C) is at a logger that Table 6.3 lists on the upper terrace, while the text puts the coldest case-night area on the lower terrace (l. 4837); not resolved.
- The thesis was read by grep only, not in full.

## What it holds

- **Site:** the main site is the McKenzie vineyard, with Canterbury House, River Terrace and Waipara West for the basin (abstract, l. 259-262). HOBO loggers sit on an upper and a lower terrace (Table 6.3).
- **Main finding:** temperature is the main climatic factor, and temperature varies across the Waipara Basin (l. 265).
- **Minima, Table 6.3 (l. 3293):** minimum 1.5 m air temperature per logger -1.51, -1.31, -1.34, -0.8 (upper terrace) and -1.26, -1.4, -1.71 °C (lower terrace). The coldest is -1.71 °C, at a lower-terrace logger. A spread of 0.9 °C among loggers (arithmetic).
- **Warmer averages on the lower terrace:** averages are higher on the lower terrace (l. 3278), so the cold pocket shows in the minima and the case night, not the mean.
- **Frost case:** the coldest event reached 0 °C at 03:41 and -1.51 °C at 07:01 at one logger (l. 4585-4587); every logger was above zero by 07:45 (l. 4591); one logger never fell as far as the others (l. 4593).
- **Where it was coldest:** the coldest temperatures in the case study were at the southern end of the lower terrace (l. 4837), the lowest elevation of the vineyard (l. 4839).
- **Mechanism as given:** cool air flows down from the upper terrace and pools (l. 4841); the area is also affected by cold air draining down Weka Creek (l. 4847), and a shelter belt on the eastern boundary may add to the pooling (l. 4847-4849).

## Dependence

- Own logger data (2003-2004). The cold-air drainage figure is credited to a person (Ben Meuli, 2005), not a paper. Geiger et al. 1977 is cited for canopy effects.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **micro.coldpool_c_per_m (0.6) and micro.coldpool_max_c (4) in Agrarium:** qualitative support that a low terrace end pools cold air under a clear night; no per-metre deficit, no depth, and the largest logger difference is under 1 °C in the minima table. It cannot test either value.
- **Kind of evidence:** field loggers at one vineyard over one growing season; a thesis, not peer-reviewed. The cold-pocket mechanism is the author's explanation, not a test.
