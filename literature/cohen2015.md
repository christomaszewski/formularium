# Cohen & Rubin 2015: solar heat against basil downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Sweet basil downy mildew (Peronospora belbahrii) in Israel, first seen November 2011 and spread by 2012; mefenoxam resistance within a year (l. 40-44).
- Growth-chamber tests: 35-45 C for 6-9 h suppressed survival of spores, infected leaves and plants (l. 20-21; 349-350).
- Younger mycelium (5 dpi) tolerated 35-40 C better; 45 C killed all ages (l. 355-357).
- Polyethylene covers raised daytime maxima by 11-22 C to 40-58 C in houses; 1-3 days of 2-7 h cover cut sporulation (l. 24-26; 598-606).
- Field and house experiments A-H in 2013-2014 at Bar-Ilan University (l. 209).
- Kennelly et al. 2007 field observations of P. viticola sporangia are quoted as background (l. 744, 765).
