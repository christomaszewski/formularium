# Hoffman et al. 2002: how long grape berries are susceptible to black rot, and how age slows symptoms

Field inoculations of Concord, Chardonnay and Riesling clusters weekly from bloom to 8
weeks after with 2 × 10^5 conidia/ml, with disease counted every 2 days for 3-5 weeks: the
berries' susceptible window and the incubation period in degree hours, by fruit age.

## What it holds

- **Window:** Concord's maximum susceptibility ran from midbloom to 2-4 weeks later, some
  berries still infected at 4-5 weeks; Chardonnay and Riesling from midbloom to 3-5 weeks,
  some until 6-7 weeks (abstract, l. 27-32), '2 to 4 weeks shorter than previously
  assumed'.
- **Incidence by age** (1997): under 1 % of Chardonnay and Riesling berries from flowers
  inoculated at 5 % bloom developed black rot, about 30 % when inoculated 2 weeks later, and
  a half to a quarter of that 2 weeks after; none within 21 days from inoculations 6 weeks
  past 5 % bloom (l. 231-237).
- **Incubation:** expressed in degree hours above 0 °C after inoculation; DH50 (degree hours
  to half the final severity) rose by at least 50 % as berries neared the end of their
  susceptible period, and new symptoms kept appearing for over a month after inoculating
  older fruit (abstract, l. 19-20 and the abstract's continuation). Accumulated degree hours
  (× 100) from 50 % bloom were 40.6 at 1 week and 300.4 at 8 weeks in 1999 (Table 1,
  l. 150-164). Probit slopes by fruit age are in Table 3 and DH50 in Fig. 3, not read here.

## Dependence

- Its own field inoculations; no model dated them. Wilcox, Gadoury and Seem are authors of
  the engine's Kennelly pieces: flags.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For a black rot truth (with `spotts1977.black_rot_wetness`): the cluster window
  (bloom to about 5 weeks, longest in vinifera) and an incubation that lengthens with berry
  age. A record of the window would carry the engine's bunch-window form
  (`bunch-susceptibility-window`), so kin to `kennelly2005.bunch_window` across diseases;
  Table 3 and Fig. 3 need reading in the page images before any record.
