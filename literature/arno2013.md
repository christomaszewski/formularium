# Arnó et al. 2013: destructive LAI of Merlot at Raimat on four dates

A ground LiDAR scanner against hand-defoliated row sections of Merlot at Raimat in 2005.
Read for its destructive LAI through the season.

## What it holds

- **Trials** (l. 303-314, Table 3 l. 330): Merlot at Raimat (Lleida), vineyard of Codorníu's
  agricultural division (l. 1060); four trials in 2005, each on a different block of three
  vines (a 4 m row section): 10 May (block I), 6 June (II), 7 July (III), 24 August (IV).
  Leaves stripped by hand in four 1 m sections and both sides; area by planimeter with a
  fresh-weight regression.
- **Destructive LAI of the 4 m zone** (Figure 5 labels, l. 1625-1642): 0.5033 (10 May),
  1.2646 (6 June), 1.6591 (7 July), 1.3601 (24 August). The 6 June label has the digits of
  Eq. 6's slope; the figure's own TAI (1.162) through Eq. 6 gives 1.28, so the label is
  plausible, but the print does not settle it.
- **LiDAR fits** (l. 788-794): LAI on tree area index R² 0.92, on canopy volume 0.81, on
  cross-section 0.72, on height 0.62. Pooled: LAI = 1.2646 TAI − 0.1935, R² 0.99 (Eq. 6,
  l. 768/385). TAI reads about 10 % under LAI (l. 876). Map in 1 m sections with 40-50
  scans (l. 985).
- **Cited** (l. 84): LAI-2000 tends to underestimate vineyard LAI.
- **Not printed:** row and vine spacing, trellis, irrigation, budburst and stages, leaf
  area per vine.

## Dependence

- TAI and the Poisson model after Walklate et al. 2002; canopy volume as a proxy after
  Llorens et al. 2011. Fits to the authors' own 2005 data. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **The one in-region seasonal series of measured LAI found:** about 30 % of the season's
  maximum on 10 May, 76 % on 6 June, 100 % on 7 July, and 82 % on 24 August (my ratios to
  1.66; four different blocks, so between-vine spread is in it). Raimat's budburst is not
  printed; with an early-April budburst these dates are about 35, 65 and 95 days after it,
  close to Agrarium's logistic with t50 = 50 d and tau = 10 d (21, 82 and 99 %). Inland
  Lleida, likely irrigated (not stated).
