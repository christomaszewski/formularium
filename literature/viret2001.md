# Viret et al. 2001: Swiss trials of a downy mildew warning station, with first oil spots at Perroy in 1998 and 1999

A Swiss review article on trials of the Lufft HP-100 warning station at four sites in 1998 to 2000, giving the first oil spot in a Perroy plot in 1998 and 1999 and the station's temperature-sum rule for the first primary infection.

**Read this first.**
- The text is an OCR of a scan. The reading JSON took its authors (Viret, Siegfried) from the in-text citation; the first page of the scan text lists five (Viret, Bloesch, Taillens, Siegfried, Dupuis). The header uses the first-page list.
- The 1998 value is not a date of first spot: the plot was "exempte de mildiou jusqu'au 26 juin" (free of mildew until 26 June, l. 519), so the first spots were on or after 26 June. Treat it as a bound.
- Table 2 (primary infections, incubation, first spots at Walenstadt, Wädenswil, Changins and Perroy, 1998 to 2000) is a graphic; the text layer does not carry its dates and they were not read.
- Which plot treatment the Perroy sentences refer to is not stated in the facts; the passage follows one on untreated plots.

## What it holds

- **Perroy 1999 (l. 512):** the first oil spots appeared on 8 June, although the station had signalled 22 infections from 12 May to 15 June (l. 510 to 512). The disease then grew fast: nearly 100 % of leaves hit with an intensity of 24.4 % on 13 July (l. 514 to 515).
- **Perroy 1998 (l. 519):** the same plot was free of mildew until 26 June; the station signalled 23 infections from May to the end of August, against 67 in 1999 (l. 521 to 522).
- **Station rule (l. 305 to 306):** the first primary infection is indicated when the sum of temperature at the 8 C threshold reaches 160 C and the infection conditions are met (10 mm of rain in 24 h and 8 to 10 C).
- **Authors' judgement on the rule (l. 475 to 479):** the 160 C sum is generally not a limiting factor for forecasting primary infection, except at Perroy in 2000; first oil spots often appear well after the end of incubation of the first primary infection signalled, even when sporulation conditions had been met several times.
- **Setting:** experimental plots of 20 to 50 ares at Changins, Perroy, Walenstadt and Wädenswil; control plots of at least 200 m2 without fungicide (l. 384). The station allowed up to 28 % fewer treatments than integrated production (l. 493); 28 % in 1998 (l. 525).

## Dependence

- **Own trials** of the Changins and Wädenswil stations. The warning station's model (the 160 C rule, the incubation and the infection rule) is the one being tested; no oospore germination test is reported in the facts read.
