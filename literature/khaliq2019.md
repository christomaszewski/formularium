# Khaliq et al. 2019: satellite and UAV imagery for vineyard variability

Research article, Serralunga d'Alba, Piedmont, northwest Italy (cv Nebbiolo).

## What it holds

- Compares Sentinel-2 NDVI with UAV NDVI in three Nebbiolo parcels, splitting UAV pixels into whole cropland, vine canopy only and inter-row only, to show how inter-row mixing biases satellite vigour maps.
- Pearson correlations between maps (Table 3) and ANOVA against in-field vigour classes (Tables 4-5) give a quantified mixed-pixel penalty; the topic is vigour, not disease.
