# Kumaşoğlu et al. 2022: infection and stilbenes in Turkish genotypes

## What it holds

- Detached-leaf inoculations of six genotypes ranked for resistance: the fewest stomata
  infected at 48 hpi in the most resistant hybrid (200); sporangia counts rose with
  sporulating area; ε-viniferin highest in hybrid 200 at 72 hpi.
