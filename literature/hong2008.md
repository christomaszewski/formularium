# Hong et al. 2008: grape disease forecast and control model, Korean plan

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Gyeonggi plan, 2008-2010: build weather-based forecast models for grape downy mildew and saenun-mounmuribyeong, then a GIS forecast and control system in year 3 (l. 58-70).
- Surveys in 2008 at the main institute, Hwaseong and Anseong: incidence by region and season, with temperature, humidity and rainfall (l. 110-114).
- Budget 450 million won over three years; grape subproject 135 million won (l. 240-242).
- Expected saving: grape sprays 8 to 4, chili 10 to 5, pear 5 to 2; grape at 350,000 won per ha per spray (l. 262-272).
- The pear saving is printed as 32.6 億 won; the arithmetic gives 28.4 億 (l. 273).

## Dependence

- none. The plan computes no model it names and gives no equation. Its forecasts are to be built from the team's own occurrence and weather data (l. 58, 64, 113), not from any engine-list formulation. The 2007 pear-rust forecast (l. 38) is cited, not reproduced. Shared authors: none with the engine list.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- For a downy mildew truth, it is a 2008 plan with no data, equations or results, and its per-spray costs (350,000 won per ha for grape, 2008) are planning figures to be checked before use as a cost source; the pear saving total is wrong.
