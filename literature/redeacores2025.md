# Azores warning network n.d.: vine downy mildew field protocol, rev. 3

## What it holds

- Azores network method for scoring grapevine downy mildew in the field, revision 3 (l. 1-10).
- Primary infection needs three conditions: shoots of 10 cm or more (l. 43), rain above 10 mm over 1 to 2 days (l. 44), mean temperature above 10 to 12 C (l. 45).
- Secondary infection needs humidity and rain, or leaf wetness above 2 hours (l. 74).
- Sampling: 2 organs on each of 50 vines, spread at random (l. 112).
- Organ score 0-3: up to 10 %, 10-25 % and above 25 % of the organ (l. 119-121).
- Plot score 0-3: foci, 25-50 % and above 50 % of plot surface (l. 126-128).
- No year printed; the scales cite DGPC 2005 and DGAV 2009 (l. 130).
