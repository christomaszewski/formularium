# Busato et al. 2022: Salvia cell-culture phytocomplex vs downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A phytocomplex from a Salvia officinalis cell line with 10.12% w/w rosmarinic acid (l. 21).
- Leaf discs sprayed with 5 g/L phytocomplex, then inoculated with 5 x 10^5 sporangia per mL (l. 23, l. 527).
- Sporulation after 7 days at 25 C in the dark, scored by image analysis (l. 25, l. 196).
- Sporulation reduced by 95% against water and by 90% when sprayed 5 days before inoculation (l. 26, l. 28).
- Rosmarinic acid alone, at the amount in the phytocomplex, gave a 77% reduction (l. 239).
- The curative test showed no effect, with data not shown (l. 416-417).
- Field degradation and persistence are untested (l. 413).

## Dependence

- none. A leaf-disc lab bioassay with no weather, infection or sporulation model computed or borrowed; the only model-like statement is the 25 C incubation, set by the protocol.
