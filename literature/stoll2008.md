# Stoll et al. 2008: thermal sensitivity of Plasmopara-infected leaves

A greenhouse research note. Thermal imaging separated Plasmopara-inoculated Riesling leaf tissue from healthy tissue 3 or 4 days after inoculation, before visible symptoms, and water stress changed the signal.

## What it holds

- Detection 3 or 4 days after inoculation, before symptoms (l. 43-44).
- Peak temperature differences of 0.89 °C in irrigated vines (day six) and 0.31 °C in non-irrigated vines (day five) (l. 103-105).
- Three runs within one growing season (l. 34-35). No detection accuracy is given.

## Dependence

- Cites Allègre et al. 2007 for leaf temperature falling under water stress (l. 60-61) and Jones et al. 2002 for thermography of water availability.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t2 (observation). Thermal imaging could flag infected tissue before symptoms, a scouting idea for a growers' engine.
- It rests on three greenhouse runs in one season, with no accuracy or field test, so it is a lead. Not read in full.
