# Akçalı & Toker Demiray 2020: reduced fluopicolide + fosetyl-Al doses

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A 2019 field trial in Tarsus, Mersin (Turkey), on 6-year-old Victoria vines, testing 2.0, 2.25 and 2.5 g/L of fluopicolide + fosetyl-Al against the registered 3.0 g/L (l. 47-49, 124).
- Four sprays from 25.04.2019; severity on 100 leaves per plot on 28.06.2019 (l. 157, 164).
- Control severity was 23.0-35.8%; efficacy was 93.6% at 3.0 g/L and 91.6% at 2.5 g/L, not different statistically (l. 236, 59).
- The authors recommend 2.5 g/L, which gives more than 90% efficacy and lower spray cost (l. 283).
