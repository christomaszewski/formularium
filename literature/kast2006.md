# Kast 2006: fifty years of mildew records at Weinsberg

## What it holds

- Disease records at Weinsberg since 1953 on one 0-5 scale (43 years for downy mildew), with
  monthly weather from 1947. Downy mildew correlated most with August weather (temperature r
  = -0.37, rain +0.40, as printed); a stepwise regression explained 75 %. No trend over the
  period; recorders changed eight times.
