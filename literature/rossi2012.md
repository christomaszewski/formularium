# Rossi & Caffi 2012: rain and the dispersal of primary inoculum

From the Università Cattolica, Piacenza.

## What it holds

- Rossi and Caffi ran three experiments in 2005-2009 (l. 66).
- Experiment 1: 20 rain periods of 0.2 to 64.2 mm (l. 142), splash samplers at three heights in a Barbera vineyard at Piacenza (l. 113). Drops per cm2 were 4.4 at 40 cm, 0.03 at 80 cm and 0.003 at 140 cm (l. 24). Splashed water correlated with maximum rain intensity, r = 0.63 (l. 225).
- Experiment 2: 61 groups of three potted vines, each exposed to one rain period on artificial leaf litter (l. 86). Germinated oospores were detected in 23 of 61 periods (l. 225). All 23 gave infection, with 1 to 24 lesions; the other 38 gave none (l. 245, 246). Infection did not depend on rain amount: 10 lesions after 0.2 mm (l. 261). Mean temperature was 18.3 C with infection and 12.7 C without (l. 366).
- Experiment 3: 120 vineyards scouted every 3-4 days (l. 134); onset is a window between two scouting dates (l. 137).
- The infection window of each vineyard is the onset minus one incubation period, computed with temperature-dependent equations from Caffi, Rossi and Carisse 2011 (l. 144). The dates are model-dated.
- 74 vineyards with one rain period in the window were kept; onset DOY 113-197 (l. 266); 25 had under 10 mm (l. 272).
- Rule drawn: any rain is a splash event when viable inoculum is on the soil (l. 311).
- Experiment 2 shares method and probably the 2008 exposures with Caffi et al. 2009.

## Dependence

- Experiments 1 and 2 are its own (splash samplers; potted vines over leaf litter). **Experiment 3's infection windows were dated by a model:** observed onset minus one incubation period from Caffi, Rossi & Carisse 2011's temperature-dependent equations (l. 144), probably Rossi's incubation (inferred; not printed here).
