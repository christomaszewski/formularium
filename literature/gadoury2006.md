# Gadoury et al. 2006: ontogenic resistance mapped against climate

## What it holds

- Regressions of relative susceptibility on days after bloom for powdery mildew (Y = 5.962 -
  3.557X, log10 scales, R² = 0.979), downy mildew (fitted to Kennelly et al.'s data;
  equation not printed) and black rot. Bloom monitored in 2002 in New York, Germany and
  Australia; mid-winter mean temperatures -4.1 °C (Geneva) to 11.8 °C (Adelaide). In warm
  climates a spread-out bloom can more than double the downy mildew susceptibility window.
