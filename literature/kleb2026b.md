# Kleb et al. 2026b: a small-scale inoculation protocol for living vines, with days to sporulation

An OENO One short communication giving a method for infecting single spots on living vines, and a table of the day sporulation first appeared.

**Read this first.**
- This is not the held `kleb2026`, the Research Square preprint (Kleb, Zia-Khan, Merkt, Krause, Zörb & Müller) on micrometeorological forecasting. It is a different paper by the same group; hence the suffix b.
- The incubation figures are from a greenhouse on potted vines under set conditions, not a field.
- The region (Germany) is inferred from the group's affiliation, not read from a location line in the facts.

## What it holds

- **Aim (l. 37):** scheduled infections "with an average incubation period of five days"; non-destructive and targeted.
- **Host (l. 69):** clonal Bacchus (Vitis vinifera), 2 to 4 year old pot vines, 4th and 5th leaves.
- **Isolate (l. 80):** field isolate 1136 of P. viticola (Gómez-Zeledón 2013), used throughout.
- **Conditions (l. 117, 119):** incubator 16 h light, 18 C, 60 % relative humidity; greenhouse light phase 22-24 C and 40-60 % relative humidity, dark phase 18 C and 85-98 % (the reader's summary of the lines).
- **First sporulation (Table 2, l. 196):** on days 4, 5, 6, 7 after inoculation on 93, 124, 40 and 9 leaves.
- **Cumulative share of leaves with sporulation (l. 200):** 35.0, 81.6, 96.6 and 100 % on days 4 to 7.
- **Context (l. 80):** cites VitiMeteo-Plasmopara (Bleyer 2011; Fouillet 2022) as forecasting systems.

## Dependence

- In vitro propagation and isolate after Gómez-Zeledón et al. 2013. No model computed. Same group as the held `kleb2026`: a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Cooptera and Agrarium, incubation:** a greenhouse distribution of days to sporulation (4 to 7 days, median near 5) at 18-24 C on Bacchus, one cultivar and one isolate. Laboratory evidence, to compare with the engine's incubation, not to replace a field value.
- **Spread of incubation:** 35 % of leaves have sporulated by day 4 and all by day 7, a width a deterministic incubation sum does not show.
