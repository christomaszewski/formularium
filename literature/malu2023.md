# Ma Lu et al. 2023: separating PAR into direct and diffuse at high latitude

The YANG2 diffuse-fraction model gets four more predictors (optical thickness, vapour pressure deficit, aerosol optical depth, albedo) to split global PAR into direct and diffuse parts.

## What it holds

- **Sites:** three ICOS Sweden stations above 58 N, Lanna, Degero and Norunda, three years of 30-minute data each (l. 28, 183-185, 202).
- **Result:** the model is calibrated, tested and validated at the three sites, beats four other models, and R2 exceeds 0.90 (l. 28-29).
- **Application:** cases for Sweden's first agrivoltaic site, with in-situ diffuse PAR from April to December 2022, and fallback cases when diffuse PAR is not measured (l. 32-37, 255).
- **Not in the first 150 lines:** coefficients and error tables.

## Dependence

- Builds on the YANG2 separation model (l. 24-25); the other four models compared are named in the text.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strand s3 (light under a canopy or panel): a simulator needing diffuse PAR could use the predictors; fitted only at three Swedish stations.
