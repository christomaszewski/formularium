# Eisenmann et al. 2023: resistant cultivars at Neustadt, 2019-2021

## What it holds

- **Trial:** DLR Rheinpfalz, Neustadt an der Weinstraße; five resistant and four
  susceptible cultivars, organic sprays, with unsprayed variants; weekly EPPO scoring.
- **Downy mildew:** none in 2019 or 2020. In 2021, unsprayed grapes: susceptible cultivars
  100 % incidence and 77 % severity, resistant ones 55 % and 15 %; oil spots 5-8 days after
  a mid-June primary infection.
- **Powdery mildew, unsprayed grapes:** 2019 susceptible 50.1 % and 9.3 %; 2020 and 2021
  in a figure only.
- **Weather:** a station within 1 km (Agrarmeteorologie Rheinland-Pfalz); in figures only.
- An infection rule (RH above 95 %, 13-19 °C, leaf wetness above 90 %) is cited to
  references including Blaeser & Weltzien 1979; it is not their rule.
