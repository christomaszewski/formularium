# Pezzotti et al. 2020: silicon nitride bioceramic against P. viticola

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Silicon nitride (a bioceramic) tested against P. viticola on grapevine leaf disks of two cultivars, Cabernet Sauvignon and Cannonau (l. 84-95, 229-241).
- Its suspension pH rose from about 7.5 to about 8.3 within seconds and settled at 6.3 to 6.7 (l. 155-157).
- Sporangia in the suspension lost viability over 3 h compared with water (l. 223-227).
- Leaf disks were incubated at 21 to 24 C for 6 days (l. 114-116).
- Cabernet Sauvignon controls all sporulated at 5 days; pre-treatment gave complete protection in some disks (l. 240-241).
- The authors call it a candidate biopesticide and call for field trials (l. 743-745).
