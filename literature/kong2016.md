# Kong et al. 2016: a LAMP test that finds Plasmopara viticola before symptoms

A loop-mediated isothermal amplification assay for P. viticola, tested for specificity,
sensitivity, on inoculated and field leaves and on spore-trap samples.

## What it holds

- LAMP for Plasmopara viticola on the ITS sequence; DNA from P. viticola alone among 38
  fungal and oomycete species gave a product (l. 17).
- Detection limit 33 fg of genomic DNA per reaction (l. 144); PCR weak at 3.3 pg (l. 145);
  LAMP at least 100-fold more sensitive (l. 146).
- Artificially inoculated leaves: LAMP positive at 4 dpi, PCR at 6 dpi (l. 147, 148);
  visible symptoms at 7 dpi (l. 419).
- Time: LAMP reaction 30 min, DNA extraction 15 min, total about one hour (l. 19, 307, 308).
- Field leaves from China: 150 samples; of 72 without symptoms, LAMP 47 (65.2%) and PCR 16
  (22.2%) positive (l. 155, 156).
- Airborne sporangia, 40 days in Changli, Hebei (l. 441): under 20 sporangia, 1 of 14
  positive; 20 to 100, 8 of 12; over 100, all positive (l. 214, 215).
- Primers from GenBank DQ665668.1 (l. 67). No fixed threshold, degree-days or model input.
- Gaps: artificial-inoculation counts are three leaves a day (l. 418); Table S1 not in the
  file.

## Dependence

- Its own assays; no model. Primers from GenBank DQ665668.1 (l. 67).
