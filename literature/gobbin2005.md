# Gobbin et al. 2005: how much of an epidemic is secondary spread

The primary source for the epidemic-structure numbers Agrarium's TRUTH-METHOD quotes through
[rossi2013](rossi2013.md) and [gobbin2006](gobbin2006.md). Eighteen central European plots,
2000-2002.

## What it holds

- **Sampling:**
  - 4,685 single lesions from 18 plots, 2 to 22 dates each, from first symptoms until the
    mosaic stage (survey windows 10 May-27 August).
  - **Total sampling** (every oil spot) where vines averaged under four; **partial** (1-3
    per vine) above that.
  - Lesion coordinates by row and vine. Four microsatellites.
- **Genotypes:**
  - More than 2,300 site-specific genotypes. About 70 % were sampled once and 14 % twice;
    only 7 (0.3 %) more than 50 times.
  - **Single genotypes per plot:** from 48 % (Vic) to 90 % (Nac) of genotypes. They caused
    37 % (SD 19 %) of disease incidence under partial sampling and 31 % (SD 25 %) under
    total.
  - **Late in the season:** even on the last dates, over 40 % of lesions were new single
    genotypes in seven plots.
  - **Twice-found genotypes:** 15 and 13 % of genotypes, causing 13.7 and 11.0 % of
    incidence.
  - Under total sampling, 1.4 % of genotypes were found more than 10 times and 0.7 % more
    than 20 times.
- **Five dispersal patterns of the successful genotypes:**
  1. clonal multiplication within 1 m of the putative primary lesion (six plots);
  2. that, followed by plot-scale dispersion (several);
  3. multicluster plot-scale dispersion without a near-source phase (two);
  4. random plot-scale dispersion (one);
  5. minor multiplication and dispersal.
  - The successful genotypes were mostly clustered (variance-to-mean ratio of lesions per
    vine).
- **Spread rates:**
  - Migration under 20 m per secondary cycle; one case study averaged 4.97 m.
  - Plot colonization about 1-2 m² a day (case studies: about 0.8, 1.3 and 2).
  - The authors attribute the slow spread to discontinuous hosts and to weather.

## Dependence

- No formulation. Raynal (ITV Aquitaine, later IFV) also co-wrote Chen's chapters and
  [poeydebat2025](poeydebat2025.md): a flag, not a shared model. Gessler and Jermini share

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- **History-matching numbers at the source,** under a sampling the truth can mimic: every
  oil spot under four per vine, else 1-3, by date.
  - 70 % of genotypes found once and 14 % twice.
  - One or two dominant clones.
  - Single genotypes giving about a third of incidence, and over 40 % of lesions late in
    the season.
  - Spread under 20 m per cycle, and plot colonization of 1-2 m² a day.
- **Comparing them needs lesion-level origin in the truth** (each primary infection its own
  genotype). The truth keeps only each vine's first focus.
