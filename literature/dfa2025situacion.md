# Diputación Foral de Álava 2025: the 23 June 2025 vineyard situation report for Rioja Alavesa

The first report of the 2025 campaign series, with a short account of the wet spring and of how waterlogged plots limited mechanised spraying.

**Read this first.** Grey literature: a regional extension report from technicians' observation. Only the first two of four pages were read. The report is about an exceptional year. It is not consistent with itself on when mildew first appeared ("the second week of May", l. 70; "the start of May", l. 91), and it disagrees with the 8 July report (`dfa2025mildiu`) on the count: "up to 4 and 5 passes" by 23 June (l. 99) against 4 by early June and 8 by the end of June there.

## What it holds

- **Second week of May 2025:** downy mildew, already suspected, appeared; treatments were made "as far as possible" because plots waterlogged by abundant rain made mechanised spraying difficult (l. 73).
- **Why control was hard:** first, conditions limited mechanised access to plots for preventive treatments; second, the continuing storms (l. 94).
- **Passes by 23 June:** in some cases already up to 4 and 5 passes, combining systemic, contact and organic products (l. 99).
- **June, first half:** high temperatures and stormy rain kept encouraging fungal disease (l. 75-77, from the text; not among the reading agent's quotes).

## Dependence

- None recorded. The same service wrote `dfa2025mildiu`; the two are one source for the 2025 wet-spring account, not two.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Delay limits (grower.delay_limits):** confirms the trafficability limit reported on 8 July: waterlogged plots limited mechanised preventive spraying in May and June 2025 (l. 73, 94). No duration, no soil-type split. Kind: observation by an extension service.
- **Sprays per season (spray.per_season):** up to 4 and 5 passes by 23 June, in some cases (l. 99); a lower figure than the 8 passes by the end of June in the 8 July report, and a reminder that the wet-year count is a range.
- **Not shown:** wind, labour, sprayer capacity, warning-to-spray delay, compliance, Catalonia.
