# Bordons & Llauradó 2013: abstracts of the first Catalan viticulture research day

The abstract book of a 2013 meeting at Tarragona. Searched for leaf area in Catalan
vineyards.

## What it holds

- **V5** (Priorat, Cariñena; l. 440-446): total leaf area per vine measured at harvest; the
  value is not printed.
- **V8** (Penedès Chardonnay, seven plots, 2011; l. 563-569): fIPAR as the vigour index,
  values not printed; NDVI did not follow fIPAR; predawn water potential -0.42 to -0.17 MPa.
- **V2** (l. 328): DOSAVIÑA, no numbers.
- **No abstract** prints leaf area, LAI, canopy size or prunings for Xarel·lo, Macabeu or
  Parellada.
