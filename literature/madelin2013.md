# Madelin & Beltrando 2013: minimum temperatures in Champagne on one frost night

A MesoNH simulation of the night of 16-17 March 2003 is set against a station network; the
  model gets the wind flow right but is too warm at the coldest sites.

## What it holds

- **Observations:** 23 weather stations in the Marne vineyard (l. 181); the night follows
  Sunday 16 March 2003 under a calm anticyclone (l. 136, 156).
- **Model:** MesoNH at 1 km resolution (l. 236); errors at single stations include -3.0 C at
  Fleury-la-Rivière and -2.2 C at Bouzy for the warm ones, 2.7 C at Chambrecy and 2.2 C at
  Germaine for the cold ones (l. 237-241).
- **Physics:** cold air gathers where wind is weak, in valley bottoms; windward slopes are
  colder than leeward ones (l. 233-236, abstract l. 31-36).
