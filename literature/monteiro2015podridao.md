# Monteiro et al. 2015: grey mould risk in three Brazilian regions (congress paper)

The Botrytis half of [monteiro2015bol](monteiro2015bol.md).

## What it holds

- Broome et al. 1995's logit printed on p. 1976 as -2,647866 - 0.374927W + 0.061601WT -
  0.001511WT²: the engine's coefficients exactly. Ten years at Bento Gonçalves, Jales and
  Petrolina; no validation.
- Wetness gaps filled by Sentelhas et al. 2006's method, not the engine's 2008 one.
- **Printed slips:** the abstract's risk classes disagree with the methods; "Broome et al.,
  2005" is cited and not listed.
