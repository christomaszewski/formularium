# Marone Fassolo et al. 2022: 72 isolates on susceptible and Rpv3-1 / Rpv29 hosts

## What it holds

- **Isolates:** 72 P. viticola isolates from 14 northern Italian vineyards (2019), on Pinot
  noir (susceptible), Bianca (Rpv3-1) and Mgaloblishvili (Rpv29).
- **Assay:** 1.5 cm discs from the 3rd-5th leaf below the apex; one 10 µL droplet of 5 x
  10^4 sporangia/mL; 22 °C, 16 h light; scored at 5, 7, 9 and 13 days.
- **Latent period:** 128 ± 31 (Pinot noir), 131 ± 32 (Mgaloblishvili) and 142 ± 45
  degree-days (Bianca), no difference (p = 0.24). Degree-days are days x 22 °C, so 5.8-6.5
  days; the scoring days were 5, 7, 9 and 13, and how a day between them was got is not
  said.
- **Infection frequency, all isolates, at 5 / 7 / 9 / 13 days:** Pinot noir 41 / 74 / 77 /
  84 %; Mgaloblishvili 20 / 33 / 34 / 46 %; Bianca 14 / 25 / 28 / 31 %.
- **Sporulating area:** Pinot noir 1 / 6 / 18 / 39 %; Mgaloblishvili 0.4 / 2 / 4 / 13 %;
  Bianca 0.1-0.7 %. A three-parameter log-logistic on log(PSA + 1) gives inflections at 7.4
  (Pinot noir) and 9.0 days (Mgaloblishvili); Bianca's fit is not significant.
- **Adaptation:** 33 isolates sporulate on all three hosts; one (CAST7) is adapted to both
  loci.
- **Oospores** of three crosses: 4.6-8.2 per cm², 67-93 % viable.
- **Printed slips:** Table 2's back-transform uses e^d + 1 for e^d - 1; the Discussion's
  40 % without adaptation matches no count in the Results (58 of 72).

## Dependence

- None. Bove & Rossi 2020 ([bove2020resistance](bove2020resistance.md)) is cited for the
  infection-frequency definition.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A latent period on a susceptible host at constant 22 °C, about 6 days, and the spread
  between isolates (± 31 degree-days): a check for a truth's incubation at one temperature.
