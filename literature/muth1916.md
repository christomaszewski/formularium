# Muth 1916: infection through the lower leaf surface

## What it holds

- Two copies were dropped. Argues that infection enters mainly through the stomata of the
  lower leaf surface, so sprays must cover it, and that spray timing relative to the weather
  decides protection; cites the Rheinhessen outbreaks of 1904 and 1906.
