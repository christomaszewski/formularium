# BUCIUMEANU et al. 2019: climate and phenology at Stefanesti

Research article, Stefanesti viticultural centre, Arges, southern Romania.

## What it holds

- A multi-factorial ANOVA/MANOVA of climate data from 1989 to 2017 (phenology 1997 to 2017) for eight grape varieties relates flowering, veraison and ripening to temperature, Huglin index and precipitation.
- It reports which factors matter (annual mean and maximum temperature, rainfall, days above 10 mm) and gives no usable predictive equation in the lines read.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- ANOVA of factors only; nothing to compute.
