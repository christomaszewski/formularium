# Ticó Maluquer 2013: fifty years of Penedès downy mildew warnings

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- 1960 downy mildew attack in the Penedès: 650,000 hl of wine lost (l. 27).
- Warning service from May 1961 (l. 33, 478): volunteer weather readings twice a day (l. 477); daily radio warnings (l. 468).
- Daily percentage growth of incubation computed from the Bologna study and checked on 1960 weather (l. 425-437).
- Reconstructed key spray dates in 1960: 24 June at Vilafranca, 25 July at Sant Jaume Sesoliveres (l. 436).
- Warnings would save two or three treatments a year, about EUR 3.5 million in Catalonia (l. 518-520).

## Dependence

- Computes a Goidanich-type incubation step (flag, probably the Goidanich 1957 table: the paper cites 'the University of Bologna study' at l. 425-427 and not by name; the identification is from memory of the Bologna incubation work, not from the text). The 1961 service computed the days of incubation at a given temperature and humidity and added a daily percentage growth of the incubation period (l. 425-430): the same form as the inverse-sum back-calculation of the incubation tables. It was checked against 1960 weather (l. 431-437), so its calibration used observed treatment dates. The French first-oil-spot method is named (l. 420-421) and is not described, so no 3-10 rule is stated. Pavia (l. 422-423) is named without an author. No shared authors flagged.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A historic warning service whose daily incubation step (Bologna study, probably Goidanich 1957) is an engine-list form, so the truth must not reuse it; its 1960 check against weather is the only season it verifies (l. 431-437).
