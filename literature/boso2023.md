# Boso et al. 2023: leaf-disc downy mildew susceptibility of Spanish minority varieties, seven from INCAVI

A laboratory leaf-disc ranking of Spanish minority vine varieties against Plasmopara viticola, in which seven genotypes come from the Catalan institute INCAVI at Vilafranca del Penedès.

**Read this first.**
- It is a laboratory test on detached leaf discs, not a field result. None of Xarel·lo, Macabeu or Parellada, the main Penedès whites, was tested.
- The count differs inside the paper: the abstract says 63 minority varieties (l. 58); the reading agent counted 64 to 65 genotypes, and the figure legend numbers up to 65 (l. 586-587). The "87 % highly susceptible" (l. 61) is the abstract's share. Sanguina appears twice with different origins (IVICAM in the abstract; INCAVI in Table 1, l. 352), and Albana appears under INCAVI in Table 1 (l. 312) and under DGA in a figure legend (l. 501): origins as printed.
- Table 1 columns are mean, SD and CV for incidence, severity and density; the letters are LSD groups. Incidence is a poor discriminator (l. 387).

## What it holds

- **Test (l. 255, 261):** leaf discs inoculated with 50 uL of sporangia suspension at 50 000 per mL, kept 6 days at 25 °C and 95 % RH; scored on bands (50 to 75 % high, 75 to 100 % very high); two inoculation rounds, June and July, over 2020 to 2021 (l. 58).
- **Overall (l. 61):** some 87 % of the varieties were highly susceptible, 11 % moderately and 2 % low (l. 58 to 61).
- **Catalan genotypes from INCAVI, incidence % / severity %** (Table 1; severity and density in the same rows):
  - Riera 2: 100.0 / 56.2 (l. 301); density 62.5.
  - Albana: 95.0 / 51.5 (l. 312); density 62.5.
  - Trobat Negre: 90.8 / 68.7 (l. 336); density 75.0.
  - Riera 46: 85.7 / 32.5 (l. 346); density 62.5.
  - Sanguina: 83.0 / 43.7 (l. 352); density 56.2.
  - Riera 43: 76.0 / 24.4 (l. 361); density 50.0.
  - Cariñena Roja: 62.0 / 26.2 (l. 367); density 32.5, the lowest incidence of the Catalan set.
- **Method choice (l. 387):** the authors say sporulation incidence discriminated poorly between varieties, which makes severity and density the better columns.

## Dependence

- None borrowed as a model. The leaf-disc method follows Rumbolz and Staudt, as cited. Sharing a field of study with Boso et al. 2014 (Crop Prot. 63, not obtained) is only a flag.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Agrarium fields.susceptibility_sd (context only):** it gives a laboratory spread between Catalan minority genotypes, incidence 62.0 to 100 % and severity 24.4 to 68.7 %. This is the spread of cultivars, not of Penedès vines of one variety, so it is not a value for the between-field SD.
- **Penedès hosts:** nothing on Xarel·lo, Macabeu or Parellada; their ranking against these genotypes cannot be set from this paper.
- **Kind of evidence:** one laboratory leaf-disc experiment with its own scale; field susceptibility may rank differently.
