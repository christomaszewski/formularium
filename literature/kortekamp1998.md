# Kortekamp et al. 1998: infection of resistant and susceptible cultivars

## What it holds

- Scanning microscopy and peroxidase staining through the infection cycle in Orion and
  Phoenix (resistant) and Kerner and Riesling (susceptible): infection starts in both, but
  stops 3-4 days after infection in the resistant ones, whose tissue turns necrotic;
  peroxidase rises earlier there.
