# Chauvin et al. 2025: model ensembles for sugar beet virus yellows

Research article, France (Hauts-de-France, Champagne, Normandy, Center, Ile-de-France, Limagne).

## What it holds

- Machine-learning model ensembles identify risk factors and predict virus yellows severity in sugar beet from 3,361 scouting observations (2019-2023).
- Spatial aggregation of models predicted more robustly than the best single model; sugar beet, not vine, and no equations in the lines read.
