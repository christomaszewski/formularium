# Bendek et al. 2007: risk-index timing and conidial germination for grape powdery mildew in Chile

Tests the Californian Risk Assessment Index for powdery-mildew sprays in Chile. It also measures conidial germination and infection against temperature, humidity and leaf wetness.

## What it holds

- **Germination, in vitro (abstract, l. 21-23):** best at 25 °C, none at 5 °C or 35 °C. At 20 °C it rises between 47 and 90 % RH and falls above that. Isolate PUC2 reached 90.5 % at 25 °C (l. 216).
- **Incubation (l. 25):** 13 to 14 days at 20 to 23 °C, 19 to 24 days at 10 °C, more than 23 days at 6 °C.
- **Wetness (l. 25-26):** wetting inoculated leaves 1 or 72 h after inoculation markedly reduced infection.
- **Index (l. 147-153):** starts at 30 points once 20 to 30 °C holds for six hours. Each further qualifying day adds 20 points, and points come off on other days.
- **Field (l. 173-176, 559-565):** sprays start at RAI 30 to 50, and repeat above 50 or every 18 to 20 days while RAI is above 60. On Cabernet Sauvignon at 87 % leaf and 99 % cluster incidence in 2002 to 2003, the index cut sprays from five (farmer programme) to three.

## Dependence

The index is borrowed. It was developed in California (Gubler et al. 1999; Jarvis et al. 2002) and is tested here, not re-derived (l. 91-93). The 30-point start follows Gubler et al. (1999) (l. 147).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t3 (powdery mildew).** The germination response (optimum 25 °C, 47 to 90 % RH) and the RAI rule could feed a spray-timing term. The 90.5 % figure is one isolate, in vitro.
- **Not read in full.** The RAI is a borrowed rule with a Chilean field check, not a new fit. A simulator should take the rule as written and the germination curve only as a laboratory shape.
