# Carisse et al. 2024: anthracnose incubation by temperature and leaf age

## What it holds

- **Chamber:** Vidal leaves 1-8 days old at 5-30 °C; days to first lesion 27.5, 15.1, 9.7,
  5.9, 3.7 and 2.3 at 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 °C.
- **Incubation:** INC = X0 e^(-0.1028 T), X0 = 4.8464 + 9.158 x leaf age (days); R² 0.93,
  RMSE 2.98 days.
- **Vineyard** (Frelighsburg, 2016-2018, 39 inoculations): relative lesion development
  1/(1 + e^(0.0548(58.74 - DD))) and relative sporulation 1/(1 + e^(0.03185(172.2 - DD))),
  degree-days above 0 °C from inoculation.

- None.
