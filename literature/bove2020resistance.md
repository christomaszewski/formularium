# Bove & Rossi 2020: six components of partial resistance in 16 varieties

Not [bove2020](bove2020.md) (Bove, Savary, Willocquet & Rossi, a simulation model): no value
in that note comes from this paper.

## What it holds

- **Material:** leaves of 15 partially resistant varieties and Merlot from three unsprayed
  vineyards (Ferrara di Monte Baldo and two at Piacenza; one printed at "35°01'N", a
  misprint for 45°), 2014-2016, at growth stages 18, 65 and 79.
- **Assay:** leaf discs, pooled field inoculum at 5 x 10^5 sporangia/mL, 20 °C, 12 h light;
  75 discs per variety and experiment.
- **Components (Table 1):** infection frequency at 11 days, its area under the progress
  curve (4-11 days), latent period LP50 (degree-days above 0 °C to half the sites
  sporulating, interpolated between days), lesion size, sporangia per lesion, infectious
  period (sporulation events) and infectivity.
- **Merlot:** IFR 0.95, LP50 94.8 degree-days (4.7 days at 20 °C), lesions 21.1 mm²,
  7,257 sporangia per lesion, 3.3 sporulation events, infectivity 0.77.
- **Range across varieties:** LP50 90.5 (Rkatsiteli) to 113.8 degree-days (Johanniter);
  IFR 0.54 (Bronner, Johanniter) to 0.97; sporangia per lesion 43 to 7,257.
- **Leaf stage:** LP50 91.1 degree-days at stage 18 against 105.8 and 106.6 at 65 and 79;
  IFR 0.85 against 0.81 and 0.82.

## Dependence

- None. Kennelly et al. 2007 and Caffi et al. 2013 are cited for the definition of the
  infectious period only. Rossi is an author of engine models: a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Measured latent periods at constant 20 °C on 16 varieties and three leaf stages, and
  sporangia per lesion: data for a truth's incubation at one temperature, its leaf-age
  effect, and its sporulation per lesion.
