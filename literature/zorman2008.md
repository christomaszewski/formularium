# Zorman et al. 2008: downy and powdery mildew incidence on Terceira, 2006-2007

Open access (RCAAP), fetched 2026-10-12. A visual survey in the Biscoitos wine region
of Terceira (the Azores extension sheets `redeacores2024` and `redeacores2025` are the
same region's later practice). No weather values and no model are printed.

## What it holds

- Seven vineyards in 2006 and five in 2007, visited every two weeks from April to
  October; incidence as the share of plants with symptoms from 10 leaves of at least 20
  plants a visit, 10 % of plants observed; V1 an untreated control (l. 24-30, 88-99).
- 2006 "main values": downy mildew 48 %, powdery mildew 30 %, against 44 % and 26 % in
  1997 (Barcelos 1997; l. 84-88); first infections at the start of May near the five-leaf
  stage, budding in the first week of April, peaks of both diseases between 10 and 20
  July (l. 120-135); V6 with fourteen treatments ended at 0 % downy and 6 % powdery
  mildew (l. 129-130); 8-10 treatments a year at about 620 EUR/ha (l. 127-128); regional
  losses of 10-20 %, to 100 % if uncontrolled (l. 80-82).
- A GIS dispersion-risk map (ArcView) with no equation; parcels said to depend linearly
  on slope, altitude and distance to the sea, no coefficient (l. 121-122, 153-160).
- EPI-Míldio appears only in the reference list (Barcelos 2001, a thesis on EPI in
  Biscoitos), not in the body (l. 184-186).

## Dependence

- none computed; the counts are the authors' observations, undated beyond the fortnight.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- A qualitative Azorean season record (first infections in early May, peak mid-July,
  about half the plants symptomatic in 2006), of use only as a coarse check for the
  Azores sheets' rules; the Barcelos 2001 thesis would hold the EPI run.
