# Lecomte et al. 2012: esca in France, slides on incidence and probable causes

A conference deck: national incidence of esca and eutypiosis and hypotheses for its rise, among them warming and drought.

**Read this first.** A trunk disease, not downy mildew; the deck has no model. Its one link to Agrarium's side is a Bordeaux temperature-sum chart credited to Pieri (personal communication).

## What it holds

- **Observatory (l. 118, 315-319):** national survey 2003-2008, 712 vineyards, 27 cultivars, two counts a year of 300 vines per plot; vines affected in their production potential about 11 % on average (5 % in 2003); plot variability 2 to 41 % (Gironde, Cabernet-Sauvignon). Mean foliar esca symptoms run from 1.04 % to 3.29 % over 2003-2008 in the chart (the bar-to-year mapping was not verified).
- **Warming (l. 375, 429-431):** chart of the sum of mean temperatures above 10 C from 1 January to 30 September at Bordeaux, 1961-2011 (axis values garbled); long hot dry periods 2003, 2005, 2006, 2009; drought may let latent wood fungi progress.

## Dependence

- Observatory data are Grosman and Doublet (Phytoma 2012) and BNIC Charentes counts, courtesy slides. Held: nothing on esca.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- None for the disease truth or engine. Could serve a later trunk-disease thread; not a model or a dataset.
