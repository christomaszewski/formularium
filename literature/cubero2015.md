# Cubero et al. 2015: bunch compactness by image analysis

An image-analysis method that predicts the OIV bunch-compactness rating from segmented photographs. It classified 85.3% of ratings correctly.

## What it holds

- 90 bunches of nine red cultivars, handpicked in October 2011 at the Agoncillo experimental vineyard, La Rioja (l. 29, 106-108).
- PLS model: 85.3% of ratings predicted correctly (l. 32-33; l. 374).
- Reference ratings: the average of 14 trained-expert scores per bunch (l. 150-152).
- Most discriminant features: visibility of berries, rachis and holes, and bunch roundness and shape (l. 33-35).

## Dependence

- OIV descriptor 204 as the reference rating (l. 148).
- Tello and Ibáñez 2014 indices, for comparison (l. 51, 80). PLS after Geladi and Kowalski 1986 (l. 282).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (bunch rot input), since the abstract ties tight bunches to fungal disease. A camera measure of compactness could feed a sorting or bunch-rot risk term.
- It is a measurement method, not a disease model, and its coefficients come from one 2011 sample. Not read in full.
