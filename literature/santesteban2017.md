# Santesteban et al. 2017: severe trimming and laterals in Navarra Tempranillo

Trimming shoots to 55-65 cm three weeks after fruit set, with extra water to push
laterals, to delay ripening; 2014-2015. Read for leaf area; it prints shoot lengths only.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 198-238): Tempranillo on 110 Richter, 17 years old, Traibuenas (Navarra),
  350 m; 3 × 1 m; VSP bilateral cordon, three two-bud spurs an arm (about 12 shoots, my
  reading of the pruning); control irrigation 36 L a vine a week.
- **Shoots** (Table 1, l. 106-109, 2015 only): main shoots 83.4 cm (control) against 56.7
  to 61.9 cm trimmed; 1.20 to 1.57 laterals a shoot; lateral length 12.6 cm a shoot
  (control) to 49.7 cm (trimmed with the most water).
- **Effect:** mid-veraison 4 days later in both years (l. 281, 284); 23.5 °Brix 9 to 10
  days later (l. 315). The abstract says trimming reduced leaf area (l. 30); no value is
  printed.
- **Not printed:** leaf area, LAI, dates of trimming or veraison.
