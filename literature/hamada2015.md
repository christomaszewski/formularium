# Hamada et al. 2015: climate change and grape powdery mildew in Brazil

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A GIS favourability study for powdery mildew (Uncinula necator) in Brazil, 1961-1990 reference and 2011-2100 under IPCC AR4 scenarios B1 and A2 (l. 29-31).
- Favourable class: RH 40 to 70 per cent and mean temperature 21 to 32 C; unfavourable outside these bands (l. 292-293, 246).
- Brazil favourable area: 16 per cent in 1961-1990, 20 per cent in 2011-2040 under A2 (l. 271, 280).
- Northeast 43 per cent favourable in 1961-1990, South 8 per cent, Southeast 15 per cent (l. 322-323, Table 1).
- Increases are larger under A2 than under B1 (l. 308).
- Cited: Caffarra et al. (2012) found lower severity in warmer future years; Pugliese et al. (2010) found no CO2 effect in controlled conditions (l. 171, 177).

## Dependence

- none. The paper uses band rules on relative humidity (40 to 70 per cent) and mean temperature (21 to 32 C) to classify favourability. That shares a form (a threshold classification on RH and temperature) with the leaf-wetness RH-threshold family, but the numbers, the disease and the purpose differ; no equation, code, fit or data is shared with any engine model (powdery mildew is not an engine model). Shared authors with the list: none noted.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)
