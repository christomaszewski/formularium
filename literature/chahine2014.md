# Chahine et al. 2014: across the rows a vineyard flows like a uniform canopy; along the rows the wind is channelled

A large-eddy simulation with field measurements of neutral flow over a vineyard, from cross-row to down-row wind; only the abstract was read.

**Read this first.** Abstract only. It prints no numbers, so there is no ratio or length to take from it. The field site is not named in the abstract.

## What it holds

- The flow was studied in neutral stratification by large-eddy simulation and in situ measurements, over wind directions from cross-row to down-row and a typical range of row aspect ratio (row separation over height) (l. 41).
- The mean flow is similar to that in uniform canopies, especially for cross-row to diagonal wind (l. 41).
- For down-row wind the mean flow varies noticeably across each row-gap pattern, because the wind is channelled in the inter-row; this variability grows with the aspect ratio (l. 41).
- The displacement height and roughness length are both smaller in down-row wind, for which the canopy appears more open. No values are given in the abstract (l. 41).

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **dm.row_barrier (2, range 1-4):** gives the direction of the effect: down-row wind meets a more open canopy and travels along the inter-row, cross-row wind meets a canopy that acts like a continuous one. It does not give a ratio. It is wind, not sporangia; an aerodynamic ratio is not a dispersal ratio.
- **Canopy wind (SWEB Eq 15):** for cross-row and diagonal wind a uniform-canopy wind formula is defensible (with `bailey2013` on effective leaf area); for down-row wind it is not.
