# Fernández González 2011: airborne sporangia and phenology in a Galician vineyard

## What it holds

- A thesis (supervisors Rodríguez-Rajo and Aira Rodríguez) on cv. Treixadura at Cenlle,
  Ribeiro (Ourense), 2007-2010: phenology, chilling and heat sums, pollen and airborne
  spores by volumetric trap.
- **P. viticola sporangia (Table 58):** seasonal totals 747 (2007), 932 (2008), 362 (2009),
  1548 (2010); peaks 32 (25 August 2007), 107 (20 September 2008), 21 (4 August 2009), 157
  (22 March 2010, as printed).
- **Weather:** Spearman r = 0.280 with the same day's dew point (2007-2010). Regression:
  spores(t) = -0.855 + 0.455·spores(t-1) + 0.305·Tdew, R² 0.227; ARIMA(1,0,1), R² 0.194;
  checked against 2006 data, the regression failed and the ARIMA passed.
- **Phenology:** chilling 730 units at 2 °C (Aron) or 818 at 0 °C; heat sums 834 growing
  degree-days to budburst (Zalom), 4614 to stage 89 (Galán method).
- Goidanich 1959 and Rosa et al. 1995 are cited only in the discussion; no infection or
  incubation model is computed.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Four seasons of daily airborne sporangia in a humid Atlantic vineyard: a pattern for a
  truth's sporangia cloud (dispersal, seasonal shape), with an observation method
