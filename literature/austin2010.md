# Austin & Wilcox 2010: sunlight and powdery mildew

## What it holds

- Vineyard: removing UV raised powdery mildew on fruit (2-5 times in the text); fruit in
  full sun had least.
- Chambers at 20-30 °C with UV-B: at 30 °C the latent period rose from about 7 to 15 days;
  hyphal growth fell linearly with dose (R² 0.94-0.99, slopes not printed). The printed
  doses do not follow from 3.0 W/m² for 6 h.
- Early leaf removal cut fruit powdery mildew by 35 %.

- None.
