# Ding et al. 2019: aerial Alternaria conidia above potato fields

Rotorod samplers at four heights above a potato canopy in two Wisconsin fields, giving height profiles and escape fractions of Alternaria solani and A. alternata. The spores are not oomycete.

## What it holds

- **Sampling:** Rotorods on a 10 m tower at 0, 1, 2 and 3 m above the canopy, hourly rods, 10:00 to 15:00 (l. 89-95); two fields, 6 days each in 2016 and 2017 (l. 102-105); rods spun at 2,600 per min (l. 85-86); canopy 0.3 m (l. 185).
- **Result:** A. solani concentration almost always fell with height, A. alternata on 4 of 12 days; A. alternata concentrations were higher than those of A. solani at both field edges (P < 0.0001) (l. 20-27, 289).
- **Efficiency:** the Rotorod collection efficiency was "calculated according to Edmonds (1972)" (l. 87-88); no value is printed in the passages read.
- **Wind:** a log wind profile from the 10 m anemometer gives wind at each rod height (Eq. 1, l. 169-178).

## Dependence

- Edmonds 1972 for collection efficiency; Neufeld et al. 2013 for escape fraction (l. 174-176).
