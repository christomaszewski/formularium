# González-Flor et al. 2019: rainfed Xarel·lo in Alt Penedès and Anoia, water status by reflectance

Reflectance indices against predawn water potential in five rainfed Xarel·lo vineyards,
2009-2010, and in potted vines. Read for canopy data on one of Agrarium's varieties.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 151-166): five rainfed Xarel·lo vineyards, Alt Penedès and Anoia, planted
  1989-1998, 2,016 to 3,086 vines/ha, double Royat; three plots a vineyard chosen for
  contrasting vigour; mean rain 479.2 mm, about 15 °C (station Els Hostalets de Pierola);
  veraison 31 July to 4 August 2009 and 10 to 18 August 2010.
- **Exposed leaf area** (l. 180-186, page image): ELA = (2h + e) × (100 − T) × d, after Smart
  & Robinson (h height, e width, T % gaps, d vine spacing; units and the /100 not printed);
  fIPAR = 41.37 + 12.14 ELA, r² 0.86 (2010, eight blocks). Neither ELA nor fIPAR values are
  printed.
- **Water:** fIPAR correlated with predawn water potential, r 0.64 (l. 1621).
- **Not printed:** LAI, leaf area, canopy height and width, spacing, shoots.

## Dependence

- ELA procedure of Smart & Robinson 1991; indices of Peñuelas, Gamon and others; the fIPAR
  line is the authors' own fit. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Xarel·lo, no leaf area printed.** If ELA is in m² a vine and fIPAR in %, the fIPAR of 66 to
  70 % the same group measured in rainfed Penedès Chardonnay (`gonzalezflor2014`) maps to an
  ELA of about 2.0 to 2.4 m² a vine (my calculation across two papers, an inference only).
