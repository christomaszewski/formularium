# Dhar et al. 2020: spore-trap qPCR for lettuce downy mildew advisories

Impaction spore traps and a species-specific qPCR for Bremia lactucae in three commercial lettuce fields, with a spore-load threshold used to time sprays. The record gave 2019; the header prints Plant Disease 2020, 104:82-93 (accepted July 2019). The disease is lettuce downy mildew, not Plasmopara, so diseases is empty.

## What it holds

- **Sampling:** two impaction traps with arms about 53 cm above soil, collected twice a week for 8 to 10 weeks (l. 93-95); three fields near Salinas in 2016 (EXP1 to EXP3, l. 131-133).
- **Air volume and threshold:** 132.89 m3 per 4,320 min sample from rod area, 2,400 rpm and arm diameter 0.085 m (l. 193-198); Cq 24, equal to 1,136 spores, is 8.548 sporangia per m3 (l. 198-199). The volume formula is geometric; no catch efficiency of the trap is given.
- **Result:** the abstract says three sprays saved in EXP1 and one each in EXP2 and EXP3 (l. 24-27); the results text says one saved in EXP1 and EXP2 and three in EXP3 (l. 249-253). The two disagree. Wind and temperature rose before the lowest Cq values (l. 246-251); the wind-speed correlation with Cq was r = 0.25 in EXP1 (l. 260).

## Dependence

- The qPCR assay and traps of Kunjeti et al. 2016; weather from Fox Weather (MtnRT) and CIMIS station 214 (l. 188-193).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s1 (autumn inoculum), gap d_spore_sampler_efficiency_oomycete:** an oomycete trap study that converts counts to sporangia per m3 by geometry only, so it does not answer sampler efficiency; it gives a field threshold of 8.548 sporangia per m3 for an advisory.
