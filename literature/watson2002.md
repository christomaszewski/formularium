# Watson, Zehr & Grimes 2002: Monilinia inoculum from peach twig cankers

From Clemson University. Peach; data only.

## What it holds

- Own lab assay of overwintered peach twig cankers of Monilinia fructicola from South Carolina orchards: 1991 (Junegold) and 1992 (Harvester) (l. 14) (l. 19) (l. 23).
- Twigs misted and held at 5, 11, 15 and 23 C; cankers scored after 12 to 72 h of wetting (3, 6, 9 h also in 1992) (l. 63) (l. 65). A canker leaves the test at first sporulation (l. 79). So the tables are cumulative percentages.
- 1991: 480 cankers; 149 (31%) sporulated by 72 h (l. 87). Averages by temperature 5.7, 18.9, 32.2, 37.5% at 5, 11, 15, 23 C (l. 139) (l. 140) (l. 141) (l. 142); 46.7% at 23 C and 72 h (l. 142).
- 1992: only 6.3% sporulated (l. 198); 13.8% at 15 C and 72 h (l. 128); 5 and 11 C rows identical, 1.2% at 72 h (l. 126). None at 3, 6, 9 h (l. 73); none at 5 or 11 C until 36 h (l. 85).
- Authors: 17 to 30 h of wetting may be needed for blossom blight (l. 22). No equation, no model (l. 166).

## Dependence

- Its own assay; no model, no equation (l. 166). Cankers left the test at first sporulation (l. 79), so its tables are cumulative.
- No author of an engine model.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Clear and of little use to a grape truth: sporulation data by temperature and wetting for another host. Several printed inconsistencies (60 h in the tables but not the methods; 4 °C in the abstract, 5 °C elsewhere).
