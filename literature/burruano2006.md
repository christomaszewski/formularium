# Burruano et al. 2006: three decades of observations in Sicily

## What it holds

- Western Sicily: primary infections in May-June, two or three cycles. Oospores need
  after-ripening and do not germinate before January (tested at 20-24 °C, > 95 % RH);
  germination 10-28 °C, optimum 20-22 °C, taking 17-46 days at 10 °C and 2-3 days at 22-24
  °C.
- Sporangia form above 90 % RH at 14-28 °C, 24 h after emergence; incubation about 4 days
  from May to October; latent period 8-16 days in spring, up to 52 days in summer.
