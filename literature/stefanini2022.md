# Stefanini & Valleggi 2022: a causal graph for whether to spray

## What it holds

- A Bayesian network for one vineyard row on a 7-day step: treatment, oospore exposure,
  humidity, temperature, a weather score, prevalence and incidence (7 classes).
- Every probability table is one expert's elicitation; no field data. Treat if P(incidence
  of at least 0.25 untreated) exceeds 0.8. 12 scenarios of 10,000 draws.
