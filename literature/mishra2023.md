# Mishra et al. 2023: azoxystrobin residues on chilli fruit and soil

A field trial in January 2021 of azoxystrobin on chilli at two doses. Residues fell by first-order kinetics, with half-lives of about four days.

## What it holds

- **Doses:** 125 and 250 g a.i./ha in 500 L water on chilli cv. Phule Jyoti, one spray (l. 11-13; l. 78-86).
- **Initial deposits:** 1.766 and 3.613 µg/g (l. 14-15; Table, l. 243 and 250).
- **Day 14:** 0.144 and 0.293 µg/g (l. 249; l. 267).
- **Half-lives:** 4.2 and 4.1 days (l. 15-16).
- **Exposure:** 1.73 to 0.065 mg per person per day, against an ADI of 0.18 mg/kg (l. 17-18).

## Dependence

- First-order kinetics (l. 286).
- Modified QuEChERS extraction (l. 138-140). SANTE 11312/2021 validation (l. 117-123).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (fungicide persistence and spray interval):** the decay shape and the four-day half-life could be a residue check in a simulator. The crop is chilli, the season is one, and each day has three replicates.
