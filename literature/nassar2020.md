# Nassar et al. 2020: 24 transposition models ranked in Palestine

A comparison of 24 transposition models against hourly measured tilted irradiance at Palestinian cities, annually, monthly and by clearness index (l. 30-36). No record.

## What it holds

- **Data:** hourly series over 15 months, June 2017 to August 2018 (l. 100).
- **Result:** the models represent the data in annual and monthly analyses, but all fail for clear skies, clearness index above 0.78 (l. 36-41).
- **Proposal:** a new model for the MENA region in clear sky conditions, said to beat the candidates (l. 41-46); its error figures were not located.

## Dependence

- Compares 24 published transposition models (Hay, Perez and others).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s3 and s5:** another sign that transposition fails in clear sky, the case that matters for a slope in summer. A simulator should not trust a tilted-plane radiation model blindly when Kt is above 0.78.
