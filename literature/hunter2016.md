# Hunter et al. 2016: row orientation of vertically trellised Shiraz changes the wind in the work rows

A multi-season field study of four row orientations on flat, semi-arid ground; only the abstract was read.

**Read this first.** Abstract only. The ratio of mid-canopy wind to the wind above the canopy (about 0.5) is not in this abstract; it reaches this strand only through `murakami2024`, which cites it. The site is not named in the page text I could check (the reading JSON and leads say Robertson, South Africa, which is not supported by that text).

## What it holds

- Four row orientations (NS, EW, NE-SW, NW-SE) of vertically trellised, shoot-positioned Shiraz/101-14 Mgt were compared in meso- and microclimate and vine physiology, under field conditions on flat terrain in a semi-arid environment, over many seasons (l. 21).
- "Novel wind velocity and direction profiles" showed the wind in the work rows following the ambient prevailing wind, with flow direction affected by canopy development and row orientation (l. 21).
- NW-SE and EW rows kept higher wind in the work rows than NS and NE-SW rows (l. 21). No speeds or ratios are printed in the abstract.
