# Calonnec et al. 2010: vine vigour drives powdery mildew

## What it holds

- Field (Latresne, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon, rootstocks and cover): vine growth
  explains about 69 % of disease in Merlot (PLS path model).
- Simulations of 1998, 2003 and 2004 with seven vigour levels: growth explains 94.8 % of
  disease; budburst at 90 degree-days above 10 °C from 1 January, flowering at 380.

## Dependence

- None. The budburst rule shares the degree-day form, not the base, of the engine's (5 °C).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Context for a truth whose host growth drives susceptibility.
