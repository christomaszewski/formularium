# Wakeham et al. n.d.: environment and Botrytis cinerea in tomato crops, manuscript

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Airborne Botrytis conidia in a UK glasshouse tomato crop before symptoms, sampled with microtitre immunospore traps (l. 55, 205-209).
- Conidia were most concentrated at 0.5 m next to the stems (l. 65, 592).
- Outside wind speed raised trapped conidia (l. 633); internal temperature and RH did not give a clear pattern (l. 71, 620).
- Trap counts on 4 November 2005 at 0.5, 1.5 and 2.2 m were 60.3, 51.1 and 38.7 (l. 596-600).
- Temperature is given as 21 C (l. 187) and 22 C (l. 612) for the same crop.

## Dependence
