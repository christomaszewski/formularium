# Gent, Turechek & Mahaffee 2007: sequential sampling of hop powdery mildew on cones

From USDA-ARS (Corvallis) and Cornell.

## What it holds

- Sampling unit: 25 cones from each of the first 60 plants of a transect, 1,500 cones (l. 88, 93).
- BPL parameters come from 221 transects in 41 yards, Oregon and Washington, 2000-2005, the authors' earlier cone study (l. 17, 19, 65). Pooled ln(Ax) = 1.81, b = 1.22 (l. 120); the captions print a = 0.15 (l. 222).
- Year affected the parameters; the authors assumed them portable and pooled anyway (l. 101, 119).
- The evaluation sets are subsets of that same construction data: 21 for bootstrap, 13 for simulated sampling. The paper says independent data were not available (l. 22, 78, 320). The 13 sets alone give ln(Ax) = 0.84, b = 1.26 (l. 152).
- Thresholds pt = 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20 (l. 196, 197). Four plans were tested: pt 0.05 and 0.15 at alpha = beta = 0.05 and 0.10 (l. 224).
- Table 2, 13 sets: correct fraction 0.92 with binomial stop lines (l. 428); 1.00 or 0.92 with beta-binomial (l. 436). Mean N 3.15 to 3.08 against 3.15 to 7.08 (l. 431, 439). The abstract's 84.6 or 100% does not match Table 2 (l. 32).
- C = 0.1 cannot be reached at low incidence (l. 25).
- A 1,500-cone sample takes 5 to 7 h; unpublished (l. 30).

## Dependence

- As Part I. Its evaluation sets are subsets of the construction data (independent data were not available). Its printed a = 0.15 does not follow from its own ln Ax and b (the reading agent's check gives about 0.5): a value to check before use.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- **Held out by structure** as Part I. A 1,500-cone sample takes 5 to 7 hours: a cost for a scouting policy.
