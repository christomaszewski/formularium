# Fall 2015: comparative aerobiology of Phytophthora infestans and Bremia lactucae

A French PhD thesis (Sherbrooke, October 2015; directors Beaulieu and Carisse) on airborne sporangia of potato late blight and lettuce downy mildew, with a dynamic model of hourly sporangia concentration. Neither disease is Plasmopara; diseases is empty. The thesis sampling years are 2010 to 2012, not only 2010 and 2011 as the record said.

## What it holds

- **Sampling:** 16 rotating-arm samplers, 8 at Florenceville and 8 at Grand Falls, on field edges 3 m above ground, run 3 days a week for 4.5 h (l. 2353-2365); effective air rate 21 L per min (l. 2366).
- **Efficiency:** taken from the literature, "rotating-arm sampler efficiency = 0.37" of a 7-day volumetric sampler (Noll 1970; Aylor 1993) (l. 2367-2370); not measured here.
- **Wetness and model:** 2 h of leaf wetness were enough for B. lactucae infection under Canadian conditions (l. 120-121, 7640); the sporangia model had R2 above 0.7 in more than 94 % of runs (l. 129-131, 6345).
- **Not read:** the model equations and the lettuce tables were not read.

## Dependence

- Noll 1970 and Aylor 1993 for sampler efficiency; a Bremia infection-efficiency model from the author's earlier papers.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)
