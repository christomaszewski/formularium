# Šebela et al. 2012: reflectance and fluorescence of P. viticola-infected leaves

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Field leaves of three susceptible varieties (Cabernet Sauvignon, Pinot Blanc, Pinot Gris) sampled on one day, 17 July 2011, at Lednice, Czech Republic (l. 26, 121-124).
- 30 leaves: 15 healthy and 15 with chlorotic spots at about 7 days after inoculation, a stage judged from symptoms (l. 128, 138).
- FV/FM is about 25% lower in infected spots; healthy leaves reach about 0.83 (l. 29, 183).
- Chlorophyll a falls 45 to 58% by variety (l. 254); carotenoids fall about 70 to 80% (l. 275-281).
- R750/R700 tracks chlorophyll (R2 0.877); CRI tracks carotenoids (R2 0.735) (l. 32, 308).
- Downy mildew needs frequent rain and cool conditions, optimum 20-25 °C (l. 72).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A one-day field snapshot of 15 infected and 15 healthy leaves with pigment and fluorescence changes, with infection age only estimated from symptoms; it could show that canopy-level indices flag downy mildew, but it cannot date infection or test an incubation model.
