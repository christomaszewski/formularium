# Margaritis 2019: a vineyard decision-support trial at Spata, with no usable disease record

An MSc thesis on integrated protection with precision-agriculture tools in two white varieties
at Spata (Attica), 2018, with in-vineyard stations; read for D58's paired records.

**Read this first.** The title is translated from the Greek, not printed in English. The OCR
loses digits; the numbers below were checked in the page images.

## What it holds

- **Disease:** downy mildew's "actual presence" at the end of 2018 was 0-5 % in Malagouzia and
  0-3 % in Savvatiano (pp. 120, 139), with no unit, dates or interval. Both were sprayed with
  copper; one passage (p. 101) calls the vines unprotected, which contradicts that.
- **Weather:** a gaiasense station in the vineyard (temperature, humidity, rain, leaf wetness at
  canopy height) and a vite.net station, March-September 2018, shown as graphs only; the step is
  not printed (the platform's menu shows "time resolution 60", unit not stated).
- **Appendix:** bulletin texts, soil analyses, fungicide lists; no weather or disease numbers.
