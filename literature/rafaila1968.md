# Rafaila, Sevcenco & David 1968: incubation and morphology in Romania

From the Plant Protection Research Institute, Bucharest. Ten pages.

## What it holds

- **Races:** conidia and conidiophores from eight Romanian vineyards (Tables 1-2) do not
  differ once propagated 3-4 generations in the laboratory: no physiological races.
- **Morphology:** conidia are largest at 20-21 °C mean temperature (Fig. 1);
  conidiophores grow longer with air humidity (Figs 2-3).
- **Leaf age (Table 3, Minis and Blaj vineyards; Chasselas doré, Italian Riesling,
  Cabernet Sauvignon):** incubation of 9-10 days on the lowest third of the shoot, 7-8 in
  the middle, 6-7 at the top: 1-4 days longer on older leaves.
- **Incubation by temperature (Table 4):** detached leaves in a polythermostat at 5-30 °C
  and 100 % humidity, Bucharest 1963-1965; the endpoint is fructification.

  | °C | 10 | 11 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 17 | 19-26 | 27 | 28 | 29 | 30 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | days | 18 | 14 | 12 | 8 | 7 | 5 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | none |

  The text states 10, 11, 12, 19-26, 27, 28 and 29 °C; the values at 14, 16 and 17 °C come
  from matching the scanned table's columns in order. At 22-24 °C incubation sometimes
  took only 3-3.5 days.
- **Below 10 °C** (5, 6, 8, 9 °C) no fructification appeared, but infections were latent,
  not absent: leaves moved to 22 °C after 2-20 days sporulated within 2-4 days. Spores
  germinate down to about 5 °C.

## Dependence

- Their own measurements. They cite neither Müller nor Goidanich; their incubation
  references are Soviet and Romanian (Oltarjevski 1955, Raicov & Ionev 1958, Săvulescu et
  al. 1959).
- No shared author with any model Cooptera runs.
