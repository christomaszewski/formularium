# Courchinoux et al. 2025: heat and compost kill oospores

## What it holds

- **Oven test:** Merlot leaves with oospores (autumn 2021) held a month at 50 °C or 70 °C,
  humid. Bait discs: 0 of 700 infected, against 28.6 ± 4.2 % of 350 for controls kept
  outdoors.
- **Compost:** leaves from autumn 2022 in mesh bags buried in March 2023 in a 50 m³ pile of
  shoots and grass, at three depths, for 3 or 4 months. The pile started at 60 °C, stayed
  above 50 °C for two months, then 37-54 °C by zone. 0 of 960 discs infected; controls
  11.5 ± 4.0 % (May) and 2.8 ± 1.5 % (June), each of 160.
- Germination is assayed by Hill's floating discs (20 °C).
- **Printed slip:** the conclusion's 40-70 °C range is not what was measured.

## Dependence

- None. Poeydebat is an author of [poeydebat2025](poeydebat2025.md): a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Oospores in leaves do not survive a month at 50 °C when moist: a bound for a truth's
  overwintering, if it ever kills inoculum in hot spells. The controls' fall from 11.5 % in
  May to 2.8 % in June is a field germinability curve.
