# Quénol et al. 2004: fine-scale temperature and spring frost in the Vinho Verde

A measurement campaign in two vineyards about 30 km apart, Arcos de Valdevez (Lima valley)
and Monção/Troviscoso (Minho valley), in 2003 (February to October); the paper presents
radiative frost nights. No equations.

## What it holds

- **Sensors** (l. 251-306): 16 + 8 open-air Tiny Talk loggers at 1.50 m (the Bilan says 50 cm,
  l. 717; unresolved) and 12 sheltered loggers (10 at Arcos, 2 at Monção/Troviscoso) in
  louvred white shelters, 15 min; placed by slope, hollow, exposure and surface. Humidity
  recorded under shelter; no humidity result printed anywhere. No accuracy or shelter
  radiation error. Vertical profiles at 1.50 to 3 m on terrace posts; a balsa-wood vane for
  weak drainage flows.
- **Spread** (Tables 1-3, l. 397-454): February monthly mean minima from 2.3 °C (P4, coldest)
  to 4.2 °C (P6), SD between posts 0.6 °C; clear-sky March-May minima P4 2.5 °C against P17
  5.1 °C (gap 2.6 °C, text 2.5 °C), cloudy 8.3 °C against 8.9 °C (gap 0.5 to 0.6 °C). Posts 4
  and 5, tens of metres apart, differ by almost 2 °C on average.
- **Nights** (l. 532-606, 758): 16-17 February, terrace posts -4.4 to -4.9 °C, slope posts
  1.5 to 2 °C warmer; 15-16 February, Troviscoso -0.1 to -0.6 °C, 3.5 to 6 °C warmer than Arcos
  (up to 7 °C, l. 758). Drainage flow under 1 m/s on terraces, over 2 m/s on slopes above 5°,
  above 3 m/s at Troviscoso.
- **Humid air:** the river's near-saturated air limited cooling and gave condensation fog in
  the hollow at dawn (l. 540, 570), qualitatively only.
- **Garbles:** Monção and Troviscoso altitudes 20 and 65 m in the text, 17 and 70 m in Table 1.
- **Data:** none offered; a second campaign (2004) is said to confirm, with nothing printed.

## Dependence

- **None computed.** The paper cites cold-air drainage theory (Mahrt 1986 and others) and
  agrees with Champagne results (Bridier et al. 1995; Beltrando et al. 2002); shared authors
  with those are a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

For strand 3 (and strand 4, a measured vineyard dataset) and what Agrarium's truth can take:

- **It gives** the size of the within-vineyard temperature spread on radiative nights in a
  Portuguese vineyard region: SD 0.6 °C in February (largest on clear nights), gaps of 2 to
  2.5 °C between terrace and slope posts a few tens of metres apart, 5 to 7 °C between valleys.
  A truth with one temperature per vineyard misses this on clear nights.
- **It gives no humidity or wetness**: humidity was recorded and not reported; the only
  humidity note is that river air near saturation limited cooling. For the engine's RH ≥ 90 %
  stand-in this paper adds nothing, except that the coldest hollows are where dew would form.
- **Season:** February to May frost, not the infection season.
- **Can it run:** no; nothing to compute and the data are not offered.
