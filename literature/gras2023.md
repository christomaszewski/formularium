# Gras et al. 2023: one 30 ha estate's harvest-sector yield and must quality (2022) with three years of 15-minute T, RH and rain

A data paper for a whole commercial estate in southern France: yield and must analyses per harvest sector for the 2022 vintage, block descriptions, and weather every 15 minutes for 2020 to 2022. No record: a dataset.

**Read this first.** The yield and quality values are one year only (l. 189), and the sector values are the trailer's weight and must analysis re-allocated to sectors by the harvester's GNSS track (l. 21), so they are derived allocations, not per-vine measures. The weather has the same three variables as the outbreak records (T, RH, rain) and no leaf wetness, but there is no disease or phenology label.

## What it holds

- **Where to get it** (l. 25): Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.8328384. Licence of the article: CC BY (l. 5). The Zenodo record's licence (CC BY 4.0) and the archive size (Data.zip 1.9 MB) are the reading agent's look at the record, not in the paper.
- **Site** (l. 39, 165): one 30 ha commercial estate at Villeneuve-lès-Maguelone (43.5323 N, 3.8642 E) in 68 blocks within a radius of 3 km of the one weather station; non-irrigated (l. 189). Varieties compared in a figure include Syrah, Marselan and Grenache (l. 179).
- **Harvest sectors** (l. 43, 175): mean area 0.3 ha, the area a harvester covers to fill a trailer, for the 2022 harvest only. Variables: mass (weighed at a winery or cooperative, scale accuracy plus or minus 50 kg, l. 81), yield, yield per plant, share of unproductive plants (l. 175), and six must parameters measured by a commercial laboratory (sugar by Fourier-transform infrared, l. 84; total acidity, pH, yeast-assimilable nitrogen, organic nitrogen).
- **Computed label** (l. 87): potential alcohol is calculated from the measured sugar: sugar divided by 16.83.
- **Weather** (l. 45, 183): 2020, 2021 and 2022 every 15 minutes, 97,988 lines, from a commercial Agriscope station in the middle of the vineyard; variables date, hour, relative humidity, rainfall and air temperature.
- **Layout** (l. 41, 130): a shapefile of 68 block features plus CSV files and a metadata workbook.
- **How labels were made:** observed (weighing, laboratory analysis, station sensors); yield and potential alcohol are calculated from observations; sector attribution is by a Python algorithm on harvester tracks.
- **Authors' limit** (l. 189): a single year of production measurements, one region of southern France, non-irrigated conditions, tied to local soil.

## Dependence

- Sector values depend on the authors' allocation algorithm and on harvester geolocation; the weather is a commercial station's. The sibling data paper Oger 2023 (same issue and group; its Field 1 is at the same town) and the water-status set Zhang 2025 (same group, the same estate per the reading agent) are not independent of it. None recorded otherwise.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **D60 tier 1 (T, RH, rain), real-data side of D58.** A real, high-time-resolution tier-1 weather series for three seasons next to a harvest outcome. It can test a synthetic or filled weather input (D60) against a real station at 15-minute steps, and a ripening or yield emulator on one vintage; the outcome is one year, so no interannual response can be learnt or checked.
- **Not disease.** Nothing here can show a disease variable is real; no scouting, no phenology dates, no leaf wetness.
- **Limits:** one estate, one weather station for 68 blocks (spatial weather variation within 3 km is not measured), harvest-only quality, a single vintage.
