# Douthe et al. 2018: whole-vine chambers on Grenache in Mallorca

Whole-plant gas exchange of field Grenache in summer 2017, irrigated at 0.5 ET0 or not.
Read for leaf area per vine; it is measured but not printed.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 110-119): Grenache on 110 Richter, planted 2009 (the abstract says 12 years
  old, l. 29), Palma; rows 2.5 m, vines 1 m, N-S; drip at 0.5 ET0 twice a week June to
  September, or rainfed. Rain under 5 mm in July and August (l. 161).
- **Leaf area** (l. 188, 195-197, 214): measured at harvest by Sánchez-de-Miguel et al.'s
  method to express fluxes per m² of leaf, not printed for field vines; a potted
  calibration vine had about 0.5 m².
- **Fluxes:** net CO2 exchange about 10 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ in July, 1-2 in August (l. 296, 302).
- **Not printed:** leaf area per vine, LAI, shoots, trellis, canopy size.

## Dependence

- Chambers after Pérez Peña & Tarara 2004; protocol of Escalona et al. 2016. No model
  shaped the data.
