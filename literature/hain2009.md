# Hain et al. 2009: available water from ALEXI

Research article, Oklahoma Mesonet, United States.

## What it holds

- Retrieves available water fraction from the ALEXI ratio of actual to potential ET using four fPET-to-fAW relationships, validated against Oklahoma Mesonet soil moisture (2002-04) and compared with EDAS.
- It is soil moisture retrieval rather than ET or wetness itself, so only indirectly useful.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Soil moisture proxy, not leaf wetness. Little.
