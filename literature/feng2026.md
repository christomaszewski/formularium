# Feng et al. 2026: Alternaria spores and weather in tobacco

A single-season tobacco study in Guizhou. Alternaria spore catches were compared at three heights and correlated with weather at lags of 0 to 5 days.

## What it holds

- Slide traps at 0.5, 1.0 and 1.5 m: 1.0 m caught the largest share, 51.8%, against 25.3% at 1.5 m and 22.9% at 0.5 m (l. 60, 269).
- Rainfall correlated best at a two-day lag, r = 0.49 to 0.52 across the three heights (l. 67, 286-287).
- Sampling: 17 days, 11 to 27 August 2025, Fuquan City, cvs. Yunyan 87 and Guiyan 20 (l. 157-158, 182).

## Dependence

- Slide-trap method of West and Kimber 2015 (l. 100). Pearson and lagged correlation (l. 237-242).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t2 (observation). The sampling height and the two-day rain lag are hypotheses for a spore-trap design in vines.
- It reports correlations only, with no thresholds or lead time against disease, and it is tobacco, not vine. Not read in full.
