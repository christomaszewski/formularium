# Romanazzi et al. 2024: chitosan and copper in three Marche vineyards, 2019-2021

## What it holds

- **Sites:** Angeli di Varano (Montepulciano, 2019-2021), Castelplanio and Matelica
  (Verdicchio), central Adriatic Italy; three blocks with untreated controls.
- **Untreated controls** (incidence, by vineyard-year):
  - Angeli 2019: leaves 60.9 % (15 July), bunches 96.1 % (31 July); first symptoms mid June;
  - Castelplanio 2019: leaves 61.0 %, bunches 50.4 % (23 September);
  - Angeli 2020: leaves 16.0 %, bunches 8.3 %; first infections late June;
  - Castelplanio 2020: leaves 51.3 %, bunches 35.4 %; first symptoms early July;
  - Matelica 2020: leaves 29.8 %, bunches 63.9 %;
  - Angeli 2021: leaves 36.4 %, bunches 9.6 %; first symptoms on bunches 6 July;
  - Matelica 2021: leaves 7.2 %, bunches 18.6 %; Matelica 2019 almost none.
- **Weather:** AMAP stations at Camerano, Castelplanio and Matelica; weekly values only in
  a supplementary figure.
- **Printed slip:** the text's 2019 Angeli bunch results do not match Table 2.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)
