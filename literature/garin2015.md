# Garin 2015: foliar epidemics in architectural canopies, with a grapevine powdery mildew case

An AgroParisTech PhD on a framework that couples foliar fungal epidemics to plant architecture models. It has a grapevine powdery mildew case and wheat cases.

## What it holds

- **Framework:** chapter 1 is a 2014 Annals of Botany article on coupling epidemic models to functional-structural plant models (l. 638-640; article header l. 756-757, doi 10.1093/aob/mcu101).
- **Vine case:** grapevine powdery mildew (Erysiphe necator) on a TopVine architecture. Lesion growth follows Calonnec et al. 2008, and the maximum colony diameter depends on leaf age (l. 1544-1571).
- **Pathosystems:** Table 1 sets the grapevine mildew (perennial, biotrophic, wind-dispersed) against wheat septoria (hemi-biotrophic, rain-dispersed) and wheat brown rust (biotrophic, rain-dispersed) (l. 572-600; dispersal row l. 589).
- **Wheat check:** wheat severity data serve as the field check for the wheat chapters (Table 4, l. 2901). The text read has no new vine dataset.

## Dependence

- Builds on the vine mildew model of Calonnec et al. 2008 (l. 1562-1566), the septoria model of Robert et al. 2008 (Table 1, l. 2413), ADEL-wheat (Fournier et al. 2003; l. 1366) and TopVine (Louarn et al. 2008; Pallas et al. 2009; l. 483, 1560).
