# Gent, Richardson & Massie 2025: a modified disease risk index for hop powdery mildew

From USDA-ARS (Corvallis) and Washington State University. The supplement (one table of P-values) is covered by this note.

## What it holds

- Hop (Humulus lupulus) powdery mildew, cultivar Cascade, Washington State. Not grape.
- The modified index keeps the form of the 2002 hop index (HOPS) of Mahaffee et al. 2003a, itself a modification of the Gubler-Thomas grape index (l. 30, 75).
- Daily rule chain evaluated at 6 a.m. from 15-min data; points added to a rolling sum clipped to 0-100; 0-30 low, 40-60 moderate, 70-100 high (l. 85, 97-98, 103-105).
- Original: >6 h >30 C -20; rain >2.5 mm -10; >=6 h at 16-27 C +20; otherwise -10 (l. 125-131).
- Modified: >=6 h >=28 C -20; new >=2 h <=4 C -10; rain >=2.5 mm -10; same +20 and -10 (l. 125-133).
- The 28 C value comes from the authors' growth-chamber work on Cascade (Gent 2022; l. 164-167). The 4 C, 2 h rule cites cold-stress papers; no data given (l. 174-180).
- The original values were set from chamber work on Symphony and adjusted to field observations in OR, ID, WA (l. 89-94).
- Validation: 2020-2023, two Cascade yards, 7 replicates; index-timed sprays matched 10-day sprays, with up to 2 fewer (l. 35, 187-195). Prosser had little disease (l. 341).
- Post-hoc, 11 stations, 2016-2022: Washington mean interval 8.6 to 9.6 days; Oregon 9.1 to 9.3 days (l. 368-369, 378-380, 408).

## Dependence

- It modifies Mahaffee et al. 2003's HOPS rules, themselves "a modification of the Gubler-Thomas" index, the engine's `gubler1999.powdery_index`: a borrowed equation at one remove (the Gubler-Thomas rules are not printed here), and the engine's form (hours in a temperature band scored daily).
