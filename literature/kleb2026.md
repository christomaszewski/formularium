# Kleb et al. 2026: VitiMeteo fed by canopy sensors against a station 23 km away

A preprint, not peer reviewed in this form.

## What it holds

- **Site:** Hohenheim, Pinot Meunier, 2021. Air temperature, RH and leaf wetness at 100,
  130 and 160 cm in 12 canopy sites; a border station at 200 cm; and the VitiMeteo network
  station at Metzingen, 23 km away.
- **Model:** VitiMeteo-Plasmopara (Bleyer et al. 2011): a leaf-growth module, infection
  levels 0-3 from degree-hours of leaf wetness (below 50, 50-100, 100-200, above 200), and
  an incubation estimate. No equations printed.
- **Observed:** expert estimates of infected leaf area every three days, in three zones,
  over three periods of about three weeks.
- **Results:** r² 0.29 with the canopy average, 0.18-0.29 by height, 0.03 with the border
  station and 0.04 with Metzingen. Station-driven forecasts overestimated risk.

## Dependence

- No engine model is named. VitiMeteo's infection classes are wet degree-hours, the form of
  the engine's UR model's 50 °C·h rule (`wet-degree-hours-infection`); Bleyer et al. 2011 is
  the source to read for them.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- For Agrarium's microclimate: a measured case that canopy weather explains disease better
  than a station, even one at the vineyard's border.
