# [Author not printed] 2017: Saxon seminar slides on downy mildew rules

Slides from a Saxon training workshop with the rules taught for oospore maturity and primary infection.

**Read this first.**
- Teaching slides: rules with no data and no source on the slides. The author is not printed.
- Only the Peronospora slides were read.

## What it holds

- **Oospore maturity (l. 195, 196):** oospores must "mature" (reifen): a temperature sum above 160 degree-days is needed before a primary sporangium can form, counted from 1 January over all temperature differences above 8 °C.
- **Primary sporangium (l. 200):** forms only in moisture (moist soil and relative humidity over 80 % for 8 h).
- **"10er Regel" (l. 201 to 203):** infection by the "10 rule": 10 cm of shoot length (l. 202), 10 mm of rain within 3 days, or 5 mm in one day (l. 203), and 10 °C air temperature.
- **Primary sporangium contents:** up to 60 zoospores (l. 206, found by grep, not among the checked quotes).
- **Infection (l. 236):** needs more than 50 °h of temperature times leaf wetness.

## Dependence

- No source is cited on the slides. The reading agent notes that the 160 degree-day rule is Gehmann et al. 1987's as used in VitiMeteo; that attribution is from outside the slides.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Primary infection rules (Agrarium D67):** Saxony's "10er Regel" and the 160 degree-day oospore maturity from 1 January are rules taught to growers. They set a maturity gate by date and temperature, not an observation of first lesions.
