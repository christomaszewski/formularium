# Maddalena et al. 2024: the Franciacorta study over three seasons (abstract)

## What it holds

- Ten Franciacorta vineyards, April to bunch closure, 2021-2023: oospore germination time
  t, and incidence and severity in untreated plots.
- t falls as vines become susceptible and together with EPI's risk; EPI is said to have
  predicted the epidemics. No value, statistic or equation is printed.
- How infection dates were set is not said (the 2022 study used Goidanich's incubation).
