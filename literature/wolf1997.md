# Wolf et al. 1997: floral debris removal and Botrytis bunch rot in Virginia

Removing senescent floral debris from Chardonnay clusters cut Botrytis bunch rot in some Virginia vineyards and not in others. A sticker adjuvant had no effect.

## What it holds

- Field trials in 1991, 1993 and 1994 in five Virginia vineyards (l. 61).
- 1993, Blue Ridge Chase: September incidence 27% with debris removed, 67% without (Table 4, l. 222-223). At Oakencroft the figures were 45% and 52%.
- 1994, Blue Run: incidence 5% with a back-pack blower, 13% without (Table 5, l. 245-246).
- The adjuvant Nu-Film 17 did not change debris retention or rot (l. 21-22). Late removal by compressed air, when berries reached 8 mm, bruised berries (l. 22-23).

## Dependence

- None recorded. The Discussion cites Savage and Sall (1981) for debris as a nutrient source for the fungus (l. 277).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (Botrytis bunch rot). Debris kept in the cluster may seed late infection, so a simulator could carry a debris-retention term. The trials give a per-site yes or no, not a rate.
- Effects were significant at some sites only (l. 20-21). The trials date from 1991 to 1994. Not read in full.
