# Biasi et al. 2019: harvest dates and climate indices in the Orvieto PDO

Winery records of harvest (technological ripening) for seven varieties in the Orvieto
PDO, Umbria, set against 1995 to 2015 temperature trends and bioclimatic indices. There
is no stage model; the results are trends and correlations.

## What it holds

- **Design** (l. 84, 94, 148, 1229-1236): two wineries; white varieties (Chardonnay,
  Grechetto) in the western sub-area, 1995 to 2015, 21 seasons; red varieties (Aleatico,
  Cabernet franc, Cabernet sauvignon, Merlot, Sangiovese) in the eastern sub-area, 2004 to
  2015, 12 seasons (Figure 6b starts in 2005; unsettled). Harvest is the BBCH technological
  ripening date from winery registers (l. 1233, 1236).
- **Climate** (l. 205-210, 341-368, 1012): annual mean temperature 13.5 °C in 1995 to 2004
  and 14.7 °C in 2005 to 2015. Growing-season temperature (GST) in the white area 19.7 °C
  and 20.5 °C in the two decades; red area 19.6 °C in 2004 to 2015; a rise of 1.4 °C in 15
  years. GST trend R² 0.45 (Figure 4, l. 399).
- **The one printed equation** (Figure 3b, rendered page 4): annual rain Y = 15.2 X + 387.3
  on rainy days X, R² 0.6; the text gives r² 0.65 (l. 241). Unsettled.
- **Harvest trends** (Figure 6, l. 581-593, 634): linear trends of harvest day on year,
  with R² labels only, slopes not printed: Chardonnay earlier (R² 0.3), Cabernet sauvignon
  later (0.6), Sangiovese later (0.4), Merlot earlier (0.2), Cabernet franc earlier (0.3);
  no trend for Grechetto and Aleatico. Grechetto spans day 218 to 264 (l. 583-585), Aleatico
  232 to 258 (l. 587-590). Yearly dates are in supplement Table S2 (l. 1269), unread.
- **Indices** (Table 1, l. 1200-1209): Winkler (daily mean above 10 °C, 1 April to 31
  October), Huglin (mean of Tm − 10 and Tmax − 10 times d = 1.03, 1 April to 30 September),
  Cool night (September mean Tmin), GST, days with Tmax of 30 °C or more, days with Tmin
  of 0 °C or less, and the Seleaninov coefficient.
- **Statistics:** Pearson correlations of harvest and quality with the indices are
  significant at p < 0.05; coefficients are in Table S2 (l. 1169-1171). PCA: two components,
  59 % of variance, white; three, 66 %, red (l. 868-870).
- **Not printed:** yearly harvest dates, slopes, correlation coefficients, budburst,
  flowering and veraison.

## Dependence

- **Borrowed formulas** applied to the authors' own data: Winkler (ref 71), Huglin (refs 39,
  68), Cool night and the multicriteria classification (ref 68), Seleaninov (ref 74); GST
  classes after Nesbitt et al. (ref 30).
- **No model fitted.** Harvest dates are observed. Salvati and Ferrara are CREA co-authors;
  nothing ties them to another model here.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **Not a truth candidate:** trends and correlations, no stage model, coefficients in a
  supplement. No record.
- **Weak use:** the temperature and harvest-trend direction for the Orvieto PDO; Winkler and
  Huglin are degree-day sums used here only as predictors.
