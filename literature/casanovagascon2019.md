# Casanova-Gascón et al. 2019: resistant varieties in Somontano, and two engine models tried

## What it holds

- **Trial:** Las Almunietas, Barbastro (Huesca), 2016-2018; resistant varieties against
  Sauvignon blanc, in untreated control rows and treated rows; weekly scoring of 55 leaves
  and 25 clusters (degree of attack, Townsend-Heuberger), mostly in figures.
- **Downy mildew, untreated Sauvignon blanc:** clusters from 28 June 2016; in 2017 only 3 of
  55 leaves (13 July); in 2018, the wet year (499 mm), clusters up to 58 %.
- **Powdery mildew:** about 20 % on clusters in mid-July 2016.
- **Weather:** in-plot sensors with regional stations (CHE, SIAR); rain 243.6, 294.3 and 499
  mm in 2016-2018.
- **The models growers use there:**
  - Goidanich's latent period dated downy mildew infections; its dates in 2016 and 2018
    "were off by several weeks", and the authors urge other models;
  - Thomas & Gubler's powdery mildew model was "very accurate", within a week.
  - Neither comparison is shown in numbers.

## Dependence

- Runs two engine models, Goidanich's incubation (`goidanich.incubation`) and the UC
  Davis powdery index (`gubler1999.powdery_index`), against observed symptoms.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- The only field verdicts so far on the engine's incubation and powdery index from a
  vineyard next to Catalonia. Qualitative, but it agrees with Chrelashvili 1993's Müller
  curve running weeks early.
