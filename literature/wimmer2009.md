# Wimmer 2009: Krems temperatures, Gladstones' index and Grüner Veltliner's flowering

A geographer's account of the Wachau's warming, 1971-2007, with Gladstones' heat index
and a 43-year flowering record of Grüner Veltliner at Krems, trended on year, and one
regional scenario for the 2040s. No fit of a date on temperature.

## What it holds

- **Data:** Krems (station moved three times, corrected against Vienna's Hohe Warte by
  the quasi-constant-difference method, l. 373-442); April-October mean 14.7 °C over
  1971-2000 (l. 94); Grüner Veltliner flowering at the Sandgrube, 1965-2007, by eye,
  from the Krems viticulture school (l. 124-127).
- **Warming:** April-October Tmin +0.38 and Tmax +0.60 °C a decade, +1.31 and +2.20 °C
  over 1971-2006 (Table 4, l. 495-497); sunshine +383 h over 1971-2007 (l. 938).
- **Flowering:** eleven days earlier over 1971-2007 (l. 476); the line on year
  y = −0.3145x + 821.07, R² 0.16, about 3 d a decade (l. 519-520). No regression on
  temperature.
- **Index:** Gladstones 1992's WI10 (degree-days above 10 °C capped at 19 °C, weighted by
  his monthly K factors, 1.036 on average at Krems; l. 183-222): trend +5.4 a year, R²
  0.41; 1,244 for 1998-2007, 1,290 after a +46 site correction for south-facing top
  sites (l. 571-594); Riesling needs about 1,200, Grüner Veltliner 1,150 (l. 536, 585).
- **Scenario:** reclip:more, one emission scenario (IS92a), one GCM, two regional models:
  Krems about +2.3 °C by the 2040s, the index 1,167 (1980s; 1,230 in Table 5) to 1,383,
  1,410 corrected (l. 632-652, 864).

## Dependence

- **None:** Gladstones' index applied; trends the author's.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- A long flowering series (1965-2007) that exists in Krems; the paper fits it on year
  only, so the days-per-degree figure would have to be derived from the school's data.
