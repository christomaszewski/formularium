# Lana et al. 2009: review of monthly and daily rainfall statistics in Catalonia

A review in Tethys that reports, among other work, how daily rain amounts and event times are distributed at 75 Catalan gauges, 1950-2000.

**Read this first.**
- It is a review: the numbers belong to the studies it cites (Burgueño et al. 2005, 2004). The Markov chain numbers are in Lana & Burgueño 1998, which this paper only lists (l. 566). Only the parts on daily amounts, durations and rain days were read.
- It prints no value for any registry parameter, only the form of the distributions.

## What it holds

- **Data (l. 396-400, from the text):** 75 gauges with daily rain, 1950-2000; an earlier, more detailed analysis of the Fabra Observatory series, 1917-1999.
- **Daily amounts (l. 401-402):** "well modelled by an exponential distribution".
- **Times (l. 402):** the time distributions "mostly accepted the Weibull model". Counts from the text: a few gauges followed neither model, 25 followed one, and 42 fitted both (l. 403-405, from the text, not a quoted fact).
- **Rain days (l. 404):** the average annual number of rain days runs from 30 to 140 across Catalonia, with a coefficient of variation of 5 to 45 % (text, next lines). The annual number had no field-significant trend, though 23 of 75 gauges had significant trends (text).
- **Gradient:** the exponential parameter has a strong east-to-west gradient, read as the influence of Mediterranean advection on average rain amount (text).
- **Markov chains (l. 566):** the review cites Lana & Burgueño 1998 (Int. J. Climatol. 18:793-815) for the dry-wet chain; no transition value is printed here.

## Dependence

- A review; it builds on its cited studies (Burgueño et al. 2004, 2005; Lana & Burgueño 1998). No model of the engine is computed. Shared authors with other notes are only a flag.
