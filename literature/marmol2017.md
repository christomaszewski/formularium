# Mármol Rodríguez 2017: a dated 2016 spray log of a Codorníu estate at Raimat, in a thesis on a vine mite

A master's thesis (Màster en Protecció Integrada de Cultius, Universitat de Lleida) on biological control of the mite Eotetranychus carpini, whose Table 1 lists every product sprayed on one plot in 2016, including five downy mildew sprays.

**Read this first.**
- The thesis is about a mite, not about downy mildew; the mildew sprays are incidental to it.
- Raimat is in the Costers del Segre (Lleida), semi-arid with 300 to 350 mm of rain a year (l. 942), far drier than the Penedès. It is not a Penedès log.
- One plot (no. 24), one season, and the source is the estate's technical team by oral communication of 2017-07-10 (l. 1179). Doses are product doses per hectare, as printed.

## What it holds

- **Site (l. 935, 942):** Raimat (Lleida, Catalonia), where Codorníu manages about 700 ha, mostly vines; Mediterranean continental sub-arid climate with mean annual rain around 300 to 350 mm.
- **Estate practice (l. 966):** a monitoring system follows all pests and diseases, and a zone of a plot is treated only when a quality-damage threshold is exceeded.
- **Source of the log (l. 1179):** Table 1, products used on experimental plot no. 24 in 2016, from the Raimat technical team, oral communication of 2017-07-10.
- **Downy mildew sprays in 2016 (Table 1):**
  - 2016-05-03 and 2016-05-12: copper hydroxide 35 % (ECO KDOS), 1.5 kg/ha (l. 1190).
  - 2016-05-28 and 2016-06-03: copper hydroxide, 2 kg/ha (l. 1209).
  - 2016-09-14: copper oxychloride, 1.75 kg/ha (l. 1229); the last mildew spray of 2016.
- **Count:** five mildew sprays in the year, four of them within 31 days from 3 May to 3 June (arithmetic on the dates). Sulphur and sulphur-copper products for powdery mildew, a mite product and a biological control release are also listed; they are not downy mildew sprays.
- **Not in the table:** no spray is listed between 2016-06-03 and 2016-09-14 for downy mildew, and no disease score or weather is given with it.

## Dependence

- None recorded. The log is the estate's own record; the thesis adds no model.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Agrarium grower model:** a dated sequence of copper doses and intervals (9 days, 16 days, 6 days, then 103 days, arithmetic) for a drier Catalan site, usable as one example of grower timing, not a Penedès one.
- **Cooptera E6 (spray timing and copper):** a real record of a grower's copper dates for 2016, with no matching weather or disease score, so it can only be a pattern of intervals.
- **Kind of evidence:** one plot, one season, oral source relayed in a master's thesis.
