# Valdés-Gómez et al. 2017: a scouting-triggered powdery mildew strategy in Chile

Research article, Maule Valley (Universidad de Talca vineyard, 35°22' S, 71°35.4' W), central Chile; DSS originally designed in France.

## What it holds

- Field trial (2010-2013) of the rule-based Decision Support Strategy of Mildium (2 obligatory sprays at flowering and 12-14 days later, 2 optional sprays triggered by leaf/bunch scouting) against untreated vines on Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay.
- It reports severity (below 3% under the DSS, about 10% and 40% untreated) and spray cost, but the epidemiological risk models are not given with equations.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- A costed scouting policy (2-3 sprays against 7-9; scouting labour a large share of cost): a baseline for Agrarium's policy comparisons (PLAN 10). Calonnec is from INRAE Bordeaux; no engine author.
