# Bernáth et al. 2021: 34 seasons of Welschriesling and Pinot Blanc in Slovakia

A long dated series (1985-2018) at one cultivar-testing station with its own weather,
analysed as trends on year and correlations with monthly temperatures. No regression of
a date on temperature is printed, so it gives the sign of the drivers, not a model.

## What it holds

- **Data:** Dolné Plachtince (228 m; 1960-1990 normals 9.1 °C annual, 16.0 °C growing
  season), Welschriesling and Pinot Blanc, BBCH onsets 1985-2018 (l. 17-18, 688-694);
  management changed after the first 20 years (l. 702). Per-year dates are in the
  supplement (l. 814-818).
- **Trends:** 2009-2018 against 1985-1994, budburst 7 and 5 d earlier, flowering 10 and
  7, softening 18, harvest 8 and 10 (l. 281, 301-302, 477, 502-507); flowering on year
  −0.42 and −0.33 d/yr (R² 0.26, 0.18; Fig. 5, the signs from the page). Mean budburst
  20 and 15 April, flowering 10 June (printed 6 June for day 161), softening 10 August,
  harvest 10 October (l. 274-276, 295, 458, 500).
- **Correlations only:** budburst with the March mean r −0.74 and −0.73 (l. 292);
  flowering with the May maximum −0.82 and −0.89 and the May mean −0.72 and −0.83
  (l. 355-380). No slope in days per °C, no intercept, no error (the abstract's GDD
  test is not in the Results, l. 18-19).
- **Heat:** GDD base 10 °C over April-September, mean 1,360, trend r 0.55; Huglin index
  (K 1.06) +12.4 a year (l. 201-239, 725-751); budburst-to-flowering 281 and 243 °C·d,
  budburst to harvest 173 and 168 d (l. 530-536, 575).
- **Warming:** +1.4 °C annual and +1.5 °C in the growing season, 2009-2018 against
  1960-1990 (l. 143-155).

## Dependence
