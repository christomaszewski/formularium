# AWRI Viti-Note (n.d.): characteristics of downy mildew, with the 10:10:24 rule

A three-page extension note of the Australian Wine Research Institute (CRCV series, no date printed) on the disease, with a germination rule and the density of first lesions.

**Read this first.**
- The 10:10:24 rule is a rule of thumb. The note does not attribute it and gives no data.
- The text layer lost the degree signs ("80C", "100C", "18-220C"). They read as 8 °C, 10 °C and 18 to 22 °C from context; the last is garbled.

## What it holds

- **Season (l. 102):** primary infection can occur throughout the growing season when conditions are suitable.
- **Density of first lesions (l. 89, 104):** 1 to 3 oil spots per 50 m of row after zoospore release (l. 89); "around one oilspot per 50 m" of row in the conditions section (l. 104).
- **Zoospore release (l. 110):** from oospores in free water above 8 °C (garbled "80C"); optimum 18 to 22 °C (garbled).
- **10:10:24 rule (l. 116):** germination when the temperature stays at 10 °C or more and there is at least 10 mm of rain over 24 h.

## Dependence

- None recorded. The rule is not attributed; the note cites Nicholas, Magarey & Wachtel (eds) 1994 for the disease in general.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **First detection is late (Agrarium D67):** with 1 to 3 oil spots per 50 m of row at primary infection, the first lesions are few and a scout will find them late. Bears on the gap between true first infection and first detection.
- **Rule, not test:** the 10:10:24 rule can be set beside Carinthia's and Saxony's rules (lkkarntennd, wackerbarth2017) as a statement of the same family. Kind: extension rule of thumb, no evidence.
