# Schumacher et al. 2024: downy mildew severity of clusters and leaves at seven growth stages

Potted vines of four cultivars were inoculated with Plasmopara viticola at seven BBCH
stages, from the first inflorescence to the start of ripening. The paper prints the mean
severity of each cultivar at each stage in the text, for clusters and for leaf discs.

## What it holds

- **Design:** 42 potted vines a cultivar (7 L pots) in a greenhouse at Freiburg im
  Breisgau, four trials in 2020-2023, each vine inoculated at one stage only; clusters
  sprayed twice 24 h apart at 40,000 sporangia/ml in a chamber above 17.5 °C, severity
  rated 4 weeks later; 1,856 clusters in all (l. 94, 104, 116, 124-129, 143-152). No
  calendar dates are printed, only BBCH stages (53-55, 57-59, 65-68, 71-73, 75, 77-79,
  81-83).
- **Pinot Noir clusters** (p. 4, l. 204-206, 230): almost 100 % up to and including
  BBCH 75 (92 % at BBCH 75 in the later paragraph, l. 230), 55 % at BBCH 77-79, 2 % at
  BBCH 81-83.
- **Souvignier Gris clusters** (l. 207-208, 233): nearly 100 % to BBCH 75, 64 % (65 % in
  the later paragraph) at 75, 41 % at 77-79, no symptoms at 81-83.
- **Cabernet Cortis clusters** (l. 210-211): 77, 86, 54, 20 and 2 % at BBCH 53-55, 57-59,
  65-68, 71-73 and 75, none after. **Solaris** (l. 214-215): 44-73 % up to BBCH 65-68,
  9 % at 71-73, none after.
- **Leaf discs, Pinot Noir** (l. 243-246): 77, 73, 50 and 16 % at BBCH 53-55, 57-59,
  65-68 and 71-73, and 3 % at 81-83. Cabernet Cortis 13 and 6 % and Solaris 6 and under
  1 % at the first two stages, below 1 % later; Souvignier Gris about 2 % throughout
  (l. 248-252). Leaf discs scored 7 days after inoculation, 36 discs a cultivar and stage
  (l. 167, 170).
- **Field:** in the 2021 open-field trial at Freiburg Pinot Noir exceeded 90 %, Souvignier
  Gris 19 %, Cabernet Cortis 6 %, Solaris 2 % (p. 6, l. 319-321). 2022 had no infection
  (hot and dry) and 2023 little (l. 322).
- **Not printed:** any fitted curve, the per-stage means and standard deviations (figures
  only), calendar dates.

## Dependence

- **None recorded.** It fits no formulation and uses no model output. It cites Kennelly
  et al. 2005 and Gindro et al. 2012 for stomata and berry resistance (l. 361-367); those
  are leads, not facts about this paper.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **First per-stage table:** the first susceptibility table by growth stage, for clusters
  and leaves, that the catalogue holds. It bears on the truth's
  berry_resistant_after_flowering window (14 days after flowering) and on its rachis
  window. Pinot Noir clusters stay near fully susceptible past flowering (BBCH 65-68) to
  BBCH 75, and fall only at BBCH 77-79 (berries touching), far later than 14 days.
- **Against Kennelly 2005:** `kennelly2005.bunch_window` has berries resistant 1-2 weeks
  after bloom at Geneva and pedicels to 4 weeks. Here severity is rated on the whole
  cluster in a wet chamber on potted vines, a harsher test than Geneva's; the two do not
  agree on timing and the difference is not explained.
- **Leaves:** the leaf-disc series shows leaves of a given growth stage, not a given
  leaf's age in days.
- **Caveats:** potted vines in an open greenhouse, heavy inoculation, means read from the
  text, stages not dates; the field result (Pinot Noir over 90 %) shows the susceptible
  cultivar is not an artefact of the chamber.
