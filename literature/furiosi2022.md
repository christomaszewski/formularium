# Furiosi et al. 2022: fungicide use and future scenarios, Veneto

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Fungicide records from 179 Veneto vineyards via the vite.net DSS, 2015 to 2020 (l. 44-45, 172-174).
- Treatments per season averaged 12 to 14 in both periods; annual ranges were 6 to 17 (2015), 7 to 25 (2016) and 11 to 17 (2018) (l. 65-73).
- Downy mildew moved from low-risk to medium- and high-risk products after Mancozeb and propineb were removed (l. 114).
- The EU copper cap fell from 6 to 4 kg/ha per season; 2018-2020 copper averaged under 4 kg/ha (l. 116-121).
- Future scenarios for 2023 assume cuts of 20%, 10% and 25% (l. 37-38, 137, 161).
- The powdery scenario's components do not add to the 13 treatments it assumes (l. 139-160).

## Dependence

- none named. The data come from farmers using the vite.net decision support system (Horta Srl), which the paper says uses models, but the paper names no model and computes none; whether vite.net runs a model that the engine also runs is not stated here (a flag to check, not a dependence).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Operational records of spray counts, copper dose and FRAC risk classes for 179 vineyards over six years, which a truth could use as observed spraying practice; it gives no disease or weather data and runs no model, so it cannot test infection timing.
