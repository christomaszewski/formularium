# Meggio et al. 2008: row orientation and chlorophyll retrieval

Research article, Ribera del Duero, northern Spain (14 vineyards of Vitis vinifera L.).

## What it holds

- Develops chlorophyll (Cab) retrieval from CASI airborne hyperspectral imagery for vineyards that accounts for row orientation, sun geometry and shadow, using the rowMCRM canopy model and TCARI/OSAVI;
- RMSE 10.2 and 10.6 ug/cm2 for morning and afternoon over 72 sites in 14 fields.
- It shows that viewing geometry and row orientation alter vineyard reflectance, but it is not about disease.
