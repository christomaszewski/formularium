# Stein et al. 1985: in vitro tests of cultivar resistance

## What it holds

- Leaf discs of in vitro plants of 18 cultivars, spores stained with fluorescein diacetate
  and Evans blue: V. vinifera types most susceptible to both mildews; for P. viticola only
  strongly and moderately susceptible groups could be told apart, perhaps because the
  inoculum was too dense.
