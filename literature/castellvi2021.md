# Castellvi & Dugo 2021: a one-source model of sensible heat

Research article, Central Iowa, USA (SMACEX/SMEX02 soybean and corn); Phoenix, Arizona, USA (wheat)..

## What it holds

- A one-source sensible heat flux model (HSR-LST) combining surface renewal and similarity theory with land surface temperature and low-frequency measurements, with a MOST variant.
- It is tested on wheat (Phoenix, AZ) and on soybean and corn (central Iowa) against residual or eddy-covariance H, and compared with one- and two-source models (RMSD 26-46 W m-2).

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Sensible heat from surface renewal and land surface temperature; not a vineyard, not wetness.
