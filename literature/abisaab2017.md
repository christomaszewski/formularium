# Abi Saab et al. 2017: spray deposit on wet, fan-dried and dry vine leaves

A saline-tracer test on wet, air-dried and naturally dry leaves. Deposit on wet leaves was 3.5 times lower than on dry leaves, and the sprayer's air jet removed the surface water.

## What it holds

- **Wet against dry:** the deposit was reduced 3.5 times when the leaves were wet (l. 156-157).
- **Fan air jet:** the air jet from the sprayer fan, at 25 km/h, removed water from the leaves (l. 103-105; abstract l. 20-22).
- **Set-up:** 840 L/ha of water wet the canopy. The spray was 280 L/ha at 900 kPa, with KCl and MgSO4 markers (l. 100-102; l. 107-108; l. 117-119).
- **Replication:** ten replicates per treatment, in a commercial orchard at Marialva, Paraná (l. 90-94). The season is not printed.

## Dependence

- Abi Saab (2002) leaf-area equation, r² 0.97 (l. 127-129).
- Saline conductimetry for deposit, and Tukey's test (l. 135-149).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (spray timing and deposit under dew):** the wet-leaf penalty is a reason to wait for drying, or to dry by fan, in a spray engine. It is one orchard with the season not printed, and the deposit values are in a figure.
