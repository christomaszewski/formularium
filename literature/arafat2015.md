# Arafat 2015: temperature models of powdery mildew germination and penetration in Egypt

Polynomial temperature models of conidial germination and penetration, run on 1997 to 2011 weather from three Egyptian stations.

## What it holds

- **Optimum temperatures**: germination peaks at 24 °C (range 15 to 33 °C) and penetration at 20 °C (range 8 to 35 °C) (l. 116–119).
- **Equations**: GR = −2.641 + 0.256T − 0.00528T² and PR = −0.639 + 0.108T − 0.00254T², with T the mean daily temperature (l. 74, 78). Liquid-water terms GRM and SRM follow (l. 83, 89).
- **Monthly outputs**: expected germination first peaks in March (Alexandria), January (Aswan) and February (Giza). Expected penetration peaks in May (Alexandria) and November (Aswan and Giza) (l. 530–533).
- **Validation**: none. The monthly fits it prints have R² of 0.10 to 0.36 (l. 270, 280, 478). The paper calls for field validation (l. 584).

## Dependence

- Equations from Delp (1954) and Chellemi (1990) (l. 66–67, 72, 81, 87). The optimum range is from Willocquet et al. (1996) (l. 52).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t3 (powdery mildew): the germination and penetration curves could be tried as a temperature-only step in a vineyard simulator. The model has no rain or humidity term beyond the liquid-water terms, and no field check.
