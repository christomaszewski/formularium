# Salotti et al. 2022: temperature responses of Colletotrichum clades

A review of 118 papers gives temperature equations for four Colletotrichum processes, by clade, across 19 species and 27 host plants, grape among them.

## What it holds

- 118 papers picked from 1,641 found, covering 19 Colletotrichum species in eight clades (l. 30-31).
- 27 host plants, grape among them (l. 37).
- Four groups of clades with similar temperature responses (l. 48).
- The equations are fitted per clade and species, not per host. Grape is one host in a mixed set.

## Dependence

- None recorded. The equations are fitted to the review's own database of published values.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (ripe rot and anthracnose). The clade equations give a coarse prior for temperature response on grape, but they are not grape-specific.
- Use for a ripe-rot model only after a check against grape data. Not read in full.
