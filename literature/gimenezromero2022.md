## What it holds

- A risk index per ERA5-Land cell: chronic infection by growing degree-days on a
  piecewise profile (base 12, optimum 28, maximum 35 °C) times winter curing A/(B +
  CDD³). 886 inoculated plants of 36 varieties; US validation 88 % accurate (R0 = 8).
