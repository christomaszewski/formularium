# Battilani and Camardo Leggieri 2015: OTA-Grapes model

A conceptual model of Aspergillus carbonarius growth and ochratoxin A production in grape berries, built from laboratory fits. The authors could not validate it.

## What it holds

- No validation: "model validation was not possible" because the OTA contamination data were poor (l. 36-37).
- Toxin production fitted with the Bete equation on all data gave R2 = 0.54 (Table 1, l. 146; l. 227-228). Two functions for the two strain groups reached R2 above 0.75 (l. 231).
- Table 1 fits run from R2 0.54 to 1 (printed as 1 for two water-activity terms); germination on temperature 0.98 (l. 142-150).

## Dependence

- Structure from the AFLA-maize model for Aspergillus flavus (l. 123).
- Bete equation for temperature responses (l. 172). Wind-borne spores after Kazi et al. (l. 130).
- Lab-derived rate data from Camardo Leggieri et al. (Table 1 note, l. 151).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (other grape diseases: berry rot and toxin). The temperature and water-activity terms are a template for a berry-rot organism.
- The toxin output was never checked against field contamination, so it is a method sketch and not a tool. Not read in full.
