# New Zealand Winegrowers 2023: downy mildew in the industry's biosecurity guide

The industry's identification guide lists downy mildew (Plasmopara viticola) among the diseases
present in New Zealand vineyards (l. 225-226) and describes its first lesions as dark and shiny on
any green part, leaves and young berries especially. No dates, places, seasons or epidemiology.
