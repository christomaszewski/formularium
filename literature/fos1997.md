# Fos et al. 1997: where green leafhopper larvae sit on a vine, and how to sample them

Counts of Empoasca vitis larvae on whole vines in a Bordeaux vineyard, with egg hatching
followed on leaves of different levels in the laboratory, to choose a sampling unit.

## What it holds

- Leaf age decides egg laying; eggs follow a 'false contagion' pattern set by the
  environment; larvae dilute through the canopy over the season whatever the cultivar;
  their distribution is alike on primary and secondary shoots in summer (l. 30-33).
- Recommendation: sample one leaf per vine at cluster level all season (l. 34).
- Around Bordeaux the species has three generations a year, laying in mid-May, late June
  and late July (l. 50).

## Dependence

- None: its own counts.
