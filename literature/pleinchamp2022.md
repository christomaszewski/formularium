# Pleinchamp 2022: trade-press rules for renewing a copper spray after rain and shoot growth

A French agricultural trade article, reprinted from Réussir Vigne, with rules of thumb for renewing copper on vines.

**Read this first.** **Grey literature (trade press):** no author, no data and no source for its figures. These are rules of thumb, not measurements. They resemble the 5 mm and 40 % figures of `molot2004` but are not the same: "totally washed off near 20 mm" here, against about 40 % still on the leaf after 50 mm there. The 20 mm figure reads as a renewal threshold, not a residue.

## What it holds

- **Wash-off (l. 10):** copper wash-off is not linear; the copper on the leaf halves during the first 5 mm of rain, and copper is considered totally washed off at about 20 mm of cumulative rain. No source is given.
- **Growth (l. 11):** "20 cm" of shoot growth should lead to a renewal of the spray (the rule is given as accepted practice).
- **Reason (l. 11):** copper is a contact product and protects only the organs that received product at the last spray, so new organs are unprotected.

## Dependence

- **None stated.** The figures resemble the rain-simulator findings of IFV and ITAB work (summarised in a Chambres d'agriculture deck, not obtained), and `molot2004`; the origin is not shown.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Wash-off per mm (E6; M4):** a trade rule only: half gone by 5 mm, all by 20 mm. Against the measured points in `molot2004`, half is gone by 5 mm only for the Bordeaux mixture (0.51 left), not for Héliocuivre (0.66 left), and 0.36 to 0.43 is still left at 50 mm. Use as the practitioner's renewal trigger, not as a deposit curve.
- **Dilution by growth:** the trigger is 20 cm of shoot growth, a length, not a leaf count; it compares with Caffi & Rossi's three new leaves (`caffi2018`, cited from Kuflik et al.). Dose-efficacy: only the trade statement that a dose above 500 g/ha has no technical or efficacy benefit (l. 18; found by grep in the page text, not among the checked quotes; no source). Decay with time and deposit variability: not addressed. Kind of result: grey trade-press rule, no measurement.
