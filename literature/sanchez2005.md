# Sánchez & Dokoozlian 2005: bud light and temperature against fruitfulness

Field and growth-chamber work on four cultivars shows that shoot light exposure, not individual
  bud light, tracks bud fruitfulness.

## What it holds

- **Design:** Thompson Seedless, Flame Seedless, Chardonnay, Cabernet Sauvignon; bud light
  interception measured weekly April to August and continuously for seven days at the end of
  April (l. 8-13).
- **Result:** potential fruitfulness peaks near one-third of full sunlight in Thompson Seedless
  and Cabernet Sauvignon (l. 15-17); midday light on single buds does not correlate with
  fruitfulness, shoot light does (l. 18-20).
- **Chambers:** maximum fruitfulness at 25 C, much lower at 32 C in Thompson Seedless and at 18
  C in Flame Seedless (l. 20-21).
