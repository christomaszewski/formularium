# Cammalleri et al. 2012: a two-source energy balance without air temperature

Research article, Southern Sicily, Italy (Mediterranean olive orchard)..

## What it holds

- Two ways to run the two-source energy balance (TSEB) model without local air temperature: scene-based internal calibration (TSEB-IC) and the DisALEXI disaggregation of ALEXI air temperature.
- Tested over an olive orchard in southern Sicily with airborne imagery, with a sensitivity analysis.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- TSEB run without in-situ air temperature (scene calibration or DisALEXI), over a Sicilian olive orchard: a method for remote ET, not leaf wetness.
