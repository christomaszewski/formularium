# Salazar-Gutierrez, Hoogenboom & Keller 2016: predicting grapevine stages (project report)

The final report of a Washington State Grape and Wine Research Program grant (AgWeatherNet and IAREC, Prosser), with cooperators Boushey Vineyards and Hogue Ranches.

## What it holds

- Final report (December 2016) to the Washington State Grape and Wine Research Program; PI Salazar-Gutierrez, with Hoogenboom and Keller (l. 4-19).
- Same team and data as the 2015 Zapata thesis: 17 cultivars at Prosser, Washington, 1990-2013 (l. 104).
- Tb per stage and cultivar by the minimum-spread method (l. 138-139); Table 1 gives Tb and DDmin-DDmax. Cabernet Sauvignon: Tb 8.3, 10.4, 12.5 C (l. 153), equal to the thesis.
- Check with 600 dates from other stations: 0.67 (budbreak), 0.89 (bloom), 0.70 (veraison) fall inside the simulated interval, 0.77 overall (l. 193-199). The counts on l. 199-200 do not match.
- Dormancy: forced-budbreak cuttings at 25 C, 2013-2015, Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay (l. 222-225). Release 6 Nov / 29 Oct 2013 (l. 236, 239), a week later than the thesis.
- Chill unit is an hour between a lower (5-7.9 C) and upper (14.5-15.5 C) threshold; requirement 590-600 CU and 320.8-326.5 CU (l. 247, 261).
- No new equations; thresholds are fitted to the authors' own dates. A progress report on the thesis work, so count as the same evidence.

## Dependence

- The same data and base temperatures as Zapata's thesis (Prosser, 1990-2013; l. 104). Degree-day sums and chilling: the engine's forms.
- Hoogenboom and Keller are authors of the engine's Ferguson 2014 model: flags.
