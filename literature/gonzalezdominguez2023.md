# González-Domínguez et al. 2023: plant disease models over fifty years

Review, General (wheat rusts, apple scab); Italian and Spanish authors; no new dataset.

## What it holds

- A review of how plant disease models changed since the mid-20th century, using wheat rusts and apple scab as case studies and ending with multi-model decision tools for IPM.
- It covers sensors and environmental data, controlled-environment methods and data analytics, and includes a generic Weibull temperature-wetness response equation.
