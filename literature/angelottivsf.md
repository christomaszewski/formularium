# Angelotti et al.: downy mildew control rules in the São Francisco Valley

## What it holds

- **Trial:** Petrolina, first-semester seasons of 2007 and 2008. Treatments: the Míldio-VSF
  model, a Lalancette-based rule, the PI-Uva norms, a calendar, and an untreated check. The
  cultivar is printed as 'Crimson Sweet', usually a watermelon.
- **Míldio-VSF's rules are not printed here** (cited to Angelotti et al. 2009). PI-Uva's
  are: leaf wetness over 2 h (cluster) or 4 h (leaf), night temperature below 30 °C, RH
  above 60 % or rain above 10 mm in 48 h, and a curative spray at 2 % of organs showing
  symptoms.
- **Results:** PI-Uva, calendar and Míldio-VSF all beat the check (P = 0.05). PI-Uva saved
  35 % (2007) and 11 % (2008) of sprays. The Lalancette treatment has no results, and the
  weather data are not given.
