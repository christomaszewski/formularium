# Ryu et al. 2024: interpolating temperature in mountains from mixed networks

Research article, Jeju Island, South Korea (2 years of data).

## What it holds

- A deterministic interpolation of 10-min temperature at 100 m resolution that harmonizes heterogeneous networks (official KMA stations plus IoT sites) and uses regression-based radial basis functions with a parameterized nonlinear elevation profile.
- Leave-one-out cross-validation MAE falls from 0.73 to 0.69 degC with all adjusted data, a 4.4% gain over a linear profile and 6.7% over regression kriging.
