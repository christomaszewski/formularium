# MARTRE et al. 2014: multimodel ensembles of wheat growth

A study of 27 wheat growth models tested at four contrasting locations, showing that the mean or median of the model ensemble simulates grain yield and protein better than single models (relative error averaged over models 24-38% for end-of-season variables). Off-topic, though its finding (an ensemble mean beats single models) is the argument for Agrarium's structural ensemble.
