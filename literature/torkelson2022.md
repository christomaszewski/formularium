# Torkelson et al. 2022: wind-tunnel flow across vineyard rows, with gaps of one, two and three canopy heights

A scaled wind-tunnel model of a vineyard canopy measured by particle imaging velocimetry; only the abstract was read.

**Read this first.** Abstract only, a scaled laboratory model, and across-row flow only. It prints no wind speeds.

## What it holds

- A scaled wind-tunnel model of a vineyard canopy with gap spacings of one, two and three canopy heights. A row-normal freestream is applied and velocity is measured on a streamwise-vertical plane centred on one gap (l. 44).
- Mean flow features include an updraft in the centre of the canopy, followed by a descent and recirculation just upstream of the downstream row. They decrease in size and magnitude for larger gaps (l. 44).
- Turbulence in the canopy sublayer is dominated by a growing mixing layer that starts at the top of the upstream row (l. 44). The abstract is cut off after that point.
