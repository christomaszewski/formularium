# Bojkov et al. 2023: downy mildew bunch severity and yield, Vranec

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- One vineyard (Smilica, near Kavadarci), Vranec, 2022: control (sprayed 18 and 22 May only) and standard-programme variants of 30 vines each (l. 78-85).
- Disease severity by ImageJ on bunches, 21 dates from 18 May to 20 July; peak severity 82% on 11 June (l. 214-224).
- Regression of log severity on log incidence: R-squared 0.327, slope 1.118, p = 0.0068, n = 21 (l. 264-274).
- Yield-loss estimate: 1.39 to 1.6 kg per vine for the control and a loss of 2.7 to 2.9 kg per vine, 10,800 to 11,600 kg per ha (l. 299-306), but these numbers do not follow from the regression.
- Standard error reported as 0.3695 in Table 3 and 0.226 in the text (l. 267, 283).
