# Alimad et al. 2017: overwintering of Erysiphe necator in southern Syria

A weekly field survey in 11 vineyards in 2014 and 2015. E. necator overwintered as mycelium in dormant buds, and no ascospores were trapped at the start of the season.

## What it holds

- **Flag shoots:** on 27.4 to 61.9% of vines in 2014 and 5.2 to 40% in 2015 (l. 29-30).
- **Chasmothecia:** seen on leaves in 45.5% of the vineyards, first in July (l. 35-37).
- **Ascospores:** none trapped at the start of the season (l. 37-39).
- **Method:** 11 vineyards at five sites, visited weekly from March to November (l. 79-83). Bud sections and bark washing (l. 143-147).

## Dependence

- Gee et al. 2000 for bud sections (l. 143); Hajjeh et al. 2008 for bark washing (l. 147).
- Pearson and Gadoury 1987 for chasmothecia and flag shoots (background).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t3 (powdery mildew):** the flag-shoot shares and the lack of early ascospores bear on the start of the season. A simulator could place a bud-borne start in its first weeks, but the paper gives no rate, threshold or fitted function.
