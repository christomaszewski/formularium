# Matić et al. 2022: a field LAMP test for flavescence dorée in the vector

A four-primer LAMP assay on crude extracts of single Scaphoideus titanus leafhoppers. It detects flavescence dorée phytoplasma in under half an hour, in the field.

## What it holds

- **Time:** detection in less than half an hour (l. 26-27).
- **Sensitivity:** 2.33 FDp copies, 28 times the real-time PCR limit of 65.7 copies (l. 346-347).
- **Specificity:** 100% (l. 626).
- **Lab infectivity:** 46 of 94 insects positive, 48.94% (l. 451).
- **Field:** adults captured in August and September 2021 at Canale and Giaglione (l. 287). 22 adults were tested, 45.45% positive (l. 492).

## Dependence

- Earlier LAMP protocols for FDp in plants (refs 25 and 35; l. 101-103).
- Real-time PCR as the reference assay (l. 346-347).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t2 (observation) and t6 (regional spread):** a field test for the vector shows where the pathogen is carried. It gives no spread rate.
