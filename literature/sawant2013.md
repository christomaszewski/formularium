# Sawant et al. 2013: warming and anthracnose shifts in Indian grapevines

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Indian grape-growing sites, Solapur (1975-2009) and Ludhiana (1977-2008): annual Tmin rose by -0.08 C at Solapur and +1.70 C at Ludhiana; monsoon Tmin rose at both (l. 71-72, 80).
- Monsoon Tmin increase is significant at both sites (Mann-Kendall, Table 2) (l. 78-81).
- Days above 35 C increased at both sites (l. 20).
- Anthracnose: the pathogen shifted from E. ampelina to Colletotrichum (C. gloeosporioides) from the 1990s, complete by 2010 (l. 60-61).
- Stepwise regression (two years): minimum temperature (12.39%) and leaf wetness (6.72%) contributed to disease index (l. 138).
- The authors link the shift to warming, not a tested causal model (l. 17, 124).
