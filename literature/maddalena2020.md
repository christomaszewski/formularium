# Maddalena et al. 2020: genetic structure of Italian P. viticola

Research article, Italy (12 regions).

## What it holds

- Microsatellite genotyping of 96 Italian P. viticola strains (19 loci kept) shows no clones, evidence of both sexual and asexual reproduction, low genetic diversity and two genetic clusters with a west-east gradient.
- It gives no epidemic formulation.
