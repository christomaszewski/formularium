# European Commission 2023 (Implementing Regulation 2023/138): high-value datasets and their licence

The EU rule that lists "high-value datasets" public bodies must publish openly, weather-station
observations among them. Read because the Catalan open-data portal tags Meteocat's XEMA data as a
high-value dataset (`meteocat.xema`), and Agrarium's D53 is held over Meteocat's terms.

**Read this first.** A legal text read for what it prints, not a legal opinion. Whether and how
it binds a given regional body (and any exemption under Directive 2019/1024's Article 14(5)) is
not settled here.

## What it holds

- **Licence (Article 4(3)):** high-value datasets "shall be made available for re-use under the
  conditions of the Creative Commons Public Domain Dedication (CC0) or, alternatively, the Creative
  Commons BY 4.0 licence, or any equivalent or less restrictive open licence ... allowing for
  unrestricted re-use"; the licensor may additionally require attribution.
- **Meteorological datasets in scope (Annex, 3.1):** "observations data measured by weather
  stations", validated observations (climate data), weather alerts, radar data and NWP model
  data; for station observations, per station at full temporal resolution, all observation
  variables measured.
- **Arrangements (Annex, 3.2):** CC BY 4.0 or an equivalent or less restrictive open licence;
  BUFR, NetCDF, ASCII, CSV or JSON; through APIs and bulk download; automated stations every 5-10
  minutes in real time, hourly unvalidated for all stations.
- **Application (Article 6):** in force on the twentieth day after publication, applying 16
  months after entry into force.

## Dependence

- Adopted under Directive (EU) 2019/1024 (the Open Data Directive). No data.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Agrarium D53 and Cooptera's Meteocat rule (D58):** the Generalitat's open-data portal tags
  the XEMA datasets (`nzvn-apee`, `7bvh-jvq2`) "Dades d'Alt Valor: Meteorologia" and links their
  attribution to the Generalitat's open licence (Llicència oberta d'ús d'informació - Catalunya),
  as it does for the farm declarations (`darp.dun_vineyards`). If XEMA is a high-value dataset
  under this regulation, its station observations are to be reusable under CC BY 4.0 or an
  equivalent licence. That bears on whether numbers fitted from them may leave Chris's machines;
  the decision stays Chris's.
