# Anco, Madden & Ellis 2013: Phomopsis sporulation by temperature and wetness

From the Ohio State University (OARDC, Wooster). Phomopsis on Catawba canes.

## What it holds

- Phomopsis viticola sporulation on infected Catawba canes, Wooster, Ohio; chambers 2009-2011 (l. 68-73).
- Design: 9 temperatures (5 to 35°C), 5 wetness durations (11 to 71 h), 15 canes per cell, 3 repetitions (l. 68-70, 112).
- Response y = log10(conidia per mm2 + 1) (l. 82).
- Model: f = alpha t^beta (1 - t)^gamma W^delta, W in h with no minimum wetness (l. 122).
- Reported as S1 0.967, S2 0.569, beta 2.353, delta 1.033 (l. 400); Tmin 4, Tmax 36°C (l. 22-23, 543). Alpha 0.4896 and gamma 1.963 are not printed; they follow from S1, S2 at (20°C, 35 h) and (12°C, 47 h) (l. 579, 596).
- Optimum near 21°C (l. 274). Beta pseudo-R2 0.89, AIC 100.6, against polynomial 113.8 (l. 288-289).
- Wetness interrupted for 8 h or more cuts sporulation (l. 469). Field: V = 0.0693 per mm rain, Omega = 1.494 (l. 313, 317).
- Dependence: the infected canes were produced after irrigation planned by the Nita warning system, which uses the Erincik Beta infection model (l. 96-97). Tmin and Tmax origin unclear.

## Dependence

- Its own chamber data (2009-2011). The canes were infected after irrigation that Nita's warning system planned, a system built on Erincik et al. 2003's infection model (l. 96-97): a model steered which canes were infected, though not the sporulation it measured. A flag.
- The form is Analytis's (l. 79); α and γ are not printed and follow from its S1 and S2 (derived by the reading agent, not checked).
- Madden and Ellis: flags (D27).
