# Viret et al. 2020: Changins germination within 24 h on 7 May 2020, first oil spots on 25 May

A Swiss vintage review giving the 2020 laboratory maturity date at Changins, the first computed primary infections, and the first oil spots on infected leaves under indicator vines.

**Read this first.**
- The text layer has garbled accents (for example "ë" and "ä" for e-acute and a-grave); the dates and numbers read cleanly.
- The 25 May first spots were on a stock of infected leaves placed under indicator vines close to the ground, not in the vineyard or an untreated control.

## What it holds

- **Oospore maturity (l. 1331):** germination within 24 h was reached at Changins on 7 May 2020.
- **First infections (l. 1331 to 1334):** the first primary infections were computed by Agrometeo on 30 April, when the vine had 5 unfolded leaves (BBCH 15). That is 7 days before the laboratory germination date (arithmetic).
- **First oil spots (l. 1335 to 1338):** the next block of infections, 11 to 14 May, gave the first oil spots at Changins on 25 May, at the end of incubation, on a stock of infected leaves placed under indicator vines near the ground.
- **Lake Geneva vineyards (l. 1339):** only a few scattered oil spots were seen after that (the sentence is cut in the text layer).

## Dependence

- **Own observations** of the Changins group (test and leaf stock); the 30 April infections are Agrometeo (VitiMeteo) output. Dubuis et al. 2012 say the test serves to validate that model.
