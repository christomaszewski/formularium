# Kremneva et al. 2023: a portable spore catcher in winter wheat

A portable spore-catching device sampled four wheat cultivars at five points in 2019 to 2021
  alongside visual disease scoring.

## What it holds

- **Design:** FRCBPP experimental fields, 2019 to 2021, four cultivars, five sampling points,
  one minute a sample (l. 19-26).
- **Result:** spore counts correlate with disease development, r of 0.5 to 0.6 for powdery
  mildew, yellow and brown rust and 0.4 for yellow spot (l. 35-41); cultivar and year effects
  by two-way ANOVA (l. 33-35).
- **Not found:** septoria leaf spot spores in samples (l. 31).

## Dependence

- The FRCBPP portable device and visual disease scales (record).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s6: a measured link between a one-minute in-canopy spore catch and later disease, r about
  0.5. A simulator could take that weak catch-to-disease correlation as an observation model.
