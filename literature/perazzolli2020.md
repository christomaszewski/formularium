# Perazzolli et al. 2020: a rare sugar and grapevine phyllosphere microbes

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Field trials in 2014 at two vineyards, San Michele all'Adige: four blocks of eight plants per treatment (l. 121-122).
- Tagatose at 8 g/l reduced downy mildew severity: 46.0 to 33.6 % in vineyard 1 and 42.0 to 21.2 % in vineyard 2 (l. 286-290).
- Powdery mildew, vineyard 2: 21.4 % untreated against 1.2 % treated (l. 292); none on either treatment in vineyard 1 (l. 293).
- Severity scored on 40 leaves per plot on 19 August 2014 at BBCH 85 (l. 162-164).
- 378,083 bacterial reads; TAG residues still detected on treated leaves (l. 295-297).
- The authors call the result preliminary, from three replicates per condition (l. 508).
