# Buesa et al. 2023: delaying ripening of Macabeo at Requena

Shading nets, double pruning and a pruning-residue mulch on a deficit-irrigated Macabeo
(Macabeu, Viura) vineyard, 2017-2019. Read for vigour and leaf area of a white variety
Agrarium simulates.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 161-190): commercial Macabeo on 161-49C planted 2002, Requena (Valencia),
  740 m; 2.5 × 1.5 m (2,666 vines/ha); bilateral Royat cordon, vertical trellis SW-NE, 16
  buds a vine; Huglin index 2,291 °C; ET0 1,114 mm and rain 402 mm a year (2002-2016); drip
  from early June to end September, volume not printed (regional norm 60 to 100 mm).
  Rain 322, 515, 354 mm and ET0 1,251, 1,144, 1,218 mm in 2017-2019 (l. 217-218).
- **Vigour** (Table 1, l. 413-430): 15.6 to 22.2 shoots a vine; control pruning mass 0.80,
  0.96 and 0.87 kg a vine (2017-2019); double pruning 0.28, 0.32 and 0.68 kg.
- **Disease:** powdery mildew at flowering on double-pruned vines in 2018 (l. 615-616).
- **Not printed:** leaf area, LAI, canopy size, dates.

## Dependence

- Stem water potential baseline from Buesa et al. 2017; ET0 by FAO-56. No model shaped the
  data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Macabeu on a bilateral Royat, no leaf area:** 16 to 22 shoots a vine (control) and
  0.8 to 1.0 kg of prunings. With Smart et al. 1990's cited 50 to 100 cm² of leaf per g of
  cane, that is 4 to 10 m² a vine, a loose bound (my calculation, not the paper's).
