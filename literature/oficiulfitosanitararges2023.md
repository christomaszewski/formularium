# Oficiul Fitosanitar Argeș 2023: vine bulletin no. 15, mildew and moth

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Official Romanian warning bulletin no. 15 of 19 June 2023, Argeș plant-health office (l. 1, 9).
- It recommends treatment for downy mildew, powdery mildew and the grapevine moth (l. 11-13).
- Downy mildew spreads fast at about 20 to 22 C with high humidity (l. 26-27).
- Lobesia: eggs hatch after 6 to 8 days; larval period 25 to 30 days; first-generation moths in early June (l. 42, 47, 49).
- Three products at 2.0 L/ha, 1.5 to 2.0 kg/ha and 1.0 L/ha; treatment after flowering, not during it (l. 59-78).
- No model, weather threshold or infection rule is printed (l. 77).
