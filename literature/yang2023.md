# Yang et al. 2023: real-time PCR of the primary inoculum of P. viticola in Ningxia

From China Agricultural University and Ningxia partners. The published paper and its Research Square preprint (2022) were dropped; one note covers both.

## What it holds

- Ningxia, NW China: soil with leaf residue sampled in early April (45 samples in 2018, 50 in 2019, l. 448); asymptomatic leaves sampled weekly from bloom (360 and 150 samples, l. 456); 9 vineyards in 2018, 10 in 2019 (l. 426), altitude 1100-1200 m, mostly Cabernet sauvignon (Table 1).
- Same assay as Valsesia et al. 2005 (l. 109). Oospores/g come from a spiked-soil curve, R2 0.9775 (l. 129). Detection of purified DNA down to 0.1 pg/ul (l. 138).
- Positive: 60% of asymptomatic leaves, 80% of leaf residues, 100% of soil (l. 24). Counts: 211/360 leaves in 2018 (l. 172).
- Oospores/g in positive soil: 156-872 (2018), 93-1035 (2019); in leaf residue 151-24,744 and 50-28,931 (l. 176, l. 177, l. 169, l. 185, l. 178).
- Previous-year disease progress vs next year: rho 0.643 (leaf-residue MDI), 0.498 (asymptomatic-leaf AUDPC-MDI), 0.595 (AUDPC-DI) (l. 31, l. 312).
- DNA in asymptomatic leaves rose 7-20 days before symptoms (l. 328). Vineyards were sprayed with Bordeaux mixture after BBCH 68 (l. 476).
- Limits: PCR does not separate viable from dead oospores (l. 400); no per-vineyard numbers or weather in the text.
- No equation to adopt; no model used to date the data. The preprint differs: see other_version.

## Dependence

- Valsesia et al. 2005's assay; its own soil, residue and leaf samples (9 vineyards in 2018, 10 in 2019). No model dated the data.
- **The two versions differ:** the preprint gives the first correlation as 0.504 (Pearson), the paper 0.643 (Spearman); the preprint omits Qiuyu Sun from the authors.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Clear: oospore densities in an arid region (156 to over 500 per g of soil), a global range for a truth's inoculum.
