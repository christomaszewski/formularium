# Toda et al. 2020: cut-and-paste synthetic images of barley seeds to train Mask R-CNN, tested on real scans

Builds 1,200 synthetic image-mask pairs by pasting real scanned seed crops onto real scanner backgrounds at random positions and rotations, trains Mask R-CNN on them alone, and tests it on real scans of 20 barley cultivars. No record: a method on seeds, not a canopy or a disease.

**Read this first.** Not 3-D rendering: the seeds carry real textures, so the gap measured is one of arrangement and background, on a flat scanner image. The real test is one scanned image per cultivar, about 100 seeds each (l. 604 to 606), so about 20 images. The strict-threshold score is lower on real images than on synthetic ones, and the authors name leakage for the synthetic figure.

## What it holds

- **Generator** (l. 548 to 562, 607, 610, 622): 400 single-seed images (20 for each of 20 cultivars), background removed with alpha masks and a blurred edge, pasted at random position and rotation on one of four real scanner backgrounds; a paste is cancelled if the overlap ratio exceeds 0.25, up to 70 trials per image; labels come free from the paste coordinates. 1,200 image-mask pairs of 768 x 768.
- **Real samples:** the seed pool holds 400 real seed crops (l. 548 to 549); no real images were used for training except as that pool. A comparison arm trained on the manual annotation of real seeds "showed poor performance", especially for touching seeds (l. 272 to 274; Supplementary Fig. 3, no number in the text).
- **Test** (l. 604 to 606, 622): real scans, one image per cultivar for 20 cultivars (about 100 objects per image), annotated by hand in Labelbox; and 200 synthetic test images not used in training or validation.
- **Scores** (Table 1, l. 230, 242, 246, 359, 385): Recall50 0.95 on the synthetic test and 0.96 on the real average; AP50 0.96 and 0.95; AP@[.5:.95] 0.73 and 0.59; AP75 0.93 and 0.86. The worst real cultivar (H602) has Recall50 0.87, AP50 0.78 and AP75 0.41 (l. 368).
- **Reading** (l. 254, 396): the higher synthetic score "possibly" derives from data leak, since the same seed images appear in the training data; the low real AP at high IoU may come from variation in manual mask annotation; performance depends on the seed image resolution, the variance of the pool and the number of images.
- **Other crops** (l. 19 to 23): the abstract says the approach is effective for rice, lettuce, oat and wheat; no number for them was transcribed.

## Dependence

- Real seed crops from the same cultivars as the test make the generator's pool, so the synthetic data are a recombination of real pixels (the authors place it in sim2real domain randomisation, l. 177 to 180). Matterport Mask R-CNN. Code and data are on a GitHub repository (l. 694).

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **A case where synthetic-only works:** pasting real object crops on real backgrounds, with labels from the paste, gives real-image AP50 of 0.95 with no real-image training. The cases here that fail (`ward2018` on tobacco, `saraceni2025`, `hutter2026`) have the generator further from real pixels. For Agrarium's viewer the lesson is the generator's input: real textures help, rendered ones have to be shown to.
- **Not a canopy:** seeds on a scanner are separable objects in one view; vine leaves overlap, vary in light and sit at several scales.
- **Not shown:** a paired real-only arm with a number, a field image, disease, or a held-out cultivar set (the pool and the test share cultivars).
