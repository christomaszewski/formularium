# Ward et al. 2018: Blender Arabidopsis rosettes mixed 50/50 with 128 real images for leaf instance segmentation (preprint)

Renders 10,000-image sets of Arabidopsis rosettes in Blender, trains Mask R-CNN on real only, synthetic only, or both, and scores it on the held-out sets of the CVPPP Leaf Segmentation Challenge, including a tobacco set. No record: a method; a preprint.

**Read this first.** An arXiv preprint (v3). The generator takes its leaf textures and backgrounds from the real CVPPP training images (l. 162), so the synthetic set is not independent of the real set. Test scores come from the challenge organisers on held-out real sets (A1 to A5), which makes this a stronger test than a split of the training data. The Mean column is the per-image mean across all test sets, not the mean of the row (l. 244 to 246).

## What it holds

- **Generator** (l. 154, 162, 196, 230): one Arabidopsis leaf traced into a 3-D mesh in Blender, randomly scaled along each axis; 30 leaf textures taken from the real CVPPP training images; Blender Cycles with diffuse shading. Three synthetic sets of 10,000 labelled images each: Synthetic-plant (one green texture), Synthetic-leaf (a random green texture per leaf) and Synthetic-COCO (textures sampled from COCO).
- **Real samples** (l. 219, 228): only the 128 CVPPP A1 training images are used (the challenge's A2, A3 and A4 training sets hold 31, 27 and 624). Mixed training uses batches of 50 % real and 50 % synthetic (l. 215). The test sets hold 33, 9, 65 and 168 images for A1 to A4, and A5 combines all (l. 219 to 222, cited from the challenge description).
- **Test** (l. 219): the challenge's held-out real test sets, scored in symmetric best dice (SBD) by the organisers; A3 is tobacco, a different species.
- **Scores, SBD on A1 / A2 / A3 / A4 / A5, mean** (Table 1, l. 251 to 255): real-only (128 images) 87 / 71 / 59 / 73 / 70, mean 71; synthetic-plant only 81 / 69 / 41 / 84 / 73, mean 74; synthetic-leaf only 82 / 74 / 32 / 86 / 75, mean 74; synthetic-COCO only 57 / 48 / 25 / 48 / 43, mean 44; synthetic plus real 90 / 81 / 51 / 88 / 82, mean 81. Which synthetic set went into the mixed run is not stated in the lines read.
- **Where it fails** (l. 262, 392): on the tobacco set real-only is best (59), mixed 51, synthetic-only 32 to 41; the authors read this as the real data holding texture that the renders lack.
- **Reading** (l. 325, 326, 395, 291): synthetic-only models on average beat the real-only model (74 against 71); the mix is best on A1, A2, A4 and A5, which they attribute to real texture plus synthetic geometric diversity; freezing the feature extractor on synthetic data degraded results (l. 291).
- **Challenge ranking** (Table 2, l. 308): 90.0 on A1 and 81.0 on A2, above the published methods listed there at the time; 51.0 on A3.
- **Release** (l. 598, 623): 10,000 top-down 550 x 550 PNG renders with leaf-instance masks on the CSIRO data portal.

## Dependence

- Leaf textures and backgrounds from the real CVPPP images; COCO-pretrained Mask R-CNN (Matterport, ResNet101-FPN per the reading agent). No shared-author flag with the other notes of this strand.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **Mixed beats real-only by 10 SBD points on the mean (81 against 71; arithmetic, not printed), with 128 real images:** the best-supported case in the strand for "synthetic plus a few real images", on challenge sets scored by others. It also shows the limit: a different species, no gain.
- **For Agrarium's viewer renders (D58, D60):** the generator is a hand-traced leaf mesh, scaled at random, textured with real leaf crops; the real texture matters, so a render of the truth's lesions would need real lesion texture to be tested.
- **Not shown:** disease, vines, field images beyond the challenge's lab-like trays, or a repeat run.
