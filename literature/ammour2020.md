# Si Ammour et al. 2020: LAMP for Botrytis in bunch trash and berries

Research article, Laboratory work, Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, Piacenza, Italy (sample origin not read).

## What it holds

- Real-time LAMP assay for detecting B. cinerea in bunch trash and in immature and ripening berries (sensitive to 0.1 ng/µL DNA, detection in under 20 min), tested on artificially colonized material.
- A diagnostic method for latent infection, with no weather-driven formulation.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- Shared authors, flags only (D27): Fedele, Giorgia, as an author of brischetto2021.secondary; Rossi, Vittorio, as an author of brischetto2020.survival, brischetto2021.secondary, caffi2013.sporulation, rossi2008.primary, rossi2008pp.dormancy.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- An observation method (latent infection by LAMP); no field detection rates. A lead for a molecular operator if Botrytis is simulated (M7).
