# Serrano et al. 2012: rainfed Penedès Chardonnay, light interception and water index

Ten rainfed Chardonnay vineyards in DO Penedès (nine in 2008), 2007-2008: the Water Index
and other reflectance indices against yield and berry composition. Read for canopy data.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 102-125): Chardonnay (K5V1) for cava; vines 15 to 22 years old; 2,083 to
  3,704 vines/ha, spacing variable; VSP, double Royat; weeds controlled by ploughing;
  rainfed; mean rain 550 mm, 15 °C. Veraison in the third week of July 2007, ten days later
  in 2008.
- **Season** (Table 2, l. 284-296, page image): growing-cycle rain 510.8 and 533.1 mm, ET0
  851.4 and 767.6 mm; P − ET0 −340.6 and −234.5 mm (l. 453). The 2007 vegetative-period
  P − ET0 is 0.0 mm in the table and about +32 mm in the text (l. 205, 290).
- **Canopy at veraison** (l. 258-260): fIPAR by ceptometer 26 to 61 % (2007, mean 46 %) and
  56 to 74 % (2008, mean 64.0 %).
- **Yield** (Table 3, l. 307-310): 3.41 and 3.77 kg a vine.
- **Downy mildew** in 2008 (l. 561-562): spots becoming necrotic, no severity given.
- **Not printed:** leaf area, LAI, prunings, shoots, spacing.

## Dependence

- Indices of Peñuelas et al. 1993, Rouse et al. 1973, Rondeaux et al. 1996; water status
  from Serrano et al. 2010 on the same vineyards (l. 160). No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **No leaf area.** Rainfed Penedès canopies intercept 26 to 74 % of PAR at veraison,
  varying widely between vineyards and years: the spread the vigour field should carry.
