# Lee et al. 2016: a fuzzy-logic leaf wetness model driven by weather outside the shade, ginseng, Korea

An existing energy-balance-style fuzzy model (Kim et al.) estimates hourly wetness under shade nets from outside weather, and its daily error and its effect on TOM-CAST spray dates are measured.

**Read this first.** The id keeps the 2014 of the DOI (accepted 2014-11-10); the paper is printed in Plant Disease 100(1), January 2016. The crop is ginseng under a 1.8 m shade net, with the wetness sensors under the net and the weather sensors outside it. The text is two interleaved columns; Tables 1-3 are from the page image. No coefficients of the fuzzy model are printed (the appendix is in words).

## What it holds

- **Sites and period:** two commercial ginseng fields, Gochang-gun (G) and Jeongeup-si (J), Jeollabuk-do, 2011 and 2012; May to August or September (l. 93, l. 28; Table 1, page image: 98 and 122 days at G, 98 and 108 at J).
- **Shade:** woven black polypropylene net 1.8 m above the beds, double shading with 3-layer and 2-layer polyethylene nets (l. 103).
- **Weather inputs:** air temperature and RH (Vaisala HMP45) and wind speed outside the shade; 60 min averages in 2011, 30 min in 2012 (l. 70). Wind is scaled from 2 m to the sensor height of 0.3 m with a neutral log profile (l. 125).
- **Reference sensors:** two unpainted flat panel sensors (Campbell Scientific Model 237) per site under the shade (l. 82). An hour is wet when at least one sensor is wet for more than 30 min (l. 83). Wetness from either sensor counts as true wetness (l. 144). Unpainted sensors may miss small droplets (text, cited).
- **Model:** a fuzzy-logic model (l. 28) with net radiation (from air temperature and dew point), vapour pressure deficit and wind as inputs; wet when the output is 0.5 or more (appendix). No physical model was run because no cloud or net radiation was measured (l. 90).
- **Daily error of the fuzzy model (Table 2, page image):** MAE all days 3.9 h (G) and 3.3 h (J); dew days 3.0 and 3.1; rain days 5.6 and 3.5. ME from -2.3 to 2.4 h per day over the four site-years. It overestimated on dew days (night ME above 2.0 h at three site-years) and underestimated on rain days (l. 32; Table 3).
- **RH > 90 % model:** on dew days at J in 2011 its MAE was 18 % lower than the fuzzy model's, and 4 %, 32 % and 46 % higher at the other three site-years (l. 357). Only these percentages are printed; its own MAE is not given.
- **Open-field comparison:** Kim et al. cited: fuzzy MAE 1.1-4.7 h per day over 43 sites (l. 368); the text puts an MAE above 3.4 h (J site) at about a 10 % chance under that distribution.
- **Under the shade:** at night 0.16 C warmer and RH 2.7 % higher than outside (l. 155).
- **Spray dates:** TOM-CAST spray dates from estimated LWD fell within 3 days of those from measured LWD in about 78 % of windows (l. 24).
- **Release:** none stated.

## Dependence

- The fuzzy model is Kim et al. (kim2005, kim2006; Kim et al. 2002 is the CART) and is not re-derived; the RH model is Sentelhas et al. 2008 (sentelhas2008). TOM-CAST follows Hill and Hausbeck 2008. Last author K.S. Kim of the fuzzy model: a shared author, only a flag.
- Not the same form as sentelhas2008.wetness / cooptera.rh90_wetness (energy-balance rules, not a threshold) but the RH > 90 % comparison is that form.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **Station-only model, shade:** a station-only wetness model (temperature, RH, wind) gave daily MAE 3.3-3.9 h against sensors in a ginseng canopy under a net; the error is larger than in open fields, and on rain days it is 3.5-5.6 h. Kind of result: a test of an existing model, two sites, two seasons, no re-fit.
- **RH > 90 % error:** the only numbers are relative (18 % lower to 46 % higher than the fuzzy model's MAE). Not a value for vines.
- **Sensor placement:** the reference sensors are at about 0.3 m under a net, so the result concerns sensor exposure as much as the model. Not shown: any vine or open-field data.
