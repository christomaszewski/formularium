# Buisán et al. 2017: snowfall undercatch of tipping-bucket gauges in Spain

Wind-dependent catch of a heated tipping bucket for snow against a double-fence reference at a Pyrenean site (WMO-SPICE).

**Read this first.** This is solid precipitation. Liquid rain is what matters for Penedès, and liquid was not studied here. Context only.

## What it holds

- **Catch in wind (l. 52):** at 1.5 m/s a heated tipping bucket recorded only 70 % of the reference snowfall; at 3 m/s, 50 %.
- **Catch ratio (l. 294):** at wind below 2 m/s the snow catch ratio was 0.7 to 0.8; it fell below 0.2 above 5 to 6 m/s.
- Both facts are for snow; the reading agent read the abstract only.

## Dependence

- Own data at Formigal-Sarrios in the Pyrenees, against a double-fence reference. None else recorded.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **sense.station_rain_factor (assumed 1.0):** context only. Snow undercatch of 30 to 50 % in light wind does not carry over to liquid rain; Penedès vineyards in the growing season see rain. It shows only that Spain's operational tipping buckets are known to lose much solid precipitation. No liquid value from this paper.
