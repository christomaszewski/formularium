# Calzarano et al. 2022: copper zeolitite vs grapevine downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Two-year trial (2015-2016) in a 16-year-old Montepulciano vineyard at Ari (Chieti), copper chabasite-rich zeolitite against a farm fungicide programme and an untreated control (l. 140-146).
- Zeolitite at 4 kg/ha gives 240 g/ha copper; the farm copper treatment gives 562 g/ha per application (l. 159-161).
- 2015 untreated vines: 86.25% with infected bunches at harvest; treated plots 15% or less (l. 357, 365).
- 2016 untreated vines: 30.36% infected bunches in August, fully infected by 30 September; treated plots at most 1.56% infected bunches (l. 443-450).
- No yield or grape composition differences between treatments; the copper zeolitite raised polyphenols in 2015 only (l. 457, 651-653).
- The authors conclude the product is a low-impact copper option (l. 688-689).
