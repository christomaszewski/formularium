# Rodríguez de Acuña y Pego 2011: downy mildew of the vine, technical leaflet

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Canary Islands leaflet (Cabildo Insular de Tenerife), April 2011, on downy mildew of the vine (l. 12).
- Primary infection: above 10 to 12 °C and at least 10 mm of rain over 1-2 days (l. 36-37).
- Secondary infection: wetting above 95% for 2-4 h, above 12 °C (optimum 18-25 °C) and 4 h of darkness (l. 44-45).
- The 3-10 rule: shoots at least 10 cm, rain above 10 mm, at least 10 °C (l. 54).
- Treatment: flowering is the critical stage, with a spray recommended at flowering regardless of risk (l. 136); at most three treatments a year to delay resistance (l. 216).

## Dependence

- The leaflet states the 3-10 rule (l. 49-54) and the primary and secondary infection thresholds (l. 36-46: 10 to 12 °C with 10 mm rain over 1-2 days; leaf wetting or humidity above 95% for 2-4 h, above 12 °C, 4 h of darkness).
