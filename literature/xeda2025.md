# Xeda Italia 2025: a copper maker's rain test on potted vines, copper left and efficacy after 0 to 50 mm

A slide deck by the maker of Microsap Cu Plus (5 % copper as tribasic copper sulphate on a carrier) reporting copper left on leaves and efficacy against downy mildew after 0 to 50 mm of artificial rain, against copper oxide and Bordeaux mixture.

**Read this first.**
- **Grey literature: a manufacturer's own test of its own product**, at an unnamed test centre, not peer-reviewed. Replication of the copper assay, rain intensity, drop size, leaf age and sampling are not stated. The title slide is dated Bologna 2025-02-20 (l. 7).
- The text layer prints the copper-loss values unlabelled. The assignment of each number to a product is by bar and legend position in the page images (slides 21 and 22). The 40 and 50 mm values and all efficacy values are image-only, read by eye by the reading agent.
- The three products are at different doses (107.6, 450 and 240 g Cu/ha), and loss is relative to the same product with no rain (l. 539). The maker's "no loss up to 20 mm" for Microsap (l. 568) is rounded: its own chart shows 6.16 % copper loss at 20 mm.
- The product order disagrees with `molot2004`: here Bordeaux mixture loses 36 % at 10 mm and copper oxide 6 %, while Molot found the Bordeaux mixture only slightly worse than Héliocuivre. Product rank is not stable across the two sources.

## What it holds

- **Design (l. 407, 435, 447):** a trial at an unnamed test centre with special equipment (l. 407); five potted vines per product per rain amount, with rain amounts 0, 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50 mm (l. 435). Protocol: copper sprayed at T0; artificial rain starts at T0 + 6 h; inoculation at T0 + 24 h with 5 x 10^4 sporangia/mL; scoring at T0 + 21 d (l. 447).
- **Products and doses (l. 423, 425, 427):** Microsap Cu Plus 500 mL/hL, label dose, 107.6 g Cu/ha in 400 L/ha (l. 423); copper oxide 75 WG at 150 g/hL, 450 g Cu/ha (l. 425); Bordeaux mixture at 300 g/hL, 240 g Cu/ha (l. 427).
- **Copper lost, % of leaf copper against the same product with no rain (l. 539):**
  - Bordeaux mixture: 35.96 % at 10 mm (l. 554), 49.25 % at 20 mm (l. 555), 59.41 % at 30 mm (l. 556), then 61.49 % at 40 mm and 67.55 % at 50 mm (image-only).
  - Copper oxide 75 WG: 6.39 % at 10 mm (l. 552), 17.98 % at 20 mm (l. 554), 50.42 % at 30 mm (l. 558), then 62.3 % at 40 mm and 66.38 % at 50 mm (image-only).
  - Microsap Cu Plus: 0.48 % at 10 mm (l. 550), 6.16 % at 20 mm (l. 553), 16.63 % at 30 mm (l. 557), then 36.69 % at 40 mm and 48.92 % at 50 mm (image-only).
- **Efficacy against the pathogen on leaves (page 18 chart, image-only, read by eye; l. 456 is its title):** copper oxide 90.6 % at 0 mm to 80.4 % at 50 mm; Microsap 85.0 % to 71.2 %; Bordeaux mixture 82.0 % to 66.0 %. Protection fell by 10 to 16 points (arithmetic, not printed) while leaf copper fell 49 to 67 %, so efficacy is not proportional to residue.
- **Maker's conclusion (l. 568):** up to 20 mm of rain no loss of efficacy or of copper for Microsap.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Wash-off per mm of rain (E6; M4):** potted vines, three copper products, copper left on the leaf after rain 6 h after spraying. The products differ greatly: at 10 mm 36 % lost (Bordeaux) against 6 % (copper oxide) and 0.5 % (Microsap); at 50 mm 66 to 68 % lost for the two conventional products and 49 % for Microsap. The copper-oxide and Microsap curves start slowly (6 % and 0.5 % lost at 10 mm) and then steepen, unlike the early loss in `molot2004`, so product (and possibly unstated rain intensity) matters. Grey source: use as a sensitivity range, not a value.
- **Dose-efficacy:** efficacy between about 66 and 91 % across 0 to 50 mm while leaf copper falls by up to two thirds, so residue and efficacy are loosely coupled; doses differ per product, so no dose curve can be drawn. Decay with time, growth dilution and deposit variability: not addressed (single 6 h drying time). Kind of result: manufacturer measurement on potted vines, partly image-only.
