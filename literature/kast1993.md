# Kast & Walter 1993: a trap for infective sporangia, and when they were caught

## What it holds

- A trap that washes sporangia from air and from rain splash into water, which is put on
  leaf discs: only infective sporangia give symptoms.
- A prototype in a vineyard at Weinsberg from July 1992 (infection rising to 100 % of leaves
  by mid-September), emptied every 2 h: infective sporangia caught 58 times; 34 in periods
  when, by Bläser (1978), fresh sporangia should be present, and 24 when the warning devices
  (Biomat, Lufft HP-100, both based on Bläser 1978) said none could be infective.

## Dependence

- Tests the Bläser-based devices' survival in the field; no model of its own.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- **Field evidence on survival:** 24 of 58 catches of infective sporangia came when the
  Bläser-based devices predicted none: in the field, sporangia lived longer than those
  devices allowed, the opposite of Kennelly et al. 2007's hot, dry days. Sent to Agrarium
  (2026-10-09) for D30.
