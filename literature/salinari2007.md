# Salinari et al. 2007: an empirical model of outbreak dates

Not the 2006 *Global Change Biology* paper (Salinari et al. 2006, GCB 12:1299-1307), which
it cites.

## What it holds

- **Data:** first-outbreak dates at Spresiano, northern Italy, 1980-2005 (26 years), in
  three groups: I on or before 19 May (the 25th percentile), II 20 May-9 June, III after
  9 June; 8, 11 and 7 years.
- **Model:** discriminant functions of January-April mean temperatures and March-April
  rain variables; the shift in days D = −6.061 − 3.686·DF1 (R² 0.62). 88.5 % right on the
  fitted years, 70 % leave-one-out. A warmer April and longer wet spells in March give
  earlier outbreaks.
- **Checks by the reading agent:** the worked example reproduces DF1 but not DF2 (−1.98
  against −3.19 printed), so a coefficient or the example is misprinted. Spresiano's
  coordinates are printed impossibly (44°63′N).
- The climate-change projections for other sites are model outputs, not data.

## Dependence

- Fitted to field onset dates and monthly weather; no engine equation or window.
  Rossi is a co-author (a flag).

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- A season-scale onset pattern for one Italian site, usable as a weak check on a truth's
  onset dates under different springs. Not a process formulation.
