# Bonada et al. 2022: Shiraz phenology across Barossa sub-regions

A bioRxiv preprint on 24 Barossa vineyard sites over three vintages, with phenology compared between sub-regions on calendar and thermal-time scales.

**Read this first.** A preprint, not peer reviewed. The reading covered the phenology spread only. The differences are between sub-regions and sites, not within a block.

## What it holds

- **Spread across sites (l. 367):** across 24 sites and three vintages, median budburst (E-L 4) was day 75; 80 % of the sites reached it between day 69 (10th percentile) and day 88 (90th percentile), a span of 19 days (arithmetic).
- **Eden Valley (l. 385-387):** on a calendar scale development was slower in the Eden Valley than in the other environments: the timing of key stages diverged by 6 to 11 days for budburst (l. 386) and 7 to 14 days for flowering and veraison (l. 387). Eden Valley is the cooler, higher sub-region (reading agent).
- **Northern Grounds (l. 387-389):** faster than Eden Valley and slower than the other sub-regions, by 1 to 7 days from budburst to 24 °Brix (text, same passage).

## Dependence

- Own observations: 24 sites, three vintages, Barossa Zone. None recorded otherwise.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **host.vine_sd_days (1.5 days) and host.hollow_delay_days_per_m (0.5 day/m):** not a test of either. The 6 to 11 day gap at budburst is regional (between sub-regions and sites), not within a block and not tied to hollow depth.
