# Sikoparija et al. 2025: a Hirst-type sampler flown on an ultralight aircraft

FlyHirst, a light Hirst-type volumetric spore trap on an ultralight aircraft, recorded pollen
  and fungal spores up to 2100 m above ground in six summer flights over Denmark.

## What it holds

- **Instrument:** a portable Hirst-type sampler optimised for size, weight, power and cost,
  with other instruments on the aircraft; flight time links sample time to 3D position
  (abstract, l. 25-32).
- **Result:** six summer flights; Urtica pollen up to 1300 m above ground, fungal spores up to
  2100 m (l. 32-35); spore diversity matched the season (l. 33).
- **Not checked:** accuracy against a reference sampler; the paper calls it a proof of concept.

## Dependence

- Follows the principles of the original Hirst volumetric sampler (l. 19-27).
