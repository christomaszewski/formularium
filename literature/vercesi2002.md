# Vercesi et al. 2002: azoxystrobin and oospore germination timing

## What it holds

- Riesling Italico at Vescovera (Pavia), 1996-1998; azoxystrobin at 25 g ai/hl at the end of
  August and mid-September. Treated leaves had fewer oospores, germinating less: inoculum
  potential down 91.88 % (1997) and 95.41 % (1998).
- Untreated oospores: no germination until December; most at the end of March; breakdown
  from the end of April. Treated ones peaked in mid-January.

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Germination timing of untreated oospores in Lombardy over two winters: a pattern for the
  truth's oospore maturation.
