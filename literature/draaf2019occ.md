# DRAAF Occitanie 2019: a leaflet quoting the 2016 French phytosanitary survey, with 12 pesticide treatments a year in the Pyrénées-Orientales

A two-page official leaflet introducing the 2019 vineyard practices survey and quoting results of the 2016 phytosanitary survey by basin.

**Read this first.** Grey literature: a leaflet of the regional statistics office, not a study. The counts are all pesticide treatments (fungicides, insecticides and others), not downy mildew passes; the downy mildew count for the same basin is in `draaf2021occ`. The digits are garbled in the text layer: "1 2", "2 0" and "2 6" are printed with spaces and are read as 12, 20 and 26 (l. 15-16); this reading agrees with the later bulletin (three downy mildew passes in the same basin). The same leaflet prints the year as "201 6" and "201 9" with a space.

## What it holds

- **Treatments in 2016:** the number of phytosanitary treatments (all pesticides) a year varies from 12 on average in the Pyrénées-Orientales basin to 26 in the Gers basin; 20 nationally (l. 15-16; digits garbled in the layer, read as 12, 26 and 20).
- **Climate reading:** the Mediterranean rim has drier climates, giving lower disease pressure and so fewer treatments (l. 21).
- **Under-dosing:** in southern vineyards downy mildew pressure is lower and under-dosing is more frequent, notably in the Pyrénées-Orientales basin (l. 41).

## Dependence

- None recorded. It summarises the Agreste 2016 phytosanitary survey; no model, no equations. The later bulletin `draaf2021occ` is the same survey in detail.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Sprays per season (spray.per_season):** 12 treatments of all pesticides in the Pyrénées-Orientales basin in 2016, the lowest of the Occitanie basins, against 20 nationally. Kind: official survey of declared practice, 2016, Roussillon (next to the Empordà). Not a downy mildew count: use the three downy mildew passes of `draaf2021occ` for that.
- **Dry-site anchor:** the leaflet states that drier Mediterranean climates mean fewer treatments (l. 21, 41). Supports a lower count for a drier site; it gives no figure for the Penedès.
- **Not shown:** timing, delay, compliance.
