# Sáenz 2005: vineyards and landscape change in the Alt Penedès pre-coastal range

A Catalan geography paper on how vineyards changed the landscape of the Alt Penedès range and its surroundings.

**Read this first.**
- The paper is in Catalan; quotes are glossed. It is a landscape description, not a measurement paper, and gives no parcel areas in the passages read.

## What it holds

- **Slope zones (l. 150):** in the north of the range slopes are mostly over 35 %, and the Pre-coastal Depression has slopes of 10-20 %; both are mainly forest.
- **Where vines are:** farming, led by vines, concentrates where slope is least, 0 to 10 %, on marls and clays (l. 152: "la zona on hi ha menys pendent", the zone with least slope).
- **Old parcels:** parcels were small ("Les dimensions de les parcel·les eren petites") but with a dense planting (l. 694). No area is printed.
- **Enlarged parcels:** parcels were later enlarged by large levelling operations ("grans processos d'esplanació i anivellament") (l. 708); the reading agent places this from the 1990s.

## Dependence

- None recorded. Landscape description; no model.

## Bearing (2026-11-04)

- **Site layout (Agrarium):** the vineyard parcels of the Alt Penedès were small and have been enlarged by levelling. Qualitative evidence, no sizes.
- **terrain.slope_pct:** vines sit on the 0-10 % slope zone here, lower than the 4-20 % of Pla & Nacci (pla2001) for cropped fields; the two are different areas and different definitions (landscape zones against cropped fields).
