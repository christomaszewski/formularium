# Stover et al. 2004: copper spray timing for melanose in Florida grapefruit (abstract)

An abstract from an ASHS abstract book. A three-year grapefruit trial compares sprays timed by a copper-residue model with sprays on a three-week calendar.

## What it holds

- **Trial (l. 4003-4004):** three years in mature grapefruit groves near Ft. Pierce, Florida. Airblast at 1180 L/ha.
- **Result (l. 4013-4014):** in no year was there a significant difference in interior-fruit marking between model and calendar timings.
- **Exterior fruit (l. 4016-4018):** in 2 of 3 years the unsprayed checks had less marking than copper-treated fruit, so copper injury, not melanose, dominated outside.

## Dependence

Builds on an earlier computer model that estimates copper on fruit and says when to reapply (l. 3998-3999).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t5, as listed.** Off the point (class C): citrus melanose on grapefruit, not grapevine disease. The model is for copper on fruit, so it offers no vine input.
