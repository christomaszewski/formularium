# Cola et al. 2014: a weather-based model of Barbera phenology, canopy and source-sink balance

A daily whole-vine model for Barbera at Piacenza: phenology as a heat sum (normal heat hours)
to stage thresholds, leaf area per shoot from stage and shoot count, light interception from
canopy size and porosity, a carbon balance shared out by source-sink rules, a water balance,
and yield. Inputs in the minimum mode are daily Tmax, Tmin and precipitation (l. 49).

## What it holds

- **Site and data** (l. 35-38, 111-143, 226-277): Barbera clone AT 84 on 420A, planted 1999,
  Piacenza (45.1 N, 9.6 E). Low density (LD, cane-pruned Guyot, about 10 shoots per m of row)
  and high density (HD, mechanically pre-pruned spur cordon, about 60). BBCH scored every
  3-4 days, 2011-2012; five shoots sampled destructively on 12 dates in 2011; light
  interception on 8 days in 2011 (3,480 line-bar readings a date). Validation 2003-2007 on
  other vines of the same vineyard (Bernizzoni et al. 2009), weather from the ARPA-SIM
  station at Pianello Val Tidone, 6 km away.
- **Phenology** (l. 313-359; Eqs 7-11; Fig. 4): Iphen's NHH threshold method (Mariani et al.
  2013); a stage is reached when cumulated normal heat hours pass a threshold. The Barbera
  thresholds are curves in Fig. 4 only (reproductive from about BBCH 52 near 100 NHH to BBCH
  89 near 1,900 NHH; vegetative from BBCH 101 near 120 NHH to BBCH 127 near 1,050 NHH), read by
  eye, not printed. A delay (PhDel) multiplies the vegetative thresholds in HD only: Eq. 8
  has base 0.08 (read in the page image), Eq. 10 gives the maximum 1 + (0.01 Nshoots - 0.1)
  for 20-65 shoots, Eq. 11 gives 1.55 above 65 shoots. Calibration MAE 0.56-1.12 BBCH
  (Table 4, R² 0.98-0.99).
- **Leaf area per shoot** (Eqs 12-13, p. 122, read in the page image): primary leaves LA1 and
  lateral leaves LA2 as quadratics in BBCHveg (101-127) and Nshoots (clamped 10-65), R² 0.99
  and 0.98, in m² a shoot. Vine leaf area is shoots times (LA1 + LA2) (Eq. 1). Leaf area from
  leaf weight: LA1 = 43.34 LW1 - 50.99, LA2 = 44.12 LW2 + 70.34 (Eqs 2-3, R² 0.95); leaf
  weight from leaf number (Eq. 4, R² 0.94). All fitted on the 2011 sampling.
- **Canopy** (l. 385-408; Eq. 15; Table 1): width and height ramp linearly from BBCH 101 to BBCH
  77 (berries touching) and then hold; minimum and maximum measured in the calibration years
  (LD 2011: width 0.3-0.8 m, height 0.2-1.2 m; HD: 0.2-0.6 m and 0.2-1.0 m). Porosity =
  0.6417 - 0.33 Wx (width in m, R² 0.82). Light interception is a modified Oyarzun et al.
  2007 (not printed). Absorbed PAR is 0.50 of global radiation times interception (Eq. 16).
- **Carbon, water, yield** (Eqs 17-39, Tables 2, 10): daily gross assimilation by Van Keulen
  & Wolf 1986 interpolation, CO2 factor 1.19 (Penning de Vries et al. 1989), respiration, dry
  matter shares to clusters by stage (Table 2), sink strength by cluster number, ET0 by
  Penman-Monteith, kc from light interception, a water-limitation factor and a soil
  balance. Not recorded here beyond this line: none of it is a leaf-area or phenology piece.
- **Results** (l. 623-760; Tables 4-9): leaf area at BBCH 65 was 4.8 m² a vine (HD) and
  1.2 m² (LD) in 2011, 4.0 and 1.1 in 2012; about 8 m² (HD) and 3 m² (LD) before trimming.
  Validation: final leaf area R² 0.92 (HD) and 0.63 (LD, four points, none in 2005); yield R²
  0.96 (LD) and 0.96 (HD, Table 9; the abstract says 0.94).
- **Unusable as printed** (the reading agent's "garbled", checked on the page images):
  - Eq. 39 (thermal limitation): -2.5105e-4 AT³ + 1.1622e-3 AT² - 0.1036 AT + 0.2437 is
    negative at every temperature from 7 to 40 °C (-0.51 at 7, -5.81 at 25.5, -12.72 at 35 °C).
  - Eq. 32 (water factor): printed as a product in mm, not a 0-1 ratio.
  - Eqs 20-21: sine argument printed 2π × 365 × (DOY - c); 2π/365 would give a yearly cycle.
  - Eq. 24: RM called dimensionless but multiplies TDW (g m^-2) in Eq. 23.
- **Other disagreements:** Table 1 spacing 2.2 × 1.8 m against 2.5 × 1.2 m in the text
  (l. 122, 169-170); C0 320 ppmv in the text and 310 in Table 10; beta 0.88 and 0.8; Eq. 1
  in m² a vine and Table 10 in m² per m of row; Table 2 shares (1, 1, 70, 5, 93 %) do not sum
  to 100; the Eq. 4 square is printed LN2, which Table 10 uses for lateral leaves; LA/Y
  threshold printed in kg/m² (l. 749). The NHH cardinal temperatures (7, 25.5 and 35 °C,
  cited to Mariani et al. 2012) and base are not printed as the NHH's own numbers.

## Dependence

- **Borrowed:** Iphen's NHH phenology (Mariani et al. 2013) with Barbera thresholds set from
  time shifts against Iphen's Cabernet Sauvignon reference, using the Susegana 1965-1970
  collection data (no citation, l. 336-347); carbon balance after Poni et al. 2006 (a new
  simplified version in STELLA, which equations carried over is not stated) with Van Keulen &
  Wolf 1986 and Lakso & Johnson 1990 coefficients; light after Oyarzun et al. 2007; water
  thresholds from Lebon et al. 2003 and FAO-56; Riou et al. 1989 (kc); Castelan-Estrada
  et al. 2002 (partitioning).
- **Calibrated with:** the Barbera thresholds rest on Iphen's Cabernet dates, which shaped them.
- **Data:** calibration data are the authors' own observations; validation data are from
  Bernizzoni et al. 2009. Neither is model output. Shared authors (Mariani, Cola) with the
  Iphen papers are a flag only.
- **Extends** Schultz 1992's and Bindi et al. 1997's leaf-area models (cited).

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **Not a truth candidate:** the most complete whole-vine model read, but its phenology is the
  engine's form (a heat sum to thresholds), and its growth clock is that phenology: Eqs 12-13
  take BBCHveg as input, so in the paper's own run the clock is the heat sum. Said so, not
  hidden.
- **Usable as published values:** leaf area per vine at flowering (1.2-4.8 m²); the
  leaf-area-per-shoot surface by stage and shoot count (Eqs 12-13); porosity 0.64 - 0.33 ×
  width; a canopy ramp from first leaf to berry touch between the Table 1 bounds.
- **Three printed equations (20-21, 32, 39) are unusable as printed.**

## Bearing (2026-10-20, vine leaf area)

Re-read by the main session (l. 120-135, 632-690 of a fresh text copy):

- **Frame** (l. 120-133): 2.5 × 1.2 m (3,333 vines/ha; Table 1's 2.2 × 1.8 m disagrees);
  cordon at 0.9 m, moveable wire pairs at 1.2 and 2.0 m; trimmed once, on 14 June 2011 and
  5 July 2012, when shoots outgrew the top wires; HD kept 8-9 spurs a vine. Irrigation is
  not stated.
- **Leaf area** (l. 637-641): at full flowering 4.8 (2011) and 4.0 m² (2012) in HD, 1.2 and
  1.1 m² in LD; before trimming about 8 and 3 m². No significant lateral regrowth after
  trimming (l. 677-679). The unit is "m² LA" a vine in the text and m² per m of row in
  Table 5: unsettled, as above.
- **For Agrarium:** LD (about 10 shoots per m, so about 12 a vine) reaching about 3 m² before
  trimming is 0.25 m² a shoot; at flowering it had 40 % of that, HD 60 %.
