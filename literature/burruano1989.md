# Burruano et al. 1989: oospore germination in seven Italian vineyards

## What it holds

- Oospores from seven localities, collected in autumn 1984: Balestrate (Palermo),
  Scillichenti and Milo (Catania), Valenzano and Locorotondo (Bari), Monterotondo and
  Velletri (Rome). Half overwintered at the site, half at Palermo, in buried pots;
  germination tested at 20-22 °C in late February and March 1985.
- Site-wintered oospores mostly germinated more: Valenzano 65.4 % (27 February), 31.3 % and
  6.0 % later; Locorotondo 53.7 %, 48.0 %, 21.4 %; Velletri 55.2 % (12 March); Monterotondo
  47.8 % (12 March); Balestrate 24.2 %, 14.8 %, 1.2 %.
- Exceptions: Scillichenti and Milo (Catania) germinated less at home than at Palermo (Milo
  at Palermo 41.3 % on 11 March).
- The authors attribute the site effect to local soil and climate together.
