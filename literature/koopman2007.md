# Koopman et al. 2007: genotypes in two Western Cape vineyards

A Mediterranean-type climate, wet early and hot and dry late in the season, with an
organic and a sprayed vineyard compared over two seasons. Its patterns sit beside the
European ones of [gobbin2006](gobbin2006.md) and Rumbou & Gessler 2004.

## What it holds

- **Sites:** two Red Globe table-grape vineyards near Paarl, about 7 km apart, spaced
  2 × 3 m.
  - **Organic:** 12 rows × 30 vines, copper and sulphur alternating weekly with compost
    tea, late August to early January.
  - **Conventional:** 8 rows × 20 vines, five or more fungicides.
  - **Seasons:** 2004/05 and 2005/06, 3-4 samplings each.
- **Sampling:** every lesion at low incidence, otherwise two per every second vine. Four
  microsatellites (GOB, CES, ISA, BER).
- **Weather:**
  - **Onset:** symptoms appeared in early to mid October, after heavy September rain.
  - **Rain events in the survey:** 13 in 2004/05, 7 in 2005/06.
  - **Mean temperature and RH, start / middle / end of season:**
    - 2004/05: 18 °C and 90 %; 22.2 °C and 62 %; 24.1 °C and 50 %;
    - 2005/06: 18.1 °C and 75 %; 26.5 °C and 50 %; 31.2 °C and 36 %.
  - **End of season:** 60-90 % of vines heavily infected in the organic vineyard (method
    not given).
- **New genotypes throughout the season:** after the first sample, which is all new by
  construction, new (oosporic or immigrant) genotypes made 30-74 % of lesions in the
  organic vineyard and 12-39 % in the sprayed one. The two sources can't be told apart.
- **Dominance:** "dominated by one or two genotypes".
  - The predominant genotype made 0-44 % of a sample in the organic vineyard and 14-67 %
    in the sprayed one (Table 1).
  - The abstract's "14-67 %" is the sprayed vineyard's range.
  - Most genotypes caused no more than about five lesions per sampling.
- **Survival between seasons:** ten genotypes were found in both seasons (Table 3). The
  authors suggest overwintering as mycelium in buds or leaves, untested. Oospores alone
  can't carry a genotype over.
- **Sprays:**
  - The sprayed vineyard had higher predominant shares and fewer new genotypes, and no
    living lesions from December to February.
  - Whether the fungicides selected this or it happened by chance is left open.
- **Genetics:**
  - Random mating, apart from null alleles at BER.
  - 41 % of genotypes had three GOB alleles, suggesting raised ploidy.
  - AMOVA puts 95 % of variation within populations.
- **Inconsistent in the text:**
  - The abstract's Fst "0.004 and 0.016" matches no table; Table 5 gives 0.04-0.06.
  - Lesion totals differ between sections (530/340 and 521/341).
  - Some of Table 3's percentages don't match its counts.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- **Closest climate to Penedès of the genotype surveys:** wet, cool spring; hot, dry
  summer.
- **For history matching, a dry-climate epidemic structure:**
  - new genotypes keep arriving all season;
  - one or two clones dominate, more so under sprays;
  - most genotypes leave a handful of lesions.
- **A gap in the truth's overwintering:** genotypes surviving from one season to the next
  point to carry-over other than oospores in a mild winter. Sarejanni 1951 found winter
  conidia made no mildew years in Attica ([sarejanni1951](sarejanni1951.md)). The truth
  assumes oospores only.
