# Kamimori 2025: a review of Delaware's spring phenology at Osaka, 1963-2010

An invited review of the Osaka institute's work on the 'Delaware' hybrid: a 48-year
series of budbreak and bloom with trends on year, and a survey of the model forms (heat
sums, development rate, PhenoFlex, PLS windows) whose parameters are in the cited
papers, not here.

## What it holds

- **Series** (Fig. 2, p. 3; from Kamimori et al. 2019): Osaka 1963-2010, annual mean
  temperature y = 0.04x − 65.94 (r² 0.61), budbreak day y = −0.15x + 398.56 (r² 0.23),
  full bloom y = −0.16x + 466.56 (r² 0.19), x the year (l. 204-211). No regression on
  temperature is printed. Delaware is Vitis vinifera × (V. labrusca × V. aestivalis)
  (l. 74).
- **Forms surveyed** (Table 1, p. 5): heat sums from a fixed start; the development-rate
  (DVR) form after de Wit et al. 1970 for Delaware (budbreak from a fixed start,
  flowering from budbreak; Kamimori et al. 2020); PhenoFlex (chill and heat
  overlapping from late January; Kamimori & Hosomi 2025); BRIN and GFV for European
  cultivars (l. 221-245, 324-354). No parameter or RMSE is printed; predictions go to
  growers 2-3 weeks ahead (l. 360-367).
- **PLS windows** for Delaware: chilling mid-October to early February, forcing from
  mid-January to budbreak (l. 394-395); at least 15 years of records needed (l. 386).
- **Position:** grapevines' low chilling need means warming autumns delay budbreak
  little and spring heat dominates (l. 134-155); inadequate chilling is projected in
  Japan after 2050 under RCP8.5 (l. 274). AMGD-NARO 1 km grids give 26-day forecasts
  and scenarios to 2100 (l. 48-56).
