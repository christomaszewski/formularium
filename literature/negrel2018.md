# Negrel et al. 2018: lipid markers of P. viticola infection in grapevine

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Non-targeted UHPLC-HRMS on P. viticola sporangia finds EPA and AA glycerides and C16:1 ceramides, not found in healthy leaves (l. 22-28).
- Seven major lipids were followed in leaf discs: AA, DAG, EPA, DEPG, TEPG, Cer(d16:1/18:0) and Cer(d16:1/20:0) (l. 183-185).
- Discs were kept at 21 °C with 16 h light and sampled at 0, 24, 48, 72 h and 6 days (l. 81-82).
- The lipids are present 24 h after inoculation, long before symptoms at about 4 days (l. 390, 468-470).
- Lipid markers correlate with image sporulation, p below 10^-9 (l. 360-361); EPA and AA correlate best (l. 364).
- Syrah accumulates more lipid than Bianca (l. 501-502); V. riparia is higher at 24 h (l. 473).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Lipid markers rise by 24 h after inoculation, so they could serve as an early pathogen-biomass check for a truth, but the data are controlled-chamber leaf discs and plants, not field observations, so the paper gives no dataset.
