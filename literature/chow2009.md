# Chow & Street 2009: LES turbulence closures over Askervein Hill

Large-eddy simulation of near-neutral flow over Askervein Hill with a dynamic reconstruction model and a mixed model, compared with the 1983 field data (l. 20-45). A research turbulence-model study; no record.

## What it holds

- **Test:** the 1983 Askervein field campaign, near-neutral stratification and steady wind (l. 27-30).
- **Result:** the dynamic reconstruction model improves flow speed-up and especially turbulent kinetic energy against the standard TKE-1.5 model (l. 34-36); a simpler mixed model gives good mean flow and turbulence (l. 41-43).
- **Cost:** unsteady simulations with inflow from a separate turbulent database (l. 31-33); no run-time figure was located.

## Dependence

- Builds on the authors' earlier dynamic reconstruction model, explicit filtering and the 1983 Askervein data.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s3 (terrain and canopy physics):** context only. LES over a hill is too costly for thousands of seasons; a simulator could take the speed-up pattern over a hill as a check on a cheaper terrain wind factor.
