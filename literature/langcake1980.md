# Langcake & Lovell 1980: light and electron microscopy of P. viticola infection

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Greenhouse V. vinifera (Cabernet Sauvignon) and V. riparia leaves inoculated with about 5 x 10^4 sporangia per ml and held at about 23 °C, 100% RH (l. 59-64).
- Infection begins only from zoospores, released within 30 min at room temperature; each zoospore gives one germ tube (l. 107, 112, 122).
- The first haustorium forms within 3.5 h; little growth follows for 12 to 15 h (l. 130, 147).
- Sporulation on V. vinifera is about 90 h (l. 177), given as 96 h (l. 66) and 4 to 5 days (l. 483).
- On V. riparia, infection matches V. vinifera to about 18 h, then stalls; browning appears at 24 to 48 h (l. 521, 531-532).
- The phytoalexin claim rests on work 'in preparation' (l. 561).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A 1980 laboratory record of the infection stages, with a first haustorium at 3.5 h and sporulation at about 90 to 96 h at 23 °C, that the truth could use only as a microscopy check on incubation time; its three sporulation figures conflict and it has no field data.
