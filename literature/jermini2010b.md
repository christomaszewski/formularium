# Jermini et al. 2010b: what downy mildew on Merlot leaves costs in sugar, not in yield

Three seasons (1996-1998) of Merlot at Cadenazzo with an untreated canopy (one cluster spray
at first sporulation), a reduced and a standard schedule: leaf severity, healthy leaf area,
yield and must quality.

## What it holds

- Field study (Vitis 49(2):77-85, 2010) in Merlot at the Agroscope Changins-Wädenswil ACW
  Centre of Cadenazzo, 1996-1998, with three spray schedules: untreated canopy with one
  cluster spray at first sporulation (A), a reduced schedule (B), and the standard schedule
  (C). Plots were 48 vines in 6 or 8 sub-plots (l. 69-70, 94). Severity was scored with the
  extended Horsfall scale, 12 classes (l. 112-113).
- Severity at the beginning of ripening in untreated canopy: 9.17 %, 4.46 %, 1.29 % of leaf
  area per shoot (1996, 1997, 1998) (l. 206-208). At the last control before harvest: 37 %,
  34 %, 48 % (l. 211).
- First sporulation: 25 June 1996, 11 June 1997, 24 June 1998 (l. 195-196).
- Final severity was higher on lateral than on main leaves (l. 212-214); the main vs lateral
  data are not printed (l. 216).
- Healthy leaf area per plant at harvest, as % of standard: untreated 27.1 % (1996);
  standard 32.7 % (1997), 22.9 % (1998); reduced 56.6 %, 54.8 %, 88.1 % (l. 247-254).
- Untreated canopy lost 70.3 %, 65.1 %, 61.8 % of its leaves by harvest, against standard
  (l. 265-266).
- Yield (kg per m2) did not differ in 1996 or 1997; in 1998 standard gave 1.357 against
  1.022 (untreated) and 1.105 (reduced) (Table 3, l. 336-338).
- Soluble solids at harvest: untreated below standard by 1.4 (1996), 0.57 (1997), 2.04
  (1998) °Brix (l. 301).
- No correlation was found between severity progress and sugar from veraison to harvest in
  1997 and 1998 (l. 297-300).
- Titratable acidity was higher in A and B than in C in 1996 and 1997 (l. 302-303; Table 3).
- One-year cane weight was above 40 g in every year (l. 364-365).
- Cited, not tested here: a 33 % defoliation at veraison (Hunter et al. 1995) and 25 to 50 %
  progressive defoliation (Duso and Belvini 1992) (l. 426-427, 446-450).
- Hypotheses only: a ripening damage threshold (l. 451) and 1-5 % severity for reduced
  schedules to match standard sugar (l. 456-458).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For a damage function in Agrarium's truth (t5): leaf downy mildew late in the season
  (37-48 % of leaf area before harvest, 62-70 % of leaves lost) left yield unchanged in two
  of three years but cut sugar by 0.57-2.04 °Brix; no correlation between severity progress
  and sugar accumulation, the vine compensating from reserves. The authors' 1-5 % severity
  threshold for reduced schedules is a hypothesis, not tested.
- Its severity-at-veraison values by year conflict in order with the companion paper's
  (jermini2010yield, l. 107-113).
