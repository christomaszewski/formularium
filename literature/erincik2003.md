# Erincik et al. 2003: Phomopsis infection of grape leaves and canes by temperature and wetness

From the Ohio State University (OARDC, Wooster). Phomopsis cane and leaf spot, not downy mildew.

## What it holds

- Grape (Catawba, V. labrusca; Seyval, French hybrid) and Phomopsis viticola, Wooster, Ohio (l. 16, 120).
- Chambers 1998-1999: 5 to 35°C in 5°C steps, wetness 5, 10, 15, 20 h, five plants per cell, three repeats (l. 13, 19, 82, 85).
- Model: Y = a t^b (1 - t)^g W^d, t = (T - Tmin)/(Tmax - Tmin) (l. 94). Tmin 4.95 and Tmax 35.5°C, fixed by eye from the data (l. 196-198). The tabulated a is log-transformed; base not printed (l. 259).
- Catawba leaf (lesions per leaf): a 1.54, b 1.84, g 2.58, d 1.43, R2 0.90 (l. 249). Internode: 1.58, 2.73, 3.68, 1.50 (l. 255).
- Seyval leaf: 1.79, 1.53, 1.67, 1.04 (l. 324). Internode: 2.12, 3.13, 5.31, 1.28 (l. 330).
- Optimum 17.7 to 20°C by tissue (l. 270-273); d near 1, so response to W is nearly linear (l. 404).
- Field test 2000-2001, 17 to 19 inoculations per vineyard (l. 154): r above 0.71 (l. 283); the model underpredicts.
- Form cited to Grove et al. 1985 (l. 92). Own data; no model dated them. Same lab as Anco et al. 2013 (a flag).
- Lesion counts and internode % are the response, not infection probability.

## Dependence

- Its own chamber data on Catawba and Seyval (1998-1999) and field inoculations (2000-2001); no model dated them. The form is the generalized Analytis Beta, cited to Grove et al. 1985 (l. 92); Tmin 4.95 and Tmax 35.5 °C were fixed by eye (l. 196-198).
- Madden and Ellis are authors of the engine's Lalancette bound (and Madden of its sampling bound): flags. Under D19 it was kin for them.
