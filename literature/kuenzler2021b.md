# Künzler 2021: Wädenswil first oil spots in 2020

A season review of 2020 signed by Lina Künzler (Agroscope, Wädenswil; l. 246-254), read only for the first oil spots at Wädenswil.

**Read this first.**
- The first-spot date is 12 June 2020 (Blauburgunder, untreated plot). The January 2021 bulletin (kuenzler2021) says 4 June 2020 for Müller-Thurgau. The cultivars differ in the two prints, so they may be two plots or one error. Both are kept; neither is resolved.
