# Ruml & Korać 2020: the average-cultivar lines with an offset per cultivar

The 2016 Sremski Karlovci relations (`ruml2016`) recast for use: one pooled line per
stage on the period's mean temperature, and a correction factor in days for each of 20
cultivars from its mean date. Twenty-two seasons, no hold-out.

## What it holds

- **Data:** the Novi Sad faculty's collection at Sremski Karlovci (45°10′N, 110 m, 12.3
  °C, 650 mm), 20 cultivars, 20 vines each, BBCH 07 and 60, 1986-2007 with the
  vineyard's own 2 m air temperature (l. 62-75).
- **Lines** (Eqs 2-3, p. 84; `ruml2020.monthly_regression`): budburst DOY = −3.62 Tb +
  133.0, Tb the mean daily temperature from 1 March to the onset (r −0.86, R² 0.71);
  flowering = −3.13 Tf + 217.1, Tf the mean daily maximum from 15 April to the onset
  (r −0.92, R² 0.85) (l. 86-88, 167-190).
- **Offsets** (Table 1, p. 83): cultivar mean minus the average cultivar's, from
  Cabernet Sauvignon +7 d (18 April) to Chardonnay, Bouvier and Petra −4 d (7 April) at
  budburst; flowering from Probus and Bagrina +4 (2 June, 30 May) to Chardonnay −3 (26
  May); the average cultivar 10 April (24 March to 28 April) and 29 May (16 May to 14
  June) (l. 96-118). The text says 28 May for the mean flowering (l. 157).
- **Error:** in-sample mean absolute error 4 d (budburst; 3 for Italian Riesling to 6 for
  Petra) and 3 d (flowering; 2-4) (l. 191-193); year-to-year misses up to 11 and 6 d
  (l. 199). No validation; the windows end at the onset (l. 174-177).

## Dependence

- **Fitted to** the same series as `ruml2016` (the variables and windows chosen there,
  l. 54, 84); the offsets are means, not fits. No engine model; no shared author.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- A runnable non-threshold phenology for 20 cultivars (Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot,
  Chardonnay, Pinots among them) at one Serbian site, with the retrospective-window
  caveat; nothing Mediterranean, no veraison here (the 2016 table has it).
