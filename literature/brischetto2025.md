# Brischetto et al. 2025: microbes in sour rot across Italian bioclimates

Sour-rotted bunches from 41 Italian vineyards in three bioclimates, 2019 to 2021, carried different yeast and bacteria mixes. Lab growth curves gave each microbe a temperature range.

## What it holds

- 41 vineyard locations in three bioclimates: south-east, central-west and north-east (l. 31, l. 115). Table 1 (l. 133) gives the regions.
- The share of Hanseniaspora rose from 33.2% in the south-east to 40.3% in the centre-west and 52.0% in the north-east (l. 284-285).
- Growth tested at 5 to 40 °C for nine yeasts and two bacteria (l. 190). Curves fitted with the Duthie (1997) equation (l. 219). Fitted values are in Table 3 (l. 342).

## Dependence

- Growth curves use the Duthie (1997) equation (l. 219). The microbes come from the authors' 2024 systematic review, cited in the Introduction.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (sour rot). Table 3 gives per-microbe growth limits and optima in a berry-like medium. A sour-rot model could use them as growth limits, after checking the table.
- The Hanseniaspora shares are a field pattern, not a rate. Not read in full.
