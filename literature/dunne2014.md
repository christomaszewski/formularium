# Dunne 2014: latent Botrytis in Tasmanian berries by qPCR, and a spray-timing trial

A duplex qPCR for Botrytis cinerea in berry tissue with a grape internal control, applied
through 2008-09 to Riesling and Sauvignon Blanc berries in the Coal River Valley, and a
2007-08 Chardonnay trial of fungicide timings and bunch-trash removal scored visually and
by the area under the progress curve.

## What it holds

- **Assay** (l. 3324, 3775-3779, 4206): grape-DNA assay limit 16 fg; the duplex detects 0.4
  pg of B. cinerea in the standard series and 0.082 pg in field samples; the lowest field
  detection was 50 fg.
- **Berry series** (l. 3962-3973, 4042-4043, 4197): nil-fungicide plots, 50 berries a
  replicate, Riesling on six dates and Sauvignon Blanc on five; 69 qPCR samples, five
  failed. The series "fed into a larger project" developing a bunch-rot risk model (Evans et
  al. 2010b, l. 3966-3968); which model, and whether these data were fitted to it, is not
  said.
- **Spray trial** (l. 4749-4834, 4798-4799, 5217): Chardonnay, six plots of five vines,
  eight treatments; Switch at 80 g/100 L on 2 and 22 January 2008, Flint at 16 g/100 L on 18
  February and 13 March; trash removed in December and January; trash carried 0.005 % B.
  cinerea on calyptras.
- **Results** (l. 5245-5308, 5506-5535): a fungicide effect at pre-bunch-closure (P 0.048)
  and veraison (P 0.004) but none on harvest severity by trash (P 0.79) or on AUDPC
  (10.31-17.22 by treatment, no fungicide effect).
- **Model-derived dates** (l. 5129-5161, 5605-5629): the date of 5 % severity per treatment
  (15-28 April, after the 9 April harvest) comes from a logit-linear regression of severity
  on time after Beresford et al. 2006, not from observation. Table 4.11 and Table 4.1 name
  treatments 7-8 differently.

## Dependence

- **Observed:** qPCR and visual scores; the 5 % dates are the thesis's own regression's.
  Methods borrowed: the logit transform (Beresford et al. 2006), AUDPC (Jeger &
  Viljanen-Rollinson 2001), the qPCR targets (published assays). Nothing fitted to anything
  held; the Evans et al. 2010b risk model is a lead.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- For Cooptera's Botrytis records and the 10-20 search's observation strand: qPCR detection
  limits for latent infection in berries, and a trial in which neither spray timing nor
  trash removal changed harvest severity. No record.
