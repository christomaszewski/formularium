# Sekulić et al. 2020: random forest spatial interpolation

Research article, Synthetic fields; Catalonia, Spain (precipitation); Croatia (temperature).

## What it holds

- Introduces Random Forest Spatial Interpolation (RFSI), which uses the nearest observations and their distances as covariates, and compares it by nested leave-location-out cross-validation with IDW, ordinary kriging, regression kriging, RF and RFsp.
- Accuracy tables are given for synthetic fields, daily precipitation (Catalonia, 2016-2018) and daily mean temperature (Croatia, 2008).
