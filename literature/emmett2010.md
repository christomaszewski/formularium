# Emmett et al. n.d.: long-term mildew R&D impact, south-east Australia

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Evaluation of a 1980-2008 R&D programme on powdery and downy mildew in south-eastern Australia (l. 41-44).
- Before the R&D, most sprays were calendar or growth-stage based; 5-6 sprays a season in the early 1980s, 8-9 in the late 1990s (l. 126-135).
- Powdery mildew sprays fell from 8-9 to 6-7 a season (23-25%) (l. 351-353).
- Downy mildew sprays fell by three, from 7-8 to 4-5, in modified routine programmes (l. 355-356).
- DModel: a downy mildew simulation model that flags primary and secondary infection and incubation (l. 222-225).
- Sunraysia: no sprays in 15% of seasons without primary infection; 6-8 sprays in another 15% with four or more (l. 301-305).
- Around 50% of Sunraysia seasons have one or no primary infection periods (l. 361-363).
- Powdery mildew patch losses fell from about 10% to 1% in Sunraysia and Riverland (l. 338-342).
