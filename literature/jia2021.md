# Jia et al. 2021: pretraining a recurrent net on an uncalibrated lake simulator, then fine-tuning on few real profiles (lakes)

Pretrains a recurrent network on the output of the General Lake Model (GLM) run with default parameters, then fine-tunes it on a fraction of the real temperature profiles of Lake Mendota, and scores it against the same net without pretraining and against a GLM calibrated on the same fraction. No record: a method with a measured result.

**Read this first.** Lakes, not plants. The simulator is a good teacher and the real check is on the variable the simulator also outputs (water temperature), so the result does not show what a student inherits for a variable the real data cannot check. One lake, one test block of years. The file is the arXiv preprint; the published version was not read. The sample-size sweep keeps the energy-conservation loss on in both arms (RNN_EC against RNN_EC,p), so the pretraining effect is isolated here, which it is not in `read2019`.

## What it holds

- **Simulator** (l. 407-408, 555): GLM with generic default parameters, not calibrated to the lake; run for every depth and every day of the driver record, 1980-04-02 to 2014-12-30 (l. 457, 558). Its RMSE against the real observations is 2.950 degC (l. 555). The count of synthetic samples is not printed in the lines read.
- **Real data** (l. 460, 462-464): Lake Mendota, Wisconsin; 13,158 observations in all. Training 8,037 observations (1980-04-02 to 1991-10-31 and 2003-06-01 to 2014); test 5,121 observations, 1991-11-01 to 2003-05-31.
- **Validation design** (l. 462-464, 497): new years (a block of 12 years held out) on the same lake. The real training fraction is varied by dropping observations at random; the test set stays fixed; 10 repeats.
- **Score, with and without pretraining** (Tables 2 and 3, l. 510-511, 591), test RMSE in degC at 0.2 / 2 / 20 / 100 % of the real training observations: pretrained net with energy loss 2.056 / 1.590 / 1.402 / 1.380; the same net not pretrained 4.107 / 2.149 / 1.489 / 1.471; without the energy loss and not pretrained 4.615 / 2.311 / 1.531 / 1.489. With no real data the pretrained net scores 2.455.
- **Simulator alone** (l. 509): the generic GLM scores 2.950 (0 %); the GLM calibrated on the same fraction scores 2.616 / 2.422 / 2.318 / 1.836.
- **Real-only baseline**: the two unpretrained nets above. The net with energy loss needed 20 % of the real data to beat the GLM calibrated on all of it (l. 502).
- **Size of the gain** (l. 565-566, 568): 0.2 % of the observations is 16; with them the pretrained net scores 2.056, below the unpretrained nets given ten times the data. Pretrained nets converge in 30-50 epochs against 150-200.
- **Student beats teacher** (l. 591, 674, 684, 703): the net pretrained and given no real data scores 2.455 against its teacher's 2.950; at 9 m depth 4.752 against 5.333. The authors' explanation, that the emulator smooths the simulator's artefacts, was not tested against alternatives.
- **Wrong-teacher tests** (Tables 5-7, l. 749-751, 760, 764, 835): pretraining on a barrel-shaped synthetic lake gives 3.239 at 0 % real data and 1.617 at 2 %; on a martini-shaped lake (teacher error 5.950 against real data) 5.340 at 0 %, 3.033 at 0.2 %, 2.216 at 2 % and 1.485 at 20 %, against 1.489 for the unpretrained net at 100 % (l. 751). Pretraining on Florida climate gives 9.106 at 0 %, 2.601 at 0.2 %, 1.759 at 2 % and 1.470 at 20 %. The authors say the nets recover with about 2 % real data (l. 764).
- **Limits** (l. 926): the authors say it is not known for which dynamical systems this works.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **Pretraining on an uncalibrated simulator helps most with few real data.** The measured gain over the same net unpretrained is 2.051 degC at 0.2 % and 0.091 at 100 % (arithmetic, not printed). For D45 (a model fitted on the truth is kin to it) this is the case to bear in mind: the student is kin to its teacher, and here the real data still judged it, on a variable both record.
- **Wrong-teacher runs are a robustness test of the kind D60 allows.** A badly wrong teacher costs only until a few real data arrive (l. 751, 835). This was checked on the variable the simulator also outputs; for a hidden variable it says nothing. Per D58, the simulator proposes and the real data judge.
- **Not shown:** any plant model, any disease; a held-out lake (one lake); the energy loss and pretraining are separated only at this lake.
