# García de Cortázar-Atauri et al. 2017: French grapevine phenology, past series and projections

A review of French grapevine phenology: the observation series, the stage models, and projected stage dates to 2100.

## What it holds

- **Riesling series:** 50 % budbreak, flowering and veraison of Riesling at Bergheim, Alsace, over about 60 years, with a break in 1988-1989 (Figure 1, l. 253-255). The text gives the spread of dates as 9 d vs 6.5 d (budbreak) and 10 d vs 8.2 d (veraison), lower in the last 30 years, and 8 d vs 8.5 d (flowering), slightly higher (l. 255-257).
- **Cultivar groups:** 43 cultivars covering 95 % of French vineyards, in five timing groups by GFV F* ranges, [1120-1411] for flowering and [2286-2941] for veraison (Table 1 caption, l. 85-86).
- **Model error:** the GFV model's average absolute mean error is 3.4 d for flowering and 4.4 d for veraison, over all varieties (l. 352-355).
- **Projections:** under DRIAS CNRM-Aladin, stages move 6 to 12 d earlier by 2050 and 15 to 30 d by 2100, depending on variety, scenario and region (l. 357-359; Figure 4, l. 388-392). Veraison moves up to 33 d earlier in Champagne under RCP 8.5 (l. 361-362).
- **Warming sensitivity:** flowering 8 d and veraison 10 d earlier per degree of warming in France, from the CLIMATOR project (l. 375-377).

## Dependence

- Builds on the GFV model (Parker et al. 2011, 2013; l. 336-340), the Wang and Engel 1998 model as adapted by García de Cortázar-Atauri et al. 2010a (l. 316-318), the BRIN model (l. 326-330), Pouget's budbreak model as amended by Riou 1994 (l. 313-315), the GDD model with a 10 °C base from 1 April (l. 341-346), the Daux et al. 2012 harvest-date database (l. 242-245) and the DRIAS CNRM-Aladin projections (l. 388-390).
