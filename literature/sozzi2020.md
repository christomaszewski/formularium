# Sozzi et al. 2020: Sentinel-2 NDVI against UAV NDVI in 30 vineyard blocks

## What it holds

- **Data:** 30 vineyard blocks in the south of France, without inter-row grass (l. 30); Sentinel-2 pixels mix vine and inter-row.
- **Agreement:** Sentinel-2 and UAV NDVI correlate at field scale R2 0.87 and sub-field scale R2 0.84 (l. 41-42).
- **Block size:** correlation is non-significant in most cases for blocks under 0.5 ha (l. 40-41).
