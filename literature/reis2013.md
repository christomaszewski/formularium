# Reis et al. 2013: a Lalancette-based spray trigger at Bento Gonçalves

## What it holds

- **Site:** Embrapa Uva e Vinho, Bento Gonçalves; cultivar Isabel; seasons 2000/01, 2002/03
  and 2003/04.
- **Model:** Lalancette et al. 1988's severity, printed with the right intercept (-0.071)
  but the exponent as 1/(-1.2) where Lalancette's is 1/(1 - 1.2) = -5. Table 1 turns it
  into daily severity values (DDSV 0-3) by temperature and wetness.
- **Trigger:** a cumulative sum (CDDSV) of 12-14, chosen from the 2000/01 data and tested
  in the two later seasons.
- **Results:**
  - **2000/01:** first symptoms on 19 October at CDDSV 32; AUDPC 1,025 untreated against
    500 conventional.
  - **2002/03:** untreated 3,267.4; conventional 511.1; trigger 465.9 (cymoxanil + maneb)
    and 696.7 (mancozeb); sprays 7 against 6.
  - **2003/04:** untreated 1,299.8, conventional 181.7, trigger 176.8 and 211.1; sprays 8
    against 5.
- **Printed inconsistency:** the 2003/04 reduction is printed as 3.5 % in the text, but 8
  to 5 sprays is 37.5 %, as the abstract says.

## Dependence

- Copies Lalancette 1988's infection equation; trigger tuned on its own first season.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- A grower-level validation of spray timing, not of infection. Untreated AUDPC for three
  seasons at Bento Gonçalves is a weak severity pattern.
