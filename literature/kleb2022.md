# Kleb, Merkt & Zörb 2022: canopy sensors against the station at Hohenheim

The 2022 paper behind `kleb2026` (the preprint that found canopy sensors beat a border
station). Three summer periods of in-canopy temperature, humidity and leaf wetness against
a standard weather station, with the VitiMeteo rules as the frame; no model is run.

## What it holds

- **Set-up:** the University of Hohenheim vineyard, 2021; Testo 174H temperature and RH
  loggers (± 0.5 °C, ± 2 %) and PHYTOS 31 leaf-wetness sensors on three vines at 100, 130
  and 160 cm (the stated canopy height is 120 cm), against the station's instruments at
  180 cm, 75 m away; ten-minute logging, hourly means split by sunrise and sunset (l.
  115-145, 180-202). Three plots, each followed from healthy to about 25 % visible
  infection: 21 July-9 August, 10-31 August, 1-27 September 2021 (l. 144-149); no dated
  disease score is printed. Wetness counted as "sufficient for infection" above about 460
  raw counts, dry about 435, after the METER manual (l. 190-191); the station's wetness
  was measured in plot III only (l. 196).
- **Temperature:** inside the canopy, 13 days (3 + 6 + 4) reached 30 °C for more than 6 h,
  the station 2 (l. 219, 244, 255, 505-508); daytime canopy means 2.6, 2.7 and 1.8 °C
  above the station (Table 2, l. 345-347; the text's "3.6 °C" at l. 334 does not match),
  up to 6 °C on 23-25 September (l. 287). The text's 9 % and 1.4 % of "lethal" days
  (l. 510-512) do not follow from the 69 days of the periods (13 and 2 of 69).
- **Humidity and wetness:** daytime station RH 2.7, 3.4 and 1.7 points above the canopy
  (Table 3; the text says 3.5 %, l. 372); in plot III the station read wet (≥ 460) every
  day while the canopy fell below it on four days, and over all plots on 19 days or nights
  (l. 263, 557); the station crosses the threshold about four hours earlier in the
  evening and stays above it 2-3 h longer, the canopy 1-2 h longer before noon (l.
  547-552, text only); the text's "above 900 counts, double the canopy" (l. 544) does not
  match Table 4's 512.5 by day and 622.4 by night (l. 433-437).
- **Rules cited, not computed:** the 50 hour-degree infection rule (temperature × wetness
  hours) credited to the IVV's VitiMeteo notes and Bläser 1978 (l. 16, 52-53, 465-467);
  sporangia dying above 30 °C for 6 h and lifespans of 9 d (10 °C, 70-90 % RH), 3 d (10
  °C, 30-40 %), 4 d (20 °C, 90 %), 3 d (20 °C, 70 %), only the last cited to Bläser 1978
  (l. 50, 503-520); primary infection at 13 °C and 8 mm (Schruft & Kassemeyer 1999,
  l. 458); 16 rain events in the three periods (l. 539).

## Dependence

- **Its own sensor data,** observed; no model dated anything. VitiMeteo is described
  (dated to 2002-2003 after Bleyer et al. 2008, l. 84-92) and the argument is that its
  station input misses the canopy, but nothing is computed with it.
- **Authors:** Kleb and Merkt wrote `kleb2026`: a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- Evidence for a truth whose weather is the canopy's: the station overstates wetness
  hours and understates heat, so the engine's station-driven 50 degree-hour rule
  (`institutvitivinicole2015`, `puelles2024`) would call infections the canopy did not
  see. The paper's own summary figures disagree with its tables in three places; take
  the tables.
