# Campos et al. 2020: variable-rate spraying on Torrelavit parcels, with canopy size by date

Prescription maps from UAV vigour zones used to vary spray volume on Chardonnay, Merlot and
Cabernet Sauvignon at Torrelavit in 2018. Read for canopy height and width through the
season.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 20-21, 241; Table 1 in the page image): Torrelavit, Alt Penedès; double
  Royat, vines 1.2 m, rows 2.2 m; 2018. Irrigation, rootstock and shoots not printed.
- **Canopy by date** (Table 4, l. 745-809; 15 hand readings a zone, l. 466): Chardonnay
  low-vigour zone height 0.65 m on 24 May (BBCH 60-61), 0.94 m on 1 June, 0.92 m on 6 and
  20 June, 0.94 m on 2 July, 0.89 m on 12 July, 0.83 m on 20 July. Over the table heights run
  0.45 to 1.35 m and widths 0.28 to 0.87 m (the 0.87 m is an outlier). Higher-vigour zones
  are taller and wider (l. 719-721).
- **Cited** (l. 723-724): LAI against NDVI R² 0.73 (Johnson et al. 2003); height against
  NDVI R² 0.79 (Montero et al. 1999).
- **Not printed:** LAI, leaf area, budburst, trimming dates.

## Dependence

- Spray rates from DOSAVIÑA (`gilmoya2019`), the same parcels; canopy measures by the EPPO
  procedure. No model shaped the canopy data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Penedès canopy height plateaus by about the end of flowering** (early June 2018) at
  about 0.9 m and then falls a little, likely by trimming (not stated). Geometry, not leaf
  area: consistent with a leaf-area curve whose main growth ends by about 80 days after
  budburst.
