# Salotti & Rossi 2021: a weather-driven model for Ascochyta blight of chickpea

A mechanistic model of Ascochyta blight in chickpea, with printed equations and validation on trials from several countries.

## What it holds

- Primary infection: the probability of infection when one is predicted is 0.838, and of no infection when none is predicted is 0.776 (l. 18-19, 483-484).
- Seasonal dynamics: CCC 0.947 and RMSE 0.103 against data from Australia (1996-1998) and southern Italy (2019) (l. 21-23).
- The infection rate is a Bete equation, parameterised from Trapero-Casas and Kaiser data (l. 271-272, 286).

## Dependence

- Builds on the Bete equation (l. 271-272) and on the Trapero-Casas and Kaiser infection data (l. 271, 286).
- Uses the Venturia inaequalis ascospore rule for rain-released spores (l. 250).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Class C: a chickpea pathogen, not a grape disease, so it is off the grape strands. Its Bete infection form and rain-release rule are general methods that a simulator could read.
