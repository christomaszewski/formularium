# Francone et al. 2012: sonic statistics above three sloping Piedmont vineyards, with no in-canopy wind

An above-canopy study of turbulent transport efficiency and ejection-sweep motion on vine slopes.

**Read this first.** The paper has no wind profile inside the canopy, so it gives no ratio of mid-canopy to open wind. Its use here is the site description and the typical above-canopy wind.

## What it holds

- Three sloping vineyards in Piedmont (Cocconato, Fubine and Castiglione Falletto; Nebbiolo and Barbera), 2009; rows 2.5 m apart, trained on vertical shoot positioning (l. 546).
- Leaf area index (LAI) varied over the season from 1.0 to 3.5 m2 m-2 at all three sites (l. 286).
- The probability density of the mean horizontal wind speed above the canopy (sonic) peaked near 0.7 m/s regardless of site, so these are low-wind vineyards (l. 574).
- Cited, not the paper's own: vineyard drag, sheltering and bulk flow properties have been studied for mean wind parallel and orthogonal to the rows (Hicks 1973; Weiss and Allen 1976; Riou et al. 1987) (l. 170).
