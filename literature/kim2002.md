# Kim et al. 2002: CART with wind corrected to canopy height

From Iowa State University.

## What it holds

- Tests Gleason et al.'s CART/SLD dew model (ref 6) at 15 sites in IA, IL, NE, May to September 1997-99 (l. 15).
- Inputs are SkyBit model-derived hourly weather, not station data (l. 85). Truth is painted Model 237 sensors, hour wet if >= 30 min wet (l. 113).
- Coefficients are those of the 1994 paper: thresholds 14.4674 and 37.0 (l. 89). Inequality 2 has RH, not RH squared (l. 87).
- New step: SkyBit 10-m wind corrected to 0.3 m by the log wind profile (l. 85). d and z0 are not printed.
- Dew-eligible hours 20:00 to 9:00 (l. 165); 5,126 station-days (l. 230).
- Mean error, all sites: SkyBit Wetness -1.4, CART/SLD with 10-m wind -4.8, with 0.3-m wind 0.1 h/day; MAE 7.2, 5.6, 4.2 h/day (Table 2, l. 190).
- SkyBit underestimated LWD by 3.4 h/day (ref 5) and 1.4 h/day (ref 20) (l. 28).
- Use as data: sensor-versus-model errors for a Gleason-type tree; the 0.3-m wind correction is the only new piece.

## Dependence

- It computes Gleason et al. 1994's CART, with the wind corrected from 10 m to 0.3 m by a log profile; nothing refitted. Its inequality 2 prints 0.0046·RH where Gleason prints 0.0046·RH² (both checked on the pages). Humidity thresholds: the engine's form.
- Gleason is a flag.
