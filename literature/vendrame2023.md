# Vendrame et al. 2023: with leaves, the flow in a hedgerow vineyard is mixing-layer-like only for wind across or diagonal to the rows

A journal paper from the Padova campaign described in the thesis `vendrame2017`; only the abstract was read.

**Read this first.** Abstract only. It prints no numbers. The thesis `vendrame2017` has the measured values but did not split mean wind ratios by direction; this paper does, and is closed. That it is the thesis chapter's journal version is the reading agent's statement; the abstract does not say it.

## What it holds

- An experimental campaign in a hedgerow vineyard in north-east Italy, with a vertical array of 3D sonic anemometers and canopy structure measurements, followed the flow from a leafless canopy to full development. The effects of wind direction relative to the rows and of atmospheric stability were also analysed (l. 19).
- The within-canopy flow became more like a well-defined mixing-layer flow as foliage developed, but only for diagonal and across-row wind (l. 19).
- With wind parallel to the rows, the effect of rising leaf density was lower and the vineyard stayed closer to an open canopy (l. 19).
- Stability mattered less than wind direction or leaf density, except in free convection (l. 19).
