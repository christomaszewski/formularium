# Bellow et al. 2013: stilbene fluorescence detects infection days before symptoms

## What it holds

- Daily for 15 days after inoculation, four fluorosensors: violet-blue fluorescence
  (stilbenes) peaks 6 days post-inoculation and detects infection from day 1 on the lower
  surface and day 3 on the upper; green fluorescence rises from day 8.

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.
