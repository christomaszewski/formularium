# Wurz et al. 2021: bud load and downy mildew on Cabernet Franc in Santa Catarina

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Four bud loads (15, 30, 50, 75 per vine) were tested on Cabernet Franc at São Joaquim (SC, Brazil) in 2016/17 under natural infection (l. 33-34).
- Maximum incidence was lowest at 15 buds, 96.2% (text) or 96.3% (Table 1); the other loads were 99.0 to 100% (l. 37, 328).
- Maximum severity was 4.3% at 15 buds and 6.5% at 30 buds, against 10.7% and 14.0% at 50 and 75 buds (l. 39, Table 1).
- The incidence AACPID was 2552.6 at 15 buds and 4074.6 at 75 buds (l. 40, 288, 330).
- Season weather: mean temperature 17.3 C and 532.7 mm of rain from December to April (l. 230-231).
- The authors conclude that more buds raise downy mildew occurrence and advise preventive management (l. 42-43).
