# Lulu et al. 2008: leaf wetness estimated over turfgrass compared with sensors in a Brazilian vineyard

A study that estimates leaf wetness duration (LWD) over turfgrass from a standard weather station with four methods and compares it with LWD measured in a 'Niagara Rosada' vineyard.

**Read this first.** The file is named lulu2005 and the reading JSON dates it 2005, but the page footer prints Sci. Agric. v.65, special issue, December 2008 (p. 10-17), so the citation uses 2008. The id is kept as given. Only the abstract and methods were read; no result was read.

## What it holds

- **Methods compared (abstract):** number of hours with relative humidity above 90 % (NHRH > 90 %), dew point depression (DPD), a classification and regression tree (CART) and Penman-Monteith (PM).
- **Vineyard sensors (l. 29):** at the top of the plants, at a 45 degree inclination, facing southwest, three replicates; the vines are in a hedgerow training system at Jundiai, Sao Paulo State.
- **Reference sensor (l. 118):** over turfgrass at 30 cm height, at 30 degrees to horizontal, facing south, in the standard weather station; records are stored as 20 min averages or totals (grep, l. 25).
