# Rouxel et al. 2014: cryptic species of P. viticola in North America

Research article, Eastern North America (USA and Canada).

## What it holds

- Sequencing of four partial genes and an ITS-based CAPS diagnostic on 896 P. viticola isolates show a new species on Vitis vulpina and give the geographic and host distribution of five cryptic species in eastern North America.
- It has no epidemiological formulation; the abstract says P. viticola clade aestivalis was 83% of isolates in V. vinifera vineyards.
