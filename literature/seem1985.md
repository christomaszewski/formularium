# Seem et al. 1985: how often a scout finds one to three simulated downy mildew lesions in a four-vine unit

A South Australian study that stuck artificial yellow disks on vine leaves to mimic oilspot lesions, and measured how often observers found them in a 2-minute search of four vines, to design a sampling plan.

**Read this first.**
- The lesions are simulated: pale-yellow transparent disks of 12 and 14 mm (l. 141), not a real epidemic. Detection of real, faint, early lesions may be lower.
- The observers were mostly the authors (l. 170). The reading agent notes experienced and inexperienced observers did alike after brief instruction (l. 494).
- Sultana's printed detection row (0.57, 0.31, 0.69) is not monotone in lesion count (l. 657); take as printed. The "0.01 to 0.07 %" incidence range is from the abstract page; Table 7 and the Fig. 2 values were not read in full.

## What it holds

- **Sampling unit (l. 120, l. 121):** a length of row 7 to 8 m holding four vines; thresholds of 1 to 5 lesions per unit.
- **Search time (l. 628):** 2 min per unit, "30 sec per vine"; 1 to 3 min made no difference.
- **Detection of at least one disk in a 2-min unit with 1, 2 and 3 disks (l. 655 to 658):** Muscat Gordo 0.26, 0.55, 0.63; Shiraz 0.28, 0.66, 0.83; Sultana 0.57, 0.31, 0.69; Rhine Riesling 0.60, 0.70, 0.65.
- **Overall rates (l. 678):** 0.64 (58 in 90 attempts) for Gordo and 0.66 (59 in 90 attempts) for Shiraz, with no significant difference.
- **False positives (l. 395):** none; "the zero disk count was always scored correctly".
- **Incidence meant (l. 696, l. 697):** one disk per unit equals 0.07 % (Gordo), 0.05 % (Shiraz) and 0.03 % (Sultana) leaf incidence midseason (Riesling not read).
- **Units needed (l. 554):** for 99 % confidence of finding at least one lesion where first disease is known to be present: Gordo 31, Shiraz 73, Sultana 39, Riesling 13 units, at 2 min each (31 units = 62 min).
- **Model (l. 208):** the detection model assumes primary infections are Poisson-randomly spread.

## Dependence

- Own experiments. The detection model follows Geng et al. (indirect bioassay), as the reading agent reads it. Leads: Madden et al. 1995 (Phytopathology 85:269-275); the preliminary report by Seem and Magarey (ref. 11).
