# Ashraf et al. 2026: oospore survival over winter in Kashmir grapes

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Oospores of P. viticola overwintered on fallen leaves at Shalimar, Kashmir, 2019 and 2020, cv. Thompson Seedless (l. 83-84).
- Oospore production on the ground surface peaked in June (976 per cm2, 61% viable, 2019 June I) (l. 221).
- Buried at 15 cm, viability fell from 18.6% to 10.3% by May 2019 (l. 160).
- Leaves at 7.5 cm decomposed 1-2 weeks earlier than at 15 cm (l. 183).
- Leaves and pruned-snag buds on the floor are the main primary source (l. 162).
