# Yu et al. 2022: rain shelters and downy mildew epidemics in China

Research article, Liaoning Province, China (Shenyang).

## What it holds

- Two-year (2016-2017) comparison of open-field, fungicide-sprayed and rain-shelter plots, with disease timing, canopy microclimate (RH, leaf wetness) and microsatellite population genetics of P. viticola lesions.
- Mainly population-genetic; the epidemic and microclimate data are of indirect use.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Rain shelters cut leaf wetness and slowed epidemics (Shenyang): a check on how Agrarium's wetness drives disease, made outside the engine's lineage.
