# Spencer-Phillips et al. 2002: a 2002 book on downy mildew research

## What it holds

- Fifteen entries: evolution of the downy mildews (Dick), host resistance (Mauch-Mani),
  Lactuca and Bremia (Lebeda et al.; Lebeda), chemical control (Gisi; note gisi2002),
  Peronospora-Phytophthora phylogeny (Cooke et al.), sunflower (Virányi), lamb's lettuce
  (Pietrek & Zinkernagel), Arabidopsis (Gunn et al.), pearl millet (Gilijamse & Jeger),
  three grapevine papers (Vercesi et al., note vercesi2002; Young et al.; Cohen et al.), and
  Dick's binomials.
- **Pearl millet (Gilijamse & Jeger):** in southwest Niger soil oospores (26-133 per g at
  Dosso, 74-768 at Gaya) correlated with incidence at 90 days after sowing (r = 0.83).
- **Lamb's lettuce (Pietrek & Zinkernagel):** Peronospora valerianellae did not sporulate at
  20 °C; it needed at least three days at 10 °C.
- **Grapevine control:** azoxystrobin stopped zoospore encystment at 0.04 mg/l and motility
  at 0.008 mg/l, and gave 80 % control curatively 2 days after inoculation (Young et al.);
  BABA cut sporulation by up to 99 % (Cohen et al.).

## Bearing (2026-11-03): curative windows, for Cooptera's E6

- **Gisi's chapter, section 3.14** (library text l. 5928-5936, read by the main session): under
  glasshouse conditions most compounds protected P. viticola for 10 days or more, except
  fluazinam (8 d), famoxadone (7 d) and cymoxanil (3 d); curative activity was seen for
  azoxystrobin (1 d), dimethomorph and fosetyl-Al (2 d), cymoxanil and iprovalicarb (3 d) and the
  phenylamides (at least 4 d), but not for famoxadone, fluazinam, mancozeb, folpet or copper.
  Syngenta's data; the figure itself is not in the text.
- **Cymoxanil on grape** (l. 5764-5765): curative from 2 days at high temperature up to 4 at
  lower, credited to Genet et al. 1997 (not held).
- The best open comparison of curative windows by class that the 2026-11-03 pass found; no field
  study gives them in hours. Agrarium's research note deep-research-11-03 (cooptera.md) carries
  the table for Cooptera.
