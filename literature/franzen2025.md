# Franzen et al. 2025: VitiMeteo forecasts for copper reduction in vines

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- German brochure (2024 edition, imprint August 2025) on VitiMeteo and the VITIFIT PIWI extension for copper reduction (l. 2, 613-614, 1032).
- VM Plasmopara has been used since 2002; its forecast uses a seven-day weather forecast from meteoblue, Basel (l. 187, 218, 134).
- Leaf wetness is the measured hours at the station; risk is shown in three levels, weak, medium, strong (l. 452, 459).
- Spray protection lasts until about 400 cm2 (two leaves) of new growth, or 600 cm2 with a phosphonate (l. 525-528).
- Potted-vine trials: over 400 vines in five years, stage-specific susceptibility per PIWI variety (l. 579-583).
- 2024 high pressure: copper treatments down two-thirds on Cabernet Cortis, one-third on Cabernet Blanc; 2023 low pressure: over half and about a quarter (l. 750-754).
