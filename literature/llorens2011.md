# Llorens et al. 2011: destructive LAI and canopy size in Catalan vineyards, 2008-2010

Ultrasonic and LiDAR sensors on a sprayer, against hand measurements of canopy height,
width and leaf area. Read for its destructive LAI at three stages.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 153-160): Cabernet Sauvignon, Tempranillo and Merlot, in Penedès and Costers
  del Segre (which variety where is not printed); rows 2.9 m (Merlot) to 3.3 m (Cabernet);
  12 field tests, June to August 2008-2010, at BBCH 65, 75 and 85. Vine spacing, trellis,
  irrigation and shoots are not printed.
- **LAI method** (l. 202-212): every leaf on 1 m of row picked, five replicates a variety and
  stage, in three height layers; area from a weight-area ratio of 50 leaves measured on a
  LI-COR 3100C. Canopy height and width by hand on 10 vines, after Manktelow & Praat.
- **Table 1** (l. 367-378; LAI m²/m², manual height and width in m, manual volume m³ per m
  of row):

  | Variety, stage (year) | LAI | height | width | volume |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Cabernet 65 (2008) | 0.6 | 0.69 | 0.62 | 0.42 |
  | Cabernet 75 (2008) | 1.1 | 1.03 | 0.56 | 0.58 |
  | Cabernet 85 (2008) | 1.0 | 0.77 | 0.59 | 0.45 |
  | Cabernet 75 (2009) | 1.1 | 1.00 | 0.54 | 0.54 |
  | Cabernet 65 (2010) | 1.1 | 1.06 | 0.48 | 0.50 |
  | Cabernet 75 (2010) | 1.3 | 0.97 | 0.78 | 0.76 |
  | Tempranillo 65 (2008) | 0.6 | 0.37 | 0.62 | 0.22 |
  | Tempranillo 75 (2008) | 1.2 | 1.14 | 0.59 | 0.67 |
  | Tempranillo 85 (2008) | 1.6 | 0.90 | 0.57 | 0.51 |
  | Merlot 75 (2009) | 1.7 | 0.94 | 0.64 | 0.60 |
  | Merlot 65 (2010) | 1.8 | 1.06 | 0.52 | 0.55 |
  | Merlot 75 (2010) | 1.5 | 1.02 | 0.73 | 0.74 |

  The divisor of LAI is not stated; on 1 m of row by the row spacing, LAI 0.6 to 1.8 is 1.7
  to 5.9 m² of leaf per metre of row, or 2.1 to 7.1 m² per 1.2 m of row (my calculation).
- **Sensors:** LAI against ultrasonic volume R² 0.51, against LiDAR volume 0.21 (l.
  455-456); LAI = 2.704 CVU + 0.01 (Figure 8). LiDAR leaf wall area about 30 % below the
  manual one (l. 481).
- **Not printed:** test dates, budburst, vine spacing, trellis, irrigation, leaf area per
  vine or shoot, whether stages share vines.

## Dependence

- Manual method of Manktelow & Praat and the weight-area ratio of Cross et al.; fits are
  the authors' own. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Catalan LAI by hand, 0.6 to 1.8:** low against Madrid's irrigated theses, consistent
  with Escalona et al. 2003's rainfed vines. Flowering (BBCH 65) LAI is half to all of the
  pea-size value. Canopy about 1.0 m high and 0.5 to 0.8 m wide. Used in Agrarium's
  research_notes/vine-leaf-area.
