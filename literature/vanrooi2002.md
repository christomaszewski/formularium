# van Rooi 2002: dry airborne conidia on bunch parts and vinelets, sprayed or not

Dauphine and Merlot bunches at five stages and vinelets from two vineyards, sprayed with
four fungicides or left, were inoculated with dry conidia in a settling tower and held 24 h
at 22 °C and about 93 % RH, then dry for 14 days; incidence by bunch part and shoot position
shows where infection takes and what fungicides stop.

## What it holds

- **Material** (l. 54, 2015-2019): Dauphine (table) and Merlot (wine) from Paarl and
  Klapmuts, 1999/2000 and 2000/2001; full bloom, pea size (3.5 °Brix), bunch closure (4),
  veraison (Merlot 12.5, Dauphine 8.3) and harvest (18.5 and 15 °Brix).
- **Sprays** (l. 60, 2043-2053): iprodione, pyrimethanil, cyprodinil/fludioxonil and
  fenhexamid at recommended dosages (Nel et al. 1999) by gun at 2 bar, held 24 h at 22 °C
  and 56 % RH.
- **Inoculation** (l. 2064-2078): 3 mg of dry conidia in a 3 m tower, germination 92-99 %;
  24 h at 22 °C and about 93 % RH, then 14 d dry at 22 °C; no leaf wetness recorded.
- **Results** (l. 95, 2125-2201, 2655-2877): unsprayed clusters highly susceptible at full
  bloom in both seasons; fungicides stopped symptoms there but not infection, treated
  laterals still yielding B. cinerea at 16-37 %; fungicide effect P 0.0001 (Table 1);
  Dauphine 1999/2000 control 84.2 % against cyprodinil/fludioxonil 28.0 % (Table 2); Merlot
  pea-size rachis 36.7 % control against 13.3 % (Table 10).
- **Not printed:** leaf wetness, the temperature range, regression coefficients, raw counts.

## Dependence

- Observed; no model in the data. The tower after Salinas et al. 1989, the 24 h dry-conidia
  incubation after Coertze & Holz 1999 and Coertze et al. 2001. Broome et al. 1995 (the
  engine's `broome1995.botrytis`) is cited only for inoculum density (l. 2394-2396): a lead,
  not a use. Holz again (`coertze2002`, `brink2005`): a flag.
