# Viret et al. 2018: Changins oospore maturity from 1 May 2018, first oil spots on 7 June

A Swiss vintage review in which a warm, dry April slowed the winter eggs: germination within 24 h from 1 May at Changins, with the 160 degree-day sum reached on 28 April.

**Read this first.**
- The 28 April date and the other three stations' dates are the model's sum (160 degree-days above 8 C), not observations.
- The leads file notes that Dubuis 2019 prints 29 May for the first spots of 2018, against 7 June here; the two are not reconciled.

## What it holds

- **Oospore maturity (l. 1164 to 1167):** the winter eggs matured (germination in 24 h) from 1 May at Changins; the warm, dry April slowed them markedly (l. 1165 to 1166).
- **Model sum (l. 1170 to 1172):** the sum of daily temperature above 8 C from 1 January generally matches maturity at 160 degree-days; in 2018 that was 24 April at Leytron, 28 April at Changins, 23 April at Yvorne and 27 April at Pully (l. 1171), at one to two unfolded leaves (BBCH 11-12).
- **Authors' reading:** long-term monitoring shows warm, dry spring conditions delay the maturity of the winter eggs (the sentence after the station dates).
- **First infections (l. 1179 to 1181):** indicated by Agrometeo on 9 May at Changins and Pully, with six unfolded leaves, flower buds separated (BBCH 55-57).
- **First oil spots (l. 1183 to 1184):** observed at the end of flowering on 7 June at Changins, from the infection period 28 May to 1 June.

## Dependence

- **Own observations** of the Changins group (germination test); the 160 C dates and first infections are model output (Agrometeo). Dubuis et al. 2012 say the test serves to validate VitiMeteo.
