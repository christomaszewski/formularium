# Jacquin et al. 2003: the French warning services' models

## What it holds

- The SRPV used 28 models in 2002: 14 in use, 6 ending validation, 5 in early evaluation, 3
  in design; 197 automatic stations, 152 their own.
- Viticulture (Table 2): downy mildew by Milvit (spring), Milstop (the Midi), and Strizyk's
  EPI for the winter phase, a new winter model in progress; grape moths by EVA and a
  Burgundy model; powdery mildew in early validation; black rot and others not covered.
- No equations.

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.
