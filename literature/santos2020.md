# Santos et al. 2020: population structure of P. viticola in subtropical Brazil

94 isolates from four vineyards genotyped with 10 microsatellites and cytochrome b; the state
  of origin shapes population structure more than production system or QoI sensitivity.

## What it holds

- **Isolates:** 94 from four organic and conventional vineyards in the two main grape states
  (l. 22-28).
- **Resistance:** G143A in 28 % of isolates under organic and 100 % under conventional
  management; three isolates carry M125I, five-fold less sensitive (l. 32-40).
- **Structure:** six cytochrome b haplotypes; Brazilian populations nearer the European than
  the North American one; great diversity and sexual reproduction (l. 43-53).
- **Season:** one of the sampling years is 2018/19 (l. 119).

## Dependence

- None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s1, weakly: evidence that sexual reproduction (so oospores) is common in subtropical Brazil;
