# Okan et al. 2026: one solar vineyard weather station in Antalya, 98.6 % of data received over a year

A Turkish open-access paper on a modular sensing station in one vineyard, compared with a regional station for reference evapotranspiration (ET0); the part used here is its data completeness.

## What it holds

- **Deployment (l. 5):** one solar-powered GSM/GPRS station in a Mediterranean vineyard in Antalya, Türkiye. The reading agent counts one station.
- **Duration (l. 166):** a full calendar year (2021 per the reading agent).
- **Completeness (l. 167):** "completeness rate of 98.6%" over the year.
- **Missing days (l. 168):** five days of missing data, non-consecutive, "attributable solely to intermittent GSM/GPRS" link problems; about 1.4 % of days (5 of 365, arithmetic).
- **Not read by the reading agent:** the ET0 bias results beyond the abstract (regional station overestimates ET0; mean bias error -0.695 mm/day in the abstract, text file header line).
