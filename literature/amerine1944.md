# Amerine & Winkler 1944: California's five climatic regions by degree-days above 50 °F

The printed source of the Winkler index: heat summation April to October in degree-days above 50 °F, with five region bounds.

**Read this first.** The record says "10 °C from 1 April". That is a conversion, not printed. The paper prints degree-days above 50 °F for the period April to October inclusive (p. 504, l. 702). Only pp. 504 and 505 and the Table 4 footnote were read; the rest of the 184-page file was not. The region bounds are in °F degree-days; no Celsius figure is given.

## What it holds

- **Definition (p. 504, l. 702, 703):** regions grouped by "summation of heat as degree-days above 50° F for the period April to October inclusive". The text has no "from 1 April" wording: the period is the months April to October.
- **Region bounds (p. 505, l. 706 to 708), in °F degree-days:** I less than 2,500; II 2,501 to 3,000; III 3,001 to 3,500; IV 3,501 to 4,000; V 4,001 or more.
- **Degree-day (Table 4 footnote, l. 791):** the mean temperature minus 50 °F, times the days. The example is a five-day mean of 70 °F giving 100 degree-days. It is computed from period means, not from daily maxima and minima.
- **Data source (l. 790):** Table 4 uses US Weather Bureau Climatological Data Reports for California. The table's page was not checked in the page image.

## Dependence

- Station data of the US Weather Bureau. The regions were set by the authors against grape composition and wine quality, not against a model.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Record amerine1944.winkler_index:** the 50 °F base, April to October inclusive and the five bounds are confirmed. "10 °C from 1 April" differs from what is printed: the unit is °F, the base 50 °F equals 10 °C exactly by conversion, and the period is April to October, not an open-ended start date. - **Daily or monthly:** the paper sums from period means. An engine that sums daily means or max/min averages differs in method and should say so.
