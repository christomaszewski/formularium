# Twomey et al. 2015: partial resistance of hop cones to powdery mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Powdery mildew (Podosphaera macularis) on hop cones; a 15-21 day window after bloom was most severe (l. 21).
- Greenhouse bract incidence fell after 30 to 40 days after bloom (l. 526); bracteoles after 20 to 30 days (l. 531).
- Field: severity greatest at stages I and II (l. 562); incidence on the last date 100%, 94% and 86% in 2011-2013 (l. 824-825).
- Logistic rate 0.52, 0.36 and 0.28 for 2011, 2012 and 2013 (l. 826-827).
- Season-long and juvenile-only fungicide gave similar incidence (l. 40).
