# Savu et al. 2020: excoriose frequency and microclimate in Transylvanian vineyards

A one-season survey of excoriose (Phomopsis viticola) on four plots. It records the attack frequency before and after ten fungicide treatments.

## What it holds

- **Plots and dates:** four plots, March to August 2020 (abstract, l. 22-25). Assessments on 13 March, 27 June and 16 August 2020 (l. 96-98).
- **Frequency:** 4 to 12% at the start of the season. It fell to 0 to 7% after the fungicide treatments (abstract, l. 28-30).
- **Treatments:** ten treatments with contact and systemic products, metiram and sulphur among them (abstract, l. 25; Table 2, l. 115-153).
- **Climate:** January to July 2020 at Ciumbrud averaged 10.35 C in air, with 420 mm of rain (l. 182-183).
- **Microclimate:** monthly rain and temperature are set against the long-term averages (l. 186-191). No fitted model of frequency against weather is printed in the part read.

## Dependence

- Spore release and dieback biology from Urbez-Torres et al. 2010 and 2013, and Gramaje et al. 2018 (l. 75-78).
- Regional survey of Tomoiaga and Chedea 2020 (l. 61-62).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (other grape diseases: excoriose). A simulator could check a Phomopsis rule against the 4-12% to 0-7% range. The data cover one season, four plots and no model.
