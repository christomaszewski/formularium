# Amirshekari & Fakhroleslam 2025: artificial light, ET and growth in plant factories

Research article, Indoor plant factory, lettuce; data from literature (authors at Tarbiat Modares University, Tehran, Iran); no field region.

## What it holds

- An integrated model of LED lamp heat loss, lettuce evapotranspiration, photosynthesis, LAI and plant weight in an indoor plant factory.
- The ET equation is a modified Penman-Monteith-type form in LAI, light and VPD, fitted to literature lettuce data.
- Class A only by the letter of the ET criterion; it is indoor lettuce under artificial light, so transfer to vineyards is nil and B is arguable.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Indoor lettuce under lamps: off-topic for a vineyard.
