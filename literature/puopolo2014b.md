# Puopolo et al. 2014: Lysobacter with copper against grapevine downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Greenhouse study (Pinot Noir, 20 C, 70 per cent RH) of the bacterium Lysobacter capsici AZ78 against Plasmopara viticola, alone and with copper hydroxide (l. 179-183, 194-198).
- AZ78 alone: incidence 63 per cent against 100 in untreated; severity 5 against 67 per cent (l. 323, 330).
- With copper hydroxide at 1.25 and 0.625 g/L: incidence 30 and 39 per cent, an additive effect (l. 326-327).
- Copper tolerance: no survival loss to 300 µg/mL copper sulphate; tenfold drop at 500 (l. 296-298).
- One day after spraying, AZ78 was 5.41 and 6.57 log10 CFU per g leaf at normal and high RH (l. 353).
- Inoculum 2.5 × 10^5 sporangia per mL; overnight sporulation at 20 C and 80 to 99 per cent RH (l. 192, 211).
