# Deore et al. 2021: polyoxin D against grape powdery mildew, and its residues

Two field seasons in India of a polyoxin D zinc salt fungicide against grape powdery mildew, with its residue half-life in grape.

## What it holds

- **Design** (l. 95–99, 134–138): Tas-A-Ganesh vines in 2014–15 and 2015–16; seven treatments in a randomised block design with four replications.
- **Efficacy** (Table 2): at 600 ml/ha, disease control was 56.4 % on leaves and 75.7 % on bunches (l. 26). The three triazoles gave 20 to 38 % control on leaves.
- **Yield**: 600 ml/ha gave 57.47 % more yield than the untreated control (l. 34).
- **Residues**: a first-order fit with r² above 0.99; DT50 of 8.0 days at the recommended dose and 14.5 days at double dose (l. 42–43; Table 4, l. 377–378). Initial deposits were 0.52 and 0.89 mg/kg (l. 370).

## Dependence

- Assumes pseudo first-order kinetics, which the paper says is generally assumed (l. 120).
- Residue extraction is a modified QuEChERS method (l. 264).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t1 (fungicide protection): the residue half-lives (l. 42–43) could set a re-spray or pre-harvest check for polyoxin D, if other sites agree. The paper gives a pre-harvest formula (l. 219) but no pre-harvest interval value.
- One site, one cultivar, two seasons. The text cites the dissipation data as Table 3 (l. 382), but they are in Table 4. Not read in full.
