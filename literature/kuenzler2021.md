# Kuenzler et al. 2021: Wädenswil downy mildew dates for 2019 and 2020

A January 2021 bulletin of Agroscope Wädenswil (German-speaking Switzerland) that prints the model date of oospore maturity for 2020 and 2019 and the first oil spots of 2020.

**Read this first.**
- Only the lines on oospore maturity and first oil spots were read. The oospore dates are model dates (Agrometeo temperature sum), not germination tests.
- The first-spot date of 4 June 2020 conflicts with the 12 June of the season review (see agroscope2021). Both are kept.
- The author list is printed as "Kuenzler, L. et al."; the other authors were not read.

## What it holds

- **Oospore maturity, 2020:** the temperature sum for spore maturity of downy mildew (Plasmopara viticola) was reached on 21 April 2020 (l. 35). The source is Agrometeo, a model.
- **Oospore maturity, 2019:** 22 May 2019, printed as the previous year in the comparison with 21 April 2020 (l. 35). Model date.
- **First oil spots, 2020:** 4 June at Wädenswil, in the untreated plot, cultivar Müller-Thurgau (l. 37).
- **Gap in the model dates:** 2020 is 31 days earlier than 2019 (arithmetic from l. 35, not printed).

## Dependence

- Model dates come from VitiMeteo-Plasmopara/Agrometeo, the model of Dubuis et al. 2012 (a temperature sum above 8 C from 1 January, 140 or 160 degree-days). The first oil spots are field observations in an untreated plot. Dubuis et al. 2012 say Changins observations serve to validate that model and let experts adjust it, so Changins dates are not independent of VitiMeteo (see dubuis2012). Nothing else is recorded.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Agrarium D67 (oospore model starts too early in Germany):** a model date for northern Switzerland, 21 April 2020 and 22 May 2019. It is a model output, so it cannot test the oospore model's start date.
- **literature/oospore_maturity_2026-11-03.csv:** two model rows (Wädenswil 2019 and 2020), not laboratory germination.
- **literature/real_outcomes_2026-11-03.csv:** one first-spot row (Wädenswil 2020, untreated Müller-Thurgau), with the 4 June against 12 June conflict flagged. Kind of evidence: a bulletin's observation by the station.
