# Pillet et al. 2012: heating only the bunch of potted Cabernet Sauvignon cuttings by 6 and 9 C, and what it did to galactinol genes

**Read this first.** This is a controlled heating experiment on potted fruiting cuttings in a greenhouse, not a vineyard measurement. Its use to Agrarium is as a record of berry-temperature differences and a method, not as a microclimate model. The degree sign is lost in the extracted text, so temperatures are quoted without it.

## What it holds

- **Plants (l. 762):** Cabernet Sauvignon fruiting cuttings (Ollat and Gaudillere 1998), 0.5 L pots, one cluster each, 16 leaves per plant, drip irrigation five times a day, chosen at veraison for similar vigour and bunch compactness.
- **Greenhouse air (l. 784):** maximum 35.4, minimum 15.5, mean 23.3 C over the period.
- **Heating rig (l. 790-795):** fan heaters of 1000 W blowing on bunches only; shoots, leaves and roots shielded by extruded polystyrene deflectors. Control bunches had fan blowers (airflow without heating) and "closely matched" air temperature. Heating 07:00 to 19:00 each day for 21 days (l. 162), "to mimic the usual diurnal temperature course of exposed berries, although in a simpler way".
- **Sensors (l. 811):** copper-constantan thermocouples inserted into berries, three replicates per treatment, on a Campbell datalogger, continuous. Height not applicable (potted plants).
- **Berry temperatures (l. 167):** daily mean 30.2 +/- 2.2 C (control), 36.5 +/- 2.5 C (TEMP1), 39.7 +/- 2.3 C (TEMP2); differences 6.3 +/- 0.3 and 9.5 +/- 0.4 C (l. 170). The set-up was repeated at veraison and mid-maturation.
- **Introduction (l. 131):** "the temperature of reproductive organs can vary considerably compared with the whole plant or surrounding air", citing Spayd 2002, Pieri and Fermaud 2005 and Cola 2009.

## Dependence

- Own experiment. The rig was reused unchanged in ouakedlecourieux2017 and lecourieux2020 and summarised in torregrosa2017. Shares no equation with the engine's pieces.
- Authors: Pieri is the third author; a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- **Berry temperature range:** the +6 to +9.5 C imposed here is stated to be in the range seen between exposed and shaded berries; the control berries (30.2 C mean over 07:00 to 19:00) were in a warm greenhouse, so the absolute values are not vineyard values. No disease measured. Not shown: any relation of berry temperature to air or radiation.
