# Bonanomi et al. 2025: compost tea and fungicides vs downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A two-year field trial (2023 and 2024) on an 18-year-old Montepulciano vineyard at Cerignola, Puglia, at 2500 plants per hectare (l. 219-221).
- Compost tea at 600 L per hectare every 10 to 14 days, 13 applications, plus 2 cymoxanil sprays, against 15 synthetic fungicide sprays (l. 236-241).
- Leaf and bunch severity, end of July, 20 plants per block (l. 247-248).
- In 2023, a rainy year (over 30 rainy days, over 300 mm in May and June), compost tea had lower severity (l. 410-417).
- In 2024 severity was low (4.1% and 4.9%) and did not differ (p = 0.78) (l. 413-414).
- Lab assays: compost tea cut infection on leaves and dried sporangia on disks (l. 505-509).
