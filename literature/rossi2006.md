# Rossi et al. 2006: the model before Rossi et al. 2008

## What it holds

- A systems-analysis model, hourly, from oospore maturation to symptoms: all oospores
  morphologically mature on 1 January; dormancy broken by cohorts with a normal density;
  germination of a cohort when rain wets the litter; survival of macrosporangia by
  temperature and RH; zoospore survival on leaf wetness; incubation 'influenced by T and
  RH'.
- 'Equations linking the meteorological parameters to the rate variables were elaborated
  from both literature and data from specific studies': no equation, dataset or regression
  is printed. A worked example, not a validation.

## Dependence

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)
