# Camargo 2017: P. viticola population genetics and downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- 516 P. viticola isolates from 11 São Paulo municipalities; clade aestivalis the only cryptic species (l. 255-257, 271).
- 2015/16: 55 multilocus genotypes; four held 65.7% of isolates; the epidemic was mostly clonal (l. 272-273, 282).
- Three seasons, 2014/15-2016/17, at Piracicaba on three training systems (l. 262, 2431).
- Maximum mean downy mildew severity 14.3% (trellis) and 30.9% (open Y) (l. 275).
- Yield loss 0.7-0.9 kg per plant and 67-78 g per bunch at those severities (l. 276).
- Plastic cover cut incidence by up to 93.7% (l. 279).
- Leaf wetness: 8.8 h mean on the trellis, 9.5 h on the open Y (l. 3313-3314).
- Season 2014/15: all plants symptomatic 54-57 days into the epidemic; final severity 0.45% (l. 2634, 3295).
- Cited thresholds: primary infection with rain of 2.5 mm or more, 11 C or more (Kennelly 2007a); 3 h of wetness at 20-25 C (Caffi 2016) (l. 2210, 3230).

## Dependence

- cited, not computed (flag). The introduction (l. 2210-2212) and discussion (l. 3217-3220) restate Kennelly et al. 2007a's primary-infection conditions (rain of 2.5 mm or more, 11 C or more, from budburst), which is the primary trigger on the engine list; the same passage gives Kennelly's 4-19 day incubation (l. 2233-2234). The thesis counts such days in its own weather (l. 2636-2665) but computes no trigger. Caffi et al. 2016 (3 h of wetness at 20-25 C, l. 3230) and Kennelly 2007b (sporangia viability, l. 2232) are cited as facts. Shared authorship only (flag): Kennelly, Rossi (Rossi et al. 2013 cited at l. 2187).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- The richest downy mildew data in the set for a truth: three seasons of weekly incidence and severity with sensor leaf wetness and station weather, at Piracicaba; its primary-infection thresholds are cited, not computed, so they are not a model.
