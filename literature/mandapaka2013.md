# Mandapaka & Qin 2013: rain correlation against distance among 49 gauges in Singapore

A dense-gauge study in the tropics giving how fast rain correlation falls with distance, from hourly to daily totals.

**Read this first.**
- Tropical convective rain, 710 km2; use for an order of magnitude only, not for Mediterranean vineyards. The correlation values at 2, 5 and 10 km below are the reading agent's arithmetic from Table 3 under an assumed stretched-exponential form, not printed values.

## What it holds

- **E-folding distance (l. 27):** the distance at which correlation drops to 1/e varies from 10 km (hourly) to 33 km (daily).
- **Table 3, 24 h row (l. 503):** correlation distance 32.61 km, exponent 0.55, in r = exp(-(d/dc)^s) (form assumed by the reading agent). That gives daily r of about 0.81 at 2 km, 0.70 at 5 km and 0.59 at 10 km (the reading agent's arithmetic).
- **Gauge spacing (l. 159):** nearest-neighbour distance 0.8 to 7.9 km, mean 2.7 km.
- **Wet days (l. 159):** it rained on 51 % of days on average (hourly data, tropical).
