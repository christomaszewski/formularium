# Rossi et al. 2025: resistance inducers on berry clusters

Research article, Piacenza, northern Italy (cultivars Barbera, Merlot).

## What it holds

- Efficacy of six plant resistance inducers on downy mildew severity of Barbera and Merlot clusters, inoculated 1-19 days after treatment and at GS 60, 69-71 and 75.
- Efficacy rises with cluster stage, interacting with berry ontogenic resistance, but no susceptibility function is given.
