# Gessler et al. 2011: the standard review of grapevine downy mildew

## What it holds

- Two copies were dropped (`PDF.pdf` and the JSTOR one).
- **Oospores:** germination needs soil at 12-13 °C (sometimes 11 °C) and moisture; Arens
  1926's germination times fell from 8.8 days (21 March) to 4.4 (27 April) and 2 (10 June).
  Gehmann et al. 1987's German rule: first maturity at 160 °C·days above 8 °C from 1 January
  at 2 m, not quantitative.
- **Incubation:** Merjanian & Lipetzkaya 1936: 61 divided by the mean daily 'active'
  temperature (13-24 °C). Goidanich's tables: incubation by temperature at low and high
  humidity.
- **Sporulation:** most models still use Blaeser's parameters: at least 98 % RH, 4 h of
  darkness, a minimum temperature, completed in about 7 h.
- **Rules:** Baldacci 1947's primary rule (minimum about 10 °C, 10 mm of rain in 48 h,
  leaves of 6-8 cm²); the 3-10 algorithm of commercial devices is 'not much different from'
  the Goidanich model.
- **Models:** Blaise & Gessler 1990 needed a field-calibrated start; Rossi et al. 2008 and
  Caffi et al. 2009 add rain and humidity to degree-days.
