# Martínez de Toda & Ramos 2019: Tempranillo maturity at three Rioja elevations, 2008 to 2018

Tempranillo phenology and berry composition in three Rioja zones at 376 to 650 m, with maturity up to 21 d later in the cooler, higher zone; the only elevation figure for veraison is a cited unpublished comparison.

**Read this first.** The 21 d is for the date of maturity (13° potential alcohol), not budbreak, and the zones differ in regional climate (Atlantic Rioja Alta against Mediterranean Rioja Oriental), not only in height. The abstract gives the lowest elevation as 367 m (l. 22) but the plot list gives 376 m (l. 88-89). The text layer interleaves two columns; the quotes are left-column fragments.

## What it holds

- **Zones and vines:** Tempranillo in Rioja DOCa, 2008 to 2018 (l. 26): RA1 (Haro and Cenicero) at 465 and 450 m, RA2 at 635 and 650 m, RO (Rioja Oriental, Alfaro and Aldeanueva de Ebro) at 376 and 396 m (l. 80-89). Vines planted 1993 to 1999, bush trained, mainly rainfed, 3,000 to 3,500 vines ha-1, yield below 6,500 kg ha-1 (l. 89-91).
- **Phenology scored:** only stages H and M (Baillod and Baggiolini) are analysed, as the date when at least 50 % of the plants reached the stage; maturity as the date a given probable alcohol (PVAD) was reached; budbreak and flowering information came from earlier works, not from this study (l. 110-122).
- **Maturity dates:** PVAD 13° was reached on average on 13 September in RO, 13 d later (26 September) in RA1 and 21 d later (4 October) in RA2 (l. 759-767). Mean growing-season temperature 16.8 °C in RA1, 15.6 °C in RA2 and 18.6 °C in RO, with the maximum in RO up to 3.3 °C higher than in the RA zones (l. 781-785).
- **Elevation in the abstract:** "differences between the analysed elevations of up to 21 days" in the date maturity was reached (l. 29), larger in extreme years.
- **Cited, unpublished:** veraison on 25 to 31 July in vineyards at about 290 m (Costers del Segre) against 15 to 20 August at 800 to 890 m (Ribera del Duero), about 20 d over roughly 550 m, near 0.036 d per m by the reading agent's division (l. 789-795).
- **Cited earlier work:** Failla et al. 2004 found elevation influenced budbreak and bloom dates (l. 50-51); Ramos et al. 2015a found Tempranillo dates differing between plots at different elevations in Ribera del Duero, earlier at lower elevation from stage G onward (l. 54-57).
