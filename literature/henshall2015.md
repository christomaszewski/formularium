# Henshall et al. 2015: modelled wetness overestimates in New Zealand

## What it holds

- CART wetness (Kim et al. 2002; rules not printed here) against 237 sensors at seven sites
  (six named), as input to the BDS early-season Botrytis model, which uses Bacchus.
- CART overestimated wetness at most sites (about 1.7-1.9 times at Te Puke, by eye) and
  underestimated at Pukekohe in two of three seasons. BDS's epidemic call agreed at two of
  three sites; at Blenheim modelled wetness called major epidemics where measured wetness
  called minor ones.

## Dependence

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Modelled wetness errs by site and season enough to flip a disease model's verdict.
