# Pelissier et al. 2026: eight years of the OSCAR network on disease-resistant grape varieties

A bioRxiv preprint on spray counts and disease on resistant varieties in commercial French vineyards, 2018 onward.

**Read this first.**
- It is a preprint. The reading agent read the abstract, methods and the discussion on treatment frequency and severity, not the whole paper.
- The 79 % cut compares OSCAR with a national reference for conventional vines, not with paired plots.
- Whether the OSCAR data are public is not stated in the text read.

## What it holds

- **Network (l. 54):** over 199 commercial plots, 127 ha, since 2018.
- **Spray need (l. 36, 508):** fungicide treatment frequency index (TFI) averaged 2.03 in OSCAR against 10.1 for the conventional reference (Agrest 2019), a 79 % cut.
- **By year (l. 511):** TFI 3.12 in 2024 (69.1 % cut) against 0.8 in 2019 (92.1 % cut): the saving is smaller in high-pressure years.
- **Leaf severity (l. 522):** mean annual probability of high downy mildew severity (score 2 or more, above 1 % severity) on leaves 0.15. In 2024, called highly conducive, 80 % of plots had under 5 % leaf severity (l. 523).
- **Leaf to cluster (l. 529):** the probability of high cluster severity given high leaf severity 0.43 for downy mildew and 0.63 for powdery mildew.
- **Erosion (l. 37):** the efficacy of some Rpv factors against downy mildew declines with plot age (highlights); the detail is not in the facts read.
- **Black rot (l. 38):** rising in low-input resistant plots.
- **Definitions (l. 249, 542):** plot severity is incidence times mean severity of affected leaves (50 % x 25 % = 12.5 %); disease pressure is scored 0 to 3 on neighbouring susceptible vines.

## Dependence

- Compared against the national TFI reference (Agrest 2019). No model is computed. Shared authors with ramirez2025 (Miclot, Mazet, Delière, Fabre, Delmotte) are a flag, not a dependency.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Agrarium, resistant varieties:** a simulator that treats resistance as fixed is contradicted for some Rpv loci: efficacy falls with plot age. Observational field evidence, 199 plots; the size of the fall is not in the facts read.
- **Cooptera, spray counts:** resistant-variety plots need about 2 treatments (TFI 2.03) against 10.1, rising to 3.12 in 2024; spray need still tracks pressure.
- **Observation scale:** leaf severity is a poor proxy for cluster severity (0.43 for downy mildew).
