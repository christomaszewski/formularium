# Jermini et al. 2010: a damage function and a minimal spray strategy on Merlot

## What it holds

- Merlot at Cadenazzo (Ticino), 1995-2004.
- **Damage function** (photosynthesis or yield against severity, own fits): main leaves y =
  -0.0087x² - 0.1338x + 100.31 (R² 0.979); lateral leaves y = 0.008x² - 1.5045x + 92.364
  (R² 0.948); units not printed.
- **1998:** untreated canopy about 48 % severity late in the season; yield 1.02 kg/m²
  against 1.36 under standard sprays.
- **Minimal strategy 1999-2002:** 43-66 % fewer sprays, 17 % less yield; target 1 %
  severity at veraison to 5 % at the end of August.

## Dependence

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A measured link from severity to yield, for an evaluator that scores losses.
