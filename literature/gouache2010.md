# CLIMATOR proceedings 2010: the vine summary (Pieri and Lebon) and the fungicide summary (Gouache et al.)

The 2010 results meeting of CLIMATOR. Two chapters matter here, each two pages.

**Read this first.** The fungicide chapter covers Botrytis on merlot, septoria and brown rust of wheat, and a wetness variable. No downy mildew was modelled (no occurrence of "mildiou" or "mildew" in the file). The volume is a book of summaries, so models are named but not printed.

## What it holds

- **Vine design (p. 38):** ARPEGE under SRES A1B, 3 regionalisations, two crop models (BHV, STICS), dry and irrigated, three cultivars (Chardonnay, Merlot, Grenache), densities 0.3 and 0.9 plant m-2, three soils with available water 73, 150 and 193 mm (the green book prints 226 mm for the large soil, brisson2010), 12 sites; periods PR 1970-99, FP 2020-49, FL 2070-99 (l. 2429-2435).
- **Results (p. 38-39):** the cycle is 20 to 40 days earlier at current sites (l. 2442); Colmar, Versailles or Rennes can grow merlot as safely as Bordeaux today; ripening temperature rises by at least 5 C (l. 2488) with a risk to polyphenols.
- **Disease models (p. 46, l. 2954-2980):** septoria, brown rust, vine Botrytis (Fermaud et al. 2003), and wetness (Lhomme and Jimenez 1992). The vine model ignores climate effects on the winter phase.
- **Wetness numbers (p. 47, l. 3014-3019):** mean wetness duration in spring and summer 8.19 and 8.27 h (recent past) falling to 7.84 and 7.32 h (distant future); wet degree-days 2.92 and 4.58 rising to 3.52 and 4.75. The text does not say they are means over sites.
- **Sprays:** fewer Botrytis sprays where merlot is already grown, especially humid Bordeaux; many at newly feasible sites.

## Dependence

- Summary of brisson2010 and gouache2011 (same runs, same authors); counts once. Botrytis and wetness models are not held.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)
