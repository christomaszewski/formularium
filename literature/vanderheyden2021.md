# Van der Heyden et al. 2021: review of airborne inoculum monitoring

A review of spore sampling systems in agriculture and the considerations in monitoring airborne inoculum (l. 24-26). No record.

## What it holds

- **Sections:** types of samplers, sampling frequency and duration, sampling height, spatial patterns and dispersion, spore sampler networks, spore counting, thresholds (l. 37-45).
- **Table 2:** volumetric spore samplers with flow rate and efficiency d50, for example Burkard 7 Day at 10 L/min and d50 2.2 um, Rotorod at 100 to 150 L/min with d50 above 10 um (l. 358-368).
- **Finding:** the use of aerobiological data to study the escape of inoculum from a source is well mastered, but landscape-scale deployment lacks knowledge, and surveillance networks are in early stages (l. 26-30).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s1 (autumn inoculum) and s5 (sensor error):** sampler efficiency by particle size, and the point that networks are still immature. A simulator could take the d50 cut-off as a size-dependent trap efficiency when it compares trap counts with modelled spore release.
