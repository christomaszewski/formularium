# Mahtour et al. 2010: leaf wetness varies with distance from a hedge

A conference abstract on leaf wetness duration (LWD) at five distances from a tall hedge in a
  wheat field; the abstract is the only text (about 20 lines in a 19,731-line abstract book).

## What it holds

- **Measured:** LWD with pairs of flat plate sensors at the flag leaf, 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100 m
  from an 18 m hedge, near Arlon, May to July 2006 and 2007 (l. 6973-6993); dew collected on
  epoxy plates beside each sensor.
- **Result:** LWD longer nearer the hedge, from its shadow; "3 to 4 hours of difference"
  between 5 m and 100 m (l. 6998-7000).
- **Not printed:** sensor model, dew amounts, thresholds, any equation.

## Dependence

- None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s4 (measured vineyard microclimate), weakly: a wheat field, not a vineyard. A simulator could
  take only the size of the effect: a tall windbreak or shade object shifts LWD by 3 to 4 h
  within 100 m. Abstract only; not read in full.
- s5 (sensor error): flat-plate wetness sensors are the instrument; no error data.
