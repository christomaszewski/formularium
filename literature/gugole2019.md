# Gugole et al. 2019: copper on vine leaves, rachis and berries across a season on two farms

A two-page conference abstract: copper per square metre of leaf, rachis and berries sampled twice a month from fruit set to veraison on two farms.

**Read this first.** Only an abstract: no table, no figure data, no rain record and no wash-off rate. The farms are not named (the authors are at San Michele all'Adige, Trento), so the region is not stated. Values are total copper per m² of organ after an acid wash, so old and new copper are mixed. The 5 to 10 mg/m² efficacy range is cited from Cabus et al. 2017 (not obtained).

## What it holds

- **Design (l. 24):** Incrocio Manzoni and Pinot noir on two farms with two training systems; four repetitions twice a month between fruit set and veraison; each leaf sample made of 90 leaf discs of 2.7 cm diameter, washed with nitric acid and read by ICP.
- **Copper by organ (l. 34):** on leaves 23 to 47 mg/m², about double the rachis (9 to 22 mg/m²) and triple the internal and indirectly exposed berries (2 to 10 mg/m²); cumulative over the season with several sprays.
- **Threshold (l. 43, cited):** on one farm (Incrocio Manzoni, 3.4 kg Cu/ha over the season) the copper on bunches reached the 5 to 10 mg/m² efficacy range of Cabus et al. 2017 only around veraison.
- **Rain (l. 46):** copper accumulates on the canopy over the season despite run-off caused by rain; no rain record or wash-off rate is printed.

## Dependence

- **Own** field data (Fondazione Edmund Mach). Uses the efficacy range of Cabus et al. 2017. See `zanzotti2022` for the same group's leaf-disc dose curve. None recorded of the engine's models.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Deposit variability and the season's copper (M4):** a field measurement on vine leaves and organs: season-accumulated copper on leaves 23 to 47 mg/m² against 2 to 10 mg/m² on inner berries. It is the contrast between organs, not between canopy positions, and the leaf values far exceed the 2.5 to 7.5 mg/m² of the single-spray dose curve in `zanzotti2022` because they hold several sprays.
- **Wash-off:** qualitative only (copper persists on leaves despite rain over weeks). Decay with time, growth dilution and dose-efficacy: not addressed. Kind of result: abstract-only field measurement.
