# Kast 1994: first results with a trap for infectious downy mildew sporangia

A two-page note on a trap that counts only viable sporangia, tried at Weinsberg in 1992 and 1993. No record. No DOI is printed; 10.5073/vitis.1994.33.253-254 is the lead key only and was not checked. Related notes: `kast1993`, `kast1999`.

## What it holds

- **Trap** (l. 7, 15-29): a fan draws air and splash into a case; the separation chamber holds about 1 ml of water (the text layer says "rnl"; the image reads ml). A rotating plate carries 14 petri dishes of agar; leaf disks on them grow sporangiophores after a week if viable sporangia were present, after storage at about 23 degC for 16 h in daylight. Leaves are not the catching surface (the 1979 Schwarzbach jet trap uses leaf segments for powdery mildew, l. 34-36).
- **Trials** (l. 32, 35, 51, 53): one trap in 1992 and two in 1993, in an untreated vineyard. Powdery mildew, not downy, was on about 0.5 % of leaves when the traps were positioned and up to 100 % of leaves in September 1992 and 4 % in September 1993.
- **Roof trap** (l. 59-63): a third trap in 1993 on the institute's flat roof, about 300 m from the next possibly infected vines and 900 m from the infected test site.
- **Counts** (l. 65, 67): viable sporangia detected in 58 cases in 1992 (one trap) and 56 cases in 1993 (three traps together). The unit of a case is not defined; there are no dated counts.
- **Findings** (l. 68-82): no difference between times of day; counts did not correlate with wind speed, temperature or humidity, and did correlate with rainfall. The HP-100 adviser (Lufft; an index of viable sporangia said to be based on Blaser 1978, equations not printed, l. 59) predicted 0 % viable in some periods when sporangia were caught (l. 75), and agreed with counts only for the trap near infected vines.
- **Hypotheses** (l. 88-93): release into water (Blaser 1978) gives new, quickly germinating sporangia, very local spread and high damage; release into dry air gives older, slower sporangia with no relevant damage potential.
- **Not printed:** any catch efficiency, counts by date, the unit of a case, a wetness measure, the time step.

## Dependence

- The trap computes no model. The HP-100 index is Lufft's, "based on" Blaser 1978; nothing printed says it was fitted to data. Nothing here is a leaf-wetness estimate and no RH threshold appears.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

Strand 1 (autumn inoculum), with strand 5 (sampler error).

- **A viability filter on a sampler:** the trap counts infectious sporangia only, which a molecular count (see `douillet2023`) does not. No efficiency is printed for it either.
- **Rain, not weather, correlates:** counts followed rainfall and not wind, temperature or humidity, in two seasons of one note; the RH threshold plays no part here.
- **A check on a viability index:** a commercial index predicted 0 % when sporangia were caught; it matched only the trap near infected vines.
- **Kind of result:** a field trial with case totals, no dates.
