# Prillieux 1883: germination of Peronospora viticola oospores, French note

## What it holds

- A note by Éd. Prillieux read to the Société botanique de France on 30 March 1883 (l. 78).
- Winter spores (oospores) are the only route to spring infection, since no mycelium survives winter in vine tissue (l. 60-63).
- At Nérac, M. Fréchou saw oospores in infected leaf fragments begin to germinate (l. 89).
- The germinating oospore sends out a branching germ tube, not zoospores as in Cystopus candidus (l. 94-99).
- The note cites Millardet's view that only grape seeds germinating in the field could be infected directly by oospores (l. 82).
