# Johnson & Lakso 1991: a short guide to orchard light models

A review for horticulturists. Used here as the source for the two hedgerow forms it describes, because the primary papers (Charles-Edwards & Thorpe 1976; Jackson & Palmer 1979) were not obtainable.

## What it holds

- **Charles-Edwards & Thorpe 1976** (l. 94-106): ellipsoid tree canopy, uniform leaf area, random leaf angles, Beer's law with a constant K for direct and diffuse; scattering ignored at first; agreed well with light interception measured in an apple hedgerow; leaf clumping was the main limit.
- **Jackson & Palmer 1979** (l. 98-103): transmitted fraction T = Tf + Tc, Tf the share of light reaching the floor without meeting a tree (from the shadow of a solid model of the hedgerow), Tc = (1 - Tf) exp(-K L); interception 1 - T. K 0.55-0.60 for apple, 0.60 used. Inputs: canopy and tree spacing dimensions and total leaf area.
- Nothing grape-specific; no error figures for either.

## Dependence

- A review: no equation is the paper's own. Beer's law and the two models above are others'.

## Bearing (2026-10-28)

- The Jackson & Palmer split (shadow of a solid hedgerow, plus Beer's law through the remainder) is the simplest complete form; Riou's walls are its porous-wall version. The primary papers are still to find.
