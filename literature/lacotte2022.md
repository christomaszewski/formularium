# Lacotte et al. 2022: hyperspectral images see downy mildew two days before symptoms

A triage note (the 2026-10-12 library backlog; the 2026-10-09 search had this DOI down as
Fenu & Malloci's machine-learning paper, which it is not).

## What it holds

- Hyperspectral images of grapevine leaves inoculated with three P. viticola strains,
  followed from 0 to 9 days post inoculation in a growth chamber, classified pixel by
  pixel with a support vector machine (l. 30-33).
- Validation accuracy 0.96, test accuracy 0.99, no false positives on healthy leaves
  (l. 33-34); infection detected at 2 days post inoculation, two days before visible
  symptoms, over a larger area than visual scoring (triage reading of the abstract).
- No field site, weather or epidemic data.

## Dependence

- none. Raynal and Delmotte (Bordeaux) are authors of `douillet2022`, `chen2019` and
  `poeydebat2025`: flags only.

## Bearing (2026-10-12)

- An observation method with a measured lead of two days over symptoms, of the kind
  `oerke2016`, `bellow2013` and `cohen2022` already record; nothing for the engine.
