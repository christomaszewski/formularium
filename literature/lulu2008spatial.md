# Lulu et al. 2008 (Eng. Agríc.): wetness across a vineyard canopy

Chapter 2 of [lulu2008thesis](lulu2008thesis.md).

## What it holds

- Four canopy positions (top and bunches, two faces), three sensors each, against turf at
  30 cm; 115 days at Jundiaí.
- No significant difference between positions. Top south-west against turf: mean
  difference -0.42 h, MAD 1.11 h; y = 0.9976x - 0.391, R² 0.86. Days without rain R²
  0.71-0.77, with rain 0.88-0.91.

## Dependence

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A standard reference sensor tracks canopy-top wetness within about an hour a day:
  context for the truth's station-to-canopy wetness gap (M1's 2.80 h/day in Catalonia).
