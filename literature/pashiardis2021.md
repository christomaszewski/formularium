# Pashiardis & Kalogirou 2021: the BRL refitted in Cyprus

Read only for the units of the BRL's time and altitude predictors (`laiti2018.brl_bolzano`).

## What it holds

- AST is "the apparent solar time for the given day in hours" (their Eq 4-5 context), and the BRL is
  refitted on 2016-2019 data at their station: kd = 1/(1 + exp(−3.03 + 5.33kt + 0.008AST − 0.005αs +
  0.77KT − 0.2ψ)) (Eq 31), "close to those obtained by Ridley et al. (2010) at Adelaide". αs is the
  "solar altitude"; its unit in Eq 31 is not stated.

## Bearing (2026-10-19)

- A refit, not Ridley's own statement: it supports AST in hours for this paper's fit only. The units
  Laiti et al. used stay unread.
