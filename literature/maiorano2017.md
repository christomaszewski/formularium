# Maiorano et al. 2017: improving wheat models cuts ensemble error under heat

Fifteen wheat models are improved with heat-stress detail and re-checked on independent trials.

## What it holds

- **Design:** 15 models are recalibrated or changed for heat effects on phenology, leaf growth, senescence, biomass and grain, using the USDA Hot Serial Cereal data, then evaluated on a CIMMYT world-wide network (l. 278-286, 288).
- **Spread:** the ensemble's 10th-90th percentile range of yield fell by 39 % on the calibration set and 26 % on the evaluation set above 24 C (l. 287-291).
- **Error:** ensemble mean squared error fell by 37 % (l. 292); a 27 % smaller uncertainty range raised prediction skill by 47 % (l. 293-294).

## Dependence

- Builds on the wheat models of a larger ensemble and the Hot Serial Cereal data set (l. 278-290).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strand s6: ensemble error split into bias and variance, with model fixes shrinking the spread; a simulator could use the same check on its sweep models.
