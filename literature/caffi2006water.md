# Caffi & Rossi 2006: leaf-litter water and oospore germination

## What it holds

- Leaf-litter moisture over a day: usually above 20 % from 22:00 to 08:00, often below 20 %
  by day, lowest near 14:00. Sorption isotherms at four temperatures (5-20 °C); the modified
  Chen-Clayton equation fitted best.
- Oospore leaves held at fixed water activity (0.123-0.991) at 20 °C for 2-15 days, tested
  by Hill's floating leaf discs: at aw ≥ 0.785 (about 40 % moisture) infection came 1 day
  after wetting; at aw ≤ 0.487 (about 20 %), 2-10 days; 50 % and 90 % severity took about
  twice as long at the lowest aw.
