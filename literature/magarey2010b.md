# Magarey & Moyer 2010: epi-season timing to cut powdery mildew sprays

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Most early-season powdery mildew inoculum travels under 100 m until about day 80 from budburst (l. 18).
- Growers averaged 6-7 fungicide sprays a season; some used 12-13; some controlled it with 3-4 (l. 53-58).
- Industry benchmark proposed: 3-4 applications a season (l. 71).
- Inland, incidence rises significantly about day 40 from budburst; severity from about day 80 (l. 184).
- Epi-season spans two growing seasons (l. 124), and within-vineyard carry-over sets season 2 (l. 162).
