# Cortiñas Rodríguez et al. 2020: airborne spores at two Galician vineyards, 2016-2018

The same group as [fernandezgonzalez2011](fernandezgonzalez2011.md); no years in common.

## What it holds

- **Sites:** Cenlle (Ribeiro, Godello, 117 m) and O Mato (Ribeira Sacra, Mencía, 332 m),
  2016-2018; both treated, O Mato not against Botrytis.
- **Trap:** Hirst-type Lanzoni VPPS-2000 at 2 m; daily spores/m³. Weather from MeteoGalicia
  stations at Leiro and Monforte (distances not printed).
- **P. viticola sporangia, season totals:** Cenlle 1,910 / 679 / 3,605; O Mato 3,618 /
  1,363 / 5,656. Peak 502/m³ at O Mato on 7 July 2016. Botrytis and Erysiphe totals are
  larger.
- **Regressions** on lagged counts and weather, in-sample: adjusted R² for Plasmopara 0.399
  (Cenlle) and 0.558 (O Mato).
- **Printed slips:** Table 6's R and R² disagree for two O Mato rows; one study period runs
  "19 September 2016 to 18 September 2016"; 1,747 against 1,547 for a Botrytis peak. The
  discussion's 747 and 578 are the 2007 Cenlle totals of
  [fernandezgonzalez2009](fernandezgonzalez2009.md), quoted without a year.
