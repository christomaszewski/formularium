# Matasci et al. 2010: cultivar mixtures against downy mildew in organic vines

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Three-year (2005-2007) trial in Ticino of eight grapevine cultivars, planted in four within-row mixture blocks and one monoculture block (l. 20-24).
- 2005: mixing lowered disease on susceptible cultivars; resistant cultivars did better in monoculture (l. 49-57).
- 2006 and 2007: the mixing effect weakened; the authors cite weather and two copper sprays in 2007 (l. 76-81, 113-115).
- Genotype data (2005-2006): lower diversity of P. viticola on resistant cultivars (l. 119-128).
- Conclusion: mixtures are not an effective way to cut copper use (l. 141-145).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A small observed incidence-and-severity dataset on cultivar mixtures that the truth could use as a cultivar-susceptibility scenario, though it reports no weather or model values and its own conclusion is negative.
