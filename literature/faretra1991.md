# Faretra & Di Tonno 1991: cultivar susceptibility in the 1989 Apulian epidemic

## What it holds

- Downy mildew on leaves and clusters of 21 table, wine and seedless cultivars in the severe
  1989 epidemic in Apulia: table grapes were generally less damaged than wine grapes; among
  seedless ones Canner, Carina, Moscatello rosa and Noica had the least leaf damage.
