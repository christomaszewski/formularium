# Chacón-Vozmediano et al. 2020: LAI of deficit-irrigated Merlot in La Mancha

Four deficit-irrigation regimes, set by predawn water potential, on Merlot over two
seasons. Read for leaf area.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 132-168, 246-264): Merlot on Fercal at Argamasilla de Alba (DO La Mancha,
  670 m); ET0 about 1,300 mm a year, 1,056 to 1,087 mm April to September; spring rain 29.8
  and 61.9 mm. Rows 3 m, vines 1.2 m (2,778 vines/ha); double Royat cordon on three wires,
  two-bud spurs; 12 shoots a vine before flowering (l. 264). Vines 5 years old.
- **Water** (Table 2, l. 302-335; the table is jumbled, averages used): drip from 56.5 mm
  (driest regime, year 2) to 132.3 mm (wettest, year 1).
- **Leaf area** (Table 3, l. 407-420; LAI-2000, five points a vine, 10 vines a series;
  measured partly at veraison, l. 484): LAI 1.62, 1.64, 1.52, 1.37 (year 1) and 1.51, 1.36,
  1.30, 1.16 (year 2) from the lightest to the hardest stress; year means 1.54 and 1.33.
  External leaf area 0.72 to 0.86 m²/m², about half of the total (l. 545).
- **Not printed:** leaf area per vine, dates, budburst, canopy size, the calendar years.

## Dependence

- LAI-2000 after Johnson & Pierce; exposed area by an image grid after Stewart et al.;
  stress classes adapted from Carbonneau 1998. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Spanish deficit-irrigated cordon, 12 shoots on 1.2 m:** LAI 1.2 to 1.6 is 4.2 to 5.9 m²
  a vine and 0.35 to 0.49 m² a shoot (my calculation, over 3.6 m² of ground). LAI-2000
  tends to read low in rows (as Arnó et al. note), so these are lower bounds.
