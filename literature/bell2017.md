# Bell et al. 2017: how far field hygrometers drift, from 152 as-found checks

As-found errors of 152 Met Office weather-station hygrometers (Rotronic HygroClip HC2-S3) against chilled-mirror references, by humidity level, with a drift and bias estimate. No record.

**Read this first.** Readings are capped at 100 %rh, so the 90 and 95 %rh sets are censored: the authors say the mean and SD there do not describe the data (l. 512-514). Equation (6) (PDF page 9, l. 437) is a quadratic in x; the superscript is garbled in the text layer ("x 2") and is unchecked, so its coefficients are not copied here. This is the one paper in this batch that treats the 100 %rh cap.

## What it holds

- **Sample** (l. 52, 284-286): records of 152 weather-station hygrometers from 2013, typically one year in service, sometimes less; capacitive polymer sensors with a 0-100 %rh range (l. 181-185), UK exposure mainly 50-100 %rh (l. 186). Calibration uncertainty +/-2 %rh at k = 2 (l. 267).
- **As-found mean error** (Table 1, l. 326-333): -0.62 %rh at 20 %rh (n = 152); +1.48 at 80 (n = 100); +4.14 at 90 (SD 4.01, n = 100); +2.98 at 95 (SD 2.46, n = 146). At 90 %rh, 19 of 100 read 100 %rh (l. 367); at 95 %rh, 80 of 146 do (l. 369). Typical over-read 3 to 3.5 %rh at 90 and 95 (l. 374).
- **Drift** (l. 58, 522): mean in-service drift up to 5 %rh, from about -1.7 %rh at 20 %rh to +5.5 %rh at 95 %rh. Not a rate per year; another study is cited at more than 5 %rh per year for some types (l. 103).
- **Bias estimate** (l. 409, 438, 442): taken as half the full drift. Unadjusted: -0.8 %rh at 20 to +2.7 %rh at 95. Met Office adjusted: within +/-0.5 %rh from 20 to 85 %rh and +1.1 %rh at 95 %rh. Eq. (6) is the polynomial fit of mean drift against RH, zero near 60 %rh (l. 439).
- **Recommendation** (l. 487): meteorological hygrometers should not be limited in reading at 100 %rh.
- **Not printed:** prices; any failure or data-loss rate (some sensors fail in service, l. 251); wet or condensing conditions; temperature dependence; more than one sensor type.

## Dependence

- None recorded. The data are the Met Office laboratory's own records. Cited for drift: Willett et al., Lacombe et al. 2011, Miloshevich et al. 2001; shared authors with other notes are not a dependence.

## Bearing (2026-10-21)

Sensor menu of D48 and its observation operators.

- **Error model supported:** an RH bias and spread that grow with RH, plus a hard cap at 100 %rh: a sensor in a humid canopy can read 100 well below saturation, and the capped share is large (80 of 146 at a true 95 %rh). An observation operator for RH should clip at 100 and not add symmetric noise above 90. The sign is positive (over-read) at high RH, so an RH >= 90 % threshold fires early.
- **Not measured:** wet periods and condensation, radiation error against wind, price, loss, packet delivery. The drift is a population statistic over about a year, not a time series per sensor.
- **Kind of result:** laboratory as-found checks on returned field units (a metrology paper), UK network sensors.
