# Srinivasan & Jeyarajan 1976: sporangia collected by day did not germinate

## What it holds

- Leaves tapped at 4 p.m. to clear sporangia; new sporangia collected every 2 h from 6 p.m.
  to 4 p.m. next day, suspended at 2 × 10⁵ per ml and incubated in darkness at 30 °C
  (Coimbatore, India).
- Table I, % germinated after 7 h: 13.7 (8 p.m.), 31.6 (10 p.m.), 38.4 (midnight), 48.7 (2
  a.m.), 33.9 (4 a.m.), 30.3 (6 a.m.), 8.3 (8 a.m.); none from 6 p.m. and none from 10 a.m.
  to 6 p.m.
- The authors: sporangia 'were short lived and did not survive exposure to sunlight at the
  temperature above 30° C' prevailing by day.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Field-produced sporangia lose viability within hours of a hot, sunny morning: the same
  direction as Kennelly et al. 2007 and Zachos 1959's sun tests. Sent to Agrarium
