# Murakami et al. 2025: leaf removal on the east or west side

First author printed "Keach Murakami". A one-season trial of removing cluster-zone leaves on
the east side, the west side, both or neither of single-cordon Pinot gris. No record.

## What it holds

- **Site and design** (l. 85-166): rainfed Pinot gris, Mikasa, Hokkaido (43.255 °N,
  141.837 °E, 70 m), planted 2015, rows 2.8 m apart, hedgerow. Leaves removed on 18 August 2023
  from two or three cluster-zone leaves; treatments E, W, E+W and control, three replicates.
  Photosynthesis measured on 19 and 23 August; module averages 19 August to 18 September
  2023; RH 25 August to 18 September 2023.
- **Season:** the hottest August on record at JMA Iwamizawa; May to September 917.3 h of
  sunshine (+8.3 %) and 477.5 mm of rain (-16 %).
- **Sensors:** a pseudo-cluster module with two PYTOS-31 leaf-wetness sensors (west and east,
  20° off nadir) used as thermal surfaces (type T thermocouples behind them; no wetness
  output used), and a U23-002A RH sensor in the canopy middle; an HMP155 for air temperature
  and RH, ultrasonic wind and a pyranometer at a meteorological station at 10-min steps.
  Shields not stated.
- **Cluster temperature above air:** on average over 19 August to 18 September the E and E+W
  clusters were up to 4.8 °C above air in the morning (controls and W lower); on two sunny days
  10.0 °C (E) and 8.9 °C (E+W); the discussion says up to 5 °C from 10:00 to 19:00 and 5-10 °C
  above shaded clusters in low-wind mornings. Wind in the open peaked at 4.0 and 3.0 m/s
  at 12:00-15:00.
- **Humidity:** RH in the canopy middle fell slightly faster in the morning where east leaves
  were removed, with no magnitude printed. Hours with RH above 90 % did not differ among treatments
  (Fig. 7, a duration count, not leaf wetness).
- **Photosynthesis:** leaf position P = 0.015, canopy side P = 0.0016, no interaction.
- **Not printed:** RH differences by side and time of day, wetness, VPD, Tcum per treatment
  (Fig. 5 only), shield types, a data statement. Article CC BY 4.0.

## Dependence

- None computed. The 25 °C threshold of the cumulative temperature (Tcum) is from berry
  studies (Poudel et al. 2009; Zhang et al. 2015); the daily weather grid is NARO's, interpolated from
  JMA stations.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

For strand 4 (what a sensor on one side of the row sees):

- **What it shows:** exposed east-facing clusters in a hot summer ran 5 to 10 °C above air by
  morning and shaded ones stayed near air, so a sensor's side of the row and its exposure
  change its reading by more than the whole canopy-versus-station offset.
- **Take for sensor placement:** a leaf-wetness or temperature sensor on a sun-exposed face
  will read warm and dry early on the east side; either place it on the shaded face or
  record the side. RH hours above 90 % were not different, so the humidity count is less
  sensitive to the side than temperature is, from this one season.
- **Limits:** LIGHT reading; one season, one site, leaf removal as the treatment; no
  wetness measured.
