# Fei et al. 2021: Helios 3-D vineyard renders, restyled by a semantically constrained CycleGAN, for grape detection (preprint)

Pre-trains a YOLOv3-tiny grape detector on renders from the Helios 3-D plant model, then fine-tunes it on a few real labelled images with or without GAN-restyled renders. No record: a method, on a preprint.

**Read this first.** An arXiv preprint (not peer reviewed). The "fine-tuned" arm in the tables is itself pre-trained on the renders; the tables hold no real-only arm without the renders, so the benefit of rendering as such is not printed. The test sets are small (25 day and 24 night images, l. 274, 283) and show one vineyard region.

## What it holds

- **Generator** (l. 248 to 252): Helios, an open 3-D plant and environmental model, gave 500 synthetic vineyard images spanning canopy geometry, trellis type and camera position, with grape-cluster boxes written by a custom Helios plugin. 345 train and 74 validation renders trained the detector (l. 318).
- **Restyling** (l. 345, 418): a CycleGAN with a semantic loss that keeps grape position ("SemGAN"). A plain CycleGAN moves the boxes, so the source labels no longer fit. Only the grape is constrained: foliage, trunks and sky are free in the output.
- **Real samples** (l. 273, 274, 282, 283, 324): California Central Valley vineyard. Day: 3,065 images, 100 labelled, 25 always held out as the test set. Night: 800 images, 150 labelled, 24 always held out. N real labelled images varied from 2 to 50 (80 % train, 20 % validation); the GAN uses the same N labelled images and no more.
- **Test:** real held-out vineyard images of the same site and camera for each domain, separate from training.
- **Synthetic only, no real labels** (Table 1, l. 379, 380): AP at IoU 0.3 / 0.5 in per cent, day 27.8 / 13.2, night 0.0 / 0.8; plain CycleGAN images 10.3 / 2.1 day and 0.1 / 0.0 night, below synthetic-only. The authors: at night "almost no ability to predict grapes" (l. 339).
- **Few real labels** (Table 2, l. 393, 394, 400), fine-tuned / SemGAN + fine-tuned, AP@0.3 / AP@0.5:
  - N = 2: day 37.4 / 16.4 and 51.0 / 23.4; night 32.6 / 8.3 and 38.3 / 12.1;
  - N = 5: day 49.7 / 21.8 and 51.8 / 23.6; night 38.2 / 10.8 and 37.8 / 12.2;
  - N = 50: day 57.4 / 31.7 and 63.9 / 36.4; night 49.7 / 19.3 and 50.5 / 20.6.
- **Full real set** (l. 401, 402): all 126 night labels give 52.8 / 22.8 and 56.0 / 26.7; all 73 day labels give 61.3 / 37.0 and 61.7 / 37.2, so the GAN's gain vanishes on the day domain when every label is used. At night the gain is 3.2 points at AP@0.3 (56.0 minus 52.8; arithmetic, not printed).
- **Reading** (l. 352, 362): fine-tuning on even one real image gives a large gain; the GAN adds most when labels are fewest.

## Dependence

- Helios (Bailey 2019; Bailey is an author here, a shared-author flag), YOLOv3-tiny and CycleGAN. WGISD (Santos et al. 2020) is shown as one more real domain in a figure and not used for the numbers (per the reading agent). The GAN is fitted on the same N real labelled images as the fine-tuning.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **Nearest to Agrarium's viewer:** a 3-D vineyard model gives labelled images for free, and the measured use is as a pre-training and domain-adaptation source for grape detection with 2 to 50 real labels. It supports tier-3 canopy imagery sensors in D60 only as a synthetic input judged on real images; here the real images are two small sets from one region.
- **What the numbers say:** renders alone detect poorly by day (27.8 AP@0.3) and not at all at night (0.0); renders plus a handful of real labels is what works; gains from GAN restyling shrink to nothing with a full real set (day). A render-only validation (D58) would not have shown this.
- **Not shown:** disease, leaves or lesions (grape clusters only); a real-only arm from scratch; the cultivar (not stated in the text read); a test outside the one region, or a test set larger than 25 images.
