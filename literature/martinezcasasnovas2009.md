# Martínez-Casasnovas & Ramos 2009: gully erosion in the Alt Penedès-Anoia vineyard area

A Spanish geography paper on gullies and ravines in the Penedès vineyard area, with slope and depth figures for one headwater catchment.

**Read this first.**
- Not a full read. The slope and depth figures are for one headwater catchment of 0.688 km2 (Rierussa, near Masquefa, per the reading agent) and a landscape unit, not for vineyard plots.

## What it holds

- **Slope (l. 340, 341):** the mean slope of the catchment outside the gully walls is 5 to 9 deg (about 9 to 16 %, the reading agent's conversion).
- **Gully depth (l. 341):** 20 to 32 m in that catchment.
- **Gully walls (l. 342):** mean slope 41 deg with a spread of 14.2 deg, maximum 78 deg.
- **Larger unit (l. 468):** gullies cover 23.2 % of a unit (glacis and piedmont, not only vineyards), with gullies up to 60 m deep.
- **Incision (l. 338, cited):** mean gully incision rates of 0.7 to 0.8 m per year, cited from Martínez-Casasnovas 1998.
