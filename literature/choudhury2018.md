# Choudhury et al. 2018: fuzzy-modified Gubler-Thomas risk index for grape powdery mildew

The Gubler-Thomas (GT) powdery mildew risk index, with fuzzy membership functions for
temperature and leaf wetness. It was tested in eight site-years in California and Oregon.
It gave disease control close to the original index and the calendar, with fewer sprays
at four site-years and higher fungicide-use efficiency.

## What it holds

- Trials: eight site-years (abstract, l. 26 to 27), at Clarksburg, Corvallis, Davis, Fresno
  and Napa (l. 158 to 168; l. 188 to 191). Replicate vines, leaf and cluster ratings, and
  AUDPC (l. 262).
- Spray count: the fuzzy index had slightly fewer applications and a longer interval on
  average, and cut applications while keeping control at four of the eight site-years
  (l. 346 to 349).
- Control: no difference in AUDPC between the two indices or the calendar at any site-year
  (l. 360 to 362). The untreated control was higher at every site-year (l. 360).
- Efficiency: the fuzzy index was "consistently and significantly" higher in fungicide-use
  efficiency than the original GT and the calendar, at all site-years (l. 363 to 366).
  The fuzzy index's risk value was 14 points lower than the original GT's across all site-years (l. 337 to 338).

## Dependence

- Modifies the Gubler-Thomas risk index (Gubler et al. 1999 and later versions, cited in the
  introduction). The fuzzy membership method is standard fuzzy logic. No dataset from another
  group is recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t3 (powdery mildew). A growers' rule that uses fuzzy bounds on temperature and
  wetness, and that spares sprays while holding AUDPC, is a candidate for a decision engine
  to test against the calendar, on the same weather.
