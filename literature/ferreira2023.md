# Ferreira & Dapper 2023: RH above 90% as disease indicator, Paraná

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A book chapter (2023) on whether relative humidity above 90 % indicates fungal disease risk in Paraná State, Brazil (abstract, l. 23-31).
- Daily RH from seven SIMEPAR and IDR-Paraná stations, 1975-2021, counted as ten-day periods above 90 % (l. 150-164).
- Hours above 90 % are highest in the coastal station Guaratuba and lowest in Paranavaí (l. 210-213).
- Ponta Grossa and Palmas exceed 90 % in September, October and December; Londrina is above 90 % mainly in late November and December (l. 214-217, 240-241).
- Grape is the most vulnerable crop; downy mildew infection is cited at 18-22 °C with high RH (l. 264-278).
- The paper computes no disease model; its disease link is RH above 90 % as a wetness stand-in, cited to Sentelhas (2004) (l. 69-72).

## Dependence

- RH above 90 % as a leaf-wetness stand-in: this is the paper's whole method. It says RH above 90 % 'prolongs the duration of the wetting period' (l. 69-72, citing Sentelhas 2004), and the frequency of ten-day periods with RH above 90 % is its disease indicator (abstract, l. 23-36; methods l. 157-164). The Agrarium brief lists 'RH of 90 % or rain taken as wetness' as a formulation the truth must not use, so this paper is held out by that rule. Its other infection thresholds (l. 270-279, 15-25 °C with 6 h wetting; 18-22 °C for mildew; 25 °C for powdery mildew) are cited to Alves et al. 2006 (soybean rust), Kimati et al. 1997 and Embrapa 2005; none is an engine model in the brief's list, and none is computed here. Shares form only, not data: no Magarey, Lalancette, Blaeser or Rossi rule is used. Lulu 2008 (l. 68) is cited for the wetness-period concept; Formularium's datasets record a lulu2008.jundiai entry, not compared here.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- The observed Paraná daily RH series (1975-2021) is a usable station record for a truth's climate; its RH above 90 % wetness indicator is the stand-in the brief excludes from the truth's biology, so only the data are of use.
