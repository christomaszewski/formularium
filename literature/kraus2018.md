# Kraus et al. 2018: a semi-minimal pruned hedge is denser, damper and more mildewed than VSP

Two seasons at Geilweilerhof compare a semi-minimal pruned hedge (SMPH) with vertical shoot
positioning for Chardonnay and the Ren3 carrier Reberger: a canopy four to five times the
leaf area with smaller leaves and bunches, a few points more humid, more downy mildew on
leaves, and less Botrytis on its smaller bunches.

## What it holds

- **Vines** (l. 121-144, 206-215, 238): Chardonnay and Reberger, 2016-2017; SMPH carries 8
  to 15 times the shoots; LAI at flowering 4.7 (SMPH) against 1.0 (VSP) in 2016; leaves
  65.9 against 106.7 cm²; bunches 96.5 against 152.3 g in 2016 and 76.2 against 163.7 g in
  2017. Canopy volume by Siegfried & Sacchelli 2005's formula, printed in m³ though the
  formula is per hectare (l. 99-100).
- **Microclimate** (l. 251-253, 271-294, 26-27): loggers at 150 cm in the canopy; 2016
  season means of relative humidity 83.4 % (SMPH) against 80.5 % (VSP) for Chardonnay, with
  131.4 mm of rain and leaf wetness 49.1 % of the time; 2017 145.2 mm and 29.0 %; after rain
  the hedge is up to 20 points more humid and up to 3 °C apart (Fig. 2); the abstract's 3
  points and 0.9 °C are not computed in the text. No humidity by canopy zone, no wetness
  hours. One Reberger humidity cell prints an SD equal to its mean (l. 274).
- **Disease** (l. 245-247, 365-367, 394): downy mildew on Chardonnay leaves at BBCH 71 in
  2016, 48 % incidence (SMPH) against 25 % (VSP), severity 19.3 %; Botrytis at BBCH 89 in
  2017, 42 % (VSP) against 11 % (SMPH), about five times the severity; Drosophila suzukii
  10.3 against 4.8 a trap in 2017.
- **Not printed:** zone-wise microclimate, wetness duration in hours, the logger model's
  errors, statistics behind the abstract's differences.

## Dependence

- Observed; no model fitted. The canopy-volume formula is Siegfried & Sacchelli 2005's
  (cited); the berry impedance measure is Herzog et al. 2015's (`herzog2022` shares the
  author). Töpfer and Kicherer again in the JKI leaf-area and phenotyping notes: flags.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- For Agrarium's canopy microclimate and the 10-20 search: a training-system contrast of
  season-mean humidity (about 3 points) and wetness fraction (29-49 % of the time) with the
  mildew and Botrytis outcomes that followed; too coarse for a record, a yardstick for a
  canopy-humidity rule.
