# Dumitriu & Mitrea 2021: P. viticola dynamics, Segarcea, 2019-2020

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A 40 ha vineyard of the Archdiocese of Craiova at Segarcea, Dolj, Romania (l. 24-25).
- Mean annual temperature 11 C and precipitation about 510 mm a year (l. 27-28).
- Seven fungicide treatments a year in 2019 (6 May to 3 August) and 2020 (to 9 August) (l. 109-112).
- 2019 leaf frequency F% from 20.41% (Merlot) to 49.91% (Tamaioasa Romaneasca) (l. 152-153).
- 2019 bunch DA% from 0.72% (Merlot) to 2.94% (Tamaioasa Romaneasca) (l. 159-160).
- 2020 leaf frequency from 20.40% to 49.89% (l. 187-188).
- No untreated check, so the 'efficacy' in the table title is not measured (l. 140).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Little for the truth: one site, two seasons of variety-level visual attack scores with no weather series and no untreated check; it is useful only as a check on the order of cultivar susceptibility (Tamaioasa and Chardonnay high, Merlot low).
