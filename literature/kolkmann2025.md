# Kolkmann et al. 2025: first occurrence of Scaphoideus titanus nymphs

A test of existing temperature-sum models and new multiple linear regressions (MLR), for the first nymphs of the flavescence dorée vector in Austria. The data are monitoring from 2013 to 2020.

## What it holds

- Monitoring from 2013 to 2020 in six wine-growing areas, 13 sites in all (l. 29, 125).
- The existing models predicted the first N1 on average 3.3 days early or 5.8 days late. The MLR for N1 was 3.4 days early, and the MLR for N3 was 6.6 days early (l. 30–33, 487–495).
- The MLR for N1 had R² 0.63 on the calibration data (Table 3, l. 413).

## Dependence

- Compares with the temperature-sum models of Cizej et al. 2017, at 640 and 740 degree-days (l. 297, 384).
- The 196.6 degree-day model follows Rigamonti et al. 2011 (l. 274–277).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t7 (vector timing) and t1 (timing of vector control, which the spread of flavescence dorée depends on). A decision engine could use the forecast to time the N3 treatment. The N3 forecast ran 6.6 days early on average.
