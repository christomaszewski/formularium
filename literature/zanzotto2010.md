# Zanzotto & Borgo 2010: eleven untreated seasons in the Treviso province

## What it holds

- **Plots:** untreated Merlot (Sylvoz) at Susegana 1999-2002 and Spresiano 2003-2009,
  assessed weekly from early May to early August (200 leaves, 150 bunches).
- **First oil spots** (Figure 5): 14 May 1999, 18 May 2000, 16 May 2001, 14 May 2002, none
  on leaves in 2003, 14 May 2004, 19 May 2005, 15 May 2006, 31 May 2007, 13 May 2008, 19
  June 2009; on bunches 10-30 days later.
- **Severity classes:** high in 1999, 2002, 2004, 2007 and 2008; middle in 2000, 2001,
  2005 and 2009; very low in 2003 and 2006.
- The 3-10 conditions were met in the first decade of May; symptoms followed 6-11 days
  later (how the infection days were set is not said).
- **Printed slip:** Figure 8's equation does not match its drawn line.
