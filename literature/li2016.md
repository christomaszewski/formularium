# Li, Hao & Kang 2016: spatial variability of bud burst percentage in a 7.5 ha Merlot vineyard

A study of how the share of buds that burst varies across a Merlot vineyard in arid north-west China, with variograms and links to soil properties, in 2012 and 2013.

**Read this first.**
- It measures the percentage of buds that burst, not the date of budburst. It gives no spread in days, so it does not bear on vine-to-vine budburst timing.
- The area is printed as 7.6 ha in the methods (l. 118 to 122) and 7.5 ha in the conclusions (l. 510).

## What it holds

- **Site (l. 122):** Huangtai wine vineyard, Wuwei City, Gansu Province, a continental temperate arid region at 1581 m; measurements 15 April to 5 May in 2012 and 2013. Merlot Noir established in 1999 (l. 158).
- **CV of bud burst percentage (l. 29):** 20 % in 2012 and 15 % in 2013.
- **Range of values (l. 510):** 8.8 to 75.0 % in 2012 and 10.2 to 81.0 % in 2013.
- **Variogram, 2012 (Table 2, l. 333):** exponential, nugget (C0) 0.55, sill (C0+C) 1.09, range 188.7 m, R2 0.86, on standardised bud burst percentage.
- **Variogram, 2013 (l. 334):** nugget 0.42, sill 1.05, range 159.0 m, R2 0.88.
- **Pattern (l. 29):** spatial variability moderate, and the pattern similar in the two years; soil properties alone explained 31 % and 37 % of it (abstract).

## Dependence

- Own field data; no model computed. None recorded otherwise.

## Bearing (2026-11-05)

- **host.vine_sd_days (Agrarium):** none. It measures the variability of bud burst percentage (CV 15 to 20 %, l. 29), not of budburst timing, so it does not give a spread in days.
- **fields.vigour_length_m (Agrarium):** variogram ranges of 159 to 189 m for bud burst percentage (l. 333, 334); a different variable from vigour and not a correlation length. Field measurement, one vineyard.
