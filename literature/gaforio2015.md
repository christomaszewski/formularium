# Gaforio, Cabello & Muñoz Organero 2015: downy mildew resistance of 158 Spanish varieties in one collection

Field scores (2011) and leaf-disc scores (2012) of resistance to Plasmopara viticola for 158 varieties at El Encín, Madrid, including the three cava whites Macabeo, Xarello and Parellada but not Chardonnay.

**Read this first.** The field scores are one year (2011), one site (Madrid, not the Penedès), natural infection after heavy rain, on a coarse 1-to-9 scale on which most varieties sit at 1. The leaf-disc scores come from another year and correlate only r = 0.55 with the field; for Xarello the two disagree (3 in the field, 7 on discs). A high score means high resistance (low susceptibility). The table's three columns are interleaved in the text layer (garbled order); the rows quoted below were each checked at their line.

## What it holds

- **Material:** 158 Vitis vinifera varieties from all over Spain, up to 8 replications of 5 plants each; Müller-Thurgau and Regent as controls for very low and high resistance (l. 44-48). Vines at El Encín (IMIDRA, Alcalá de Henares): planted 2002 on 110 Richter, single cordon, 8 buds a vine, 4,808 vines ha-1 (0.80 × 2.60 m), drip irrigation, no chemical treatment during the study (l. 55-58). The Regent control scores only 5 in the field (9 on discs; l. 94).
- **Field score (2011):** OIV 452, scale 1 (very low resistance) to 9 (very high or total), visual, on ten leaves chosen at random, 3 weeks after the onset of flowering and before veraison (l. 126, 135-139). The 2011 infection followed 121 mm of rain from 26 May to 6 June at mean temperatures of 16.1 to 23.3 °C, and attacked the whole collection (l. 175).
- **Leaf-disc score (2012):** OIV 452-1, modified; twenty discs of 16 mm per variety, 25,000 spores mL-1 from a Galician (CSIC) source (l. 147, 154-156). Incidence converted to 1-9: 9 = 0-20 %, 7 = 25-40 %, 5 = 45-60 %, 3 = 65-80 %, 1 = 85-100 % of discs with sporulation (l. 161-162). Scales: field l. 126, discs l. 127.
- **Scores (field / leaf disc) for the cava whites:** Macabeo 1 / 5 (l. 121), Xarello 3 / 7 (l. 100; "Xarello Rosado" 3 / 9, l. 99), Parellada 5 / 7 (l. 89). So Xarello's discs: 25-40 % sporulating; Macabeo's: 45-60 % (l. 161-162).
- **References in the same table (field / disc):** Müller-Thurgau 1 / 1 (l. 91), Tempranillo 1 / 1 (l. 112), Chenin Blanc 1 / 1 (l. 84), Cabernet Sauvignon 1 / 3 (printed "Cabernert S."; l. 75), Albariño 3 / 5 (l. 104). Chardonnay is not in the table.
- **Distribution:** none of 158 reached very high resistance; 117 (74 %) are described as "low or very low", then 15 (9.5 %) low, 21 (13.3 %) medium and 5 (3.2 %) high (l. 180-187). The wording overlaps low and very low; the counts sum to 158, so 117 is most likely class 1 (my reading). On discs, 65 % of varieties scored 1 or 3, 10 % scored 7 and 4.4 % scored 9 (l. 202-204).
- **Agreement:** field and leaf-disc scores correlate at r = 0.55, p < 0.01 (l. 205). Boso et al. 2011 found similar field results for some shared varieties (Chenin Blanc, Tempranillo among them) and different ones for others (Godello, Garnacha Tintorera, Moscatel de Grano Menudo more susceptible here; l. 172-179); the authors cite over 30 % inconsistency between studies (l. 181).
- **Regional pattern:** the medium-resistance group includes varieties from Mediterranean regions such as Catalonia, Valencia and the Balearic Islands, which the authors call a "regional origin trend" (l. 161-163).

## Dependence

Own field and leaf-disc scoring; the disc method follows Staudt & Kassemeyer 1995, Rumbolz et al. 2002 and Boso & Kassemeyer 2008 (cited methods), and results are compared with Boso et al. 2011 (not read; not held). Shares its authors and its institute with `gaforio2011` (powdery mildew in the same collection, on a different plot): a flag, not a dependency.

## Bearing (2026-10-22)

- **Cultivar susceptibility:** Macabeu 1 (most susceptible class) in the field and 5 on discs; Xarel·lo 3 and 7; Parellada 5 and 7. Both tests put Macabeu as the most susceptible of the three; Xarel·lo is more susceptible than Parellada in the field and equal on discs. The result is an ordinal ranking, not a susceptibility ratio. Chardonnay is not in this test.
- **What is not shown:** any sporulation area or severity ratio; a Penedès trial; a second field year. With 117 of 158 varieties in the lowest classes, the field scale barely separates varieties at the susceptible end. The two tests disagree by two classes for Xarel·lo, so its position is uncertain.
- **Host parameters in host.yaml:** none; the paper has no phenology, bunch or budbreak data. Kind of result: ranking under natural infection, one year, one site, plus one confirming laboratory test.
