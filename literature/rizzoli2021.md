# Rizzoli et al. 2021: alder and Orientus ishidae as wild reservoirs of flavescence dorée

In three southern Swiss vineyards, the leafhopper Orientus ishidae and common alder carry the FD phytoplasma genotype M50. The vines carried M54 only.

## What it holds

- O. ishidae acquired FDp at 69 % in nymphs and 85 % in adults (abstract, l. 38–39).
- Alder and O. ishidae carry map genotype M50 (FD1), which is part of the vine and S. titanus cycle. All tested grapevines carried M54 (FD2) (l. 39–47).
- Conclusion: alder and O. ishidae do not seem to drive the southern Swiss epidemic, though wild hosts may keep inoculum (l. 48–50).
- Symptomatic grapevines were 1.37 to 1.78 % in the three vineyards (l. 567–568).

## Dependence

- None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t6 (regional spread): wild hosts and a secondary vector can keep inoculum that restarts a vine epidemic. A spread model would need their presence as well as the vines. This survey gives presence and genotypes, not rates of spread.
