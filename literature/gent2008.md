# Gent et al. 2008: A decade of hop powdery mildew in the Pacific Northwest

Review, Pacific Northwest, USA (Washington, Oregon, Idaho).

## What it holds

- Review of hop powdery mildew (Podosphaera macularis) since its 1997 arrival in the US Pacific Northwest: damage, fungicide registrations (Table 1), cultural control and the weather-based risk index.
- The risk index (a modified Gubler-Thomas index) is described without its rules or equations.
