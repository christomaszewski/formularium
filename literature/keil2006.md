# Keil et al. 2006: infection severity by temperature and wetness, Freiburg

## What it holds

- Infections at 5-30 °C with 1-24 h of wetness; severity as encysted zoospores at stomata
  over the estimated number of stomata (fluorescence). Wetness raised severity significantly
  after 3-6 h at 5-10 °C, 3-10 h at 15-20 °C, and within the first hour at 25-30 °C (Table
  1); maximum severity depends on temperature (Fig. 2).
- Meant as an algorithm for VitiMeteo-type models, which predict infection but not severity;
  the algorithm is not printed.
