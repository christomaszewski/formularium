# Orlandini & Giuntoli 1998: photosynthesis of downy-mildew-infected grape leaves

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Downy mildew oilspots gave negative net photosynthesis (Table I: -2.20 umol m-2 s-1 in oilspot, against 14.02 in healthy leaves) (l. 19, 275, 303-306).
- Tissue beside the oilspot also lost assimilation: fitted intercepts of 73-77% imply a 23-27% drop with no visible symptoms (l. 386-394).
- Linear fits of assimilation on the oilspot share in the cuvette (Table II, R2 0.62-0.78); perimeter gave an intercept near 100% (l. 418, 436-439).
- Stomatal conductance fell to a very low level at 50% infected area (image analysis) (l. 474, 500).
- Field diurnal values were lower on diseased leaves, not significantly so at p < 0.05 (l. 515, 524).
- The authors propose a source-to-sink switch near 60% disease for simulation models (l. 556-562).
