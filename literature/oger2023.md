# Oger et al. 2023: per-vine yield components in two Syrah fields, 2022 and 2008

A data paper for vine-by-vine maps of productive, unproductive, dead and missing vines, inflorescence counts and bunch weights, with soil resistivity and a vegetation index. No record: a dataset.

**Read this first.** Two fields in two different years (2022 and 2008) with different protocols (l. 143), so the fields cannot be pooled as a multi-year series. Field 1's bunch weights cover half the field only (l. 156). The Zenodo accession is 8318944 (l. 43); the 2026-10-22 catalogue's 8171030 is wrong.

## What it holds

- **Where to get it** (l. 43): Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.8318944. Licence of the article: CC BY (l. 5). The Zenodo record's licence (CC BY 4.0) and the archive size (Data.zip 0.7 MB) are the reading agent's look at the record, not in the paper.
- **Sites and years** (l. 7, 143): two Syrah fields in southern France. Field 1: 0.8 ha at Villeneuve-lès-Maguelone, data in 2022. Field 2: 0.5 ha at Gruissan, data in 2008. One season per field.
- **Size** (l. 79): 2,614 planted vines in Field 1 and 1,030 in Field 2; three CSV files of aggregated data (two for Field 1, one for Field 2) beside raw shapefiles.
- **Variables** (l. 93, 151-152, 156, 160, 164-165): vine status (productive, unproductive, dead, missing; Field 1 only for the last two); inflorescence counts per vine in both fields (counted by hand, l. 151); Field 1 total yield and bunch weights on half the field (7 of 14 rows, weighed on 2022-09-09 to plus or minus 1 g, l. 156); Field 2 yield only at sampling sites of five consecutive vines on a 15 m x 9 m grid (l. 157); soil electrical resistivity to 1 m depth (l. 160-161); a vegetation index: FCover at 2.25 m2 pixels from SPOT 6-7 for Field 1 (a commercial service, l. 164) and NDVI from an airborne image of August 2007 for Field 2 (l. 165).
- **How labels were made:** observed by hand and GNSS RTK positioning (l. 147, 152, 156); the inflorescence and bunch counts are manual. FCover is a retrieval from satellite images by a commercial service.
- **Time step:** no time series; dated surveys, for example vine positions on 2022-05-10 (l. 152) and yield on 2022-09-09 (l. 156) in Field 1.
- **Weather:** none here. The sibling data paper Gras et al. 2023 (same issue) has the weather of the Field 1 domain (l. 57).

## Dependence

- FCover comes from the Oenoview service (SPOT 6-7 images, a retrieval model); soil resistivity from a service provider's device. Same group as Gras 2023 and Zhang 2025. None recorded otherwise.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **No D60 weather tier; imagery and soil sensing at tier 3.** A real, per-vine map of how many bunches and what weight each position bears, which could check the spatial pattern of yield in a simulated vineyard (missing and dead vines, row structure) and the viewer's vine placement. It gives no disease or weather.
- **For D58, a yield-component truth, not a risk one.** An emulator of bunch number would need the weather of the season, which comes from Gras 2023 for Field 1's year only.
- **Limits:** two fields in two years with unequal protocols, half-field bunch weights in 2022, sampled yield in 2008, no leaf, disease or phenology data.
