# Bove et al. 2020: a daily epidemic model parameterised from the literature

## What it holds

- One plant of 10,000 sites, 200 days, daily step; latency, infectious period and a daily
  multiplication rate Rc (Vanderplank; Rc from the rate r by Sun & Zeng 1994), with
  modifiers for leaf age, temperature and wetness; clusters linked to foliage.
- **Table 1:** latent period 6 days, 10 long (Müller & Sleumer 1934; Goidanich 1959);
  infectious period 15 days, 20 long (Caffi et al. 2013; Kennelly et al. 2005); onset day
  120, or 130 and 160 (Rossi et al. 2008, Fig. 4); primary period 60 or 10 days; primary
  rate 0.2 or 0.02 per day (Gobbin et al. 2005; Rumbou & Gessler 2004); RcOPT 0.30; age
  modifiers 1, 0.56, 0.39 (Reuveni 1998); temperature 1 (13-28 °C) or 0.8 (0-13 °C)
  (Lalancette 1988); wetness 1, 0.6, 0.4, 0.1 by rain and RH classes (Lalancette 1987).
- Six scenarios of conduciveness; no field validation, only patterns 'derived from the
  literature'.
- Printed inconsistencies: r = 0.12 against ln(2.8)/8 = 0.129; RcA 0.39 against 0.33/0.81 =
  0.41.

## Dependence

- Computes no engine equation. Takes values from Goidanich 1959 (latency), Caffi 2013 and
  Kennelly 2005 (infectious period), Rossi et al. 2008's output (onset), Lalancette 1987 and
  1988 (modifiers).
