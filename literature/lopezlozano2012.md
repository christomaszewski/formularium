# Lopez-Lozano, Casterad & Herrero 2013: ceptometer LAI in VSP vines depends on sun position

Transmitted PAR readings at seven sun positions in two vineyards, with destructive LAI, to find which protocol gives unbiased LAI.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 128 ff.): two vineyards near Zaragoza, vertically trained, rows 3 m (l. 176); actual LAI 0.2-2.2, mean 0.74 and 0.99 (l. 198).
- **Sun angles** (Table 1, l. 128-134): zenith 58.30 to 27.82 deg and sun-row azimuth 33.64 to 4.77 deg at one site; 62.51 to 29.03 deg and 58.77 to 19.67 deg at the other.
- **Leaf angle** (l. 146, 152-153): SunScan ELADP (Campbell's x) left at 1, spherical; cites Louarn 2005: three-wire VSP near 60 deg, one or two wires 40-50 deg.
- **Clumping condition** (l. 250-256, Eq. 3 from Baret et al. 2010): Beer-Lambert holds only if W + H tan(theta) >= D; with the sun near zenith clumping biases LAI low.
- **Not printed:** the raw readings as a table, a data release.

## Dependence

- Borrows Baret et al. 2010, Nilson 1971, Chen & Black 1992. Own data. No engine overlap.

## Bearing

- A published criterion for when a row canopy acts as a turbid medium, and a dataset (figures only) of transmitted PAR with known sun angles.
