# Antoneli et al. 2021: a bibliometric synthesis of stemflow and throughfall in crops, with one grape row

A tally of 100 Scopus articles from the last forty years, with a table of stemflow and throughfall ranges per crop.

**Read this first.** This is a synthesis, not a measurement. The grape figure (stemflow 2-6 %, throughfall 65-85 %) is second-hand: it comes from one study that the text read does not name (l. 244). The only vineyard paper in the reference list is Brecciaroli et al. 2012 (l. 417-418), so that may be the source; this is an inference, not printed, and that paper was not obtained. The table prints an amplitude of 1.1 beside 65-85 where the ratio is 1.3 (arithmetic, not printed): either the range or the amplitude is a misprint, so the grape throughfall range is not reliable as printed. The table values are means taken from each article (l. 250), not per-event figures.

## What it holds

- **Scope:** a bibliometric analysis of one hundred articles from the last forty years in Scopus, by continent and country, and by annual and perennial crops (l. 29-30). The method and the other sections were not read.
- **Grapes (perennial table, l. 244):** 1 study; stemflow 2-6 % of rain (amplitude 3.0); throughfall 65-85 % (amplitude printed 1.1, garbled).
- **Other perennial crops, for scale (cited from the same table):** citrus, 5 studies, stemflow 0.5-4.7 %, throughfall 82-92 % (l. 237); apple, 3 studies, 0.8-10 % and 50-84 % (l. 241); olive, 3 studies, 1.5-24 % and 56-70 % (l. 242).
- **Table note:** the rates are the means extracted from the articles analysed (l. 250). The table gives ranges of those means; the conditions of each study are not in the lines read.
- The text names grapes (Vitis vinifera L.) among the perennial crops it classified (l. 140) but, in the parts read, says nothing else about vines.

## Dependence

- A synthesis of other authors' measurements. Per-crop values are means from the source articles. Not independent of the studies it tallies; for grapes the source study is not named in the parts read.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **micro.interception (0.6):** the grape throughfall range (65-85 %) is a share of rain reaching the ground under the canopy, the same kind of quantity as Myburgh's 80-86 % (`myburgh2004`), not a share caught until full. It cannot be used while its source study is unnamed and its range is possibly misprinted.
- **micro.storage_mm_per_lai, micro.wet_threshold_mm, drying parameters:** not addressed (no storage, no wetness, no drying).
- **Kind of result:** second-hand synthesis table (means of studies, one vine study, source unnamed). The identifying step, reading Brecciaroli et al. 2012, remains open.
