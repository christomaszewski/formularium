# Xiao et al. 2022: emulators of APSIM wheat-maize yield and soil carbon over 3,409 grid cells, scored against APSIM (crops, North China Plain)

APSIM is run for 101 years (1980-2080) over scenarios of nitrogen, irrigation, residue, manure and climate model in each grid cell of the Huang-Huai-Hai plain; three machine-learning methods (multivariate adaptive regression splines, random forest, boosted trees) are trained on the runs to emulate yield and change in soil organic carbon, so that regional maps can be made fast. No record: a method, on simulator output.

**Read this first.** The emulators are judged only against APSIM's own output, by random 10-fold cross-validation (in-distribution). APSIM itself is compared with field data at three long-term sites (Fig. 3, a figure whose values were not read), but the emulators are not. There is no pretraining step and no real-only baseline.

## What it holds

- **Simulator** (l. 70, 214, 261, 231-233): APSIM version 7.8 (l. 214), run from 1980 to 2080 (l. 70); cultivar and soil-carbon parameters were adopted from earlier studies that calibrated them for wheat and maize in this area (l. 258-262; Zhao et al. 2014-2017, Wang et al. 2018, cited). The full scenario space is about 1.1 x 10^15 combinations (l. 231), too many to run, so a random subset was run.
- **Synthetic samples** (l. 109, 232, 265): 3,409 grid cells, with APSIM results from 2000 scenarios in each cell's database (about 6.8 million scenario runs, arithmetic, not printed). Climate inputs from 28 climate models (per the reading agent).
- **Real samples**: none for the emulators. Observations from three long-term sites (Fengqiu, Xuzhou, Yucheng) were used to check APSIM, shown in Fig. 3 (l. 281).
- **Validation design** (l. 331): 10-fold cross-validation of the three methods on random folds of the simulated set.
- **Scores against APSIM** (l. 403, 407): at the grid level the emulators explain more than 96 % of the variance of APSIM's soil-carbon change; across all grid cells, on average more than 92 % (soil carbon change), 95 % (wheat yield) and 93 % (maize yield) of APSIM's variance.
- **Authors' claims** (l. 75, 574): the emulators reproduce APSIM yield and soil carbon accurately across the region under scenarios, and need much less input information than the process model.

## Dependence

- Emulates APSIM 7.8; cultivar and soil-carbon parameters come from the group's earlier papers. The paper does not cite `johnston2023`, which found the failure of emulators at unseen locations (a link made by this note, not by the authors).

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **The usual emulator check:** more than 92 to 96 % of the variance of the simulator under random folds. It says nothing about places or years outside the simulated set, which `johnston2023` measured as a collapse for biomass and yield. For Agrarium's engine runs (D60) this is a development-level check.
- **D45 and TRUTH-METHOD:** an emulator trained on a simulator is kin to it, and a score against that simulator cannot be quoted as a skill against the real world. The one real comparison here (APSIM against three sites) is of the simulator, not the emulator.
- **Not shown:** any score of the emulator against field data, new-place splits, or a disease.
