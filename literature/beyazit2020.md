# Beyazit et al. 2020: three local hourly diffuse-ratio models for Sanliurfa

Three diffuse-ratio models fitted to eight years of hourly data and compared with 15 literature models (l. 27-32). No record.

## What it holds

- **Data:** solar radiation measurement system with sun tracking at Harran University, 2009 to 2016 (l. 24-27).
- **Result:** Models 1 and 2 are said to do better than others for clearness-index limits (l. 33-35); Model 2 gives MAPE 11.983 % and RMSE 41.348 and Model 3 gives MAPE 11.652 % and RMSE 40.959, against MAPE 39 to 88 % for most literature models (table at l. 566-590).
- **Measures:** MAE, MAPE, RMSE, t-statistic and Bayes information criterion (l. 434).

## Dependence

- Compares 15 diffuse models from the literature.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s3 and s6:** one more local diffuse-ratio fit, showing again that foreign diffuse correlations err by tens of percent. A simulator would not take the coefficients for a vineyard. The units of the RMSE column were not located.
