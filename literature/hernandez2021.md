# Hernández et al. 2021: image scoring and early detection of downy mildew

A laboratory study on grapevine leaf discs. RGB images estimated sporulation against expert scores, and hyperspectral images gave early detection by machine learning.

## What it holds

- **RGB severity:** against expert visual scores, R² 0.76 and RMSE 20.53% (l. 26-28).
- **Hyperspectral detection:** accuracy 81% (l. 28-29). Table 1 gives CNN 82% and MLP 81% (l. 633-635).
- **Timing:** discs were imaged daily up to nine days after inoculation (l. 194; l. 476-477).
- **Scope:** laboratory conditions only (l. 20-21).

## Dependence

- Expert visual scores as the reference (l. 26-29).
- The authors' own CNN, KNN, MLP and PLS-DA classifiers (l. 572-576).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t2 (observation):** the scoring and early-detection figures suggest a check for a sporulation scale. They have no field data and are not a tool input.
