# Aoki et al. 2021: warm nights and downy mildew in growth chambers

## What it holds

- **Treatments:** nights at 25 °C against 15 °C (21:00-05:00, dark); days at 30 °C (cell
  cultures) or 27 °C (seedlings).
- **Zoospores:** about 45 % germinated in 8 h at 25 °C against about 15 % at 15 °C.
- **Seedlings** (8, inoculated at 50,000 sporangia/mL): sporulation by day 4 and higher
  scores on days 4-5 with warm nights; no difference by days 7-10. PR-protein genes lower
  with warm nights. All in figures.
- **Kofu weather** 2016-2019 (Table 2): July minima above 20 °C.
- The paper cites Lalancette et al. 1988 for an infection peak at 15 °C; Formularium's
  note on that paper gives 17.5 °C.
