# Asinari et al. 2026: soil management and the airborne fungal community

Two seasons in one Piacenza vineyard compared tillage and three inter-row treatments, with airborne fungal sampling and mildew scoring. Tillage shaped the airborne community. Compost mulch lowered mildew only in the drier season.

## What it holds

- **Design:** 2022 and 2023. Conventional tillage and no-till, with compost, pruning-wood mulch and living cover between rows (l. 13-15).
- **Mildew:** compost mulch lowered downy and powdery mildew under relatively dry seasons, not under wetter ones (l. 21-22).
- **Erysiphe reads:** 0.5% in May, 10.2% in June and 18.9% in July 2023 (l. 538-545). These are relative sequence reads, not spore counts.

## Dependence

- Growth stages from Lorenz et al. 1995 for the disease scale (l. 222-223).
- UNITE v10.0 for taxonomy (l. 246). DESeq2 within MicrobiomeAnalyst (l. 258-259).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strands t3 (powdery mildew) and t4 (downy mildew):** cover and mulch effects on mildew are a check for a cover term in a simulator. The Erysiphe reads are not spore counts, so they cannot set an inoculum level.
