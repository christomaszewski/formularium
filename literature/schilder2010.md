# Schilder et al. 2010: organic fungicides for downy and powdery mildew, Michigan

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Organic fungicides against downy and powdery mildew, Niagara vineyard, Fennville, Michigan, 2008 and 2009 (l. 28-31).
- Design: 3-vine plots, four blocks, seven sprays on fixed dates (l. 52-53, 61-73).
- 2008 downy mildew pressure high after heavy June and early July rain; no powdery mildew (l. 88-91).
- 2009 Table 1: untreated downy mildew incidence 91.3%, severity 50.4%; Phostrol programme control 94.9% (l. 150, 169).
- 2009 Table 2: untreated powdery mildew incidence 89.0%, severity 31.1%; best programme control 96.4% (l. 189, 211).
- Serenade and Sonata the most promising organic products overall (l. 101, 121-122).
- Full and half rates of Serenade and Sonata differed little (l. 125-126).

## Dependence

- none. A field fungicide-efficacy trial; no model is computed, fitted or shared in form. Spray dates are tied to shoot and bloom stages, not to any listed model.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A field record of organic fungicide control, with incidence and severity per treatment for two Michigan seasons, useful as a check on product efficacy; it holds no weather, so it cannot test an infection model.
