# Ramírez Martínez et al. 2025: a Réunion Plasmopara viticola strain overcomes Rpv1, Rpv3.1 and Rpv10

A bioRxiv preprint on leaf-disc tests of a strain from the 2023 Cilaos outbreak against resistant grapevine genotypes.

**Read this first.**
- Leaf-disc assays and genomics on one strain from Réunion; no field epidemic data. Only the abstract and inoculation methods were read.
- It is a preprint.

## What it holds

- **First evidence (l. 36):** breakdown of Rpv1, previously effective, by the Cilaos strain; the outbreak was spotted in 2023 (l. 138, personal communication).
- **Three loci (l. 38):** one strain overcomes Rpv1, Rpv3.1 and Rpv10 at once, the first such case.
- **Rpv10 (l. 47):** complete loss with the Cilaos strain, whereas European strains only partly overcome it; a novel homozygous deletion in an avirulence locus is reported.
- **Assay (l. 347, 402):** 1x10^5 sporangia/mL; sporulation and necrosis read at 6 days after inoculation; 240 inoculated discs and 30 mock.
- **Spread concern (l. 51):** the abstract raises concern about emergence and spread, and the strain shares a genetic background with mainland French populations (the claim as recorded by the reader).

## Dependence

- A reference strain panel of known virulence; avirulence loci from Dvorak et al. and Paineau et al. Shared authors with pelissier2026 are a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **Agrarium, resistant varieties:** a simulator should not treat Rpv1, Rpv3.1 and Rpv10 resistance as fixed; one strain has overcome all three on leaf discs. Laboratory evidence from Réunion, not a Penedès or mainland result.
- **Cooptera:** no direct bearing on the warning engine for susceptible vines; context for any future resistant-variety option.
