# Tello & Forneck 2018: bunch compactness through the season as a double sigmoid

Bunch compactness (bunch volume over rachis length squared) of ten cultivars at one Austrian
site in 2017, measured every 3-4 days from berry set to harvest and fitted by a double-
sigmoid curve.

## What it holds

- Model: double-sigmoid curve for bunch compactness CI13 (bunch volume / rachis length
  squared) from berry set to harvest, four stages in two cycles (l. 27-32, eq. 3 at l.
  124-138).
- Ten Austrian cultivars, one site (BOKU, Tulln), one season (2017); six inflorescences per
  cultivar, 60 in total (l. 93-108, 129).
- Measurements every 3-4 days from EL-12 (rachis, peduncle) and EL-29 (berry width) to EL-38
  (l. 143-151).
- Budburst 4-18 April; veraison 26 July to 4 August; harvest 28 August to 1 September (l.
  179-221).
- Overall R2 of the fit 0.48 (l. 398); by class 0.53 (very loose) to 0.89 (very compact) (l.
  456, 487).
- Bunch counts: very loose 3, loose 16, medium 19, compact 9, very compact 11 (l. 484-486);
  two bunches discarded (l. 229).
- Compactness at harvest correlates with berries per bunch (tau 0.43) and rachis length (tau
  -0.25) (l. 421-422).
- The fitted constants are not printed; no external validation (l. 512-513).

## Dependence

- Its own measurements; no model produced them.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For a Botrytis truth (compactness drives bunch rot): compactness follows two growth cycles
  to harvest, overall R² 0.48; the fitted constants are not printed, so there is nothing to
  run; one site, one season.
