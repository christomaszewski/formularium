# Oerke et al. 2016: hyperspectral signs of infection before sporulation

## What it holds

- Hyperspectral reflectance of inoculated leaves of Müller-Thurgau (susceptible), Regent and
  Solaris (resistant) in a greenhouse at Siebeldingen: reflectance changed 1-2 days before
  sporulation on the lower surface of the susceptible cultivar; resistant ones showed brown
  spots earlier.
