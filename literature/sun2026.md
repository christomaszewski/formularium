# Sun et al. 2026: endodormancy release in two table grape cultivars

From Shanghai Jiao Tong University and the Henan Academy of Agricultural Sciences. A greenhouse forcing study.

## What it holds

- Two table grape cultivars, Muscat Hamburg (low chill) and Shine Muscat (high chill), seven-year-old own-rooted vines in an unheated glass greenhouse in Shanghai, 31 deg 11' N (l. 145-146).
- Temperature logged every 5 min from October 2025 to April 2026, aggregated to hourly (l. 155-159).
- Endodormancy release: forced-budbreak assay on 17 dates (8 Oct 2025 to 4 Feb 2026, l. 165-166); first date with day-21 bud break above 50% (l. 186-187).
- Release on 16 December at 10.95 CP for Muscat Hamburg and 6 January at 22.78 CP for Shine Muscat, about 2.1-fold (l. 397-398).
- Chill by the Dynamic Model (chillR) (l. 193-194); heat by Asymmetric Curvilinear GDH with chillR defaults 4, 25, 36 deg C (l. 197-201), not refitted (l. 204).
- Heat requirement from release to 50% bud break: 10,813 and 12,062 GDH (l. 403-404).
- One season only (l. 721). Biochemistry (starch, sugars, ABA, GA3) is not about the engine's models.
- Out-of-region (East Asia, subtropical), table grapes, greenhouse; HC part comes from the 2024-2025 season (l. 301, 906).
- Uses published equations (Dynamic Model, Anderson GDH) rather than a new form; the requirements are fitted to the authors' own dates.

## Dependence

- Its own forced-budbreak assays; it computes the Dynamic Model and Anderson et al.'s growing degree hours (l. 194, 202). Chilling then forcing: the engine's forms (`chilling-dormancy`, `degree-day-phenology`).
- No author of an engine model.
