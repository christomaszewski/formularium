# Lei et al. 2018: image counting of wheat stripe rust spores on trap samples

A K-means and contour-splitting algorithm counts urediniospores on volumetric trap images,
  tested on indoor simulated samples only.

## What it holds

- **Method:** K-means segmentation, shape-and-area tests for touching spores, concavity-based
  contour splitting, compared with watershed (l. 19-26).
- **Result:** lowest counting accuracy 92.7 %, highest 100 %, mean 98.6 %, 6 points above
  watershed at 92.6 % (l. 130-132).
- **Limit:** images from an indoor simulation, not the field (l. 20-21).

## Dependence

- Volumetric spore traps; watershed transform as comparator (record).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s6 (methods): automatic counting of trapped spores, 98.6 % mean accuracy indoors. For a sweep
  simulator, the useful part is the counting error rate as an observation error on trap data.
  Wheat rust, not grape. Not read in full.
