# Ortega 2024: fungicide contamination in vineyards, a UPC thesis

A thesis on the fate of copper and other fungicides. Read only for canopy and leaf-area
data; it has none.

## What it holds

- **Field trial** (l. 2637-2656): Cabernet Sauvignon at the UPC Agròpolis campus,
  Viladecans (not Penedès), 1,500 m², 3.0 × 1.2 m (which is the row not stated), double
  Royat on two wires; at BBCH 55; 80 L/ha set by DOSAVIÑA from canopy measurements whose
  values are not printed.
- **Deposits** (l. 2882): copper 2.07 against 0.80 µg/cm² of leaf.
- **Cited** (l. 2607): an artificial canopy of LAI 1.0 (López-Lozano et al. 2009).
- **Not printed:** LAI, leaf area, canopy size, defoliation.
