# Jermini et al. 2010: downy mildew and Merlot carbohydrate compensation

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Field trials 1996-1998 at Cadenazzo (Ticino) on Merlot grafted on 3309; untreated, reduced and standard fungicide schedules (l. 59-61, 67-70).
- Untreated-canopy severity at veraison was 1.3 %, 4.5 % and 9.1 % in 1996-1998 (l. 107-108).
- Untreated vines cut root reserves by 37-58 % against the standard schedule (l. 144); root starch in 1998 was 27.67 against 129.79 mg/g DM (Table 1, l. 183).
- The reduced schedule limited mobilisation but left reserves below the standard schedule (l. 145-148).
- Incidence on 13 August 1998 was 76.3 % untreated and 50.1 % reduced (l. 138).
- The paper gives no yield values; they are in the companion paper cited at l. 390.

## Dependence

- none. The paper computes no listed model. It names Dietrich et al. 1997's yield simulation model (l. 376-378) as a route for later use, not run here; that model is not on the engine list. Severity is scored on the Horsfall scale and phenology on BBCH (Baillod and Baggiolini 1993); neither is on the list. Jermini, Blaise and Gessler are also authors of the Bordeaux chapter jermini2010 (a shared author is a flag only).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A measured site dataset of reserve starch and sugars and of severity under downy mildew defoliation, usable as an observed check on a compensation term in an evaluator, with no model run here.
