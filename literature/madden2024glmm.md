# Madden & Ojiambo 2024: generalized linear mixed models in plant science

Review, Not region-specific (authors at Ohio State and NC State, USA); wheat example.

## What it holds

- A tutorial review of linear mixed models and generalized linear mixed models (conditional versus marginal means, overdispersion, fitting methods, link functions), with an example on percent diseased plants in a wheat field study and simulated data.
- It also gives a brief synopsis of published GLMM analyses in plant agriculture; it is a statistical method, not a disease model.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
