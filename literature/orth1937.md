# Orth 1937: air humidity and late blight sporangia

## What it holds

- Laboratory experiments on Phytophthora infestans sporangia held at graded humidities and
  temperatures: a small, short drop below 100 % RH (about 5 %) lowers their ability to form
  zoospores, while direct germination rises (summary, p. 446).
