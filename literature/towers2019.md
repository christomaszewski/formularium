# Towers et al. 2019: vegetation indices against LAI of Malbec VSP canopies

Proximal multispectral images of Malbec canopies stripped step by step, with and without
hail net, in early March 2011 near Mendoza. Read for LAI values.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 275-288): two Malbec VSP vineyards 27 km apart; 265 mm rain a year; 1,600
  degree-days above 10 °C from budburst to harvest. One sampling day, about four weeks
  before harvest (l. 481).
- **Method** (l. 435-440, 516): LAI from leaf weight (R² 0.905) and canopy area on the
  images; leaves stripped one in four or five a shoot.
- **Fits:** NDVI log model R² 0.98 with soil (l. 1080); RVI the most sensitive index (l. 974).
  LAI below 1.0 is said to be unlikely except very early or after damage (l. 698).
- **Not printed:** measured LAI as numbers, spacing, shoots, irrigation.

## Dependence

- Beer's-law form for the index fits (cited); fits to the paper's own data.
