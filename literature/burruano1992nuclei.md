# Burruano et al. 1992: nuclei in germinating oospores

## What it holds

- DAPI staining of oospores overwintered near Palermo and sampled from 15 February: resting
  oospores held 1-16 nuclei, germinating ones more than 40; up to 72 nuclei in one
  macrosporangium; nuclei migrated into the macrosporangium within about 24 h of
  germination.
