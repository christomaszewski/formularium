# de Groot & Hendriksen 2026: canopy leaf wetness for warning systems

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A review of 58 papers from 2010 to 2025 on canopy wetness and disease-warning thresholds (l. 25-27, l. 110-113).
- Minimum wetness for infection: 4 to 6 h for grapevine grey mould, 9 to 12 h for apple scab (Table 2, l. 169-170).
- Closed canopies: about 10 h more wetness than open stations (3.2 h vs 13.2 h) (l. 146-148).
- Above-canopy sensors give 22 to 38% false negatives; mid-canopy 8 to 17% (Table 3, l. 194, l. 211).
- A canopy-corrected model gives sensitivity 89 to 96% and specificity 81 to 91% (l. 199, l. 216).
- The review states a 3.4 to 6.8 h extra wetness figure (l. 31, l. 227) that conflicts with its own 10 h result (l. 148).
- Several citations and reference years are wrong (see garbled).

## Dependence
