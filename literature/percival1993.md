# Percival et al. 1993: cluster exposure, cuticle and bunch rot in three cultivars

Leaf removal at pea size thickened berry cuticles and cut bunch rot in three Ontario cultivars, in 1990 and 1991.

## What it holds

- Riesling (1990, 1991), Cabernet franc and Optima (1991), at the Horticultural Research Institute of Ontario, Vineland Station. Leaves were stripped at pea size, in four replications (l. 111, 126-128).
- Exposed Riesling clusters had 18.6% more epicuticular wax and 35.0% more cuticle proper than shaded ones (l. 25).
- All three cultivars had less bunch rot when exposed. Cabernet franc was the most tolerant and Optima the most susceptible (l. 31-33).
- The paper gives means by cultivar and exposure. It gives no rate and no fitted function.

## Dependence

- Builds on the enzymatic cuticle isolation of Norris and Bukovac (1965), and on Rosenquist and Morrison (1989) for cuticle and berry contact.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (berry susceptibility to bunch rot). A simulator could take the direction of the exposure effect on wax, cuticle and rot. The data come from one site in two seasons and give no rate.
