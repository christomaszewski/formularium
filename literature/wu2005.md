# Wu, Hubbard & You 2005: daily temperature and rain at nearby Nebraska COOP and AWDN stations

A Notes and Correspondence paper comparing daily temperature and precipitation between COOP stations and nearby AWDN stations less than 10 km apart, using hourly AWDN data to explain the differences.

**Read this first.**
- Most of the differences come from observation time, not distance. Pairs with different observation times differ far more than pairs with matching times.
- The DOI is not printed on page 1 of the text (only the tag "JTECH1733"), and the reading agent's DOI was marked "not checked"; the field is left empty.
- Table 3 is headed in inches; the 6.61 in maximum daily precipitation difference at 2.38 km looks like a unit or observation-day artefact (garbled, not verified). Degree C values below are the reading agent's conversions from degF.

## What it holds

- **Scottsbluff, 5.90 km (l. 350):** AWDN against airport COOP, matching observation times. RMSE Tmax 2.14, Tmin 1.67, Tave 1.38 degF (0.77 degC for Tave; conversion), precipitation 0.08 in (about 2 mm).
- **Lincoln, 7.08 km (l. 344):** IANR AWDN against Lincoln Airport COOP. Tave RMSE 1.91 degF (1.06 degC); precipitation RMSE 0.13 in (about 3.3 mm).
- **North Platte, 9.48 km (l. 334):** AWDN against WSO COOP. Tave RMSE 1.99 degF (1.1 degC); precipitation RMSE 0.11 in.
- **Minden, 8.74 km (l. 338):** different observation times. Tave RMSE 5.75 degF, about 3.3 degC; the paper's pairs at 3 to 10 km with different times reach about 6 degF. Observation time dominates, not distance.
- **Rain (l. 445):** 19 of 28 pairs within 10 km have daily precipitation RMSE above 0.2 in (about 5 mm).
- **Maximum differences (l. 307):** between COOP pairs 1.66 to 9.8 km apart, the maximum daily precipitation difference is 0.82 to 7.29 in; one pair at 2.38 km shows 6.61 in (garbled, see above).
- Daily Tave is (Tmax + Tmin) / 2.

## Dependence

- None recorded. Own comparison of COOP and AWDN data. Authors Hubbard and You also wrote coop2014; a shared author is only a flag.

## Bearing (2026-11-04)

- **weather.site_t_offset_sd_c (Agrarium):** co-located and nearby stations 5.9 to 9.5 km apart differ by a daily-mean RMSE of about 0.8 to 1.1 degC when observation times match. Evidence: a measured network comparison in Nebraska.
- **weather.site_rain_extra_mm and site_rain_cv:** daily rain differs by about 2 to 5 mm RMSE between stations within 10 km. These pairs include day-shift effects, so the figures are an upper bound for rain.
- **Limits:** manual COOP readings with observation-time effects; plains, not vineyard terrain.
