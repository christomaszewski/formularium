# Fernández-González et al. 2009: Cenlle's 2007 season, published before the thesis

The 2007 season of [fernandezgonzalez2011](fernandezgonzalez2011.md): same site, trap and
totals.

## What it holds

- **Site and trap:** Cenlle (Treixadura, Godello, Loureira), a Lanzoni VPPS-2000 at 2 m, 1
  April to 30 September 2007 (gap 10-16 September). Treated: 11 sprays against downy
  mildew.
- **Season totals:** Botrytis 16,145, Plasmopara 747, Uncinula 578; Plasmopara on 120 of
  176 days, mean 8 and maximum 32/m³ in BBCH stage 8.
- **Regression (Table 3, in-sample, n 173):** Plasmopara = -0.699 + 0.419 P(t-1) + 0.298
  dew point(t-2), R² 0.255. The thesis gives a different fit (same form, same-day dew
  point, R² 0.227, 2007-2010).
- **The 2006 check:** "good results, particularly for Uncinula and Plasmopara", shown only
  in figures. The thesis says its regression failed on 2006 and its ARIMA passed: the two
  are different models.
- Computes an Infection Risk Index from maximum temperature (Campbell et al. 2007) and a
  1,100 °C·day threshold from 1 November (Jailloux et al. 1999), neither an engine model.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Nothing beyond the thesis, except the form of the 2007 fit.
