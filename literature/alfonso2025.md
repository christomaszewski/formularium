# Alfonso et al. 2025: whey hydrolysates vs heat, drought, P. viticola

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Greenhouse and climate-chamber study of whey protein hydrolysates (PHs) on Cabernet Sauvignon, against heat, drought and two pathogens (l. 29-35).
- Against P. viticola on leaf discs, 10 % PHs cut sporulation by 51 % at 7 days after inoculation; 2 %, 5 % and 7 % gave 0 %, 10 % and 35 % (l. 389-392).
- Incubating sporangia with PHs significantly inhibited zoospore release, similar to copper hydroxide (Kocide) (l. 421-422).
- Sporangia treated with PHs gave less sporulation on fresh leaf discs (l. 416).
- The defence-gene response to PHs was minor (l. 594-595).
- Against Botrytis cinerea, 10 % PHs inhibited in vitro growth by 57 % (l. 459).
- Field validation is not yet done; downy mildew depends on prolonged leaf wetness (l. 648-650).
