# Garcia et al. 2021: tilted diffuse irradiance with sky and circumsolar view factors

Three diffuse models (five variants) are adapted to an urban canyon through a sky view factor and a circumsolar view factor, then tested against a standard-sky reference.

## What it holds

- **Scenarios:** 4,864 combinations of plane tilt and orientation and canyon aspect ratio and azimuth (l. 50-51).
- **Reference:** diffuse irradiance from the ISO 15469 / CIE S 011 sky model, with the sky type of each record found from sky-scanner data at the UPNA radiometric station, observations July to December 2018 (l. 45-50, 255-263).
- **Result:** the Perez model with a circumsolar half-angle of 35 degrees performs best, then 45 degrees (l. 52-54). The triage record said 30 degrees; the text layer shows 35.
- **Not in the first 150 lines:** error values.

## Dependence

- Builds on the Perez et al. 1990 anisotropic model and the Liu-Jordan isotropic model (l. 98-106).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strand s3: how obstacles (a canyon, by analogy a hill or row) cut the sky and circumsolar view of a tilted plane; a simulator could take the view-factor method for a slope or a row wall.
