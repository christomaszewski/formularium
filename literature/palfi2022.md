# Pálfi et al. 2022: paraffin oil and powdery mildew in Hungarian vine gas exchange

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A paraffin oil (PFO) field trial against grape powdery mildew (not downy mildew) in Eger, Hungary, in 2013 and 2014 (l. 19-20).
- Two cultivars, Chardonnay and Kékfrankos, three blocks of 20 vines per treatment, five treatments, doses 1.1, 2.2 and 3.3 v/v% (l. 171-177).
- 2013 was wet in May and June (159 and 131 mm) (l. 351-352).
- In 2014 untreated vines reached over 90% GPM intensity (l. 486).
- PFO cut GPM intensity in 2013 but not as well as the conventional programme (l. 464, l. 474).
- The abstract reports lower gas exchange and berry yield under PFO (l. 24); the conclusions report no relevant negative effect (l. 1011).

## Dependence

- none. The paper uses no engine model: no Goidanich table, 3-10 rule, Magarey or Rossi equations, or phenology sum is computed. The effective heat sum (DD, l. 188) is named without a base, so it cannot be matched to the GDD5 sum; the paper's own GPM data are from ref. 50 (its authors' earlier paper), not a model's shape.
