# Buttaro et al. 2015: a leaf's area from length times width

One line for eight table-grape genotypes, 1,630 leaves, validated on a ninth: the per-leaf
allometry that a leaf count and leaf sizes turn into leaf area per shoot.

## What it holds

- **Data:** a commercial vineyard at Rutigliano (Bari), 250 m, 2.5 × 2.5 m, 2010, leaves
  from 10 to 540 cm² sampled at anthesis, flowering, veraison and ripening, about 200 a
  genotype (l. 113-149); validation on about 200 leaves of Italia in 2011 (l. 157).
- **Model** (Table 3, p. 346; `buttaro2015.leaf_area`): LA = −0.465 + 0.914 (L × W), r²
  0.986, L and W in cm; length alone −88.6 + 20.76 L (0.884), width alone −112.9 + 20.08 W
  (0.754); squared forms 0.865 and 0.765 with identical printed constants (l. 265-269).
  Validation r² 0.986, overestimate 0.8 % (l. 28, 339, 358).
- **Comparators** (Table 1): twelve single-cultivar models, Elsner & Jubb 1988 and
  Tsialtas et al. 2008 biased by 9 and 18 % (l. 95-106, 212-249).

## Dependence

- **None:** a regression on leaf dimensions; genotype lines pooled because alike (not
  shown, l. 237).

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- Allometry only; it gives the truth nothing about time, but turns leaf counts into area.
