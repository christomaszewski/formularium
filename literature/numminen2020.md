# Numminen & Laine 2020: road network and spread of a wild powdery mildew

Presence-absence data for a wild powdery mildew on Plantago in the Åland archipelago. A transmission model gives road and land dispersal separate scales.

## What it holds

- **Study system:** 3,448 host populations on the main island of Åland (l. 266). The introduction says "almost 4000" (l. 118).
- **Dispersal:** posterior mean 404 m (95% CI 341 to 473 m) in one fitted model (l. 619).
- **Roads:** transmission is faster along roads. Infections cluster at the central hubs of the road network (abstract, l. 41-43).
- **Earlier work:** a positive road effect within local populations had been seen before (l. 136-139).
- **Data:** on Dryad, doi 10.5061/dryad.2ngf1vhjq (l. 46-48).

## Dependence

- Road-network summaries as predictors, and the explicit transmission model (l. 38-39, 139-144).
- Earlier P. plantaginis presence-absence analyses (l. 136-139).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t6 (regional spread). A simulator could take the form of separate road and land kernels, plus a hub effect. The numbers come from a wild mildew on a wild host, so they transfer only as a form.
