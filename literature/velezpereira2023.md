# Vélez-Pereira et al. 2023: forecasting airborne fungal spore thresholds in Catalonia

Logistic regression and regression trees forecast whether daily airborne spore concentrations pass four thresholds, for six taxa at eight Catalan stations, using 1995 to 2014 data.

## What it holds

- Six taxa: Alternaria, Cladosporium, Agaricus, Ganoderma, Leptosphaeria and Pleospora (l. 27). None is studied here as a vine disease.
- Eight aerobiological stations in Catalonia, 1995 to 2014 (l. 28-29).
- Logit model sensitivity 62 to 76% and specificity 72 to 81% (l. 569-570). Average of 73% across thresholds (l. 37).
- Spores are linked to weather only. The paper has no disease records.

## Dependence

- None recorded.
