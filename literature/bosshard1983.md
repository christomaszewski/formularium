# Bosshard & Schüepp 1983: metalaxyl sensitivity of P. viticola strains in the field

## What it holds

- Field trials at Wädenswil, Switzerland, 1979-1981, with vineyards inoculated with
  characterised isolates: two isolates were metalaxyl-resistant; sublethal selection did not
  raise resistance, and sexual reproduction did not change it.
