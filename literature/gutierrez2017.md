# Gutierrez et al. 2017: climate warming, grape and the grapevine moth

Linked physiologically based demographic models of grapevine and the grapevine moth Lobesia botrana are run on daily weather for 1980-2010 and an A1B +1.8 C scenario across 4506 lattice cells in the Palearctic. An insect pest model; off-topic for the disease truth.
