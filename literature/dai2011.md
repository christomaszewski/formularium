# Dai et al. 2011: a review of what makes grape berry weight and composition vary

**Read this first.** A review of berry biology. It prints no model, no equation and no measured berry, bunch or canopy temperature. Pieri is one of twelve authors.

## What it holds

- **Temperature and acids (l. 367):** "Elevated temperature clearly decreases" malic acid concentration; cool climates give more (cites Keller 2005, Koundouras 2006, Pereira 2006). No numbers.
- **Light and heat (l. 421-422):** light and temperature "appear to have a synergistic effect at moderate temperature and an antagonistic effect at high temperature" on anthocyanins (Tarara 2008); vigor may act through canopy architecture and "light and thermal microclimate" (l. 420).
- **Modelling (l. 505-510):** closes by calling for systems biology and "modeling of fruit growth and metabolism" (Dai 2010, Genard 2010, Sadras 2008). No model is printed.

## Dependence

- A synthesis of other papers (Pereira et al. 2006, Tarara et al. 2008, Jackson and Lombard 1993); no own data on microclimate. The Pieri-measured cluster microclimate data are behind the Pereira 2006 reference (J. Agric. Food Chem. 54:6765), which is not held and is the better target.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- None for the truth or the engine. It confirms, qualitatively, that cluster temperature and light are modelled outside this paper. Not shown: any berry temperature.
