# Albetis et al. 2017: UAV detection of flavescence dorée

Research article, Gaillac AOC, southwest France; September 2015.

## What it holds

- UAV multispectral discrimination of flavescence dorée symptomatic vines from healthy ones in four Gaillac vineyards (Gamay, Duras, Sauvignon, Colombard), with 20 variables and univariate (ROC AUC) and multivariate classifiers.
- Sensitivity and specificity are tabulated, best for red cultivars (e.g.
- RGI sensitivity 1.00 / specificity 0.98 in Gamay) and poor for white cultivars (e.g.
- Colombard Green 0.46 / 0.81).

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Detection by UAV multispectral images is good on red cultivars and poor on white ones: a reason a drone operator's sensitivity should depend on cultivar. Not downy mildew.
