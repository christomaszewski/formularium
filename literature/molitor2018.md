# Molitor et al. 2018: bunch rot on nine Riesling clones

Four seasons in the Moselle valley: nine Riesling clones differ by about nine days in when their bunch rot reaches 5 % severity.

## What it holds

- Sigmoid curves fitted the progress of bunch rot severity and of grape maturity, with R² above 0.89 (l. 43).
- The earliest clone (Trier 34) and the latest (Heinz 65) differed by 9 days to 5 % severity (l. 45–47). The detailed value is 8.9 days (l. 1122).
- Four seasons, 2013 to 2016, at Zeltingen-Rachtig (l. 34–35, 89).

## Dependence

- Builds on the group's earlier Riesling work on bunch rot and weather (Molitor et al. 2016, l. 36).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t4 (Botrytis bunch rot): clone timing could enter a simulator as a cultivar parameter. A clone is a planting choice, so a decision engine could use the ranking as a fixed input, not as a spray trigger.
