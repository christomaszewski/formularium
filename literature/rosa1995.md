# Rosa et al. 1995: PLASMO 2.11, with a new incubation

The DOS program, and the model behind it, after Rosa et al. 1993 and Orlandini et al.
1993.

## What it holds

- **What changed (pp. 312-313):**
  - Fixed values became tuning parameters.
  - Inoculation and sporangia survival keep the 1993 equations, with n = 75 for
    inoculation and a = 1.7 for survival. Where a enters is not printed.
  - Incubation was reformulated "because of deficiencies shown by the previous model".
- **Incubation (p. 313):** f1(t+1) = f1(t) + d1(T)·d2(RH), hourly.
  - d1 = 4(T - Tmin)(Tmax - T)/(Tmax - Tmin)²;
  - d2 = m·(RH - 30)^(1/2), for T in [Tmin, Tmax] and RH in [30, 100];
  - Tmin = 10 °C and Tmax = 34 °C, "identifiable from literature" (Goidanich 1959,
    Zachos 1959, Magarey et al. 1991);
  - m = 0.082, "derived by comparing several infections and durations of incubation
    periods, with the results of simulations".
  - At 22 °C and 100 % RH this is 0.69 % an hour, about 6 days (arithmetic). The 1993 form,
    m(RH - 30) with m = 0.097, gave under 15 h.
- **How the defaults were set (p. 317):** "by various field trials performed in vineyards
  located in different regions and climatic conditions", chosen where the timing error of
  sporangia emergence was smallest. This is the 1993 paper's wording.
- **Field test, 1992-1993:**
  - **Vineyards:** three in Tuscany: Paretaio (south-east, 15 %, 150 m), Pulizzano
    (north-west, 17 %, 180 m) and Sassuolo (east, 20 %, 170 m) (Table 1).
  - **Plots:** each vineyard was split into untreated, calendar and PLASMO plots, with a
    weather station in each vineyard. 200 leaves a plot were scored in five classes
    (McKinney).
  - **Sprays (Table 2):** PLASMO used 5, 6 and 5 in 1992, and 3, 5 and 5 in 1993. The
    calendar used 8, 8 and 8, then 7, 7 and 7.
  - **1992 disease intensity, untreated (Table 3):** 16.03, 8.37 and 11.94 % on
    10 August; 34.39, 22.71 and 9.75 % on 8 September, averaging 22.28 %. Treated plots
    reached at most 5.10 % (PLASMO) and 3.30 % (calendar).
  - **1993:** dry and hot. Untreated plots stayed under 1 %, and PLASMO overestimated the
    attack.

## Dependence

- **The incubation is fully printed,** so it can be run.
  - Its cardinal temperatures come from Goidanich's manual and Zachos 1959. Its m comes
    from PLASMO's own emergence data.
  - It is recorded calibrated on `goidanich1957`, `zachos1959` and
    `orlandini1993.emergences`, the strict reading.
  - It is the rate-summation form of an incubation table.
  - So it is kin to `goidanich.incubation` and `zachos1959.incubation`, both engine models.
- The rest is the 1993 model ([orlandini1993](orlandini1993.md)): infection fitted to
  Blaeser & Weltzien's data, survival credited to them.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Not the source of Franche 2012's 2.616. That form is linear in humidity; this one is a
  square root.
- A PLASMO truth would hold out the engine's incubations, whichever version it took.
