## What it holds

## Read again (2026-10-27), for Agrarium D60

- **Placement:** install at 30 cm, at a 30-45 degree angle to the ground, facing north in the Northern Hemisphere (l. 90, 106-110, 350-352).
- **Sensor types:** resistance flat plates (Campbell 237L), paper-based resistance (Pessl) and dielectric plates (l. 71-76). Unpainted flat-plate resistance sensors may not detect small droplets, especially at dew onset (l. 77-78; cited from Davis and Hughes 1970).
- **Ageing:** the coating of dielectric plates degrades (yellowish, rougher) and the sensor underreports wetness; replace it every year or when the surface changes (l. 259-269). Paper sensors: check the paper at least every 30 days (l. 355).
- No error in hours is printed in the passages read.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **Tier:** common (the sensor types on most stations). A measured-wetness series should carry its sensor type, height, angle and age; wetness from an old dielectric plate reads low. Turf-height guidance (30 cm), not vine canopy. Kind of result: an extension guide (practice), not a measurement.
