# Onofre et al. 2020: installing leaf wetness sensors

Report, Florida, USA (FAWN stations); no numeric dataset in the lines read.

## What it holds

- An extension guide to installing and maintaining resistive and dielectric leaf wetness sensors, with a list of common faults and fixes.
- It says to mount the sensor at 30 cm, facing north, at 30-45 degrees, over turfgrass, and describes faults such as wrong height, dirty sensors and degraded coating.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- The usual faults of wetness sensors (height, angle, orientation, coating): a checklist for Agrarium's logger operator.
