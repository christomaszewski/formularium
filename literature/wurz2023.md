# Wurz et al. 2023: more buds, more downy mildew, at São Joaquim

## What it holds

- **Trial:** Sauvignon Blanc at São Joaquim (about 1,230 m), 2016/17 and 2017/18; 15, 30,
  50 and 75 buds per vine, all under the same fungicide list (timing not printed).
- **First symptoms:** 1 December 2016 and 30 November 2017.
- **Maximum leaf severity** rose with bud load: 4.4, 7.9, 12.0 and 13.8 % (2016/17); 4.3,
  5.5, 11.7 and 16.7 % (2017/18). Incidence reached 90-100 % at 30 buds and more.
- Symptoms appeared 14-37 days after the first assessment, sooner with more buds.
- Weather from a CIRAM (Epagri) station near the vineyard; December-April rain about 520-533
  mm.

## Dependence

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Denser canopies, more disease, under sprays: context for a truth's canopy and
  microclimate. No untreated data.
