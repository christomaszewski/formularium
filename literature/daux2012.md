# Daux et al. 2012: the open database of grape harvest dates

The reference for long phenology series: 380 harvest-date series, 93 % French, back to
1354, grouped into 27 regional composites and checked against each other and against
instrumental temperatures. A data paper, not a model.

## What it holds

- **The data** (`daux2012.harvest_dates`): 380 series and 16,921 dates as of June 2011
  (l. 35, 714), 1 to 479 points each, mean 45 (l. 37-38), coded as days after 31 August
  (l. 219); the longest composite Burgundy, 20 series over 1354-2006 (Table 1, l. 238).
  A harvest date is a decision of a lord, a town or a prefect, not a stage observation
  (l. 78-99); about 25 % of Dijon's dates differ between Angot 1883 and a recompilation
  from the archives (l. 146).
- **Composites:** the median of series adjusted by their mean difference from a reference
  over the common years, after Chuine et al. 2004 (l. 278-286); mean pairwise correlation
  0.58, the highest 0.91 (High Loire against Ile-de-France, l. 50-52).
- **Temperature:** the composites correlate negatively with April-September Tmax, mean R
  down to −0.67 for Burgundy, Ile-de-France, the Low Loire, the Northern Rhône,
  Champagne and Switzerland, and only to −0.44 for Languedoc, the Southern Rhône and
  Luxembourg (l. 603-617). The days-per-degree figure, 10.2 ± 0.9 d per °C, is Menzel
  2005's and about 10 d per °C in Switzerland, France and the western USA is cited
  (l. 122-126); the paper prints no slope of its own.
- **Held at** the GDR2968 phenology database and NOAA's World Data Center for
  Paleoclimatology, free for research and teaching (l. 211-216, 715).

## Dependence

- **None:** nothing is fitted; the composite method is Chuine et al. 2004's. García de
  Cortázar-Atauri (`cortazar2009`) is a co-author: a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- A reference dataset for a days-per-degree sensitivity of harvest, not of budburst: the
  stage Chris asked about is not in it. Useful if Agrarium ever needs a long
  harvest-date series to test a season-length model against.
