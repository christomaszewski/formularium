# Pérez-Roncal et al. 2022: esca detection in asymptomatic leaves by hyperspectral imaging

Near-infrared hyperspectral images, classified by PLS-DA in the lab, separate healthy, asymptomatic and symptomatic leaves of esca-infected Tempranillo vines.

## What it holds

- 72 leaves, 24 per class, from an esca-infected rainfed vineyard at Olite (Navarra). Leaves were taken near the September 20 harvest at BBCH 89 (l. 111-113, 131).
- Near-infrared imaging over 900-1700 nm (l. 39). Pixel classes use PLS-DA with iPLS and selectivity-ratio variable selection.
- Validation rates of 82.77 to 97.17% for the three-class and two-class models (l. 46). Table 3 gives per-class sensitivity and specificity (l. 496).

## Dependence

- Builds on PLS-DA with iPLS and selectivity-ratio variable selection, as its methods.
- Cites earlier esca leaf spectral studies. None recorded beyond that.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (esca, another grape disease) and t2 (detection before symptoms show). The result is lab only, on leaves. A growers' engine would need a field sensor, and the paper does not test one.
