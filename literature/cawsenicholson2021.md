# Cawse-Nicholson & Anderson 2021: the ECOSTRESS DisALEXI-JPL ET algorithm

Report, Contiguous United States (CONUS) and global ECOSTRESS coverage; evaluation at US flux sites (LTAR, etc.).

## What it holds

- Algorithm document for ECOSTRESS ET: Two-Source Energy Balance (TSEB) with a modified Priestley-Taylor canopy transpiration, ALEXI regional air temperature closure, DisALEXI disaggregation, daily upscaling, and land-cover-based inputs (LAI, cover fraction, roughness, optical properties).
- Gives numbered equations and a parameter table.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- TSEB equations and inputs as an operational product: the reference for ECOSTRESS ET, not a wetness model.
