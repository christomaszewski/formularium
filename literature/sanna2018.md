# Sanna et al. 2018: station calibration and siting change a forecast's dates

## What it holds

- The journal version of Sanna 2017's chapter 4: two stations at Vezzolano (one near trees),
  calibrated in a chamber against fixed points; the EPI downy mildew model run on calibrated
  and uncalibrated data. Calibration moved the predicted infection by up to 4 days, position
  by about 1 day (temperature up to 0.33 °C, RH up to 6.5 %).
- Figure 5 labels the calibrated infection at 25 April where the text says 29 April.
