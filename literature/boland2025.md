# Boland 2025: the BRL described by one of its authors

Read for the units of the BRL's predictors (`laiti2018.brl_bolzano`); Boland is the B of BRL.

## What it holds

- "Additional predictors were added in Ridley et al. (2010), producing the BRL model ... Apparent
  Solar Time (AST), solar altitude, the daily clearness index and a variable that is the mean of the
  clearness index values at the hour before and hour after"; built for hourly data. No units and no
  coefficients are printed; the model is shown only as a figure.

## Bearing (2026-10-19)

- Confirms the predictors, not their units. The units remain to be read at Ridley 2010 or Lauret 2013.
