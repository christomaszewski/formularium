# Nogueira Júnior 2016: rust and downy mildew damage to Vitis labrusca, thesis

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A 2016 doctoral thesis (ESALQ/USP, Piracicaba) on damage by rust (P. euvitis) and downy mildew (P. viticola) in Vitis labrusca cv. Niagara Rosada (l. 3399, 3401).
- Downy mildew cut photosynthesis at the lesions and in adjacent tissue, mainly through lower Rubisco activity (l. 4465, 4467).
- Field yield loss came mainly from berry drop; 40% leaf severity also reduced root starch (l. 4479, 4483).
- The Genecrop-P growth model simulated stages and biomass in 2014-2015; its pathogen-damage part is untested (l. 4692, 4696).
- The beta parameter is 5.8 for P. euvitis and half that for P. viticola (l. 6281).
