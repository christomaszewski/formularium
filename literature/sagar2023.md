# Sagar et al. 2023: E. necator conidial germination in vitro

## What it holds

- Germination peaks at 24 h (31.0 % at 25 °C), at 80 % RH (82.7 %), and at 25 °C (83.7 % at
  24 h; 0 at 5 and 40 °C).
- The same nominal condition (25 °C, 24 h) gives 31.0 %, 83.7 % and 5.7 % in three
  tables; the abstract's 20-25 °C optimum is not in Table 3 (20 °C: 38.6 %).
