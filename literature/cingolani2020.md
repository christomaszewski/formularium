# Cingolani 2020: chitosan against grapevine downy mildew (BSc thesis)

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A 2020 BSc thesis (Università Politecnica delle Marche) on chitosan against grapevine downy mildew in two Marche vineyards, as an alternative or partner to copper (l. 161-168, 2020 season).
- Four treatments in three replicates: chitosan at 0.5 %; half-dose chitosan plus half-dose copper; copper only; and an untreated control, sprayed weekly from late April (l. 174-176, 2010-2046).
- Low disease pressure: at Mulino on 1 July, untreated diffusion was 7.17 % against 3.7 to 5.2 % for the treatments (l. 2338-2341).
- Clusters at Mulino on 1 July: diffusion 20.17 % untreated against 9.22 % (chitosan) and 10.63 % (copper with chitosan) (l. 2516-2518).
- Severity rose on chitosan leaves (2.04 %) and copper-chitosan clusters (3.23 %), which the text does not discuss (l. 2339, 2518).
- Conclusions: no significant differences overall; the low pressure of 2020 hid the effect (l. 2777-2781). Rain in the trial weeks is 172 mm by table, not the 24.6 mm the text gives (l. 2191-2192).

## Dependence

- None computed, fitted or borrowed by the trial. Treatments were weekly, not forecast-timed (l. 176), and the field data are used only for the disease scores. The background describes engine-list rules as the models in use, with citations: the Silimet '10-10-10' rule and Goidanich's incubation table (Baldacci 1947; Goidanich 1957, l. 837-839); the PRO 'three-ten' rule and Müller & Sleumer (1934) incubation curves (l. 849-853); Blaeser (1978) sporulation (13 °C, RH 98 %, 4 h dark, l. 412-414; 12.5 °C and 94 %, l. 854-855) and the 50 °C·h wetness product (l. 498; the brief lists Blaeser & Weltzien's 60 °C·h); incubation of 5 to 18 days (l. 465-466) and Kennelly 2007 for the infectious life of primary lesions (l. 492). These are restated, cited and not tested here; they are flagged, not counted as borrowed. The sporulation numbers (13 °C, 98 %, 4 h) are the same as in the GWRDC sheet magarey2010, which gives them without a citation.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A 2020 field trial of a copper-sparing alternative in two vineyards, with plot-level leaf and cluster scores and weather from two stations: observed data a truth could use, though the disease was at low pressure, the trial is short, and the thesis's model background (10-10-10, Goidanich, Blaeser) is cited, not tested.
