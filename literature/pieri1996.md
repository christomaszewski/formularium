# Pieri et al. 1996: water balance of grassed and tilled vines in the 1995 Bordeaux drought

Two adjacent 1 ha plots of young Merlot, one with grass between rows and one tilled, measured through one very dry summer with sap flow, ETR/ETP and a layer water balance.

**Read this first.** One year, one estate, 4-year-old vines, an unusually dry summer (l. 39-41 of the OCR, page 3). The authors themselves warn against generalising. Figures give no printed numbers; values below are read in the page images and are approximate.

## What it holds

- **Site:** Chateau Talbot, Saint-Julien, Medoc. Merlot on 3309, 4 years old. Sandy gravelly soil, stones plus gravel about 33 % (tilled) and 27 % (grassed), organic matter high at the surface. Grass: ryegrass and creeping fescue mix, 2 years on alternate rows, 1 year on the rest. The plots differ only in soil management, since 1994.
- **Season 1995:** budbreak late March, flowering early June (earlier in tilled), veraison from 26 July, very uneven; berry growth disturbed by drought; rain deficit significant from late June.
- **Table 1 (15-25 June, before trimming):** leaf area per vine 2.7 m2 (sd 0.92) grassed against 3.4 m2 (sd 0.73) tilled; 7700 vines per ha; leaf area index 2.08 grassed and 2.62 tilled.
- **Fig. 1 (page image):** mean predawn leaf water potential from about -0.2 MPa late June to about -1.2 MPa near day 230 (mid-August) in both plots; the tilled plot is more negative earlier.
- **Fig. 2:** vine transpiration by sap flow, day 175 (24 June) peak about 0.4 L per h per vine against below 0.1 L per h on day 205 (24 July); the tilled plot is lower earlier, and the two converge after veraison.
- **Fig. 3:** measured ETR/ETP falls from about 0.5 in late June to 0.2 to 0.3 by mid-August in both plots; ETR by direct measurement, ETP Penman. The text reads 0.5 as proof that neither grass nor tilled soil gave significant losses from late June, so the later course is the vine's own transpiration.
- **Fig. 4:** root-zone water store from a balance using ETR/ETP, ETP and rain, started on the measured 0-60 cm store; the grassed plot's store empties near day 230, the tilled plot's slightly later, from a start at about 80 to 90 mm.
- **Interpretation:** early grass competition for nitrate, with deeper vine roots as a result (must nitrogen 83 against 81 mg per L in this plot, but 7 against 29 and 27 against 75 at two other sites, Table 2).
- **Table 2:** three sites (Talbot Merlot, Couhins Merlot, Larrivet Haut-Brion Sauvignon blanc): grass changes wood weight by -5 %, -29 %, -35 %; yield by +15 %, -19 %, -42 %; must sugar by +2 %, +6 %, +11 %.

## Dependence

- Same group as riou1994 and riou1995, same measurement techniques (sap flow, Penman ETP); no equation is new. Independent of the engine.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- **Agrarium water status:** gives one season of ETR/ETP, predawn potential and sap flow for a Medoc Merlot under drought, a check on a simulated store reaching zero in mid-August; it does not give a stress function.
- **Canopy size:** the grass reduced leaf area index from 2.62 to 2.08, a reference for grass cover effects on leaf area.
- **Not shown:** weather, microclimate, leaf wetness or disease.
