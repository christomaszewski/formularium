# Schnee et al. 2010: ontogenic resistance and growth in powdery mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Powdery mildew (Erysiphe necator) on Cabernet Sauvignon leaves, Couhins 2005; two sampling dates, 5-6 and 10-12 leaves (l. 60-63).
- Sporulation peaks on 5-6 day old leaves and falls with age; inoculum amplitude twice as high at the late date (l. 116-119).
- Leaf glucose rises from under 1 % to over 4 % dry weight, with a sharp rise at 8-9 days (l. 120-123).
- Latresne 2009, cv. Merlot, low and high vigour: maximum sporulation at 6-8 against 8-12 days old (l. 176-179).
- Glucose at 12 days separates the two vigour levels (l. 182).
- Discussion: a low-vigour canopy gives earlier ontogenic resistance (l. 217-221); a PLS-path model is planned for 2010 (l. 232).
