# Vázquez Abal et al. 2019: Galicia's extension monograph on vine diseases

An extension monograph from Galicia's oceanic vineyards. The file was keyed 2011; the
printed year is 2019. It has no bibliography.

## What it holds

- **Downy mildew, primary infection:** shoots above 8-10 cm, mean T above 11-12 °C, rain
  above 10 mm; usually mid-April in the Atlantic zones.
- **Secondary infection:**
  - wetness hours × T above 50, with no unit or source;
  - rain from 4-5 mm, or 6-8 h of night dew;
  - mean T at or below 13 °C practically none; about 15 °C economic damage; above 17 °C
    severe.
- **Incubation:** 8-20 days, and 4-5 days at 20-24 °C with RH above 75 %, credited to "the
  Goidanich model".
- **Sporulation:** optimum 18-22 °C, reduced from 25 °C, inhibited above 30 °C (Ravaz).
- **Treatment:** at the first oil spot, or preventively 1-3 days before a forecast
  infection (6-8 h of wetness at a mean above 14-15 °C).
- **Oospores** mature in spring; no degree-day rule is given.
- **Black rot:** wetness hours needed by temperature, credited to R. A. Spotts (Ohio State,
  1977), p. 24, read in the page image:
  - 7.0 °C: 24 h; 10.0: 12; 13.0: 9; 15.5: 8; 18.5: 7; 21.0: 7; 24.0: 6; 26.5: 9;
    29.0: 12; 32.0: blank.
  - The text says "dende 24 horas de humectación a 10 °C"; the table puts 24 h at 7 °C and
    12 h at 10 °C.
  - **Shifted one row against Spotts 1977** (read 2026-10-10,
    [spotts1977](spotts1977.md), page image): Spotts gives 24 h at 10 °C, 12 at 13 °C, 9
    at 15.5 °C ... 12 at 32 °C, and no infection at 7 °C after 48 h. The monograph's nine
    values are Spotts's in order, one temperature row too low; its text is right. Its
    incubation figures (28 days cold, 8-9 warm) are not in Spotts.
  - Incubation runs from 28 days in the cold to 8-9 days when warm, and stops below 7 °C
    and above 32 °C.
