# Bernizzoni et al. 2011: shoot thinning of potted Barbera, whole-canopy photosynthesis

Four-year-old Barbera in 90 L pots, thinned in early May, with whole-canopy gas exchange
through the season, 2009-2010. Read for leaf area per vine and per shoot; it gives one
value a year, after harvest, not a series.

## What it holds

- **Vines** (l. 96-122): clone R4 on SO4, pots kept well watered; hedgerow, unilateral cane
  at 90 cm, canopy wall 1.3 m above it; tipped at the top wire on 29 May 2009. Thinned on
  3 May 2009 and 7 May 2010; veraison 14 and 24 July; harvest 20 August and 3 September
  (l. 135-137).
- **Leaf area after harvest** (Tables 1, 4, page images; whole vines stripped, area from
  lamina fresh weight × 44.128 (main) or 43.34 (laterals) cm²/g, l. 153-155): 2009 control
  2.93 m² (17.5 shoots, 1,674 cm² a shoot; main 2.01, laterals 0.92), thinned 3.09 m² (10.5
  shoots, 2,942 cm²; main 1.90, laterals 1.19); 2010 control 2.63 m² (30.5 shoots, 862 cm²;
  laterals 0.30), thinned 2.62 m² (15 shoots, 1,746 cm²; laterals 0.34).
- **Compensation** (l. 222-223, 292): thinned shoots grew 75 % (2009) and about 103 % (2010)
  more leaf area each; laterals +116 % in 2009.
- **Unsettled:** Table 1's leaf area removed by trimming prints 2,172 and 1,503 "m²" (cm²
  surely, l. 206); shoot density is 10-15 a metre in the abstract and a vine in the methods.
- **Not printed:** a leaf-area series by date, budburst, LAI.

## Dependence

- Whole-canopy chambers of Poni et al. 1997; the fresh-weight factors are uncited. No model
  shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Leaf area a vine is set more by the vine than by its shoot count:** halving the shoots
  left 2.6 to 3.1 m² a vine, the shoots compensating with larger laterals. Laterals were 10
  to 39 % of the vine's area. Potted, well watered; no timing.
