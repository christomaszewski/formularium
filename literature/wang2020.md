# Wang, Li & García de Cortázar-Atauri 2020: European phenology models moved to China

The transfer test the truth question asks for: six published models (GDD at 5 and 10 °C,
GFV, Duchêne's GDD, BRIN, Caffarra, Wang-Engel) run with their European parameters on
budburst, flowering and veraison of four cultivars at five Chinese sites, four of them
with vines buried for winter, then only the critical sum refitted. Every model is a heat
sum or a chilling sum, so none is a candidate form; the result is what transfer costs.

## What it holds

- **Data:** winery records of the day 50 % of vines reach the stage, Cabernet Sauvignon,
  Cabernet franc, Merlot and Chardonnay at Yanqi, Changli (two wineries), Laixi, Xiaxian
  and Shangri-La (2,381 m, the only site without soil burial), 2009-2017 by site (Table 1,
  l. 177-183); station temperatures 1.4 to 27.8 km away (l. 148-151, 854). The dates by
  site and year are not printed.
- **Models and parameters** (Table 2, l. 335-395): all taken from European studies;
  budburst sums from 1 January (GDD5, GDD10), from 15 February at base 2 °C (Duchêne),
  BRIN's cold action from 1 August with Q10 2.17 and Caffarra's chilling from 1 September
  with the Chardonnay set the catalogue holds (`caffarra2010.chardonnay`), GFV from day
  60 at base 0 °C (`parker2013.gfv`), Wang-Engel with 0 and 40 °C fixed. Run in PMP 5.5
  (l. 435).
- **Transfer:** budburst, every model but GDD10 "performed very poorly" (l. 417); GDD10
  Cabernet Sauvignon RMSE 6.2 d, EF 0.64, 82.5 % of points within 10 d (l. 473-501).
  Flowering, GFV simulated 90.5 % of cases more than 10 d late (l. 512-514); GDD10 best
  for Cabernet franc (RMSE 4.4 d). Veraison, Duchêne's model worst, GFV EF 0.49 for
  Cabernet Sauvignon on 19 observations (l. 596-614); Shangri-La against Laixi show the
  same model at 1 and 27 d of error (l. 671-674).
- **Refit** (Table 6, l. 718-775): only F* re-optimised by simulated annealing on the
  Chinese dates: GDD10 budburst Cabernet Sauvignon 52.5 to 48.8, Chardonnay 33.3 to 18.9,
  Merlot 38.7 to 27.9 °C·d; Wang-Engel flowering 20.3 to 16.5; GFV veraison 2,689 to
  2,610; Caffarra veraison 51.1 to 53.8.

## Dependence

- **Computes** the catalogue's threshold models as published (`cortazar2009.brin`,
  `caffarra2010.chardonnay`, `parker2013.gfv`, GDD) on the wineries' observed dates;
  García de Cortázar-Atauri is an author of BRIN and of PMP.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- The size of the transfer penalty for heat-sum phenology moved a continent: 10 d or
  more for most models and stages until the critical sum is refitted, and half the
  budburst sums for Chardonnay and Merlot after. Any truth phenology fitted elsewhere
  should expect the same unless it is checked against local dates.
