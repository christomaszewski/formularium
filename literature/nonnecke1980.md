# Nonnecke 1980: cluster thinning and shoot-tip removal on Seyval

A 1980 Ohio dissertation on cluster thinning and shoot-tip removal in Seyval, in two Ohio vineyards and in greenhouse vines, in 1979.

## What it holds

- **Field trials:** Vineyards I and II (Chalet Debonne and Valley Vineyards, Ohio), 1979, with yield, cluster number and soluble solids by thinning and tipping treatment (Tables 3 to 18, from l. 2643; experiment list l. 334-352).
- **Greenhouse trials:** Greenhouse Experiments I and II, 1979, with fruit-load and shoot-tip treatments (Table 24 and the contents, l. 281-285).
- **Leaf area at harvest, greenhouse vines (Table 24):** 4665.8 cm2 with no clusters per shoot and 2561.2 cm2 with three; control 5214.3 cm2 against 2309.1 cm2 with 50 % shoot-tip removal (l. 4817-4835).
- **Gas exchange:** net photosynthesis by fruit load and shoot-tip treatment (Table 20, l. 4566-4580).

## Dependence

- Builds on Kliewer and Ough's source-sink and leaf-area work (cited, l. 771 and 1197), net photosynthesis and transpiration measurements, and 12-element petiole analysis (Table 1, l. 2082).
