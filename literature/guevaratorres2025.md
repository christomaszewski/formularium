# Guevara-Torres et al. 2025: grapevine crop coefficients from Sentinel-2

Research article, Barossa and Eden Valleys, South Australia.

## What it holds

- Combines Sentinel-2 vegetation indices with linear spectral unmixing and canopy-cover models to predict grapevine crop coefficient (Kc) for Shiraz, Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay (Tables 4-6).
- It notes the pixel (~100 m2) vs canopy (~2 m2) mismatch, but is about irrigation and not disease.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

- Pixel (about 100 m2) against canopy (about 2 m2) mismatch, for irrigation; context for the satellite operator.
