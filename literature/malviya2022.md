# Malviya et al. 2022: microbial biocontrol of powdery mildew in India

Research article, Pune district, Maharashtra, India (ICAR-NRCG, MRDBS, farmers' fields at Narayangaon and Junnar).

## What it holds

- Field trials at four sites of three microbial biocontrol products, alone and with sulfur, against grape powdery mildew, reporting percent disease index on leaves and bunches and fruit quality.
- It is efficacy data for products, not a weather or infection formulation.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Product efficacy trials; nothing for either tool.
