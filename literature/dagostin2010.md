# Dagostin et al. n.d.: screening copper substitutes vs downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Copper use in organic viticulture is restricted or banned in several European countries (l. 16-19).
- The REPCO project (2004-2007) screened 113 substances at several doses for downy mildew (l. 24-25, l. 38).
- Indoors, on potted Pinot Gris and Chasselas, 63.4% of 219 substance-dose combinations significantly cut severity; 36.5% matched copper (l. 104-108).
- 24% of substances went to field trials in Italy and Switzerland, 2004-2007 (l. 110, l. 66).
- Natural products often lost efficacy in the field (l. 113-119).
- Some products were as good as copper on bunches; a low-dose copper formulation, fatty acids and synthetic compounds protected leaves (l. 121-122, l. 195-198).
- No product could yet fully replace copper (l. 207-208).

## Dependence

- none. An efficacy screen of products against inoculated leaves and in field plots; no weather, infection or sporulation model is computed, fitted or shared in form. The 'humid chamber, 12 h, 20 C' step (l. 59-60) is a protocol condition, not a model.
