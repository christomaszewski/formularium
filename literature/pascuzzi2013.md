# Pascuzzi 2013: forward speed and fan airflow in a tendone vineyard

Spray trials in a tendone table-grape vineyard near Bari, with three forward speeds and two fan airflows. Foliar deposit was read by spectrophotometry.

## What it holds

- **Vineyard:** double-grid tendone at Adelfia, Bari province (l. 139-141).
- **Design:** three forward speeds (0.67, 1.08 and 1.58 m/s) and two airflows (1.68 and 2.73 m³/s), three replicates, at two phenological stages (l. 41-44; l. 200-205).
- **Airflow:** the fan's airflow was unsuitable for the dense canopy, but its diffusers could reach the cluster zone (l. 50-52; l. 56-59).
- **Measured air speeds:** Table 1 (l. 311). Trial plan: Table 2 (l. 270).

## Dependence

- Spectrophotometric deposit method from Cerruto 2007b (l. 149-150).
- Pergher 2006 on hedgerow vineyards, where speeds above 2.5 m/s could reduce penetration (l. 143-145).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (spray deposition):** the airflow finding is a rule for dense tendone canopies. It comes from one vineyard, and the cluster-zone values were not read in full.
- Not read in full: the sector values (Fig. 8) and the discussion were read only in part. The trial year is not printed.
