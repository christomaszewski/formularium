# Viret and colleagues 2012: Changins downy mildew, 2003 to 2011 (Tableau 10)

Agroscope's activity report for 2011; one table gives, per year at Changins, oospore maturity, the first primary infection, the first oil spot in the control and a separate indicator-plant series, and the text says 2011 was nearly free of mildew.

**Read this first.**
- Two things printed in this report sit badly together for 2011. Tableau 10 prints 16 May 2011 as the first oil spot in the control (l. 3252, a lesion seen). The viticulture section says the disease "a été quasiment absente du vignoble jusqu'à mi-août" (was nearly absent from the vineyard until mid-August; l. 3077) and is headed "Situation mildiou: le calme presque absolu" (mildew situation: almost absolute calm; l. 184). Both are printed; this note does not choose. The text speaks of the whole vineyard and the table of the Changins laboratory, but the report does not say how they fit.
- The authors printed on the cover are O. Viret, C. Balmelli, K. Gindro, T. Steinger and J. Wirth; the table's caption says "années 2000-2009" yet its columns run 2003 to 2011.
- The "plantes indicatrices" row is a different series from the control row; do not mix them.

## What it holds

- **Tableau 10 header (l. 3230-3234):** "Changins, laboratoire extérieur", columns 2003 to 2011; 2005 onward are marked "*" (based on the VM-Plasmopara software; 2003 and 2004 on SmartGraph).
- **First primary infection (l. 3250):** 2003 11 May, 2004 1 June, 2005 14 May, 2006 18 May, 2007 30 April, 2008 16 May, 2009 27 May, 2010 5 May, 2011 11 May. These are infections, as printed in the row "Date 1ère inf. primaire".
- **First oil spot, control (row "Date première tache huile: témoin", l. 3252; lesions seen):** 2003 26 May, 2004 11 June, 2005 6 June, 2006 22 May, 2007 29 May, 2008 26 May, 2009 25 May, 2010 18 May, 2011 16 May.
- **Indicator plants (row "Première tache d'huile: plantes indicatrices", l. 3254; lesions seen):** 2003 11 May, 2004 3 August, 2005 "Jamais" (never), 2006 12 May, 2007 12 June, 2008 26 May, 2009 31 May, 2010 and 2011 "n.d.".
- **Other rows (l. 3236-3247):** 2003 oospore sporulation maximum 23 April, 2004 19 April, 2005 6 May, 2006 11 May, 2007 30 April, 2008 5 May, 2009 11 May, 2010 12 April, 2011 5 April. The 140 degree-day sum (>8 degrees C) was reached on 30 April 2003, 15 May 2004, 3 May 2005, 7 May 2006, 22 April 2007, 11 May 2008, 30 April 2009, 30 April 2010 and 19 April 2011.
- **Indicator-plant dates against the control:** the indicator plants showed spots before the control in 2003 (11 May against 26 May) and 2006 (12 May against 22 May), and after it in 2004, 2007 and 2009; in 2005 they never did.
- **2011 section (l. 3074-3080):** the first five months of 2011 were "extrêmement secs et chauds"; mildew was nearly absent until mid-August, then appeared, "parfois de manière assez importante", on the upper foliage.

## Dependence

- Years from 2005 on are based on VM-Plasmopara; 2003 and 2004 on SmartGraph, as the caption says. First spots are observations; the first primary infection is a modelled or judged day. The 2011 text is silent on how the table's 16 May relates to its "calme" text.

## Bearing (2026-11-05)

- **Agrarium D70:** Tableau 10 gives the laboratory's first oil spots (lesions seen) for 2003 to 2011 next to a first primary infection (an infection dated back or modelled) in the same table, so the two series can be told apart for these years.
- **The indicator-plant series (2003 to 2009)** is separate from the laboratory's, with different dates in most years; it should not stand in for the laboratory.
- **2011:** the table's 16 May and the section's mid-August do not agree; treat 2011 as unsettled. Kind of evidence: an institute activity report, not peer-reviewed.
