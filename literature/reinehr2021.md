# Reinehr et al. 2021: prohexadione-calcium and downy mildew on Merlot at 1,430 m

## What it holds

- **Trial:** Merlot at São Joaquim, Santa Catarina (1,430 m), 2017-18 and 2018-19;
  prohexadione-calcium at 0 to 1,500 g/ha over the grower's fungicide programme (no plot
  untreated).
- **First symptoms:** early November 2017, mid-November 2018.
- **Downy mildew AUDPC:** 678.2 (no ProCa) against 216.1 at 1,000 g/ha; maximum severity
  28.2 % against 8.8 %. Botrytis AUDPC 73.2 against 10.4. Yield fell with ProCa.
- **Infection dates** are counted back from symptoms with a 5-18-day latent period (cited
  to Kennelly et al. 2005): a model's dates.
- Weather from Epagri (distance not printed); season means 14.8 °C, 80.3 % RH.
