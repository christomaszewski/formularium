# Lafond et al. 2010: field sensitivity to downy mildew mapped for decisions

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Field check of a soil-based sensitivity map for downy mildew (soil class from water holding capacity, drainage and rock type), applied in Anjou and Saumur, Loire Valley (l. 53-74; image p. 176).
- Nine plots, two sets of 5 to 7 vines per soil class; weekly frequency and intensity from 1 May to 15 August 2009 (l. 99-110).
- 2009 results: early symptoms came first in the high-sensitivity part in 2 of 9 plots, the low-sensitivity part in 2 others, and the same week in 5 (l. 120-123).
- One plot had significantly more attack on the high-sensitivity part; two others showed the reverse (l. 128-130). Figure 5 shows only two plots.
- Authors: no global trend; the design gave one random primary contamination an outsized effect; the 2009 data cannot validate the map (l. 160-190).
- Plan for 2010: a wider untreated area of more than 100 stocks (l. 192-194).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A field test of whether soil-based intrinsic sensitivity classes explain downy mildew severity, which the truth could use as a site covariate, but this paper reports only a 2009 trial whose authors judge it inconclusive and does not give the 2010 result.
