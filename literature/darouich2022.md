# Darouich et al. 2022: ground cover and height of VSP vineyards in a water-balance model

SIMDualKc fitted to a rainfed Barbera vineyard in Monferrato (tilled or grassed) and a
drip-irrigated vineyard at Samora Correia. Read for canopy data.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 723-805): Monferrato, rainfed Barbera planted 1988, rows 2.75 m, vines
  1.0 m, VSP, tillage or spontaneous grass since 2000; Samora Correia, VSP planted 2008,
  2.8 × 1.0 m (the text swaps the terms, l. 795), 465 to 625 mm of drip a year.
- **Canopy by stage** (Table 4, l. 1126-1162): ground cover at mid-season 0.35 to 0.39
  (tilled) and 0.25 to 0.29 (grassed) at height 1.70 to 1.90 m; Samora Correia 0.35 to
  0.36 at 1.70 m; late-season height 1.10 to 1.20 m. Grassed inter-row cover 0.65 (l. 1136).
- **Not printed:** LAI, leaf area, shoots, canopy width. The GDD base is not printed.

## Dependence

- FAO-56 dual Kc in SIMDualKc (Allen, Pereira); calibrated on the authors' soil water.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **No leaf area.** A spontaneous grass cover cut the vines' ground cover by about a quarter
  against tillage at Monferrato (0.25-0.29 against 0.35-0.39), a pointer, not a value.
