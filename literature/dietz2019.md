# Dietz et al. 2019: oat foliar diseases, nitrogen and healthy area duration

## What it holds

- Two years of split-split-plot field trials at La Plata, Argentina: fungicide, nitrogen and
  oat genotype. Fungicide lowered crown rust and leaf spot severity and raised healthy area
  duration (HAD) and yield; nitrogen raised rust severity.
- The authors propose HAD-yield relations for modelling losses.

## Dependence

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Another crop and disease. Of use to Cooptera only as an example of yield loss through
  healthy area duration.
