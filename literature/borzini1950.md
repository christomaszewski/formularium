# Borzini 1950: the incubation calendar in Italian spray practice

## What it holds

- Italian extension article on the cost of plant protection in viticulture. It recommends
  timing copper sprays by Baldacci's incubation calendar, which it says saves one or two
  treatments a season, and combining powdery mildew sprays with them.
