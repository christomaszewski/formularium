# Kabela et al. 2009: dew in corn and soybean during SMEX05

From Iowa State University and USDA-ARS.

## What it holds

- Field study SMEX05 near Ames, Iowa, 14 June to 5 July 2005, corn (WC10, WC22) and soybean (WC11, WC15) (l. 130, 147).
- Dew duration from painted Model 237 sensors, 15-min records (l. 142). Dew amount by weighing towels on leaves, scaled by LAI.
- Dew on 86% of days in corn WC10 and 81% in soybean WC11 (l. 255). Corn at 1:30 and 6:00 CST: 76% and 71% (l. 267); soybean 76% and 62% (l. 279).
- Amounts 0.01 to 0.6 mm in corn, 0.003 to 0.8 mm in soybean (l. 318); the 0.8 mm is probably too high (l. 593).
- ALEX (Anderson et al. 2000): Wmax = 2 Wleaf LAI, Wleaf <= 0.15 mm after Wilson et al. 1999 (l. 440, 445); dew when vegetation is below the in-canopy dew point (l. 466). Not tuned (l. 454).
- Judged on 6 days (l. 122): onset or dry-off within 30 min of sensors; amount within 0.1 mm except three soybean observations (l. 564).
- Co-authors Anderson (ALEX) and Gleason (1994 model): flags only.

## Dependence

- Its own dew measurements, SMEX05 (14 June to 5 July 2005), compared with ALEX run untuned. M. C. Anderson, an ALEX author, is a co-author; Gleason (engine's Sentelhas 2008) is a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Clear. Dew amounts and timing in two crops: a check on a truth's dew, though not in a vineyard. The authors think the 0.8 mm soybean maximum is too high.
