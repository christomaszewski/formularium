# Ágoston 2020: downy mildew in an epidemic and an average year

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Downy mildew in an epidemic year against an average year, Bács-Kiskun County, Hungary, 2009 and 2010 (l. 21-22, 69).
- 2009: 4 % of fruit bunches in mid-June and 14 % by late August, 1 % of it medium severity (l. 77-78).
- 2010: 74 % in mid-June and 90 % by late August, of which 37 % low, 42 % medium and 11 % high severity (l. 85-87).
- Epidemic: over 25 % of fruit-bunch occurrence, a threshold with no source (l. 99-100).
- Cited primary-infection rule: mean daily temperature above 10 °C and at least 10 mm of rain within 48 hours (l. 38-39).
- Data from the NIR system and inspectors' reports (l. 70-72). No model is fitted.
