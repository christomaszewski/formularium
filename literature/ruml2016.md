# Ruml et al. 2016: days per degree for 20 cultivars at Sremski Karlovci

Twenty-six years of stage onsets for 20 cultivars in one collection, each stage regressed
on the mean or maximum temperature of a period before it: the clearest table of
sensitivities in days per °C the search found, per cultivar and for the average. From
Belgrade and Novi Sad.

## What it holds

- **Data** (`ruml2016.karlovci`): the Novi Sad faculty's collection at Sremski Karlovci
  (110 m, 12.3 °C, 650 mm; l. 138-147), 20 cultivars (7 red, 13 white), 20 vines each,
  phenology 1986-2011 (l. 151-160); the national service's daily Tmax and Tmin at the
  site 1981-2007 (l. 176-200), so the relations use 1986-2007 (l. 207).
- **Trends** (Tables 3-4): average cultivar budburst 10 April, −0.25 d/yr (not
  significant); flowering 28 May, −0.42; veraison 26 July, −0.72; harvest 17 September,
  −0.65 d/yr (l. 441, 507). Growing-season degree-days 1,528, +12 per year (l. 320).
- **Regressions** (Table 8, p. 200; `ruml2016.monthly_regression`): onset = k + α·T, T
  the mean daily temperature from 1 March to the onset (budburst), the daily maximum
  from 15 April to the onset (flowering), the maximum over 1 April-30 June (veraison)
  and 1 April-31 August (harvest), periods picked by correlation (l. 262, 629-639).
  Average cultivar: −3.6 d/°C (k 133.0, R² 0.71), −3.1 (217.1, 0.85), −5.2 (332.9,
  0.90), −7.4 (442.3, 0.90). Budburst slopes from −2.6 (Cabernet Sauvignon) to −4.4
  (Prokupac, Chardonnay); Pinot Noir's is printed 4.0 without its sign (l. 657-659,
  766). The summary's 74-90 % of variance explained overstates budburst (l. 37).
- **Degree-days** are an index here (sum above 10 °C), correlated with budburst (r −0.82
  with the sum from 1 January) and harvest (−0.89), not a fitted predictor (l. 253,
  621, 651).
- **Cited sensitivities:** Tomasi et al. 2011 (Veneto) 2.9, 4.1, 3.2 and 8.0 d/°C;
  Jones et al. 2005 3-6 d/°C (l. 914-919).

## Dependence

- **Fitted to** the authors' own observations; no model dated them. No hold-out: the
  period and the fit come from the same years.
- **Form shared** with `fraga2016.monthly_regression`; Didevarasl 2026 cites it.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- The best source of a prior on sensitivity: 3-4 d of budburst, 3 d of flowering, 5 d
  of veraison and 7-8 d of harvest per °C, with cultivar spread. The budburst and
  flowering windows end at the onset, so as printed the model cannot forecast; the
  veraison and harvest ones close before the stage.
