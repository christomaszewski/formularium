# Latinovic & Latinovic 2010: Profiler fungicide against downy mildew in Montenegro

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Field trial, Biotechnical faculty, Podgorica, 2009, cv. Vranac, four randomised blocks (l. 17-19, 27-29).
- Seven sprays from 15 May to 8 July (l. 32-35): Profiler at 2.25 or 3.0 kg/ha against Verita at 3.0 kg/ha (l. 37-38).
- Severity on 20 June: untreated 20.8 %, Profiler 0.0 %, Verita 0.4 % (Table 1, l. 100-106).
- Severity on 14 July: untreated 43.6 %, Profiler 0.8 and 0.3 %, Verita 8.0 % (Table 2, l. 129-132).
- Yield per vine: Profiler 4.70 and 4.83 kg, Verita 3.58 kg, untreated 1.83 kg (Table 3, l. 153-158).
- June rain 84 mm and 107 mm; July mean 27.3 °C (l. 75-82).
