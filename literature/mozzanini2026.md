# Mozzanini et al. 2026: spray deposit and ground loss from fixed hydraulic layouts

Two hydraulic fixed-spray layouts are compared across three growth stages and two canopy densities. An airblast sprayer is the reference.

## What it holds

- One 13-year-old Barbera vineyard at Grugliasco, Turin, with trials at BBCH 57, 79 and 89 (l. 84, 98).
- Layout L2 gave 1.75 and 1.55 times the canopy deposit and ground loss of L1 (l. 35).
- Mean ground loss was 2.06 µl/cm² at BBCH 57, and 0.75 and 0.70 µl/cm² at BBCH 79 and 89 (l. 564-565). The season mean fell from 2.06 to 0.72 µl/cm² (l. 964).
- Data are available on request. No repository or licence is printed (l. 962).

## Dependence

- Builds on the point quadrat technique for canopy density (Palleja and Landers 2015) and on the Grella et al. canopy protocols (2019, 2020).
- Builds on the authors' own FSDS layout and cleaning work (Mozzanini et al. 2023, 2024).
- Uses a 30% spray coverage threshold for under- and over-spray, with the Syngenta criteria (l. 386, 421-423).
