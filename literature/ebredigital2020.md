# Ebredigital.cat 2020: how the Catalan downy mildew warning service describes its method

A news item of 2020-06-09 that relays the Catalan Department of Agriculture's description of its warning service and its prize for the first mildew spots.

**Read this first.**
- It is a press relay of a department's statement, not the service's own document. The model is not named, and the item gives no thresholds, no dates and no season data.
- It is Catalonia-wide (the networks in the main wine zones), not Penedès only.

## What it holds

- **Models (l. 7):** by "applying certain prediction models" the best moments for the grower's fungicide sprays can be found. The model is not named.
- **Inputs (l. 7):** daily temperature, relative humidity and rain, with follow-up of phenology and of the fungus's progress. The department's plant-health service keeps manual weather stations in "anti-mildew" networks in the main wine zones, read daily with the plant-protection groups (ADV) and growers, and complemented by the Generalitat's automatic weather station network (l. 7).
- **First spots (l. 8):** locating the first mildew spots, the primary infections, is a "basic factor" of the system. Since the 1980s the department has awarded prizes for finding them in each wine zone (l. 8). The 2020 prizes total 3 180 euros in 30 awards (l. 8).
- **Advice (l. 9):** after the first spots, technicians use the weather data to judge the moments of maximum infection risk and, through notices to growers, recommend the most suitable sprays and avoid unnecessary ones.
- **What a first-spot date therefore is:** a detection date from growers claiming a prize, not a measured onset of infection.

## Dependence

- None stated. The model is unnamed, so no equations or data are recorded.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)
