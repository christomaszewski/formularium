# Keil 2007: infection by temperature and wetness, sporulation, and sun on sporangia

A Hohenheim dissertation (the file name says 2008; the cover says 2007). Its main use for
us is a full table of infection against temperature and wetness, from data that are not
Blaeser & Weltzien's.

## What it holds

- **Infection on leaf discs:**
  - **Method (p. 41):** Müller-Thurgau leaf discs, 20 µl of 25,000 sporangia/ml (about 500
    sporangia a disc), 5-30 °C, 1-23 h of wetness, 20 discs a cell. Output: the percent of
    stomata infected in ten intercostal fields.
  - **Data:** every disc value of the 55 cells is printed (Anhang 7.1).
  - **Pattern (Table 10, p. 74):**
    - lag 3 h at 5 °C, under 1 h at 22-30 °C;
    - plateau by about 10 h at every temperature;
    - maxima 9 % (5 °C), 16 (10), 19 (15), 22 (20), 23 (22), 20 (25) and 11 % (30).
  - Not printed: the wetting method, the temperature after wetting, and when discs were
    scored.
- **Against a degree-hour sum** (arithmetic on the appendix means): cells at 60 °C·h (10 °C ×
  6 h, 15 × 4, 20 × 3, 30 × 2) gave 8.7-13.6 %. Some cells below 60 infected as well: 15 °C
  × 3 h gave 10.7 %, 22 °C × 2 h gave 14.2 %. Beyond 10 h the product keeps rising while
  infection does not (22 °C: 23.0 % at 10 h, 19.9 % at 23 h). No 60 °C·h rule is stated.
- **Sporulation (p. 53):** sporangia per cm² of field lesions = -747.5 T² + 33397 T - 330581
  (R² 0.8955), fitted to Keil's means at 15-30 °C. Optimum "ca. 23 °C"; sporulation seen
  down to 8 °C; almost none at 30 °C (record `keil2007.sporulation`).
- **Sporangia quality:** formed at 27.5 °C against 15 °C, 5.9 against 4.3 nuclei, and
  vitality 40.5 % against 72.7 % (Table 8).
- **Sun (p. 63):** on glass in direct sun, the excess dead over shaded controls is 3 ×
  hours - 1.4 points (R² 0.85); about 10 % after 3-5 h, 23 % after 7 h, at 0.65 kWh/m²
  (record `keil2007.sun_mortality`).
- **Dispersal:**
  - wind released sporangia at 1-10 m/s;
  - insects carried them;
  - 2 of over 50 air-filter samples were positive, at least 15 km from vines;
  - wounded leaf tops infected at 42 % against 4 % unwounded.
- **Field (WBI Freiburg, untreated, 2004-2006):**
  - Large jumps in incidence followed long rains with more than 500 degree-hours of leaf
    wetness (p. 79).
  - Incubation in five assignable cases averaged 6.4 (±0.7) days.
  - How infection dates were assigned is not printed.
- **Printed inconsistencies:** several, listed by the reader. For example, 2006's final
  assessment is 65.8 % in the text and 89.2 % in the appendix.

## Dependence

- Its formulations are fitted to its own counts; it computes no engine model. Goidanich,
  Rossi 2008, Magarey, Kennelly and the 3-10 rule are absent.
- **VitiMeteo:** the infection strength of VitiMeteo-Plasmopara was set "nach
  Erfahrungswerten" (Bleyer, personal communication), and Keil's results are "to be
  integrated". No values are printed, so the link is intent only. VitiMeteo's infection is
  not an engine model.
- Keil, Immink & Kassemeyer 2006 ([keil2006](keil2006.md)) is not cited.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- **An infection dataset independent of Blaeser & Weltzien's** (`keil2007.freiburg`).
  - A truth's infection response fitted to it would not share calibration data with the
    engine's Magarey (Brischetto) or Rossi infection.
  - It also contradicts a cumulative degree-hour rule beyond about 10 h.
- **For D30:** sun alone kills sporangia slowly; heat and dryness must do most of what
  Kennelly 2007 saw.
