# Brink et al. 2006: fungicide spray cover and grey mould in bunches

A controlled study of spray cover on Botrytis infection of table-grape bunches. Infection was measured by isolation after spraying in a chamber and inoculation in a settling tower, and fitted as linear regressions on cover.

## What it holds

- Spray volumes of 1 to 15 mL per bunch on Dauphine bunches from vineyards at Paarl and Worcester, Western Cape (l. 70, 103-105).
- Controlled, not field: conidia dusted in a settling tower, then 24 h at 98% relative humidity (l. 14-16).
- Pea size, rachis on Kerssies medium: y = 46.54 - 8.40x, R2 79.79%, with x the percent cover (Table 1, l. 180).
- At bunch closure the fits were weaker, R2 from 3.71% to 80.92% (Table 2, l. 191-196).

## Dependence

- Fluorescent-pigment coverage protocol of Brink et al. 2004 (l. 79-80).
- The thesis behind it, `brink2005` (read 2026-10-26), prints the same regressions
  (its Chapter 2 Tables 1-2) and gives the incubation as 24 h at 93 % RH or more in its
  methods, 98 % in its summary; it develops the pigment protocol itself.
- Infection biology cited as Holz et al. 2003 (introduction).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strands t1 (fungicide protection) and t4 (grey mould). The linear incidence-on-cover fits are a dose-response input for a spray model.
- The fits come from one study with two sites, and the bunch-closure fits are weak. Not read in full.
