# Chalfant 2012: early-season leaf removal timing and method

An Ohio MSc thesis on early-season leaf removal, by hand or by machine, in Chambourcin and Cabernet Franc in 2010 and 2011.

## What it holds

- **Design:** leaf removal at pre-bloom, bloom or fruitset against a control, in Chambourcin in 2010 and 2011; manual against mechanical removal in Cabernet Franc (abstract, l. 60-75).
- **Leaf area removed:** 71 %, 59 % and 54 % at pre-bloom, bloom and fruitset in 2010; 77 %, 57 % and 46 % in 2011 (l. 1354-1360).
- **Leaf area over the seasons:** leaf area at harvest did not differ among treatments. Leaf area at treatment rose 30 % (pre-bloom and bloom) and 85 % (fruitset) from 2010 to 2011 (l. 1361-1367).
- **Yield and crop load:** early leaf removal lowered the Ravaz index in both years, and pre-bloom or bloom removal reduced Chambourcin yield (abstract, l. 69-74).
- **Botrytis:** in 2010 incidence was highest after fruitset removal and lowest in the control. No difference in 2011 (l. 1404-1407).

## Dependence

- None recorded. Crop load is measured as the Ravaz index and the leaf area to fruit ratio (l. 546-548). Leaf area per vine is estimated from two shoots per vine with a leaf area meter (l. 1054-1066).

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- classes.tsv's reason asks for the leaf area values. The thesis gives leaf area removed as a percentage, and leaf area per vine at treatment and at harvest, over two seasons (l. 1054-1067, 1351-1367). The table values did not extract cleanly, so only the percentages are in the text read.
