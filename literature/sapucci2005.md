# Sapucci et al. 2005: relative humidity of six radiosonde types flown together in Brazil

Radiosondes of several makers were flown on shared rigs and compared with the Vaisala RS80 as reference (l. 41-46). They measure humidity profiles aloft, not near-surface air at saturation.

## What it holds

- **Experiment:** CLA station, 2 deg 18 min S, 44 deg 22 min W (l. 126-127); 18 days, 21 May to 7 June 2001 (l. 129-130); 43 flights (l. 135).
- **Sensors:** RS80, RS90, MKII, DFM-97, GL-98 and the Snow White hygrometer (l. 146-152).
- **Headline:** bias median against RS80 about 1.8% in the low troposphere; median rms about 14.9% in the upper layers (l. 44-46).
- **Table 3** (l. 397-409): e.g. in the first layer MKII minus RS80 bias +7.44% RH and rms 10.18% (l. 402); RS90 minus RS80 bias +1.47%, rms 3.49% (l. 401).
- Snow White accuracy is said to be of the order of 2% at saturation under tropical conditions (l. 214); RS80 dry bias is about 10% in the wet lower troposphere in earlier work cited (l. 93-95).

## Dependence

- None recorded beyond the cited WMO experiment design (Silveira et al. 2003, l. 116-143).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **s5 sensor error** (context only): shows that humidity sensor types differ by a few percent RH in the low layer and much more aloft. Not a surface T and RH study, and not near saturation at vineyard height. Not read in full.
