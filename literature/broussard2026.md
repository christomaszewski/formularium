# Fittipaldi Broussard et al. 2026: sage VOCs vs downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Sage (Salvia officinalis) and Chardonnay vines shared airtight boxes for 24 or 48 hours, with grape-only controls (l. 163, 192).
- Leaves exposed to sage were inoculated with P. viticola from Northern Italy; mean severity was 64.6 per cent after grape-only and 32.1 per cent after sage, about half (l. 587).
- Sporulation intensity did not differ (l. 588), though the Discussion says it did (l. 715).
- The boxes reached 100 per cent RH within four hours and stayed there (l. 225).
- Field validation was not done (l. 790); the box experiment is a glasshouse proof of concept.

## Dependence
