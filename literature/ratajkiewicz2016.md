# Ratajkiewicz et al. 2016: a leaf-number spray-volume rule for processing tomato

A spray volume set by leaf number, with a spray-volume index, gave less late blight at half the fungicide dose than a fixed 300 L/ha. The trials ran over three seasons in Poland, on tomato.

## What it holds

- Proportionate spray volume: PSV = LN × SVI, with SVI = 300/LN1 (l. 222-226). The fixed reference is 300 L/ha (l. 213).
- At half dose without adjuvant, the PSV model gave less late blight than the fixed model (l. 35-36).
- Trials ran in 2009, 2010 and 2011 at Poznań (l. 215). The NegFry model gives the accumulated late-blight risk used in the analysis (l. 315, 360, 411).
- Residue method: detection limit 0.02 mg/kg (l. 501).

## Dependence

- Builds on the treated-area concept (Weisser and Koch 2002, l. 64), and on the NegFry late-blight model (Hansen et al. 1995).
- Uses a modified QuEChERS residue method (l. 32).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t1 (fungicide protection, spray volume). This is tomato, not vine. A canopy-scaled volume rule like this one is something a vineyard tool could test.
