# Colombo et al. 2020: NoPv1 aptamer vs grapevine downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- NoPv1, an 8-residue peptide, binds the P. viticola cellulose synthase PvCesA2 in a yeast two-hybrid screen (l. 99-101).
- On Pinot noir leaf disks, 200 uM NoPv1 stops sporulation completely with no tissue damage (l. 205-206).
- Nebulized 800 uM NoPv1 matched the copper fungicide Kocide 2000 (l. 254).
- In greenhouse plants, 1 mM NoPv1 sprayed 7 and 2 days before inoculation kept efficacy above 60% (l. 270-272).
- At 200 uM no zoospore formed a germ tube (n = 160; control n = 90) (l. 368-371).
- No effect on three bacteria or on Erysiphe necator (l. 397). The authors say field use is held back by cost and by the high dose (l. 497).
