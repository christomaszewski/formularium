# Viret et al. 2019: Changins oospores mature on 23 April 2019, then held back by a cold May; no oil spots in untreated controls before mid-August

A Swiss vintage review with a 13-row station table (model 140 C date, first computed primary infection, first oil spots) and the Changins germination date, in a year when the cold May stopped the winter eggs.

**Read this first.**
- Table 2's 140 C column is the model. At Changins it reads 19 May, 26 days after the laboratory maturity date of 23 April: the model came after maturity, the reverse of 2017.
- In the text layer the Table 2 rows for Villeneuve/Salquenen and Yvorne/Pully are displaced (a model date sits on the line of the neighbouring row), so only the dates the reading agent checked are used below.
- "n.d." (not determined) means no first-spot date for that station.
- The Changins 15 August date is the untreated controls' first oil spot; the same sentence calls it the first time in twenty years the controls had none before mid-August.

## What it holds

- **Oospore maturity (l. 1050 to 1052):** the winter eggs matured (germination in 24 h) on 23 April at Changins, then regressed in May because the cold May blocked their development ("se rétracter en mai").
- **Untreated controls (l. 1052 to 1054):** for the first time in twenty years, the untreated controls at Changins showed no oil spots before mid-August.
- **General indicator (l. 1054 to 1058):** the 140 degree-day sum at 8 C was reached between the end of April and mid-May; first primary infections were indicated by Agrometeo between 8 and 25 May (Table 2); the vine had 3 to 4 unfolded leaves (BBCH 13-14).
- **Table 2, Changins (l. 1027):** 140 C reached 19 May, first primary infection 25 May, first oil spots 15 August.
- **Table 2, other stations (first oil spots):** Fully-Branson (Valais) 12 June (l. 1014); Salquenen (Valais) 12 June (l. 1017); Aigle (Vaud) 20 June (l. 1019); Marcelin-sur-Morges (Vaud) 20 June (l. 1028).
- **Table 2, 140 C and first infection for those stations:** Fully-Branson 29 April, 8 May (l. 1014); Aigle 8 May, 8 May (l. 1019); Marcelin-sur-Morges 21 May, 25 May (l. 1028). The other eight rows (Vétroz, Villeneuve, Leytron/Saillon, Martigny-Combe, Dézaley, Chardonne, Yvorne, Pully) have "n.d." for first spots.
- **Lake Geneva basin (l. 1062 to 1063):** first infections 18 May at Pully and 25 May at Changins and Marcelin-sur-Morges.

## Dependence

- **Own observations** of the Changins group for the germination test and the untreated controls; the 140 C dates and first infections are Agrometeo/VitiMeteo output. Dubuis et al. 2012 say the Changins test and controls serve to validate that model.
