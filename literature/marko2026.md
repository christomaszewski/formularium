# Markó et al. 2026: long-term disease occurrence and vineyard factors

Research article, Hungary (grower questionnaire / citizen science data).

## What it holds

- Effect-size (meta-analytic style) analysis of how vineyard, technology and soil variables relate to long-term occurrence of Botrytis cinerea, Plasmopara viticola and Erysiphe necator, using grower questionnaire (citizen science) data.
- It finds low overall effect and says PV and EN depend mainly on climatic variables; no infection or risk equations are given in the lines read.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Grower-survey effect sizes from Hungary; weak and heterogeneous effects. Not a pattern Agrarium's truth can be matched to.
