# Mori et al. 2024: a diffusion model tuned on grape leaves makes apple black-rot images, and a classifier learns "generated"

Uses image-to-image generation with a Stable Diffusion-type model, tuned on grape leaf images, to make images of a disease class that is not in its data (apple black rot), then trains a ResNet18 on real healthy leaves plus these generated diseased ones. No record: a method; a short technical note with a 20-leaf test.

**Read this first.** The test is 10 healthy and 10 black-rotted real apple leaves (the paper's wording is loose, l. 283 to 284); only 10 generated images were good enough to keep, from a generator the authors call unstable (l. 280, 281). Image quality was judged by eye and the authors leave quantitative evaluation to future work (l. 330). All images are PlantVillage lab photos.

## What it holds

- **Generator** (l. 16, 134, 191 to 192): a latent diffusion model (Stable Diffusion, with image-to-image generation to keep the healthy leaf's features) tuned on healthy and diseased grape leaf images only, then asked for black-rotted apple leaves, a class never in its tuning data.
- **Numbers** (l. 281, 283 to 284): 10 generated black-rot images kept; the classifier is trained on real healthy apple leaves (10, per the reading agent) plus those 10 generated ones, and tested on 10 healthy and 10 black-rotted real apple leaves.
- **First result** (l. 284 to 285, 294): accuracy 0.463, "below 0.5", so no better than chance; recall for black rot 0.1000, 90 % of the real black-rotted leaves called healthy. The authors conclude the classifier learned to tell generated images from real ones and not to tell disease.
- **Fix and result** (l. 310): adding 10 generated healthy leaves to the healthy class, so that "generated" no longer predicts the class, gives mean accuracy 0.819 at epoch 10, standard error 0.006, over ten trainings. The error is over training runs on the same 20 test leaves, not over test leaves.
- **Reading** (l. 280, 330): generating many satisfactory images was difficult; quality was only judged by eye.
- **Without synthetic data:** none, since the black-rot class has no real training images by design.

## Dependence

- Stable Diffusion (via the Diffusers library), ResNet18, PlantVillage. Nothing else recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **The failure to guard against:** a classifier fitted on a synthetic class and a real class learns the difference between synthetic and real (here 90 % of real diseased leaves called healthy). For Agrarium's rendered lesions (D58, D60) the check is cheap: put generated images in every class, or test on real images of both classes, as the second run here does.
- **Scale:** a 20-leaf test and 10 generated images. It shows a failure mode, not an accuracy for a method.
- **Not shown:** a field image, a vine leaf, downy mildew, a larger or repeated test set, or a baseline trained on real black-rot leaves.
