# Caffi et al. n.d.: primary and secondary infection sub-models

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Italian two-page conceptual paper (pp. 17-18); no data and no fitted numbers.
- Oospores (sexual) overwinter and drive primary infections; asexual spores drive secondary cycles (l. 36-38).
- A life-cycle frame computes primary and secondary disease rates separately and links them (l. 39-41).
- Primary sub-model: Rossi et al. 2008, a mechanistic flow model driven by weather (l. 86-90).
- Secondary sub-model not yet built; secondary inoculum production and dispersal under study (l. 94-101).
- Validation against unnamed published European vineyard data is claimed (l. 47-49), not shown.
