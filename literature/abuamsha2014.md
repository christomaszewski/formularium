# Abuamsha et al. 2014: overwintering of grape powdery mildew in Palestine

A survey of 17 Palestinian vineyards for flag shoots and cleistothecia, and PCR biotyping of 397 field samples.

## What it holds

- Neither flag shoots nor cleistothecia were found in the 17 vineyards (abstract, l. 22–23; l. 141).
- All 397 samples from eight vineyards were of the ascospore biotype. None was of the flag-shoot biotype (l. 28–29; l. 248, 264).
- PCR worked on 85 % of the field samples (l. 200).

## Dependence

- Uses SCAR primers for the two biotypes that were already available (abstract, l. 24–25).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t3 (powdery mildew): with no flag-shoot biotype found, early primary inoculum cannot be set from this survey. It covers one season of sampling, 2012–2013 (l. 76), and has no weather or spray data.
