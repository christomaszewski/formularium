# Lareau et al. 2012: the Persistent Cold-Air Pool Study (PCAPS) in the Salt Lake valley

An overview of a wintertime field study of multiday cold-air pools in the Salt Lake valley, with its two-tiered observing design and instruments (l. 9-10, 83-97). No record.

## What it holds

- **Design:** continuously operating meteorological instruments through the whole campaign, and intensive observation periods declared when a pool was expected to last more than a day (l. 85-92).
- **Instruments:** a minisodar, a laser ceilometer at an NCAR facility, a lidar, special radiosondes and existing MesoWest surface stations (l. 113-146).
- **Setting:** the valley sits near 1,300 m, with the Wasatch (about 3,000 m) and Oquirrh (about 2,500 m) ranges around it (l. 46-50); the study is part of a 3-year National Science Foundation investigation (l. 71). No statistics were located.
