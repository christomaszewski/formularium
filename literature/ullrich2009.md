# Ullrich et al. 2009: black rot development in Riesling leaves

A histology study of the black rot fungus in Riesling leaves, from spore germination through growth under the cuticle to fruiting bodies.

## What it holds

- Riesling cuttings, four weeks old, inoculated with pycnidiospores or ascospores by spray or drop (l. 33, l. 74).
- Spores germinate and form melanised appressoria within 6 hours of attachment, citing Shaw et al. 1998 (l. 102).
- Subcuticular hyphae are present 2 days after inoculation (l. 248).
- Stains and microscopy (bright field, fluorescence, electron) were compared for the same tissues (Methods).

## Dependence

- None recorded. It cites earlier histology of the same fungus (Kuo and Hoch 1995, 1996; Shaw et al. 1998) as context.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (black rot). The timings here are qualitative: no rates or thresholds a simulator can take.
- The subcuticular growth stage is the one a leaf infection model would need to represent. Not read in full. The paper is in German with an English abstract.
