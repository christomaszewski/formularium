# Lukas et al. 2016: copper alternatives as stop-sprays

## What it holds

- Greenhouse trial on potted Edelvernatsch: of six copper alternatives sprayed after
  infection, potassium bicarbonate (Armicarb) and lime sulphur cut incidence by up to 46 %
  and 22 % and severity by up to 73 % and 77 %.
