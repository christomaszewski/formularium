# Rusconi et al. 2026: spray volume and dose from smartphone LAI on four farms

Four Lombard farms in 2024 sprayed by a LAI-based rule (PocketLAI, droplet density and
volume, Gil & Escolà's volume rule) against their own volumes: less water and fungicide, no
clear loss of control or yield.

## What it holds

- Trials in 2024 on four farms in Northern Italy: Almenno San Salvatore (farm 1), Cenate
  Sopra (farm 2), Rodengo Saiano (farm 3), Scanzorosciate (farm 4) (l. 382, 404-415).
- Method: LAI from a smartphone app (PocketLAI); volume from droplet density, droplet volume
  and LAI, corrected by efficiency; doses from a product database (l. 362-378). Dose rule:
  Gil & Escolà (2009), as in Paleari et al. (2024) (l. 362-363).
- Mean recommended-volume reduction 17.6 % (532.1 L/ha) (l. 108). Operational volume 9.8 %
  lower (300.0 L/ha) (l. 111). Fungicide dose 9.4 % lower (l. 115).
- Conventional farms: systemic -5.1 %, contact -10.5 % dose reduction (l. 118).
- Early season recommended-volume reduction up to -73.3 % (farm 1) (l. 130). Pruning: LAI
  0.68 to 0.28 (-59 %) and reduction -9.9 % to -16.5 % (l. 284).
- Disease (Townsend-Heuberger): no evident control loss, except farm 1; farm 3 had high
  pressure (l. 170-190).
- Yield: no clear difference (l. 195-196); no yield numbers printed.
- Must copper -8.6 % (range -19.0 to -3.7 %); must values 1.62 to 10.76 mg/L, below the 20
  mg/L EU limit (l. 198-201). Wine copper about -18 % (0 to 52.2 %) (l. 233).
- Stained-area drift index 39.8 % versus 63.4 % control, farm 2 (l. 258). Abstract prints
  37.3 % reduction; the printed values give 37.2 %.
- Fungicide mass saved 9.8 kg/ha per year (7.3 to 14.4) (l. 304).
- Data on request from the corresponding author (l. 501-502).

## Dependence

- Its own trials. The dose rule is Gil & Escolà 2009's, as in Paleari et al. 2024 (l.
  362-363). Cola and Confalonieri are authors of literature/cola2014 (the catalogue's
  cola2014.barbera_canopy); no engine author.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's grower and spray operator: dose scaled to canopy size, with early-season
  volume cuts up to 73 % and about 10 % over a season, and must copper down 8.6 %; disease
  scored but no infection model. Data on request only.
