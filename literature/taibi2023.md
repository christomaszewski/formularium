# Taibi et al. 2023: risk-timed resistance inducers

Research article, Colli Piacentini, Castell'Arquato, Emilia-Romagna, Italy (Res Uvae farm).

## What it holds

- A 3-year field trial of six plant resistance inducers applied only when weather-driven models advised, giving 41% fewer downy mildew interventions and 88% disease control efficacy.
- The models used (Caffi et al. primary, Brischetto et al. secondary downy mildew infection) are cited, not given with equations.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
