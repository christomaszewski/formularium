# Raynal et al. 2010: EPIcure GIS decision support for Bordeaux mildew risk

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- IFV's EPIcure runs the Potentiel Systeme models at almost 6000 points, replacing 50 stations for 120,000 ha (l. 41-44).
- Aroma (2 x 2 km) temperature replaces Safran (6 x 8 km); Antilope radar rain at about 1 km (l. 18-26).
- 24 May 2007: radar 106.4 mm (Figure 1) against 8 mm at the stations; the PSM gave 60 % downy mildew nearby and 2 % at 3 km (l. 57-65, 76-78).
- Almost 60 untreated plots did not confirm the storm-site estimate (l. 68).
- 2009 hail survey: 232 technician and 126 web observations; radar hail detection was about 75 % accurate for parcels (l. 132-133, 147, 262).
- Web platform: more than 100 observations, with no false positives (l. 272, 231-232).

## Dependence

- none checkable. The Potential System model (PSM, software by S. Strizyk) is named and its incidence outputs quoted (l. 64-65), but no equation, parameter or calibration is printed, and none of the listed engine models (Goidanich, Rossi, Magarey, Blaeser, Kennelly, 3-10, VitiMeteo, Leoni, Zachos, Puelles) is named. Shared authors: none on this slice; Strizyk is named only as the developer (l. 37).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Offers the truth a fine-scale observed hail and damage dataset and a reason to vary rain within a region (radar and station differ by a factor of 13 on one day); the PSM outputs quoted are not usable as data, and no listed engine model is present.
