# Bendel et al. 2020: hyperspectral and UAV imaging of Esca symptoms

A triage note (the 2026-10-12 library backlog). Another disease, an imaging method.

## What it holds

- Ground-based hyperspectral imaging (400-2500 nm, l. 113) and UAV multispectral imaging of
  Esca foliar symptoms at the JKI Geilweilerhof vineyards, Siebeldingen (l. 46, 506), in
  2016, 2017 and 2018 (l. 150, 158), with per-pixel and per-plant classifiers reaching
  accuracies up to 95 % (l. 121, 134) and a test of transfer between years (l. 181).
- No weather, no epidemic series, no model.

## Dependence

- none.

## Bearing (2026-10-12)

- Only as a measure of what imaging can see of a trunk disease; nothing for the downy
  mildew truth or the engine.
