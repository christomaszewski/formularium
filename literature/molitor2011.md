# Molitor et al. 2011: leaf removal timing against grey mould in Luxembourg

Manual leaf removal in the cluster zone, at six stages from pre-bloom to veraison, on four
white varieties in Luxembourg. Removal between flowering and berries-touching cut grey mould
most, and gave no yield loss.

## What it holds

- **Trial:** Sauvignon blanc and Auxerrois (2009), Pinot gris and Riesling (2010), at Remich
  (l. 94). Six removal stages, BBCH 57 to 81, against an untreated control; four replicates
  of eight vines per variant (l. 116).
- **Disease:** untreated control at harvest 18.6 % (Auxerrois) to 23.7 % (Riesling) (l. 216).
  Average efficiency 22.4 % (BBCH 57) to 55.0 % (BBCH 77) (l. 270-271). Removal from BBCH 63
  to 77 gave 47.2 to 55.0 % (l. 413-414).
- **Cluster structure:** percentage of interior clusters against severity at BBCH 79, R2 0.93
  (Sauvignon blanc) and 0.88 (Pinot gris) (l. 276).
- **Yield and sugar:** no significant difference in total yield in any variety (l. 291-292).
  The paper's advice: between flowering and berries-touching, about 50 % efficiency (l. 401-403).

## Dependence

None recorded. The paper uses the point quadrat method of Smart and Robinson (1991) and the
EPPO guideline PP1/17 for severity, as methods, not as borrowed models.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t4 (other grape diseases): a cultural grey mould control with no fungicide. A
  vineyard simulator could take the interior-cluster link to severity, and the flowering to
  berries-touching window, as a canopy rule for organic systems.
