# Si Ammour et al. 2020: qPCR counts of P. viticola oospores in leaves

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A real-time PCR (Giop probe, ITS1-5.8S rDNA; Valsesia et al. 2005) quantifies P. viticola oospores in senescent Merlot leaves (l. 202-207).
- Specificity: no amplification of non-target fungi, oomycetes, E. necator or Vitis DNA (Table 1, l. 257).
- Standard curves: efficiency 110% (sporangia DNA) and 121% (oospore DNA), R2 0.99; detection down to 10^-5 ng DNA (l. 265-300).
- Against microscope counts: log10 R2 0.70, slope 1.157 not different from 1, intercept not different from 0 (l. 297-301).
- qPCR separated 25% from 0% infested leaves; microscopy did not (l. 313-316; Table 3, l. 331).
- Microscope counts depended on sample weight (P = 0.002), qPCR did not (P = 0.094; l. 336).
- Not tested: leaf litter, field samples, living versus dead oospores (l. 411-439).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A molecular measurement of overwintering oospore density in leaves, which could serve the truth as an observation operator for the oospore pool once its field validation is in hand, but it measures total DNA and is not a field series.
