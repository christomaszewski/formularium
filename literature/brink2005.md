# Brink 2005: pigment coverage on bunch parts against spray volume, and Botrytis incidence against coverage

The thesis behind `brink2006`: a fluorescent-pigment and image-analysis method for spray
coverage on berry skin, rachis, pedicel and receptacle, then Dauphine bunches sprayed with
fenhexamid at six volumes, inoculated with dry conidia and scored for Botrytis, so that
incidence can be regressed on the coverage each part got.

## What it holds

- **Trial** (l. 1323-1350): Dauphine from Paarl and Worcester, sprayed at pea size and bunch
  closure with fenhexamid at 75 ml/100 L and pigment at 2 L/100 L; 1, 3, 6, 9 and 15 ml a
  bunch and unsprayed, five blocks; the volumes equal 111-1667 L/ha.
- **Inoculation** (l. 1376-1389, 1435): 3 mg of dry conidia settled 20 min in a 3 m tower,
  germination 86-96 %; 24 h at 22 °C and 93 % RH or more (the summary says 98 %, l. 88).
- **Coverage** (l. 1441-1456, 1160-1185): 0-1.89 % of the surface at pea size and 0-1.61 %
  at bunch closure across the volumes; coverage on volume is linear, berry skin at pea size
  with slope 0.32 and R² 64.2 %, bunch-closure rachis slope 0.09 and R² 44.6 %, the other
  parts between (Chapter 1 Table 1).
- **Incidence** (l. 1443, 1626-1658): at pea size incidence fell from 46.5-67.5 % unsprayed
  to 29.4-47.9 % sprayed; on coverage, pea-size rachis (Kerssies medium) incidence = 46.54 -
  8.40 × coverage %, R² 79.79 %; at bunch closure R² from 3.71 % (rachis, paraquat) to 80.92
  % (receptacle, Kerssies).
- **Not printed:** a "Brink et al. 2004" protocol (the thesis develops the protocol itself),
  the points behind each fit, what the standard-error column means.

## Dependence

- Observed throughout; the tower after Salinas et al. 1989, inoculum density after Coertze
  & Holz 1999, isolation media after Kerssies 1990 and Grindrat & Pezet 1994. No model in the
  data. Holz and Fourie again (`brink2006`, `coertze2002`): flags.
- **Confirms** the regressions `brink2006` quoted from its published paper (the same
  numbers at l. 1640-1657), which that note can now call read.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- For Agrarium's fungicide-protection strand (the 10-20 search): coverage of 1-2 % of the
  surface at normal volumes, and a linear fall of incidence with coverage on the rachis at
  pea size. No record: coverage is a measured quantity, not a model state; a protection
  model would take the slope (8.4 incidence points per coverage point) as a lead.
