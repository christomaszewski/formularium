# Zhu et al. 2021: a grapevine model in APSIM Next Generation

A whole-vine model for Sauvignon blanc in Marlborough: phenology by a chilling then
forcing sum to budburst and by Wang-Engel development days after, leaf and shoot growth
as beta functions of development days, radiation-use-efficiency biomass, and a yield and
fruit-composition module. Parameters are in Table 2.

## What it holds

- **Phenology** (l. 167-219, 245-251, 274): budburst by a negative-sigmoid chilling sum
  (Eq. 1) and then a forcing sum above Tb (Eq. 2), chosen over forcing-only and
  chilling-forcing-overlap as the best budburst timing (l. 173-180); flowering, fruit set
  and veraison by Wang-Engel development days (Eqs 3-4), fitted jointly for flowering and
  veraison. Endo-dormancy starts at a critical photoperiod of 13.1 h (l. 167, from
  Camargo-Alvarez et al. 2020) or the winter pruning date (l. 282).
- **Table 2 (pp. 11-12; l. 591-669):** chill c 12.42 and d -3.865; forcing Tb 0.008 °C
  (printed as is, not explained); Wang-Engel Tmin 3.9, Topt 29, Tmax 37.9 °C, Tref 25 °C;
  phyllochron 1.64 development days (Ds) for the first five leaves, 1.82 from the tenth
  (l. 295); leaf maximum area 12,000 mm², 14 Ds to full size, lag 40 Ds, senescence 5 Ds;
  SLA 20,000 to 15,000 mm²/g; RUE 0.8 before and 0.9 g/MJ after flowering; extinction 0.6;
  shoot structural dry matter peaks at 43 Ds after budburst (ends 79.2); NSC kmax 0.03 g/g
  (tm 34.4, te 54 Ds); berry maximum dry weight 0.6 g (tm 47.8, te 70 Ds from flowering);
  TA 7.8 to 30.3 g/L with rate 0.0923 per Ds.
- **Fit** (l. 487, 764-771): Sauvignon blanc, four Marlborough long-term sites. RMSE in
  calibration 4.1 (budburst), 3.1 (flowering), 6.8 d (veraison); in validation 4.9, 4.2,
  5.3 d; for Chardonnay, Merlot, Pinot gris and Pinot noir 6.5, 4.85, 6.5 d. Yield
  validation RMSE: 6.3 bunches a vine, 9.8 berries a bunch, 2.0 kg a vine (l. 785-786).
- **Data** (l. 465-546): four data sets, 2001-2020, listed in `zhu2021.marlborough`.
- **Not settled:** the forcing base 0.008 °C (l. 594); Eq. 10 gives TA of 38.1 g/L at
  t = 0 against 30.3 g/L in the text (l. 458, 516); StructuralDMmax has no printed value
  (l. 651); c and d are called unitless (l. 591).

## Dependence

- **Borrowed equations:** Eqs 1-2 follow Chuine 2000; Eqs 3-4 are Wang & Engel 1998; the
  row light interception is Goudriaan 1977 with Pronk et al. 2003; Eqs 6 and 8 follow
  Cieslak et al. 2011; Eq. 5 adapts Buwalda 1991 (l. 184-186, 245-251, 340-346, 364-366).
  Values: photoperiod from Camargo-Alvarez et al. 2020, phyllochron from Greer & Weston
  2010, Q10 from Poni et al. 2006, NSC maximum from Greven et al. 2016.
- **Fitted** to its own four data sets. No model is said to have produced them; the yield
  module reuses Zhu et al. 2020 for weather inputs. Shared authors in the data sets are
  not a dependence.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **The engine's form twice over:** a chilling sum then a forcing heat sum to a threshold
  for budburst (Chuine 2000), and growth as beta functions of development days (Wang &
  Engel's rate integrated: thermal time). A kin report lists it with the engine's
  degree-day pieces. Said so, not hidden.
- **Truth candidate:** no. It has printed parameters and an out-of-region, out-of-variety
  test, but it is kin to the engine by form.
- **Usable as published values:** the leaf module (maximum leaf 120 cm², 14 Ds to full
  size, phyllochron 1.6-1.8 Ds). It needs APSIM Next Generation to run as published.
