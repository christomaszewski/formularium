# Bleyer et al. 2020: VitiMeteo's leaf-area spray rule with phosphonates

Research article, Freiburg, Baden-Wuerttemberg, Germany (cv Mueller-Thurgau).

## What it holds

- Reports 2014-2016 field trials in Freiburg of folpet and copper hydroxide, with and without potassium phosphonate, sprayed at 400, 600 or 800 cm2 new leaf area per shoot.
- States the VitiMeteo-based rule that one spray protects until 300-400 cm2 of new leaf area has grown, and untreated incidence curves (Figure 1); fungicide-efficacy data, not a disease-process formulation.
