# Hug 2005: P. viticola genetic structure and epidemics in Australia

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- One unsprayed Ribier vineyard, Caversham (Swan Valley, WA), 12 Nov 2004 to 19 Jan 2005: 789 lesions collected, 709 genotyped at four microsatellite loci (l. 64-67).
- 32 distinct genotypes; the abstract's counts of lesions per genotype (17, 94, 4) do not add to 32 (l. 67, 71-73).
- Primary (oosporic) share 10 % at the first sampling, 5-7 % after day 14; secondary 95 % at day 68 (l. 1412-1420).
- Four to six primary lesions per sampling over 68 days, read as oospore release for up to three months (l. 1786-1788, 1955).
- Australian rules 10-10-24 (primary) and 98-13-4 (secondary) flag infection days (l. 1740-1744).
- Leaf wetness threshold of 50 degree-hours, cited to Siegfried et al. 1997 (l. 1773).
- Shannon diversity 0.28 (Caversham) to 0.84 (Yarra Glen); Fst 0.20-0.22 between east and west (l. 78, 82).
