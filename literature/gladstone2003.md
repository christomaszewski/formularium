# Gladstone & Dokoozlian 2003: leaf area density of six trellis systems in California

Leaf area, canopy surface and volume, and light inside the canopy for six trellis systems on
irrigated cordon-trained vines, with two steps of shoot removal. Read for leaf area per vine
and leaf area density by system.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 72-78): Chardonnay on 5C at Parlier (6 years, 1.8 × 2.8 m) and Cabernet
  Sauvignon on 3309 at Oakville (10 years, 1.8 × 2.4 m); drip at about 70 % of ET; measured
  within two weeks of berry softening. The year is not printed.
- **Table 1** (l. 165-183; canopy surface m²/m, volume m³/m, surface to volume, leaf area a
  vine m², mean leaf area density m²/m³): single curtain 4.5, 1.6, 2.8, 12.6, 4.4; VSP 3.0,
  0.6, 5.0, 11.4, 10.1; Smart-Dyson 4.0, 0.8, 5.0, 11.4, 7.9; Smart-Henry 3.7, 0.7, 5.3,
  11.5, 9.1; lyre 5.8, 1.3, 4.4, 11.7, 5.7; double curtain 6.8, 2.3, 2.9, 11.6, 2.8. Leaf
  area a vine did not differ (n.s.). The text swaps the VSP and DC densities once (l. 194).
- **Fruit zone:** density from near 6 (DC) to over 12 m²/m³ (VSP, lyre) (l. 186-188); gaps
  above 40-50 % in low-density canopies and below 20 % in high-density ones (Table 3, l. 392).
- **Criteria** (l. 347, 391): positioned canopies low below 5 and high above 7 m²/m³; leaf
  layers 1 to 1.5 for positioned, 2 to 4 for non-positioned canopies.
- **Cited** (l. 189-190): Mabrouk et al. 1997 about 5.0 m²/m³ (DC, lyre); Schultz 1995 3.7
  (VSP) and 2.0 (single curtain).
- **Not printed:** LAI, shoots a vine, canopy height and width (Figure 1 gives wires only).

## Dependence

- Canopy surface and volume after Smart et al. 1982; point quadrat after Smart 1988. Fits
  are the authors' own.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Irrigated Californian VSP, an upper comparator:** 11.4 m² a vine over 1.8 m of row
  (6.3 m² per m of row; LAI about 2.3 at 2.8 m rows, my calculation), packed at 10 m²/m³.
  For the viewer, a VSP wall of 0.6 m³ per m of row (for example 1.0 m tall by 0.6 m wide)
  holds about 6 m² of leaf per m at that density.
