# Linares 2009: shoot growth and leaf area of Syrah and Merlot under cover crops, El Socorro

A Madrid doctoral thesis on temporary cover crops against tillage and herbicide in
vineyards at El Socorro (Comunidad de Madrid): weekly shoot length on marked shoots, leaf
area from midrib lengths, and LAI and external leaf area at flowering, veraison and
harvest, Syrah in 2005 and Merlot in 2006 and 2007. Only the sections above were read;
369 PDF pages in all. The treatments are herbicide (Herb), bare tillage (Lab) and cover
crops (Cent3, CNA).

## What it holds

- **Thermal time Ite** (l. 6571, 9214): the effective thermal integral, daily mean
  temperature above 10 °C, summed from 1 April (the Ite (1-Abril) rows). The daily or
  monthly rule of the sum is not printed (l. 126).
- **2007 Ite from 1 April at the dated samplings** (Tabla 24, l. 9214, checked on rendered
  page 186): 10.1 at budburst (17 April), 114.2 (16 May), 354.4 (19 June), 749.4 (23 July),
  1056.9 at veraison (14 August) and 1426.6 (17 September). The first date column
  (29 November 2006) is blank. Accumulated Et on the same dates: 0.0, 103.7, 236.7, 434.7,
  562.1 and 711.4 mm (l. 9213; the Et method is not printed).
- **2008 Ite** (Tabla 25, l. 9257): six values for seven date headings, so the column
  alignment is unverified and no 2008 Ite is used.
- **Observed stage dates** (figure labels, l. 10131-10446): budburst 17 April 2007 and
  28 April 2008; veraison 14 August 2007 and 14 August 2008. No 2005 or 2006 dates were in
  the sections read.
- **Shoot growth method** (l. 6563-6571, 6592-6593): 4 plants a treatment and block, one
  marked shoot measured weekly; final length on 2 shoots a plant for Merlot. Velocity is
  shoot length divided by the Ite increase between dates, in cm per °Cd.
- **Tabla 48** (l. 12817-12844; length in cm at the second shoot removal, at flowering and
  final; velocity in cm per °Cd for budburst to second removal, second removal to
  flowering, flowering to growth stop; the table prints the unit as cm/Ite):
  - 2005 Syrah: Herb 67.4, 165.2, 203.2 cm and 0.32, 0.37, 0.08; Cent3 50.4, 111.0,
    123.1 cm and 0.24, 0.23, 0.02; CNA 59.2, 133.8, 155.3 cm and 0.28, 0.28, 0.04. No
    tillage row.
  - 2006 Merlot: Lab 62.8, 105.2, 140.0 cm and 0.30, 0.36, 0.15; Herb 73.8, 106.0,
    139.3 cm and 0.35, 0.27, 0.15; Cent3 50.4, 68.6, 82.0 cm and 0.24, 0.15, 0.06; CNA
    55.3, 86.0, 108.3 cm and 0.26, 0.27, 0.09.
  - 2007 Merlot: Lab 28.2, 102.8, 167.3 cm and 0.28, 0.33, 0.29; Herb 35.3, 122.5,
    212.7 cm and 0.36, 0.38, 0.41; Cent3 26.8, 92.3, 134.0 cm and 0.27, 0.28, 0.20; CNA
    30.5, 105.5, 164.2 cm and 0.31, 0.32, 0.26.
  - Herbicide alone had the highest velocity from budburst to flowering in all three years
    (l. 12791). Figure 28 plots length against Ite from 0 to 1000 with no fit printed.
- **Leaf area per leaf** (Ecuaciones 11-12, l. 6612-6614; cm² a leaf, L the midrib length in
  cm; linear fits to 50 leaves of different sizes, l. 6606): Syrah 21.41 L − 75.409; Merlot
  18.29 L − 58.452. These are the same lines as Sánchez-de-Miguel et al. 2011's Eqs 3-4 (Merlot
  18.291 L − 58.452 there, Syrah the same); that paper has Linares as a co-author.
- **Leaf area and LAI method** (l. 6602-6636): after Carbonneau 1976, from the midrib
  length. One leaf in three is measured and the sum is multiplied by three (l. 6618-6619).
  LAI is leaf area a vine over the planting frame (l. 6626). Measured at flowering,
  veraison and harvest, 4 plants a treatment and block.
- **External leaf area SA** (Ecuación 13, l. 6677): SA = (2 (H − h) + Am) × L / Dp, with H − h
  the canopy height, Am the mean width, L the row spacing and Dp the planting density. Its
  source is not stated. The lower face of the canopy is left out, after Smart et al. 1985
  (l. 6654).
- **Total LAI, m² m⁻², at flowering / veraison / harvest** (Tabla 50, l. 13126-13141):
  - 2005 Syrah: Herb 2.48, 2.66, 2.66 (principal 1.64, 1.80, 1.82; laterals 0.83, 0.86,
    0.84; laterals 33.7, 32.2, 31.5 %); Cent3 1.38, 1.65, 1.73 (laterals 0.33, 0.42,
    0.43; 24.0, 25.5, 24.7 %).
  - 2006 Merlot: Herb 1.42, 2.15, 2.44 (laterals 0.41, 0.74, 0.87; 29.2, 34.3, 35.8 %);
    Cent3 0.96, 1.51, 1.59 (laterals 0.16, 0.43, 0.43; 16.8, 28.1, 27.1 %).
  - 2007 Merlot: Herb 2.84, 4.00, 4.09; Cent3 1.18, 1.63, 1.63 (l. 13137-13138; from the
    reading's summary).
  - Herbicide LAI rose 0.69 from flowering to veraison, the cover crops 0.52 (l. 13078-13079).
    Cent3 had the lowest total leaf area in all three years (l. 13059).
- **SA, m² m⁻²** (Tabla 51, l. 13180-13191): at harvest, Herb 1.12 (2005), 1.05 (2006), 1.27
  (2007). In 2006 Herb at flowering, veraison, harvest 0.92, 0.94, 1.05; Cent3 0.70, 0.77,
  0.85. Cited thresholds: SA at least 0.9 (Carbonneau 1980) and 1 to 1.2 (Kliewer et al.
  1971; Smart et al. 1990b) for ripening (l. 13152-13154).
- **LAI/SA** (Tabla 52, l. 13230-13235): Herb at harvest 2.4 (2005) and 2.3 (2006); in 2006
  at flowering and veraison 1.6 and 2.3. Above 2 at ripening in every treatment (l. 13214),
  against the 1.5 Smart et al. 1991 propose as the limit (l. 13211).
- **Not settled:** the Tabla 52 block after 2006 is labelled 2006 again and is read as 2007
  by sequence; the significance footnote has a typo; Figure 28 reads "Shyra".
- **Not printed in the sections read:** bunches a vine, canopy height and width values,
  the Ite formula, the 2005 and 2006 stage dates.

## Dependence

- **Method:** the leaf-area method is a modification of Carbonneau 1976 (l. 6602-6603); the
  exclusion of the lower face follows Smart et al. 1985. Ecuaciones 11-12 are the author's
  own fits.
- **Shared with** `sanchezdemiguel2011.shoot_leaf_area`: the same Syrah and Merlot leaf
  lines, from the same Madrid group (the sample is the El Socorro vineyard). A shared
  group, and the same lines, not a model.
- **Fitting data:** none. No model shaped these data, and nothing is fitted to the Ite
  series; the velocities are ratios.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **Observed series free of engine fitting:** shoot length and LAI by stage for Syrah and

## Bearing (2026-10-20, vine leaf area)

Re-read by the main session for the vine-leaf-area search (l. 5366-5392, 5970-6010,
13110-13141):

- **Frames** (l. 5371, 5380): Syrah at 2.4 × 1.1 m (2.64 m² a vine), unilateral Royat
  cordon, 14 buds a vine; Merlot at 2.2 × 1.5 m (3.30 m²), bilateral Royat, 18 buds in
  2006 and 16 in 2007 (l. 5375-5386); shoots vertical on a trellis; both 0.8 m trunks.
- **Water** (l. 5972-6002): regulated deficit irrigation, started only once the main shoot
  stopped growing and midday leaf water potential reached -1 to -1.2 MPa; Ki 0.3 to 0.4 of
  ET0, 165 to 248 mm (Syrah 2005 from 30 May; Merlot 2006 and 2007 from 12 and 23 June).
  Canopy management was only shoot thinning at 15-20 cm (l. 6008): no trimming recorded.
- **CNA is a spontaneous (self-seeded) cover** (l. 99). Its LAI (Tabla 50, l. 13128,
  13134, 13140) at flowering / veraison / harvest: 1.58 / 1.78 / 1.70 (Syrah 2005), 1.08 /
  1.77 / 2.00 (Merlot 2006), 1.92 / 2.79 / 2.60 (Merlot 2007); laterals 20 to 45 %.
- **Per vine** (my calculation, LAI × frame): herbicide Syrah 2005 6.5 / 7.0 / 7.0 m²;
  CNA Syrah 4.2 / 4.7 / 4.5 m²; herbicide Merlot 2006 4.7 / 7.1 / 8.1 m² and 2007 9.4 /
  13.2 / 13.5 m²; CNA Merlot 2006 3.6 / 5.8 / 6.6 m² and 2007 6.3 / 9.2 / 8.6 m².
- **Timing:** the share of veraison LAI already present at flowering is 0.89 to 0.93 (Syrah
