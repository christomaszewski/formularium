# Schmidt et al. 2003: a weather-driven model of the grape berry moths

## What it holds

- A generalised Leslie (age-class) model of Lobesia botrana and Eupoecilia ambiguella,
  fitted to 7 years of pheromone-trap flights and 1992-1998 egg and larval time courses at
  one site in the Rhine-Main region: development driven by temperature, survival by
  temperature and RH.

## Dependence

- None: no model the tools run is computed, fitted or tested here.
