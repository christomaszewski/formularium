# Smart et al. 1990: the canopy ideotype

A review of canopy-management principles with the authors' trellis trials on Cabernet
franc at Rukuhia, New Zealand. Read for its target canopy values, which are cited widely.

## What it holds

- **The ideotype** (Table 4, l. 475-525; the text layer prints "-" where the page image has
  "~"): row N-S; canopy height to alley width about 1:1 (l. 479); exposed surface area SA
  about 21,000 m²/ha (l. 485); leaf area over SA under 1.5 (l. 488); about 15 shoots per m
  of row (l. 490); canopy width 300 to 400 mm (l. 493); shoots of 10 to 15 nodes, about 600
  to 900 mm (l. 496); leaf area to fruit about 10 cm²/g, 6 to 15 (l. 502); yield over SA 1
  to 1.5 kg/m² (l. 505); canopy gaps 20 to 40 % (l. 520); leaf layer number 1 to 1.5
  (l. 522). The values are the authors' judgement from cited work and their scorecard
  vineyards (l. 312).
- **Cane mass and leaf area** (l. 512, 516; cited): 50 to 100 cm² of leaf per g of winter
  cane mass; canes of 20 to 40 g; 0.3 to 0.6 kg of cane per m of row.
- **Trial** (l. 450-904): Cabernet franc, 3.6 m rows. Standard trellis SA 10,556 m²/ha
  (l. 552); RT2T 21,668 m²/ha (l. 560), 15 shoots/m and 111 shoots a vine (l. 752, 890),
  yield 21.5 against 8.9 kg a vine (l. 748). Cluster layer number 1.21 (l. 904).
- **Not printed:** leaf area per vine, LAI, any series in time.

## Dependence

- Judgement and cited work (Smart 1973-1988, Shaulis & Smart 1974 and others). No model.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **For the viewer and as a check:** a well-managed VSP wall is 0.3 to 0.4 m wide, about as
  tall as the alley is wide, with 1 to 1.5 leaf layers and 20 to 40 % gaps. At 15 shoots/m,
  Agrarium's 15 shoots on 1.2 m (12.5/m) is near the target. Out of region (New Zealand
  and cited sources).
