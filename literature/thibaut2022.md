# Thibaut et al. 2022: choosing boreholes or ERT for a 4D temperature field

Bayesian Evidential Learning scores combinations of temperature logs and time-lapse electrical resistivity data for monitoring design.

## What it holds

- **Method:** Monte Carlo training of a predictor-to-target link in a latent space; designs are ranked by the RMSE of the predicted target (l. 50-56).
- **Result:** the generated models agree with the true ones; a training set of only 200 suffices (l. 56-61).
- **Test:** a synthetic case built on a time-lapse ERT investigation; no field data in the first pages.

## Dependence

- Builds on Bayesian Evidential Learning (Hermans et al.) and time-lapse ERT (l. 85-89).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strands s5 and s6: a method for deciding which sensors or where, using a small training set; a simulator could take the idea for logger placement, not its numbers.
