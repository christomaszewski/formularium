# Bloesch et al. 2009: Chasselas stages at Changins, 1958 to 2008

Changins's own phenology series, 51 years, cépage Chasselas. Read for hunt test H2b, which
needed budburst there. A reading agent first cited it as "Spring, Viret & Bloesch"; the title
page gives the order above.

- **Printed budburst (BBCH 09), p. 146:** mean 23 April (day 113), standard deviation 7 days
  (Table 2). Early years: 7 April 1959, 9 April 1961, 10 April 2007. Late years: 8 May 1970,
  7 May 1979, 8 May 1986. 2008: 30 April.
- **Other means (Table 2):** BBCH 51 11 May (131), 6 days; BBCH 65 25 June (176), 8 days;
  BBCH 81 22 August (234), 11 days; BBCH 89 8 October (281), 9 days.
- **Trend:** over the 51 years budburst is about a day earlier than in the 1950s (p. 146). The
  abstract finds no earliness from BBCH 09 to 51, and about ten days from flowering on.
- **Read from a chart, not printed:** Fig. 4's squares give 2003-2006 as 23, 28, 25 and 25
  April, about ±2 days. The same measurement returns the printed 1959, 1970, 2007 and 2008.
  The other years of Fig. 4 were not measured.
- **Bearing:** a usable own-site budburst for Changins to 2008. Nothing found for 2009-2020:
  two Agroscope reports that may hold them refused the fetch (HTTP 403).
