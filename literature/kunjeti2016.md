# Kunjeti et al. 2016: spore-trap qPCR for lettuce downy mildew

A species-specific TaqMan qPCR assay for Bremia lactucae sporangia caught in spore traps,
  validated on diverse isolates and deployed in the Salinas Valley.

## What it holds

- **Assay:** target in mitochondrial DNA; amplicon sequencing of geographically diverse
  isolates from 14 Bremia species; one sporangium detected at Cq 32 (abstract, l. 24-30).
- **Calibration:** R2 of 0.86 between sporangial density from flow cytometry and qPCR values
  (l. 30-32).
- **Field:** samplers in the Salinas Valley in 2015, one beside a commercial lettuce field
  August to September (l. 228-231); the pathogen was detected during the 2-week lettuce-free
  period (l. 35).

## Dependence

- Follows Klosterman et al. 2014 for the trap and extraction method (l. 132, 230); not read.
