# Naud et al. 2010: testing a decision system for downy and powdery mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- GrapeMilDeWS, a decision workflow in Statecharts (named Mildium in French), for downy and powdery mildew, modelled from expert elicitation in 2007 (l. 15-20).
- Seven stages from bud-break to ripening; stage 3 (BBCH 61-65) is one combined spray against both diseases (l. 124-140).
- Plot surveys of 100 stocks (about 1000 in the area) and thresholds for downy (M) and powdery (O) mildew decide sprays (l. 150-152, Table 2).
- Bioclimatic indicators: PA (rain forecast) and ILM (expert risk, from models not named) (l. 202-208).
- Farm trials in 2008-2009: 39 cases (Fig. 1); a case with first treatment on 12 May 2009 failed (more than 15 % severity) (l. 277, 350).
- Downy sprays could be cut to 4 in some Bordeaux 2009 cases; difficult years needed 5 to 6 (l. 365-370).

## Dependence

- none named. The paper computes no listed model. Its decision rules (stages, survey thresholds, PA rain forecast, ILM expert risk) are GrapeMilDeWS's own; ILM rests on 'bioclimatic risk models' (l. 208) that are not named, so a dependence cannot be judged from this slice. The PA indicator (rain forecast) and the 2-week stage timing do not reproduce the 3-10 rule or any listed model. Shared authors, flags only: Cartolaro (cartolaro2010 and cartolaro2010b are in this batch) and Delière (also author of the Leroy et al. 2010 paper that follows, not read here).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A field-tested decision workflow with observed farm records for 2008-2009 (severities, spray counts, grower judgement); useful to an evaluator as a rule-based comparator, not as a disease model for the truth.
