# Henshall et al. 2005: wetness inside against outside a canopy

## What it holds

- Unpainted Campbell 237 sensors at 0.8 m beside the Pukekohe station and inside a
  Sauvignon blanc canopy, mid-December 2002 to March 2003, ten sensors each.
- Canopy wetness on outside wetness: y = 2.61 + 0.55x on non-rainy days, y = -1.49 + 0.94x
  on rainy days (R² 69.7 %).
- Bacchus is credited here to Agnew et al. 2004, a third attribution.

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Canopy wetness differs from outside wetness, unequally on dry and rainy days: a check
  for a truth's station-to-canopy wetness.
