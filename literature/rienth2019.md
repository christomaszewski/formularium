# Rienth et al. 2019: oregano oil vapour prevents downy mildew infection in vines

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Potted two-year-old Chasselas cuttings, 12 plants, inoculated at 10^5 sporangia/mL (l. 252-254).
- Continuous 10-day or 24 h fumigation with Origanum vulgare or Thymus vulgaris vapour in a climate chamber at 26/20 °C and 50% RH (l. 270, 298, 401).
- Vapour measured by GC-FID: O. vulgare 0.015-0.023%, T. vulgaris 0.06-0.1% (l. 385-386).
- 10-day fumigation cut severity by up to 98%, with phytotoxicity (l. 392-393).
- Abstract says a 24 h treatment cut development 95% (l. 53); the results give 95% for the 10-day arm (l. 419). The two conflict.
- Mode of action not resolved (l. 985).
