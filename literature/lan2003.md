# Lan & Scherm 2003: moisture sources and peach scab dispersal

Research article, Georgia, USA (University of Georgia Horticulture Farm, Athens).

## What it holds

- A four-year orchard experiment using rain shields and cotton wicks shows that waterborne splash and twig runoff, not airborne conidia or dew, drive peach scab infection of fruit.
- It gives treatment effects as percent reductions in scab severity but no equation or parameter table.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Splash and runoff, not dew or air, carried infection (rain shields cut severity most): supports Agrarium's splash-driven dispersal, for another host.
