# Zhang et al. 2018: Sentinel-2A against Landsat-8 reflectance and NDVI

Research article, Southern Africa, about 10° × 10° (MODIS tile h20v10), 2016.

## What it holds

- Regresses 65 million contemporaneous Sentinel-2A MSI and Landsat-8 OLI pixel pairs (TOA, surface, NBAR reflectance and NDVI) and gives OLS coefficients to make the two sensors consistent.
- MSI surface NDVI was greater than OLI surface NDVI.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Coefficients that make the two sensors consistent: needed only if a satellite operator merges them.
