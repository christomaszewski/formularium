# Dubuis et al. 2020: the Agrometeo station network and what each station measures

A slide deck, not a paper; the facts are one slide list. Related held note: dubuis2019 (the VitiMeteo/Agrometeo paper).

## What it holds

- **Network:** 31 stations at Agroscope and 147 at partners (cantons, groups, private) (l. 39-40). Station makes shown include Lufft and Campbell (l. 49).
- **Measurements:** one every 10 min; at least 7 parameters per station: air temperature at 2 m, +5 cm and -10 cm, RH, wetting duration in minutes, rain and radiation (l. 60-63, quote at l. 61).
- **Cost of a station:** purchase about 12,000 CHF; upkeep and transmission 636 CHF a year (l. 53-54).

## Dependence

- None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **Tier:** common, for a disease-warning network (every Agrometeo station has leaf wetness in minutes; sensor type and height not given in the slides). Supports "measured wetness exists on Swiss vineyard networks". Kind of result: a network description (slides).
