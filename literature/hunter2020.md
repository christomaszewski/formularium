# Hunter et al. 2020: row orientation and the vine

Shiraz in four row orientations (north-south, east-west, NE-SW, NW-SE) at one South African
site, measured on one day in each of two seasons. No record.

## What it holds

- **Site and design** (l. 143-286): ARC Robertson Experiment Farm, 159 m, flat, about 3 ha,
  clayey loam; Shiraz (SH 9C) on 101-14 Mgt, planted 2003, 1.8 × 2.7 m, vertical shoot
  positioning, canopy 1.2 m, cordon 0.7 m; four orientations × five replicates, blocks about
  800 m². Measurements on 4 March 2010 and 8 March 2011; canopy physiology and soil on one day
  and one replicate per treatment. Clear sky; air 30 to 35 °C, wind up to 4-6 m/s.
- **Sensors:** LI-191R line quantum sensors (up, down and sideways) at 08:00, 10:00, 13:00,
  16:00 and 18:00 h; LCA2/DL2 photosynthesis meter; Scholander chambers; ETI 2202 thermometer
  for soil. Weather from the farm station about 200 m away.
- **PAR, daily average (Table 1, µmol m-2 s-1):** top of canopy 284 (NS), 339 (EW), 415 (NE-SW),
  388 (NW-SE), with outside ambient 581, 430, 512 and 547; bottom of canopy 35 (EW), 55
  (NE-SW), 51 (NW-SE), 146 (NS).
- **Soil (Table 2, averages):** water content 9.42, 8.61, 8.59, 8.63 % and temperature 28.61,
  29.70, 28.11, 29.18 °C for NS, EW, NE-SW, NW-SE.
- **Leaf water potential:** EW -1199 kPa, NS -1273, NW-SE -1404, NE-SW -1476. Photosynthesis
  highest for NS and EW canopies, lower on the sides facing S, SE and SW.
- **Model:** a SketchUp sun and shade drawing of single vines with hourly shadows; qualitative,
  no equations or errors.
- **Disagreements:** the methods give an LSD at 5 % and the table notes say p ≤ 0.10. Tables 3
  and 4 have lost their headers in the text layer and were not used. Humidity recorded during
  photosynthesis was not reported; no leaf wetness.

## Dependence

- None. Same vineyard and cultivar as Hunter et al. 2016 and 2017 (cited for the weather);
  the data here are new. Shared authorship is a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

For sensor placement (which side of which row):

- **What it shows:** row orientation shifts the light reaching the canopy interior (top-of-canopy
  mean PAR 284 for north-south against 415 for NE-SW) and the sunlit sides; east-west rows have
  the warmest soil and the best leaf water potential in this trial.
- **Take for sensor placement:** a sensor's side of the row matters in an east-west row, where
  one face is in the sun all day; north-south rows share the sun between faces. Record the
  orientation with every sensor.
- **Limits:** LIGHT reading; one site in a dry, sunny climate; two single days; no humidity
  or wetness; an unclear significance rule.
