# Ferrer et al. 2008: Merlot on Lira and on vertical trellis in Uruguay

Yield components, shoot growth, leaf area and water potential of Merlot on SO4 in a
commercial vineyard in Canelones, Uruguay, 2001 to 2004: the Lira (divided canopy, 3,333
plants/ha) against the vertical trellis (3,200 plants/ha), with and without cluster
thinning. Spanish text, two columns; the figures hold most of the series.

## What it holds

- **Design** (l. 27-30, 172, 186): both systems on Royat cordon with 18 buds a vine on
  average; thinning of 50 % of the clusters at veraison, when 5 to 10 % of berries had
  turned (l. 186). Labels: LT and LR for Lira control and thinned, ET and ER for vertical
  (the table labels vary).
- **Yield components 2001** (Cuadro 2, l. 322-327): yield a vine 10.79 (LT), 6.88 (LR) and
  2.37 kg (ET); per hectare 35,957, 22,921 and 7,581 kg; clusters a vine 30.6, 17.2 and
  21.9; cluster weight 348, 444 and 197 g; berry weight about 1.7 g; pruning weight 1.01,
  0.98 and 1.20 kg. Lira yield exceeds vertical by 36.4 to 66.3 % in control vines (l. 300).
- **2003** (l. 332-334): yield a vine 5.95 (LT), 4.38 (LR), 3.94 (ET) and 1.54 kg (ER);
  clusters a vine 25.7, 11.8, 18.5 and 7.6.
- **Ravaz index** (l. 492, 499): 2001 10.95, 7.88 and 2.09; 2003 10.9, 7.41, 6.31 and 2.57.
- **Shoot growth** (l. 237-244, 469-482): 20 whole shoots a plot in 2003, measured every
  7 days from flowering to growth stop. Vertical shoots were 21.7 cm (22.3 %) longer and
  grew 18.48 days longer (l. 469-470). Fitted quadratics: vertical −15.31 + 2.811 t −
  0.0108 t²; Lira −57.6 + 1.707 t − 0.0071 t² (l. 481-482). The unit and origin of t are
  not printed, so it cannot be said whether t is days or a heat sum. Table 3 F values:
  architecture 20.7, thinning 0.37, days 61.18 (l. 459).
- **Leaf area** (l. 226-231, 355, 536, 549): total leaf area on 10 plants at veraison, no
  numbers printed; potential exposed leaf area SFEp (Carbonneau 1995) peaks at 7,629
  m²/ha for Lira at l. 355 and 7,317.9 at l. 536 (unsettled), and 5,240 for vertical. The
  per-hectare conversion is not explained; the series are in Figures 1 to 4. SFEp over
  pruning weight 1.54 (Lira control) against 1.28 (vertical) (l. 556); SFEp over yield at
  least 0.35 m²/kg (l. 673).
- **Water use and light** (l. 133, 155, 700): crop coefficient kc 0.61 (Lira) and 0.44
  (vertical), against 0.81 and 0.66 in Williams 2001; formula not printed. Lira intercepts
  60 to 70 % of light against 40 to 50 %, cited (l. 133).
- **Water potential** (l. 198-210, 669-687): predawn by pressure chamber from budbreak to
  harvest; Lira differs from veraison and is in moderate stress in three of four years.
  The values are in Figure 6 only.
- **Not printed:** calendar dates of the stages, leaf area a vine, the shoot series, the
  water potential values, the kc formula, the definition of t. The climate class code is
  garbled (l. 41, 162).

## Dependence

- **Computes cited formulas on its own data:** CPMS = 0.5 pruning weight + 0.2 yield, after
  Carbonneau 1996 (l. 177); RF after Carbonneau & Cargnello 2003 (l. 209); SFEp after
  Carbonneau 1995 (l. 230); the Ravaz index after Champagnol 1984 and Ollat 2002 (l. 187).
  The indices are ratios of observed values, not fits.
- **Compared, not fitted:** Ravaz classes of Ferrer et al. 1997; the 0.4 to 1.5 m²/kg range
  of Kliewer & Weaver 1971 and Murisier & Zufferey 1997; the stress limit of Zufferey &
  Murisier 2005.
- **Flag only:** Carbonneau is a co-author and the author of most of the cited formulas.
  The formulas computed here are the substantive link.
