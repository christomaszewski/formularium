# Gil et al. 2014: a review of canopy sensing for spraying in Spanish vineyards

A review by the UPC and Lleida groups of their LiDAR and ultrasonic work. Read for leaf
area; it restates the groups' papers.

## What it holds

- **Restated:** LAI = 1.2646 TAI and 0.1935 from LiDAR's tree area index (Eq. 2, l. 233;
  the operator is lost in the text layer, and Arnó et al.'s manuscript prints a minus),
  which is Arnó et al. 2013's Eq. 6; the manual canopy method of Llorens et al. 2011 (l. 259-262);
  a non-linear relation of tree row volume to leaf area density (l. 161). Each is `trail`
  here and `read` in its own note (arno2013, llorens2011).

## Dependence

- A review of the authors' own work.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Nothing new on leaf area.** Read the original papers instead.
