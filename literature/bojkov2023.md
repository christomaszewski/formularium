# Bojkov et al. 2023: a one-season infection regression, North Macedonia

## What it holds

- One untreated plot of Vranec at Smilica near Kavadarci, 2023: 90 vines, 30 leaves a date,
  six dates from 25 May to 29 June; severity by ImageJ.
- Linear and quadratic regressions of severity on temperature and wetness (R² 0.860 and
  0.706). The stated optimum, 26.8 °C, does not follow from the printed coefficients (a =
  -1.700, b = 77.400 give 22.76 °C); the '2 h 29 min' of wetness is a regression output read
  as a time.

## Dependence

- Cites Lalancette et al. 1988 and Yin et al. 1995; computes none of the engine's models.
