# Shmuleviz et al. 2026: sensor mapping of intra-vineyard variability in Valpolicella

Sentinel-2, UAV, canopy water stress index (CWSI), soil water content and NDVI are compared with agronomic checks in two pergola vineyards; no wetness, temperature or humidity is mapped by slope, aspect or row side.

## What it holds

- **Sites:** Pedemonte, 0.7 ha at 120 m, rows about 20 degrees east of north-south, monitored 2023-2024 (l. 76-78, 87-88); Nassar, 0.9 ha at 83 m, rows about 15 degrees west of north-south, four cultivar plots, monitored 2022 (l. 93-98, 101-102).
- **Method:** Sentinel-2 and UAV indices with ground CWSI, soil volumetric water content and NDVI, checked against vine and berry measurements (l. 35-40); UAV flights on 2 July 2022 (Nassar) and 24 June 2023 and 8 June 2024 (Pedemonte) (l. 170-172).
- **Result:** the Sentinel-2 and UAV patterns were highly similar and agreed with ground data; skin thickness correlated negatively with CWSI (l. 41-47).

## Dependence

- Uses Sentinel-2 and UAV imagery, NDVI, CWSI and ground sensors; no model is borrowed.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strand s4 context: canopy and soil status across parcels of overhead-pergola vines; a simulator could take the row orientations and parcel layout, but not any wetness or microclimate by position.
