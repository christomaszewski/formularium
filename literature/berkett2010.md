# Berkett et al. n.d.: mildews on cold-climate grape cultivars, 2009

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Vermont 2009: 16 cultivars (8 wine, 8 table) in one vineyard; five fungicide sprays (l. 109).
- Season unusually wet and cool; 57 rain days; 2384 growing degree days base 10 C over ten years (l. 38-39, 154-156).
- Powdery mildew on leaves highest on Prairie Star, 60.83% (l. 171).
- Downy mildew on leaves: Vignoles 93.33%, Corot Noir 79.17%; none on fruit (l. 179, 204).
- Eight sprays a year typical in the region (l. 199).
