# Ninyerola et al. 2005: a digital climate atlas of Iberia

Other, Iberian Peninsula (including Catalonia)..

## What it holds

- A book (UAB, first edition September 2005, ISBN printed as '932860-8-7') describing the method and maps of a digital climate atlas of Iberia, from multiple regression plus residual interpolation of station data at about 200 m resolution.
- It is climatology (mean monthly and annual maps, with applications), not leaf wetness or energy-balance dynamics.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Mean climate surfaces at about 200 m from regression and residual interpolation: a possible source for Agrarium's regional climate, if read.
