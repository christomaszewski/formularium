# McCoy et al. 2021: three over-the-row sprayers in Washington

Three commercial over-the-row sprayers (multi-fan, pneumatic air-shear and electrostatic) were compared in 2016 and 2017. Most deposit fell in the upper canopy, and drift stayed low.

## What it holds

- **Deposit:** most deposit in the upper canopy rather than the fruit zone, for all three sprayers (abstract l. 14-15).
- **Canopy capture:** 80.0 to 99.3% of the tracer collected was on the canopy (l. 609-610).
- **Drift:** ground drift 0.23 to 16.0% and aerial drift 0.01 to 7.3% of total spray collected. Ground drift was the larger (l. 611-613).
- **Sites:** site 1 in the Horse Heaven Hills AVA (l. 77-79); site 2 in the Columbia Valley AVA at Mattawa, WA (l. 288-290).

## Dependence

- ASABE droplet classes (2009) and 40 CFR 170.405 exclusion zones (l. 63, 72-74).
- Fox et al. 2008 and Pergher et al. 2013 on sprayer technology (l. 22-25).
