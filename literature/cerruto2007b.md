# Cerruto 2007b: how much less spray reaches the inner canopy of a Sicilian vineyard, by forward speed

An airblast field study with a dye tracer at two growth stages and three forward speeds: the deposit on the inner canopy against the outer, and the spread of normalised deposits.

**Read this first.** The CVs printed (60 to 73 %) are those of the normalised, power-transformed deposit, as the tables are titled; the CV of the raw deposit is not printed, so they are not leaf-to-leaf CVs of raw deposit. The deposit is a water-and-dye tracer in µL per cm², with the leaf side and area basis not stated. The vines are spur-cordon Nero d'Avola, not a VSP trellis.

## What it holds

- **Setting (l. 45):** spur-cordon Nero d'Avola in south Sicily, 2.50 x 1.25 m, tested at BBCH 77 (2002-06-12) and BBCH 81 (2002-07-11) with a towed axial-fan airblast sprayer.
- **Sampling (l. 539):** one spray pass per replicate, a water solution with a food dye tracer and a surfactant; 36 leaves per sample tree, equally over 3 heights and 2 depths (external and internal of the sprayed side).
- **Inner against outer canopy:** at BBCH 77 the mean deposit on the external layer, 0.403 µL/cm², is 1.51 times that on the internal layer, 0.266 µL/cm² (l. 306), an inner/outer ratio of 0.66 (arithmetic, not printed). At BBCH 81 (full foliage) the external layer had 0.386 and the internal 0.215 µL/cm², a ratio of 1.80 (l. 449), an inner/outer ratio of 0.56 (arithmetic, not printed).
- **Forward speed (l. 451):** the deposit in the inner canopy was reduced by 37 % at 0.9 m/s, 42 % at 1.4 m/s and 54 % at 2.8 m/s (BBCH 81).
- **CV of the normalised deposit (l. 287, 439):** at BBCH 77, 60 %, 60 % and 64 % at 0.9, 1.4 and 2.8 m/s (Table 3); at BBCH 81, 71 %, 71 % and 73 % (Table 5). Power-transformed data.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Deposit variability through a canopy (M4):** a measurement on vine leaves with an airblast sprayer: the inner canopy had 0.56 to 0.66 of the outer deposit, with the inner deficit growing from 37 % to 54 % as speed rose from 0.9 to 2.8 m/s at full foliage. The CVs of 60 to 73 % are of transformed data and cannot be set directly as a leaf CV.
- **Not shown:** an abaxial/adaxial split, fungicide mass, a VSP trellis, efficacy. Wash-off, decay, growth dilution and dose-efficacy: not addressed. Kind of result: field measurement with a dye tracer (spur cordon, Sicily).
