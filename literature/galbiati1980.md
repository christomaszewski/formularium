# Galbiati 1980: when oospores form in leaves

## What it holds

- Weekly samples of Barbera leaves in September-November (year not printed), stained with
  acid fuchsin: antheridia, oogonia and oospores fill the mesophyll between veins.
- Cites onset of oospore formation in June-July (Arens 1929, Germany), mid-July (France),
  5-20 August (Sicily), July (Greece), and densities above 1000 oospores per mm² (Laviola
  1964). The stated aim, a link with temperature, humidity and photoperiod, is not reported.
