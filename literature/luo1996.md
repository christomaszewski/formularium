# Luo 1996: dew onset and duration on rice from net radiative loss and vapour pressure deficit, with a glass dewball

A thesis. Dew forms when nocturnal net radiative loss passes a threshold that grows with vapour pressure deficit.

**Read this first.** Rice, not a vine canopy, one season. Equations were read in OCR text: spaces are lost, the exponent in Eq. 6.1 is garbled, and Eq. 4.1 (cosine law) lost its signs.

## What it holds

- **Site and observation:** IRRI, Los Banos, Philippines; 16 rain-free nights in 1994; wetness seen on top rice leaves and on a glass dewball at 1.0 m (l. 630). Inputs: net radiation, temperature and humidity at 1.5 m (VPD D, kPa).
- **Eq. 4.2:** total nocturnal threshold for dew on the dewball Rnt,thresh = -2.5 Dmin (MJ m-2; Dmin in kPa) (l. 1829). **Eq. 4.4:** instantaneous threshold -57.9 D W m-2; 2.5 MJ over a 12 h night is 57.9 W m-2 (l. 1904).
- **Eq. 4.5:** daily dew duration (h) = 14.57 - 1.1 t0, t0 hours since sunset of onset (l. 2015). Fit: r2 0.90, SE 0.51 h with observed onset; r2 0.76, SE 1.07 h with onset from the threshold (l. 2046).
- **Comparison:** Penman-Monteith for dew on top leaves gave daily duration r2 0.49, SE 2.24 h (l. 2081).
- **Eq. 6.1:** dew duration after sunrise from dew amount at sunrise; exponent garbled (l. 2860), probably 0.5 (JSON note), not confirmed in the image.

## Dependence

- Penman-Monteith and the single-layer dew models of Pedro and Gillespie 1982 and Goudriaan 1977 (cited, not read). Independent of the engine's RH/DPD threshold and logistic wetness pieces: no shared threshold or calibration data.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **What it supports:** a physically based wetness onset from net radiation and VPD (needs Rn, so it pairs with carrasco2008 or duarte2026 fills), with duration SE about 1 h per day on rice dew.
- **Not shown:** vines, rain wetness, a transfer of the -57.9 coefficient to any other canopy. Kind of result: a site- and season-fitted model with measurement.
