# Murolo et al. 2020: spatial pattern of Bois noir in Marche

Seven years of Bois noir in Chardonnay in Marche. Symptomatic vines cluster at short range and along vineyard borders, and about half of each year's symptomatic vines are new.

## What it holds

- **Pilot vineyard (MOV), 2009 to 2015:** 814 of 1,629 plants (50%) were symptomatic in 2011, and 9.2% in 2015 (l. 212; l. 245-246).
- **New cases:** around 50% of the symptomatic total each year were new (l. 246-247).
- **Never symptomatic:** 237 plants (14.5%) in MOV (l. 253).
- **Clustering:** aggregated at under 10 m in 2013. Significant clustering reached 10 m in 2013, 13 m in 2014 and more than 15 m in 2015 (abstract l. 20-21; l. 399-400).
- **Diagnosis:** nested PCR for 'Ca. P. solani' (l. 104-106).

## Dependence

- Point and surface pattern analysis (l. 79-80). Mark-correlation function (l. 185-187).
- Nested PCR of Maixner et al. (l. 105-106). Empirical severity scale (l. 102).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t6 (regional spread) and t4 (Bois noir):** short-range clustering and border effects are a check for a local-spread term. The paper measures symptoms, not vectors.
