# Lunel et al. 2024: the marinada wind in the eastern Ebro basin

A modelling paper on the marinada fall wind; only its introduction was read, for what it cites about the Barcelona sea breeze.

**Read this first.**
- Every number below is cited in the introduction from Redaño et al. 1991 (Barcelona sea breeze), not measured here. The marinada itself was not read.

## What it holds

- **Onset (l. 74, cited):** the sea breeze at Barcelona typically starts between 05:00 and 06:00 UTC, which the reading agent gives as 07:00 to 08:00 local standard time; the maximum speed comes near 12:00 to 13:00 UTC.
- **Speed (l. 77, cited):** the maximum speed is 8 to 12 m/s in 80 % of sea-breeze events. It is a peak, not a daily mean.
- **Direction (l. 79, cited):** the breeze starts from the southeast and turns to the southwest later in the day, by the Coriolis effect (as the reading agent summarised the sentence).

## Dependence

Builds on Redaño, Cruz & Lorente 1991, "Main features of the sea breeze in Barcelona" (reference list); not read.

## Bearing (2026-11-04)

- weather.wind_day_dir_deg and wind_night_dir_deg: background that the day wind at Barcelona is a sea breeze turning from southeast to southwest; no night value.
- weather.wind_mean_ms: the 8 to 12 m/s is an event peak, not usable as a mean. Kind of evidence: a cited secondary statement; the primary paper (Redaño 1991) is the lead.
