# Kim et al. 2006: forecasting site-specific leaf wetness

Research article, 15 weather stations in Iowa, Illinois and Nebraska, May-September 1998 and 1999 (SkyBit site-specific estimates)..

## What it holds

- It runs the existing CART/SLD/Wind and fuzzy-logic LWD models on 24 h SkyBit forecast and hindcast weather.
- It adds a corrected fuzzy-logic (CFL) model, which raises the FL output by a 2.5% increment found in training, and tests the LWD forecasts as inputs to the Melcast and TOM-CAST warning systems.
- It gives no full LWD equations; the models are cited from earlier papers.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- Shared authors, flags only (D27): Gleason, M. L., as an author of sentelhas2008.wetness.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Forecast-driven wetness was biased low for every model it ran (its error tables): the size of the error an engine meets when it runs on forecasts. Gleason, an author of the engine's sentelhas2008, is a flag; CART shares the engine's threshold form (kim2002, gleason1994).
