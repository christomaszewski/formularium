# Grünzel 1961: oospore formation in German isolates

## What it holds

- Naumburg, 1952-1958. In autumn 1952, more than 700 infected leaves held no oospores.
- Isolates from German sites formed oospores in few leaves: Geilweilerhof 12 of 312,
  Kaiserstuhl 8 of 222, Bad Kreuznach 8 of 286, Veitshöchheim 7 of 272, Oppenheim 3 of 268
  (printed as 1.6 %), Ockenheim 1 of 174, Geisenheim and Weinsberg none.
- Single-sporangium lines formed no oospores (he takes the fungus as heterothallic);
  oospores formed in older leaves and moderately resistant hosts; temperature, humidity and
  light had no clear effect on their formation.
- Cites 200-250 oospores per mm² (Arens; Müller-Thurgau) and oospores after 14 days at
  5.5-6.4 °C (Gessner 1932).
