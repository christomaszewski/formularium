# Billet et al. 2020: leaf metabolite biomarkers of field downy mildew

Malbec leaves in a Loire Valley vineyard, sampled 6 and 15 days after natural downy mildew infection and screened by semi-targeted UPLC-MS for infection biomarkers.

## What it holds

- 47 metabolites were measured in leaves (l. 26). Of these, 33 at 6 days and 27 at 15 days after infection are infection biomarkers (l. 32–33).
- Flavonoids fell in infected leaves. Stilbenoids DP1 and DP2 rose, and DP3 stilbenoids were found only in necrotic lesions at 15 days (l. 34–35).
- A PCA separated infected and control leaves clearly at 15 days, with slight overlap at 6 days. PC1 explained 46.3 % (l. 282–284).
- Two adjacent organic plots: one treated with copper, one left open to natural infection (l. 1112–1115).

## Dependence

- Extends a metabolite database built on grape stem extracts (ref 25, l. 111). For 32 analytes, identity was confirmed with pure standards (l. 114–115).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t2 (observation): leaf metabolite markers are a lab method, not a field sensor. They give a sensor search a target at 6 and 15 days after infection, but no simulator input.
