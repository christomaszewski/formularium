# Tai et al. 2023: TimeGAN-synthesised weather and thrips series for forecasting, a mixed result (an insect pest on mango, analogue)

Trains a time-series GAN on 59 weekly instances of weather and pest counts, then trains RNN, LSTM and GRU forecasters on the original and on the generated data and scores both on real data. No record: a method with measured results.

**Read this first.** The target is an insect (the thrips Scirtothrips dorsalis on mango), not a disease. The nearest thing in this strand to training on generated weather-and-pest series and scoring on real, and its two tables disagree: a slight gain in Table 3, a loss in Table 5. Many body paragraphs have each word doubled by a duplicated text layer, so Table 5's column headings are inferred from clean sentences (the error change is defined as the mean absolute error with augmentation minus without, l. 64). No intervals or repeated runs are printed.

## What it holds

- **Real data** (l. 13, 98): weather from Taiwan's agricultural weather observation network and pest survey counts from the Kaohsiung bureau of agriculture, 2019 to 2021 (as the reading agent records; the licence was not read). Hourly weather, 17,000 values per feature, was resampled to 105 weekly samples (l. 98).
- **Labels are observed** field survey counts (l. 13, 52); no process model.
- **Synthetic data, how made** (l. 3, 29): TimeGAN trained on "59 instances of raw data with 22 characteristics" (21 weather variables and the pest count); its output was checked by PCA and t-SNE overlap (l. 116).
- **Table 3, train on synthetic, test on real** (l. 129): model trained on real data R2 0.3358, MAE 0.1424, RMSE 0.2041 (normalised units); trained on generated data R2 0.3799, MAE 0.1418, RMSE 0.1970. A gain of 0.044 in R2 (arithmetic, not printed), with no repeated runs.
- **Table 5, forecasters with and without augmentation** (l. 204; test-data block, columns read from the clean sentences): GRU mean absolute error 77.67 without and 87.53 with augmentation (error change 9.86, about 13% worse, arithmetic); RNN 79.86 to 120.39 (change 40.53); LSTM 73.29 to 107.34 (change 34.05). The augmented models are worse on that block, which the abstract reads as "similar" (l. 3).
- **Authors' claim** (l. 3, 212): the generative model "effectively synthesizes multivariate agricultural sensing data and can be used to make up for the lack of actual data"; "data augmentation has not yet been applied to agricultural sensing data".
- **Validation design:** the base is 59 instances from three years; the split used for the forecasters is not stated in the parts read; no held-out season is named.
