# Pola et al. 2017: Goethe's budburst from winter monthly means in Santa Catarina

Seven seasons of a hybrid at Epagri's Urussanga station, humid subtropical with 234 chill
hours a year: budburst regressed on the June-August mean (no heat sum), and the lengths
of the next two phases on hours in a temperature band. A small, clean example of the
requested form, far from the project's climate.

## What it holds

- **Data** (`pola2017.urussanga`): Goethe on Paulsen 1103, planted 2004, at 28°31′S,
  49 m (l. 70-80); budburst at 50 % green tips, full bloom at 50 % open, harvest at 50 %
  picked, three plants each (l. 77-90); 2007-2011, 2013, 2014 (l. 75). Mean budburst 8
  September (30 August to 14 September), bloom 12 October, budburst-bloom 28-42 d,
  bloom-harvest 87-114 d (l. 105-107, 275-278, 363-364). Station 100 m away; daily mean
  (Tmax + Tmin + T9 + 2 T21)/5, hourly values by interpolation (l. 96, 117, 129-131).
- **Budburst on monthly means** (Quadro 2, p. 240; `pola2017.monthly_regression`): DB,
  in days from 1 August, = 82.65 − 2.98 T(July) (R² 0.62, Se 3.5 d), 116.10 − 5.24
  T(June-July) (0.72, 3.0), 157.34 − 7.87 T(June-August) (0.88, 2.0); June, August and
  July-August alone not significant. Quadro 1's lines on an accumulated mean from 11
  June (R² up to 0.86, eq. 3) have no units printed and a start chosen on the same data
  (l. 143-152, 182-186).
- **Phase lengths on hours in a band** (Quadro 3, p. 243): budburst-to-bloom = 51.2 −
  0.17 × hours at 19-25 °C in the first 14 days (R² 0.83, Se 2.1 d) or 46.17 − 0.4 ×
  hours at 22-25 °C (0.90, 0.9); bloom-to-harvest = 166.7 − 0.18 × hours at 19-25 °C in
  the first 30 days (0.64, 6.2 d), down to 220.4 − 0.14 × hours over 70 days (0.61).
  The phase mean temperature explains nothing of budburst-to-bloom (R² 0.05, l. 282);
  hours below 16 °C lengthen it (l. 23).
- **Not modelled:** the harvest date itself; no hold-out test; seven points a line.

## Dependence

- **Fitted to** the authors' own records; the hourly temperatures are interpolated (a
  rule, not a model). The daily mean is INMET's formula (Medeiros et al. 2012).
- **Form shared** with `fraga2016.monthly_regression`; the hour-count form has no kin in
  the catalogue.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- The requested form at its simplest: a winter mean against a budburst date, seven
  seasons, R² 0.88, with slopes (−3 to −8 d/°C) of the same size as Fraga's and Ruml's.
  The numbers belong to a hybrid in Brazil; the design is what transfers.
