# Cabrefiga 2025: an Empordà technical sheet on an adapted RIMpro that cut organic (copper) sprays by 17 to 29 % against reference strategies

A two-page IRTA sheet on a two-season trial (2023 and 2024) in one organic Garnatxa roja vineyard, reporting percentages of treatments saved and no spray counts.

**Read this first.** Grey literature: a technical sheet of an applied-research project, not a journal paper. Four caveats. (1) Percentages only: the number of sprays per arm and year is in Figure 3, which was not read, so no absolute spray count can be taken from it. (2) The vineyard is organic, so the treatments are copper sprays under a yearly copper cap, not a conventional programme. (3) The comparison base of the 52 % figure is not stated. (4) The year 2025 comes from the repository record; the sheet itself prints no year (it names the 2023 and 2024 seasons). The trial applied sprays as the models advised, so it measures what following a model saves, not how many growers follow one.

## What it holds

- **Trial:** a commercial organic vineyard of Garnatxa roja, "very susceptible"; four arms (untreated, the zone's reference strategy, standard RIMpro, adapted RIMpro); two seasons, 2023 and 2024 (l. 75).
- **Result of the efficacy trial:** the adapted model gave efficacy similar to the standard-model strategy with 29 % fewer treatments than the standard-model strategy and 17 % fewer than the zone's reference strategy (l. 102-104).
- **Summary figures:** the abstract says up to 30 % fewer sprays than the standard RIMpro strategy and 17 % fewer than the Goidanich-based strategy (l. 18-19). The two readings (29 and "up to 30") are the same result, rounded differently.
- **The zone's reference is a warning model:** the reference strategy is "based on the Goidanich model" (l. 19, 104). So the Catalan reference practice in this trial is a Goidanich-based warning, not a calendar.
- **Historical analysis:** modelling oospore maturation by estimated soil moisture gives a mean 52 % reduction in the number of treatments, varying by year and largest in years with little downy mildew; the comparison base is not stated (l. 77). Of three modulations tried (a fixed number of days after the last rain, estimated soil moisture, vapour pressure deficit), soil moisture fitted best (l. 126-130; two-column text interleaved).
- **Uptake:** the sheet says risk models such as vite.net and RIMpro are used systematically in Italy and France (l. 36), which implies they are not yet in Catalonia; the study is to give the base for commercial use in DO Empordà and DO Penedès, and it is not implemented yet (l. 19).

## Dependence

- RIMpro (Trapman, Wageningen) adjusted by the IRTA team with primary-inoculum (oospore) modelling; the reference arm is Goidanich-based. The reference arm rests on the Goidanich model; the soil-moisture oospore step is the adaptation. No shared author with a held note was checked.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Savings when a model is followed (grower.dss_saving):** 17 % (against the Goidanich-based zone strategy) to 29 % (against standard RIMpro) fewer copper sprays, in one organic Garnatxa roja vineyard in the Empordà, 2023 and 2024. Kind: field trial (measurement), two seasons, one site, advice followed by researchers, so an upper bound for real growers.
- **Compliance (grower.dss_compliance):** not shown. The sheet says the model is not yet in commercial use in Catalonia (l. 19, 36); there is no uptake share.
- **Not shown:** spray counts per season, intervals, delay from warning to spray, cost per pass.
