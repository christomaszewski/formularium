# Miele & Rizzon 2013: ripe rot and Cabernet-Sauvignon wine

Microvinifications with 0 to 20% ripe-rot berries. Rot raised must sugar, density and pH, and it lowered wine colour and absorbance at 520 nm.

## What it holds

- **Design:** six treatments of 0, 2.5, 5, 7.5, 10 and 20% rot berries, three replicates, 20 L wines (l. 25-28; l. 107-110). Grapes sampled in February 2004 (l. 99).
- **Must:** °Brix up 14.7%, density up 1.1% and pH up 4.1% (l. 165-166).
- **Wine:** colour intensity, anthocyanins and absorbance at 520 nm fell (l. 32-34; conclusion l. 37-38).
- **Tables:** Tables 1 to 4 and Figs 1 to 5 hold the regressions. The table values did not come through in the text layer.

## Dependence

- Analytical methods from the literature, for example Ribéreau-Gayon et al. 1982 (l. 169-170).
- Ripe-rot background from Samuelian et al. 2012 and Miele et al. 2005 (l. 71, 92).
