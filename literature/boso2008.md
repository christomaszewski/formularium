# Boso & Kassemeyer 2008: eight European cultivars ranked for downy mildew

A triage note (the 2026-10-12 library backlog; the file was dropped under a 2015 name,
the paper is Vitis 2008). Not a source for any record.

## What it holds

- Leaf-disc, detached-leaf and whole-plant inoculations of eight V. vinifera cultivars held
  susceptible (Albariño in three clones, Tempranillo, ..., Müller-Thurgau and Cabernet
  Sauvignon; l. 28-31) and resistant references, scored for sporulation and necrosis
  incidence and severity, with microscopy of infection and colonisation.
- A principal component analysis groups the cultivars into susceptibility classes (l. 46,
  155), Albariño the most susceptible and Cabernet Sauvignon among the least (l. 48).
- Fixed greenhouse and laboratory conditions; no weather, no field epidemic, no model.

## Dependence

- none: a susceptibility screen. Kassemeyer is a VitiMeteo author (a flag only, D27).

## Bearing (2026-10-12)

- A cultivar-susceptibility ranking a truth could use to scale infection by cultivar, as
