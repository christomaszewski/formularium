# Šebela et al. 2014: optical signs of infection in the field

## What it holds

- At Lednice (Czech Republic): Fv/Fm fell about 25 % in infected spots, chlorophyll a about
  50 % and carotenoids about 70 %; a stilbene glucoside rose with severity (up to 79 µg/g);
  reflectance indices SRI and CRI700 separated infection best.
