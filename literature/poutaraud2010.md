# Poutaraud et al. 2010: sporangia per leaf disc by leaf position on the shoot, in four unnamed genotypes

A conference poster with preliminary results: detached leaves of levels 3 to 9 from the shoot top, inoculated and counted at 7 days, which show the smallest leaves sporulating least in the susceptible genotypes.

**Read this first.**
- **A poster, preliminary, one experiment.** The authors call it "This preliminary study" (l. 154). There is no replication count, no statistics on the sporangia counts and no later full paper (the reading agent found none). Each count is the mean of 10 one-centimetre discs (l. 207, 215).
- **The genotypes have no names.** Four genotypes are labelled only by resistance class: very susceptible (OIV1), susceptible (OIV3), partially resistant (OIV5) and resistant (OIV7) (l. 53, 104, 138, 172). They cannot be tied to a cultivar. The place is not stated: the first two authors' unit is in Colmar (l. 34-35), and the plants were "cultivated in open field" (l. 197).
- **Leaf level is a position, not an age.** Level 1 is "the first developed leaf at the top of the stem" and only levels 3 to 9 were tested (l. 199). No day count, no leaf size or area (only dry leaf mass per area, in Fig. 4) and no lesion count, latent period or incubation is given. The two youngest leaves are not in the test.
- **Text and table disagree for the resistant genotype.** The text says counts "generally" rise from level 3 to 6 in the most resistant genotypes (l. 218-221). The OIV7 row (l. 206) reads 95, 45, 18, 69, 8, 7, 9: highest at level 3, no rise. The conclusion's rise from level 3-4 to 6-7 (l. 160) holds for OIV1 and OIV3, and for OIV5 only to level 6.
- **Detached leaves, one dose.** Leaves were cut, rinsed and floated on inoculum for 5 h; the dose is printed "104 sporangia/ml" in the text layer, a lost superscript (the page image shows 10 to the 4) (l. 201-204).

## What it holds

- **Design (l. 194-207):** four genotypes of varying resistance; two stems per plant; on the first, seven leaves from level 3 to 9 were harvested, rinsed and inoculated by 5 h flotation at 10^4 sporangia per ml; sporangia counted per foliar disc with a particle counter at 7 days post inoculation (dpi). On the second stem, levels 5 and 6 were used: level 6 inoculated, level 5 the control (l. 204-206); this stem served the metabolite comparison.
- **Very susceptible (OIV1), sporangia per disc at levels 3 to 9:** 21, 732, 1100, 1136, 1251, 633, 29 (l. 96). A rise of about 60 times from level 3 to 7 (arithmetic, not printed), then a fall at 9.
- **Susceptible (OIV3), levels 3 to 9:** 172, 95, 1602, 828, 1036, 733, 1353 (l. 131). Low at levels 3 and 4, then 1602 at level 5; no fall at level 9.
- **Partially resistant (OIV5), levels 3 to 9:** 43, 31, 222, 258, 19, 25, 15 (l. 167). Highest at levels 5 and 6.
- **Resistant (OIV7), levels 3 to 9:** 95, 45, 18, 69, 8, 7, 9 (l. 206; read from the text layer by the note writer, the page image agrees). It is not in the reading agent's facts.
- **The authors' summary (l. 160):** "there is an increase in sporulation from leaf level 3-4 to 6-7", the youngest leaves (levels 3-4, small in the pictures) sporulating least.
- **Metabolites:** flavonoid content fell linearly from level 3-4 to 9 and was equal in inoculated and control leaves; stilbenes were absent in controls and rose after inoculation, "data not shown" (l. 221-223). In the two susceptible genotypes, sporangia per disc against flavonoid and stilbene content showed negative linear relations from level 3 to 6 or 7 (l. 77-79; the fits are in Table 1, in the page image only, and are not quoted here).
- **Hypothesis (l. 169-172):** two kinds of ontogenic resistance, one in very young leaves (perhaps phenolic compounds) and one in older leaves after level 7 (perhaps tied to leaf mass per area, which rose linearly from level 3 to 9; l. 135-136).

## Dependence

- **Own experiment.** It cites Kennelly et al. 2005 and Smith, Wilson & Evans 2006 for the physiological stage of plants and leaves as a factor (l. 41-42, 219-221), and Goulas et al. 2004 for the Dualex leaf clip (l. 222). No equation is borrowed.
- **Authors:** it is from the Colmar unit (INRA, UMR 1131), as is Wiedemann-Merdinoglu, an author of `possamai2022`, which does not cite it. A shared unit is a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Decision 19 (susceptible from budburst, or from 10 cm or four unfolded leaves):** the small leaves are not immune. Level 3, the smallest leaves tested (small in the pictures), gave 21 and 172 sporangia per disc in the two susceptible genotypes, far below the 828 to 1602 at levels 5 to 7 but not zero (l. 96, 131). It is a detached-leaf test of positions 3 to 9, not of shoots at budburst or of leaves at one to two leaves out, so it does not reach the engine's trigger stage. Kind of result: a preliminary measurement on detached leaves, genotypes unnamed.
- **Leaf ontogenic resistance (the truth has no leaf cohorts):** it supports a direction at the shoot top (smallest leaves sporulate less) and in the OIV7 row (levels 7 to 9 sporulate least: 8, 7 and 9; l. 206), but no curve in days. A leaf-age rule in the truth cannot be fitted to it: the position-to-age conversion is not given, and the peak sits at levels 5 to 7 in the three genotypes other than OIV7.
- **host.inflorescence_after_budburst_days and host.rachis_only_factor:** nothing. It is leaves only.
