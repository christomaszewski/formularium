# Hu et al. 2024: DMFGAN, a depthwise-separable GAN that adds generated grape leaf images to a classifier's training set

Trains a GAN variant on 300 PlantVillage grape leaf images per class and adds 480 generated images per class to 240 originals to train ResNet50 and the authors' own M-AORANet. No record: a method, scored on the same lab-photo set.

**Read this first.** Both the real and the generated images are PlantVillage grape leaves (a lab photo set); no field image was scored. The test sets of the two arms differ: 120 images without augmentation and 360 with it, and by the counts the larger one holds generated images (inferred; the sentence on how test and validation images were kept apart from generated ones is garbled in the text layer, l. 1223 to 1225). The gain of 16 points is therefore not a clean paired comparison.

## What it holds

- **Generator** (l. 393, 394, 398): DMFGAN, a depthwise-separable multifeature DCGAN variant trained on PlantVillage grape leaves only: 1,200 images in four classes (healthy, black rot, esca, Isariopsis leaf spot), 300 per class.
- **Numbers of images** (l. 1211 to 1218): without augmentation 1,200 images, 960 train, 120 test and 120 validation. With augmentation 3,600 images, 2,880 train, 360 test and 360 validation; each class holds 240 original and 480 generated training images. (3,600 minus 1,200 is 2,400 generated images in all; arithmetic, not printed.)
- **Test** (l. 1212 to 1216, 1309): a split of the same PlantVillage set, not another set and not the field. The authors note that PlantVillage images "have higher transparency" and less light and shadow than the Plant Photo Bank of China (PPBC) images (l. 1309), and that generated quality is lower for small targets on complex backgrounds (l. 1321).
- **ResNet50 accuracy** (Table 9, l. 1236 to 1244): no augmentation 79.44 %; flip, pan and rotate 81.68 %; DCGAN 84.59 %; DMFGAN 95.82 % (+16.4 points on none and +14.1 on traditional augmentation; arithmetic, not printed).
- **M-AORANet accuracy** (l. 1237, 1249): none 76.67 %; DMFGAN 96.97 %.
- **Other plants** (l. 1288 to 1325): a generalisation test on four non-grape categories from the Plant Photo Bank of China (Camellia, Camellia oleifera, Golden flower tea, Wood lotus; 300 images each), with DCGAN and DMFGAN images added to 200 training images per class. The text read describes FID, SSIM and M-AORANet classification metrics for it but gives no number in the lines read; the generated images are shown in Figure 17.

## Dependence

- PlantVillage grape images only; comparators DCGAN, SAGAN, WGAN-GP and MMDGAN; ResNet50 and the authors' M-AORANet. Nothing in the text read says the classifier was ever run on images from outside PlantVillage.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **Only grape-disease classifier gain from generated images found in this strand,** and it is the weakest design: the same lab set on both sides, unequal test sets, a test set that seems to include generated images, one split, no repeat. Under D58 it is not evidence that augmentation helps a classifier in a vineyard; it shows the GAN pipeline runs and what a random split of a lab set can reward.
- **For Agrarium's rendered lesions:** a classifier fitted on generated lesions is judged here on lesions of the same kind of generated image; the lab-to-field gap (`mohanty2016`, `singh2020plantdoc`, `noyan2022`) is the thing not measured.
- **Not shown:** field images, a held-out set free of generated images, repeats or error bars, a real-only arm of equal training size.
