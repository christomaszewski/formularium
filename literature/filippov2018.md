# Filippov et al. 2018: potato late blight yield-loss simulator

A potato late blight simulator that links five-day weather windows to yield loss.

## What it holds

- Built on 30 seasons of field severity in the Moscow Region (l. 24–25). Yield losses ran from 1 % to 66 % (l. 181–182).
- Eqs (2) and (3) classify each five-day weather period as favourable to reinfection or not (l. 130–149).
- Checked on nine unprotected fields in the Netherlands and Germany. The mean difference was below 5 % (l. 32–33). Table 2 gives a mean deviation of 4.5 % (l. 199–201).

## Dependence

- Yield loss from AUDPC, Eq. (1), following earlier work (l. 82–86).
- Test data from the EuroBlight consortium (l. 145–149).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Off the point: potato late blight, not a vine disease. Only the five-day weather-window idea could be borrowed for t1.
