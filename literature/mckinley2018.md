# McKinley et al. 2018: ABC and history matching for stochastic epidemic models

A review of approximate Bayesian computation and other simulation-based inference, shown on a real-world HIV transmission model, with history matching as an alternative for high-dimensional, costly models (l. 15-23). No record.

## What it holds

- **Content:** ABC variants including ABC-MCMC (Marjoram et al. 2003) and ABC-SMC as Algorithm 1 (l. 99).
- **Case:** an HIV transmission model used to show the difficulties of ABC in high dimensions (l. 19-21).
- **Compare:** history matching with emulation is set against ABC (l. 21-23). No field data.

## Dependence

- Builds on ABC methods (Marjoram et al. 2003; Toni et al. 2009) and a real HIV transmission model.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s6 (methods):** how to fit or reject parameters of a costly stochastic simulator by emulation and history matching. A simulator could take history matching to rule out parameter space before any field fit. HIV case; no plants.
