# Workshop proceedings 2006: the 2006 workshop on grapevine downy and powdery mildew

## What it holds

- About 75 short papers in sessions on host-pathogen interaction, epidemiology and
  population biology, models and warnings, and control. The downy mildew epidemiology papers
  have notes of their own: rossi2006 (the dynamic primary-infection model),
  caffi2006validation (its validation in 77 vineyards), caffi2006water (leaf-litter water
  and oospore germination), kennelly2006 (the origin of the engine's trigger, berry
  susceptibility, survival), gadoury2006 (ontogenic resistance mapped), dallamarta2006
  (modelled leaf wetness for PLASMO), keil2006 (temperature, wetness and infection
  severity), burruano2006 (three decades in Sicily), prodorutti2006 and toffolatti2006
  (oospore germination series in Trentino and Veneto), and kast2006 (fifty years of
  Weinsberg records).
- **Population genetics:** Gobbin et al. (32 plots in five countries, 2000-2002, about
  10,000 oil spots: clonal spread under 20 m per cycle, populations 5 km apart distinct);
  Gessler et al. (primary lesions 30-40 % on average in Europe, a few % to 83 %); Hug et al.
  (Caversham, Western Australia: 636 lesions, 31 genotypes, about 12 % primary; Gessler's
  Table 2 gives 32 genotypes and 0.05); Koopman et al. (South Africa: 58-87 % of each date's
  genotypes new); Delmotte et al. (908 oil spots, Champagne and Bordeaux: low dispersal).
- **Warnings and models:** Raynal et al. (Epicure GIS, 40 stations and 50 untreated plots,
  Bordeaux, from 2000); Berkelmann-Löhnertz et al. (a splash algorithm and a
  soil-microclimate model for soil-borne primary infection, Rheingau; 2005 primary infection
  on 7 May, incubation about 14 days); Spera et al. (neural networks); Magarey et al.
  (CropWatch Online, South Australia, alerts at 75 % perceived risk); Jermini et al. (the
  minimal fungicide strategy, 1999-2002).
- **Not in the volume:** what Rossi's incubation regressions were fitted to; Goidanich's
  table is not mentioned.
