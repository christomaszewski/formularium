# Jiao et al. 2024: S-vine copula weather generator with hourly expansion, tested at Osaka

A generator of daily noon temperature, solar radiation and absolute humidity by an S-vine copula, then expansion to 24 h by change probabilities from observations (l. 16-22). No record.

## What it holds

- **Method:** copula series for 365 days at 12:00, then 24 h expansion from the probability of change of each element (l. 17-22); Richardson-type and ARMA generators are the comparison class (l. 50).
- **Test:** Osaka, observations 1981 to 2020 (l. 323) and the Osaka 2010 typical-year data (l. 415); 30 years of generated data compared with the original (l. 22).
- **Result:** temperature and radiation distributions match, with a minor deviation in the kurtosis of absolute humidity (l. 23-24); expansion MAE 1.96 C for temperature, 1.06 g/kg for absolute humidity, 51.21 W/m2 for radiation (l. 396).

## Dependence

- Builds on vine-copula methods, Richardson-type generators and typical meteorological year data.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s2 and s6:** a joint temperature, humidity and radiation generator whose hourly expansion error is printed. A simulator could take the copula idea for keeping co-variation of temperature and humidity; it has no rain or leaf wetness and is tied to one city.
