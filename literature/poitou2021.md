# Poitou et al. 2021: methyl salicylate as a wine marker of downy mildew, black rot and Esca

Methyl salicylate is highest in wines from grapes with downy mildew or black rot, and it is also found in wines from Esca vines, with a sensory threshold near 75 µg/L in model wine.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- Off the point: a chemical marker and sensory threshold for wine, with no model, rate or
  coefficient a vineyard or decision tool could use.
