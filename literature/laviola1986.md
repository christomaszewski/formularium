# Laviola et al. 1986: oospore germination against temperature, Sicily

## What it holds

- **Material:** Catarratto leaves collected in November in Palermo province (Partinico 1979,
  San Giuseppe Iato 1980, Balestrate 1982), overwintered there; germination tested at
  constant temperatures from 4 to 30 °C in 1980, 1981 and 1983, by the mean germination time
  Σ(n·d)/Σn.
- **1980:** none at 4, 7, 9, 12 or 28 °C; 9.7 % at 15 °C, 38.7 % at 20 °C; minimum time 12
  days at 15 °C and 5-7 days at 20-26 °C; mean time 19.5 days at 15 °C and 8.2 days at 20
  °C.
- **1981:** germination at 12-16 °C in small amounts, most (about 24 %) at 20-22 °C; minimum
  time from 18 days at 12 °C to 3 days at 20-26 °C; mean time 20.5 days at 12 °C, 5.7 days
  at 22 °C.
- **1983:** 39.8 % at 22 °C, 36.7 % at 24 °C, 18.8 % at 26 °C, 7.7 % at 28 °C; minimum time
  17 days at 10 °C, 3 days at 20-24 °C; mean time 32.2 days at 10 °C, 26.2 at 12 °C, 13.4 at
  14 °C, 5.7 at 24 °C. Germination was most in early March and less by 1 April.
- Range 10-28 °C (10 °C in one year of three, 12 °C in two), optimum 20-22 °C. At 10-12 °C,
  where the 3-10 rule would start, oospores took at least 13-17 days. The authors call the
  data indicative only.

## Dependence

- Their own experiments. Rossi et al. 2005 and 2008 cite them for the germination rate's
  dependence on temperature; eq. 5 gives 18 d at 10 °C against 17 d here (inferred fit).
