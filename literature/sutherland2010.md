# Sutherland et al. 2010: honeybees conditioned to detect powdery mildew infection

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Honeybees were conditioned with a sucrose reward to the headspace of powdery-mildew-infected grape leaves (l. 113-117).
- Conventional acquisition gave 75.9% positive responses at trial 4 and 89.7% at trial 6 (l. 198-199).
- A modified protocol, with uninfected-leaf headspace as background, gave at most 37.9% (l. 241).
- Conventionally trained bees gave 46.7 to 78.6% false positives to uninfected headspace; modified-protocol bees gave 0 to 20.0% (l. 243-245).
- Re-presented with infected leaves, 30.0 to 40.0% of modified-protocol bees responded (l. 250-251).
- The authors call it a preliminary feasibility step for early-season powdery mildew detection (l. 312-314).
