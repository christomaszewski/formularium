# Caffi et al. 2010: photosynthesis around downy mildew lesions

Not [caffi2010](caffi2010.md) (the warning system).

## What it holds

- Potted Barbera: oil spots 7-9 days after inoculation, sporulation 3-5 days later.
  Photosynthesis is unchanged until about 70 % of incubation, then falls; a zone about 2 cm
  around an oil spot also loses photosynthesis.
- Bastiaans's virtual lesions: (1 - y) = (1 - βα)^n, β = 9.0 (visible against virtual
  severity); P_x/P_0 = (1 - x)^β with β = 4.5 (relative assimilation). α and n are not
  printed.

## Dependence

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A damage term (photosynthesis lost beyond the visible lesion) for an evaluator.
