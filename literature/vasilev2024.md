# Vasilev & Tahir 2024: black rot on eight varieties near Burgas

Two seasons, 2022 and 2023, of black rot counts on eight varieties at Galabets (Pomorie, Bulgaria), under three spray schemes, ranking the varieties by foliar attack rate.

## What it holds

- Galabets, Pomorie municipality, 2022 and 2023. Eight varieties, four variants, four repetitions of 10 vines (l. 19-20; l. 150).
- Table 3 (l. 332), variant V1: attack from 10.3% (June Blanc) and 12.1% (Naslada) to 86.4% (Cabernet Sauvignon).
- The text gives Cabernet Sauvignon at 62.2% (l. 363) without naming the variant. That does not match the V1 value in Table 3 (86.4%, l. 332). Check before use.
- First leaf symptoms are said to follow 175 cumulative day-degrees, summed daily means between 6 and 24 °C from the day after infection (l. 72-73). The paper cites this from other authors and does not test it.

## Dependence

- The 175 day-degree model is cited from other authors (text refs 19 and 20) and not tested here. Susceptibility classes follow McKinney (1923) (l. 26-27).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (black rot). The 175 day-degree first-symptom model is a candidate prior for a leaf-symptom timer. The paper does not test it.
- The variety ranks come from one site over two seasons. The text and Table 3 disagree in places, so use the table numbers with care. Not read in full.
