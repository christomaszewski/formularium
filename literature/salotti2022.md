# Salotti et al. 2022: resistance of 16 varieties at Piacenza, unsprayed

A field trial, not a model.

## What it holds

- **Trial:** 16 varieties on the Piacenza campus, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2021, no fungicides;
  three plots of four plants each; Merlot as the susceptible control.
- **Assessment:** EPPO severity classes on 33 leaves and 33 bunches per variety and plot,
  every 7-14 days from BBCH 57 to 83; AUDPC by trapezoids.
- **Merlot, downy mildew AUDPC (leaves, bunches):** 2018 0.53 and 25.97; 2019 11.77 and
  20.34; 2021 14.89 and 1.84. In 2017 black rot, not downy mildew, was the disease scored.
- **Clusters (Ward):** downy mildew means 9.355 (Merlot alone), 1.298 (Rkatsiteli alone),
  0.303 (12 varieties) and 0.020.
- **Leaf against bunch:** r 0.890 (downy mildew), 0.786 (powdery), 0.986 (black rot).

## Dependence

- None. Rossi et al. 2008 and Brischetto et al. 2021 are cited as models that ignore
  resistance.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Observed variety-by-season severity under one weather record: data for a truth's host
  susceptibility, which the truth does not yet vary by variety.
