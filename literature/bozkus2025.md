# Bozkus & Bozkus 2025: grapevine powdery mildew, a review chapter

A review chapter on Erysiphe necator. It states spring infection conditions in words and gives no model or coefficients of its own.

## What it holds

- Spring infection conditions, as the chapter states them: 15 to 25 °C, at least 2 mm of rain, 40% relative humidity and at least 12 h of darkness (l. 188-189).
- A review of the pathogen, its effect on yield and quality, and management: fungicides, biocontrol, scouting and molecular detection (abstract; no numbers).
- No model, rate or data of its own in the text read.

## Dependence

- None recorded. It is a review and cites general mildew epidemiology work.
