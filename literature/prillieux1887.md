# Prillieux 1887: spread of Peronospora viticola by oospores, French note

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A note to the Société (25 February 1887) on how the vine downy mildew oospores reinfect vines in spring (l. 19-29).
- Millardet (1885) sowed Chasselas in April in pots with oospore-bearing leaves; downy mildew appeared on seedlings a month later (l. 33-42).
- Prillieux and Frechou saw oospores form germ tubes, but no fruiting bar one case (l. 63-70).
- Oospores often above 200 per mm2 of leaf (footnote l. 55).
- Vines with shoots on the ground were hit first and hardest (l. 88, l. 126).
- The author's working hypothesis: splashed soil carries oospores on to ground-trailing shoots (l. 75, l. 132).
- A copper-sulphate stake dip was judged ineffective by its inventor (l. 190).
