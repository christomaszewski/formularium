# Menezes et al. 2024: tree shade in a Douro vineyard, simulated

## What it holds

- A system-dynamics model (STELLA) of olive-tree shade on a vine near Pinhão: shadow
  geometry, sunshine with shade counted as 0.5, and shaded temperature T = ½(H/(A H0))² +
  Tmin (A 0.16 inland, 0.17 coastal), from IPMA and ERA-Interim data.
- Radiation falls 8-13 % under the trees simulated. No observed radiation or temperature
  is compared; only phenology dates are.

## Dependence

- None.
