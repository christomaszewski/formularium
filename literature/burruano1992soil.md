# Burruano et al. 1992: soil moisture and oospore maturation

## What it holds

- Catarratto leaves from near Palermo (8 November 1988, 7 November 1989) overwintered in
  soil held at fixed moisture, and naturally; germination tested at 20-22 °C after Laviola
  et al. 1986.
- 1988-89, natural: 13.5 % in January, 22.8 and 42 % in February, 5.7 and 1.2 % in March,
  0.5 % on 20 April. At 90 % soil moisture about 8 %; 70 %, 11.5 %; 50 %, 19.7 %; 30 %,
  about 1 %.
- 1989-90: low everywhere (at most 3.9 % natural), which the authors put down to a warm
  autumn and winter; varying moisture (20-10 %) gave the most, 14.7 %.
- Conclusion: varying moisture favours maturation more than constant moisture; the results
  are preliminary.
