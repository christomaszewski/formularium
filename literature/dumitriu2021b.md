## What it holds

- Segarcea wine farm, 40 ha, Archdiocese of Craiova, Dolj County, 2021 season (l. 6, 15).
- Five varieties: Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon, Sauvignon Blanc, Romanian Tămâioasă, Chardonnay (l. 16-17).
- Six fungicide sprays on the dates in l. 80, with products and doses in Table 1 (l. 62-77).

- Bunch DA% from 0.72 % (Merlot) to 2.94 % (Tămâioasă) (l. 120).
- Table 2 has no untreated control, so efficacy is not measured (l. 93).
