# Sagarna 2025: a workshop slide deck with a Spanish grower survey (58 % rely on official alerts) and the dates of the digital spray register

Slides from the SUPPORT project's vineyard-sector workshop of 24 June 2025, summarising a survey of Spanish vineyard growers and the coming digital phytosanitary register.

**Read this first.** Grey literature: a slide deck, not a paper; the charts are images and were not read. Four caveats. (1) The sample size and the regional split (the share of Catalan growers) are not printed in the text layer. (2) The author's own caveat: a low number of responses, so the picture may not be precise (l. 291). (3) The 58 % is stated reliance on official forecast and alert systems, not measured compliance (l. 206). (4) The month-by-month chart comes from a different survey, of cooperative technicians in 2022 (l. 34), not of growers.

## What it holds

- **Timing of treatments (technicians, 2022):** a chart from a 2022 survey of cooperative technicians (Spain, vineyards, all treatments) (l. 34); May is clearly the month of most intensive plant protection product use, and antifungal treatments can extend over the whole crop cycle, while insecticide and herbicide treatments are more occasional (l. 36).
- **Reliance on alerts (growers):** 58 % say they rely on official forecasting and alert systems; 64 % use periodic monitoring, the most used preventive measure (l. 206).
- **Information sources:** manufacturers 60 %, other farmers 44 %, private consultants 41 % (l. 271).
- **Caveat on the survey:** "a low number of responses; the picture may not be precise" (l. 291).
- **Digital register:** from 1 January 2026 a digital register of plant protection products is mandatory for all farms (EU Regulation 2023/564) (l. 399). The digital field notebook (CUE) is delayed, mandatory from the next CAP period (2028) for holdings above 30 ha or above 5 ha irrigated (l. 399); farms of 5 ha or less are exempt from the CUE (l. 395).
