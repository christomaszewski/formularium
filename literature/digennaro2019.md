# Di Gennaro et al. 2019: Sentinel-2 against UAV in overhead-trellis vineyards

Research article, Orsogna Winery vineyards (Costa di Moro and Magliano) near Chieti, Abruzzo, central Italy; cv. Montepulciano; 2015.

## What it holds

- Validates Sentinel-2 NDVI against filtered and unfiltered UAV NDVI and ground data in two tendone vineyards (R2 0.80 in V1 and 0.60 in V2 for S2 vs UAV;
- R2 0.60 to 0.95 against yield and biomass).
- Gives a measured satellite-vs-UAV mixed-pixel agreement for a tall overhead trellis; vigour, not disease.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Satellite NDVI agrees with UAV NDVI only so well on a tall trellis (R² 0.80 and 0.60): the mixed-pixel penalty for a satellite operator.
