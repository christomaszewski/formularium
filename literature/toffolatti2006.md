# Toffolatti et al. 2006: oospore germination and primary infections, Veneto

## What it holds

- Two vineyards (Montorio, Monselice), 2004-2005: leaves sampled in October; 1200 oospores a
  sample on 1 % water agar at 20 °C, tested twice a week from November to June.
- Germination capability first at the end of November; macrosporangia available until late
  May or early June; effective inoculum until 7 June (Montorio) and 27 May (Monselice).
  Final infection 38 % of leaves and 63 % of clusters at Montorio; 10 % and 7 % at
  Monselice.
- Germination driven mainly by water and endogenous factors; oospores take about a week to
  form macrosporangia.
