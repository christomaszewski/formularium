# Mijailovic et al. 2022: tagatose-based spray induces vine resistance to downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Laboratory study (Agronomy 12, 498, 2022) of the D-tagatose product IFP48 and pure D-tagatose on in vitro grapevine against Plasmopara viticola (l. 18-27, 149-150).
- Leaf treatment at 5 g/L was the most efficient; root treatment gave scarce protection (l. 26, 992).
- At 5 g/L on leaves, sporangia density fell by 100 per cent (IFP48) and 90 per cent (TAG) at 8 dpi (l. 664-665).
- Protocol: inoculum 1 x 10^5 sporangia per mL; 24 C, 65 per cent RH, 16/8 h light; overnight dark at 100 per cent RH, 25 C to sporulate (l. 200, 203-204).
- The abstract claims germination was inhibited; the methods do not measure germination (l. 27, 216-224).
- Authors: IFP48 under EU registration; a co-author works for the supplier (l. 151, 11).
- Authors suggest use from April and a weekly spray schedule, based on cucumber residual data (l. 1083-1085).
