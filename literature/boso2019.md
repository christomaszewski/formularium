# Boso et al. 2019: isolation and diversity of P. viticola in Galicia

Vineyards in a closed interior valley, open valleys and the coast were sampled; microsatellites
  show that strong isolation gives dominant clonal races and less diversity.

## What it holds

- **Design:** four plots (A to D) by valley type, SSR markers on primary and secondary
  infections, and airborne sporangia on sticky glass traps facing N, S, E and W, two supports
  per plot (l. 166-184); study year 2014 (l. 209).
- **Result:** strong isolation tied to dominant races and reduced diversity (abstract, l.
  16-19).
- **Sporangia:** highest in May in all plots; Plot D highest, then B, C, A (l. 274-277); totals
  per plot, Plot A 317, B 638, C 527 sporangia per 1.57 mm2 field (Table 3, l. 297-312).
- **Damage:** less downy mildew than normal in the study period (l. 279-280).

## Dependence

- Uses the SSR marker set of Gobbin et al. 2003 (l. 77-79); not read.
