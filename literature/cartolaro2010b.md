# Cartolaro et al. n.d.: early powdery mildew symptoms to time sprays

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- The study ran from 2003 to 2009 on commercial vineyards, testing decision rules that start sprays from early incidence (l. 42-51).
- The 2003-2004 rule used two systematic sprays at flowering and about 14 days later, and optional sprays at pre-flowering (I > 5% on leaves) or bunch closure (I > 10%) (l. 65, 73).
- In 2004 at Latresne, leaf incidence was 23.5% and bunch incidence 70.4% (l. 251).
- A four-spray programme gave 63% bunch incidence and 2.3% severity at harvest (l. 253).
- In 2008-2009, 41 of 59 national-network parcels (around 70%) got only the two systematic sprays (l. 371).
- The authors conclude that the rule cut treatments by 35-65% (l. 434).
- No weather model is used; the authors cite Messelink et al. 2000 for disease progress independent of wetness and temperature (l. 531).
