# Yang et al. 2026: GEE-DisALEXI, ET on Google Earth Engine

Research article, Contiguous United States (flux sites, HUC08 basins), OpenET context.

## What it holds

- Model description paper for DisALEXI (TSEB with ALEXI air-temperature closure) implemented on Google Earth Engine, with evaluation against flux towers across biomes and against basin water-balance ET (annual bias -0.36%; cropland monthly NMAE 16.8%).
- Includes ET-to-reference-ET anomaly versus US Drought Monitor.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Cropland monthly error about 17%: the size of error a satellite ET operator carries.
