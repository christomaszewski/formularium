# Alfieri et al. 2022: the wind profile above a California vineyard departs from the usual similarity law

A spectral study of sonic data above one vineyard in 2017; only the abstract was read.

**Read this first.** Abstract only. It prints no wind speeds and no ratios, and it is about the air above the vines, not inside the canopy.

## What it holds

- Data from sonic anemometers at 2.5, 3.75, 5 and 8 m above the surface, in 2017, as part of GRAPEX; turbulence at the different heights was related by spectral analysis (l. 44).
- The turbulent structure above 2.5 m differs from what is expected over other crops, because the biomass sits in the upper part of the canopy and the inter-row spacing is wide (l. 44).
- The mean wind profile deviates from the profile predicted by classical Monin-Obukhov similarity theory, which the abstract says causes errors in heat flux and so in modelled evapotranspiration (l. 44).

None recorded. Same project as `alfieri2016ams` and `alfieri2018roughness` (a flag, different year and instruments).

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Canopy wind (SWEB Eq 15):** a warning, not a value. Any scheme that scales a wind measured above a vineyard down to the canopy with a log-law factor rests on the profile this paper finds wanting. It gives no replacement ratio. SWEB's 0.3 U_Z, found by trial, cannot be checked against it.
- Kind of result: measurement, in an abstract; no number to take.
