# Szpyrka & Sadło 2009: fungicide residues on greenhouse tomato leaves

Residues of azoxystrobin, cyprodinil and fludioxonil on greenhouse tomato leaves in Poland fell by first-order kinetics. Half-lives averaged about 13, 9 and 31 days, and the authors advise respraying no earlier than 10 days.

## What it holds

- **Half-lives:** on average 13 days (azoxystrobin), 9 days (cyprodinil) and 31 days (fludioxonil) (abstract l. 27-28).
- **Respray advice:** not earlier than 10 days after the previous spray, under high pressure (abstract l. 28-30).
- **Table 6:** R0, r, R² and t1/2 and t1/10 by test. For example, azoxystrobin in test 1 had t1/2 of 13 days (l. 216-225).
- **Trials:** applications on 24 September 2002, 11 April 2003 and 26 May 2004 (l. 102-103).

## Dependence

- First-order rate equation (l. 106-107).
- Extraction from Sadło (1998), on Luke et al. 1975 with the Ambrus et al. 1981 change (l. 61-65).
- Cabras et al. 1997 gave 15.2 days on grapes (cited, l. 94-96).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (fungicide protection and respray interval):** the decay shape and half-lives are a persistence check for a spray interval. The crop is greenhouse tomato, and the compounds are not all vine products.
