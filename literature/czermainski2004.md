# Czermainski & Sônego 2004: fungicides on Tannat at Bento Gonçalves, 1995-96

## What it holds

- **Trial:** Tannat at Embrapa Uva e Vinho, Bento Gonçalves; four blocks of 12-plant plots;
  1995 and 1996; an untreated control not sprayed during the trial.
- **First symptoms:** 20 October 1995 (24 October elsewhere in the text) and 7 October
  1996.
- **Control:** leaf incidence 42.3 % (10 November) to 99 % (11 December 1995), and 2.75 %
  (11 October) to 100 % (20 November 1996); leaf index at harvest 80.7 % and 95.2 %.
- **Fungicides:** copper oxychloride failed on bunches in wet 1996 (index 90.6 %);
  cymoxanil + mancozeb held below 1.2 %.
- **Weather** (source not printed): daily temperature, rain, and nights with RH above
  95 % for 3-19 h before symptoms in 1996.
- **Rules applied:** Tran Manh Sung et al. 1990's oospore maturity and germination
  (above 11 °C with free water); a sporulation range of 5-25 °C, uncited.

## Dependence

- Applies Tran Manh Sung et al. 1990's criteria (`tranmanhsung1990.pom`, a candidate, not
  an engine model). RH above 95 % is used as a wetness condition, of the engine's
  `rh-threshold-wetness` form at another threshold.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Two seasons of untreated incidence by date in the Serra Gaúcha, with first symptoms in
  early October: a southern-hemisphere season-timing and severity pattern.
