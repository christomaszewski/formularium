# Gadoury, Seem & Wilcox 1997: the first printing of the engine's Kennelly trigger

## What it holds

- DMCAST, a research model, simplified for regional IPM from "fifteen years of weather,
  vine growth, and first occurrence of disease".
- **The rule:** first infections when vines reach 5-6 flat leaves, with flower clusters
  visible, and there is 0.10 in (2.5 mm) or more of rain with temperatures during the rain
  above 52 °F (11.1 °C).
- DMCAST's secondary warnings cut sprays; no numbers.

## Dependence

- This is the criterion Kennelly et al. 2007 credit to Gadoury et al. 1998 and 2000 and
  the engine runs as `kennelly2007.trigger`. The fifteen years are presumably the Geneva
  Chancellor data (`kennelly2006.chancellor`, inferred: the page names no site).
- The engine's form, 2.5 mm in a day at a daily mean of 11 °C, differs from "during the
  rain", and drops the growth stage.
