# Mahaffee & Stoll 2016: monitoring airborne pathogens for disease management

A review of how airborne inoculum is monitored and used in management decisions, and the limits of what inoculum data represent (l. 16-27). No record.

## What it holds

- **Argument:** molecular assays now allow detection of pathogen movement instead of assuming it, and management can target early epidemic phases (l. 20-24).
- **Limit:** there are limitations in what inoculum data represent that are often unrecognised, and knowledge of where and how to monitor is limited (l. 24-26).
- **Need:** better knowledge of dispersion mechanisms across scales, leaf to leaf and beyond (l. 26-27). No equations or field figures.
