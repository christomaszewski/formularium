# Everhart and Scherm 2015: brown rot clones within peach canopies

Peach brown rot in one Georgia orchard. Microsatellite genotypes of isolates from mapped
symptoms show clonal foci within each tree canopy.

## What it holds

- **Orchard:** six peach cultivars, Watkinsville, Georgia, 2009 to 2011. No fungicide was
  applied against brown rot (l. 84-92).
- **Isolates:** 65 to 173 per tree, typed at 13 microsatellite loci (l. 30-31).
- **Diversity:** average unbiased haploid diversity 0.529 and genotypic diversity 0.928
  (l. 17-18).
- **Clones:** 78.2 % of blossom blight isolates were unique genotypes, against 51.3 % of fruit
  rot isolates (l. 19-20). Fruit rot isolates fell in one to four dominant clones per tree
  (l. 22-23). Spatial autocorrelation was positive to 0.37 to 1.48 m (l. 25).

## Dependence

None recorded as a model or dataset. Markers come from earlier work (refs 7 and 18 in the
paper); the canopy maps come from an earlier study (refs 8 and 9).
