# Roda et al. 2024: less copper against downy mildew in Catalan vineyards

Field trials in 2019-2020 at Raimat, Falset and Gelida comparing standard copper oxychloride
with three low-copper products and an untreated control: copper applied, incidence,
severity, yield and soil copper.

## What it holds

- Catalan field trials, 2019 and 2020: Zone A at Raimat (Sauvignon blanc, 2019 and 2020),
  Zone B at Falset (Grenache, 2019), and Zone C at Gelida (Macabeo, 2020) (l. 26-29,
  127-129, 143). Four strategies: untreated (NTC), three low-copper products (T1 zinc-copper
  microelements; T2 Equisetum arvense; T3 copper complexed with heptagluconic acid), and
  standard copper oxychloride (T4) (Table 1).
- Copper per season, g per ha (Table 3): T4 1652.8 (Zone A 2019), 1177.9 (Zone B 2019),
  1260.0 (Zone A 2020), 2136.1 (Zone C 2020). Reductions against T4: T1 45 to 72 %, T2 48 to
  77 %, T3 35 to 56 % (l. 712; Table 3). The text's 0.3 kg per ha minimum conflicts with
  Table 3 (l. 800).
- Disease incidence, Zone A 2020 (BBCH 75): NTC 94.0 %, T4 58.5 %, T2 64.0 %. Zone A 2019:
  NTC 53.5 %, T1-T4 37.5 to 45.9 % (Table 4).
- Zone C 2020 (BBCH 57): NTC 70.8 %, T2 21.9 %, T4 13.3 % (Table 4). Severity in Zone A
  2020: NTC 11.2 %, T4 3.5 % (Table 4).
- Yield per vine (Table 6): Zone B 2019 untreated 2.74 kg, T4 2.43 kg; Zone A 2020 untreated
  3.70 kg, T1 1.83 kg, T4 2.71 kg.
- Soil Cu, Zone A 2019: untreated -6.97 %, T1 -10.04 %, T4 +19.36 % (Table 5, 2019 rows
  only).
- Abstract: copper inputs cut 63 % and 47 % (T2, T3 averages), up to 77 % (l. 21-31).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's grower and its Catalan sites (the real site is near Sant Sadurní, close to
  Gelida): standard copper programmes put 1.2-2.1 kg Cu/ha a season; low-copper products cut
  that by 35-77 % with control close to copper in the drier year and weaker under pressure
  (Raimat 2020 incidence 94 % untreated, 58.5 % copper, 64 % Equisetum). A measured efficacy
  range for the truth's spray response in Catalonia.
