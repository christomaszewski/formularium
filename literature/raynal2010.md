# Raynal et al. 2010: radar rain in Bordeaux's downy mildew model

## What it holds

- IFV's EPIcure serves about 120,000 ha from about 50 stations; the model named is
  "Potentiel Système" (2007), not EPI; no equation is printed.
- On 24 May 2007 radar gave 106.4 mm where the stations gave 8 mm.
- Against untreated-plot incidence in 2007: simulated = 0.4 x observed (r 0.6) from
  stations, 0.2 x observed (r 0.5) from radar (Antilope).

## Dependence

- None that can be checked: Potentiel Système is not printed. Strizyk (EPI's author) is an
  author.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Rain varies over a few km more than a station network sees: a reason for the truth's
  weather to vary within a region. No numbers to fit.
