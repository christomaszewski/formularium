# Austin et al. 2011: shaded clusters get more powdery mildew and less spray

Cluster powdery mildew severity against canopy density (shading layers and light at the
clusters) in Washington, New York and South Australia, 2008-2009, with sulphur rates and
spray deposit in New York.

## What it holds

- Three regions: Prosser WA (Chardonnay, 2008-09, biweekly sulphur at 0, 2.24 and 8.96
  kg/ha), Dresden and Geneva NY (2008, 2009), and Barossa and Riverland SA (2008-09,
  unsprayed) (l. 92-165).
- Canopy measured with EPQA; cluster exposure layer (CEL, shading layers) and cluster
  exposure flux availability (CEFA, proportion of above-canopy PPF) (l. 113-119).
- Unsprayed WA 2008: severity regressed on CEL, R2 0.58, slope 52.07 (p = 0.0006); 2009 R2
  0.42, slope 21.84 (p < 0.0001) (Table 3, l. 281, 293).
- With sulphur, the CEL link weakened: 2 kg/ha in 2009 R2 0.03 (p = 0.31); 9 kg/ha in 2008
  R2 0.32 (p = 0.023) (Table 3).
- Sulphur at 2 kg/ha cut severity about two-thirds (59.3% to 20.0% in 2008; 77.0% to 24.3%
  in 2009); at 9 kg/ha, about 87-91% (Table 4, l. 213-215, 292-296).
- Barossa: severity 9.3% where CEL <= 1.5 vs 53.1% above (Table 5); CEFA > 0.2 vs <= 0.2 at
  5.0% vs 37.4%.
- Riverland: training system not significant (p = 0.44) (l. 348-349).
- Geneva 2009: spray deposit nearly doubled per shading layer removed; about +50% per 10%
  light penetration (l. 383-397).
- Not printed: regression intercepts, n, deposit slopes, Figure values, and the data
  licence. The abstract overstates the 2009 sulphur result.

## Dependence

- Its own field measurements; canopy measured by EPQA (Meyers & Vanden Heuvel 2008) and PQA
  (Smart & Robinson 1991), methods. The UC Davis index and Sall 1980 are named as possible
  hosts for a light term, not used. Wilcox is an author of engine pieces: a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's canopy and spray operator: each shading layer over a cluster raises
  severity (WA unsprayed, slope 52.07 and 21.84 per layer) and lowers deposit (deposit
  nearly doubled per layer removed); a powdery mildew truth would need light at the cluster,
  which Agrarium's canopy state could supply. The abstract overstates the 2009 sulphur
  result (R² 0.03 at 2 kg/ha).
