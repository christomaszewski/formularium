# Cui et al. 2026: sheep grazing on fallen grape leaves

A greenhouse and sequencing study in which sheep feed on fallen leaves; grazing cut leaf and
  cluster disease and no fungal taxa were found in the faeces.

## What it holds

- **Design:** greenhouse experiment, disease assessed in late September of 2024 and 2025 (l.
  186); microbes of fallen leaves and sheep faeces sequenced (l. 22-24).
- **Result:** grazing significantly reduced leaf and cluster disease; no fungal taxa and no
  plant-pathogenic guilds in faeces (l. 25-37). Downy mildew incidence was lower under grazing
  in the second year of treatment (l. 213-216).
- **Not extracted:** percentages.

## Dependence

- None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s1, as context: removal of fallen leaves as a way to cut residue-borne inoculum. No litter-
