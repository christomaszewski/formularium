# Answer to leoni-dates-d70.md

2026-11-05, by an `ingest` session. The main session read the passages below in the thesis, the
paper and the two Dubuis papers. All 19 quotes were found at their lines by
`scripts/verify_quotes.py`. Thesis pp. 66, 96, 100 and 183, Dubuis 2012 p. 196 and Dubuis 2019's
Table 1 were also read in the page images. No new paper was needed.

## Short answer

**Infections dated back, not symptoms seen.** Leoni's "observed primary infection" is the day of
the infection judged to have caused the first oil spots at the outdoor laboratory, usually a
VitiMeteo-Plasmopara event. No sentence defines the column, so this is an inference, but it rests
on four years that match to the day and on a gap that holds in every year it can be measured:
7 to 16 days, median 10, over 17 years. So Agrarium's scores at Changins read about 10 days too
late, as the request feared. 2019 and 2020 do not fit and are set out below.

## 1. What the term means

Table 3.3 heads the column "Observed day" under "Primary infection" (thesis p. 183). Neither the
thesis nor the paper defines it. Where a source gives both dates, the table's day is the infection,
not the spot:

| Year | First oil spot | Infection named for it | Table 3.3 | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021 | 25 May | 16 May, "as indicated by the VitiMeteo-Plasmopara model" | 16 May | thesis p. 66 |
| 2023 | "around 11 May in LEX" | "predicted on 1 May" by VitiMeteo | 1 May | thesis p. 96 |
| 2024 | 16 May, at LEX | "predicted by VM-Plasmopara model on 6 May" | 6 May | thesis p. 100 |
| 2009 | 25 May | "vraisemblablement" (probably) the rain of 14-15 May | 15 May | Dubuis 2012 p. 196 |

- **The method, in the thesis's own words** (2024, the PIN and CHA plots, p. 100): "the first oil
  spots were observed on 11 June, implying a primary infection occurring around 25 May, according
  to VM-Plasmopara".
- **It is not always a model output.** In 2009 VitiMeteo computed no infection for that rain,
  because the 3 mm/h minimum was not reached (Dubuis 2012 p. 196). The group chose the rain itself.
- **It is not the model's first event.** Table 3.3's VitiMeteo column gives 9 May in 2023 and 28
  April in 2024, yet the text credits 1 May and 6 May to the same model. VitiMeteo computes several
  soil infections a season (thesis p. 60), so the day kept is, as I read it, the one that fits the
  spot.
- **Days between spot and infection:** the three thesis years give 9, 10 and 10. The incubation
  model behind them is VitiMeteo-Plasmopara's. No fixed number is stated.

## 2. Which plot

The outdoor laboratory (LEX): three rows of Pinot noir, Gamay and Chasselas with a cane of each vine
bent to the ground over oospore-rich leaves, never sprayed (paper p. 3; thesis p. 61). From May to
June, to detect the first symptoms, "all leaves from these vines were counted and assessed for downy
and powdery mildew on a weekly basis" (thesis p. 61, l. 3025-3026). The paper says "at the
experimental site" (p. 11). As I read it, a weekly round fixes a first spot to about a week, which
is part of the spread in the gaps below.

## 3. Whether the two Changins series are the same observations

**Viret's and Dubuis's are; Leoni's is a different quantity derived from them.**

- Viret 2014's Table 12 "témoin" and Dubuis 2019's Table 1 "field laboratory" print the same first
  oil spots in 10 of 11 years, 2003-2013. Only 2012 differs: 14 May against 10 May. The same group
  labels the series both ways: Dubuis 2012's Figure 4 calls it "le témoin non traité", while its
  text treats the control and the outdoor laboratory as two small areas. Which plot each date came
  from cannot be settled from these sources.
- Leoni's dates are earlier than these spots in all 14 years shared with Dubuis 2019:

| Year | Leoni | First spot, field laboratory | Gap (d) | VitiMeteo infection, Dubuis 2019 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2004 | 1 Jun | 11 Jun | 10 | 1 Jun |
| 2005 | 23 May | 6 Jun | 14 | 14 May |
| 2006 | 13 May | 22 May | 9 | 8 May |
| 2007 | 17 May | 29 May | 12 | 30 Apr |
| 2008 | 16 May | 26 May | 10 | 16 May |
| 2009 | 15 May | 25 May | 10 | 27 May |

The gaps are arithmetic on printed dates. Dubuis's dates are read from its Table 1 (day.month).

## What does not fit

- **2020:** Leoni's 7 June is 13 days *after* first oil spots on 25 May at Changins, on leaves under
  indicator vines, which Viret 2020 credits to infections of 11-14 May. It cannot be the infection
  behind those spots. Unresolved.
- **2019:** Leoni gives 20 June. Viret 2019 prints no oil spots in Changins's untreated controls
  before mid-August. No first spot for the laboratory that year is held.
- **2011:** the paper leaves the year out because "no symptom was observed at the experimental site"
  (p. 11), yet Dubuis 2019 and Viret 2014 both print first oil spots on 16 May 2011. Viret 2014 also
  has germination "non atteinte" that year, which may be the real reason.
- **Agroscope's own dates differ in three years:** 2021 is 31 May in Viret 2021 against the thesis's
  25 May; 2017 is 24 May in Viret 2017 against Dubuis's 23 May; 2018 is 7 June in Viret 2018 against
  Dubuis's 29 May.
- **2022 and 2025:** no first spot held.
