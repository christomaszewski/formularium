# Datasets for Agrarium D58: downy mildew outcomes against weather, outside Catalonia

Answer to `datasets-d58.md` (this folder), 2026-10-27, by an `ingest` session. Two strand agents
(claude-sonnet-5-5) audited Formularium's held datasets and searched repositories, services and
theses; every quote they cite (105) was found at its line by `scripts/verify_quotes.py`. No data
file was downloaded. Each row is a record in `src/formularium/datasets.py`.

**In short.** Nothing open pairs dated first symptoms with weather measured at the plots outside
Italy and Catalonia. One deposit can be downloaded now with daily on-site weather:
`taibi2023.castellarquato` (Emilia-Romagna, untreated plots, 4 stages a year 2020-2022, CC BY 4.0).
The best paired records are on request: Rioja (`puelles2024.rioja`, a station in each vineyard),
Nietvoorbij (`haasbroek2006.westerncape`, hourly stations 1-50 m from the blocks, ARC), 100
Italian vineyard-seasons (`caffi2009.italy100`, the Rossi group), Treviso (CREA), Mondeggi.
New regions found: Murfatlar, Romania (`dina2023.murfatlar`, two seasons, station in the plot);
Bento Gonçalves, Brazil (`reis2013.bentogoncalves`); Coimbatore, India. IFV's EPIcure network
(about 1200 untreated controls since 2002) is closed and has no weather.
