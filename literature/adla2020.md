# Adla et al. 2020: laboratory test of two low-cost capacitive and two very low-cost resistive soil moisture sensors

Laboratory work in repacked soils, against a ThetaProbe secondary standard.

## What it holds

- **Accuracy after soil-specific calibration:** SM100 MAE 1.67 %, RMSE 2.36 % VWC (l. 42); SMEC300 MAE 2.12 %, RMSE 2.88 % (l. 40-41, abstract). The resistive YL100 and YL69 were less accurate and precise (l. 48).
- **Prices (Table 1, l. 181-194):** SMEC300 USD 219, SM100 USD 89, YL100 USD 3.89, YL69 USD 2.11, ThetaProbe ML3 USD 516.33.
- Laboratory only; accuracy needs a soil-specific calibration.
