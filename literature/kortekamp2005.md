# Kortekamp 2005: stains for P. viticola structures in leaf semi-thin sections

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Semi-thin sections (1.0 um) of ethanol-fixed leaves from field-grown Riesling vines infected with P. viticola (l. 54-55).
- Toluidine blue O stains fungal cytoplasm blue and cell walls red (metachromasia), the choice for overview sections (l. 36-41).
- Stains-all separates plant and fungal walls: plant bluish-purple, fungal red to pink (l. 42-45).
- Haustorial head stains like fungal wall; the neck like plant wall (l. 49-50).
- Acridine orange with Calcofluor: hyphae white to pink, haustoria orange under UV (l. 52-54).
- Eosin B and Chlorazole Black E stain oogonia, antheridia and oospores (l. 106-108).
