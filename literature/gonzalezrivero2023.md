# González Rivero et al. 2023: low-cost calibration of temperature, RH and CO2 sensors

Simple laboratory set-ups to calibrate low-cost sensors, with a short field campaign in Santa
  Clara, Cuba; it is a calibration method, not a shield or field-error study.

## What it holds

- **Ranges:** temperature 0 to 50 C, RH 0 to 90 %, CO2 0 to 1100 ppm (l. 23-24).
- **Checks:** airtightness of the calibration box, similarity with factory calibration, with a
  reference, between sensors, replicability and a golden standard (l. 26-30).
- **Field:** a short campaign at three locations in Santa Clara (l. 469); no headline error
  figure extracted.

## Dependence

- Factory calibration and reference instruments as comparators.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s5 (sensor error): a recipe for calibrating cheap RH and temperature sensors in a box,
  relevant to how much sensor error a vineyard network starts with. No error numbers extracted,
  so nothing quantitative for a simulator. Not read in full.
