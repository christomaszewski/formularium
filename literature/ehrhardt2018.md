# Ehrhardt et al. 2018: 24 models for crop and grass yield and N2O

Twenty-four process models are run in five calibration stages against nine sites and ranked by relative RMSE, alone and as an ensemble.

## What it holds

- **Design:** 24 models, four temperate grassland and five arable rotation sites on four continents, stage 1 blind to stage 5 fully calibrated (l. 159-166, 325).
- **Blind skill:** 23 to 40 % of uncalibrated models were within two standard deviations of observed yields; 42 (rice) to 96 % (grassland) were within one for N2O (l. 170-176).
- **Calibration:** partial calibration cut the E-median ensemble error for grain yield from 36 % to 4 % and for grassland from 44 to 27 % (l. 181-186).
