# Gadoury et al. n.d.: prior growth, inoculum and light on conidia

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Powdery mildew (Erysiphe necator) on detached leaves, Geneva and Adelaide isolates: excising the colony centre at 4 DPI delayed sporulation to 9-10 DPI; at 5 DPI it did not (l. 129-133).
- Latent period fell from about 9 to 5 days as inoculum density rose; 25 to 250 conidia/mm2 gave only a slight further fall (l. 137-145).
- Light started sporulation: about 10% of colonies sporulated on day 5 under light, all by day 7; dark-held colonies sporulated one day after light (l. 147-158).
- Figure 2 power fit: latent period = 9.227 X^-0.081, R2 0.95 (l. 351).
- Authors suggest extant models may overstate epidemic rates if inoculations used high conidia numbers (l. 215-218).
