# Ishiguro et al. 1992: tricyclazole residue on rice leaves, decay and protection

A field and greenhouse study at Koriyama, Fukushima: how a protectant's residue on leaves
falls over days, how much residue still protects, and how little reaches leaves that
emerge after the spray. No record: rice and one active ingredient, but the forms and
magnitudes are the ones a vineyard truth's spray model needs.

## What it holds

- **Field, 1989:** cultivar Sasanishiki, tricyclazole at 300 g a.i./ha in 1,500 L/ha
  (l. 170, 174); plot A sprayed on 19 July, two days after the first lesions (l. 176),
  plot B on 28 July after second-generation lesions (l. 178); one plot per timing, two
  checks (l. 180-182).
- **Decay:** y = a e^(-kt), fitted by least squares on ln y, half-life ln 2/k (l. 370-379);
  k is not printed. On fully developed leaves, initial deposits 4.39-19.69 μg/g fresh leaf
  and half-lives 2.46-5.33 days (l. 36, 38; Table 1 in the page image).
- **Leaves emerging after the spray:** flag leaves not yet or partly emerged at spraying
  carried 0.16 and 1.08 μg/g at full development (abstract, l. 39-41), against 4-20 μg/g on
  leaves present at spraying. The results give 0.56 and 2.57 μg/g at first sampling
  (l. 304, 308); which figure belongs to which leaf is printed both ways (the Japanese
  summary reverses the English, l. 671).
- **Protection:** in the greenhouse complete protection above 0.34 μg/g and no significant
  effect below 0.20 μg/g (l. 48-50); EC50 0.24 μg/g from a line through two points (l. 52,
  389-391). In the field fully developed leaves were protected for about two weeks (l. 44);
  newly emerged leaves only 'somewhat' (l. 46). Plot A's lesions rose again about two weeks
  after spraying (l. 405-407).
- **Rain:** rain more than a day after spraying is called 'unlikely' to remove the residue
  (l. 448-450); not measured.

## Dependence

- None: its own residue and lesion data; spray dates set by observed lesions, not by a
  model. A cited rule (Kobayashi's) dated when blast conditions first became favourable
  (the reading's dependence note); it set nothing measured here.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's grower and Cooptera's E6 (treatment protection): a measured case of the
  three processes a spray model needs: first-order decay on leaves (half-life 2.5-5.3
  days), a residue threshold for protection, and leaves grown after the spray holding a
  tenth or less of the deposit. Rice and tricyclazole, so the numbers are magnitudes, not
  parameters for a grape fungicide.
- Residues are per gram of fresh leaf; leaf area was not measured (l. 462-463).
