# Avesani et al. 2023: linalool and grapevine defence vs downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Pinot noir leaf disks: linalool at 10 mg/L cut downy mildew severity with 74.5 ± 4.2% efficacy and no visible phytotoxicity (l. 272, 278).
- Concentrations of 25 and 50 mg/L showed phytotoxicity (l. 274).
- Callose deposition rose and defence genes of the salicylic and jasmonic acid pathways were upregulated (l. 39, 39).
- Metabolome changes at 1 and 6 days post inoculation (l. 42).
- Inoculum was 2.5 × 10^5 sporangia per mL on leaf disks, with sporulation scored as a percentage (l. 120, 159).
