# Hong et al. (Gyeonggi ARES, 2010 report): a rain-and-temperature spray rule for Kyoho

In Korean, with an English abstract. Author romanisations are the main session's.

## What it holds

- **Data:** a CR10 station (hourly temperature, RH, rain, dew time) for 2008-2010, and
  lesion-area ratio in an unsprayed Kyoho vineyard in 2009 and 2010. First symptoms on 22
  July 2009 and 3 August 2010 (the text says 7 days later; the dates give 12).
- **Rule:** first spray when the daily mean reaches 25 °C and daily rain 40 mm; then a spray
  whenever 20 mm or more is forecast, at least 10 days apart.
- **Correlations:** at 8 days' lag, a temperature variable r = -0.532 and rain r = 0.601; no
  n or test. Table 1 gives -0.532 for the daily temperature difference, the text for the
  mean temperature; the abstract calls them R².
- **Incubation:** about 8 days, the authors' reading of the lag.
- **Trial (2010):** 7 sprays; peak lesion area 29 % against 81 % unsprayed. No replicate
  statistics.

## Dependence

- No engine model is computed. The thresholds were read off 2008-2010 weather and the 2010
  trial uses the same year, so the trial does not test the rule.
- Jones et al. 1984 (dew time and mean temperature; apple scab) is cited as the approach.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- Context: a humid East Asian monsoon setting where first symptoms come in late July. No
  usable model.
