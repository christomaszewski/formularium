# Gan et al. 2023: mmWave radar leaf wetness detection

Poster, Indoor potted plant (Michigan State University authors); the dataset is 120 dry/wet pairs at several distances, and no vineyard or field data are shown in the lines read.

## What it holds

- A two-page poster on a millimetre-wave radar with a 2D scanning system and a VGG-16-based classifier that labels leaves wet or dry.
- It reports about 88% average and 93% highest accuracy on an indoor potted plant, with the lowest accuracy at 200 mm distance.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- About 88% accuracy on an indoor plant; not yet a field sensor.
