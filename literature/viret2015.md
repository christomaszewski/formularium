# Viret et al. 2015: Changins oospore maturity from 4 May 2015 and first oil spots on 11 May

A Swiss vintage review that gives the 2015 oospore maturity date at Changins and the first oil spots, on infected leaves laid on the ground, 7 days later.

**Read this first.**
- The method of the 4 May date is not stated ("œufs d'hiver", winter eggs).
- The 11 May first spots were on a stock of mildewed leaves laid on the ground the autumn before, not in the vineyard or an untreated control. The vineyard first spots in the next sentence are other places and later dates.

## What it holds

- **Season (l. 699 to 700):** mildew held back early by relatively dry conditions, and stronger at the end of summer.
- **Oospore maturity (l. 701 to 702):** the winter eggs reached maturity from 4 May at Changins, with the foliage already well developed, 4 to 5 leaves unfolded, BBCH 14-15 (l. 702).
- **First oil spots, Changins (l. 702 to 704):** at the end of incubation on 11 May, observed on a stock of mildewed leaves put on the ground the previous autumn.
- **First oil spots, vineyards (l. 704 to 706):** 12 May in Lavaux, 16 May in the Chablais and 20 May on La Côte.

## Dependence

- **Own observation** of the Changins group; method of the maturity date not stated, and the text does not say how the end of incubation was dated.
