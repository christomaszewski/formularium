# Reis et al. 2022: the Douro's vine stages and moth flights under climate scenarios

The generalized sigmoid model of Reis et al. 2020 run over a 1 km grid of the Douro for
Touriga Nacional and Touriga Franca, historic 1989-2015 and EURO-CORDEX futures, next to
a degree-day model of Lobesia botrana's three flights.

## What it holds

- **Phenology model:** the GSM of Reis et al. 2020 (ref 5) for budburst (BBCH 07), flowering
  (65) and veraison (81), calibrated on 51 varieties in Douro, Dão, Vinhos Verdes and
  Lisboa, 1990-2018 (l. 155-163). Only the critical forcing sums F are printed here: Touriga
  Nacional 46 (1 January to budburst), 36 (budburst to flowering), 65 (flowering to
  veraison); Touriga Franca 50, 39, 58 (l. 299-301). The sigmoid's own parameters are not
  printed (the 2020 note holds the generalized ones).
- **Moth model:** degree-days above 7.3 °C with a 33 °C ceiling from 1 January to each
  flight's start and peak (l. 169-174, 291-293); the flight sums are not printed.
- **Weather:** E-OBS (25 km) and a 1 km Portuguese grid for 1989-2015; four EURO-CORDEX
  chains at 11 km, RCP4.5 and RCP8.5, 2021-2050 and 2051-2080, bias-corrected (l. 233-282).
- **Results:** maps and means only (Figs 2-7); no yearly observed dates and no error
  statistics are printed.

## Dependence

- **Computes** Reis et al. 2020's GSM (`reis2020` note); shares the Portuguese stage
  observations that fitted it. Nothing engine-related.
