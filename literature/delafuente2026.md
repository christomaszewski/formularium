# de la Fuente et al. 2026: sprawl against VSP canopies, radiation interception and water use in Syrah

Two training systems (vertical shoot position, VSP1, and sprawl, S1), both at 12 shoots per m, are compared for PAR interception, gas exchange and leaf water potential.

## What it holds

- **Site and years:** a commercial vineyard in Castilla-La Mancha, 2005 to 2007 (l. 152-153).
- **Interception:** S1 intercepted +18% radiation before harvest (l. 341), with +19% average efficiency (l. 343); at 16:00 solar time +40% radiation and 35% higher efficiency (l. 369-370).
- **Canopy:** LAI similar between systems in all seasons (l. 314); exposed surface area differs.
- **Water:** under stress leaf water potential from -1.4 to -0.6 MPa; S1 gs 10 to 30% lower (l. 27-29).
- Interception measured with a LI-1000 logger (l. 236).

## Dependence

- Builds on PAR interception and LI-6400 gas exchange methods. None recorded as a model.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **s4 measured vineyard microclimate** / **s3 canopy physics:** gives measured interception efficiency by training system and year, a number a canopy-radiation step could be compared with. It is not about row orientation. Not read in full.
