# de la Fuente et al. 2026: VSP against sprawl in irrigated Syrah, Toledo, 2005-2007

Light interception and physiology of Syrah trained VSP or sprawling, same shoot density.
Read for LAI.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 152-204): commercial Syrah on 110R planted 2001, Toledo province, 488 m;
  rows 2.7 m, vines 1.2 m; spur-pruned bilateral cordon at 1.40 m; 12 shoots per m of row
  in both systems; VSP wires from 0.3 m to 1.5 m. Drip 231, 248 and 162 mm a year; rain
  199, 271, 274 mm; ET0 1,269, 1,211 and 1,065 mm (l. 159-165). The coordinates on l. 153
  do not fit Toledo.
- **Leaf area** (Figure 2 bar labels, page image; four shoots a vine by Carbonneau's method
  as modified by Sánchez-de-Miguel et al. 2011; at ripening, l. 218-221): LAI 2.19 (VSP)
  and 2.24 (sprawl), not different (l. 314); exposed area 1.06 and 1.30 m²/m² (l. 317).
  Pooled over the three years.
- **Interception:** efficiency 0.27 (VSP) against 0.33 (sprawl) (Table 2, l. 349-350);
  sprawl intercepted 18 % more radiation before harvest, with 19 % higher mean efficiency
  (l. 341, 343), and 40 % more at 16:00 solar time (l. 369-370); LI-1000 logger (l. 236).
- **Water** (l. 27-29): under stress leaf water potential -1.4 to -0.6 MPa; sprawl's
  stomatal conductance 10 to 30 % lower.
- **Not printed:** leaf area per vine, dates, laterals, measured canopy size.

## Dependence

- Leaf-area method of `sanchezdemiguel2011` (the same Madrid group; Linares and Lissarrague
  are authors of both): a shared method. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **s4 measured vineyard microclimate** / **s3 canopy physics:** gives measured interception efficiency by training system and year, a number a canopy-radiation step could be compared with. It is not about row orientation. Not read in full.

## Bearing (2026-10-20, vine leaf area)

- **Irrigated Spanish VSP at Agrarium's shoot density:** LAI 2.2 over 3.24 m² is about 7.1 m²
  a vine and 0.49 m² a shoot at 14.4 shoots a vine (my calculation). An upper bound for
  Agrarium's unirrigated vines.
