# Juroszek 2015: a review of disease models under climate scenarios

## What it holds

- Reviews 70 studies since 2000 that drive disease risk models with climate projections,
  grapevine downy mildew among them; about 40 % project risk unchanged or lower; most cover
  Brazil and western Europe.
