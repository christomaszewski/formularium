# Carisse et al. 2010: airborne powdery mildew conidia and spray thresholds in Québec

## What it holds

- A Richards degree-day model (base 6 °C from growth stage 7) for the season's share of
  airborne conidia, fitted 2000-2002 and validated 2003-2004; it cut sprays 40-55 %.
- A Weibull link from cumulative conidia to incidence (R² 0.95-0.97), validated on 2007;
  an action threshold of 50 conidia/m³ a day, tested in 2008.
- No coefficients printed. Conidia, not ascospores: no relation to the engine's Thiessen
  2018.

## Dependence

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- For Cooptera's powdery mildew and M2's spore traps: thresholds on airborne inoculum.
