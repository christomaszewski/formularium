# Lebeda & Schwinn 1994: a 1994 overview of the downy mildews

## What it holds

- Fungicide market of 4.7 billion US$, about 1 billion spent on downy mildews (grapes,
  vegetables, tobacco, hops). Oospores of P. viticola matured in the laboratory by
  alternating 10 and -5 °C weekly, germinating 6-10 days after at least 5 months
  (Ronzon-Tran Manh Sung & Clerjeau 1988); rainfall after formation affects maturity and
  next season's severity (Tran Manh Sung et al. 1990's model).
