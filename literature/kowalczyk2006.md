# Kowalczyk et al. 2006: CABLE, the CSIRO land surface model

Report, Offline sites: Tharandt (Germany), Tumbarumba (Australia), an Amazon Basin site, FLUXNET sites; coupled global runs.

## What it holds

- Full description of the CABLE land surface scheme: aerodynamic resistances, a two-leaf (sunlit/shaded) canopy with coupled stomatal conductance, photosynthesis and energy partitioning, a multilayer soil/snow model, plus offline and coupled (C-CAM) example simulations.
- Includes soil surface energy balance and fluxes, a Penman-Monteith form for canopy latent heat, and in-canopy air temperature and humidity from the Raupach plant turbulence model.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- A two-leaf canopy with in-canopy temperature and humidity, but no printed wet-canopy equation (read 2026-10-07): little for leaf wetness.
