# Blackall et al. 2020: a passive sampler for yellow rust spores

A power-free spore sampler designed with CFD and Lagrangian particle tracking and checked in a
  wind tunnel.

## What it holds

- **Method:** Lagrangian particle tracking inside CFD over a range of spore Stokes numbers,
  with wind-tunnel validation and weathervaning shown (l. 28-32).
- **Result:** the sampler raises the probability of yellow rust spores reaching an internal
  sensor by a factor of 20 to 25 (l. 32-34).
- **Context:** regional yellow rust losses of up to 5 % common, 25 % in rare cases (l. 21-22).
  No field test in the abstract.

## Dependence

- CFD with Lagrangian particle tracking; spore Stokes-number framing (record).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s6 (methods) and s3: a worked CFD-plus-particle-tracking design for catch efficiency; the 20
  to 25 factor is for one geometry and a lab wind. A simulator could take the Stokes-number
  approach for spore capture on a trap or a leaf. Not read in full.
