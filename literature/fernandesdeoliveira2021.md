# Fernandes de Oliveira et al. 2021: tillage against cover crops and downy and powdery mildew

A three-season trial in a Sardinian vineyard comparing tillage with grass and legume cover
  crops, relating mildew development to weather, leaf area, leaf nitrogen and canopy density.

## What it holds

- **Design:** soil treatments from November 2012 to December 2015 (l. 111); tillage, grass and
  legume covers; inoculation of downy and powdery mildew was also done artificially (l. 450).
- **Result:** higher infection percentages under tillage; weather is the main factor for downy
  mildew, and high canopy light lowered spread under grass cover (abstract, l. 24-29).
- **Leaf-area regressions:** primary shoot LA, MAE 123.84, MA%E 5.7, R2 0.904; lateral shoot
  LA, MAE 137.25, MA%E 8.95, R2 0.986 (Table 1, l. 326-348; the text layer of this table is
  garbled).
- **Not checked:** disease severity numbers; no weather-model fit read.
