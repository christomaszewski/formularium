- Fungicide market in 1996 about 7.2 billion SFr, downy mildews about 1.2 billion, grapevine
  54 % of that (Syngenta data); 4 to 8 sprays on grapevine under heavy pressure.

  Siegfried et al. 1992), Germany (the Freiburg model, Kassemeyer 1996), Italy (PLASMO,
  Orlandini et al. 1993), France (MILVIT, Muckensturm 1995); no equations.
