# Basso et al. 2020: real-time spore counters in Swiss vineyards

## What it holds

- **Sites:** five stations about 400 m apart on 50 ha at Dardagny (Geneva), from 2018; two
  at Pully (Vaud) from 2019, one in an untreated Chardonnay plot and one in treated Gamay
  60 m away.
- **Instruments:** an optical particle counter (16-20 µm class for P. viticola), with
  7-day impaction filters identified by microscopy and machine learning; each station also
  logs temperature, RH, leaf wetness and radiation.
- **Results:** daily counts change a hundredfold within days; in October 2018 one station
  saw one episode in 15 days and another three. Counts follow leaf-wetness duration and
  partly rain days, and VitiMeteo-Plasmopara's sporulation less well. At Pully, spores and
  symptoms lag VitiMeteo-Oidium's risk by about ten days.
- **No statistics:** no regression, coefficient, R² or n is printed.

## Dependence

- VitiMeteo-Plasmopara and VitiMeteo-Oidium outputs are used as comparisons; their
  equations are not printed. Dubuis, an author, is VitiMeteo's: a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Sporangia vary sharply between stations 400 m apart: a pattern for a truth's spatial
  sporangia cloud. No numbers to fit.
