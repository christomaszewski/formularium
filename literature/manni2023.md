# Manni et al. 2023: staged validation of decomposition, transposition and PV models at Trondheim

A five-stage validation workflow for a solar model chain, with satellite, reanalysis and ground inputs compared with the NTNU-Solarnet network at Trondheim (l. 16-23). No record.

## What it holds

- **Data:** two periods, 24 August to 18 December 2021 and 4 July to 11 November 2022, at one minute to one hour resolution (l. 366-368); NMBE, CV(RMSE) and R2 are the measures (l. 281-282).
- **Result:** decomposition and transposition models with measured inputs are the most accurate but do not meet the validation criteria (l. 23-25); the PV energy models with on-site inputs are validated (l. 25-26).
- **Finding:** monitored irradiation beats satellite and reanalysis inputs at high latitude, and the shortest model chain is preferred with equal data (l. 26-29). Error figures were not located in the first pages.

## Dependence

- Builds on satellite and reanalysis irradiance products and published decomposition and transposition models.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s5 (sensor error) and s3:** a case that errors accumulate along a model chain, so ground measurement beats the chain. A simulator could take the rule 'use the shortest chain' for radiation at a vineyard slope. A Nordic city; no vineyard.
