# Steel et al. 2011: temperature and mixed bunch-rot infections

From the National Wine and Grape Industry Centre, Charles Sturt University, Wagga Wagga.

## What it holds

- Detached Cabernet Sauvignon berries (12.5° Baumé), 10⁴ spores each, 5 days at high
  humidity, at 20 or 27 °C, alone and in mixtures.
- Berries infected by *B. cinerea* alone: 92 % at 20 °C, 65 % at 27 °C. *G. uvicola*: 57 %
  and 99 %. *C. acutatum*: high at both.
- In mixtures, Botrytis was suppressed at 27 °C, and dominated at 20 °C.
- Quotes Nair & Allen 1993's optimum of 20.8 °C for Botrytis berry infection.

## Dependence

- Independent of the engine; no shared author.

## Bearing (2026-10-07)

- Not a formulation: two temperatures, one wetness condition, one time.
- A possible check for a future Botrytis truth (M7), and the pointer to Nair & Allen 1993,
  whose numbers Magarey et al. 2005 later supplied ([magarey2005](magarey2005.md)).
