# Kraus et al. 2021: Warburgia extracts against downy mildew, as copper substitute

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- In vitro (microtitre): bark extract MIC100 5 µg/mL against leaf extract 500 µg/mL; copper 5 µg/mL (l. 124-129).
- Leaf discs: bark extract 500 µg/mL protected up to 48 h, leaf extract only at 500 µg/mL and at 0 h (l. 238-249).
- Seedlings in semi-controlled field exposure: bark extract 500 µg/mL cut severity 86% (24 h) and 98% (48 h) (l. 375-376).
- Two Siebeldingen vineyards, 2021: untreated control 100% leaf infection and about 55% severity; best WU extract 30.3% efficacy against 57-72% for the copper treatment (l. 455-458, 643-645).
- Authors attribute the field fall to rain washing off extracts (l. 663-669).
