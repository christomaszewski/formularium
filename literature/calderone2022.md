# Calderone et al. 2022: COS-OGA oligochitosan vs vineyard mildews

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Field trials in 2020 at Rodi Milici (Messina, Sicily): Nero d'Avola and Inzolia, two trials per cultivar, four blocks of 12 plants per treatment (l. 555-589).
- COS-OGA with AMF cut powdery mildew severity by about 15 % (Nero d'Avola) and 33 % (Inzolia) (l. 28).
- COS-OGA gave 65-100 % reduction of gray mould and sour rot, similar to Cu-S (l. 30).
- Treatments changed yield only in the two cultivars (l. 412).
- Downy mildew was not assessed: powdery mildew, gray mould and sour rot were scored (l. 617-621), although the discussion says dry sites limited P. viticola (l. 472).
- EU copper cap: 28 kg/ha over 7 years, about 4 kg/ha a year (l. 86).

## Dependence

- none. The paper names P. viticola and says a dry site limited its infections (l. 472), but it reports no downy mildew assessment, no leaf-wetness or infection model, and no equation from any engine model. Shared author with no dependence: none.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- None for downy mildew: the trials score powdery mildew, gray mould and sour rot and never P. viticola, so the paper offers no downy mildew data or model input to the truth or the engine.
