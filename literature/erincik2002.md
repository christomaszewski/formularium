# Erincik 2002: when Phomopsis infects grape berries and rachises, and what weather it needs on leaves and canes

An Ohio State dissertation (advisor L. V. Madden): greenhouse and field inoculations of
Seyval, Chambourcin and Catawba clusters at stages 12-35 for the susceptible period of fruit
and rachis, and the chamber study published as Erincik et al. 2003.

## What it holds

- Dissertation (Ohio State University, 2002, 114 pp.) on Phomopsis viticola, two parts (l.
  50-222).
- Ch. 2, greenhouse and field, Seyval, Chambourcin and Catawba, growth stages 12 to 35:
  fruit rot 54% (stage 12) to 75% (stage 33) in 1998 greenhouse, 92% at stage 33 in 1999 (l.
  1772-1782, Table 2.2-2.3).
- Rachis severity stays high from stage 12 to 33 and falls to 30% at stage 35 (l.
  1830-1837).
- Field 1998 control had 10.2% fruit rot; stages 31 and 33 reached 63% and 58% (l. 1887,
  Table 2.5).
- Ch. 4: 5 to 35 C at 5, 10, 15, 20 h wetness; five plants, three runs, inoculum 1 x 10^7
  conidia/mL (l. 3152, 3206-3212).
- Generalized Beta model, eq. 2, Tmin 4.95 C and Tmax 35.5 C (l. 3659-3660).
- Optimum 17.7 C (Catawba leaf), 18.0 C (internode); 20 C and 16.0 C (Seyval) (l.
  3728-3730).
- R2: Catawba 0.90 leaf, 0.85 internode; Seyval 0.88 and 0.76 (l. 4443-4445).
- 30 lesions per leaf at 18 C need 7.4 h wetness (Catawba) and 4.2 h at 20 C (Seyval) (l.
  3754, 4595).
- Field, 2000 and 2001: 19, 18, 17 natural-rain inoculations; r 0.577 to 0.837 (Figs
  4.7-4.10, l. 3827-3835).
- The text says all but one r exceeds 0.71; two do not (l. 3833).
- Bugaret (1984) reported 23-25 C optimum and 4 h wetness (cited, l. 2951-2957).

## Dependence

- Its own inoculations. The generalized Beta form is taken from Analytis 1977 and Grove et
  al. 1985 (l. 3271), the coefficients its own; Tmin and Tmax read from its own figures (l.
  3659). Madden is an author of engine pieces: a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- The chamber chapter is erincik2003's paper with more detail; its worked example (30
  lesions at 18 °C need 7.4 h, l. 3754) settles that the tabulated alpha is base 10, now in
  `erincik2003.phomopsis_infection`.
- For a Phomopsis truth: unlike the mildews, fruit and rachis stay susceptible from stage 12
  to about 33 (fruit rot 54-92 % when inoculated), falling only near stage 35 (l.
  1772-1837): no early berry window.
