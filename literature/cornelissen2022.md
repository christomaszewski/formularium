# Cornelissen et al. 2022: must chemistry that flags rot-affected grapes

240 must samples from white cultivars of one South African winery's growers, 2013-2019, with
visually estimated rot severity (0-91 %), and ROC-derived thresholds of must markers that
separate healthy from rot-affected fruit.

## What it holds

- Chemical study of 240 must samples from one producer winery's white-cultivar vineyards in
  the Olifants River and Cape Coastal regions, 2013 to 2019 (l. 97-99; Table 1). Each sample
  was ten pooled bunches (l. 185). Severity was estimated visually by a viticulturist from 0
  to 91 % (Table 1). Consistency among three assessors was tested on 45 samples (l. 191);
  ICC 0.90 (assessors 1 and 2, SEM 4 %) and 0.78 (assessors 1 and 3, SEM 7 %) (Table 2).
- Gluconic acid (and glycerol) rose with severity, r = 0.7 (p < 0.01); alcohol and sugar r >
  0.5; acetic acid and TA r = 0.3 (l. 308-311).
- Maximum gluconic acid 32.26 g/L at 91 % severity (l. 309-310).
- Thresholds set at 80 % sensitivity and 100 % specificity (l. 400): alcohol 0.10 %v/v
  (sensitivity 47 %, Table 4); acetic acid 0.17 g/L (80 %); glycerol 0.79 g/L (80 %);
  gluconic acid 0.99 g/L (90 %; 1.40 g/L at 80 %); TA 8.86 g/L (80 % sensitivity, 83 %
  specificity, AUC 0.84) (Table 4; l. 449).
- Optimal ROC cut-off for gluconic acid 0.16 g/L, not adopted (l. 389).
- The thresholds separate healthy from rot-affected must, not severity levels. The paper
  does not state a severity matched to each threshold.
- Cited: 3 to 5 % severity price penalties (Hill et al. 2013); 5 % severity affects quality
  (Steel et al. 2020); gluconic acid above 1.0 g/L (Fischer and Berger 2007).

## Dependence

- Its own winery data; severity estimated visually by viticulturists, no model.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's economics (t5): the intake side of a rot penalty: gluconic acid rises with
  rot (r 0.7, up to 32.26 g/L at 91 %), and thresholds such as gluconic acid 0.99 g/L or
  glycerol 0.79 g/L separate rot-affected loads; they are not tied to a severity level.
