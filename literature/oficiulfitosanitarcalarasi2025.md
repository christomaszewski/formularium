# Oficiul Fitosanitar Călărași 2025: vine warning bulletin no. 65

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Warning bulletin 65 of 3 July 2025 from the Călărași plant health office, for vines (l. 8, 10).
- Recommends treatment against downy mildew, powdery mildew and grey mould (l. 11-12).
- Downy mildew runs fast at about 20-22 °C in humid weather, and the oil-spot stage is then skipped (l. 25-26).
- Grey mould: infection from 1 to 32 °C, optimum 22-23 °C, favoured by RH 80-100 % (l. 53-55).
- Five products with label doses; BOSKET 1.0-1.2 kg/ha once a year; YUKON at most 8 sprays at 7-day intervals (l. 58-85).
- Bee protection and spray-compatibility advice; no model, forecast or observed data (l. 91-108).
