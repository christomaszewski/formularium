# Fedele et al. 2026: canopy density and leaf predisposition

Research article, Italy (Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, Piacenza area; Merlot VSP vineyard, 2020-2022).

## What it holds

- A three-year VSP Merlot field experiment varies bud load, fertilization and irrigation to give four canopy-density clusters, then measures leaf-disc susceptibility to P. viticola, in-canopy microclimate and epidemic progress as AUDPC.
- Denser canopies had more susceptible leaves and longer leaf wetness, but field epidemics did not differ.
- Borderline with class A: Table 1 and 2 (correlations, cluster means) are of indirect use, with no formulation.
