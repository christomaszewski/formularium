# Mozzanini et al. 2026: low-volume drone spray rates in a vineyard

Field trials of a canopy-targeted drone (UASS) spray at three growth stages and five rates. The trials measured canopy deposit, ground loss and spray coverage.

## What it holds

- **Site:** cv Ruche at Castagnole Monferrato, Asti. Planting is 5,000 vines/ha, and one 60 m row was used (l. 189-192). Stages are BBCH 55, BBCH 75 and a late stage (l. 195-199).
- **Rates:** 54.2, 72.9, 90.6, 105.8 and 142.2 L/ha (l. 26-27, 296-297).
- **Ground loss:** 0.18 uL/cm2 at 54.2 L/ha and 0.60 uL/cm2 at 142.2 L/ha, +233% (abstract, l. 34; l. 862). Canopy deposit skewed to the upper canopy, more so at later stages (abstract, l. 31-33).
- **Settings:** ISO 015 at 0.30 MPa (54.2 L/ha) early, ISO 03 at 0.30 MPa (105.8 L/ha) middle, and ISO 04 at 0.25 MPa (142.2 L/ha) late. The authors say these hold only for this nozzle, pressure and flight height (l. 37-42).
- **Data:** "No datasets were generated or analysed" (l. 1409). The trial year is not printed in the text read.

## Dependence

- Deposit normalised with the Biglia et al. 2025 equation (l. 426-427).
- Ground-loss sampling as in Mozzanini et al. 2024 and 2026 (l. 390-391).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t1 (fungicide protection: how a spray reaches the canopy). A growers' engine could take the stage-by-rate ground-loss trend. A simulator would need the deposit fractions, and these come from one row in one vineyard.
