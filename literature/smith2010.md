# Smith et al. 2010: powdery mildew severity peaks at the sink-to-source leaf

## What it holds

- Glasshouse Cabernet Sauvignon at 25 °C and 18 °C: severity peaks on leaf positions 3-5,
  where leaves turn from sink to source (modal positions 3.7 and 4.7 against 3.8 and 4.7).
- A Bayesian model of logistic leaf growth times leaf resistance; no coefficients printed.

## Dependence

- None.
