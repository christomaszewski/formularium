# Kalt et al. 2026: a machine-learning optical spore sentinel for wheat rust

A compact sampler and dark-field and UV imaging unit that detects and tells apart airborne urediniospores of wheat leaf and stripe rust with a Bayesian classifier, tested mainly on spores dusted in a growth chamber.

## What it holds

- **Device:** a passive funnel sampler onto collagen-coated windows, imaged by a Raspberry Pi HQ
  camera (Sony IMX477) under white-light dark-field and near-UV illumination (l. 82, 122-126).
  Spores are about 10 to 13 µm in half-axis (l. 92).
- **Training and test set:** wheat 'Carthago' inoculated with two isolates, yellow rust and
  brown rust, sprayed onto 10 to 13-day-old plants, then 14 days in a growth chamber (l. 216-226).
  The text says the spore density is higher than in real field samples (l. 205-207).
- **Detection:** a positive-class F1 of 97.7 % for the whole detection chain (l. 482). The
  identification model gives a G-mean of 93.3 % on validation and about 90.8 % on unseen test
  data (l. 486-487). The abstract's 91.6 % identification F1 (l. 33) is not in the text read.
- **Mixed samples:** 204 true positives, 2 false positives and 11 false negatives, precision
  99.0 %, F1 96.9 % (l. 525-527).
- **Data:** exemplary data in a public repository, full data on reasonable request (l. 604-608);
  code on a KIT GitLab site (l. 229, 693).

## Dependence

- **Builds on** the passive spore sampler of Blackall et al. (l. 135-136), the Zemax OpticStudio
  optics design (l. 122), the IMX477 sensor on a Raspberry Pi HQ camera (l. 82, 126), and a
  Bayesian classifier with mRMR feature selection (l. 388, 526).
- No shared dataset with another model is named.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- classes.tsv names no search for this key. A growers' decision engine could take an early
  warning input from a sampler like this, since it reports rust spores before symptoms.
- It is trained and tested on wheat spores, mostly from dusted plants, and has no vine or field
  validation in the text read. The paper was not read in full.
