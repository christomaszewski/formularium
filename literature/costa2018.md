# Costa et al. 2018: a diagram set for bacterial spot on passion fruit

An eight-level standard area diagram for bacterial spot on passion fruit fruit, validated by 20 raters. Aided raters had less constant and systematic error.

## What it holds

- **Diagram:** eight levels, 1, 3, 5, 10, 21, 38, 65 and 80% lesion area (abstract, l. 13-14).
- **Images:** 50 fruits from a commercial orchard at Planaltina, DF. Lesion area came from ImageJ (l. 70-82, 112).
- **Raters:** 20 in four groups of five. Two groups were inexperienced and two experienced. Each group rated the images unaided, and two groups rated them again with the diagram (l. 101-117).
- **Result:** the diagram helped inexperienced raters most. 60% and 100% of them had no constant and no systematic error (abstract, l. 21-23). Per-rater regressions and Lin's concordance are in Table 1 (l. 158-160).

## Dependence

- Accuracy and precision by linear regression and Lin's concordance, after Nutter and Schultz 1995 (l. 122-129, 160).
- Earlier passion-fruit diagram for anthracnose, Fischer et al. 2009 (l. 119-122).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t2 (observation). It is another crop and pathogen, so only the rater-error method carries over. A simulator or growers' engine would take the lesson that a diagram cuts rater bias, which matters when visual scores feed a model.
