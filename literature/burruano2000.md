# Burruano 2000: the life cycle of Plasmopara viticola

A short review of the author's own cytology and leaf-inoculation work (Burruano et al. 1992, 1994,
1995, 1996, 1997, 2000; Burruano & Ciofalo 1990), from Palermo.

## What it holds

- **Oospores overwinter in fallen leaves** (l. 19). Antheridia appear at the end of July in cleared
  leaf tissue (l. 22-24); nuclear fusion marks the oospore in which the inner wall thickens (after
  Vercesi et al. 1999, `vercesi1999`).
- **Dormancy** (l. 21-34): the after-ripening period is set by temperature and soil moisture
  (credited to Burruano et al. 1994, 1995). In several years in Sicily oospores at 20 °C and above 95 %
  RH did not germinate before January (l. 27-28); the optimum is between the end of February and
  the middle of March (Burruano & Ciofalo 1990, cited); germination from January to May depends on the
  developmental stage when leaves were sampled (Burruano et al. 2000, cited). No percentages and
  no after-ripening length in days are printed.
- **Germination:** more than 40 nuclei in the oospore before the germ tube emerges (l. 38); migration into the
  germ-sporangium complete 24 h after emergence (l. 44). Zoospores of the germ-sporangium are the
  only source of primary inoculum (l. 92).
- **Lesions and secondary cycle:** oil spots 4 to 5 days after infection (Di Graziano et al. 1988,
  cited); detached leaves at 14-28 °C and above 90 % humidity make sporangiophores overnight
  (Laviola 1964, cited); inoculated at 2e4 sporangia per ml, sporangiophores come out of stomata 2-4 h
  after inoculation and sporangia detach after 14 h; zoospores released within 2 h of collection in
  water, 40-50 min after 16-24 h; zoospores of sporangia are the source of secondary infections.
- **Not printed:** oospore counts, any germination percentage, rain or temperature effects on
  overwintering in numbers.

## Dependence

- No model or data. It reviews the author's cytology and cites Vercesi et al. 1999 for the
  nuclear-fusion point.

## Bearing (2026-10-19)

For the autumn before a season:

- **Oospore formation starts in summer** (antheridia from end of July), so leaves that fall in
  autumn already hold them; maturity and germination are later (see `vercesi1999`: type II from
  November).
- **Germination is not before January** in Sicily under the best conditions (20 °C, above 95 % RH),
  peak late February to mid-March, and what happens in January-May depends on the leaves'
  stage at sampling. Credited, not read here.
- **After-ripening depends on temperature and soil moisture** (credited), with no values; nothing on
  the previous season's amount of disease.
