# Ojwang et al. 2021: multi-source spread model for cucurbit downy mildew

A spread model with several inoculum sources and a direction term, fitted to 2008 to 2010 sentinel-plot reports of cucurbit downy mildew in the USA.

## What it holds

- Sentinel-plot disease reports for 2008, 2009 and 2010, with 25, 65 and 28 data points in the fits (l. 33–37; Tables 3 to 5, l. 823, 855, 902).
- An anisotropic spread term gives a small but consistent cut in temporal prediction error (l. 49–50).
- No sign of a yearly alternative source at northern latitudes. A sign of one on the western edge of the Gulf of Mexico (l. 51–54).

## Dependence

- Builds on the logistic model and the power-law spread models (refs 1, 15 and 16, l. 105–125).
- Direction is modelled with a von Mises distribution (l. 810).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Off the point: cucurbit downy mildew on cucumber, not a vine pest. Its multi-source spread method could serve t6 only if a grape pathogen's reports were fitted to it.
