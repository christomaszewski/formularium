# Cabras et al. 2001: folpet and mancozeb residues on vine leaves and grapes after 45 mm of artificial rain (abstract only)

A vineyard experiment with artificial rain on folpet and mancozeb residues, known here only from its abstract: folpet resists rain, and about a fifth of mancozeb on leaves washes off easily.

**Read this first.** Abstract only (Europe PMC); no full text was obtained, so no paper is held. The abstract prints no residue fraction per rain amount and no result for the spray-to-rain interval, which are the two things E6 needs. It does not name the vineyard, country or year; Sardinia is inferred from the authors' institution. The percentages below are the abstract's own.

## What it holds

- **Design (l. 7, 8):** artificial rain in a vineyard, varying the amount and the interval between application and rain, on folpet and mancozeb residues on grapes and on vine leaves. A total of 45 mm was given as 45, 30 + 15 or 15 + 15 + 15 mm.
- **Folpet (l. 9, 10):** good rainfastness on grapes and leaves; a modest decrease only in the experiments that had 45 mm at one go.
- **Mancozeb (l. 11, 12):** lower rainfastness, as a portion of the deposit washed off easily even in modest rain. That portion was 38 % on grapes and 20 % on leaves.
- **Authors' advice (l. 13):** after rain on the day after spraying, folpet need not be repeated; repeating mancozeb is recommendable.
