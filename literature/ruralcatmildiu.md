# Guia de bones pràctiques n.d.: organic vine downy mildew control without copper

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Copper formulations are the only products with proven efficacy for organic downy mildew control (l. 40-41).
- EU copper maximum: 6 kg/ha a year before, 4 kg/ha a year from 31 January 2019 for seven years (l. 49-52).
- Primary infection rule 10:10:24: 10 mm rain at 10 C for 24 h (l. 85).
- Secondary infection: night humidity or rain at about 13 C; 3 weeks from infection to symptoms at 10 C, 5 days at 20-24 C (l. 87-90).
- Treat when monitoring reaches 90% development in wetter zones (l. 99-100).
- Highest risk before and after flowering (l. 102).
- Four applications on average: shoots over 20 cm, flowering, set, and berry closure (l. 258-273).
- Apply before and after rain above 10 mm, because organic products wash off (l. 282-283).
- Catalan organic vineyards are 15% of Spanish organic activity (l. 116).

## Dependence

- none identified. The 10:10:24 rule (l. 84-85) is a rain-temperature primary-infection rule of the same family as the listed 3-10 rule, but the guide does not name a source or derivation.
