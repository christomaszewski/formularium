# Gonzalez Dominguez et al. 2019: vite.net pilot runs in the Penedès and the Alto Douro, abstract only

A meeting abstract saying the vite.net decision support system was compared with observations in 18 Penedès vineyards in 2016 and 2017, with the result "substantial agreement" and no numbers.

**Read this first.**
- Only the abstract was read. The proceedings paper (IOBC-WPRS Bull. 154:88-92, 2020), which would hold per-vineyard dates and spray counts, was not obtained.
- The abstract prints no number for agreement, no dates, no spray counts and no vineyard list. "Substantial agreement" is the authors' words, not a statistic.

## What it holds

- **The system (l. 1 to 6):** vite.net is a web-based decision support system by Horta, a spin-off of the Università Cattolica del Sacro Cuore, that takes real-time field data and models growth, diseases, pests, fungicide protection and abiotic stress. It has been available to Italian growers since 2013 (l. 6); and, the abstract says, in Spain and Portugal since 2015 (l. 6).
- **Penedès pilot (l. 8, 9):** in the Penedès the system was evaluated by the regional plant-health service (the SISTEMIO project) in 18 vineyards in 2016 and 2017.
- **What was compared (l. 9):** the system's output on primary and secondary infections of Plasmopara viticola, against the growers' fungicide treatments and the disease development.
- **Result (l. 12):** "substantial agreement" between the system output and the observations, for both pilot runs. No figures.
- **Portugal (l. 10):** two vineyards in the Alto Douro from 2016 to 2018, with ADVID, and four more vineyards still running in 2019.

## Dependence

- The system is the Horta vite.net DSS (Università Cattolica models); the abstract does not list the equations. Authors Gonzalez Dominguez and Rossi belong to that group, which is only a flag for dependence with other vite.net notes.
