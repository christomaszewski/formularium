# Roberts et al. 2017: cross-validation for structured data

Review, Ecological data in general (authors in Germany, USA, Canada, Australia, France).

## What it holds

- A review of block and non-random cross-validation for data with time, space, hierarchy or phylogeny structure, with simulations and case studies showing that block cross-validation is nearly always more appropriate than random cross-validation for predicting to new data.
- It is a method paper from ecology (species distribution models), with no disease content.
- Year, journal, volume, pages and DOI are not printed in the text (an author manuscript; its references cite Ecography and 2016 papers).
