# Faralli et al. 2024: 37 years of Chardonnay and Teroldego in the Piana Rotaliana

Two vineyards planted in 1980, stages scored by one operator from 1986 to 2022, with the
station's weather: trends on year for each stage and interval, the degree-days each stage
needed, and carry-over from the previous season. Trends and correlations, no model of a
date on temperature.

## What it holds

- **Data:** Chardonnay (SMA130 on SO4) and Teroldego (SMA138 on 101/14) on pergola, Piana
  Rotaliana, replanted and moved to adjacent vineyards in 2016 and 2018 (l. 144-158);
  station gaps filled from Mezzacorona Novali (l. 150-153); BBCH 07, 61, 81 and harvest,
  1986-2022 (l. 285).
- **Climate:** annual mean +0.036 °C/yr (R² 0.43), days below 0 °C −0.8 a year, radiation
  rising (R² 0.65); GDD10 +6.0 a year, GDD6 +7.8 (Fig. 3, l. 296-378); Winkler region IV
  by 2022 (l. 529). Huglin with k 1.05 (l. 240).
- **Stage trends on year** (Fig. 5, p. 7): budburst −0.38 (Chardonnay) and −0.43
  (Teroldego) d/yr, flowering −0.30 and −0.38, veraison −0.32 and −0.45, harvest −0.48
  and −0.54 (R² 0.2-0.3); flowering to harvest 105 and 115 d in 1986-1990 against 95 and
  110 in 2018-2022 (l. 443). The abstract's interval rates (−1.2 d every two years for
  budburst-flowering, +0.25 d/yr for veraison-ripening) are Fig. 6B's slopes on the
  stage's day of year, not on the year; on the year, veraison to harvest shortens for
  Chardonnay only (−0.13 d/yr) (l. 390-438).
- **Heat to each stage:** GDD10 to budburst fell 0.66 and 0.73 a year, to flowering 1.1
  and 1.8 (Fig. 7); none to veraison or harvest (l. 401-429).
- **Carry-over:** budburst's GDD10 inversely correlated with the previous year's GDD10 and
  the De Martonne index; the previous cold season's soil temperature inversely with
  the onsets; previous-year evapotranspiration with the heat requirements (Figs 8-10,
  coefficients not printed; l. 502-512, 681).

## Dependence

- **None computed:** descriptive; GDD after Jones et al., Huglin, De Martonne. The
  phenology was the Fondazione Mach's observers' (one person, l. 285).

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- Not the requested form; a long dated series with a carry-over finding (last year's
  heat and the winter's soil temperature move this year's budburst requirement) that a
  degree-day model with a fixed sum cannot carry.
