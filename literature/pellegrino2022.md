# Pellegrino et al. 2022: 50 % of buds or 50 % of shoots as the budburst date

Chardonnay, Shiraz and Cabernet-Sauvignon in one Sunraysia vineyard over 2004-2005,
under three pruning systems and three irrigation levels, with cumulative budburst fitted
to a logistic in the day: the date at 50 % of shoots holds across treatments where the
date at 50 % of buds fails. A scoring method, not a model.

## What it holds

- **Design:** planted 1994, 34°25′S (l. 159-166); Shiraz mechanical, spur and minimal
  pruning (622-790 buds a vine under mechanical, Table 1), Cabernet-Sauvignon control,
  regulated and prolonged deficit (l. 24, 191-214); weekly counts on six vines a
  treatment, a logistic per vine with maximum M, slope a and inflexion day tI (eq. 1,
  l. 179-190; spur M 67, a 0.30, tI 266; minimal M 43, a 0.39, l. 517).
- **Finding:** the 50 % of shoots date comes up to 8 d earlier than 50 % of buds
  (l. 307); Shiraz 12-17 d and Cabernet-Sauvignon about 15 d after Chardonnay (l. 310-
  315); no pruning or year effect on Shiraz's shoot-based date, a 6 d spur-against-
  mechanical difference on the bud-based one (l. 316, 399); minimal-pruned Shiraz
  reached only 43 % final budburst, so its bud-based date is undefined (l. 405).
- **Heat:** GDD base 10 °C from 1 July: 122 (Chardonnay), 205 (Cabernet-Sauvignon), 226
  (Shiraz) °C·d to 50 % of buds, 4-55 °C·d less to 50 % of shoots (l. 213-219, 406-409);
  the authors doubt a 1 July start in a warm winter (l. 580).

## Dependence

- **None:** the base is Winkler & Williams 1939's; nothing fitted but the logistic.
