# Liu 2017: the economic value of BlightPro timing for potato and tomato late blight

A PhD dissertation that compares calendar and BlightPro decision-support fungicide timing by simulated net return, for risk-averse growers, at 59 potato and 25 tomato locations.

## What it holds

- **Simulation basis:** 14 years of simulated seasons at US locations; three strategies
  (calendar, BlightPro DSS, unsprayed control); net return to all costs except fungicide and
  application (l. 423-436).
- **Potato:** the DSS-based strategy is preferred; under high disease pressure its benefit to
  growers is $30 to $573 per acre (l. 445).
- **Tomato:** the result turns on an assumed yield gain. At 0 % gain the DSS benefit is
  -$28 to $48 per acre; at 5 %, 10 % and 15 % gains it is $496 to $603, $1,020 to $1,158 and
  $1,543 to $1,714 per acre (l. 460-464).
- **Thresholds** (essay three): 10 years of simulated seasons at 152 locations; the economic
  thresholds improve disease suppression and profit against the previous thresholds while
  keeping fungicide use efficiency (l. 474-481).
- **Extract limits:** the extract holds the front matter, the abstract (p. xiii) and the
  start of Chapter 1, and it stops at l. 904. Chapters 3 to 5 are not in it, so the tables
  named in the contents were not read; the figures above come from the abstract.

## Dependence

- **Builds on** the BlightPro DSS (Small, Joseph & Fry 2015b, l. 569) and the Simcast
  thresholds it is compared with (contents, Table 16, l. 313). The simulations and risk methods
  are those named in the abstract (l. 431-433); they are not described in the extract.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- classes.tsv names no search for this key. The method (certainty equivalents and risk

  spray timings by value, not only by disease suppression.
