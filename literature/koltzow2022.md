# Køltzow et al. 2022: CARRA against ERA5 for 2 m temperature and 10 m wind in the Arctic

A comparison of the 2.5 km CARRA regional reanalysis and the 31 km ERA5 against station observations over Svalbard and northern Fennoscandia (l. 16-21). A reanalysis check; no record.

## What it holds

- **Test:** CARRA-East against ERA5 and point observations; Year of the Polar Prediction Special Observing Period, February and March 2018, mapped in Fig. 1 (l. 74-76).
- **Result:** CARRA agrees better in most respects; the largest differences are in complex terrain and at coasts (l. 21-24). The gain is larger for temperature than for wind speed (l. 26).
- **Representativeness:** a substantial part of the reanalysis-to-station difference is sub-grid variability; it is larger in ERA5 than CARRA (l. 33-37). No headline figure was located in the first pages.

## Dependence

- Builds on ERA5 as host model on the CARRA boundaries and on Yang et al. 2020 (l. 46, 56-59).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s2 and s5:** a worked case that grid-box error against a point station has a representativeness part that finer resolution only partly removes. A simulator could take the idea of a representativeness term when it compares gridded weather with a vineyard sensor.
- Polar domain, temperature and wind only, no humidity. Triage-grade: not read in full.
