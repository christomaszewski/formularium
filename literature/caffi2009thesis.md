# Caffi 2009 (PhD thesis): front matter only

The file holds the front page, the contents and the general abstract. The chapters are not
held.

## What it holds

- **Chapter I** (p. 12): water in the leaf litter and oospores; VPD below 2.13 hPa gives
  enough water for oospore development. Its subject is [caffi2006water](caffi2006water.md)
  and the engine's dormancy rule (`rossi2008pp.dormancy`).
- **Chapter II** (p. 34): five years of germination (relative infection incidence on leaf
  discs) against hydro-thermal time, fitted by a Gompertz equation.
- **Chapter III** (p. 57): a mechanistic primary-infection model on cohorts of a seasonal
  oospore dose, hourly; by the notes, Rossi et al. 2008's (`rossi2008.primary`).
- **Chapter IV** (p. 82): its evaluation in Italy, 100 vineyards 1995-2007 and 42 potted
  groups 2006-2008, without calibration; the title of [caffi2009](caffi2009.md).
- **Chapter V** (p. 105): a warning system over three years in northern Italy, efficacy
  above 90 %, 174-224 EUR/ha saved; the subject of [caffi2010](caffi2010.md).
