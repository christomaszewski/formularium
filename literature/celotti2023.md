# Celotti et al. 2023: rapid leaf copper test by image analysis, Friuli

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Field survey on five organic farms in Friuli-Venezia Giulia, 2020 and 2021 (l. 117, 121-122).
- Copper is measured on leaves by ICP-AES (0 to 20 µg/L standards) and by an image-analysis app (l. 82-83, 30).
- Organic copper is limited to 28 kg/ha over seven years under EC 889/2008 (l. 81-82).
- Copper accumulates on adult leaves in both years, while young leaves reflect the last treatment (l. 176, 204, 224).
- Rain washes copper off the lower leaf surface (l. 210).
- The app and ICP-AES correlate only in a few adult-leaf cases (l. 245-248).
- Good defence reported below the organic copper limit under optimal conditions (l. 324).

## Dependence

- none. This is a field survey of copper on leaves and grapes, with ICP-AES and an image-analysis app. It computes, borrows and fits no engine model. The copper accumulation and rain wash-off are observed patterns. Shared authors with the engine literature: none noted.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Gives the truth an observed field record of copper deposition on leaves against downy mildew incidence on five farms over two seasons, the kind of data the copper-reduction branch of the simulator would need, though it is sparse, uncorrected for units, and correlational.
