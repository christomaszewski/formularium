# Bérubé et al. 2022: insect-trap fluid against oiled cheesecloth for fungal spores

Fungi found in Lindgren trap cups and on oiled-cheesecloth slides are compared by
  metabarcoding; it is a detection comparison, not a catch efficiency.

## What it holds

- **Samples:** 90 samples with 1,277 fungal OTUs, ITS1F and gITS7 primers, Illumina sequencing
  (l. 35-39).
- **Result:** for OTUs found by only one method, insect traps had three times as many as slides
  (l. 40-41).
- **Sites:** six sites in cities and rural areas, Sault Ste. Marie (Ontario) and Fredericton
  (New Brunswick), sampled in August 2018 in New Brunswick (l. 120-157).

## Dependence

- Metabarcoding with ITS1F and gITS7; Lindgren funnel traps (record).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s6: sample type matters for what a trap reports (fluid cups find many more rare taxa than
  slides). Forest fungi, not grape. A simulator gets nothing quantitative for a vineyard. Not
  read in full.
