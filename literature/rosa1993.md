# Rosa et al. 1993: PLASMO's first published equations

The software paper for PLASMO (Plasmopara Simulation Model), from Rosa's 1988 thesis
(Firenze, not held). Turbo Pascal, hourly inputs. Its equations differ from Orlandini et al.
1993's (literature/orlandini1993).

## What it holds

- **Primary infection (p. 208):** started by rain ≥ 8 mm once the minimum temperature stays
  above 10 °C; data input starts when shoots reach 100 mm.
- **Incubation (p. 208):** "a function deduced from the Goidanich table (Goidanich et al.,
  1958)", fitted to the table as x-y points: f4(T, RH) = f1(T)·f2(T)·f3(RH);
  f1 = exp(-[(T - 23.5)/11.2]²); f2 = 1 for T ≤ 10 °C or T ≥ 23.5 °C, 0.00577(T - 16.7)² +
  0.737 for 10-16.7 °C, 1 - 0.00577(T - 23.5)² for 16.7-23.5 °C; f3 = 0.15·√(RH - 30).
  f2 corrects the Gaussian to the table's points; f3 raises the maximum with humidity.
- **Inoculation (p. 208):** the "Blaeser table (Blaeser and Weltzien, 1979)" as a hyperbola,
  f5(T) = 52.7/T wet hours for 6 ≤ T ≤ 26 °C, otherwise 0; progress f6 = 100/f5.
- **Survival (p. 209):** f7 = 100/f8; f8(T, RH) = (f9(T) - 12)·RH/100 + 12; f9 = 235.8
  (T < 10 °C), 1.8T + 217.8 (10-15 °C), 15.52T + 12 (15-30 °C), 12 (T > 30 °C), credited to
  Blaeser & Weltzien 1979. The 15-30 °C piece is probably a misprinted sign (see
  `orlandini1993.survival`).
- **No sporulation or oospore step:** sporangia emerge when incubation reaches 100 %; an
  unstopped infection starts the next.
- **Test (pp. 212-214):** Pratantico (Arezzo), 1988, cv. Sangiovese, weather station in the
  vineyard, observations twice a week (data from the Istituto Sperimentale per la
  Viticoltura). Three sprays by the model against six by the calendar; untreated rows
  "agreed" with the simulation. No statistics.

## Dependence

- **Incubation:** fitted to Goidanich's table, the data the engine's `goidanich.incubation`
  is, and Rossi 2008's regressions very probably are (`goidanich1957`). The 1958 book (I
  nemici della vite, Goidanich, Cesarini [sic] & Foschi) is taken to print the 1957 table
  (inferred).
- **Inoculation:** fitted to Blaeser & Weltzien 1979's Tab. 1, a wet degree-hour form, as
  `blaeser1979.survival`'s infection rule.
- **Survival:** credited to Blaeser & Weltzien 1979, who print no function of this form.
- **Authors:** Orlandini is an author of Sentelhas et al. 2008 (the engine's wetness
  thresholds): a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-09)

- PLASMO in this version is kin to the engine through Goidanich's data (incubation) and
  Blaeser & Weltzien's data and form (inoculation). A development family at most.
