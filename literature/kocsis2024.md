# Kocsis et al. 2024: vineyard and management factors in a Hungarian citizen-science survey

A citizen-science survey in Hungary, July 2020 to March 2023, linked vineyard, management and variety features to grey mould, downy mildew and powdery mildew.

## What it holds

- An online questionnaire from July 2020 to March 2023. About 5000 people were recruited (l. 150).
- Grey mould occurrence was tied to row orientation (l. 277-279). It rose with the number of chemical treatments and with canopy wall height (l. 292-294).
- Downy mildew occurrence rose with the inclination of the plantation (l. 290).
- Many tested factors showed no significant effect (l. 516).

## Dependence

- Method follows Beza et al. 2018, a citizen-science approach (Materials and Methods). No model is borrowed.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (grey mould), with t3 for powdery mildew. The effects are associations from self-reported severity classes, binarised at the yearly median. They are not causal rates.
- A simulator could use the direction of the effects (more sprays and taller canopy walls go with more grey mould) as priors only. Not read in full.
