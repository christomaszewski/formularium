# Rose et al. 2016: decision support tools for agriculture

Research article, England and Wales (UK).

## What it holds

- A mixed-methods study (expert-collated list of tools, interviews and a farmer survey in seven areas of England and Wales) of why UK farmers and advisers use or do not use decision support tools.
- It finds fifteen factors that affect uptake, such as usability, cost-effectiveness, performance and relevance to the user.
