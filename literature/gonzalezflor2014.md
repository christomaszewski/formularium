# González-Flor et al. 2014: rainfed Chardonnay in Alt Penedès and Anoia

Reflectance indices against yield and berry composition in seven rainfed Chardonnay
vineyards for cava, 2009 and 2011. Read for canopy data; it has light interception only.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 66-73, 121-123): seven commercial vineyards in Alt Penedès and Anoia; mean
  annual temperature 15 °C, rainfall 550 mm (l. 67; 530 mm at l. 144); planted 1990-2001
  at 2,083 to 3,703 vines/ha, double Royat; rainfed.
- **Season** (l. 80, Table 1 l. 139, 152): veraison about the third week of July 2009 and
  about 10 days earlier in 2011; growing-season rain 687.6 and 662.6 mm, ET0 928.2 and
  1,031.8 mm (the text's 499 mm at l. 86 does not match).
- **Canopy at veraison** (l. 158-162): fraction of PAR intercepted 69.8 ± 2.0 % (2009) and
  66.2 ± 1.3 % (2011), vineyards 56.4 to 78.7 % and 61.4 to 71.9 %, by ceptometer, at
  full canopy (l. 96-98).
- **Vines:** predawn water potential -0.39 and -0.27 MPa (l. 166); yield 5.3 and 6.2 kg a
  vine (l. 202); harvest in the second and third weeks of August (l. 171).
- **Not printed:** leaf area, LAI, shoots, canopy size, spacing other than density.

## Dependence

- WI after Peñuelas et al. 1993, fIPAR method after Serrano et al. 2012; fits to the
  vineyards' own means (n 6 or 7). No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Context for Penedès whites, no leaf area:** rainfed double-Royat Chardonnay intercepts
  about two thirds of PAR at veraison. Serrano et al. 2012 itself is not held.
