# Martínez-Casasnovas & Ramos 2009: soil alteration by erosion, ploughing and levelling in Penedès vineyards

A soil study of vineyards in a 30,000 ha area of the Penedès, with a description of the terrain, traditional terraces and levelling.

**Read this first.**
- This is the author's accepted manuscript from a university repository, not the typeset paper; page numbers of the journal are not shown. The slope figure is the authors' own description of the study area; the levelling statement is cited from another paper.

## What it holds

- **Study area:** 30,000 ha in the Penedès, Catalonia, about 30 km south-west of Barcelona, between the Sierra Prelitoral and the Anoia and Llobregat rivers (l. 470). Vineyards occupy 35 % of the area (l. 474, the line after the quote).
- **Slope:** the area has a network of gullies; inter-gully areas are "usually undulating to rolling", with an average slope of 10-15 % (l. 497 and the next line).
- **Levelling:** extensive land levelling to reduce slope has occurred in recent decades in Penedès vineyards (l. 386). A figure of +26.5 % soil loss, cited from Jiménez-Delgado 2004, is attached to it by the reading agent.
- **Field size:** levelling also increases field size to permit mechanisation, in the last few decades (l. 388).
- **Traditional terraces:** broad terraces locally named "rases", with eight to ten rows of vines, which intercept runoff and direct it out of the field by lateral dirt tracks (l. 550).

## Dependence

- None recorded for the terrain statements. The levelling and soil-loss figures are cited from Jiménez-Delgado 2004; the paper's own soil measurements are not used here.

## Bearing (2026-11-04)

- **terrain.slope_pct (Agrarium):** inter-gully slopes of 10-15 % over a 30,000 ha Alt Penedès-Anoia area. This is a mean slope of the land between gullies, not of vineyard blocks; it sits inside the 4-20 % range of pla2001.
- **Site layout:** rases of 8-10 rows are the old terrace unit; levelling since the 1990s has enlarged fields. Qualitative evidence.
