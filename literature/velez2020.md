# Vélez et al. 2020: missing vines and Sentinel-2 NDVI

Research article, Greenhouse at Stellenbosch University, South Africa; field block of cv. Cabernet Sauvignon (location not read; authors are at ITACyL, Valladolid, Spain).

## What it holds

- Technical Note on how removing vines changes Sentinel-2 pixel NDVI in a greenhouse simulation and a field vine-removal experiment on Cabernet Sauvignon, giving a linear NDVI-reduction vs Total Leaf Area-reduction relation (R2 = 0.986 and 0.72).
- Useful as a mixed-pixel/missing-vine loss factor for satellite detection, though it is about vigour and not disease.

## Dependence

- No disease formulation; nothing it computes is a model the engine runs.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- NDVI falls about 0.3 per unit of lost leaf area: how canopy gaps blur a satellite operator's signal.
