# Michael et al. 2020: spray volume and sprayer type for leaf deposit and ground loss in mountain vineyards

A tracer trial of four sprayer and volume set-ups, in trellis and goblet training, at a mountain vineyard in Cyprus. It measures leaf deposit and loss to the ground.

## What it holds

- **Set-ups (l. 19-21):** spray gun at 1000 L/ha (HVS); knapsack sprayer at 200 L/ha (LVS); orchard sprayer at 500 L/ha (OS500) or 250 L/ha (OS250).
- **Deposit standardised to 1 kg/ha (l. 25-26):** LVS highest, then HVS, OS250 and OS500. The goblet system held about half the deposit of the trellis.
- **Ground loss (l. 26-27):** per kilogram of tracer per hectare, HVS lost twice as much to the ground as LVS, and four times as much as OS250 or OS500.
- **Conditions (l. 355-357):** trial air at 33.7 and 35 °C and 25.8 to 26.9 % RH, so the trials were hot and dry.

## Dependence

Uses the deposit equation credited to Gil et al. (l. 506-507) and the standardisation credited to Codis et al. (l. 559). The tracer is tartrazine at 4000 mg/L (l. 303).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (fungicide protection, spray application).** Deposit per kilogram of tracer favours the low-volume knapsack, and the spray gun loses most to the ground. For a spray-protection term the usable output is relative deposit by volume, not an absolute dose.
- **Not read in full.** One site, one season (2020), a tracer rather than a fungicide, and hot, dry trial conditions (l. 355-357). Use the ratios, not the absolute deposits.
