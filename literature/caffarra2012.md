# Caffarra et al. 2012: grapevine, moth and powdery mildew phenology under climate change

Grapevine phenology is coupled to phenology models of Lobesia botrana and of Erysiphe necator and run under climate scenarios for Trentino. The disease is powdery mildew, not downy mildew.

## What it holds

- **Method:** detailed grapevine phenology models combined with models of the moth and of powdery mildew, then applied to climate scenarios for an eastern Alpine study area (l. 55-60).
- **Sites:** Cembra, San Michele, Besagno, Cognola, Faedo and Tenna, each with a weather station under 1 km from its monitoring site (l. 130-134, 234).
- **Result:** simulated powdery mildew severity decreases, most in years with later symptom onset and in the scenario with the larger warming (l. 68-69).
- **Not in the first 150 lines:** model equations, parameters and scenario data.

## Dependence

- Builds on grapevine, Lobesia botrana and Erysiphe necator phenology models and on climate scenarios, as the abstract says (l. 55-60).
