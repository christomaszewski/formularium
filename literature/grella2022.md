# Grella et al. 2022: PWM airblast spraying at 12 duty-cycle and speed settings

A PWM airblast sprayer in an espalier Barbera vineyard, tested at 12 duty-cycle and forward-speed settings. The paper names spray volume bands for fungicide and insecticide use.

## What it holds

- **Settings:** duty cycles of 30, 50, 70 and 100%, and forward speeds of 4, 6 and 8 km/h (abstract l. 27-29).
- **Field results:** pulsing changed coverage variability under controlled conditions, but not canopy uniformity in the real field (l. 36-38).
- **Volume bands:** 200 to 250 L/ha for insecticides and 300 to 370 L/ha for fungicides, from the coverage and deposit-density relation (abstract l. 39-41).
- **Table 5:** linear fits of deposit density on coverage for cards below 20% coverage. r² was 0.845 at 4 km/h and 30% duty cycle, and 0.496 at 100% (l. 1004-1011).
- **Trial:** September 2020, BBCH 89 (l. 285-286).

## Dependence

- Sampling strategy of Grella et al. 2020 (l. 360).
- PWM work of Salcedo et al. 2020 and Mangus et al. 2017 (l. 133-134, 143).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (spray volume and coverage):** the fungicide volume band and the coverage-to-deposit fit could feed a spray term. The fit holds for cards below 20% coverage, one cultivar and one date.
