# Szabó et al. 2023: review of grape black rot

A review of black rot of grapes: the pathogen, its disease cycle, forecast models, fungicide timing and resistance.

## What it holds

- Heavy infections can cause up to 100 % yield loss (l. 59).
- Forecast models (§ 7.4, l. 817–890): Northover et al. found 196 to 248 degree-days, with a 7 and 26 °C threshold, before leaf symptoms, and spore exudation peaked at 400 degree-days (l. 864–869). Smith and Sutherland used second-order equations for hours above 85 % relative humidity (l. 873–875). Rossi et al. built a mechanistic life-cycle model (l. 876–881). VitiMeteo Black Rot is a working system in several German, Luxembourg and Austrian locations (l. 887–890).
- Control: fungicide rotations of 12 to 14 days in humid weather (l. 918). Smith's advisory system cut fungicide sprays by about 45 % (l. 870–873).

## Dependence

- None recorded: this is a review with no data of its own. It builds on the studies it cites, such as Northover et al. (l. 864) and Rossi et al. (l. 877).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t4 (black rot): the degree-day and humidity-hour rules give a starting list for a black-rot module in a vineyard simulator. The review prints no fitted values of its own, so each rule needs its original source.
