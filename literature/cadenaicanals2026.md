# Cadena i Canals et al. 2026: flavescence dorée genotypes in Swiss vineyards

Genotyping of symptomatic vines in western Switzerland finds nine genotypes. The clusters point to infected planting material plus local spread.

## What it holds

- 4,212 symptomatic grapevines sampled in Vaud, Valais and Geneva in 2015 to 2022. Of these, 26.3% tested positive for FDp (l. 48-50).
- Established markers (map, dnaK, vmpA, malG) give homogeneous profiles. Eight new SNP markers, with malG, give nine multilocus genotypes (l. 53-59).
- The paper ties the clusters to planting material plus local dissemination. It gives no spread rate and no kernel (abstract, l. 53-62).
- Reference sequences are in NCBI (PX849515 to PX849530), and reads in SRA under PRJNA1417603 (l. 580-587).

## Dependence

- Builds on the MLST markers map, dnaK, vmpA and malG (Arnaud et al. 2007; Rossi et al. 2019; Malembic-Maher et al. 2020).
- Cites the Ticino outbreak data as the nearest published comparison (Casati et al. 2017; Oggier et al. 2023, 2024).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t6 (regional spread), with t7 for the vector context. A simulator could take the planting-material route as a source of long-distance jumps. The paper gives no kernel or rate, so it helps attribute sources, not fit a spread model.
