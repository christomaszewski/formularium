# Liu et al. 2024: a GRU pretrained on 14 million ecosys points, fine-tuned on county yields and eddy-covariance towers (crops, Corn Belt)

KGML-ag-Carbon: a hierarchical GRU whose structure follows the process model ecosys, pretrained on ecosys output over 293 counties and fine-tuned on county yields and tower carbon fluxes, with remote-sensing GPP as an input; compared with pure ML and with ecosys alone. No record: a method with measured results.

**Read this first.** The sample-size curves, the ablation of pretraining against structure against loss, and the extreme-year results are in Figures 2 and 3, which are images; none of their values is in the text, so what follows is the text's own wording. Whether ecosys was calibrated is not stated in the lines read; the paper calls it "well-validated" (l. 416, 500). The pure-ML (real-only) arm is described, not scored, in the text.

## What it holds

- **Simulator and synthetic data** (l. 197, 480, 484): ecosys, 10,335 simulations over 293 counties of Illinois, Iowa and Indiana, over 14 million synthetic data points; the inputs include soil information and random planting and harvest dates and rotations.
- **Real data** (l. 206, 232, 234): county yields (320 of 630 counties used for fine-tuning; each real training sample is a county with a 21-year yield series) and 11 eddy-covariance cropland towers (1 to 7 training sites in the tower sample-size runs, 48 independent experiments).
- **Validation design** (l. 172-174, 229): yield tested on 210 held-out counties (new places); tower fluxes on 2 held-out years at the 11 towers; sample-size, spatial, temporal and extreme-year splits (extreme years 2002, 2003 and 2012 withheld, l. 842-843; the text claims the model beats pure ML and ecosys on them, l. 209, figures not in the text).
- **Emulation of the simulator** (l. 162-164): on two held-out synthetic years the pretrained model reproduces ecosys with R2 0.99 (yield), 0.99 (autotrophic respiration), 0.97 (heterotrophic respiration) and 0.97 (net ecosystem exchange).
- **The simulator against real data** (l. 166, 169): ecosys alone scores R2 0.49 (corn) and 0.42 (soybean) on county yields, and R2 0.67-0.89 for daily ecosystem respiration and 0.59-0.88 for net exchange at the towers.
- **After fine-tuning** (l. 172-174): yield R2 0.91 (corn) and 0.88 (soybean) on the 210 test counties; daily ecosystem respiration R2 0.94 and net exchange R2 0.96 on two held-out years at 11 towers.
- **Real-only baseline: pure ML** (l. 178, 185-186, 195): the same GRU inputs without the knowledge-guided parts. The text says KGML-ag-Carbon "consistently outperforms" it and is much less sensitive to the number of real samples; pure ML approached it at large sample sizes but "performed poorly with small training sets, particularly for crop yield"; with zero real training samples the model, using GPP as input, can outperform ecosys and pure ML trained on small sets. Values are in figures only.
- **What carries the gain** (l. 249-250, 258, 260, 317): the ablation arms are ML, ML plus GPP, plus pretraining, plus structure, plus knowledge-guided loss. With larger real sets the GPP input contributes most; pretraining matters more when the real fine-tuning set is small and less with plentiful good observations; structure and loss add little. Pretraining cut the carbon mass-balance residual sharply; structure and loss pushed it near zero (l. 240).
- **Authors' cautions** (l. 413, 417, 427): choosing a well-validated process model is critical; explicit process models help most where there are few real samples; which process model is used for pretraining is untested.

## Dependence

- Trained on ecosys output; ecosys's carbon-allocation theory sets the network's hierarchical structure (l. 164), and a knowledge-guided loss is added (l. 260). The GPP input is a remote-sensing product. Authors in common with `liu2022kgml`, `jia2021` and `read2019` (a flag).

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **The strongest crop case for pretraining on a process model, and still a partial one:** a real-only arm exists and the text says it loses with few real samples, but the numbers are in figures, and the gain of pretraining alone is not separable from the GPP input and the structure in what was read. Do not quote a size of effect from the text.
- **D45 and TRUTH-METHOD:** the student's structure is taken from the teacher (l. 164), so it is kin to ecosys by construction; the teacher's own real-data score (0.49 and 0.42 on yield) is a measured gap between simulator and field, the kind an evaluation family would be asked to report.
- **Not shown:** a disease or a vine, a second process model as teacher (l. 427), or any hidden simulator variable judged against measurement.
