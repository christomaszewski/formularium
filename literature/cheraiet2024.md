# Cheraiet et al. 2024: an artificial vine test bench for vineyard sprayers

An adjustable artificial canopy that ranks vineyard sprayers. Its dye deposits matched those on real vines, and 65 sprayer settings fell into three behaviour classes.

## What it holds

- **Canopy:** adjustable in height and width. It mimics three growth stages of Royat cordon and Guyot training, with 2.5 m inter-row (l. 35-39).
- **Tracer:** tartrazine dye, rinsed from artificial leaves, under the ISO 22522:2007 sampling approach (l. 354-356, l. 40-42).
- **Real vines:** deposit patterns and mean deposits on artificial and real vines were comparable (l. 44-46).
- **Ranking:** a PCA of 65 spraying modalities gave three behaviour classes (l. 47-49).

## Dependence

- ISO 22522:2007 sampling standard (l. 103-105) and the tartrazine tracer it recommends (l. 354-355).
- Earlier sampling protocols by Codis et al. 2018 and Cheraiet et al. 2021 (l. 100, 107).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t1 (spray deposition efficiency):** the three-stage canopy and the deposit-per-foliage idea could feed a simulator. The output is a sprayer ranking, not a canopy dose curve.
