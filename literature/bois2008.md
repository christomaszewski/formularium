# Bois et al. 2008: satellite solar radiation for daily reference ET, and what E0 is sensitive to

Daily FAO-56 Penman-Monteith E0 at 19 French stations is compared with three radiation formulae driven by satellite irradiation (HelioClim-1) and with the Hargreaves temperature formula, with a Sobol sensitivity analysis of PM.

**Read this first.** The equations are standard (FAO-56, Hargreaves, Turc, Priestley-Taylor); the paper's own results are the error figures. HelioClim-1 is 30 km, 3-hourly source data, not a field-scale product. Reference E0 is PM computed from the same stations, not a lysimeter. The file name says 2007 (online); the printed year is 2008 (volume 148).

## What it holds

- **Data:** 19 INRA-Agroclim Cimel stations, daily 2000-2004: 8 in the Southwest (oceanic; Villenave d'Ornon 44.789 N, 25 m, is one) and 11 in the Southeast (Mediterranean). Min and max T and RH, class 2 pyranometer, cup anemometer at 2 m (l. 111-118). Mean PM E0 per station 2.29 to 2.55 mm per day (SW), 2.45 to 3.41 (SE) (Table 1). Average T is the mean of min and max.
- **Equations printed (daily):**
  - FAO-56 PM: E = [0.408 Delta Rn + gamma 900/(T+273) u2 (es - ea)] / [Delta + gamma (1 + 0.34 u2)], Rn = Rns - Rnl, Rns = (1 - 0.23) Rs, Rnl by the FAO-56 form with 0.34 - 0.14 sqrt(ea) and 1.35 Rs/Rso - 0.35, Rso = (0.75 + 2e-5 z) Ra, ea from RHmax at Tmin and RHmin at Tmax, Delta = 4098 e0(T) / (T + 237.3)^2 (the text prints 237.2, garbled) (Eq. 1-7).
  - Hargreaves radiation, E_HR = 0.0135 (Rs / lambda)(T + 17.8), lambda = 2.45 MJ per kg (Eq. 8).
  - Hargreaves temperature, E_HT = 0.0023 (Ra / lambda)(T + 17.8) sqrt(Tmax - Tmin) (Eq. 9; page image, the root is lost in the text).
  - Turc, E_TU = 0.013 (23.88 Rs + 50) T / (T + 15), Rs in MJ per m2 (Eq. 10; page image).
  - Priestley-Taylor, E_PT = alpha Delta / (Delta + gamma) Rn, alpha 1.26, with ea = 0.611 exp(17.27 Tmin / (Tmin + 237.3)) (Eq. 11-12).
- **Sensitivity (Sobol, 10 000 samples per month, Tables 2, l. 330-348):** the PM variance split by T, daily T range, Rs, RH, daily RH range and u2. January SW: wind 38 %, RH 32 %, T 17 %, Rs 6 %. July SW: Rs 70 %, T 13 %, wind 9 %, RH 5 %. April to September E0 is mostly set by Rs (up to 74 % in May). SE is much the same (July: Rs 71 %).
- **HelioClim-1 against pyranometer (Table 3):** daily irradiation bias -1.87 MJ per m2 (-14 %) and RMSE 2.67 (20 %) in the SW, bias -1.07 (-7 %) and RMSE 2.16 (14 %) in the SE; in July the RMSE is 15 % (SW) and 10 % (SE), in January 26 % and 18 % (l. 368-369). Errors are largest on partly cloudy days.
- **Error carried into E0:** PM with HelioClim-1 instead of the pyranometer, RMSE 0.25 mm (11 %) SW and 0.21 mm (7 %) SE; the radiation formulae 0.34 to 0.49 mm (12 to 19 %) (Table 3).
- **Daily E0 against PM (Table 5, annual):** SW: PT R2 0.938, bias -0.29, RMSE 0.52 mm; TU 0.914, -0.30, 0.58; HR 0.906, -0.21, 0.59; HT 0.894, +0.32, 0.67. SE: HR RMSE 0.77 mm (25 %), TU 0.84, PT 0.86, HT 0.96 mm (31 %). July SW: PT 0.50 mm (11 %), HT 0.96 mm (22 %). In summer radiation formulae beat the temperature formula; in January HT is as good or better.

## Dependence

- Computes FAO-56 PM (Allen 1998), Hargreaves, Turc and Priestley-Taylor; uses HelioClim-1 / Heliosat-2 (Rigollier 2004, Lefevre 2007). No equation of the engine's disease models is involved.
- Authors: Van Leeuwen and Pieri co-author with the groups of vanleeuwen2023 and pieri2003; a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- **Agrarium weather and microclimate:** a source of E0 and of error sizes for a station that lacks radiation: if the sensor model gives Rs with 14 to 20 % daily RMSE, E0 by PM inherits 7 to 11 % RMSE, and by a radiation formula 12 to 19 %. These are daily figures in France, not hourly.
- **Candidate formulations:** Hargreaves temperature and radiation, Turc and Priestley-Taylor as cheap E0 options with their printed coefficients above; the paper does not validate any of them against a lysimeter.
- **Not shown:** in-canopy conditions, hourly values, leaf wetness.
