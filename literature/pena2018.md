# Peña Quiñones 2018: radius of influence of daily temperature at 158 stations

A doctoral thesis; chapter 2 gives how far from a weather station its daily temperature stays within a tolerance.

**Read this first.**
- A thesis, not peer-reviewed as such. The per-kilometre difference below is the reading agent's inference, not printed. The reading agent noted that the journal version of the chapter was not searched.

## What it holds

- **Mean radius of influence (RI) (l. 1457):** 20 km for daily minimum and 23 km for daily maximum temperature, from 158 AgWeatherNet stations in the Pacific Northwest, 2017 (the quote shows the minimum RI of 20 km was significantly different from the maximum RI).
- **General value (l. 1910):** the general RI for a daily minimum at a weather station is 20 km, 3 km shorter than for the maximum (the quote is cut off after "3").
- **Definition (l. 1748, eq. 5):** RI = MT / m, with m the slope of cumulated absolute daily difference against cumulated distance for the 15 nearest stations, and MT a 1 deg C tolerance (reading agent). So the mean absolute daily difference is about 1/RI per km, about 0.05 deg C/km at RI 20 km (inference, not printed).
