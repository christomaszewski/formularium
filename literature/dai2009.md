# COST 858 final meeting, Bordeaux 2009: two abstracts with Pieri (a berry growth model; heat and light on berries)

**Read this first.** The file is the whole book of abstracts (HAL hal-02752739, printed pages up to about 97 plus the index; the text starts with the Dai abstract's HAL cover). Pieri is an author of two abstracts, each one page, with no figures, tables or equations.

## What it holds

- **P10, p. 67, Dai et al.:** a biophysical berry growth model "originally designed for peach (Fishman and Genard 1998) was successfully adapted to grape" (l. 3254), coupled with the Genard and Souty 1996 sugar sub-model and published as Dai et al. 2008 and 2009. It treats leaf-to-fruit ratio and water supply. No equation, parameter or temperature input is printed; the climate-change sentence says warming would act on the berry "directly (by modifying berry microclimate)".
- **P24, p. 81, Kappel et al.:** an effect of defoliation, sun exposure and east/west side on berries after veraison (l. 3897); sampling 0, 1, 4, 7 and 14 days after defoliation; sun-exposed west berries against shaded east berries. About 50 genes differed; some "closely correlate with cumulated berry temperature difference after defoliation or with daily berry temperature difference between the two conditions" (l. 3906). No berry temperatures, sensors, heights, site or year are printed.

## Dependence

- P10 shares its model with Fishman and Genard 1998 (not held). P24 is a first report of the work that became pillet2012 and ouakedlecourieux2017 (same group, same berry-temperature framing).
- Authors: Pieri and Vivin appear in vivin-group papers; a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- **Nothing to build from:** neither abstract holds a number Agrarium can use. P24 shows that Pieri's group measured berry temperature on both sides of a row after defoliation in 2009, but the data are in Pieri and Fermaud 2005 (not in this collection) and later papers.
- The rest of the volume was not read; the volume's contents list shows Botrytis and downy mildew abstracts by other authors (for example Evers et al. on Botrytis management, p. 71) that this reading did not open.
