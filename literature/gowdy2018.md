# Gowdy et al. 2018: predawn potential and berry delta13C against modelled soil water, in a 52-variety garden

Preliminary results from the VitAdapt common garden: a Lebon-type water balance model gives FTSW, and berry delta13C (39 varieties, 2012-2016) and predawn potential (8 varieties, 2017) are compared with it.

**Read this first.** Preliminary, a short conference paper, with the soil water model run with a TTSW of 160 mm that the authors say is hard to estimate (absolute FTSW probably underestimated). No equation of the model is printed; it is Lebon et al. 2003.

## What it holds

- **Vineyard (l. 99-125):** VitAdapt at ISVV, Villenave d'Ornon (44 47 N, 0 34 W), planted 2009; 52 varieties in randomised blocks, 10 plants per variety per block, 5 blocks; double Guyot, 100 cm by 180 cm, VSP, canopy about 160 cm; SO4 clone 761; gravelly soil.
- **Water balance (l. 133-151):** Lebon et al. 2003 model, daily, 2012-2017; inputs phenology, canopy dimensions, porosity, orientation, hedging dates, solar radiation, ET0, temperature, rain; TTSW = 160 mm; output FTSW; FTSWBR = mean FTSW from veraison to 75 % of berry sugar accumulation.
- **delta13C (l. 152-180):** berry juice at one mature date per variety and year, 2012-2016; 2012 and 2016 had lower FTSWBR and less negative delta13C (more stomatal closure) than 2013-2015. Power-function fits of delta13C against FTSWBR (similar exponents to a van Genuchten retention curve); the wet to dry difference is 3 to 4 per mil for many varieties (3.2 and 3.4 for Petit Verdot and Tinto Cao).
- **Predawn potential (l. 220-232):** in 2017, predawn leaf water potential was never below -0.16 MPa while modelled FTSW was above 0.4, then dropped sharply after 28 July as FTSW fell under 0.4; the threshold matches the literature. Semillon differed significantly from Grenache, Petit Verdot and Cabernet-Sauvignon late in the season.
- **Measurements:** midday leaf, midday stem and predawn leaf potential (pressure chamber) on 8 vines per variety (2 vines in each of 4 blocks) for 8 varieties from 2017. Hydroscape (midday against predawn) started for Semillon; midday potential reached about -3.2 MPa at predawn -1.4 MPa in the hypothetical boundary of Fig. 5.

## Dependence

- Lebon et al. 2003 (not held; uses riou1989's interception model), Farquhar 1989, Meinzer et al. 2016. Same garden and group as gowdy2022 and gowdy2022b.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- **Vine water status:** a measured link between a modelled FTSW and predawn potential for a Bordeaux garden: no stress (above -0.16 MPa) until FTSW falls below about 0.4. A candidate check or break-point for Agrarium's predawn potential, with a TTSW of 160 mm to begin from; the base is a year of 8 varieties, not a fitted curve.
- **Not shown:** the model's parameters, variety-level curves, any disease or microclimate.
