# Jiye et al. 2010 (as printed): downy mildew and weather in Shandong, 2009

## What it holds

- One Cabernet Sauvignon site (Zhangyu Company base), 2009; incidence on 100 plants; seven
  peaks, the highest about 25 % in late July and early August.
- Incubation periods of 5-12 days, with infection days counted back from outbreaks (Table
  1).
- Spore traps every two hours; most sporangia at 08:00-10:00.
- Regressions on seven outbreaks (R² 0.998) and on sporangia (printed R 0.83 with R² 0.76,
  which disagree).

## Dependence

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A humid East Asian season peaking in midsummer. Context.
