# Escalona et al. 2003: whole-vine leaf area of irrigated and unirrigated vines in Mallorca

Leaf photosynthesis and transpiration by canopy location, and the leaf area of each
location, for Tempranillo and Manto Negro with and without irrigation, 1997 and 1999.
Read for its whole-vine leaf area, measured destructively.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 43-58): the commercial vineyard Ca´n Ribas, Mallorca; vines 20 years old in
  1997 on Richter 110; rows 2.5 m, vines 1.4 m (3.5 m² of ground a vine, my calculation),
  rows SE-NW. Tempranillo on a bilateral cordon with two wires at 0.7 and 1.1 m;
  Manto Negro in gobelet (four arms, 0.5 m high).
- **Stages** (l. 52-55; means of the two years): Tempranillo budburst 13 April, bloom
  11 May, veraison 12 July, ripeness 28 August; Manto Negro 21 April, 22 May, 27 July and
  10 September.
- **Water** (l. 98-102): unirrigated until 1992; then five rows a cultivar rainfed and five
  drip-irrigated June to August at about 30 % of pan evaporation, twice a week. Predawn
  water potential of rainfed vines fell to -0.2 to -0.4 MPa by late August (l. 144).
- **Leaf area method** (l. 86-97): all leaves of each of eight canopy locations collected
  immediately after harvest; area of 10 leaves by area meter, the rest by dry weight over
  specific leaf weight. The methods say 6 plants, the table captions 4 plants (l. 89, 299).
- **Whole-vine leaf area at harvest, m² a vine** (Tables 5-6, l. 306, 328):

  | | 1997 irrigated | 1997 rainfed | 1999 irrigated | 1999 rainfed |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Tempranillo (cordon) | 4.20 ± 0.77 | 4.12 ± 1.09 | 4.79 ± 1.00 | 2.60 ± 0.82 |
  | Manto Negro (gobelet) | 7.77 ± 1.88 | 4.81 ± 1.14 | 5.99 ± 0.85 | 2.47 ± 0.68 |

  Drought cut it by 1.9 % and 45.7 % in Tempranillo, 38.1 % and 58.8 % in Manto Negro.
  Over 3.5 m² of ground, rainfed Tempranillo is LAI 1.18 (1997) and 0.74 (1999), my
  calculation.
- **Inner canopy** (l. 252): location 8 holds 20 to 50 % of the leaf area. Senescent leaves
  were seen low in the canopy at sampling (l. 250-251).
- **Growth stop** (l. 257-264): Tempranillo's vegetative growth stopped in early July,
  Manto Negro's continued to the end of July.
- **Not printed:** harvest date, leaf area at any earlier date, shoots a vine, LAI, canopy
  height and width, main against lateral leaves, irrigation in mm.

## Dependence

- The eight-location scheme is Zufferey & Murisier's (1997); the irrigation rule follows
  Escalona et al. 1999 and Flexas et al. 2002. The leaf areas are the authors' own
  measurements. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **The nearest measured match to Agrarium's vineyards found:** Mediterranean, rainfed, a
  bilateral cordon with two wires, about 3.5 m² of ground a vine. Rainfed Tempranillo
  carried 2.6 to 4.1 m² a vine at harvest, after summer leaf loss; the season's maximum is
  not printed and is at least that. Used in Agrarium's research_notes/vine-leaf-area.
