# Cerruto 2007a: spray deposit through a Sicilian spur-cordon canopy at two airflow rates and volume rates

A second field study of an air-assisted sprayer in a Nero d'Avola vineyard, with deposit measured on leaves at several canopy depths and heights; little in it can be used for a deposit ratio because its layer labels are inconsistent.

**Read this first.** The words "internal" and "external" layer mean the sprayed and the unsprayed side of the tree in stage 1 but depth in the canopy in stage 2, so the direction of the stage 1 contrast (0.542 against 0.137 µL/cm²) is ambiguous. It is not used here as an inner/outer ratio. The CVs printed (24 % and 16 %) are ANOVA error CVs of transformed deposits, not the spread of raw leaf deposits. A water-and-dye tracer deposit, not fungicide.

## What it holds

- **Design (l. 82, 109):** spur-cordon Nero d'Avola, 2.50 x 1.25 m, tested at BBCH 57 (2003-05-15) and BBCH 77 (2003-07-04) (l. 82). Stage 1: leaves sampled on both sides of the tree (internal layer = sprayed side, external layer = unsprayed side); stage 2: on the sprayed side only; 6 leaves per zone, 576 and 864 leaves in all (l. 109).
- **Stage 1 means as printed (l. 210, 211):** average deposit on the "external" layer 0.542 µL/cm² and on the "internal" layer 0.137 µL/cm². With the paper's own labelling (internal = sprayed side) this would mean more on the far side, which is why the labelling is judged inconsistent.
- **Variability (l. 290):** ANOVA error CVs of the transformed deposit in stage 2: CV(1) 24 % and CV(2) 16 % (Table 4).

## Dependence

- **Own** field tests with the same sprayer as Cerruto 2005 (reference 2 of the paper). The companion paper is `cerruto2007b`. None recorded of the engine's models.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Deposit variability through a canopy (M4):** almost nothing to take. The two printed means cannot be turned into an inner/outer ratio, and the CVs are ANOVA terms. Use `cerruto2007b` for the inner/outer ratio of an airblast sprayer. Kind of result: field measurement with a dye tracer on vine leaves (an airblast sprayer, Sicily), too ambiguous to quote. Wash-off, decay, growth dilution and dose-efficacy: not addressed.
