# Kim et al. 2005: DMCast run on simulated weather over the Finger Lakes

Fetched 2026-10-12 from KoreaScience (a scan, read through OCR). The Cornell group's
test of running DMCast's secondary-infection model on a mesoscale weather model (LAWSS)
with SWEB wetness, instead of station data; the fullest printed sketch of DMCast's
structure held here (`magarey2007` and `kontogiannis2024` describe it second-hand).

## What it holds

- **The chain** (pp. 112-114): LAWSS, a simplified form of the MASS mesoscale model, nested
  from 9 km (90 × 90 cells over New York State) through 3, 1 and 0.333 km to 0.150 km
  grids, driven by the NCEP/NCAR 2.5° reanalysis (a model product), USGS 100 m terrain and
  1:250,000 land use; it produces hourly temperature, humidity, wind and net radiation
  but no rainfall. SWEB (Magarey 1999; Magarey et al. 2005), a big-leaf surface wetness
  model with four sub-modules and a transfer function "calibrated to determine surface
  wetness under controlled conditions", gives the canopy wet area; its leaf area index
  comes from NDVI as LAI = −0.25198 + 5.70850 × NDVI (Johnson et al. 2003; p. 114, read
  in the image); Wmax and the other plant parameters are Magarey's, values not printed.
- **DMCast** (Park et al. 1997, Vitic. Enol. Sci. 52:182-189), as Fig. 4 prints it (p. 114,
  image): sporulation when "Nighttime & RH ≥ 90", sp = f(temp); spore survival spmort =
  f(temp, RH), sv = sp × spmort; infection infect = f(temp, wetness); DMrisk = sv ×
  infect, each on 0-1, a warning for any hour with DMrisk above zero. The f() functions
  and their coefficients are not printed. Only the secondary cycle was run, for want of
  cultivar and phenology data, from 15 June to 15 August of 1987 (unfavourable), 1994
  (favourable) and 2003; wetness came from dew only.
- **Check against stations (2003):** daily means of temperature, RH, canopy wet area and
  DMrisk against the NEWA stations at Geneva and Fredonia; wetness above 0.1 agreed on
  53 % (Geneva) and 63 % (Fredonia) of 62 days and on 88 % and 76 % of non-rainy days;
  DMrisk above zero on 66 % and 58 %, 88 % and 73 % on non-rainy days (Table 2, p. 116);
  temperature "reasonably well", RH underestimated later in the season (Fig. 6). The
  differential map of 1994 minus 1987 warning days shows 2-10 more days along the lakes
  (Fig. 5, from the 1 km grid, p. 117).
- **Inconsistencies:** the abstract claims 0.150 km was reached, the results say the system
  finished only to 1 km (p. 117); the text's running times do not match Table 1 (p. 115).

## Dependence

- **Computes** DMCast (Park et al. 1997) and SWEB (Magarey) on LAWSS output; no data
  fitted here. SWEB's calibration data are cited, not printed.
- **Engine pieces:** DMCast's sporulation (night, RH ≥ 90 %) and survival-times-infection
  form are the family of `caffi2013.sporulation` and the Lalancette pieces; Magarey's SWEB
  is the physical wetness model `magarey2005` cites. Nothing fitted to observations.
- **Authors:** Seem (`gadoury1997`, Kennelly models), Magarey (`magarey2005`): flags.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- The structural sketch of DMCast and a measured agreement of simulated against station
  wetness and risk of 53-66 % of days (73-88 % without rain). For Agrarium: a precedent
  for driving a disease model with modelled rather than measured weather, and its error.
