# Raihani 2025: transposition models compared at Rabat

Transposition models with complete formulations are compared with two years of hourly measured tilted-plane irradiance in Rabat (l. 26-28). No record.

## What it holds

- **Data:** hourly measurements from a pyrheliometer and Kipp & Zonen SP-Lite pyranometers (l. 27-28), recorded 2017 to 2018 (l. 370).
- **Result:** no great discrepancy between the models for the same sky type (l. 30-31); Perez is best, with regression slope 0.9716, determination 0.9849, MBE -4.16 % and RMSE 9.29 % for all skies (l. 31-33).
- **Use:** the author names mountain-slope agronomy among the uses (l. 36-37).

## Dependence

- Evaluates Perez, Hay-Davies and other published transposition models; no new model.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s3 and s5:** a size of error, near 9 percent RMSE, for turning horizontal radiation into radiation on a tilted plane such as a slope or canopy facet. A simulator could take Perez as the default slope transposition. A flat-city site; no slope with vegetation.
