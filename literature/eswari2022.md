# Eswari et al. 2022: a mass-action yield model for Theni

The same author and seasons as [eswari2021](eswari2021.md).

## What it holds

- Three mass-action equations for climate, disease and yield, solved by homotopy
  perturbation; no weather enters the rates. The rates equal means of the field tables.
- "Validation" compares the closed form with a numerical run of the same equations; no
  yield is printed.
