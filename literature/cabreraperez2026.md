# Cabrera-Pérez et al. 2026: cover crops and mulch in an organic rainfed Penedès Chardonnay

Barley and ryegrass cover crops in the alleys and pine-bark mulch under the vines, against
tillage, at the Jean Leon estate (Familia Torres), 2020-2022. Read for vine vigour: the
vineyard is the closest to Agrarium's found.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 151-231): Torrelavit, DO Penedès, 200 m; Csa, 505 mm and 15.1 °C (2006-2022
  means); Chardonnay on 140Ru planted 2003 at 2.2 × 1.1 m; bilateral cordon, vertical on
  two catch wires; organic and rainfed. Winter-spring rain 53 % (2021) and 51 % (2022)
  below average (l. 319-320).
- **Vigour, alleys tilled against cover-cropped** (Tables 3A-3B, l. 422-443; 2021, 2022):
  shoots a vine 14.7 and 13.66 against 13.6 and 15.44; pruning weight 194 and 222 g against
  114 and 126 g (the methods say kg per vine, l. 275; the tables print g); shoot weight 13.2
  and 16.31 g against 8.4 and 7.98 g; clusters 12.2 and 13.3 against 11.3 and 14.3; yield
  0.69 and 0.84 against 0.53 and 0.56 kg a vine; Ravaz index 3.8 to 5.7.
- **Exposed leaf area** (Table 3A, l. 425-427; Smart & Robinson's protocol): tillage 8,673
  and 9,084, cover crop 7,692 and 7,152 (under-vine trial 9,769 to 10,265, l. 540-542),
  printed "m²/vine". That unit cannot hold. As m²/ha (Smart's usual unit) it is about 1.7 to
  2.5 m² of exposed wall a vine at 4,132 vines/ha (my reading and calculation): unsettled.
- **LiDAR** (l. 282-286, Tables 4, 7): canopy cross-section on 30 June, 0.133 to 0.227 m²,
  lower with cover crops.
- **Not printed:** total leaf area, LAI, canopy height and width, budburst and stage dates.

## Dependence

- Vigour protocol of Smart & Robinson 1991; LiDAR proxy after Cabrera-Pérez et al. 2023 and
  Escolà et al. 2023. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **The vineyard nearest Agrarium's:** Penedès, rainfed, cover in the alleys, bilateral
  cordon, about 14-15 shoots a vine. In two dry years it was low in vigour: 8 to 16 g of
  cane a shoot and 0.11 to 0.22 kg of prunings a vine. With Smart et al. 1990's cited 50
  to 100 cm² of leaf per g of cane, that is 0.6 to 2.2 m² of leaf a vine (my calculation, a
  loose bound), the low end of everything else read. It argues for a range reaching down
  to about 1.5 m² in dry years under cover.
