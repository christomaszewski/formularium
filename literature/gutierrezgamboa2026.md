# Gutierrez-Gamboa et al. 2026: shade nets and leaf removal in east-west Chardonnay

One season of east-west Chardonnay with a control, basal leaf removal, a black Raschel net and a black-white photoselective net; cluster-zone PAR, temperature and humidity are recorded and sunburn scored.

## What it holds

- **Site:** Molina, Maule Region, Chile; 2023-2024 season; vertical shoot-positioned vines, row azimuth about 115.8 degrees, 2.3 m by 1.0 m (l. 133-134, 140-143). The triage record's site and the missing season are corrected here.
- **Design:** three replicates per treatment; leaf removal took six basal leaves from the southern face; nets give 35 % (Raschel) and 16 % (photoselective) nominal shade and hang on the south-facing side (l. 148-163).
- **Result:** PAR at the cluster is 124.0 % higher under leaf removal than under the Raschel net, 112.6 % higher than under the photoselective net and 80.9 % higher than the control (l. 39-41); the photoselective net removed afternoon temperatures above 35 C but had the highest sunburn severity (l. 41-43).
- **Heat metrics:** hours above 30 and 35 C are counted (l. 186).

## Dependence

- Builds on bioclimatic and heat-risk indices, PAR and shade-net optics (l. 82-93); no model is borrowed.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strand s4 context: measured cluster-zone temperature and PAR by treatment and by the south face of an east-west row; a treatment experiment, not a multi-logger survey of slope, aspect or row side, and no wetness.
