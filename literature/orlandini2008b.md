# Orlandini, Dalla Marta & Mattii 2008: Sangiovese on two trellis systems, with a daily carbon-balance model

Field measurements of shoot growth, leaf area, light interception and yield on single
cordon and lyre Sangiovese in 2001 and 2002, with a Stella model of daily photosynthesis
minus respiration that fits the cordon and underestimates the lyre. (The file is named
simone_2015; the paper is 2008, l. 1.)

## What it holds

- **Site:** eight-year-old Sangiovese on 420A near Montalcino, south Tuscany (43°03' N,
  11°29' E), 3 x 0.5 m, 6,667 vines/ha, soil 42 % clay, annual rain about 450-500 mm;
  5 buds a vine on the cordon, 10 on the lyre (l. 29, 68-76, 90). Destructive sampling at
  day 155 (flowering), 178, 211 (veraison, 30 July) and 265 (harvest, 22 September)
  (l. 72-74). Harvests 18 September 2001 and 16 September 2002 (l. 103-104).
- **Leaf area:** mean single leaf 142 cm² on the cordon and 74.3 cm² on the lyre, both
  years (l. 180-181). Shoot growth stopped in the second half of July 2001 (l. 199).
- **Light interception:** 2002 maxima 24.3 % (cordon) and 31.6 % (lyre) as daily
  averages; 2001 maxima 52 % (lyre) and 32 % (cordon) (l. 244-247).
- **Yield and clusters** (Table, l. 301-303): yield a vine 2001 2.23 kg lyre, 1.13 kg
  cordon; 2002 2.7 and 1.5 kg. Clusters a vine 6.9 against 5.0 (2001), 11.3 against 7.0
  (2002). Cluster weight 323 against 226 g (2001), 243 against 217 g (2002).
- **Model:** Stella 7.0.3, three submodules (photosynthesis, respiration, biomass), daily
  step from budbreak to before harvest, output g dry matter a vine (l. 34, 120, 147, 183).
  Temperature enters as a rate effect on photosynthesis and an exponential in respiration
  (l. 168-179). The cordon fits; lyre biomass is underestimated, by nearly half at the end
  (l. 445-446, 479).
- **Not printed:** every equation and coefficient (the "several coefficients" of l. 163-165
  are not given), parameter units, starting values. The model cannot be run from the paper.

## Dependence

- **Borrowed structure,** as the paper says: adapted from Lakso's apple dry-matter model
  (Lakso & Johnson 1990; Lakso et al. 2000; l. 123-129), organ temperatures after Lakso &
  Johnson, the photosynthesis temperature effect after Charles-Edwards 1982, hedgerow
  extinction coefficients from Poni et al. 2006 (l. 164-172, 177-178).
- **Fitting data:** none stated. Mattii's co-authorship of cited papers is a flag only.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **Carbon balance, not thermal time:** photosynthesis minus respiration on a daily step,
  not the engine's growth on thermal time. With no printed equations it yields no record.
- **Usable part:** the measured numbers (single leaf area 142 against 74 cm², clusters a
  vine, light interception maxima, shoot growth stopping in late July 2001), as observed
  values for two trellis systems at one site.
