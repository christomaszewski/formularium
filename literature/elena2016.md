# Elena & Luque 2016: pruning-wound susceptibility in Catalonia

Research article, Catalonia, northeast Spain.

## What it holds

- Trunk-disease study: susceptibility of pruning wounds to D. seriata and P. chlamydospora fell from 97.5% and 75% at first inoculation to about 10% 12 weeks after pruning, with Table 2 correlations to weather and cane colonization versus internode length.
- Results are in figures and a wood-disease context, not foliar epidemics.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Wound susceptibility falls to about 10% twelve weeks after pruning: a fact for a trunk-disease module, which neither tool has.
