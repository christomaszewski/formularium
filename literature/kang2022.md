# Kang et al. 2022: six satellite LAI methods against ground LAI in four California vineyards

## What it holds

- **Finding:** radiative-transfer approaches predict low to medium LAI well but underestimate high LAI in highly clumped vine canopies, RMSE about 0.97-1.27 (l. 24-25).
- **Empirical:** Cubist regressions trained on ground LAI from all vineyards reach RMSE about 0.3-0.48 (l. 25-26), but do not generalise between sites (abstract).
- **Sensitivity:** ET from the TSEB model is more sensitive to positive than negative LAI bias (abstract); Sentinel-2 red-edge bands add LAI information.
