# Kogovšek et al. 2017: LAMP test for bois noir phytoplasma

A real-time LAMP assay for bois noir phytoplasma on crude leaf-vein homogenate, checked against qPCR on 286 field samples from 2015. The paper was published online in 2016.

## What it holds

- The secA LAMP assay gave a positive result in under 35 minutes, without DNA extraction, and was as sensitive as qPCR (l. 22–24, 43).
- In the 2015 survey of 286 samples, LAMP gave the right sanitary status for 284 (99.3 %). Two samples were misread (l. 379–380).
- Validated against EPPO (2014) recommendations (l. 229–230).

## Dependence

- Uses the FDp LAMP assay of Kogovšek et al. 2015 alongside (l. 109–110). The qPCR reference is Hren et al. 2007 (l. 67–68).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t4 as a diagnostic, not a model: a 35-minute field test for bois noir, with no input for a simulator.
