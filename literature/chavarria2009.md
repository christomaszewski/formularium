# Chavarria et al. 2009: airborne sporangia under plastic cover and in the open

## What it holds

- **Site:** Moscato Giallo at Flores da Cunha, Serra Gaúcha (541 m), 2005/06 and 2006/07;
  12 covered rows against five open ones.
- **Traps:** one Burkard per area (the abstract says two), hourly counts on 20 days a cycle,
  October to April.
- **Counts:** covered 834 per day (34.8 an hour) against open 447 (18.6); covered higher at
  some hours.
- **Sprays:** open 17 and 15 against downy mildew; covered none. Free water under the cover
  was nil.
- **Microclimate regressions** (cover against open): temperatures with slopes 0.90-0.97,
  RH 0.85, wind 0.11. Correlations of spores with weather are weak (|r| 0.40-0.54).

## Dependence

- None. The oospore rule quoted (above 10 °C during rain above 10 mm) is uncited.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Under cover sporangia are airborne but leaves stay dry and no disease is reported: a
  check that a truth's infection needs wetness, not inoculum alone.
