# Pereira et al. 2018: downy mildew on 'BRS Vitória' at Marialva, Paraná

## What it holds

- **Site:** Marialva, northern Paraná (602 m), table grape BRS Vitória, two crops a year.
  Only the summer-autumn crops of 2014 and 2015 had epidemics; winter-spring 2013 and 2014
  had none.
- **First symptoms:** 25 February 2014 and 20 February 2015.
- **Untreated control:** maximum leaf severity 17.3 ± 16.4 % (2014) and 21.3 ± 7.7 %
  (2015). Weekly sprays 1.3 and 8.5 %; the grower programme (28-32 sprays) 0 and 6.2 %.
  Bunches stayed clean in every treatment.
- **Weather:** a uMETOS station inside the vineyard; 754 mm on 41 rainy days (2014) and 830
  mm on 52 (2015) in the summer-autumn crop.
- No progress model or AUDPC.

## Dependence

- None.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A subtropical double-cropping case: one crop escapes, the other has an epidemic. A
  season-timing pattern far outside the engine's calibration.
