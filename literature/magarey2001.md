# Magarey et al. 2001: site-specific weather without on-site sensors

Review, Mostly USA (New York, Pennsylvania/SkyBit E-Weather); author affiliation also lists Australia.

## What it holds

- Feature article on obtaining site-specific weather without on-site sensors: spatial interpolation, mesoscale forecast models, and canopy-model use, plus leaf-wetness difficulties.
- It quotes a few interpolation errors (daily mean temperature as low as 0.2 °C; high-RH events predicted within 1 h on 83% of occasions; forecasts add 0.3 °C and 3% RH) but gives no equations.
