# MAPA 2026: sixteen fungicides authorised on vine against downy mildew in Spain

The Spanish Ministry's pesticide register, read for 16 products in force ("Vigente") on vine and Plasmopara viticola: active ingredients, maximum applications, interval and pre-harvest interval (PHI), and what each label says about preventive and post-infection use.

**Read this first.**
- The register is official but the label text is the registrant's. It is not Catalan guidance (no DARP or Penedès bulletin was found).
- The register never states a curative window in hours or days. Labels say "preventive" (before the disease establishes) except XANILO 45 WG, which also claims post-infection activity.
- The 16 are those the strand queried by active. Ametoctradin, pyraclostrobin, azoxystrobin and copper were not queried; dimethomorph may sit under another crop node (not checked).

- **Counts by active (grid total, vine + P. viticola, in force):** fosetyl-Al 19; folpet 10; potassium phosphonates 7; zoxamide 6; cymoxanil 5; mandipropamid 4; cyazofamid 3; metalaxyl-M 2; amisulbrom 1; oxathiapiprolin 1 (only Zorvec Vinabel); fluopicolide 1; benalaxyl-M 1.
- **Not listed:** no product with dimethomorph, iprovalicarb, benthiavalicarb, valifenalate, mancozeb, dithianon or fluazinam is listed for vine downy mildew (the grid returned no results on 2026-10-30).
- **Curative wording:** the register states no curative window. Only XANILO 45 WG claims post-infection activity (l. 2746); FOLPAN GOLD, with metalaxyl-M, is preventive only on its label (l. 1495).
- **Highest and lowest limits:** FOLDER 80 WG up to 10 applications at 10 d; REVUS 4 per season; DARAMUN (cyazofamid) and AMPEXIO C 1 per season.

## Dependence

- An official register; label text is the registrant's. No model, no paper. Not Catalan guidance.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **What a Penedès grower can legally spray (Agrarium M4 grower; Cooptera E6):** the actives, the maximum applications per season, the minimum interval (7 to 14 d) and the PHI (15 to 41 d) above. Kind of evidence: the official register as of 2026-10-30.
- **Treatment protection (E6):** the label wording is "preventive" nearly everywhere, so the register cannot supply a curative window; those come from trials (see nanni2016).
- **Rain rule:** the only quantified rain wording read is Zorvec's 20 min after the spray has dried (l. 2883).
