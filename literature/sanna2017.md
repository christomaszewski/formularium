# Sanna 2017: measurement uncertainty in a downy mildew forecast

## What it holds

- Thesis supervised by Calvo, co-tutor Merlone (INRiM). Chapter 3 runs Strizyk's EPI model
  on calibrated and uncalibrated station data at Vezzolano (Monferrato), 2011-2013; chapter
  4 is Sanna et al. 2018.
- **Dating infections (pp. 75-76):** the most probable infection period was found by going
  back from the first symptoms 'according to Giosuè et al. (2002)'; incubation 'calculated
  as a function of air temperature, using two regression equations accounting for both low
  and high level of relative humidity, adapted to the evaluation table of the incubation
  period of Goidànich (1957)', summed daily to 1 (Caffi et al. 2011). The regressions'
  coefficients are not printed.
- **Effect of calibration:** the infection period fell on 28-29 April with calibrated data,
  4-5 days earlier with uncalibrated ones; temperature differences up to 1.07 °C, RH 3-14 %.

## Dependence

- Infection dates in chapter 3 were back-calculated with the Giosuè/Caffi incubation
  regressions: an observation shaped by a model kin to the engine (Goidanich's data).
