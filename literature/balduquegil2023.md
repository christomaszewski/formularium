# Balduque-Gil et al. 2023: machine learning for grapevine moth flights

Machine-learning models of Lobesia botrana flights trained on field observations and
half-hourly weather, against the Touzeau temperature-development model; a neural network
gave the higher F1. An insect pest with no disease content: off-topic for Formularium,
of interest only to Cooptera's pest models. Recorded so that it is not read again.
