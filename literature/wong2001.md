# Wong & Wilcox 2001: what a protectant, a systemic and a strobilurin do to P. viticola, by timing

Riesling seedlings in growth chambers, sprayed to runoff at fixed days before or after
inoculation with P. viticola (2 × 10^4 sporangia/ml), no rain or weathering: the
grape downy mildew reference for how long a spray protects and how late it still acts.

## What it holds

- **Protectant mode:** azoxystrobin (250 µg/ml), mancozeb (1,790 µg/ml) and metalaxyl (260
  µg/ml) each gave complete control when applied 1 to 5 days before inoculation (abstract,
  l. 16, 29, 34-35).
- **Post-infection:** metalaxyl gave complete control 1 day after inoculation, and 46 %
  less severity and 94 % less sporulation when applied 3-5 days after (l. 35-36).
  Azoxystrobin changed incidence little but cut colony area 47 % and sporulation 96 % up to
  5 days after (l. 19). Mancozeb had little post-infection effect on incidence; it reduced
  sporulation 38 % after infection and 89 % after symptoms (l. 29-33).
- **Post-symptom:** azoxystrobin cut resporulation 85 % over 1-14 days after a spray 6 days
  after inoculation (l. 21-22); metalaxyl 84 % (l. 38).
- **Translaminar:** azoxystrobin on the upper surface gave 94 % control of lower-surface
  inoculation 7 days later, under 50 % at 1 and 3 days (l. 24); vapour activity was weak
  (l. 27).
- **Design:** seedlings from open-pollinated Riesling seed (l. 107-108); six replicate
  seedlings per treatment, each experiment run three times (l. 73-74); isolates from
  Gewürztraminer leaves (l. 96).

## Dependence

- None: its own controlled inoculations; no model dated or timed them.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Cooptera's E6 and Agrarium's grower: the grape downy mildew magnitudes for a
  protection-window rule. Protectants hold for at least 5 days without rain (the longest
  tested); only the systemic (metalaxyl) cures, fully at 1 day and partly at 3-5 days;
  strobilurins and mancozeb after infection mostly cut sporulation, not incidence.
- Weathering, rain and new growth were not tested; field protection periods need the
  residue and wash-off sources (ishiguro1992, lewis2017).
