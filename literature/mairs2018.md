# Mairs 2018: a Bacillus mycoides supplement in a reduced fungicide programme, Kentucky

A one-season field trial in Kentucky compares a 14-day and a 28-day fungicide programme, with and without the biocontrol supplement LifeGard, and reports no disease rating.

## What it holds

- **Trial:** Bowling Green, Kentucky, 2017; 9-year-old Chambourcin vines at 1 350 vines per
  ha (l. 513-523); three replicate blocks of four treatments (l. 166-167); harvest on 29
  September (l. 568).
- **Treatments:** an untreated control; a Midwest Fruit Pest Management Guide (2017) programme
  on 14-day intervals; the same with 140 g/ha LifeGard; and a 28-day programme with 140 g/ha
  LifeGard (l. 158-166, 533-541).
- **Yield:** total yield did not differ by treatment (l. 684-686); the traditional and reduced
  programmes both gave 16.6 t/ha (l. 692). Brix and titratable acidity did not differ
  (l. 179-181).
- **Quality:** treated plots had 82 % more grade 1 fruit (0 to 33 % cluster damage) than the
  control (l. 907-908). The paper reads this as disease, but no disease rating is reported;
  the grades are the only disease-related measure (l. 899-901).

## Dependence

- **Builds on** the Midwest Fruit Pest Management Guide (2017) programme (l. 533-535) and
  LifeGard, Bacillus mycoides isolate J (l. 515-516). Analysis in SAS PROC GLIMMIX (l. 174-175).
