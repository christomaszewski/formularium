# Levrault, Bancal, Pieri and Bosc 2011: regional summaries of the CLIMATOR vine runs (English)

English twin of levrault2010 (same chapters, same figures); only what differs or adds is listed.

**Read this first.** No downy mildew (no "mildew" in the text). Botrytis appears as one quoted result (l. 796).

## What it holds

- **Limits (l. 179):** models ignore slope.
- **Feasibility (l. 238):** Chardonnay possible at all stations in the distant future; Grenache noir up to the present northern limit of vine growing (Figure 1 uses STICS and the WT downscaling).
- **Night coolness (l. 254):** a mean September minimum above about 14 C limits accumulation of phenolic and aromatic compounds (Tonietto and Carbonneau 2004, not held); the runs exceed it at Avignon in the near future and at Bordeaux, Toulouse and Avignon in the distant future.
- **Centre-North (l. 796):** new feasibility with a low to moderate Botrytis risk. **South-West (l. 2092, 2111):** the QQ downscaling gives more variability than WT; Lusignan soon suitable for merlot. **South-East (l. 2314):** Avignon, grenache.

## Dependence

- Same runs as brisson2010. The night coolness threshold is from Tonietto and Carbonneau 2004.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- Duplicate in English of levrault2010; keeps the 14 C night coolness threshold as a quoted quality limit. Nothing for the truth's disease.
