# Kandel et al. 2019: a review of spinach downy mildew

A review of the Peronospora effusa disease cycle, with new work on oospore germination, seed
  infestation and windborne sporangia caught in spore traps in coastal California. Title and
  author list are not printed in the text layer; the record's author field named only the
  corresponding author.

## What it holds

- **Aim:** the disease cycle in light of new findings on oospore germination, seed infestation
  with oospores and root infection, plus spore-trap work in coastal California (l. 22-31).
- **Sporangia:** germinate over 5 to 25 C, less at higher temperature (l. 262-264); sunny
  conditions cut germination; airborne P. effusa DNA is always present but rises from March
  through October in the Salinas Valley (l. 280-283); cold nights and coastal fog favour
  windborne survival (l. 284-287).
- **Not extracted:** oospore counts or germination rates.

## Dependence

- Cites Choudhury et al. 2016, 2017 for the Salinas Valley spore-trap series and Klosterman et
  al. 2014 for the airborne assay; not read.
