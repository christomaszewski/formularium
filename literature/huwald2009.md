# Huwald et al. 2009: albedo and radiative error of shielded air temperature

Naturally ventilated multiplate shields are set against sonic anemometer temperature over snow
  and vegetation; errors grow with reflected shortwave radiation, so albedo is the key variable
  for a correction.

## What it holds

- **Design:** shielded sensors against sonic temperature, winter seasons 2007 (5 February to 10
  April) and 2008 (8 February to 11 March) (l. 103-104); Pt100 and thermocouple at the sonic
  comparison sites (Table 1, l. 275-281).
- **Result:** over snow, 30 min mean differences up to 10 C; errors fall with less solar
  radiation and more wind but do not vanish with good ventilation (abstract, l. 17-21).
- **Result:** errors grow faster with reflected than with incident shortwave; an extension of a
  similarity regression with albedo is proposed (l. 21-28). Correction equations (4) and (5)
  were not extracted.

## Dependence

- Extends the similarity regression for radiative error of Nakamura and Mahrt 2005, as cited;
  uses sonic anemometer temperature as the reference.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s5 (sensor error): a radiative error of shielded air temperature that depends on reflected
  shortwave, so on ground albedo and canopy cover. A simulator of sensor error in a vineyard
  (grass, soil, canopy) could take the form of the correction, not the snow numbers. Not read
  in full.
