# Viret & Spring 2014: Changins oospore maturity from 9 May 2014

A Swiss vintage review that gives, in one sentence, the date the winter eggs (oospores) of downy mildew matured at Changins in 2014 and when the first oil spots were seen.

**Read this first.**
- The method of the 9 May date is not stated here ("œufs d'hiver", winter eggs). The same group's 2017 to 2021 reviews write "germination en 24 heures" for their dates, so it is probably the laboratory test, but this paper does not say so.
- The first spots are a regional statement ("vers fin mai", around the end of May), not a Changins date.

## What it holds

- **Oospore maturity (l. 278):** the winter eggs of downy mildew reached maturity from 9 May at Changins, when the foliage was already well developed, 4 to 5 leaves unfolded, BBCH 14-15 (l. 278 to 279).
- **Season (l. 275 to 277):** the mildew was held back early in the season by warm, dry conditions and grew in summer and at the end of the season.
- **First oil spots (l. 280):** seen "vers fin mai" (around the end of May) in the Lake Geneva basin and in German-speaking Switzerland.

## Dependence

- **Own observation** of the Changins group, as the same authors' series; method not stated. If it is the laboratory test, Dubuis et al. 2012 say that test serves to validate VitiMeteo.
