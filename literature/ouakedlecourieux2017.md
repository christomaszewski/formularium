# Ouaked-Lecourieux et al. 2017: a local +8 C on Cabernet Sauvignon clusters at three stages delays veraison and cuts anthocyanin

**Read this first.** Same rig as pillet2012, now 14 days at three stages, over three years. It is a manipulation, not a measurement of vineyard microclimate. The paper's journal header says "Lecourieux et al."; the first author on the cover is Ouaked-Lecourieux, hence the id.

## What it holds

- **Plants and rig (l. 189-203):** Cabernet Sauvignon fruiting cuttings in 0.5 L pots in a greenhouse; sets of 25 per condition; fan heaters of 1000 W blowing on bunches only, with polystyrene deflectors shielding shoots, leaves and roots; control bunches under fan blowers with temperature "closely followed greenhouse ambient air temperature". Heating 07:00 to 19:00 for 14 days at middle-green, veraison and middle-ripening (GHT, VHT, RHT).
- **Sensors (l. 204-207):** flesh temperature by copper-constantan thermocouples inserted in berries, 10 replicates per treatment, Campbell datalogger, continuous.
- **Temperatures (l. 288-294):** average daytime berry flesh temperature of controls 26.5 +/- 1.4 (green), 25.8 +/- 2.2 (veraison), 26.2 +/- 2.5 C (ripe); heated 34.7 +/- 1.4, 33.7 +/- 1.9, 35.2 +/- 2.5 C; mean differences 8.2, 7.9 and 9.0 C (Figure 1B; differences at l. 297-299).
- **Field reference (l. 757):** "the temperature of exposed Merlot berry can exceed air temperature by more than 10 C", clusters directly in sun (Pieri and Fermaud 2005). Introduction (l. 140): berry temperature depends on row orientation and differs between east and west sides for north-south rows.
- **Biological results (abstract):** heat delayed veraison when applied at the herbaceous stage; anthocyanin at maturity fell about 50 % in VHT and RHT (Table 1 text); more than 7000 genes were deregulated in at least one of nine conditions.
- **Data:** microarray raw data at GEO accession GSE86551.

## Dependence

- The rig is that of pillet2012 (Pillet et al. 2012), and the data are the transcriptome pools reused in lecourieux2020. The Pieri and Fermaud 2005 reference is the field source for the 10 C excess.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- **For a berry temperature module:** it gives a field figure only second hand (exposed Merlot berry more than 10 C above ambient, Pieri and Fermaud 2005); the primary source, Acta Hortic 689:109-116, doi 10.17660/ActaHortic.2005.689.9, is not in this collection and is the paper to get. No disease and no leaf wetness here. Not shown: any air-to-berry relation or sensor height.
