# Davy et al. n.d.: Optidose trials of adjusted fungicide doses

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A dose table scales fungicide dose by canopy, pressure and growth stage; IFV Bordeaux trials since 1996 (l. 19, l. 34).
- Average over 2002-2009 trials: 8.2 treatments a year for downy mildew, 5.9 for powdery mildew (l. 115).
- Dose reduction against the reference: 41 % for downy mildew (49 % against the full dose), 45 % for powdery mildew (52 %) (l. 117-119).
- Untreated-control damage ranged from 0 to 100 % of the harvest (l. 127).
- Conclusion: about 40 % less dose; about 1 % more damage in low-pressure years (l. 351-354); about 10 % on leaves and 4 % on harvest in high-pressure years (l. 355-357).
- The main risk is a dose cut in exceptional conditions (l. 360-362).

## Dependence

- none. The paper compares fungicide doses by plot treatment and gives dose-adjustment rules by canopy and pressure class. It computes no infection, incubation, sporulation, wetness or phenology model and cites none of the engine-list models. The Optidose 'pathogenic virulence' class in Figure 1 is a qualitative input, not a model.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- A dose-adjustment rule set and trial summary: a reference for how the simulator's spray decisions might scale dose with canopy and pressure, with no infection model to borrow.
