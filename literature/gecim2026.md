# Geçim et al. 2026: leaf removal timing in the Baran system

A two-season trial in Erzincan on removing five basal leaves at three stages (full flowering, pea-size berry, veraison) in the Karaerik table grape. It reports yield, berry quality and winter bud cold injury.

## What it holds

- Five basal leaves removed at full flowering, pea-size berry or veraison, against an untreated control, in 2023 and 2024 (l. 26–28).
- Yield was 8.98 kg per vine in the control and 7.43 kg per vine after removal at full flowering (l. 31–34).
- At full flowering, cluster compactness (OIV-204) was 4.58, against 7.58 for the control. Cracked and decayed berries fell by about 80% (l. 35–37).
- At full flowering, total soluble solids were 19.07% and total phenolics 504.57 mg per L (l. 40–41).

## Dependence

- None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t5 (damage and economics). No disease is named or scored; decayed berries are counted, not attributed. A cluster-zone model could take the timing trade-off between yield and cluster looseness.
- Off the point for the disease strands: a table-grape trial with no pathogen or pest measured. Not read in full.
