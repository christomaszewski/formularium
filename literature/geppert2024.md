# Geppert et al. 2024: climate, landscape and pesticide use in Italy

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- 38 pairs of conventional and organic vineyards across Italy (l. 107).
- Conventional yield 40% higher in cold, wet climates; the gap vanished in the warmest regions (l. 112-113).
- Warm, dry climate linked to lower fungicide pressure in both systems (l. 111-112).
- Mean fungicide TFI for conventional vineyards 24.21 (SD 21.51); management did not change it (l. 324).
- Climate normals: mean annual rainfall 495-1294 mm (l. 252).
- Fungicide TFI fell as semi-natural area grew (l. 321).
