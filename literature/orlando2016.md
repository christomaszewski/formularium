# Orlando et al. 2016: a seasonal LAI series in a rainfed Lombard vineyard

PocketLAI and hemispherical photographs against destructive LAI, six surveys in 2015.
Read for its destructive LAI over the season.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 144-171): Barbera and Chardonnay at Riccagioia (Pavia, 44.98° N, 138 m),
  planted 2008, rows 2.4 m, vines 1.0 m, Guyot; rainfed; green pruning in most areas.
- **Surveys** (Table 1, l. 177-193): six, BBCH 56 to 81, 5 May to 20 July.
- **Destructive LAI** (Figure 3, approximate): about 0.18 (5 May), 0.25 (12 May), 0.45
  (22 May), 0.65 (5 June), 1.36 (23 June); 0.13 to 2.84 over all data (l. 404), 2.84 in
  an area without green pruning (l. 28, 747). LAI is per ground area, converted from the
  row-level reading by 2D/R (D 0.4 m, R 2.4 m).
- **Unsettled:** the outlier's conversion (2.84 from 6.8, factor 0.42) does not match the
  printed 0.33.
- **Not printed:** budburst date, LAI as numbers by date, shoots a vine.

## Dependence

- PocketLAI rests on Warren-Wilson's gap-fraction model (cited); destructive method after
  Bréda and Johnson & Pierce. Fits to the paper's own data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Out-of-region shape check:** rainfed LAI was still climbing steeply in late June (1.36,
  about 1.4 × 2.4 = 3.3 m² a vine, my calculation), after a slow May. Budburst is not
  printed, so days after budburst cannot be set.
