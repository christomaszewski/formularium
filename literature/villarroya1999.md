# Villarroya et al. 1999: eight white varieties at Sant Martí Sarroca, Penedès, 1987-1999

A Catalan variety trial report (Chardonnay, Parellada and six other whites on two rootstocks) with yields, bunch weights and a table of earliest, mean and latest budburst, flowering and harvest dates over 12 years.

**Read this first.**
- No date or venue is printed in the file; the trial ran 1987 to 1999 and the results stop in 1999 (l. 33-36, 152).
- Bunches per vine are not printed. They are derived here from yield per hectare and mean bunch weight (ratio of means, 1989-1999, both rootstocks pooled). Treat them as rough.
- The text says seven foreign varieties plus Parellada (l. 33); the phenology table has eight rows in all (Parellada is one). The count is consistent.
- The budburst table gives only the earliest, mean and latest date over 12 years (l. 152). It does not print yearly dates or a standard deviation. The spread is between years on one plot, not between vines.

## What it holds

- **Site and layout (l. 42-44, 97-98, 101):** Sant Martí Sarroca, Penedès, NE exposure, 360 m above sea level; 2.40 x 1.20 m planting, 3,400 vines/ha (l. 97-98); three-level trellis; bud load 40,000-50,000 buds/ha (l. 101), which is 11.8-14.7 buds/vine (arithmetic, not printed). Grafted on 41B and 110R (l. 33-35).
- **Pruning (l. 102-103):** double Guyot for Riesling, Gewürztraminer and Muscat Ottonel; single Guyot for Chardonnay, Sauvignon and Chasan; Royat for Parellada and Chenin.
- **Years covered (l. 150-152):** yields 1989-1999 (11 years), wines 1990-1999 (10 years), phenology and disease 1988-1999 (12 years).
- **Budburst, earliest / mean / latest, with the range in days (arithmetic, not printed; table l. 296-310):**
  - Chardonnay 15 Mar / 23 Mar / 3 Apr, range 19 d (l. 296).
  - Gewürztraminer 21 Mar / 28 Mar / 6 Apr, range 16 d (l. 298).
  - Chenin 16 Mar / 27 Mar / 2 Apr, range 17 d (l. 300).
  - Chasan 20 Mar / 31 Mar / 9 Apr, range 20 d (l. 302).
  - Muscat Ottonel 21 Mar / 1 Apr / 9 Apr, range 19 d (l. 304).
  - Riesling 20 Mar / 31 Mar / 9 Apr, range 20 d (l. 306).
  - Sauvignon 28 Mar / 6 Apr / 15 Apr, range 18 d (l. 308).
  - Parellada 4 Apr / 9 Apr / 17 Apr, range 13 d (l. 310).
- **Between varieties:** the means run from 23 Mar (Chardonnay) to 9 Apr (Parellada), 17 d (arithmetic). The text says Chardonnay's mean budburst, 23 Mar, is 8-10 d ahead of Xarel.lo, the earliest native variety, with a greater spring frost risk (l. 282-283, text of the paragraph).
- **Parellada yield and bunch weight:** 1989-1999 mean 17,100 kg/ha (20,812 on 110R, 13,387 on 41B) (l. 174); mean bunch weight 464 g (l. 210). Per vine 17,100 / 3,400 = 5.03 kg, so about 10.8 bunches/vine (derived).
- **Chardonnay yield and bunch weight:** 8,581 kg/ha (l. 186); bunch weight 146 g (l. 218). Per vine 2.52 kg, so about 17.3 bunches/vine (derived).

## Dependence

None recorded. The trial is the authors' own; the report cites outside work only for acidity and vigour (Champagnol 1984; Wagner & Bois 1992).

## Bearing (2026-11-05)

- **host.budburst_sd_days (Agrarium):** a between-year spread of budburst in 8 varieties on one Penedès plot at 360 m: ranges of 13-20 d between the earliest and latest of 12 years, and 17 d between the earliest and latest variety mean. Kind of evidence: a trial table of extremes. It does not give vine-to-vine spread; an SD from the range is a rough guess, not a measurement.
- **host.bunches_per_vine (Agrarium):** about 10.8 for Parellada (Royat) and about 17.3 for Chardonnay (single Guyot) at 3,400 vines/ha, derived from trial yield and bunch weight. Kind of evidence: derived, pruning-dependent, a ratio of means from a trial, not a count.
