# Würz et al. 2025: bud load and Botrytis bunch rot in Sauvignon Blanc

Two seasons in a commercial vineyard at four bud loads. Loads above 50 buds per plant raised Botrytis incidence, severity and the area under the progress curves.

## What it holds

- **Design:** 15, 30, 50 and 75 buds per plant on Sauvignon Blanc at São Joaquim, SC (abstract, l. 24-26). Two vintages, 2016/17 and 2017/18 (l. 104). Four blocks of ten plants (l. 107-108).
- **Headline:** above 50 buds per plant, incidence, severity and AUDIPC and AUDSPC rose. Symptoms appeared earlier (abstract, l. 29-32).
- **No effect:** time to maximum incidence and severity did not change (abstract, l. 32).
- **Scale:** 20 clusters per plot, rated on the diagrammatic scale of Hill et al. 2010 (l. 137-141).
- **Data:** "can be made available upon request" (l. 371).

## Dependence

- Hill et al. 2010 diagrammatic scale (l. 140-141).
- AUDPC by the trapezoid sum (l. 150-151).
- Earlier bud-load work by the same group (l. 77-82). Mossetti et al. 2016 on cluster microclimate (l. 83-84).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (other grape diseases: Botrytis) and t5 (damage). A simulator could take the ordering: heavier bud loads bring earlier and heavier rot. The paper gives no fitted rate, and two seasons in one vineyard limit it.
