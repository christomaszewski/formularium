# Magarey et al. 2007: NAPPFAST weather-based pest mapping

Research article, United States (North American daily data set, ~2,000 stations) and global monthly CRU data set.

## What it holds

- Describes the NCSU-APHIS Plant Pest Forecasting System (NAPPFAST), a web system that links daily weather databases to model templates (generic infection template, generic empirical-equation template, degree-day template) to make risk maps for exotic pathogens and pests, with case studies (soybean rust, Sudden Oak Death).
- The templates are described in prose and figures (Fig. 3 shows the infection template for Phytophthora ramorum); no model equations are printed in the lines read, and grape powdery mildew (Uncinula necator) is mentioned only as an example of a rain-splash requirement.
