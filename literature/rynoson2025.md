# Rynoson et al. 2025: reverse transposition and separation of tilted irradiance at high latitude

GTI-DIRINT and a Perez-Driesse model, plus four GHI separation models used inside an optimisation loop with two transposition models, are tested on six-second tilted data.

## What it holds

- **Data:** six-second quality-controlled irradiance at latitude 59.53 N with GTI at 30, 40 and 90 degree tilts, GHI and DHI (l. 35-37, 241-245); collected in September and December 2022 and March and June 2023 (l. 243-244).
- **Models:** GTI-DIRINT and Perez-Driesse, and Erbs, Skartveit1, Engerer2 and Yang4 with Hay & Davies and Perez 1990 transposition (l. 37-42).
- **Result:** Engerer2 with Hay & Davies, or Perez-Driesse, is recommended; GHI bias is -2.0 and -2.3 W/m2 (l. 44-47, 393). Models are compared by statistics and the Diebold-Mariano test (l. 43).

## Dependence

- Uses GTI-DIRINT (Marion 2015) and the separation and transposition models named above (l. 37-42).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strands s3 and s5: how to rebuild horizontal and diffuse irradiance from a tilted sensor, a sensor-error issue on slopes; a simulator could pick the recommended pairs.
