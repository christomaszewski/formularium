# Li et al. 2015: uncertainty of 13 rice models across four Asian sites

Thirteen rice models are run against multi-year yield data at four sites and against 16 climate-model scenarios; the ensemble mean is much better than single models.

## What it holds

- **Design:** 13 models, four sites with diverse climates, and temperature and CO2 sensitivity (l. 36-39, 133).
- **Result:** single models did not reproduce yields consistently, and uncertainty was largest at the warmest and coolest sites (l. 40-41); spread among crop models exceeded the spread from 16 climate scenarios (l. 41-43).
- **Ensemble:** the mean of all models reproduced data to under 10 % of measured yield; eight phenology-calibrated or five fully calibrated models matched field-measurement uncertainty (l. 43-46).

## Dependence

- Follows the AgMIP rice protocol, with 13 rice models and calibration protocols in supplementary tables.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strand s6 (methods): evidence that an ensemble of models beats one, and that phenology calibration matters; a simulator could take the bias and variance split of ensemble error.
