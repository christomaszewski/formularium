# Ritter et al. 2019: dew frequency at NEON sites from radiometric surface temperature

Abstract only. Dew is detected from measured canopy radiometric temperature against dew point; not a model from station weather.

## What it holds

- **Data:** NEON, 11 grassland and 19 forest sites, 2015-2017, dew at 30 min from in situ radiometric surface temperatures at several heights plus meteorology (l. 22).
- **Result:** grassland dew frequency 15-95 % of nights, strongly linear in night-time RH (l. 26).
