# Aslanov et al. 2019: winter temperature windows and wheat yellow rust

## What it holds

- 29 field cases in Luxembourg, 2010-2016, split into epidemic and non-epidemic: air
  temperature differed significantly in windows at 21-28 and 132-134 days after sowing; a
  leave-one-out classification was right in 86.7 % of cases.
