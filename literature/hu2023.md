# Hu et al. 2023: ERA5 wind downscaled from 31 km to 1 km with station data and terrain metrics

A statistical downscaling of ERA5 wind speed from about 31 km to 1 km using machine-learning regression on station observations and topographic metrics (l. 61-67). No record.

## What it holds

- **Method:** stations are split into three classes by the quality of ERA5 wind (l. 62-63); a regression per class relates ERA5, station wind and terrain (l. 65-67). Class 1 holds 276 stations (l. 256).
- **Test:** compared with COSMO-REA6 and checked with independent datasets (l. 68-69); Germany, DWD stations.
- **Result:** the abstract says wind in complex terrain improves significantly (l. 67-68); no error figures were located in the first pages.

## Dependence

- Builds on ERA5, COSMO-REA6 as reference and terrain indices such as TPI (l. 266).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s3 (terrain and canopy physics) and s2:** a route from coarse reanalysis wind to a 1 km value using terrain position. A simulator could take the idea of terrain-conditioned wind scaling; hourly wind at vineyard scale still needs local data.
- Wind only; no humidity or wetness. Triage-grade: not read in full.
