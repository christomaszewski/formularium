# Cendrós 2025: shoot thinning of young rainfed Macabeu at Vila-rodona

A Rovira i Virgili undergraduate thesis on three levels of shoot removal (esporga) in a
young Macabeu vineyard of the DO Cava, 2024-2025. Read for shoots per vine and canopy size.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 351-357): 0.50 ha at Vila-rodona (Alt Camp), 41°19'48" N 1°22'48" E;
  Macabeu on 140 Ruggeri planted 2020; 2.9 × 1.2 m; trellis (espatllera) with double
  Royat; 1,175 vines, unirrigated; integrated production, DO Cava. Macabeu is called
  vigorous, with large compact bunches (l. 364-366).
- **Shoots and bunches a vine** (Taula 3, l. 696-699): control 20.11 shoots and 13.44
  bunches; partial thinning 16.44 and 10.78; total thinning 16.33 and 10.67.
- **Canopy** (Taula 2, l. 666-670): the change between the first and fifth measurement, not
  the size itself: height +0.34 to +0.46 m, depth +0.22 to +0.23 m, sun-exposed surface
  +1.12 to +1.44 m², volume +0.49 to +0.54 m³; no significant differences. Width does not
  change on this trellis (l. 673-675).
- **Not printed in what was read:** leaf area, LAI, measurement dates, budburst.

## Dependence

- None: field measurements, no model.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Shoots a vine for Macabeu on double Royat, 1.2 m apart:** 16 to 20, near Agrarium's 15.
  No leaf area.
