# Ramos & Martínez-Casasnovas 2010: Penedès climate trends and Xarel·lo's veraison

A six-page congress paper from Lleida on the project's own region: warming and drying
trends at four Penedès observatories, a stage-by-stage water balance, and single-predictor
fits of Xarel·lo's veraison date and yield. The text file's private-use glyphs lose minus
signs and dashes; the page images were used where they mattered.

## What it holds

- **Stations and years:** Vilafranca del Penedès (230 m) from 1952, Sant Sadurní d'Anoia
  (164 m), La Granada (238 m) and Els Hostalets de Pierola (312 m) from 1996, daily
  rain, Tmax and Tmin for April-September 1996-2009 (l. 88-93); stage dates in two
  dry-farmed Xarel·lo vineyards over 14 seasons (l. 98, 181; `ramos2010.penedes`).
- **Trends (Table 1):** growing-season mean temperature 18.7-22.2 °C across the four
  (l. 130); at Vilafranca about 0.04 °C/yr over 1952-2006, 2.2 °C in all (l. 134-135);
  Winkler index 2066-2120, region IV, against 1860 a decade earlier (l. 142-144);
  evapotranspiration up 3-11 mm/yr (l. 148-150); bloom-veraison rain down 4.6-5.9 mm/yr,
  about 10 % (l. 157-158). Two of the text's trend values do not match Table 1
  (l. 136-137, 141).
- **Water balance** (Els Hostalets only, l. 162): P minus FAO-56 crop ET by stage; five
  of 14 seasons had rain at or above ET over the cycle (l. 181), two covered no stage
  (l. 187); the bloom-veraison and ripening deficits grew in almost all years (l. 193).
- **Fits (Table 2, p. 3-27):** yield on accumulated water in budbreak-bloom, slope 17.97
  kg/ha per mm, R² 43.47 % (the text says about 47 %, l. 244), the only term kept by
  forward stepwise selection (l. 243); veraison date on the bloom-veraison degree-day
  sum above 10 °C (Tef, l. 96), slope −0.106 d per °C·d, R² 28.78 %, p 0.07 (called
  significant, l. 281); veraison date on bloom-veraison water, slope 0.081 d/mm, R²
  37.95 %, p 0.02 (l. 284). No budbreak or bloom relation and no intercepts are
  printed; no validation.

## Dependence

- **Computes** no phenology model: the degree-day term is an index, the ET a FAO-56
  model's (Allen et al. 1998, cited without a reference entry, l. 105). The dates are
  observed.
- **Authors:** Ramos also wrote `ramos2017` (the Penedès degree-day fits on cava
  varieties); a shared author, not a dependency.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- For Agrarium's region: the four observatories and their 1996-2009 trends, and a
  dry-farmed Xarel·lo record of 14 seasons the authors hold (the dates are not printed).
- The non-threshold form Chris asked for is not here: only a weak veraison-on-water line
  (0.08 d/mm, p 0.02); the paper is a climate-trend piece.
