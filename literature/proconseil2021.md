# Proconseil 2021: Chasselas budburst and flowering in Vaud, 2011-2021

A grower advisory service's annual report for the canton of Vaud. Section 1 tabulates Chasselas
budburst (BBCH 09), full flowering (BBCH 65) and harvest start for 2011-2021.

**Read this first.** The site is not named. It is not Changins: 2021's budburst is 7 April here
and 20 April in Agroscope's Changins observations (`leoni2026thesis`, Table 1.1).

## What it holds

- Budburst 5 April (2011) to 26 April (2013); full flowering 1 June (2011) to 3 July (2013);
  budburst to flowering 49-72 days (p. 2).
- 2011-2020 means: budburst 12 April, flowering 14 June, 63 days, harvest 26 September.
- Transcribed in `pipeline_stage_dates.csv`.

## Dependence

- None. The report credits Agrometeo for a figure; the table's source is not stated.

## Bearing (2026-10-28)

- A Vaud Chasselas series, 2011-2021, near Changins but not of it. Usable as a regional check on
  a phenology model, not as Changins' own dates. Its licence is not stated.
