# Barajas Tola 2010: Tempranillo vine spacing at four Duero vineyards

A Spanish doctoral thesis on Tempranillo on 110 Richter at three in-row spacings (1.2, 1.5
and 1.8 m) in four vineyards of the Duero valley, 2005 to 2007. Read for its observed stage
dates and its leaf area at veraison, not for the yield or grape quality.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 380, 4029; l. 6644): Castrillo de Duero, Pollos, Rodilana and Toro, 2005 to
  2007; Tempranillo on 110 Richter, planted 2000 (l. 4031), bilateral Royat cordon on a
  trellis, rows about north-south. The cover names the school and its department but no
  university.

## Dependence

- **Method, not model:** LAI follows Carbonneau 1976 (a, b), cited (l. 5446-5447): a
  non-destructive leaf area from midrib length. The coefficients of the midrib equation
  are given without a source; they may be a local fit, but the thesis does not say. The
  method is that of the Madrid group (Carbonneau-style leaf lines, as in
  `fernandezgomez2013`); a flag only. SA cites Shaulis et al. 1966 (in Smart 1985,
  l. 5508-5510).
- **Own regressions:** Tablas III.15 and III.20-III.23 are fitted to the thesis's own
  measurements. No borrowed equation is recorded.
- **No model shaped the dates;** the stage dates are field scores, and no calibration
  with another model is stated.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **Observed seasons free of any engine fitting:** stage dates for Tempranillo at four
  Duero vineyards in 2006 (budburst to harvest) and veraison in 2007: a check set for a
  truth phenology. Each date is a three-spacing mean, and no heat sum is printed, so the
  dates need an outside temperature series to test a thermal-time model. It prints no
  fitted curve for phenology, so it gives no record.
- **Field values for leaf area:** LAI at veraison 1.3 to 3.4 and SA 0.76 to 1.08 m²/m²,
  SA/LAI 0.28 to 0.61, over 12 vineyard-spacing cells in three years. They are reported,
  not judged against any assumption.
- **Allometry only:** the shoot-length lines and the midrib equation are leaf-area-from-size
  fits with no time term, so no record.
