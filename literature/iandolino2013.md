# Iandolino, Pearcy & Williams 2013: simulated 3D vine canopies and light interception by row direction

Canopies of Cabernet Sauvignon (VSP) built from resampled shoot allometry in YPLANT, with light interception computed by ray projection and compared with ceptometer profiles.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 128): Cabernet Sauvignon clone 8 on 110R, VSP, 1.0 x 1.83 m, Oakville, 2001-2002.
- **Leaf elevation** (Table 1, l. 267-268): mean 77.2, 79.5, 58.1, 63.2 deg for non-irrigated, N only, irrigated and irrigated plus N (west side 75.1, 77.4, 50.2, 60.1). Total leaf area 3.4, 4.8, 6.7, 7.9 m2 per vine (l. 264).
- **Row direction** (l. 212, 346): 310 and 225 deg rows; daily absorbed light 92 and 97 % of the true N-S maximum. Leaf angles assumed independent of row direction (l. 222).
- **Check** (l. 206): ceptometer profiles every 0.2 m.
- **Not printed:** extinction coefficients, G; agreement statistics were not quoted.

## Dependence

- Uses YPLANT (Pearcy & Yang 1996). No engine overlap.

## Bearing

- Water and nitrogen supply moved mean leaf elevation by about 20 deg in one cultivar and system: an angle is not a fixed cultivar constant.
