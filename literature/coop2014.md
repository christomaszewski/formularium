# Coop et al. 2014: how far apart two Nebraska stations' daily Tmax and Tmin differ, by distance

A study of the RMSE between a candidate station and surrounding stations in three Nebraska networks (CRN, AWDN, HCN), as a measure of the spatial accuracy of a station's daily maximum and minimum temperature.

**Read this first.**
- It covers daily Tmax and Tmin, not the daily mean and not dew point. The extremes may differ more than the mean does.
- The reading agent's JSON gave the DOI as 10.1175/JAMC-D-13-0282.1; page 1 of the text prints 10.1175/JAMC-D-13-0296.1, which is used here. The end page was not checked.
- Table 1 has OCR artefacts: "2.321" and "2.011" are 2.32 and 2.01 with a mark (garbled). The 10 km values below are the reading agent's linear scaling, not printed.

## What it holds

- **Design:** RMSE between a candidate station and the surrounding stations, for daily Tmax and Tmin. Periods 2005-09 for CRN and AWDN, and 1985-2005 for AWDN and HCN. Nebraska, semiarid and subhumid areas. The Fig. 2 curve is fitted through zero. Elevation difference is not given in the text read.
- **Gordon (AWDN, semiarid), January (l. 366):** Tmax RMSE 0.52, 1.01, 1.48, 1.91, 2.32 degC at 25, 50, 75, 100, 125 km; Tmin 0.48, 0.94, 1.37, 1.77, 2.15 degC. Roughly linear in distance.
- **Mead (AWDN, subhumid), January (l. 371):** Tmax RMSE 0.44 degC and Tmin 0.49 degC at 25 km. Linear scaling gives about 0.18 and 0.20 degC at 10 km (reading agent's inference).
- **Mead, June (l. 372):** Tmax 0.36 degC and Tmin 0.32 degC at 25 km; about 0.14 and 0.13 degC at 10 km if linear (inference).
- **Harrison (HCN, semiarid), January (l. 364):** Tmax RMSE 0.80 degC and Tmin 0.71 degC at 25 km. HCN (COOP-type) stations are worse than AWDN.
- **Pattern in words:** accuracy is higher in summer, higher in the subhumid area than in the semiarid area, and higher for AWDN than HCN in all months (abstract).
- **Lead:** the first author's 2011 MS thesis (Univ. Nebraska) has curves for every station and month, "not shown" here.

## Dependence

- None recorded. Own analysis of network data (CRN, AWDN, HCN). Authors Hubbard and You also wrote wu2005; a shared author is only a flag.

## Bearing (2026-11-04)

- **weather.site_t_offset_sd_c (Agrarium):** the difference between two automatic stations' daily Tmax and Tmin as distance grows: RMSE about 0.4 to 0.5 degC at 25 km (Mead and Gordon, January), smaller in summer. Evidence: a measured network comparison in Nebraska.
- **Limits:** extremes, not the daily mean; a continental plains climate, not Penedès terrain; the 10 km values are linear scaling, not measured.
