# Ju et al. 2017: warming and a lace bug on plane trees

A two-spring field warming experiment in Shanghai on an invasive lace bug, Corythucha ciliata, and its host, the London plane. Warming advanced both the host leaves and the insect's spring activity.

## What it holds

- Heated and unheated plots in the springs of 2014 and 2015 (l. 66–67).
- A 2 °C rise in air temperature advanced leaf expansion and overwintered adult activity, with synchrony kept (l. 20–21). The advance was 3–4 days (l. 106–107).
- Warming increased reproduction of overwintered adults and nymph survival (P = 0.004) (l. 22–23, 161).

## Dependence

- None recorded.
