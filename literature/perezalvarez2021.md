# Pérez-Álvarez et al. 2021: leaf area per vine of rainfed and irrigated Bobal at Requena

Rainfed vines against drip at 35 % and 100 % of ETc, 2012-2014, in a commercial Bobal
vineyard. Read for leaf area per vine: it measures it, with a rainfed treatment, on the
same frame as `escalona2003`.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 102-118, 176-182): Bobal on 161-49C planted 2002, near Requena (Utiel-Requena),
  750 m; vertical trellis on a bilateral cordon, N-S; 2.5 × 1.4 m (2,857 vines/ha); deep
  calcareous clay loam of low fertility; continental Mediterranean, semi-arid; 12-year means
  ETo 1,127 mm and rain 380 mm; shoots trimmed after fruit set.
- **Water** (Table 1, l. 171-173): rain 291.2, 345.3 and 271.8 mm (2012-2014); drip 0 mm
  (rainfed), 74-125 mm (35 % ETc), 225-375 mm (100 % ETc).
- **Leaf area method** (l. 216-222): after veraison, five shoots a replicate measured on a
  LI-3100 (primary and lateral leaves separately; the split is not printed); vine leaf area =
  mean shoot leaf area (from a shoot-length allometry, not printed) × shoots a vine (counts
  not printed).
- **Table 3** (l. 465-479, page image; 2012, 2013, 2014):
  - leaf area, m² a vine: rainfed 1.47, 1.12, 0.48; 35 % ETc 1.49, 1.33, 1.00; 100 % ETc
    1.86, 1.47, 2.94. The printed averages for rainfed (1.07) and 100 % (2.03) are not the
    means of the three years (1.02, 2.09).
  - pruning wood, g a vine: rainfed 326.28, 345.09, 125.00; 35 % 350.06, 436.02, 329.00;
    100 % 499.91, 495.53, 568.25.
  - total shoot length, cm a vine: rainfed 879, 877.15, 325.62; 100 % 1,114.60, 1,145.05,
    1,659.05.
  - Ravaz index 3.76 to 7.79.
- **Unit:** the Discussion calls these values "leaf area index" (l. 526); the table says
  m² a vine. The table holds: 1.47 m² over 8.79 m of shoot is 0.17 m² a metre of shoot, as in
  `barajas2010`'s shoot lines; as LAI (5.1 m² a vine) it would be 0.58 m² a metre (my
  calculations).
- **Not printed:** dates, budburst, shoots a vine, LAI, laterals' share.

## Dependence

- ETc with Kc 0.6 after Williams & Ayars 2005; water stress integral after Myers 1988; the
  leaf-area allometry is the authors' own. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Rainfed Spanish cordon vines can carry much less than `escalona2003`'s:** 0.48 to 1.47 m²
  a vine after veraison and trimming, in years of 272 to 345 mm of rain, against 2.6 to 4.1
  m² in Mallorca on the same 2.5 × 1.4 m frame. Leaf area per g of prunings here is 32 to
  45 cm²/g rainfed and 37 to 52 cm²/g irrigated (my calculations), below Smart et al.'s
  cited 50-100. Used in the 2026-10-20 revision of Agrarium's research_notes/vine-leaf-area.
