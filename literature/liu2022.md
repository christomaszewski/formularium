# Liu et al. 2022: deep segmentation of mildew severity from vineyard images

A vehicle-mounted stereo camera and a deep network (HMASS) score downy and powdery mildew severity in vine panels, checked against human scouting.

## What it holds

- **Accuracy**: on test images, HMASS segmentation reached mIoU above 0.84 for downy mildew and above 0.74 for powdery mildew (l. 60–61).
- **Severity**: image-derived severity correlated with human scouting, r = 0.96 (l. 600–601). The abstract gives r above 0.95 (l. 68–69).
- **Data**: 224 annotated subimages for training and validation, and 463 test subimages from another row (l. 425–430). The powdery mildew trial had 18 treatments with four replicates (l. 473–474). Field images were taken in 2019 (l. 479).
- **Hardware**: a custom stereo camera with strobe light and RTK-GPS, on a utility vehicle (l. 45).

## Dependence

- Uses the HMASS network of Tao et al. 2020 (l. 222–223), started from weights pre-trained on Microsoft COCO (Lin et al. 2014) (l. 232–234).
- Class-uniform sampling follows Zhu et al. 2019 (l. 244).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t2 (observation), and t1 for trial ratings: a per-panel severity number from images could check a fungicide trial. The model was built on New York trial rows only.
