# Kim et al. 2005: empirical wetness models moved to Costa Rica

Two empirical models trained in the US Midwest, CART (decision tree on dew-point depression, RH and
wind; Kim et al. 2002) and a fuzzy-logic model FL (Kim et al. 2004), run at six stations in
north-western Costa Rica against painted Campbell 237 sensors over turfgrass. Not
`kim2005`, a different paper.

## What it holds

- **Sites and sensors** (l. 98-135): Ceiba, Garza, Liberia, Mojica, Puntarenas, Santa Cruz;
  sensors at 0.5 m over managed turfgrass, facing north at 45°, three latex coats; an hour is wet
  if the sensor reads below 1000 (kV printed, likely kOhm) for 30 min. Wind from 10 m is corrected
  to sensor height at night only.
- **1999 wet season** (Table 2, 649 days): measured 12.0 h a day; mean error CART +5.1 h, FL +0.5
  h; MAE CART 5.4, FL 2.8 h. The abstract says CART overestimated by 5.4 h, which is the MAE.
- **2000-2001 dry season** (Table 3, 505 days): measured 4.3 h; ME CART 0.9, FL 1.1; MAE 2.9 and
  1.7 h.
- **2002-2003 dry season** (Table 4, 407 days): measured 3.0 h; ME CART 0.8, FL 2.4, FL corrected
  by x 1.05 (CFL) 0.7; MAE 1.2, 2.4 and 1.1 h. RMSE on wet days: CFL 2.7, CART 3.0, FL 5.7 h.
- **Other:** CART MAE 3.9 h in the US Midwest against 5.4 h here; on dew-only days measured
  wetness averaged 6.3 h; Papastamati et al. 2004's physical model RMSE 3.4 h, and
  energy-balance models within 1 h (Pedro & Gillespie 1982b; Madeira et al. 2002), all cited.
- **FL internals** (Eqs 1-6): downward longwave by Idso & Jackson 1969, surface temperature at
  the dew point, Penman latent heat, log wind profile with d = 0.32 m and z0 = 0.065 m.
- **Not tested:** an RH threshold (Wilks & Shen 1991's RH > 90 % is cited only); a physical model.
  The decision-tree nodes are in an appendix not extracted (one node, DPD > 3.7 °C meaning dry).
- **Garbles:** the training set of the 1.05 factor is described three ways (l. 202-205, 309-311,
  581-582); the night solar elevation is printed as 208 (20°).

## Dependence

- CART and FL are Kim's own earlier models (a shared first author with this paper); CART rules were
  trained on Midwestern data. No model here is one the tools run.

## Bearing (2026-10-19)

- **Empirical models travel badly.** Trained in the US, they err by 5.4 h (CART) in a wet season
  with 12 h of wetness and 1.2 to 2.9 h in dry seasons with 3 to 4 h. That is the size of error an
  uncalibrated RH or DPD rule can have in a new climate, not a test of RH >= 90 %.
- **No vineyard, no canopy:** turfgrass sensors in the tropics. Nothing here compares with the
  micro.* storage, interception, dew or threshold parameters.
