# Manik & Sidle 2003: daily rain correlation at 0.2 to 8 km in a highland Indonesian watershed

A conference paper with manual gauges across a 729 km2 coffee watershed.

**Read this first.**
- Tropical highland, manual gauges, conference paper. Weak support for the temperate Penedès.

## What it holds

- **Close gauges (l. 16):** daily rain correlation r of 0.85 to 0.93 at 200 m; r 0.83 at 1 km (the quote shows 17.69 and 19.93 mm/day at two gauges 1 km apart).
- **Farther (l. 18):** r 0.23 at 2 to 3 km, falling to 0.13 at 8 km.
- **Summary line (l. 336):** r dropped to 0.22 to 0.50 at 5 km (reading agent's reading) and 0.13 at 8 km.
