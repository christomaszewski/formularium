# Lahlali et al. 2007: Botrytis colony growth on media at set water activity and temperature

Quadratic models give the growth rate of B. cinerea on four solute-modified media, at three temperatures and six water activities.

## What it holds

- **Design:** temperatures of 25, 15 and 5 C; water activities from 0.995 to 0.890 on glucose, NaCl, glycerol and sorbitol media; each combination in triplicate (l. 21-23, 93-96). The strain came from rotting strawberry (l. 108).
- **Limits:** no growth at aw 0.93 in NaCl, or at 0.89 in a non-ionic solute (l. 24-25).
- **Optimum:** aw 0.981 to 0.987 in the glycerol, sorbitol and glucose media, close to 1 in NaCl (l. 27-28).
- **Model:** ln g = C0 + C1 bw + C2 bw squared, with bw = 1 - aw (eq. 2, l. 145). Coefficients per solute are in Table 2 (l. 262-270). Glucose: C0 1.290, C1 10.848, C2 -42.715 (l. 265).
- **Accuracy:** bias factors 0.957, 1.036, 0.950 and 0.860, and accuracy factors 1.089, 1.070, 1.120 and 1.260 for glucose, NaCl, glycerol and sorbitol (l. 31-32).

## Dependence

- Gibson et al. 1994 model form for the aw term, with the bw transform (l. 125-136).
- Lahlali et al. 2005 media and aw method (l. 68-69).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (other grape diseases: grey mould). The water-activity and temperature terms are context for a grey-mould growth term. They describe colony growth on media, not infection or leaf wetness, so a simulator cannot take them as an infection rule.
