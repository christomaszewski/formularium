# Comșa et al. 2023: mildew on five new cultivars at Blaj

Two years of field scoring (2021, 2022) of downy and powdery mildew on five new cultivars at
  Blaj, with monthly climate means described in words; cultivar tolerance is the finding.

## What it holds

- **Design:** five cultivars (Selena, Blasius, Rubin, Radames, Brumăriu), frequency and
  intensity of attack scored on leaves and grapes (l. 20-30, 96-97).
- **Climate:** mean monthly temperature 18.4 C and humidity 73.5 % in 2021 against 19.2 C and
  69.3 % in 2022 (l. 126, 136).
- **Downy mildew:** first symptoms 2 June 2021 (l. 146);  attack degree (AD) on leaves 4.9 % and grapes 1.1 % for the worst cultivar (Blasius),
  lowest on Brumăriu, 0.7 % leaves and 0.1 % grapes (l. 150-154); the worst was Blasius in 2021 (l. 149).

## Dependence

- None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- s4, weakly: monthly means only, no within-vineyard microclimate. A simulator could take
  nothing but the order of magnitude of attack degree under mean humidity 69 % to 74 %. Not
  read in full.
