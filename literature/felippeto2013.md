# Felippeto et al. 2013: chilling sums against Cabernet Sauvignon's budburst in the Serra Gaúcha

Ten years (1984-1993) of Cabernet Sauvignon budburst at Embrapa's Bento Gonçalves
collection, with hourly temperatures read off thermograms, used to rank nine chilling
methods by how well the chill accumulated to a biofix predicts the days to budburst. A
chilling form, with linear regressions whose slopes are printed and whose adapted weights
are not.

## What it holds

- **Data:** Embrapa Uva e Vinho's germplasm bank, 200-800 m, 28-30 °S; budburst at 50 %
  of buds at Eichhorn-Lorenz stage 5; hourly temperatures 1 April to 30 September from
  the unit's station (l. 8, 78-115).
- **Methods:** chill hours below 7.2 and 10 °C, Utah (Richardson et al. 1974) and North
  Carolina (Shaltout & Unrath 1983) units as printed in Table 1, each original, modified
  after Ebert et al. 1986 (negative units only within 96 h of the last positive) and
  adapted to the Serra Gaúcha (weights not printed), and the Dynamic model (Erez et al.
  1988; E° 0.4153 × 10⁴ K, A° 0.1395 × 10⁶) (l. 10, 118-167); chill counted from 1
  April; the biofix is the date whose chill correlates best with days to budburst, 4
  August (l. 128-258).
- **Regressions** of days to budburst on chill at 4 August (Figures 3-7, page images):
  Utah original y = 0.0099x + 44.9 (R² 0.33), North Carolina original 0.0097x + 45.8
  (0.43), modified 0.0193x + 40.0 (0.62) and 0.015x + 42.5 (0.58), adapted 0.0247x + 35.7
  (0.78) and 0.0217x + 36.8 (0.78), Dynamic 0.6307x + 23.3 (0.76) (l. 304-439). Residual
  SD (Table 2): 4.23, 3.93, 3.19, 3.37, 2.41, 2.44 d, chill hours 2.79 and 2.76 d, Dynamic
  2.53 d, against 5.19 d for the date alone (l. 396-425; the conclusion misprints 8.67
  for 2.44).

## Dependence

- **Borrows** the Utah, North Carolina, Ebert and Dynamic forms; fitted to Embrapa's
  observed dates with no hold-out (ten years).

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- A subtropical counter-example to "chilling does not matter": at Bento Gonçalves the
  chill to 4 August explains three quarters of the budburst variance with any sensible
  weighting, and the date alone has twice the error. The adapted weights are not
  printed, so nothing can be run.
