# Dong et al. 2020: a national web GIS for wheat yellow rust and locust forecasting

A web GIS system that monitors and forecasts the national infected area of wheat yellow rust and oriental migratory locust from Earth observation, meteorology and field surveys (l. 12-32). No record.

## What it holds

- **Scope:** the national disease is wheat yellow rust (Puccinia striiformis) and the pest is the oriental migratory locust (l. 27-29).
- **Result:** forecasts of 2019 infected areas have R-square above 0.87 (l. 30-32); for locust, R-square 0.92 for summer and 0.87 for autumn (l. 465-466).
- **Inputs:** MODIS and Landsat/Sentinel imagery, 30 m land use, rainfall, temperature and wind, field survey data (record); the abstract names no model equations.

## Dependence

- Builds on remote-sensing indices (a PSRI index appears at l. 221), regression models and RustTracker-type monitoring (l. 38).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s1 (autumn inoculum) and s6:** a system description of national-scale forecasting from satellite and weather inputs. A simulator would take nothing to run; it shows the type of remote-sensing input that may feed a regional risk map. No grapevine and no downy mildew.
