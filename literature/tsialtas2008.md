# Tsialtas et al. 2008: leaf area of Cabernet Sauvignon from leaf length and width

Leaf area against leaf length, width and perimeter for 324 Cabernet Sauvignon leaves in
Thessaly, and a test of eleven published leaf-area lines on the same leaves.

## What it holds

- **Data** (l. 81-107): 10-year-old vines on 1103 Paulsen and SO4 in a commercial vineyard
  at 39°48' N, 22°27' E, 190 m; three irrigation regimes (100 % ETc, 50 % ETc, none); three
  samplings a year at bunch closure, veraison and ripeness in 2005 and 2006; three leaves
  a plot; 324 leaves in all, measured by image analysis. No dates are printed.
- **Own line** (l. 121): LA = 18.379 L - 151.41, r² 0.97, p < 0.001, n = 18. The 18 are
  the means of rootstock × irrigation × sampling (l. 116-118), not the 324 leaves. Leaf
  area against aspect ratio gave r² 0.98 and was rejected as hard to measure in the field
  (l. 151). CV of leaf area 8.12 % (l. 153).
- **Eleven published lines** (l. 93-102), tested on the measured lengths and widths with
  r² 0.87-0.97 (l. 193): Manivel & Weaver 1974 (1.162 L² - 0.802 L + 1.051; 0.644 W² +
  0.469 W + 0.109), Sepúlveda & Kliewer 1983 (0.69 LW + 3.17; 0.68 LW + 2.49), Elsner &
  Jubb 1988, Schultz 1992, Montero et al. 2000 (0.587 LW; 0.647 L^1.956), Williams &
  Martinson 2003 (0.637 W^1.995; 0.672 W^1.963).
- **Best by r² and mean squared difference** (l. 100, 215-218): LA = 0.587 (L × W), from
  Montero et al. 2000's Cencibel work; MSD 0.82, the lowest (model 1: 1601.59; model 7:
  4579.62). Cited to Montero et al., not this paper's fit.
- **Not printed:** coefficient errors, sampling dates, leaf counts or leaf area per shoot
  or vine, the eleven lines' source data. The DOI is not in the text layer.

## Dependence

- **Tests others' lines, borrows none:** the eleven lines are other authors' regressions
  applied to these leaves, which is evaluation. The 0.587 line is Montero et al. 2000's.
  The own fit is to this study's means. No model output is an input, and no source data
  are shared.

## Bearing (2026-10-14)

- **Allometry, independent of the engine:** a per-leaf line for Cabernet Sauvignon that
  complements Sánchez-de-Miguel et al. 2011's. Fitted to 18 treatment-stage means.
  Buttaro et al. 2015 found the 0.587 line biased by 18 % on table grapes
  (literature/buttaro2015). It gives leaf area, not leaves per shoot, so it does not set a
  canopy's area alone.
