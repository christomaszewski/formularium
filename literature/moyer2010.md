# Moyer et al. 2010: cold nights and powdery mildew

The same lab as [moyer2016](moyer2016.md); no shared data.

## What it holds

- Chardonnay leaves at 2-8 °C for 2-8 h, inoculated 24 h later: colonies 37-55 % smaller,
  with no dose effect. Resistance peaked 24-36 h after 8 h at 2 °C. Colonies 3-4 days old
  lost 57-67 % more hyphae.
- Leaves ran 3.8-7.6 °C below air 30 min after sunset on still, clear nights, 0.9 °C on
  breezy or overcast ones; -1.7 °C against 1.7 °C air on 19 May 2009 at Geneva (NY).
- Five regions average 16-21 days at or below 6 °C between budbreak and bloom (Table 1).
