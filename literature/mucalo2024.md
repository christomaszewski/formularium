# Mucalo et al. 2024: review of satellite tools for vineyard management

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- A PRISMA-adapted systematic review of satellite use in precision viticulture; 308 papers from 2019 onward, 120 after dropping UAV-only studies, 92 reviewed in detail (l. 146-174).
- Satellite history: Landsat 1 (1972) and Landsat 8 (2013) (l. 224-228).
- Prescription maps from satellite canopy data and a decision support system cut pesticide use by up to 40%; a variable-rate study cut spray volume by 58% (l. 488, 516-521, 818).
- Introduction: downy and powdery mildew forecasts are weather-based and set 7-14 day spray intervals (refs 17-18) (l. 84-86).
- Detection of pests and diseases is an aim, not a reported result (l. 109).
- The review adds no downy mildew data, model or threshold (l. 17).
