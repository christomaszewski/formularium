# van Rooi and Holz 2003: fungicide efficacy by shoot position against grey mould

Four fungicides against Botrytis cinerea infection at four shoot positions, on grape vinelets inoculated with dry airborne conidia, at Stellenbosch.

## What it holds

- **Design:** Dauphine and Merlot noir vinelets from cuttings, sprayed at Eichhorn-Lorenz stage 12 with iprodione, fenhexamid, cyprodinil/fludioxonil or pyrimethanil at recommended doses, then dusted with dry conidia (l. 104-137).
- **Position:** on unsprayed vinelets, incidence was significantly higher on leaf blades than on petioles, shoot segments and inflorescences (l. 264-267).
- **Surface sterilisation:** on shoot segments, incidence fell to 6.3 % on the paraquat medium and to 0 % on the Kerssies medium (l. 269-270). Leaf blades kept high incidence after sterilisation (l. 271-272; the OCR figure is garbled).
- **Fungicides:** all four were "highly and nearly equally efficient" in reducing B. cinerea, and reduced leaf-blade incidence on both cultivars (l. 271-276).

## Dependence

- Builds on Coertze and Holz 1999 for the isolate and infection method (l. 74-75, 124), Holz et al. 2000 (l. 75), Eichhorn-Lorenz growth stage 12 (l. 104), Nel et al. 1999 dosages (l. 107) and the Salinas et al. 1989 inoculation tower (l. 137).
