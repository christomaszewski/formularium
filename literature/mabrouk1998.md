# Mabrouk & Sinoquet 1998: foliage gaps and leaf area density of eight Merlot canopies from 3D digitising

Eight canopies (four trellis systems x two vigour classes) of Merlot digitised leaf by leaf and rendered as virtual images, from which structure and light indices are computed and related to berry composition.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 112): 15-year-old Merlot on SO4, Montpellier, 1.2 x 2.5 m, ESE-WNW; E = espalier, SC, OL = Open Lyre and GDC; M and H = moderate and high vigour.
- **Foliage gaps FG** (l. 287): porosity perpendicular to the row, after Riou et al. 1989, from images of virtual canopy sides. Table 1 (l. 305-313): 24, 29, 36, 44 % (EM, SCM, OLM, GDCM) and 14, 16, 28, 33 % (EH, SCH, OLH, GDCH); range 14-44 % (l. 332).
- **Leaf area density** (l. 305-313, 325-326): 8.29, 4.15, 7.03, 6.46 and 10.33, 5.71, 7.10, 6.75 m2/m3; 4.2-10.3 overall, higher for upright shoots between wires.
- **Not printed in the part read:** a k or G value; the columns for SKY (bunch zone sky) are in Table 1 but not quoted.

## Dependence

- Same vines and digitiser as mabrouk1997a; uses Riou et al. 1989's FG definition. No overlap with the engine.

## Bearing

- Measured gap fraction by trellis system and vigour: more vigorous means fewer gaps; divided canopies more gaps (33-44 %) than undivided (14-29 %).
