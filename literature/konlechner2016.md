# Konlechner and Sauer 2016: static water contact angles of 18 Vitis vinifera cultivars, 62 to 125 deg above and 59 to 152 deg below the leaf

A leaf-surface study of 18 cultivars by microscopy and goniometry in which dense reclining hairs on the underside produce the most water-repellent leaves.

**Read this first.** The angles are static angles of single water drops on fresh, briefly rinsed leaves (a goniometer, KSV CAM 101), not retention or drying. The drop volume is given only in the Figure 8 caption (6 microlitres of deionised water, l. 486); the number of drops per cultivar is not in the lines read. The text is two columns interleaved: the Riesling sentence is garbled in the text file, so the Riesling figures below are from the clean Table 1. Compare `gaskin2005`, where the liquid is 20 % acetone, not water, and `kortekamp1999`, which measured adhesion force: these methods do not give the same ranking (Riesling is "completely wettable" there).

## What it holds

- **Setting:** 18 cultivars of Austrian importance, leaves of the same developmental stage so that leaf age is excluded (8th or 9th node of the year's shoots, stored at 4 degC); most sampled at a university research garden in Tulln, Austria, a few supplied by a winery (l. 109-118). Contact angle by goniometer on leaves rinsed briefly with dH2O, from leaf base, middle and head (l. 185-190).
- **Range (Table 1, l. 264-323):** adaxial (upper) 62 +/- 14 deg (Veltliner gruen, the lowest; l. 264) to 125 +/- 16 deg (Riesling; l. 277); abaxial (lower) 59 +/- 19 deg (Chasselas rouge, the lowest; l. 289) to 152 +/- 8 deg (Veltliner gruen, the highest; l. 266). Ranges taken from the table (arithmetic, not printed). Individual values exceed 150 deg where dense reclining hair covers the lower side (abstract; l. 27), and single drops on Veltliner gruen exceed 160 deg (Figure 8 caption, l. 488).
- **Selected pairs, adaxial / abaxial (Table 1):** Riesling 125 / 133; Welschriesling 104 / 145 (l. 267, 269); Chardonnay 103 / 119 (l. 280, 282); Sauvignon blanc 98 / 143 (l. 284, 286); Mueller-Thurgau 90 / 111 (l. 270, 272); Merlot 118 / 135 (l. 310, 312); Saint Laurent 98 / 126 (l. 304, 306); Chasselas rouge 81 / 59 (l. 287, 289), the only cultivar whose lower side is the lower and the lowest lower-side angle. The text calls its pair the lowest (l. 492), but in Table 1 two cultivars have a lower upper-side angle: Veltliner gruen 62 and Zweigeltrebe blau 71 (l. 264, 291).
- **Two sides:** for all but three of the 18 cultivars the upper and lower angles differ significantly (t-test, alpha = 0.01), by 12 to 59 % (l. 501-504). The three without a significant difference are Pinot noir, Portugieser blau and Riesling (l. 501-508). In the other 15 the lower side is the higher in 14, all but Chasselas rouge (counted from Table 1; arithmetic, not printed).
- **Hair:** the text says all cultivars with high or very high reclining-hair density had angles above 135 deg and lists Riesling among them (l. 522), but Riesling's lower side in Table 1 is 133 +/- 14 deg (l. 279). Pinot noir and Portugieser blau, with very low or no hair, show low hydrophobicity on both sides: 96 +/- 7 and 105 +/- 25 deg, 95 +/- 15 and 101 +/- 8 deg (l. 505-509).

## Dependence

- Own measurements. No model or equation borrowed. Contact angles for 18 cultivars of one species; leaves of the same stage, so leaf age is not a factor but weather, microclimate and sun or shade leaves may still differ (discussion).

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **micro.wet_threshold_mm (0.02 mm) and micro.storage_mm_per_lai (0.25):** static angles of 90 deg or more on the upper side of 14 of the 18 cultivars and above 100 deg on the lower side of 17 of 18 (counted from Table 1; arithmetic, not printed) say a vine leaf is not easily wetted and that a drop sits on it rather than spreading into a film. It gives no film thickness and no retained volume per leaf area, so it cannot set either value. A leaf that beads may hold less than a flat film for the same rain; this is not shown here.
- **Drying and interception parameters:** not addressed.
- **Kind of result:** measurement (static water angles, fresh rinsed leaves, single research garden and a few supplied samples). Hairy lower sides raise the angle above 135 deg; the effect on rain held or drying time is not measured.
