# De La Fuente et al. 2021: copper alternatives and legal limits, vines

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Review chapter on alternatives to copper for downy mildew control in EU vineyards, framed by R&D projects and copper limits (l. 17-26, 135-136).
- Copper in vineyard soils: JRC 2018 mean topsoil Cu 49.26 mg/kg against 16.85 overall (l. 65-68); a protective range of 26.3-31.8 mg/kg proposed (l. 81-83).
- EU limit 28 kg Cu/ha over seven years; Germany and Austria 3 kg/ha per year; France 6 kg/ha per year (l. 120-128).
- Modelling section: the 3-10 rule and the Rossi et al. 2008 primary-infection model are described; the model reportedly cut fungicide sprays by 50-66% (l. 447-472).
- Decision support: vite.net in 21 organic farms cut copper applications by 24% and total copper by 37% (l. 496-500).
- Conclusion: R&D results should inform copper limits before standards are set (l. 896-902).
