# Burruano et al. 1990: cold held oospores germinating into summer

## What it holds

- Catarratto leaves collected near Palermo on 3 November 1987, overwintered on soil; from 14
  March 1988, pots kept at 3, 9 or 12 °C with 20 mm of simulated rain every 5 days, with
  rain at ambient temperature, or outdoors.
- Outdoors: 4.1 and 9.5 % (15 and 30 January), 16.5 and 31.5 % (13 and 29 February), most 37
  % on 14 March (minimum 4 days), 10.2 % on 29 March, 2.4 % on 2 June.
- Rain alone at ambient temperature changed nothing: 13.3 to 7.5 % until 13 May, then none.
- Cold with rain kept oospores germinable all year: at 3 °C 60.3 % on 12 July, 1.7 % in
  December; at 9 °C 40.4 % on 22 June; at 12 °C 84.7 % on 22 June, exhausted by 7 December.
