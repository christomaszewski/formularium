# Jia et al. 2019: three years of leaf-wetness-sensor dew on the Loess Plateau

A three-year (2014 to 2016) in-situ record of dew with a leaf wetness sensor and four meteorological instruments in semi-arid Sanyuan County (l. 19-22). No record.

## What it holds

- **Amount:** average annual dewfall 32.8 mm, daily maximum 0.88 mm (l. 22-23); dew days 224 (61 % of the year) against 87 rain days (l. 32-33).
- **Timing:** dew mostly between 18:00 and 8:00, maximum condensation rate near 4:00 (l. 23-24); residence time up to about 18 h a day, with production about 14 h in spring, autumn and winter and 11 h in summer (l. 24-27).
- **Drivers:** dew amount correlated with relative humidity above 81 % (r = 0.78) and, for a dewpoint gap under 4 C, with the gap (r = -0.66); weakly with wind speed (l. 36-39).

## Dependence

None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s4 (measured microclimate) and s5 (sensor error):** a measured leaf-wetness record with a humidity threshold of 81 % for dew and a dewpoint-gap relation. A simulator could take it as a check on how an RH threshold for wetness (the engine uses RH 90 % or above) compares with sensor-observed dew. A grassland-type site, not a canopy; sensor error not assessed in the first pages.
