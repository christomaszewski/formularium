# Reis et al. 2020: a sigmoid forcing model of phenology in four Portuguese regions

From ADVID and CoLAB Vines&Wines, UTAD (CITAB), INIAV and CERNAS.

## What it holds

- Hanninen's two-parameter sigmoid rate, f = 1/(1+exp d(Td-e)), applied to daily mean temperature (l. 94, 254).
- F is the sum of that rate from the onset date to the observed stage date; onset is 1 January for budburst, then the preceding stage for flowering and veraison (l. 257-260).
- Fitted per variety and stage (153 fits, l. 33) with PMP 5.5 and Metropolis annealing (l. 264, 267); checked by leave-one-out cross-validation (l. 296).
- Data: 51 varieties on 19 plots in Douro, Dao, Vinhos Verdes and Lisboa, 1990-2018 (Lisboa) and shorter elsewhere (l. 121, 142-156). Stages observed in the field (50% of plants, Baggiolini scale, BBCH 07/65/81, l. 135-139), not model-dated.
- Generalized centroid parameters (l. 37): budburst d -0.6, e 8.6; flowering d -0.6, e 13.7; veraison d -0.5, e 13.2.
- RMSE < 7 days; Lisboa budburst RMSE 5 d, EF 0.53 (l. 484).
- F printed for Lisboa varieties only: budburst 50.57-66.70, flowering 38.84-48.78, veraison 60.27-84.23 (l. 577-591); full list in Table S2, not held.
- Authors conclude step models such as GDD suit veraison poorly (l. 527).
