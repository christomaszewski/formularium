# Campos et al. 2021: canopy size of five unirrigated Alt Penedès vineyards from UAV and satellite

Manual canopy measurements on 1,400 vines at three stages in 2018 and 2019, against UAV
and PlanetScope NDVI. Read for its canopy dimensions in Penedès.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 164-187; Table 1 from the page image): Alt Penedès; plots A to D at
  Torrelavit (Chardonnay, Merlot, two Cabernet Sauvignon; 2018 and 2019), plot E at El Plà
  del Penedès (Macabeu, 2018 only). Rows 2.2 m (A to D) and 2.8 m (E), vines 1.2 m;
  double cordon, spur pruned, green-pruned when shoots pass 10 cm (l. 170-171);
  unirrigated; vines 21 to 31 years old (l. 172); soil harrowed (l. 173-174).
- **Method** (l. 445, 472-490): height and width by tape, three readings by two people,
  six a vine, at BBCH 59, 75 and 81; LWA and TRV computed from them.
- **Canopy by stage** (Figure 5, read off the box plots, approximate): height means about
  0.17, 0.31 and 0.32 m; width about 0.20, 0.46 and 0.45 m; LWA about 2,500, 7,500 and
  7,700 m²/ha; TRV about 300, 1,800 and 1,750 m³/ha. Size rose about 1.5 times to BBCH 75
  and then held (l. 967, 989).
- **Unsettled:** the plotted heights do not match the plotted LWA: at 2.2 m rows an LWA of
  7,500 m²/ha is a wall about 0.8 m high (my calculation), against 0.31 m in the height
  panel. Either the height is measured above the cordon or a panel is mislabelled; the
  paper does not say. Campos et al. 2019 print 1.02 m at BBCH 75 in a nearby vineyard.
- **Fits:** NDVI × projected area is the best UAV predictor (TRV rho 0.86, Table 4, l.
  1170); clustered R² 0.94 (TRV), 0.91 (LWA), 0.93 (height), 0.84 (width) (l. 1384);
  PlanetScope R² about 0.5 (l. 1698-1705).
- **Not printed:** leaf area per vine, LAI, shoots a vine, porosity, rainfall, budburst
  dates.

## Dependence

- The UAV method is the authors' own (campos2019; Campos et al. 2020); LWA and TRV are EPPO
  definitions. Fits are to the paper's own measurements. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Unirrigated Penedès canopies stop growing in size after pea size** (BBCH 75), and are
  about 0.45 m wide. Their height is unsettled here; campos2019 gives about 1 m. No leaf
  area per vine.
