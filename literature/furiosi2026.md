# Furiosi et al. 2026: a European survey of why farmers and advisers adopt or skip decision support systems

An online survey of 107 respondents across crops in 20 countries, split into 78 users and 29 non-users of decision support systems (DSS), with clustering of their answers.

**Read this first.** A survey of perceptions, not of behaviour, and not about grapes: it covers all crops, with a convenience and snowball sample of IPMWORKS hub coaches and social-media contacts that the authors call indicative of the sampled network and not a population estimate (l. 206). The spray-saving figures in it are cited from earlier work (Caffi et al. 2010; Rossi et al. 2014), not its own. Only the abstract, introduction, methods summary, results 3.1 and the discussion were read.

## What it holds

- **Sample:** an online survey of 107 respondents in 20 countries (57 % from Southern Europe): 78 classified as DSS users and 29 as non-users; all crops (l. 54).
- **Sampling caveat:** convenience and snowball sampling (IPMWORKS hub coaches, social media); the paper says its results are indicative of the sampled network, not population estimates (l. 206).
- **Main barrier:** no DSS tailored to the cropping system; limited awareness and concerns about cost were also important (l. 69).
- **Cited from Caffi et al. 2010:** following DSS recommendations potentially cut sprays by 36 and 75 % in grapevine powdery mildew (l. 127).
- **Cited from Rossi et al. 2014 (vite.net):** a 24 % reduction in downy mildew sprays on grapevine (l. 129), and in organic vineyards a cut in plant protection costs of EUR 195 per ha per year, from fewer treatments and lower doses (l. 136).

## Dependence

- Survey of perceptions. The saving numbers are cited from Caffi et al. 2010 and Rossi and Salinari 2014 (closed; not obtained here). Caffi is also an author of `rossi2019` and of held notes such as `caffi2010`, and Fedele of the held `fedele2020b`; a shared author is only a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Compliance (grower.dss_compliance):** 78 of 107 respondents are DSS users (73 %, arithmetic, not printed), but the sample is a DSS-network convenience sample across all crops, so this is not a share of growers who follow a warning. Do not use it as an uptake rate. Kind: survey, perceptions.
- **Reasons not to use a DSS:** no DSS for the cropping system, limited awareness, cost (l. 69); qualitative support for a compliance factor below 1 in a grower model, with no value.
- **Savings (grower.dss_saving):** the 24 % and EUR 195 per ha are Rossi et al. 2014's, cited (l. 129, 136); nothing new for grapes here.
- **Not shown:** spray counts, timing, delay, Catalonia.
