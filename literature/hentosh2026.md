# Hentosh & Harmash 2026: a temperature-sum forecast of powdery mildew near Odesa

## What it holds

- One farm near Baraboi, Odesa oblast, 2023-2025; 11 table-grape cultivars in untreated
  control plots (four replicates of ten vines).
- Spread (%) = 0.0276 x (sum of active temperatures above 10 °C) - 9.0397, R² 0.9956. The
  abstract prints 0.276, which does not reproduce Figure 2; Figure 2's six points are the
  2024 monthly sums, though the text says 2023-2025.
- Forecast first appearance against observed: 2 June against 5 June 2023, 28 May against 1
  June 2024, 18 May against 20 May 2025; the same seasons as the fit.
- Cultivars from sensitive (Sublima Seedless, Italiya, Super Nova) to tolerant (Black
  Magic, Cardinal, Michele Palieri, Muscat Hamburg).

## Dependence

- None. Its predictor is a sum above 10 °C, the Winkler index's form: a flag.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- For Cooptera's powdery mildew: a Black Sea steppe season, first symptoms in late May.
