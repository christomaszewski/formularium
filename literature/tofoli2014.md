# Töfoli et al. 2014: 20 mm of simulated rain after potato fungicide sprays

About twenty fungicides on potato leaf discs, with 20 mm of simulated rain 30 min to 4 h
after spraying, scored for late and early blight severity: how much of a spray's effect rain
removes, by product and drying time.

## What it holds

- Greenhouse and laboratory tests, two experiments on potato: late blight ('Agata') and
  early blight ('Monalisa') (l. 147, 209, 153-154).
- Spraying: CO2 pressurised, 3 bar, 300 L/ha, nozzle 0.5 m from target (l. 133-139).
- Rain: 20 mm for 6 min, at 30 min, 1, 2 and 4 h after spraying (l. 15-16, 163-165). Rate
  not printed; 200 mm/h implied (derived here).
- Experiment 1: 22 fungicides plus control, five intervals, six repetitions of 10 discs (l.
  149-171, 195). Late blight severity 1–5 scale, scored at 6 days (l. 185-191).
- Table 1 (late blight): no-rain treated severity 1.00 for all products; control 4.96 to
  5.00 (l. 304-305). Dimethomorph + ametoctradin 2.85, 2.44, 2.08, 1.85 at 30 min to 4 h (l.
  305). Mefenoxam + chlorothalonil 2.60 to 1.32 (l. 309). Mancozeb WP highest at every
  interval, 4.84 to 3.84 (l. 326). CV 14.52 % (l. 327).
- Experiment 2: 20 fungicides plus control, early blight 1–6 scale, scored at 7 days (l.
  210-240, 250).
- Table 2 (early blight): picoxystrobin 3.10, 2.48, 1.92, 1.20 (l. 368). Mancozeb WP 5.08 to
  4.14 (l. 387). Control 5.82–6.00 (l. 388). CV 14.23 % (l. 389).
- Systemic and translaminar fungicides, and those with inherent tenacity, were least
  affected by rain; contact fungicides more (l. 654-656). Mancozeb WP most affected (l.
  663-664).
- Not printed: per-class or per-interval loss figures, rain rate in mm/h, drying times, raw
  disc scores, any data licence.

## Dependence

- None: its own greenhouse and laboratory tests; the protocol follows Schepers, Nielsen &
  Duvauchelle 2004 and the rain prototype Santos 2000 (l. 156-162), methods, not models.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's grower and Cooptera's E6: rank-order evidence that rain soon after spraying
  removes contact products' protection (mancozeb WP worst at every interval) far more than
  systemic or translaminar ones, and that loss shrinks as the deposit dries from 30 min to 4
  h. Potato and one rain amount; no wash-off coefficient per mm.
