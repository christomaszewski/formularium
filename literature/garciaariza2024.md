# García-Ariza et al. 2024: ITACyL vineyard downy mildew support sheet

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Spanish grower sheet VI-E-01 (ITACyL, 2024), 4 pages, no data.
- Oospores overwinter in leaves and germinate in spring above 10-13 °C with rain above 10 mm; no source (l. 17-20).
- Primary infection enters through stomata on a wet surface (l. 22-23); secondary cycles best at 20-25 °C with repeated rain (l. 27).
- Largest damage after a warm, humid spring and a stormy summer (l. 28-29).
- Sensitive stages: leaf extension E to veraison M1; flowering and set I-J (l. 180-181).
- No predefined treatment threshold; preventive spray before flowering may help; the calendar marks April to August (l. 194-196; page 4).
