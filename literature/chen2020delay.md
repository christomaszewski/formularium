# Chen et al. 2020 (Sci. Rep.): chapter 6 of the thesis, published

The published form of [chen2019](chen2019.md)'s chapter 6. About 25 numbers checked
against the thesis; all match.

## What it holds

- Onset (1 % of vines) from a Cox model on 266 untreated IFV site-years, 2010-2017.
- A spray every two weeks from onset gives 3.4 sprays a year, 56.0 % fewer than the
  surveys' baseline (CI 51.0-61.3); 70.4 % fewer in dry springs, 55.6 % in wet.
- Operator exposure by EFSA's AOEM: the delay cuts exposure as much as protective
  equipment.
- The spring window is March-May in the methods and March-June in the results, as in the
  thesis.
