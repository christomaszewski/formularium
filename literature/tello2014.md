# Tello & Ibáñez 2014: indexes of grape bunch compactness

Eleven published and eight new bunch-compactness indexes, tested against panel scores of 110 bunches from 11 varieties.

## What it holds

- 110 bunches from 11 varieties, rated by 14 judges on the OIV descriptor 204 (l. 24, 82, 99).
- Best agreement with the panel's mode score: CI-19 (ρ = 0.618), CI-16 (0.612), CI-18 (0.611) and CI-12 (0.600) (l. 334–336).
- CI-12 uses two easy measures, bunch weight and length, and is proposed as a fast estimator for the sector (l. 39–40).

## Dependence

- Eleven indexes from the literature and eight new ones (l. 17–18). The visual reference is the OIV descriptor 204 (l. 24).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t4 and t7 as a trait only. Compact bunches favour bunch rot and moth damage (l. 50), but the paper has no disease or pest data. A growers' engine could take CI-12 as a bunch-risk input once tied to a disease study.
