# Di Gennaro et al. 2016: UAV NDVI for leaf stripe symptoms

UAV multispectral images at 0.05 m per pixel separated vines with leaf stripe symptoms from healthy ones in a Tuscan vineyard in 2012 and 2013. Vines below 20% severity were not detected.

## What it holds

- **Images:** 0.05 m per pixel, flown in June and July 2012 and 2013 (l. 26-30; l. 227-228).
- **Vineyard:** 500 vines in rows 50 m long, mapped since 2003 (l. 30-31; l. 196).
- **Detection floor:** vines with less than 20% severity were not detectable by the UAV survey (l. 169-173).
- **NDVI classes:** differences were significant from the first flights and largest at the July flight in both years (P < 0.001) (l. 367-371).
- **Ground scale:** 0 (no symptoms) to 5 (l. 179).

## Dependence

- NDVI as the index, from Rouse et al. 1973 (l. 147-148).
- UAV survey methods from Matese et al. 2015 (l. 146).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Strand t2 (observation) and t4 (other grape diseases):** the NDVI gap between classes could seed a scouting prompt. The 20% floor and the lack of an accuracy figure limit its use.
