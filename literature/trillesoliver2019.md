# Trilles et al. 2019: hourly warnings for four vine diseases from IoT weather nodes

Four vine disease warning models, run hourly on low-cost weather nodes at four Castelló smallholdings in 2018.

## What it holds

- Four models were adapted to run hourly: downy mildew, powdery mildew, black rot and Botrytis (l. 78–79).
- Four SEnviro nodes sent a reading every ten minutes for 130 days in 2018 (l. 100–103, 590–591).
- Alerts at 50 % (information), 75 % (warning) and 100 % (critical) of the accumulated incubation value (l. 251–253).
- In three cases, the value at sensor 1 reached 100 % before day 10 (l. 470–473). The paper does not check that the alerts matched real infections (l. 570).

## Dependence

- The models are adapted from earlier warning work (refs 12 to 15, l. 132–133). The SEnviro nodes come from ref 11 (l. 97).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Serves t4 (black rot, Botrytis), and the mildew warnings of t1 and t3. The hourly accumulation tables and three alert levels are a template for a grower alert, but no field check was made.
