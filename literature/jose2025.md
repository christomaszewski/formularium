# José & Postolache 2025: LoRaWAN packet delivery of 89 to 100 % to 1 km in rural Mozambique

A field test of one Arduino-based LoRaWAN node at four points up to 1 km from a gateway, in an area with no mains power or mobile network.

**Read this first.**
- Packet delivery, not lost days: one node, one session of under 24 h (the reading agent's note). It says nothing about a season.

## What it holds

- **Mean (l. 255):** "an average PDR of 94.25%" from a single-node deployment, four points.
- **By distance (l. 163, the table line, as the reading agent read it):** delivery 100, 97, 91 and 89 % at 100, 500, 750 and 1000 m.
- **Terrain (abstract, text file header):** signal strength and quality fall at lower altitudes; the authors advise placing gateways on natural elevations.
