# Rienth et al. 2021: pathogens and grape berry composition under climate change

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Review of fungal and viral effects on berry composition; downy mildew acts mostly by cutting leaf area (l. 36-39).
- Rain and relative humidity are key for DM; temperature drives mildew development (l. 67, 83).
- Cited models: higher temperature advances DM outbreak timing and adds cycles (l. 104-109).
- DM infects early berries, pre-bloom flowers, pedicels and rachises; mature berries are restricted by ontogenic resistance at véraison (l. 253-258).
- Berry studies on DM are few: DM after fruit set is largely absent, and studies focus on leaves (l. 260, 466).
