# Catania et al. 2011: wind and air-assisted spraying in an artificial vineyard

A laboratory artificial vineyard is used to test how wind changes spray coverage and leaf deposit.

## What it holds

- **Set-up:** an artificial vineyard of synthetic sprouts on an iron-tube frame in the Agricultural Mechanics laboratory, University of Palermo, tests in 2009; sprayer travel speed 3 km/h, working width 2.5 m (l. 82-98).
- **Result:** coverage did not show an effect of moderate wind (2 m/s), but leaf deposit showed an effect at 2 m/s and above (l. 16-19); at 5 m/s treatment efficiency fell by about 70 % against no wind (l. 20-22).

## Dependence

- Builds on air-assisted sprayer CFD and wind-tunnel work (Endalew et al. 2010, Cross et al. 2003) (l. 28-48).

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- Strand s3 (wind in and around rows): a laboratory interception result, not a field record of wind through rows; a simulator could take the drop in deposit with wind speed only for spray timing.
