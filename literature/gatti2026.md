# Gatti et al. 2026: vigor zones in a steep Barbera vineyard over three seasons

Proximal-sensing vigor maps at veraison for three years, with growth, water status and soil temperature in 2022 (l. 18-24). It is a vigour and yield study, not a microclimate network.

## What it holds

- **Site:** a 1.31 ha vineyard planted in 2015 in Pianello Val Tidone, Colli Piacentini (l. 228-231); slope about 30% (l. 239); seasons 2020-2022 (l. 228).
- **Water:** in low-vigor plots pre-dawn and midday leaf water potential reached -0.6 and -1.4 MPa (l. 29-30).
- **Size and yield:** low vigor was -49% in vine size and -62% in yield against high vigor (l. 33).
- **Soil temperature:** growth of low and high vigor diverged at a cross-over soil temperature of about 16 C (high) and 18 C (low) (l. 685-686).

## Dependence

- Uses an LCi canopy analyser and Saxton and Rawls (2006) for soil water; no model borrowed.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **s4 measured vineyard microclimate** (context): soil temperature and slope sit with vigor zones, but air T, RH and wetness by slope or aspect are not the aim. Not read in full.
