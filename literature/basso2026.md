# Basso et al. 2026: holographic AI spore counts to time sprays

## What it holds

- Holographic spore counts against qPCR at Changins (2024): r = 0.68 for downy mildew, 71 pairs (l. 329-331).
- Chateau Le Puy 2021: 400 g/ha less copper (about 13%), incidence about 80% of leaves and 50% of bunches (l. 437-441).
- 2022: spray decision at 10 sporangia in 3 hours for three consecutive days; 30% less fungicide, with comparable yield (l. 446-456).
- Thresholds from the literature: 2.52 viable sporangia/m3 (l. 153); 1 to 15 sporangia/m3 across methods (l. 618).
- Own observation: 5-10 sporangia/m3 commonly associated with symptoms (l. 671).
- Savings estimate: about EUR 3000 a year on 10 ha at a 30% cut (l. 664-665).
