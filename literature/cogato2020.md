# Cogato et al. 2020: Sentinel-2 and late frost damage

Research article, Veneto, northern Italy (Vicenza province area); frost of 2017, images 2018-2019.

## What it holds

- Sentinel-2 vegetation indices and bands compared between a frost-affected area and a control area in northern Italian vineyards after the 2017 late frost, with percent differences and a recovery time of about 40 days, plus must-quality differences.
- It is damage detection by satellite, not disease, and is of indirect use for image detection accuracy.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Frost damage visible for about 40 days in Sentinel-2 indices; not disease. Little.
