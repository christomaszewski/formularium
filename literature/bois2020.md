# Bois et al. 2020: rain differences among 45 gauges over 28 km2 of Burgundy vineyards

A dense gauge network in Burgundy vineyards, 2014 to 2016, used to ask how much rain differs from place to place within a few kilometres and what that does to a soil-water balance.

## What it holds

- **Network (l. 25, 299):** 45 tipping-bucket rain gauges over 28 km2, extent 6.3 km north-south and 6.1 km west-east (l. 299), in Burgundy vineyards, 2014 to 2016. Separations range from 0.1 to 6 km (reading agent's reading).
- **Largest single-day spread (l. 292):** the range of daily rain across the 45 gauges reached 42.1 mm on 2016-05-09, and a second date is cut off in the quote; showers and storms.
