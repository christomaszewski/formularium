# Mandelli et al. (n.d.): subperiod lengths and degree-days for eleven cultivars at Bento Gonçalves

An Embrapa Uva e Vinho report, undated in the copy (its references end in 1994): ten
seasons of eleven cultivars at the germplasm bank, summarised as the length in days and
the degree-days above 10 °C of each subperiod from budbreak to leaf fall, with maturity
classes by heat. Tables, not a model.

## What it holds

- **Data:** Bento Gonçalves, 29°09′S, 640 m; 1984/85 to 1993/94 (two cultivars 1985-1995,
  one 1989-1997 per the footnotes); Chardonnay, Moscato Branco, Riesling Itálico,
  Trebbiano, Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Pinot Noir, Concord, Niágara
  Branca, Ives on 101-14, five or six plants each, scored on Lorenz et al. 1994's scale;
  the station beside the collection (l. 31-56, 88).
- **Lengths** (Table 1): budbreak start to end 29 d, flowering 16 d (Trebbiano 13,
  Chardonnay and Cabernet Sauvignon 18), maturation 37 d (Pinot Noir 26, Cabernet
  Sauvignon 48); budbreak to end of maturation 142 (Pinot Noir, Ives) to 163 d (Cabernet
  Franc), mean 152, SD 8 d against Jones & Davis 2000's 19 d for Bordeaux; to leaf fall
  245 (Concord) to 280 d (Pinot Noir) (l. 62-112, 148).
- **Heat** (Table 2; Winkler's sum above 10 °C of daily means, l. 50-52): budbreak to
  maturity 1,248 (Pinot Noir) to 1,560 °C·d (Moscato Branco), to leaf fall 2,255
  (Concord) to 2,416 (Pinot Noir); classes under 1,385, 1,385-1,485 and over 1,485 °C·d
  (l. 117-146). No calendar dates, no per-season values, no rule for days below the base.

## Dependence

- **None:** descriptive; the same Embrapa series as `felippeto2013` for Cabernet
  Sauvignon.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- Subtropical reference values (Chardonnay, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon among them) for
  the heat each subperiod takes; nothing to run.
