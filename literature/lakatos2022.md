# Lakatos & Mika 2022: dryness indices and wine grape yield in Hungary

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Five dryness indices (NDD, CDDmax, CWB, DI, VWI) are tested against yields in 22 Hungarian wine regions, 2005-2021 (l. 21, 1419).
- Residual yield series show a significant quadratic fit in slightly over 50% of index-region pairs, 29% and 27% for the short series (l. 24-25).
- Per-index significance rates: CWB 81.8% (l. 1429), DI 72.7%, VWI 68.2%, NDD 36.4% and CDDmax 22.7% (l. 1427-1431).
- Future weather comes from ten ENSEMBLES RCMs; the periods are 1986-2005, 2016-2035 and 2081-2100 (l. 183, 19).
- Potential evapotranspiration uses Hargreaves-Samani (l. 374).
- The authors expect lower, more variable yields and fewer sprays under drier futures (l. 27, 2061).
- The row of pooled rates (51.8%) does not reconcile with its five rows (l. 1432).

## Dependence

- none. The paper computes Hargreaves-Samani potential evapotranspiration (l. 374) and the Dryness Index, CWB, VWI, NDD and CDDmax. None of these is an engine model. It never computes or fits an incubation, infection, sporulation, wetness or phenology model. The only disease statements are qualitative and cite no model (l. 2070-2072).

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- None for the truth or the engine beyond a citable, uncomputed remark that downy mildew follows rainfall (l. 2070).
