# Steiger 2010: economics of grape powdery and downy mildew protection

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Bayer farmer-level market data: in 2008, 20.5 million treated hectares of pure powdery mildew, 19.4 million of pure downy mildew and 1.4 million mixed (l. 15, 16, 19).
- Downy mildew was 33% of grape protection spending over 2005-2008, powdery mildew about 22% (l. 24, 25).
- Downy mildew protection averages 22 € per hectare, from 10 to over 45 €/ha by product (l. 55, 57).
- Europe takes over 90% of downy mildew product use (l. 40).
- A mixed product costs 25-50 €/ha, about the cost of two separate products (l. 96).

## Dependence

- verdict: none; text: No epidemiological or weather model is named, computed or fitted. The data are market and cost figures from one company.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)

- Offers the truth only a cost frame (22 €/ha average downy protection; 2008 treated hectares), which a spray-economics comparison might use; it holds no disease observations.
