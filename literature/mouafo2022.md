# Mouafo-Tchinda et al. 2022: two clades competing

From Carisse's AAFC laboratory (Saint-Jean-sur-Richelieu) and the Université de
Sherbrooke.

## What it holds

- Mixes of clade riparia and clade aestivalis (qPCR) on leaf discs (8 cycles) and living
  leaves (5 cycles) of cultivar Vidal, at one condition only: 20 °C, 95-100 % RH.
- Aestivalis out-produced riparia after two cycles whatever the starting mix; the share of
  riparia sporangia decays with cycles (Table 1, a fit per mix). An index of aggressiveness,
  IA = ln(incidence × sporulation efficiency / latency).
- No temperature or wetness response.

## Dependence

- Independent of the engine: its own data, no engine model used, no shared author.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- Little for European vineyards, whose populations are thought to be one lineage (from
  memory). Its decay fit could shape a simulation of two competing strains (for example
  fungicide-resistant and sensitive), if Agrarium ever needs one.
- The same team's 2021 temperature paper (Can. J. Plant Pathol.) is the one to read for
  incubation or sporulation responses.
