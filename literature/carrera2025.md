# Carrera et al. 2025: two seasons of airborne sporangia in three Galician vineyards

Open access (MDPI), fetched 2026-10-12. The Vigo group's continuation of
`fernandezgonzalez2011` and `cortinas2020`: daily Hirst-trap sporangia at an ultra-oceanic
site (Areeiro, Rías Baixas, Albariño) and two subcontinental ones (Alongos and Cenlle,
O Ribeiro, Treixadura) in 2023 and 2024, against on-site weather.

## What it holds

- **Sampling:** Lanzoni VPPS-2000 samplers at 2 m, 10 L/min, sporangia counted at 400×
  (17-40 µm long) as daily sporangia/m³ (l. 216-259); HOBO stations on site for
  temperature, RH and dew point, rain and wind from the nearest MeteoGalicia stations
  (l. 263-267); the cycle split into phenological stages (Table 1: Alongos 2023, 142 d;
  mean cycles 177, 175 and 155-159 d; l. 294-300).
- **Counts:** season totals 2023 of 53,447 sporangia/m³ at Areeiro against 2,483 at
  Alongos and 1,581 at Cenlle, and 37 %, 47 % and 71 % less in 2024 (l. 304-311); Areeiro
  2023 peaks of 1,045 (7 June), 1,474 (13 June), 1,193 and 1,454 (7 and 24 July) and a
  maximum of 5,604 on 29 May at flowering, with 17,303 (32 %) in the five days 28 May-1
  June (l. 392-417); Alongos's 2023 peak 455 (Table 2, l. 348).
- **Climate:** Currey's continentality index 0.43 at Areeiro (ultra-oceanic), 1.52 and 1.65
  at Alongos and Cenlle (l. 272, 475-477); mean RH 83, 70 and 75 %, mean rain 475 L/m² at
  Areeiro against 247 and 288-321 (text and Table 3 differ for Cenlle), mean temperature
  17.1-19.1 °C (l. 483-488, 888).
- **Correlations** (Spearman, Table 4 keeps only the best lag): the strongest are with
  temperature, r 0.626 for the 7-day mean at Areeiro in 2024 and 0.627 for the same-day
  mean at Alongos in 2023; RH positive at Areeiro in 2023 and negative in 2024, rain
  negative in 2024 (l. 522-542); the results call the water variables weaker, the abstract
  stronger at the oceanic site (l. 24-25, 524). PCA: two components explain about 74 %
  (l. 714).
- **Cited, not computed:** sporulation at 13 °C minimum, 19 °C optimum, RH above 95 % and
  about 4 h of darkness (Gessler et al. 2011, l. 884-885); Albariño's susceptibility
  (Boso et al., l. 893). The Goidanich index appears only in a reference title (l. 1092).
  Several citations do not match the claims they carry (l. 69, 893, 900, 926, 935, 948).

## Dependence

- **Its own counts and weather,** observed; no model dated or chose anything.
- **Engine pieces:** none computed. The authors' earlier Goidanich-index work
  (Fernández-González et al. 2019) is cited, not run.
- **Authors:** Fernández-González and Rodríguez-Rajo: `fernandezgonzalez2009`,
  `fernandezgonzalez2011`, `cortinas2020` flags.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- A dated airborne-inoculum series with weather for two Galician climates, the oceanic
  site twenty times richer in sporangia: an observation a truth's dispersal layer could
  be checked against, with the finding that temperature, not humidity, tracks the daily
  counts.
