# Bläser 1978: the dissertation behind Blaeser & Weltzien 1978 and 1979

Only the contents pages are held. They show where the data the engine's survival and
infection rest on are printed in full.

## What it holds (the contents)

- **3. Methods (pp. 11-23):** laboratory (host plants, inoculation, germination test, dew
  chamber, setting RH) and field (sites, instruments, scoring, spore traps), statistics.
- **4.1 The least leaf wetness for infection (pp. 24-28):** with a sporangial suspension
  and with dry inoculation. This is the source of the 60 °C·h rule (Blaeser & Weltzien
  1979, Tab. 1, `blaeser1979`).
- **4.2 Severity by temperature, wetness and leaf age (pp. 29-32).**
- **4.3 Sporulation by temperature, RH, light and on wetted leaves (pp. 33-42).**
- **4.4 Release and spread of sporangia by wind and water (pp. 42-46).**
- **4.5 The lifetime of sporangia (pp. 47-60):** on the leaf (4.5.1, p. 49) and detached
  (4.5.2, p. 56). These are the data behind the survival polynomials
  (`blaeser1978.survival`), which the engine's `blaeser1979.survival` computes.
- **4.6 The fungicide Curzate (pp. 60-66).**
- **5. Field studies (pp. 67-103):**
  - mildew in German wine regions, 1975-1977;
  - control on selected farms;
  - the weather;
  - temperature and leaf wetness from dew and rain at several sites (5.4, pp. 78-103).
- Discussion p. 104, summary p. 113, references p. 115.

## Dependence

- The primary source of `blaeser1978.survival` and `blaeser1979`, the data under the
  engine's survival, its wet degree-hour infection and Brischetto 2021's Magarey parameters.
- Hoppmann & Wittich 1997 call Bläser 1978 the laboratory basis of the Geisenheim model and
  of its survival function ([hoppmann1997](hoppmann1997.md)).
