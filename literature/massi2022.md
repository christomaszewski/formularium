# Massi et al. 2022: single-sporangium infectivity by flow cytometry

Research article, Not stated in the lines read; authors at the University of Milan, Italy.

## What it holds

- A flow cytometry and FACS method for sorting intact P. viticola sporangia and inoculating single sporangia on leaf discs.
- In the authors' conditions 8% of sporangia infected, and 50% of total infection was reached in about 10 days on a susceptible variety.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- About 8% of single sporangia infected on leaf discs at 22 °C: a bound on infection efficiency, from Toffolatti's laboratory (a flag through Fedele 2025).
