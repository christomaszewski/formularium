# Wang et al. 2019: re-fitting RH, dew point depression and CART thresholds, and a neural network, for greenhouse leaf wetness

Four station-type wetness models are calibrated with a decision tree and tested on later data in a tomato greenhouse (Almeria) and a cucumber greenhouse (Beijing).

**Read this first.** These are greenhouses, not open air, and the inputs are measured inside. The text layer has overprinted duplicates; the tables and Eqs. 1-2 are from the page images. The discussion swaps the sites for the uncalibrated neural network error (l. 1674); Table 7 has Spain 5.10 h and China 5.38 h. The neural network's calibration-set MAE of 0.23 and 0.34 h (Table 7, page image) is not a skill estimate.

## What it holds

- **Sites:** tomato, plastic greenhouse of 877 m2 in Almeria, Spain, leaf wetness 2016-2018; cucumber, solar greenhouse of 210 m2 in Beijing, 2014-2015 (l. 214 and page 4 image). Air temperature and RH at 2.0 m (Spain, Vaisala HMP45P) and 1.5 m (China), every 15 min in China (page 4 image).
- **Reference sensors:** Spain, two Decagon dielectric sensors at the bottom and top of the canopy at 30 degrees; China, five Davis 6420 sensors at the leaf apex, both sides, underside and 0.05 m below, mid-canopy. A leaf is wet when 50 % of the samples in an hour exceed the sensor threshold (page 4 image).
- **RHM:** hours with RH at or above a constant threshold, set to 90 % (l. 513; Table 1 and page 5 image); quoted as the Sentelhas et al. practice (l. 81). Calibrated by decision tree to 84.53 % (Spain) and 80 % (China) (l. 1344).
- **DPM:** dew when air temperature minus dew point is under 2 C; calibrated to 2.7 C (Spain) and 5.42 C (China) (Table 5, page 13 image).
- **CART:** Eqs. 1-2 of Gleason et al./Kim et al. kept unchanged, thresholds 14.4674 and 37 recalibrated (l. 833, l. 846). Page 6 image prints Eq. 1 as 1.6064 T^0.5 + 0.0036 T^2 + 0.1531 RH - 0.4599 WD x DPD - 0.0035 T x RH > 14.4674 and Eq. 2 as 0.7921 T^0.5 + 0.0046 RH^2 - 2.3889 WD - 0.0390 T x WD + 1.0613 WD x DPD > 37.0000. Calibrated: DPD 3.7 C to 2.7 C (Spain) and 6.3 C (China), and RH 87.8 % to 84.53 % and 80 % (l. 1469, page 13 image).
- **Calibration-set MAE (h per day):** RHM Spain 7.12 to 5.31 and China 6.12 to 2.73 (l. 1348); CART calibrated 4.60 (Spain) and 2.13 (China) (l. 1480).
- **Independent set** (Spain March 2017-February 2018, China December 2014-September 2015; Table 8, page 16 image): MAE Spain RHM 5.45, DPM 5.46, CART 3.46, NNM 1.85; China RHM 3.42, DPM 3.29, CART 2.75, NNM 1.30 (l. 1645; l. 33). PPV for RHM 0.71 (Spain) and 0.85 (China); accuracy 0.73 and 0.87.
- **NNM inputs:** with T, RH, rainfall, global radiation and wind the independent MAE was 5.10 and 5.38 h; the good fit needed RH, dew temperature, transpiration and radiation, which are not standard station variables (l. 1674, Table 7).
- **Cited, not run:** Sentelhas et al. Penman-Monteith MAE about 1.6 h (l. 1662); other sites' calibrated RH thresholds 94 % (Colombia, l. 87), 84 % (Zimbabwe).
- **Release:** none stated; funded by the EU IoF2020 project (l. 1731); article is open access.

## Dependence

- RHM, DPM and CART are existing models (Sentelhas et al. 2008, Huber and Gillespie 1992, Gleason et al. 1994 via Kim et al. 2002); only the thresholds are re-fitted here. Same CART coefficients as park2019 and gillespie2021.
- Same form as sentelhas2008.wetness and cooptera.rh90_wetness (RH threshold, dew point depression); kin by threshold form. The NNM is an own fit.

## Bearing (2026-10-27)

- **RH threshold error:** an RH threshold of 90 % had MAE 5.45 h (Spain) and 3.42 h (China) per day on held-out later data, against 5.31 and 2.73 h after re-fit on the calibration set. The best fixed threshold was 80-85 %, not 90 %. For tomato and cucumber under glass, so it supports only the direction (a local threshold helps), not a value for vines in the open.
- **Not shown:** open-field vines, or a Penman-Monteith run. Kind of result: re-fit on two small greenhouse data sets, tested on a later period of the same greenhouses.
