# Lecourieux et al. 2020: proteins and metabolites of Cabernet Sauvignon berries heated by 8 C at three stages

**Read this first.** A proteomics paper on the same berry pools as ouakedlecourieux2017. It measures no microclimate. The temperature facts are one line.

## What it holds

- **Treatment (l. 117-119, 650):** fruit-bearing cuttings of Cabernet Sauvignon; berries at three stages exposed 12 h a day (07:00 to 19:00), sampled after 1 and 7 days; "the average pulp temperature of the heat-treated berries was about 8 C higher than in control berries". Protocol, sensors and temperatures are deferred to "Lecourieux et al. (2017)", which is ouakedlecourieux2017.
- **Introduction (l. 79):** fruit temperature "may differ very significantly from that of the whole plant or surrounding air" (cites Cola et al. 2009).
- **Stated aim:** to learn what high temperature, "inherent to leaf removal", does to berries.

## Dependence

- Same plants and pools as ouakedlecourieux2017 and the rig of pillet2012. Not independent evidence of any temperature.

## Bearing (2026-10-30)

- None for the truth or the engine. The temperature figure (+8 C) is the same as in ouakedlecourieux2017, so it should not be counted twice.
