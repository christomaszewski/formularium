# Muir 2019: tealeaves, a leaf energy budget in R, with free, forced and mixed convection written out

A software paper. It prints the leaf Nusselt-number coefficients that Monteith & Unsworth give, the way the two modes are combined, and the criterion for which mode rules.

**Read this first.** Not a wetness paper and not a measurement. The coefficients are Monteith & Unsworth 2013's (not held) and the criterion is Nobel 2009's (not held), both quoted second hand. Water-vapour exchange is by analogy (Sherwood for Nusselt).

## What it holds

- **Mixed convection (Eqs 9-11):** Nu^3.5 = Nu_forced^3.5 + Nu_free^3.5, Nu_forced = a Re^b, Nu_free = c Gr^d (l. 372).
- **Forced, laminar (Re < 4000):** a = 0.6, b = 0.5; turbulent above: a = 0.032, b = 0.8. The cut-off is lower than for plates because leaf hairs and veins trip the flow (l. 372).
- **Free, laminar:** d = 0.25; c = 0.5 for the upper surface when the leaf is warmer than the air, or the lower surface when it is cooler; c = 0.23 for the other two cases (l. 372).
- **Criterion:** Ar = Gr/Re^2 << 0.1, free convection dominates; Ar >> 10, forced dominates (l. 372).
- **Gr uses virtual temperatures** (Eqs 12, 14-15): a saturated leaf airspace is lighter than the air, which adds buoyancy to an evaporating leaf (l. 373-377).

## Dependence

Equations after Monteith & Unsworth 2013 and Nobel 2009. Shares nothing with an engine model.
