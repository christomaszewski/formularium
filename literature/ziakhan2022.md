# Zia-Khan et al. 2022: thermal images of a downy mildew outbreak in Hohenheim

Thermal imaging of an unsprayed Pinot Meunier plot through a natural downy mildew epidemic
in 2020, against disease levels.

## What it holds

- Field study on Pinot Meunier at Hohenheim, Germany, 2020: 250 m² plot, 4 rows of 25 plants
  (l. 123, 125).
- Thermal (InfraTec VarioCAM) images on sunny days, 22 July to 8 September 2020; emissivity
  0.95 (l. 267, 299).
- Abstract: 3.2 °C increase in leaf temperature 'long before visible symptoms' (l. 22); the
  body gives a canopy rise on the last day against the start (l. 731), undated; the
  conclusion repeats the timing claim (l. 883).
- Thermal measurements stopped once symptoms were clear (l. 591); first symptoms at the end
  of July, 30 to 40% incidence a month later with no fungicide (page 6 image; l. 555).
- MTD against disease level: R2 = 0.76, p ≤ 0.001 (l. 23); ACT and CTD correlations poor and
  not significant (l. 812).
- Sunlit MTD higher than shaded, p ≤ 0.001 (l. 395).
- Wet period: mean RH above 85% (l. 244); LWD 9 to 24 h (l. 616); outbreak at 20 to 25 °C
  (l. 625).
- No inoculation and no day-after-inoculation result; data statement 'Not applicable' (l.
  976).

## Dependence

- Its own field measurements; no inoculation and no model.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For a thermal sensor operator: maximum temperature difference across the canopy tracked
  disease (R² 0.76) but mean canopy temperature did not; the '3.2 °C long before visible
  symptoms' of the abstract is a canopy value on an undated last day, and imaging stopped
  once symptoms were clear, so no presymptomatic lead time is shown.
