# Pla & Nacci 2001: vineyard slopes and layout in Anoia-Alt Penedès, and what mechanisation changed

A short conference paper on erosion in Anoia-Alt Penedès vineyards, with a description of the terrain and of the change from contour terraces to mechanised straight rows.

**Read this first.**
- The first page prints the year 2001 and the venue (Sustaining the Global Farm, Purdue and USDA-ARS). The statements below are the authors' description of the area, not a measurement made for this paper; the reading agent found no model and no data of its own.

## What it holds

- **Slope of cropped land:** the area is "highly undulating and even hilly", with cropped areas on 4-20 % slopes (l. 47).
- **Traditional layout:** free-growing vines planted along the contour lines, generally on bench terraces supported by stone walls (cited to Ramos & Porta 1997), in small fields surrounded by drainage ditches (l. 52).
- **New layout:** in the last 10-20 years, new plantations with wire-frame vine control, in straight and long rows more than 2 m apart, built for mechanised operations including harvest (l. 56).
- **What it required:** larger fields, smoothing of slopes, and the removal of the traditional bench terraces and other conservation practices (l. 59).
- No field size or row azimuth is given in the passages read.

## Bearing (2026-11-04)

- **terrain.slope_pct and site layout (Agrarium):** 4-20 % slopes for Alt Penedès vineyard fields, as an author's description (a review statement, not a measurement). Levelling has smoothed slopes since the 1990s, so older slope maps overstate current slope.
