# Trefilová et al. 2026: qRT-PCR counts of hop sporangia in spore traps

Three Czech hop yards in 2021 and 2022: a qRT-PCR assay reads Pseudoperonospora humuli sporangia from volumetric spore traps.

## What it holds

- The qPhu2* assay was chosen for monitoring; its fit to sporangia counts had R² = 0.7958 (l. 431–432).
- Sporangia peaked 1 to 2 weeks after the peak of the hop weather index (l. 29–31).

## Dependence

- Compares the trap data with the short-term forecast of the hop weather index, which the paper calls a modified method (ref 5).

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Off the point: this is a hop pathogen, not a grape one, so only the trap-plus-qPCR method could be borrowed by a grape tool.
