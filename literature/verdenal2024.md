# Verdenal et al. 2024: removing main leaves or laterals before flowering, Petite Arvine

Four pre-flowering treatments in the cluster zone (laterals removed; half or all of the main
leaves removed; main leaves removed and laterals kept), 2016-2021. Read for main against
lateral leaf area.

## What it holds

- **Site** (l. 130-160): Petite Arvine on 3309C planted 2011, Agroscope at Leytron (Valais),
  480 m; 9.7 °C, 580 mm (normals); Guyot, 1.8 × 0.9 m (6,200 vines/ha).
- **Exposed leaf area** (Eq. 1, l. 228, page image: ((2H + W)(1 − T))/E; August): 1.16, 1.20,
  1.09 and 1.34 m²/m² of ground over 2016-2021 by treatment (l. 354), 1.1 to 1.3 by year
  (l. 429). Keeping laterals in place of the main leaves gave 15 % more (l. 42).
- **Ratios** (l. 361-362): 1.1 to 1.9 m² of exposed leaf per kg of fruit; prunings 44 to 48 g
  per m of shoot.
- **Not printed:** total leaf area, leaves a shoot, lateral leaf area (laterals were not
  measured), canopy height and width values.

## Dependence

- Eq. 1 is Carbonneau's exposed-area formula (not credited at the line). Fits are the
  authors' own.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **No main-against-lateral split printed.** Laterals regrowing in the cluster zone make up
  for removed main leaves (+15 % exposed area), a point for a viewer that draws laterals.
