# Marcianò et al. 2023: nitrogen supply and grapevine susceptibility to downy mildew

A light read (the deep-research B list).

## What it holds

- Greenhouse grapevine plants grown at five nitrate levels (0, 0.05, 1, 2, 5 mM Ca(NO3)2) and sampled at 7, 17 and 27 days (l. 117-119).
- Leaf N correlated with sporulated area: Kendall tau 0.39, Spearman rho 0.52, n = 45 (l. 387).
- Sporulation lowest at 0 mM N (Kruskal-Wallis KW = 35.02, p < 0.0001) (l. 552).
- At 27 days, sporulated area 2.99 mm2 at 0 mM and 15.37 mm2 at 2 mM; sporangia 56.97 and 244.04 per mm2 (Table 2, l. 493, 508).
- Text claims a 4-fold drop at 0 mM; the table gives 5.1-fold (l. 464).
- Photosynthetic efficiency highest at 2 mM (l. 888).
- Expression of VvNRT3.1, VvGS and VvAS1 not changed by N; named as candidate genes (l. 1250).
- Inoculum: three northern Italian P. viticola populations, 5 x 10^5 sporangia per mL, seven days at 22 C (l. 1145-1149).

## Dependence

- none. A greenhouse nitrogen experiment; no model is computed, fitted or shared in form. The inoculation protocol uses leaf discs in Petri dishes, not an infection model.

## Bearing (2026-10-10)
