# Chambres d'Agriculture France 2023: the French machinery cost reference, with vineyard sprayer work rates in ha/h and costs per ha

A machinery cost reference (the "barème d'entraide") whose vineyard tables give, for each sprayer type, a work rate in ha/h and a cost per ha with and without labour.

**Read this first.** Grey literature: a cost reference compiled by advisers, not a study. Five caveats. (1) The performances are "field averages given as an indication" and the annual areas are "technically feasible" volumes, from advisers' experience, not timed trials (l. 41). (2) Sprayer areas are "deployed" areas, each pass counted (l. 41), so the cost per ha is a cost per ha and per pass. (3) The cost is machinery, fuel and driver labour only; the plant protection product is not in it. (4) The text layer does not define the codes VL (wide vines) or VE (narrow vines); only VTE (very narrow, inter-rows 0.90 to 1.30 m) is defined (l. 7365). The column meaning was checked on the page image of the VTE page only; the VL and VE pages were read from the text layer. (5) Prices are 2023 euros and French; the chambers named in the text are those numbered 10, 30, 33, 34 and 89 (department numbers as printed).

## What it holds

- **Method:** sprayer areas are deployed areas (each pass counted); performances are field averages given as an indication; annual work volumes are technically feasible and consistent with the days available (l. 41).
- **Wide-row vines (VL), towed air-blast (jet porté) sprayer, 1,000 L:** 2.00 ha/h; 27.4 EUR/ha without and 36.4 EUR/ha with labour at 125 ha a year (22.8 and 31.8 at 200 ha; 20.7 and 29.7 at 275 ha) (l. 7553).
- **Wide-row, other machines:** towed pneumatic 1,000 L 2.00 ha/h, 37.8 EUR/ha with labour at 150 ha a year (l. 7544); confined (recovery-panel) sprayer, 2 rows, 4 panels, 1.80 ha/h, 52.5 EUR/ha with labour at 200 ha a year (l. 7567); sprayer on a self-propelled carrier 2.50 ha/h, 50.5 EUR/ha with labour at 300 ha a year (l. 7585).
- **Small-farm case:** a simple tank-and-pump kit with recovery panels, 1.00 ha/h; 79.9 EUR/ha with labour at 20 ha a year (58.7 at 40 ha, 51.7 at 60 ha) (l. 7526).
- **Narrow vines (VE):** 6-row mounted jet sprayer 1.50 ha/h, 35.9 EUR/ha with labour at 120 ha a year; pneumatic 6-row 1.50 and 7-row 1.60 ha/h in the same table (l. 7454).
- **Very narrow vines (VTE):** 7-row face-to-face air-blast sprayer on a straddle tractor 2.00 ha/h, 63.3 EUR/ha with labour at 100 ha a year; the 9-row version 2.60 ha/h; pneumatic over the top, 7 rows 2.40 and 9 rows 3.00 ha/h (l. 7383).

## Dependence

- Built from advisers' field experience, not from a model or from timed trials. The DEPHY data brief (held note `nefti2024data`) says its work rates in h/ha come from the 2017 edition of this reference, so the two are not independent sources of work rates (cited from that note, l. 352 there).

## Bearing (2026-10-25)

- **Sprayer capacity (spray.work_rate_ha_h):** 2.0 ha/h for towed air-blast and pneumatic sprayers in wide-row vines, 1.8 for the confined type, 2.5 on a carrier, 1.0 for a simple panel kit (l. 7553, 7544, 7567, 7585, 7526). Supports a capacity of 1 to 2.5 ha/h per machine in a farm-manager model. Kind: adviser reference, French, not Penedès, not measured in trials; row spacing for the Penedès is not given here. Derived, not printed (arithmetic, not printed): a 10 ha block needs 5 machine-hours per pass at 2 ha/h and 10 at 1 ha/h.
- **Cost per pass (spray.cost_per_pass, 90 EUR/ha estimate):** machinery, fuel and driver cost 36.4 to 52.5 EUR/ha for the towed and confined machines at 125 to 200 ha a year (l. 7553, 7544, 7567) and 79.9 EUR/ha for the 20 ha kit (l. 7526), product excluded. Against 90 EUR/ha, the product would then be about 10 EUR/ha (small kit) to about 54 EUR/ha (large machine) (arithmetic, not printed). Kind: reference costs, 2023, France.
- **Not shown:** time lost to wind, rain, weekends or competing work; any warning-to-spray delay; sprayer capacity in Penedès row layouts.
