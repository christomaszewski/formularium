# Benet Porta 2025: Macabeu, Malvasia de Sitges and Trobat in Costers del Segre

An undergraduate thesis characterising three historic varieties at L'Olivera (Vallbona de
les Monges) over 2024: Macabeu on vertical trellis against Lys, Malvasia de Sitges on
single and double curtain, Trobat by vigour. Read only for leaf area.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 573-578, 605-607): Malvasia at 3 × 1 m, vines 1.60 m high, planted 2011;
  Trobat at 2.5 × 0.8 m on Monastrell planted 2007.
- **Leaf area:** no measurement found. The text says the vertical trellis has less leaf area
  than the Lys (l. 1292-1294), without numbers.
