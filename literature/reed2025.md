# Reed et al. 2025: clustering AmeriFlux sites by the dynamics of their fluxes (dynamic time warping)

Groups 343 eddy-covariance sites by composite annual curves of fluxes and meteorology, and scores each site's uniqueness. Not a placement method, by the authors' own account. No record.

**Read this first.** Not a site-placement method: the authors say it cannot estimate which parts of the network are poorly represented and call it "a parallel method" (l. 864, 868). Equations 1 to 3 are garbled in the text layer: the DTW cost d_phi is not written out, the min and max sets of Eq. 2 are inferred, and Eq. 3 lost its superscript and sum limits (garbled/unreconciled). The linkage method is not named. The Supplement (Tables S1-S9, Figs S1-S68, Text S3 with the validity indices) was not read.

## What it holds

- **Sites** (l. 445, 568-569): 343 sites with at least one flux variable, 191 with at least 6 years. Univariate runs use 215 (SWC) to 338 (USTAR) sites; the multivariate run uses 313.
- **Feature** (l. 471, 482): all years pooled into 52 non-overlapping 7-day periods, median diel curve per period at 2-hour steps; a site-variable is dropped if interpolation fills more than 20 % of gaps. Other windows (15-day/1-h, 5-day/3-h) changed pair distances marginally (l. 488-491).
- **Distance and clusters** (Eq. 1-2, PDF page 7; l. 548-571): dynamic time warping per variable, min-max normalised over site pairs; hierarchical clustering with the number of clusters chosen by six validity indices over 30 to 75 clusters (l. 559-560).
- **Groups** (l. 618, 634, 650, 654, 660, 666): NEE has one group of 156 sites; LE two of 114 and 110; H two of 121 and 119; USTAR one of 174. Multivariate: largest group 70 sites; 84 sites sit in 58 small groups.
- **Uniqueness** (Eq. 3, PDF page 8; l. 576-577, 712): the Harmonic Uniqueness Parameter is a site's mean normalised DTW distance to all others; most values 0.15 to 0.35; above 0.24 is flagged (about 13 mountain sites, urban and open-water sites).
- **Checks** (l. 583, 612): ANOVA and Tukey tests on DTW distances within against across IGBP and ecoregion groups; every multivariate group holds at least one NEON or AmeriFlux Core site.

## Dependence

- DTW after Giorgino (2009), R packages proxy and dtwclust; cluster validity indices after Arbelaitz et al. (2013) (l. 559). Frames itself against Villarreal & Vargas (2021), who find optimal added sites could raise Latin American FLUXNET representativeness from about 30-40 % to 80-85 % (l. 387, cited, not tested here).

## Bearing (2026-10-21)

Representative-site clustering (a similarity view of an existing network), for Agrarium's placement-methods study (PLAN 15, D48).

- **Features, k and representative:** features are composite diel-seasonal curves compared by DTW, variable by variable. k comes from validity indices over 30 to 75. No representative is chosen; HUP ranks uniqueness only.
- **Validation:** no held-out sites, no reconstruction error, no comparison with random, entropy or kriging layouts; only consistency checks.
- **Takeable:** DTW distance for series with shifted timing, and a per-site uniqueness score for spotting sites that no other site stands in for. Not shown: that a cluster's member predicts another.
- **Kind of result:** descriptive clustering of a large network; the reading draws no conclusion on how far site type explains the groups.
