# Buesa Pueyo 2018: leaf area per vine in three Valencian experiments

The thesis behind the closed Buesa et al. 2017 (Muscat deficit irrigation) and 2018 (late
leaf removal of Bobal and Tempranillo) papers the vine-leaf-area search wanted, plus a
row-orientation trial on potted vines. Read for one thing: leaf area per vine and LAI.

## What it holds

- **Experiment 1, Muscat of Alexandria** (l. 2637, 3114-3117): field at Vilamarxant,
  2012-2014, 2.75 × 1.8 m; LAI in 2012 2.1 (control), 1.4, 1.6 and 1.4 m² m⁻² under early,
  late and sustained deficit, averaging 1.8, 1.2, 1.3 and 1.3 over the years; leaf area from
  shoot length by y = 16.719 x + 486.64 cm² (r² 0.95) for main shoots and y = 19.129 x for
  laterals (l. 2881-2885, 20 shoots). Leaf area per vine in m² is not printed.
- **Experiment 2, potted Bobal and Verdejo, N-S against E-W rows** (l. 148, 3911,
  4153-4181, 4226): pots at 2.5 × 2 m, 2014-2016; leaf area per vine 6.3, 5.0 and 7.4 m²
  (Bobal N-S) and 6.7, 4.9 and 6.2 (E-W); Verdejo 6.1, 5.3 and 8.4 (N-S) and 6.7, 7.1 and
  8.0 (E-W); E-W rows cut transpiration 13 % (Bobal) and 7 % (Verdejo). Interception was not
  measured (l. 6021); the only interception evidence is Campos et al. 2017's simulations,
  cited (l. 4505).
- **Experiment 3, late leaf removal** (l. 5054-5070, 5192-5197): Bobal at 2.5 × 1.4 m and
  Tempranillo at 2.5 × 2.45 m, 2014-2015; total leaf area per vine after removal (Table 11)
  Bobal 2.9 and 5.7 m² (irrigated, undefoliated) against 1.7 and 3.7 (rainfed, defoliated),
  Tempranillo 5.5 and 4.4 against 3.0 and 3.1; LAI (Table 12) Bobal 0.76 and 1.52,
  Tempranillo 0.87 and 0.72 for the irrigated undefoliated vines; 28 % (Bobal) and 25 %
  (Tempranillo) of the leaf area removed. Table 12's column order is inferred from its note.
- **Caveats:** every leaf area is an allometric estimate from shoot lengths, not a measured
  area; Tables 11 and 12 do not reconcile exactly per ground area (the agent's arithmetic).

## Dependence

- Measurements and allometries of the author's own trials; a water-use to interception
  relation credited to Williams & Ayars 2005 and interception simulations to Campos et al.
  2017 (`campos2016`, listed in the 10-18 unread list). Nothing fitted to anything held.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- For Agrarium's `host.leaf_area_max_m2` (the 10-14 and vine-leaf-area searches): 3-6 m²
  per field vine for Bobal and Tempranillo at Valencian spacings, 5-8 m² for potted vines,
  with LAI 0.7-2.1. The two closed papers are no longer needed for these values.
