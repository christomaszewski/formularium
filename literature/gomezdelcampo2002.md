# Gómez-del-Campo et al. 2002: leaf area of Chardonnay and Airén through a season, with and without water stress

Three-year-old vines in 35 L weighing lysimeters at Madrid in 1995, watered to field capacity
(NS) or given half the unstressed vines' use, scaled by leaf area (S). Leaf area was followed
every two weeks. Read for the shape and timing of leaf-area growth in a Spanish white
variety.

## What it holds

- **Vines** (l. 70-90): Chardonnay and Airén on 1103 Paulsen, two shoots a vine, 11 vines a
  treatment; rain kept off by plastic. Stressed vines got W_S = 0.5 × W_NS × LA_S / LA_NS
  (p. 139). Predawn water potential about -0.17 MPa (NS) and -0.32 to -0.33 MPa (S) (Table 1).
- **Stages** (l. 115-120): budbreak 12 April (DOY 102), fruit set 14 June (DOY 165), veraison
  26 July (DOY 207), harvest 30 August (DOY 242).
- **Method** (p. 139): Carbonneau's vein-length method with a second-order fit per cultivar;
  all leaves measured with a LI-3100 at the end.
- **Leaf area at harvest** (Table 3, l. 288-291, page image; m² a vine, % in laterals, leaves,
  mean leaf size cm²): Chardonnay NS 0.998, 36.9, 355, 28.1; Airén NS 1.159, 46.7, 343, 33.8;
  Chardonnay S 0.251, 39.1, 159, 15.8; Airén S 0.563, 42.5, 212, 26.6. Stress did not change
  the share in laterals.
- **Mean leaf area by phase** (Table 4, l. 318-319, page image; budbreak-fruit set,
  fruit set-veraison, veraison-harvest, season): Chardonnay NS 0.143, 0.567, 0.867, 0.527;
  Airén NS 0.302, 0.813, 1.085, 0.731; Chardonnay S 0.095, 0.194, 0.215, 0.168; Airén S
  0.225, 0.475, 0.517, 0.406.
- **Timing** (l. 236-241): by fruit set (63 days) Airén had formed 47 % (NS) and 62 % (S) of
  its season's leaf area, Chardonnay 33 % (NS) and 66 % (S). Under stress growth came earlier:
  Chardonnay's almost stopped after fruit set, Airén's rose a little to veraison; unstressed
  Airén stopped at veraison, unstressed Chardonnay grew until harvest.
- **Figure 1** (page image, approximate): stressed Airén reached half its final area near DOY
  150 (about 48 days after budbreak) and its plateau near DOY 186 (about 84 days); unstressed
  Airén half near DOY 165 (63 days), plateau near DOY 214 (112 days).
- **Leaf size:** stress cut leaf number by 45 % and leaf size by 33 % on average (l. 250-253).

## Dependence

- Carbonneau's leaf-area method; Lissarrague's group (`baeza1994`, `linares2009`): a flag.
  The leaf areas are the authors' own. No model shaped the data.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- **Timing for a stressed Spanish white:** stressed Airén's curve (half at about 48 days,
  plateau at about 84) matches Agrarium's t50 = 50 d and tau = 10 d; unstressed vines grow
  later and longer (half at about 63 days, to veraison or beyond), the case for tau's range
  reaching 20 d. Potted two-shoot vines: the timing transfers, the areas do not (0.13 to
  0.58 m² a shoot at harvest).
