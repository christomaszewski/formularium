# GDPM 2017 (Corvallis): the downy mildew abstracts

The 8th workshop's abstract volume, fetched 2026-10-12 from a Wayback capture of the
WSU extension page (the 2006 and 2010 volumes are `pdmildew2006` and `gdpm2010china`'s
companions). Abstracts only: no equation, and most numbers are counts of trials.

## What it holds

- **Bleyer & Lösch (Freiburg), l. 705-764:** VM Plasmopara was VitiMeteo's first module,
  2002, computing "the most important steps of the infection cycle" (Bleyer et al. 2008);
  VM Growth, programmed with H.-R. Schultz, gives the leaf area of primary shoots by
  trellis; under heavy pressure 300-400 cm² of unprotected leaf (2-3 leaves) a shoot may
  grow before the next spray (Bleyer et al. 2003, IOBC Bull. 26(8):7-12); four trials
  2013-2016 of intervals at 400, 600 and 800 cm² with potassium phosphonate (registered
  in Germany in 2014): 600 cm² held with folpet, 800 did not under high pressure.
- **Díez-Navajas et al. (NEIKER), l. 337-384:** LIFE FITOVID spray schedules in one season,
  two areas a disease; the downy mildew risk schedule, from a station model the abstract
  calls "Gubler and Thomas", halved treatments against the grower's programme; the
  powdery schedule rests on Arens, Blaeser and Gehmann through a Pessl indicator.
- **Bove, Caffi, Rossi et al. (Piacenza), l. 1053-1086:** a generic STELLA simulation of
  downy mildew on leaves and bunches with five process groups and partial-resistance
  components, the model later published as `bove2020` and `bove2021`.
- **Gindro, Dubuis et al. (Agroscope), l. 1087-1135:** in vitro bunch infection only at
  BBCH 53, none at 69 and 75; two field seasons of artificial infections at BBCH 55, 65,
  75 and 81 on Chasselas under a roof, none at veraison; Divico infected sparsely at 55
  and 65 (the study published as Gindro et al. 2022, not held).
- **Mahaffee et al.:** large-eddy simulation of vineyard turbulence and spore plumes for
  sub-block risk (l. 1136-1167). **Hong et al. (Georgia):** 195 isolates from 12
  vineyards over 3 years, 77.9 % clade aestivalis; 344 isolates from one vineyard
  2014-2016, 173 genotypes, 62.5 % clonal; a spectrophotometric viability test, no
  response from 17-day-old sporangia (l. 1168-1250; published as `hong2020` leads).
  **Taylor & Wicks (WA):** no oospores in 2001-2003 leaf discs, oospores in 2015 samples,
  three of eleven pairings fertile (l. 1360-1382; cf. `hug2005`).

## Dependence

- none computed. The abstracts name VitiMeteo (`dubuis2012`), the Gubler-Thomas index
  and a Pessl indicator as the models the trials ran.

## Bearing (2026-10-13)

- Pointers only: the growth-based spray interval's thresholds (300-400 cm², Bleyer 2003)
  for a spray policy, and the 2017 state of the Piacenza simulator and the Swiss bunch
  infection work, both now held as papers.
