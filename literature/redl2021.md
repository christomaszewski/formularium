# Redl et al. 2021: when Austrian chasmothecia release ascospores

Potential release (laboratory assays of field-stored chasmothecia from three Austrian sites,
2017-2019) and actual release (spore traps in a fungicide-free plot at Krems), against
degree-days, rain and wetness.

## What it holds

- Potential release (24 h at 25 C, discs of chasmothecia from Krems, Vienna and Illmitz) (l.
  84-105).
- October 2017: no release. October 2018: 1.28, 3.68 and 12.10 ascospores per 100
  chasmothecia at Krems, Vienna and Illmitz (l. 163-168; Table 1). Viability 60-70% (2017)
  and 55-70% (2018) (l. 171-172).
- Maturity threshold: more than 480 degree days, base 8 C, counted from 15 Aug (abstract l.
  22-24; l. 131; l. 317-318). No fit or method for the 480 is printed.
- Degree days base 8 C, mid-Aug to Oct: 409-478 (2017) and 502-569 (2018). To March: 484-586
  (2017/18) and 638-765 (2018/19) (l. 260-268; Table 2).
- Spring 2018 release start: Krems 10 March, Illmitz 21 March, Vienna 31 March (l. 182-186).
  Last release 19 June, Vienna (l. 227).
- Potential release before bud break: 2017/18 4, 13 and 46%; 2018/19 42, 53 and 52% (l.
  243-249). The abstract's claim of more than 50% in both years (l. 25-26) is not supported
  for 2017/18.
- Field trapping at Krems (fungicide-free plot): before bud break 16% (2018) and 5% (2019);
  bud break to flowering 84% and 95% (l. 230-233).
- Correlations of trapped release with precipitation (2018 r = 0.94; 2019 r = 0.62) and leaf
  wetness (0.88; 0.56). Temperature not significant (l. 422-423; Table 3).
- Release peaks at 58 mm (2018) and 26 mm (2019) weekly rain (l. 244-246). The text says
  release bursts after more than 25 mm per week (l. 439-441).
- Claims of first autumn release in Central Europe (l. 276-278), which the authors state.
- Not printed: the fit for the 480 threshold, n for the correlations, per-date counts,
  weather data behind 'data not shown', data licence.

## Dependence

- Its own assays and trapping; no model dated the data. The degree-day method is credited to
  Moyer et al. 2014 (l. 129-130; literature/moyer2014), with base 8 °C from 15 August, not
  Moyer's base 0 °C from 1 January. The 480 degree-day maturity threshold is the authors'
  own; how it was fitted is not printed.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Cooptera and a powdery mildew truth: Central European release is spread from autumn to
  June, with 5-16 % of the trapped season's ascospores before budbreak and 84-95 % between
  budbreak and flowering at Krems, tied to weekly rain (r 0.94 and 0.62); a check on Moyer's
  New York curve, which put half the release before budbreak. No record: the 480 degree-day
  threshold has no printed fit.
- The journal's volume 128 is 2021; the article was online in 2020.
