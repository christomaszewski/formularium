# Vivin et al. 2017: process-based grapevine models linked to QTLs

A review of the French LACCAVE work that links process-based grapevine models to QTL analysis for stress tolerance and fruit quality. It prints no equations.

## What it holds

- **Model types:** crop models of the whole plant, phenology models (Parker et al. 2011, 2013), leaf gas exchange, plant water dynamics (Lebon et al. 2003) and berry growth (Dai et al. 2008) (l. 212-218).
- **QTL case:** a progeny of Vitis vinifera Cabernet-Sauvignon and V. riparia Gloire de Montpellier, with phenology-model parameters mapped to QTLs (Figure 2, l. 273-281).
- **Virtual genotypes:** budbreak, flowering and veraison dates computed for virtual genotypes under an IPCC scenario (l. 326-332).
- The text read prints no equations, coefficients or error statistics.

## Dependence

- Builds on Garcia de Cortazar-Atauri 2006 (STICS adapted to vine), Parker et al. 2011 and 2013 for phenology, Lebon et al. 2003, Dai et al. 2008, and Marguerit et al. 2011-2012 for the QTL approach (l. 212-218, 296-300; references).
