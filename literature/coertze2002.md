# Coertze & Holz 2002: dry airborne Botrytis conidia need free water to infect berry wounds

Fresh needle wounds on Dauphine berries at bunch closure, harvest and after cold storage
were exposed to dry airborne conidia, fresh or deposited four days earlier, and held dry,
moist or wet for 24 h at 22 °C. Infection stayed rare except on wet, cold-stored berries, and
conidia deposited before wounding hardly infected at all.

## What it holds

- **Berries** (l. 140-143): cv. Dauphine at bunch closure (6.3 °Brix) and harvest
  (16.6 °Brix), and mature berries after 3-4 weeks at -0.5 °C.
- **Wounds and inoculum** (l. 163-193): five needles 5 mm apart, 0.8 mm wounds 1.5 mm deep;
  3 mg of dry conidia settled for 20 min, about three conidia per mm², 91-99 % germination
  at 12 h; a Discussion figure of about 200 conidia on a 50 mm² court does not match
  (l. 507).
- **Regimes** (l. 204-211, 438-440): dry (40-50 % RH), moist (93 % RH or more; the OCR
  prints 293) and a water film, 24 h at 22 °C; wounds made 1 h after inoculation then 13 d
  dry, or conidia deposited 24 h wet then 3 d dry before wounding.
- **Infected wounds out of 120** (Table 2, page image): freshly deposited conidia, bunch
  closure 0.0 dry, 0.3 moist, 5.3 wet; harvest 0.2, 1.3, 3.5; cold-stored 3.8, 8.3, 24.8.
  Previously deposited: 0.0-0.2 at bunch closure and harvest, cold-stored 1.3, 2.0, 3.7.
- **ANOVA** (Table 1, l. 368-375): wetness F 136.94, stage F 124.78, inoculum type F 71.18,
  the three-way interaction F 14.55, all P 0.0001; experiment F 1.35, P 0.25 (two runs
  pooled).
- **Whole-berry rot** (l. 340-344): 6.7, 10.6 and 35.2 % with fresh conidia and 0, 0.9 and
  1.9 % with previously deposited, in the order dry, moist, wet.
- **Histology** (l. 307-311, 522-525): exudate on 49, 78 and 96 % of wounds at bunch
  closure, ripe and cold-stored stages; germ tubes 8 µm on moist berries, 30 µm on immature
  and 121 µm on mature berries.
- **Conclusions** (l. 494-496): free water was a prerequisite; the proportions infected were
  extremely low.

## Dependence

- **Observed** throughout: counts scored daily on 30-berry screens, no model in the data.
  The isolate and the 24 h wetness period come from Coertze & Holz 1999 and Coertze et al.
  2001 (l. 171, 212); the settling tower follows Salinas et al. 1989 (l. 183).
- No formulation. An observation dataset for Botrytis infection models.

## Bearing (2026-10-26)

- For Cooptera's Botrytis records (`broome1995.botrytis`, `gonzalezdominguez2015.botrytis`,
  the 10-20 search): a berry-wound infection series against wetness and stage at one
  temperature, with very low infection under dry and moist regimes. It calibrates nothing
  held; it bounds what a wetness-driven infection rule should give for wounds.
