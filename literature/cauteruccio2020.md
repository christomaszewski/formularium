# Cauteruccio & Lanza 2020: collection efficiency of a cylindrical rain gauge by rainfall intensity

A paper on a collection-efficiency parameterisation; only its introduction's cited figures for liquid undercatch were read.

**Read this first.** Both undercatch figures are cited from other works (Sevruk; Pollock et al. 2018), not measured here. The paper's own method was not read.

## What it holds

- **Typical loss (cited from Sevruk, l. 86):** wind-induced loss of catching gauges is 2 to 10 % for liquid precipitation and 10 to 50 % for solid.
- **Observed loss (cited from Pollock et al. 2018, l. 88):** an observed undercatch of about 10 to 23 % for liquid precipitation, at a lowland and an upland site.
- Pollock et al. 2018 (Water Resources Research 54) is open access but was refused to the reading machine; Sevruk 1982 (WMO Operational Hydrology Report 21) was not found online.

- Cites Sevruk (WMO) and Pollock et al. 2018. Nothing else recorded for the passage read.

## Bearing (2026-11-03)

- **sense.station_rain_factor (assumed 1.0):** cited figures only. They put the wind loss of a liquid-rain gauge at 2 to 10 % typically and 10 to 23 % in one field study, so a factor below 1 (about 0.77 to 0.98 on those figures, arithmetic not printed) is the documented direction.
