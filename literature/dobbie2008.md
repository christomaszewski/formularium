# Dobbie et al. 2008: spatial designs for sparse stream-network monitoring

A review of spatial sampling designs for monitoring stream networks, especially ephemeral ones, so that practical and optimal designs can be chosen (l. 20-26). No record.

## What it holds

- **Scope:** probability and model-based designs, variable-probability designs and stratification (l. 573), and the generalised random-tessellation stratified (GRTS) design used in the US EMAP programme (l. 783-795).
- **Content in the abstract:** a review for decisions on future stream monitoring; no data, equations or error figures are given (l. 24-26).

None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-18)

- **Strand s6 (methods):** context for how to place a few sensors in a spatial domain with a design that stays balanced. A simulator could take GRTS-type balanced sampling when choosing sensor sites in a block. Streams, not vineyards.
