# Kocsis et al. 2018: BABA at flowering, cluster compactness and Botrytis bunch rot

Spraying BABA on flowers of 'Királyleányka' in Pécs, 2012 to 2014, made clusters looser through female sterility of the flowers. In the wet 2013 season it also cut bunch rot.

## What it holds

- Compactness index: control 2.87 ± 0.83 berries per cm, 2.18 ± 0.77 at 2.0 g/L BABA and 1.90 ± 0.72 at 3.0 g/L (l. 32-34; table l. 165-167).
- 2013 incidence: 46.3% on control clusters (l. 147). At 2.0 g/L it fell below 11.6% (l. 151). At 1.0 and 3.0 g/L it was 17.1% and 33.0% (l. 152-153).
- Rates of 1.0, 2.0 and 3.0 g/L were sprayed at BBCH 65 (l. 67). In 2012 only 2.0 g/L was used (l. 123).
- Incidence depended on disease pressure, set by rain during ripening (l. 35).

## Dependence

- The concentrations were based on the authors' earlier grey mould trial (Csikász-Krizsics et al. 2013) and on Reuveni et al. 2001, a field trial against downy mildew.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (Botrytis bunch rot), with t5 (cluster compactness as a quality factor). Looser clusters cut rot in 2013, but the paper ties incidence to ripening rain (l. 35). A simulator would need a weather term with it.
- Three seasons, one cultivar and one vineyard. The best rate tested was 2.0 g/L. Not read in full.
