# Fouillet et al. 2024: ten years of cutting pesticides in French DEPHY vineyards

161 vineyard systems of the DEPHY network in 12 French regions with at least six years of
data, 2010-2019: how treatment frequency, costs, work time and yield moved as growers cut
pesticides.

## What it holds

- HAL copy of Fouillet et al. (European Journal of Agronomy 158:127199, 2024; doi
  10.1016/j.eja.2024.127199). Data from 161 DEPHY network vineyard systems in 12 French
  winegrowing regions, with at least six years of data over 2010 to 2019 (l. 102-113).
- Fungicide TFI (TFIf) fell from 10.6 ± 4.9 to 7 ± 5.4, a 34 % mean reduction; herbicide TFI
  (TFIh) from 1.2 ± 1.5 to 0.5 ± 0.9, 58 % (l. 281-284).
- Systems using herbicides: 27 % in 2019 against 43 % at the start (l. 284-285).
- Operating costs fell from 648.2 ± 402.7 to 349.9 ± 707 € per ha, 46 % (p < 0.01) (l.
  292-294).
- Mechanical work 26.6 ± 17.3 to 21.6 ± 28 h per ha; fuel 247 ± 132 to 286 ± 288 L per ha;
  tractor labour cost 427.2 ± 311.8 to 347.3 ± 445.8 € per ha: no significant change (l.
  295-299).
- Yield 63.4 ± 22.3 to 65.7 ± 23.9 hL per ha (l. 299-300); no fungicide-yield effect
  (abstract, l. 43-44).
- PLS-PM: GoF 0.44; weed-control path beta 0.18 (l. 84).
- Cited: 33 % TFI reduction (Fouillet et al. 2022); French vineyard TFI 12.4, fungicides 81
  % (Simonovici and Caray 2021).
- Not reported: disease incidence or severity; year-by-year yields (Fig. 4 not printed).

## Dependence

- Network data reported by farms and advisers; a PLS path model, no epidemic model.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- For Agrarium's economics and Cooptera's case to growers (t5): fungicide treatment
  frequency fell 34 % (10.6 to 7.0) and operating costs 46 % (648 to 350 € a ha) with yield
  unchanged (63.4 to 65.7 hL a ha) and work time not significantly changed; disease levels
  are not reported, so it bounds what fewer sprays cost, not what they risk.
