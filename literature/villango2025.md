# Villangó et al. 2025: late pruning and apical defoliation on Kékfrankos

A two-vintage trial in the Eger region of late pruning and late apical leaf removal, alone and together, on Kékfrankos. The paper reports phenology, yield, berry composition and xylem sap carbohydrates.

## What it holds

- Six treatments: control, late apical defoliation, late pruning 1 and 2, and their combinations, in 2022 and 2023 (l. 151).
- Late pruning cut yield and bunch size, most in LP2 in 2023 (l. 26–27).
- LP2 gave the highest total polyphenol content in the grape skins in both years (l. 31–34).
- Xylem sap glucose, fructose and myo-inositol did not change significantly (l. 29–31).

## Dependence

- None recorded.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Off the point for the disease strands: no disease or pest is scored. It is a canopy-management trade-off (t5).
