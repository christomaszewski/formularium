# Latorre et al. 2015: review of grey mould on Chilean grapes

A review of grey mould on grapes, with Chilean table and wine production in view. It covers biology, epidemic rate, control and harvest tolerance levels.

## What it holds

- Epidemic rate: in New Zealand vineyards, severity can rise by 1 to 2% per day near harvest (l. 417-418, citing Evans 2010).
- Harvest tolerance: below 0.5% for table grapes and 2.0% for wine grapes (l. 440-441).
- Removing senescent debris cut grey mould incidence and severity by about 30% (l. 534-536), a figure the review takes from other studies.

## Dependence

- Extends Latorre (2007), a review by the first author (l. 77). The 1 to 2% per day rate and the 30% debris figure are cited from other studies.

## Bearing (2026-10-20)

- Strand t4 (grey mould) and t5 (losses). The harvest tolerance levels (0.5% and 2.0%) are thresholds a yield or quality model could report against.
- The 1 to 2% per day rate is one New Zealand estimate, not a Chilean fit. Not read in full.
