# Nityagovsky et al. 2025: qPCR identification of P. viticola

Research article, Russian Far East (Primorskii and Khabarovsk krai, Sakhalin; samples collected July 2022).

## What it holds

- A SYBR Green real-time PCR method with six primer pairs detects P. viticola early in Far Eastern grape species and varieties; the best pair, PvITS1_2-real-s/a, correlates R = 0.86 with metataxonomic data.
- It is a detection method with no epidemic formulation.

## Dependence

- No formulation of its own that a tool could run.
- No author of a model the engine runs.

## Bearing (2026-10-08)

- A detection method from the Russian Far East; no field detection rates.
