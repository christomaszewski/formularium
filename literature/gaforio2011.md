# Gaforio, García-Muñoz, Cabello & Muñoz-Organero 2011: powdery mildew resistance of 159 varieties at El Encín

Natural-infection scores of leaf and bunch resistance to Erysiphe necator for 159 Vitis vinifera cultivars, 2006 to 2009, in one Madrid collection; it holds Macabeo, Xarel·lo and Parellada but not Chardonnay.

**Read this first.** This is powdery mildew, not downy mildew. The table gives one modal class (l, m or h) per cultivar for bunches (B) and leaves (L), not a ratio. The authors say their Macabeo result differs from Li 1993 and may be due to "diverse homonyms for Macabeo".

## What it holds

- **Material:** 473 samples from 159 cultivars, 2 to 7 clones a cultivar, 4 plants a clone, in the El Encín germplasm bank (IMIDRA, Alcalá de Henares), 2006 to 2009; all grafted on 41B and almost 30 years old, 2.5 m × 2.5 m, dry land, no phytosanitary treatment; Mazuela as the susceptibility control, spread over twelve places in the plot (l. 15-30).
- **Scoring:** natural infection, visual, June to September (about 3 weeks after the onset of flowering for leaves, before the vintage for bunches), OIV 455 (leaves) and 456 (bunches), 1 (very low resistance) to 9 (high); classes l = 1 and 3 (high susceptibility), m = 5, h = 7 and 9 (low susceptibility) (l. 34-44, 68). The table lists the modal class over the years for the maximum resistance each year; its columns are B then L (l. 71).
- **Cava whites (B / L):** Xarel.lo h / l, that is bunches resist and leaves are susceptible (l. 108); Parellada h / h (l. 113); Macabeo m / m (l. 78). Cabernet Sauvignon l / l, like the control Mazuela (l. 75). Chardonnay is not in the table.
- **Distribution:** the medium level was the most frequent, 35 to 50 % of varieties depending on the year (l. 135); 13 to 52 % were very susceptible on bunches and 13 to 36 % had low susceptibility, by year (l. 139-142). Over the years 35 cultivars (22 %) were very susceptible on bunches and another 83 (52.2 %) had low susceptibility (l. 148-150).
- **Macabeo caveat:** the authors found Macabeo less susceptible, relative to the control, than Li 1993 reported, and suggest that homonyms could explain it (l. 173-175).
- **Morphology:** leaf and bunch resistance correlate, with rho 0.75 in 2008, 0.52 in 2007 and 0.38 in 2009 (l. 177-185). Of fourteen ampelographic characters, only two correlated with resistance: leaf goffering (rho −0.16 to −0.22) and bunch density against berry resistance (rho −0.17), so denser bunches are slightly more susceptible, a weak effect (l. 176-179).

## Dependence

Own natural-infection scoring; results are compared with Li 1993 and Pérós et al. 2006 (cited, not read). Shares its authors and its collection with `gaforio2015` (downy mildew, a different plot): a flag, not a dependency.

## Bearing (2026-10-22)

- **Cultivar susceptibility (powdery mildew only):** Macabeu medium on bunches and leaves; Xarel·lo high bunch resistance but low leaf resistance; Parellada high on both, the least susceptible of the three. Chardonnay is absent, so the four cultivars cannot be ranked here, and the Macabeu class carries the authors' homonym doubt.
- **Host parameters in host.yaml:** none; the paper has no phenology or bunch counts. host.rachis_only_factor concerns downy mildew on rachises and gets nothing from this paper.
- **Kind of result:** modal class under natural infection, four years, one site, three classes only; a ranking, not a susceptibility ratio.
