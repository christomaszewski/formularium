# Herrera et al. 2017: a laboratory life table for Lobesia botrana

A horizontal life table from one cohort of 250 eggs reared at INTA Mendoza. It gives the demographic parameters of the European grapevine moth under laboratory conditions.

## What it holds

- Cohort of 250 eggs, reared at 25 °C, 16:8 light and 30–50% relative humidity (l. 124, 155, 232).
- Intrinsic rate of increase 0.137, net reproductive rate 66.82, generation time 30.51 days, finite rate 1.073, doubling time 9.76 days (l. 33–35).
- Gross fecundity 134.84 ± 15.68 eggs per female (l. 35, 188). Female longevity 9.25 ± 1.67 days and male 8.64 ± 0.83 days (l. 37–38).
- The results give generation time as 30.61 days (l. 238), against 30.51 in the abstract (l. 34). Check which is right.

## Dependence

- Uses the horizontal life table method of Rabinovich 1978 and Portilla et al. 2013. Rearing follows Herrera et al. 2016.
