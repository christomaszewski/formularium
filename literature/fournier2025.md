# Fournier et al. 2025: a microbiome paper that names IFV's EPIcure network of untreated controls

Read only for where its disease records came from: IFV's EPIcure network.

**Read this first.** The citation is Crossref's record (`trail`), not read on the paper's first page. The network is
the source family of Chen et al. 2020's S1 data, which Agrarium already knows.

## What it holds

- **The network:** IFV observes downy mildew on "a network of 1200 Untreated Controls" (l. 326),
  and the paper's records were "downloaded from" EPIcure (l. 333).
- **Terms:** the EPIcure site is behind a login, marked "Tous droits réservés" (page, l. 43);
  it carries no weather of its own beyond forecast links.
