# Jensen et al. 2025: trends in disease prediction models

Review, Global literature (authors at CQUniversity, Australia).

## What it holds

- A systematic review of 104 publications reporting 146 disease predictive models for four crop groups (cereals, grape, potato, citrus), assessed for modelling approach, data inputs and performance.
- It reports the shift from rule-based and mechanistic models to machine learning (SVM, random forest) and deep learning; it gives no model equations in the lines read.
